SACTCheck v0.76.3 - HCC anatomical attribution and connector update

Install
Unzip at the top of the SACTCheck repository and copy the included js/,
css/, data/, assets/, docs/, tests/ and tools/ folders over the matching
folders. Replace matching files. Commit the unpacked files, not the ZIP.
Publish to the branch used by the Pages workflow (main) after review.

This archive is consolidated with v0.76.2 and the QR publication guard. It
contains the updated app preview and print output, public HCC portal, A4 PDF,
anatomy asset, reusable CSS and connector renderer, icons, passport, card,
and relevant tests. Other regimens and files are not changed by this archive.

What changed
- Purple numbered circles and clearly labelled cards identify possible
  atezolizumab immune effects. A coral numbered diamond, dashed line and
  clearly labelled card identify bevacizumab vessel/healing effects.
- The kidney and bowel cards separately state the relevant bevacizumab risk
  instead of merging both medicines into an undifferentiated label.
- The bowel marker is over bowel, heart over heart, liver over liver, kidneys
  over kidney, joint over knee and vessel marker over calf vessels.
- Thin lines connect cards to numbered markers in desktop and A4 print.
  On narrow screens the lines disappear and numbers preserve the mapping.
- The two-page PDF has a larger QR for the comprehensive public HCC guide.
- Shared CSS and positioning script support later reuse with other anatomy
  assets. The final Pages build checks for the HCC route and these assets.

Verification completed
