Commit description
Expands the SACTCheck knowledge base from eighteen to twenty one detailed regimen profiles.

Adds complete knowledge profiles for enfortumab vedotin plus pembrolizumab, nivolumab plus modified FOLFOX6, and nivolumab plus ipilimumab.

Adds structured treatment context, patient selection, supportive care, monitoring, priority toxicity, administration, medicine mechanism and evidence completeness content for each regimen.

Adds indication specific evidence for CheckMate 648 so the oesophageal squamous indication within NCCP 00844 is not incorrectly represented by CheckMate 649 alone. Adds CheckMate 067 and final ten year follow up for advanced melanoma, plus CheckMate 204 as clearly labelled contextual CNS evidence. Adds mature EV 302 and CheckMate 649 follow up.

Corrects three source confirmed emetogenic classifications identified during the evidence review. NCCP 00844 now maps to moderate risk through oxaliplatin. NCCP 00945 now maps to low risk through enfortumab vedotin. NCCP 00431 now maps to low risk through ipilimumab. The existing traffic light system and national NCCP antiemetic guidance links are preserved.

No deterministic treatment rule, eligibility rule, dose action, treatment schedule, organ function pathway or clinical input definition is changed. Independent consultant oncology and oncology pharmacy validation remains pending.
