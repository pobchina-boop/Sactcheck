SUMMARY
Fix patient QR links to use the public SACTCheck regimen URL

DESCRIPTION
- Fixes patient-facing QR codes that were generated from the current browser URL and therefore encoded unusable file:// or localhost addresses during local demonstrations.
- Makes every regimen QR use the canonical public HTTPS destination on sactcheck.com.
- Keeps the QR patient agnostic by encoding only the regimen identifier and no patient data.
- Makes the on-screen QR clickable for easier desktop demonstrations.
- Updates the Copy regimen link action to copy the public regimen URL.
- Patches the printable treatment passport so its displayed URL and QR use the same canonical public destination.
- Retains the existing clinician/patient interface split, toxicity-at-a-glance view, treatment schedule and print-first patient passport.
- Adds regression coverage preventing local file paths or localhost addresses from being encoded in patient QR links.
- Makes no change to protocol JSON, treatment thresholds, eligibility rules, dose actions or deterministic clinical assessment logic.
