# SACTCheck v0.42.0 — Complete Neuro-oncology Library

## Scope

This release adds the complete current NCCP Neuro-oncology SACT catalogue as active encoded assessment protocols.

## Inventory

- 10 active Neuro-oncology protocols
- No Neuro-oncology placeholders or draft cards
- 280 distinct protocols across the complete SACTCheck library
- 201 Neuro-oncology input definitions
- 245 source-specific decision rules
- 180 rule-linked fields individually checked for single-entry assessment

## Clinical pathways added

- Bevacizumab monotherapy for recurrent glioblastoma
- CLV for high-risk medulloblastoma / PNET
- PCV 42-day and 56-day schedules
- Lomustine monotherapy
- Lomustine plus bevacizumab 5 mg/kg and 7.5 mg/kg schedules
- Recurrent temozolomide therapy
- Standard concomitant radiotherapy plus adjuvant temozolomide
- Short-course radiotherapy plus adjuvant temozolomide for patients over 65 years
