SUMMARY
Streamline consent PDFs and add modular agent search

DESCRIPTION
- Replaces the full-screen consent-builder workflow with direct two-page PDF generation from regimen cards.
- Adds a Consent PDF button that immediately downloads a compact A4 consent form for the selected regimen.
- Constrains consent output to exactly two pages so it can be printed as one double-sided A4 sheet.
- Structures the PDF around generic SACT/chemotherapy consent, regimen- and agent-specific material risks, and immunotherapy risks when applicable.
- Retains blank patient identifiers, expected-benefit, alternatives, no-treatment, patient-specific-risk and signature fields for completion in the approved clinical record or printed form.
- Adds a compact + Agent control to each regimen card.
- Adds generic-name and trade-name search for clinician-selected additional agents, including aliases such as Avastin and Keytruda.
- Allows multiple clinician-added agents to be selected, displayed as removable chips and incorporated into the generated consent PDF.
- Automatically activates the immunotherapy consent section when a clinician-added immune-checkpoint inhibitor is selected.
- Clearly labels added medicines as CLINICIAN ADDED and states that their inclusion does not represent NCCP endorsement of the modified regimen.
- Keeps added-agent selections in session memory only and does not persist consent or patient data to localStorage or sessionStorage.
- Preserves explicit manual-review warnings for unmapped medicines rather than inventing consent content.
- Keeps current NCCP sources, HSE consent policy, current medicine information and clinician judgement authoritative.
- Preserves v0.70.0/v0.70.1 clinical reconciliation and makes no change to protocol JSON, treatment thresholds, dose actions or deterministic assessment logic.
