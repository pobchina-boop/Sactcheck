# SACTCheck v0.67.0 evidence source register

Source check date: 15 August 2026

## NCCP 00689 Version 4

Atezolizumab, carboplatin AUC 5 and etoposide 100 mg/m², 21 day.

Official NCCP source:
https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6882/689_V4_Atezolizumab_Etoposide_CARBOplatin.pdf

Principal evidence:

* IMpower133 primary publication
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1809064
* Updated overall survival
  https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.20.01055
* Five year survival follow up
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39306923/

Evidence boundary:
The original randomised trial used intravenous atezolizumab. Current NCCP intravenous and subcutaneous formulation options and alternative administration schedules are represented as current protocol context rather than as randomised IMpower133 comparisons.

## NCCP 00204 Version 11

Pertuzumab, trastuzumab and docetaxel, 21 day cycle.

Official NCCP source:
https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6545/204_v11_PertuzumabTrasDoc.pdf

Principal evidence:

* CLEOPATRA primary publication
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1113216
* Final overall survival analysis
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1413513
* End of study analysis
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32171426/

Evidence boundary:
CLEOPATRA defines the first line metastatic strategy. Later HER2 directed therapies affect subsequent sequencing but are not used to rewrite the encoded first line NCCP pathway.

## NCCP 00415 Version 4

Dabrafenib and trametinib.

Official NCCP source:
https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6732/415_v4_Trametinib_and_DabrafenibTherapy.pdf

Principal evidence:

* COMBI d
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1406037
* COMBI v
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1412690
* Pooled five year metastatic outcomes
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1904059
* COMBI AD primary publication
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1708539
* COMBI AD five year analysis
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2005493
* COMBI AD final overall survival analysis
  https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2404139

Evidence boundary:
The metastatic and adjuvant settings are shown separately. The 2024 final COMBI AD analysis is presented accurately: relapse free and distant metastasis free benefit remained durable, while the overall survival analysis did not meet the prespecified statistical threshold for significance.
