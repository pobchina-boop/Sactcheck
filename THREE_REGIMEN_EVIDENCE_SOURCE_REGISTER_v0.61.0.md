## NCCP 00321 v9 — XELOX/CAPOX

- Official NCCP protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6719/321_v9_XELOX_.pdf`
- NO16968 — adjuvant stage III colon cancer: PMID 21383294; final survival PMID 26324362.
- NO16966 — first-line metastatic colorectal cancer: PMID 18421053; updated survival PMID 21673685.
- CLASSIC — adjuvant gastric cancer after D2 gastrectomy: PMID 22226517; five-year follow-up PMID 25439693.
- IDEA collaboration — three versus six months of oxaliplatin-based adjuvant therapy: PMID 29590544; final overall-survival analysis PMID 33271092.

**Boundary:** IDEA is duration context and does not create a universal three-month rule. Disease risk, regimen and consultant judgement remain relevant.

## NCCP 00226 v9 — weekly paclitaxel 80 mg/m²

- Official NCCP protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6562/226_V9_Paclitaxel_80.pdf`
- CALGB 9840 — weekly versus every-three-week paclitaxel in metastatic breast cancer: PMID 18375893.
- GOG weekly paclitaxel phase II — platinum/paclitaxel-resistant ovarian cancer: PMID 16325893.
- Yamamoto phase II — relapsed/refractory small-cell lung cancer: PMID 16739353.
- Sideris cohort — post-platinum metastatic urothelial cancer: PMID 27284445.

**Boundary:** the breast indication has randomised phase III evidence. The ovarian and SCLC evidence is phase II, while the bladder record is retrospective/contextual. These are not presented as equivalent evidence levels.

## NCCP 00776 v3b — trastuzumab deruxtecan

- Official NCCP protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6661/Trastuzumab_776_Deruxtecan_5.4mg_Therapy.pdf`
- DESTINY-Breast03 — HER2-positive metastatic breast cancer after trastuzumab and a taxane: PMID 35320644; updated analysis PMID 36495879; long-term survival PMID 38825627.
- DESTINY-Breast04 — HER2-low unresectable/metastatic breast cancer after chemotherapy: PMID 35665782.

**Boundary:** the evidence profile does not replace NCCP eligibility, HER2 testing requirements, ILD/pneumonitis safeguards, LVEF monitoring or local prescribing governance.
