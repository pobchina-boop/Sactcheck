SACTCheck v0.74.1 - QR ROUTING HOTFIX
OLD-STYLE DRAG / DROP / REPLACE

WHY THIS IS NEEDED
The v0.74.0 QR used the current browser address. When you demonstrated SACTCheck
from C:\...\index.html or localhost, the QR encoded that local address.
A phone cannot open a path on your PC, so the QR appeared to do nothing.

WHAT v0.74.1 DOES
- Every patient QR now points to:
  https://sactcheck.com/?patientSupport=<REGIMEN_ID>
- The QR contains a regimen identifier only. No patient data.
- The QR image is also clickable during a desktop demonstration.
- "Copy regimen link" copies the public HTTPS link rather than file:// or localhost.
- Printable passports have the public URL and canonical QR patched in before printing.
- Existing clinician / patient interface and printable passport remain unchanged.

IMPORTANT
The QR will open the deployed public patient-facing experience only AFTER this
update has been pushed to the live sactcheck.com site. Local testing can verify
the encoded destination, but a phone cannot load unpublished local changes.

APPLY
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Ctrl+A -> Copy.
3. Paste into:
   C:\Users\Paul\Documents\GitHub\Sactcheck
4. Choose Replace.
5. Close all SACTCheck tabs.
6. Re-open and press Ctrl+F5 once.
7. Run:
   node tests\patient-qr-hotfix-v0741.test.js
   npm.cmd test
8. Push to GitHub / Pages.
9. Once the live deployment is complete, scan a newly generated QR with your phone.

SMOKE TEST
- Open any regimen -> Patient support -> Printable passport.
- The displayed URL should begin https://sactcheck.com/?patientSupport=
- Click the QR on desktop: it should open the public regimen link in a new tab.
- Scan the same QR on a phone after deployment: it should open SACTCheck directly
  into the patient-facing support for that regimen.
