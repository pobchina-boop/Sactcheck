# SACTCheck v0.60.0 — Five-Regimen Knowledge-Base Source Register

Source-check date: 6 August 2026

## Governance

This register documents the sources used for the v0.60.0 knowledge layer. It does not replace the official NCCP protocol and does not validate the deterministic assessment rules. All five profiles remain pending independent consultant oncology and oncology-pharmacy review.

## NCCP 00227 v9 — FOLFIRI

- Official source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6563/227_v9_FOLFIRI.pdf
- Source title: FOLFIRI Therapy — 14 day
- Protocol version: 9
- Stated review date: 27 January 2030
- Knowledge scope: advanced colorectal cancer, metastatic oesophageal cancer and second-line locally advanced/metastatic pancreatic cancer; shared-indication limitations are displayed.
- Primary evidence: V303; Douillard JY et al. Lancet 2000.
- PMID: 10744089
- DOI: 10.1016/S0140-6736(00)02034-1
- Mapping limitation: evidence supports the metastatic colorectal backbone and is not exact evidence for the other NCCP indications.

## NCCP 00303 v6 — Carboplatin AUC 5–7.5 and paclitaxel 175 mg/m²

- Official source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6709/303_V6_CARBOplatin_AUC5-7.5_PACLitaxel_175.pdf
- Protocol version: 6
- Stated review date: 16 April 2030
- Knowledge scope: multi-indication gynaecological platinum-taxane regimen.
- Primary evidence: GOG-158; Ozols RF et al. J Clin Oncol 2003.
- PMID: 12860964
- DOI: 10.1200/JCO.2003.02.153
- Mapping limitation: pivotal evidence is from optimally resected stage III ovarian cancer at carboplatin AUC 7.5 and does not validate every NCCP indication or AUC choice.

## NCCP 00579 v5a — Pembrolizumab, paclitaxel and carboplatin AUC6

- Official source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6887/579_V5a_pembrolizumab_carboplatin_paclitaxel.pdf
- Protocol version: 5a
- Stated review date: 24 May 2029
- Knowledge scope: first-line metastatic squamous non-small-cell lung cancer.
- Primary evidence: KEYNOTE-407; Paz-Ares L et al. N Engl J Med 2018.
- PMID: 30280635
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa1810865
- Mapping limitation: the trial permitted paclitaxel or nab-paclitaxel; NCCP 00579 specifies paclitaxel 200 mg/m².

## NCCP 00794 v3 — Sacituzumab govitecan

- Official source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6417/794_v3_Sacituzumab_Govitecan_Therapy.pdf
- Protocol version: 3
- Stated review date: 4 March 2030
- Knowledge scope: unresectable or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer after at least two prior systemic therapies, including at least one in advanced disease.
- Primary evidence: ASCENT; Bardia A et al. N Engl J Med 2021.
- PMID: 33882206
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2028485
- Mapping limitation: the primary efficacy population excluded patients with brain metastases.

## NCCP 00897 v3 — Durvalumab, gemcitabine and cisplatin

- Official source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6652/897_v3_Durvalumab_Gem_Cis.pdf
- Protocol version: 3
- Stated review date: 20 February 2026
- Knowledge scope: first-line unresectable or metastatic biliary tract cancer.
- Primary evidence: TOPAZ-1; Oh DY et al. NEJM Evidence 2022.
- PMID: 38319896
- DOI: 10.1056/EVIDoa2200015
- Updated survival publication: PMID 38823398; DOI 10.1016/S2468-1253(24)00095-5
- Source-status limitation: the official PDF remains available on the NCCP site, but its stated review date has passed. The live NCCP listing must be checked before relying on version status.
