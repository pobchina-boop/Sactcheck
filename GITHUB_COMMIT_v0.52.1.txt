Commit summary

Remove dosing-panel clutter and show only genuine protocol dose data (v0.52.1)

Commit description

Introduces SACTCheck v0.52.1 as a corrective refinement of the embedded Protocol Dose & Schedule workspace following clinical interface review.

Changes include:

- Removes the duplicated protocol assessment and CTCAE rule cards from the dosing panel
- Stops treating eligibility, blood-count, contraindication and general assessment rules as dose-modification pathways
- Removes all “Not structured” dose and route placeholders
- Hides the Dose & Schedule button when useful structured dosing data are unavailable
- Displays a compact schedule only when actual component doses and treatment days are structured
- Displays genuine protocol dose-level tables with starting dose, dose level -1, dose level -2 and subsequent defined levels
- Adds a single selector to highlight the chosen dose-level column without calculating a patient-specific dose
- Prevents partial single-component data from being presented as the full schedule for a multi-agent regimen
- Retains direct access to the official NCCP protocol

The corrected viewer is available for 82 protocols: 77 with complete structured schedules and 23 with genuine dose-level tables. The button is hidden for the remaining 294 protocols until adequate dosing metadata are added.

This release does not calculate BSA, renal function, carboplatin dose or a final prescribed dose, and it contains no ONCOassist link.

All clinical protocol JSON files remain byte-for-byte unchanged from v0.52.0. No clinical assessment rules, thresholds, dose recommendations or treatment outputs were changed.

The full security, protocol-publishing, historical regression, Pages-build and deployable-site validation pipeline passed. Independent Consultant Oncology and oncology-pharmacy validation remains required.
