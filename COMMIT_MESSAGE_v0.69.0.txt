Summary
Add Sustainability module and evidence framework v0.69.0

Description
Adds a clinician-facing Sustainability module to SACTCheck with neutral visual guidance covering clinical value and treatment optimisation, route and administration, treatment attendance and travel, medicines waste, deprescribing, and lifestyle/supportive care.

Adds a Sustainability entry point to the main SACTCheck workflow and regimen-level Sustainability actions that carry available catalogue context into the module.

Introduces evidence-maturity language distinguishing measured, modelled, mechanism-supported and unquantified sustainability information.

Adds source-linked evidence covering de-escalation, ESMO magnitude of clinical benefit, vial sharing, dose banding, subcutaneous formulations, sustainable prescribing, intravenous versus oral medicines, deprescribing, lifestyle medicine and nutrition.

Avoids unsupported regimen carbon scoring, invented kg CO2e values and red/amber/green environmental rankings. Sustainability information remains explicitly secondary to clinical efficacy, safety, patient preference, NCCP treatment criteria and clinical judgement.

Preserves the existing deterministic assessment engine, protocol JSON library, Rule Explorer, NCCP Change Tracker and clinical validation workspace unchanged.
