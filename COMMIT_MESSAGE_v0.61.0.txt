feat(knowledge-base): add three evidence-audited regimen profiles

Expands the SACTCheck knowledge base from fifteen to eighteen complete regimen profiles by adding XELOX/CAPOX, weekly paclitaxel and trastuzumab deruxtecan.

Adds indication-specific evidence mapping for NO16966, NO16968, CLASSIC, IDEA, CALGB 9840, weekly paclitaxel studies in ovarian cancer and SCLC, the contextual bladder cohort, DESTINY-Breast03 and DESTINY-Breast04. Mature follow-up publications are linked where available, and each evidence record is visibly classified as protocol-defining, indication-supporting, practice-changing subsequent, contextual/sequencing or limiting evidence.

Introduces an evidence-completeness audit for all eighteen knowledge profiles. The audit checks foundational evidence, every encoded indication, later add-on or combination evidence, mature follow-up, sequencing context and important applicability limitations. It closes material gaps in previously published multi-indication profiles, including metastatic pancreatic evidence for modified FOLFIRINOX, pertuzumab component evidence for TCHP, metastatic colorectal and upper-GI evidence for mFOLFOX-6 and FOLFIRI, and cervical/endometrial evidence for the shared carboplatin-paclitaxel profile.

Preserves RECOURSE and SUNLIGHT as separate monotherapy and contextual-combination evidence on the Lonsurf page. Residual uncertainties are declared rather than hidden, including the evidence-strength gradient across weekly paclitaxel indications and the lack of an exact all-histology oesophageal randomised FOLFIRI trial in this audit.

Adds visible evidence-relationship badges, per-profile evidence-audit panels, a source register, gap-audit report, focused regression coverage and a new protocol hash register. No clinical protocol JSON, deterministic threshold, dose action, organ-function rule or assessment-engine behaviour is changed. Independent consultant oncology and oncology-pharmacy review remains pending.
