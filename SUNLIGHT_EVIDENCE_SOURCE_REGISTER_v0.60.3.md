## Primary publication

Prager GW, Taieb J, Fakih M, et al. Trifluridine–Tipiracil and Bevacizumab in Refractory Metastatic Colorectal Cancer. *New England Journal of Medicine*. 2023;388:1657–1667.

- PMID: 37133585
- DOI: 10.1056/NEJMoa2214963
- Design: international phase 3 randomised open-label active-controlled trial
- Population: 492 adults with refractory metastatic colorectal cancer after two previous chemotherapy regimens
- Intervention: trifluridine/tipiracil plus bevacizumab
- Comparator: trifluridine/tipiracil alone
- Primary endpoint: overall survival
- Median overall survival: 10.8 versus 7.5 months; hazard ratio 0.61
- Median progression-free survival: 5.6 versus 2.4 months; hazard ratio 0.44
