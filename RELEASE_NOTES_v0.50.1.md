# SACTCheck v0.50.1 — Clinical Copy Refinement

This release implements direct consultant feedback on clinical readability and removes duplicated Haematology navigation from the Solid Tumour library.

## Changes

- Removes the Haematology tile and limited coverage landing panel from Solid Tumour tumour site navigation.
- Keeps Haematology available only through the dedicated Haematology portal.
- Corrects the Solid Tumour “All” count to exclude Haematology protocols.
- Filters favourites and recent protocols by the active library so Haematology regimens do not appear in Solid Tumour quick access.
- Removes the large patient information card from the study hero.
- Retains a shorter privacy reminder in the dismissible feasibility notice.
- Replaces technical generic result wording with concise clinical wording.
- Removes unnecessary hyphenated wording from prominent interface copy.

## Revised restrictive result

**Treatment criteria not met**

One or more values entered fall outside the protocol treatment criteria.

## Clinical content

No protocol rule, threshold, source link or treatment encoding was changed. The catalogue remains 376 protocols: 361 Solid Tumour and 15 Haematology.
