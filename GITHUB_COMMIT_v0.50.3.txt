Commit summary

Improve regimen card clarity and attribute component-specific toxicities (v0.50.3)

Commit description

Introduces SACTCheck v0.50.3 with clearer regimen cards and more clinically specific toxicity labels across the full protocol library.

Changes include:

- Replaces the homepage “361 + 15” statistic with the single total of 376 protocols
- Shows component medicines directly on cards for structured combinations and recognised regimen acronyms, including CyBorD
- Standardises Haematology cycle metadata to the Solid Tumour card format, such as q21d and q28d
- Adds the causative medicine in parentheses to CTCAE and toxicity labels when one component is consistently identified by the encoded source-linked rules
- Applies toxicity attribution across both Solid Tumour and Haematology regimens
- Leaves ambiguous or multi-agent toxicity fields unattributed to avoid overstating causality
- Preserves all existing clinical thresholds, rule conditions, dose modifications and treatment recommendations

This is a presentation and metadata release only. No clinical decision logic was changed.
