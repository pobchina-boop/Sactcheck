# SACTCheck v0.64.0 Three Regimen Evidence Source Register

## Scope

This register documents the three regimen knowledge profiles added in v0.64.0 and the evidence completeness pass performed on 14 August 2026. The current NCCP protocol remains the operational source for treatment assessment.

## 1. NCCP 00945 v1, Enfortumab Vedotin and Pembrolizumab Therapy

Official NCCP source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/10493/945_Enfortumab_vedotin_Pembro.pdf

Current NCCP context: first line treatment of adults with unresectable or metastatic urothelial cancer who are eligible for platinum containing chemotherapy. The protocol provides both a 21 day and a 42 day schedule.

Evidence reviewed:

* EV 302 / KEYNOTE A39 primary publication, New England Journal of Medicine, 2024, PMID 38446675.
* EV 302 2.5 year follow up, Annals of Oncology, 2025, PMID 40460988.
* Prespecified subgroup evidence was checked for consistency with the primary population.

Evidence interpretation: EV 302 is protocol defining evidence for the regimen. Mature follow up confirms sustained progression free and overall survival benefit. Current NCCP eligibility, dose modification, monitoring and toxicity rules remain authoritative.

## 2. NCCP 00844 v4a, Nivolumab and FOLFOX 6 Modified Therapy

Official NCCP source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6445/844_Nivolumab_FOLFOX6_.pdf

Current NCCP context: two separate first line indications are encoded. The first is advanced oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma with tumour cell PD L1 expression at least 1 percent. The second is HER2 negative advanced gastric, gastroesophageal junction or oesophageal adenocarcinoma with PD L1 CPS at least 5.

Evidence reviewed:

* CheckMate 649 primary publication, The Lancet, 2021, PMID 34102137.
* CheckMate 649 3 year follow up, Journal of Clinical Oncology, 2024, PMID 38382001.
* CheckMate 649 5 year follow up, Annals of Oncology, 2026, PMID 41687718.
* CheckMate 648 primary publication, New England Journal of Medicine, 2022, PMID 35108470.
* CheckMate 648 29 month follow up, Cancer Medicine, 2024, PMID 38716626.

Evidence interpretation: CheckMate 649 supports the gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma indication. CheckMate 648 independently supports nivolumab plus fluoropyrimidine and platinum therapy for oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma. CheckMate 648 used cisplatin plus fluorouracil rather than the exact modified FOLFOX6 backbone, so the evidence relationship is explicit rather than presented as an exact schedule match.

## 3. NCCP 00431 v10, Nivolumab 1 mg/kg and Ipilimumab 3 mg/kg Therapy

Official NCCP source: https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6734/431_Nivolumab_1mg_Ipilimumab_3mg.pdf

Current NCCP context: advanced unresectable or metastatic melanoma in adults. Four combination cycles are followed by nivolumab monotherapy using the current NCCP maintenance options.

Evidence reviewed:

* CheckMate 067 primary publication, New England Journal of Medicine, 2015, PMID 26027431.
* CheckMate 067 final 10 year outcomes, New England Journal of Medicine, 2025, PMID 39282897.
* CheckMate 204 long term CNS outcomes, The Lancet Oncology, 2021, PMID 34774225.

Evidence interpretation: CheckMate 067 is the protocol defining phase 3 evidence and now provides final 10 year survival context. CheckMate 204 is contextual evidence for selected patients with melanoma brain metastases. It does not override the current NCCP caution for symptomatic CNS metastases. The CheckMate 067 trial was not designed for a definitive powered comparison of nivolumab plus ipilimumab versus nivolumab alone, and the knowledge page states this limitation explicitly.

## Antiemetic classification reconciliation identified during the evidence pass

The knowledge review identified three existing traffic light classifications that did not match NCCP SACT Antiemetic Guidance V6 Table 7.

* NCCP 00844 changed from minimal to moderate because oxaliplatin is classified as moderate emetogenic risk.
* NCCP 00945 changed from minimal to low because enfortumab vedotin is classified as low emetogenic risk.
* NCCP 00431 changed from minimal to low because ipilimumab is classified as low emetogenic risk.

The traffic light system and national NCCP antiemetic links are unchanged. Only the source supported risk categories for these three regimens were corrected.

## Governance

All three knowledge profiles remain draft pending independent consultant oncology and oncology pharmacy review. The official current NCCP protocol remains authoritative for treatment decisions.
