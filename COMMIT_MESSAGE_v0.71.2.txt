SUMMARY
Harden consent schedules, PDF rendering and immune toxicity content

DESCRIPTION
- Hardens the streamlined two-page regimen-specific consent PDF before the Regimen Workflow Engine release.
- Removes replacement-question-mark artefacts by normalising common oncology symbols and stripping unsupported PDF glyphs.
- Builds consent schedule text directly from structured regimen treatment-phase and administration data, including cycle length, treatment day, medicine and encoded route.
- Stops inferring schedules from regimen titles; where structured schedule data are unavailable the consent explicitly directs verification against the current NCCP regimen.
- Expands the immunotherapy consent module to explicitly include myocarditis/pericarditis, neurological immune toxicity including encephalitis and myasthenic syndromes, myositis/neuromuscular toxicity, ocular inflammation and pancreatitis.
- Retains explicit pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathy, nephritis, severe skin toxicity and delayed/persistent immune toxicity.
- Adds an explicit warning that rare immune toxicity can be life-threatening or fatal and can require steroids, other immunosuppression or hormone replacement.
- Preserves the concise two-page/double-sided A4 consent layout.
- Preserves the modular + Agent workflow and clinician-added-agent NCCP disclaimer.
- Adds regression tests for structured schedule generation, PDF glyph handling and the audited immunotherapy consent domains.
- Makes no change to protocol JSON, treatment thresholds, eligibility rules, dose actions or other deterministic assessment logic.
