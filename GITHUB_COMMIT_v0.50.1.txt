Refine clinical wording and separate Solid Tumour navigation (v0.50.2)

Commit description

Implements consultant feedback to reduce textual clutter and make assessment results more natural and clinically readable.

Removes the Haematology tile and limited coverage panel from the Solid Tumour tumour site navigation while retaining the dedicated Haematology portal. Solid Tumour counts, favourites and recent protocols now exclude Haematology regimens.

Removes the large patient information card and retains a shorter privacy reminder in the feasibility notice. Replaces technical generic assessment wording with concise outputs, including “Treatment criteria not met” and “One or more values entered fall outside the protocol treatment criteria.”

No clinical protocol rules, thresholds, source links or treatment encodings were changed. The catalogue remains 376 protocols: 361 Solid Tumour and 15 Haematology.
