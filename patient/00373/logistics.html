Cobimetinib + vemurafenib — advanced melanoma

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking or receiving treatment

Usual starting doses are cobimetinib 60 mg once daily on days 1–21 and vemurafenib 960 mg twice daily, about 12 hours apart, continuously. Cobimetinib can be taken with or without food. Avoid regularly taking both daily vemurafenib doses on an empty stomach. Swallow both medicines whole and follow the separate labels.

STEP 2

Missed, vomited or held treatment

For cobimetinib, take a missed dose only if at least 12 hours remain before the next dose. For vemurafenib the interval is at least four hours. Otherwise skip the missed dose; do not double up. If you vomit after either medicine, do not take an extra dose. A hold or reduction may apply to one medicine or both: obtain a written component-specific restart plan.

STEP 3

Appointments and supply

Arrange blood tests, transport, medicine supply and interpreter or carer support. Check who reviews results and how you receive the decision before the next dose.

STEP 4

Paper copies and contacts

Keep the full handbook, three-page summary and wallet card together. The QR code is optional. Ask the team to replace every labelled example contact with your real oncology and out-of-hours details; ask for a new printed plan when treatment changes.
