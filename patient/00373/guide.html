Cobimetinib + vemurafenib — advanced melanoma

To control advanced BRAF V600-mutated melanoma by blocking BRAF and MEK in the same growth pathway; the two medicines have different daily schedules.

Advanced melanoma · NCCP 00373 v3 · Document revised 6 November 2026

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

Only cobimetinib has the planned days 22–28 break. Vemurafenib continues. electrocardiogram (ECG) measures rhythm; an echocardiogram or other left ventricular ejection fraction (heart pumping measurement) (LVEF) test measures pumping function, and one does not replace the other. Radiation treatment needs a separate coordinated plan.

My overall treatment

Every 28 days: cobimetinib once daily on days 1–21, then seven days off; vemurafenib twice daily continues on all 28 days unless held by the team.

Between treatments

  • Report vision changes, breathlessness, bleeding or muscle pain with dark urine. Missed-dose windows: cobimetinib 12 hours; vemurafenib 4 hours; no extra after vomiting.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

2Heart function / rhythm
Cobimetinib + vemurafenib

Cobimetinib can reduce heart pumping; vemurafenib can alter rhythm. Breathlessness, swollen ankles, palpitations or fainting need review.

4Liver
Cobimetinib + vemurafenib

Blood tests can detect liver injury before symptoms. Report jaundice, dark urine or upper-abdominal pain.

6Muscles
Cobimetinib

Severe muscle pain, weakness or dark urine may signal muscle breakdown. The team may check muscle enzymes and kidneys.

Cobimetinib

Unusual bruising, persistent nosebleeds, black or bloody stools, coughing blood or a severe new headache need prompt assessment.
