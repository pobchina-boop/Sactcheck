1. Treatment and purpose

Cobimetinib + vemurafenib — advanced melanoma · NCCP 00373 v3

Take both medicines according to their separate labels. Review symptoms, tests and dose changes before repeating. Do not stop vemurafenib just because cobimetinib has its planned break. A toxicity hold is a different instruction and must name which medicine is affected.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Cobimetinib · MEK targeted treatment

Report new vision changes, muscle pain with dark urine, breathlessness, ankle swelling or unusual bleeding.

Vemurafenib · BRAF targeted treatment

Report new skin growths, sunburn-like reactions, eye pain or blurred vision, palpitations or fainting.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the exact diagnosis and treatment intent, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives, regimen-specific risks, tests, fertility, practical burdens and your preferences. This guide supports the consent conversation and does not replace it. Only cobimetinib has the planned days 22–28 break. Vemurafenib continues. electrocardiogram (ECG) measures rhythm; an echocardiogram or other left ventricular ejection fraction (heart pumping measurement) (LVEF) test measures pumping function, and one does not replace the other. Radiation treatment needs a separate coordinated plan.
