1. Treatment and purpose

TCF induction followed by carboplatin and radiotherapy · NCCP 00323 v7b

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² and cisplatin 100 mg/m² are given on Day 1. Fluorouracil 1,000 mg/m² per day runs continuously over 96 hours on Days 1–4; the source also allows a separate 22-hour infusion on each of those four days. Induction repeats every 21 days for 3 planned cycles unless cancer progression or unacceptable side effects require a change. If the cancer has not progressed and blood counts have recovered adequately, the planned next phase is carboplatin AUC 1.5 once weekly with radiotherapy for seven weeks (NCCP 00332). The oncology and radiation teams must confirm the actual transition date: the source documents use different anchors for the 3–8 week interval. Surgery may be considered afterwards; it is not automatic.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Docetaxel · Taxane chemotherapy

Take prescribed steroid premedication. Report fever, lasting numbness, swelling, sore nails or infusion symptoms.

Cisplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report reduced urine, persistent vomiting, tinnitus, hearing change or new tingling.

Fluorouracil · Antimetabolite chemotherapy

Report severe diarrhoea, mouth sores, chest pain, sudden confusion, sore palms or soles, or unexpectedly early severe symptoms.

Carboplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report fever, unusual bruising or bleeding, marked tiredness or an infusion reaction.

Radiotherapy · Local treatment

Report difficulty swallowing, weight loss, mouth pain, skin breakdown or problems managing saliva.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the individual aim, expected benefit, alternatives and uncertainty, including the role of induction before definitive treatment. Explain serious infection and sepsis, low blood counts and bleeding, nausea and dehydration, mouth and bowel toxicity, hair and nail changes, infusion allergy, fluid retention, lasting kidney/hearing/nerve damage, fluorouracil heart or early severe toxicity despite dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (a medicine-processing enzyme) (DPD) testing, fertility and pregnancy, line/pump risks, steroid and growth-factor plans, and treatment burden. Later surgery, radiation or chemoradiation needs its own consent discussion.
