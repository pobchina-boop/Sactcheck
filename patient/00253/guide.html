Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

Take tamoxifen once daily continuously. There is no planned week off. The prescriber confirms the duration for the treatment setting.

Swallow the tablet whole, with or without food. If a dose is missed or vomited, do not replace it; take the next scheduled daily dose.

Checks and follow-up

FBC, renal and liver profile before treatment. Check clot history, medicines and pregnancy possibility. ECG and electrolytes are added when cardiac / QT risk makes them clinically indicated. Liver tests, INR, ophthalmology and ECG are repeated when clinically indicated. NCCP recommends an annual gynaecological examination; new abnormal vaginal bleeding needs prompt assessment.

The whole-treatment map distinguishes early-breast follow-up from advanced-disease response assessment.

Support between reviews

Symptoms and daily life

Report abnormal vaginal bleeding, clot symptoms and visual changes promptly. Discuss hot flushes, sleep and sexual symptoms early.

Support medicines

No routine antiemetic, steroid premedication or G-CSF is required by this endocrine monotherapy. Ask for individual symptom support.

Tests and appointments

Ask who books each check, when it is due and who reviews it. Tell the team if an expected appointment has not arrived.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
