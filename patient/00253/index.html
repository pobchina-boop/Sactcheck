Know the plan before your next visit.

Tamoxifen · daily hormone therapy

After early breast cancer treatment, endocrine therapy reduces the risk of recurrence. In advanced disease, the aim is to control cancer and symptoms. Your setting determines the duration and follow-up.

My schedule at a glance

Your treatment pattern

Continuous daily tablets

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Take tamoxifen once daily continuously. There is no planned week off. The prescriber confirms the duration for the treatment setting.

Tests and checks

Before treatment, check blood count, kidney and liver function, clot history, medicines and pregnancy possibility. The current Irish Tamox product information also recommends an eye examination before treatment and regular blood-count, liver and calcium checks; the team agrees their timing for your product. electrocardiogram (ECG) and electrolyte checks depend on heart-rhythm risks or symptoms. National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) recommends an annual gynaecological examination. Report unusual vaginal bleeding or visual changes promptly.

Get urgent help for a swollen painful leg, chest pain, new breathlessness, stroke symptoms, fainting with palpitations, swelling of the face or throat, or a severe blistering rash.
