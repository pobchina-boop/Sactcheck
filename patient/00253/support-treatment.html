NCCP 00253 · version 6

Tamoxifen · daily hormone therapy

Take tamoxifen once daily continuously. There is no planned week off. The prescriber confirms the duration for the treatment setting.

Clinician supportive-care discussion draft. This is not an issued prescription.

Minimal emetogenic risk

No routine prophylactic antiemetic, corticosteroid premedication or G-CSF order is populated for this endocrine monotherapy. Review persistent nausea or other symptoms and prescribe individually if needed.

Medicine-specific support

  • Report abnormal vaginal bleeding, clot symptoms and visual changes promptly. Discuss hot flushes, sleep and sexual symptoms early.
  • History of significant thromboembolic disease is an NCCP exclusion. Severe hepatic impairment is not recommended. Interactions, including potent CYP2D6 inhibitors, need medication review.
  • Assess personal fracture and menopausal risks. Tamoxifen has a different effect on bone from aromatase inhibitors; do not automatically copy an AI bone-density schedule.
  • Assess persistent symptoms and prescribe individually; no automatic prophylactic antiemetic, steroid or G-CSF.
