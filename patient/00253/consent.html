NCCP 00253 · version 6

Tamoxifen · daily hormone therapy

Take tamoxifen once daily continuously. There is no planned week off. The prescriber confirms the duration for the treatment setting.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

After early breast cancer treatment, endocrine therapy reduces the risk of recurrence. In advanced disease, the aim is to control cancer and symptoms. Your setting determines the duration and follow-up. Adjuvant or advanced oestrogen-receptor-positive breast cancer in pre- or postmenopausal women, within the exact NCCP eligibility criteria.

Material risks and support

  • Hot flushes, sweating and sleep disturbance
  • Vaginal / sexual symptoms and menstrual changes
  • Blood clots in the leg or lung; stroke
  • Endometrial changes and cancer risk
  • Visual disturbance / cataracts
  • Liver effects and uncommon serious skin reactions
  • QT rhythm risk in susceptible patients
  • Reduced bone density can occur before menopause; the effect on bone differs after menopause
  • Rare blood-count changes, high calcium and worsening of hereditary angioedema

Discuss the expected benefit in your treatment setting alongside clot, womb, eye and liver risks and effects on daily life. Tell the team about previous clots, severe liver disease and all other medicines. Tamoxifen is not contraception: discuss an effective non-hormonal method during treatment and for nine months after stopping, where relevant. Absent periods do not prove infertility. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are excluded from this NCCP pathway. Do not change antidepressants, blood thinners or other regular medicines yourself.

Monitoring and understanding

Before treatment, check blood count, kidney and liver function, clot history, medicines and pregnancy possibility. The current Irish Tamox product information also recommends an eye examination before treatment and regular blood-count, liver and calcium checks; the team agrees their timing for your product. ECG and electrolyte checks depend on heart-rhythm risks or symptoms. NCCP recommends an annual gynaecological examination. Report unusual vaginal bleeding or visual changes promptly.
