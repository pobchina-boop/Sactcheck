Your supportive care plan

Carboplatin every three weeks for ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer

Practical support

Carboplatin

Phone urgently for fever, chills or unusual bleeding. During an infusion, tell the nurse immediately about itching, flushing, wheeze or throat tightness.

Between visits

Use the recovery days to take prescribed medicines and note symptoms. Contact the 24-hour number for urgent problems.

Getting help

Call the 24-hour oncology line for fever, chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting or markedly reduced urine. Tell the nurse immediately about itching, wheeze, throat tightness or faintness during treatment.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology line for fever, chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting or markedly reduced urine. Tell the nurse immediately about itching, wheeze, throat tightness or faintness during treatment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999.
