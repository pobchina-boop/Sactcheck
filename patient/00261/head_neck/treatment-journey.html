Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenBefore starting and before each treatment cycle

Checks blood cells and helps assess infection, anaemia and bleeding risk.

Kidney and liver tests

WhenBefore starting and before each cycle

Checks organ function; kidney function is also used to calculate carboplatin dose.

Kidney-function measurement

WhenBaseline and repeat or reassess when clinically required; estimated dosing needs a current creatinine result.

Provides the renal value needed for the carboplatin dose calculation; measured function is preferred when feasible.

Scans and head and neck examination

If part of your plan
WhenAs prescribed in the individual disease-assessment plan

Assesses the cancer and the response to treatment separately from safety bloods.

The whole treatment journey

Carboplatin every 3 weeks with head and neck radiotherapy: treatment and checks

Locally advanced stage III–intravenous (IV) head and neck squamous cell carcinoma when cisplatin is not appropriate.

Carboplatin is given through a vein over about 30 minutes on Day 1 every 21 days. The head and neck prescription uses AUC 5, a target used with your checked kidney function to calculate the dose. The whole visit includes safety checks, anti-sickness medicines, preparation and observation. Three carboplatin cycles are planned with concurrent radiotherapy. Radiation has a separate appointment calendar; the team coordinates any interruptions or changes.
