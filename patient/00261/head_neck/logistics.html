Carboplatin every 3 weeks with head and neck radiotherapy

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

Carboplatin is given through a vein over about 30 minutes on Day 1 every 21 days. The head and neck prescription uses AUC 5, a target used with your checked kidney function to calculate the dose. The whole visit includes safety checks, anti-sickness medicines, preparation and observation. Three carboplatin cycles are planned with concurrent radiotherapy. Radiation has a separate appointment calendar; the team coordinates any interruptions or changes.

Appointments

STEP 2

Course length

Three carboplatin cycles are planned with concurrent radiotherapy. Radiation has a separate appointment calendar; the team coordinates any interruptions or changes.

STEP 3

Tests and review

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests before every cycle. The team verifies the current kidney result and dose calculation, and reviews infection, bleeding, hearing and nerve symptoms.

STEP 4

Home record

Carry your treatment record, actual urgent contacts and medicine list. Tell the team about line redness, swelling, leakage, pain or fever.

STEP 5

Follow-up

Continue mouth, swallowing, dental, skin and nutrition care after radiation. Blood tests and hearing or nerve review follow any persisting problem; scans and examinations follow your individual oncology and radiation plan.
