When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Contact oncology promptly for fever, shivering, sudden illness, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting, much less urine or inability to drink. Report worsening hearing, numbness or tingling before the next dose. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or severe chest pain is an emergency.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

Understand your treatment

Your treatment medicines and modalities

Carboplatin is given with radiotherapy to treat locally advanced head and neck cancer when cisplatin is unsuitable. Your team explains the intended chance of cure or long-term control for your diagnosis.

Platinum chemotherapy

Carboplatin

IV infusion
Damages deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA) through cross-links

Carboplatin forms links in DNA that interfere with DNA copying and repair. This can stop cancer cells dividing. Normal blood-forming cells can also be affected, and kidney function determines how the medicine is cleared.

Platinum reaches cells↓DNA cross-links form↓Copying and repair are disrupted

What to watch for
Contact the oncology emergency service immediately for fever or shaking chills, sudden illness, bleeding, new breathlessness, chest pain or severe allergy. Report reduced urine, hearing changes and new numbness promptly. Do not wait for the next visit.

Local treatment

Radiotherapy

Radiotherapy
Damages cancer cells in the planned treatment area

Radiotherapy directs radiation at a carefully planned area. It is delivered on its own appointment calendar. Effects depend on which tissues receive radiation and can build up during the course or continue after it finishes.

Treatment area planned↓Repeated local treatment↓Recovery and late-effect review

What to watch for
Report difficulty swallowing, weight loss, mouth pain, skin breakdown or problems managing saliva.

Make the plan practical

Support around the medicines

Anti-sickness medicines

This carboplatin regimen has high sickness risk. Take the prescribed prevention and rescue medicines on their written schedule. Call if nausea or vomiting prevents drinking.

Eating and weight

Tell the team early if swallowing, appetite, pain or taste changes reduce intake. A dietitian can agree practical food, drink or feeding support with you. Do not wait for substantial weight loss.

Mouth and dental care

Agree a mouth-care plan and tell your dentist about previous or current cancer treatment. Arrange oncology advice before dental procedures, particularly after head and neck radiation.

Swallowing and communication

Ask for speech and language therapy if swallowing or speech is difficult. Use the food textures, exercises and communication support advised for you; a general guide cannot establish safe swallowing.

Medicines and fertility

Bring prescribed, over-the-counter and herbal medicines and supplements for review. Discuss fertility preservation and breastfeeding before treatment. Carboplatin can impair fertility. Do not breastfeed during treatment. The linked Irish product information advises contraception for women during treatment and at least six months afterwards; men should avoid fathering a child during treatment and at least three months afterwards. Confirm the actual product and personal advice.
