Treatment context
Aim to slow or shrink metastatic breast cancer and help symptoms, balancing possible benefit, side effects and treatment burden.
Schedule
Carboplatin is infused on Day 1 of each 21-day cycle. The team selects the dose using the prescribed AUC and kidney function. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerated; there is no fixed breast course length.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
Kidney, hearing and nerve effects
Infusion allergy, particularly with repeat platinum exposure
Fertility effects and harm to an unborn baby

Discuss fertility preservation before treatment if relevant. Carboplatin may affect periods and fertility, sometimes permanently. Do not breastfeed during treatment. Discuss reliable contraception and contact the team immediately if pregnancy is possible. The linked Irish SmPC advises effective contraception for women during treatment and at least 6 months afterwards; men should avoid fathering a child during treatment and at least 3 months afterwards. Confirm the actual product/local advice and any longer requirement from other medicines. Discuss low blood counts and sepsis, bleeding, nausea/vomiting, fatigue, kidney, hearing and nerve effects, and infusion allergy. Benefit is uncertain and alternatives depend on your cancer and priorities. Carboplatin for metastatic breast cancer is an off-label use under this Irish NCCP protocol. The treating team must explain this, obtain informed consent and follow local Drugs and Therapeutics Committee approval requirements.
