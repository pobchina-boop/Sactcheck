Carboplatin · 21-day breast schedule

Carboplatin is infused on Day 1 of each 21-day cycle. The team selects the dose using the prescribed AUC and kidney function. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerated; there is no fixed breast course length.

Important medicine-specific effects

Carboplatin

  • Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
  • Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
  • Kidney, hearing and nerve effects
  • Infusion allergy, particularly with repeat platinum exposure
  • Fertility effects and harm to an unborn baby

Make the plan practical

Preventing sickness

Take the prescribed anti-sickness medicines before treatment and on the following days, even if you feel well. Follow your own prescription and contact the team if nausea or vomiting is not controlled.

Blood counts

Keep blood tests and use the emergency contact for fever, chills or bleeding. Preventive growth-factor injections depend on individual risk.

Kidneys and other medicines

Tell the team about every medicine or supplement, especially treatments that affect kidneys, hearing or bleeding. Follow individual drinking advice.

Steroid and rescue plan

The anti-sickness plan may affect sleep, glucose and bowel symptoms. Confirm the exact doses and last day of each medicine; contact the team if symptoms persist.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
