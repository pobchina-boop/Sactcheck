Olaparib and bevacizumab maintenance

Help sustain cancer control after first-line chemotherapy, using olaparib to target impaired deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA) repair and bevacizumab to limit signals for new blood-vessel growth.

What to watch for

You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

A picture of side effects

Match each symptom box to its numbered body location. Tell your team about new symptoms.

1Headache, vision and energy
Olaparib / bevacizumab

Olaparib can cause fatigue or headache. Severe headache, confusion, seizures or new vision changes need urgent assessment for severe blood-pressure effects or PRES with bevacizumab.

3Breathing, lung inflammation and clots
Olaparib / bevacizumab

New cough or breathlessness may need assessment for infection, olaparib-related lung inflammation, bleeding or a clot. Chest pain or severe breathing difficulty needs emergency help.

5Nausea, bowels and severe pain
Olaparib / bevacizumab

Use prescribed sickness support for olaparib. Severe new abdominal pain, persistent vomiting or a swollen abdomen needs urgent assessment; bevacizumab can cause perforation or fistula.

7Bleeding, skin and wound healing
Bevacizumab / olaparib

Report unusual bleeding and wounds that are slow to heal. Tell the team before surgery. Bevacizumab can impair healing; olaparib-related low platelets can also increase bleeding risk.

12345678
2Mouth, taste and eating
Olaparib / bevacizumab

Olaparib can affect taste, appetite or the mouth. Report persistent soreness or bleeding; tell the team before dental procedures because bevacizumab can affect healing.

4Liver and kidney safety
Olaparib / bevacizumab

Olaparib needs liver and kidney blood tests. Bevacizumab can cause protein loss through the kidneys, so urine tests are separate. Report jaundice, dark urine or new swelling.

6Blood cells and marrow
Olaparib

Low red cells, white cells or platelets can cause tiredness, infection or bleeding. Persistent unexplained abnormalities may need specialist marrow tests; do not wait for the next routine review if unwell.

8Swelling and blood clots
Olaparib / bevacizumab

A new painful swollen limb needs urgent assessment. Stroke symptoms, chest pain, collapse or sudden severe breathlessness need emergency help.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

Call your oncology team urgently for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bruising or bleeding, marked weakness, new or worsening cough or breathlessness, a new painful swollen limb, yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, persistent vomiting or diarrhoea, or inability to keep fluids down. Follow your own temperature and contact plan; do not wait for the next appointment. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, seizures or stroke symptoms: call 112/999. Severe new abdominal pain, major bleeding, worsening wounds or severe headache/visual change also need urgent assessment; tell the infusion nurse immediately about symptoms during a visit.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
