Additional phase-specific support

  • Multiday SACT: reconcile antiemetics for each day’s medicines and the two days after; one combined prescription is required. Do not add independent overlapping courses.
  • CarboplatinAUC3 is moderate risk here; do not import the AUC≥4 high-risk course simply because the drug is carboplatin.
  • Coordinate radiation-related nausea, swallowing, nutrition, skin care and respiratory assessment with radiation oncology.
  • No automatic routine G-CSF, antibacterial, antiviral or PJP prophylaxis is specified in these NCCP regimens; consider individual risk and local policy.
  • Review etoposide interactions, live vaccines, nephrotoxic/ototoxic medicines and any duplicate QT-prolonging antiemetics.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
