Treatment scope · NCCP 00382 This guide covers Lonsurf on its own for previously treated metastatic colorectal cancer under NCCP 00382a. It does not cover gastric cancer or Lonsurf with bevacizumab. Confirm the exact regimen on your prescription.
NCCP 00382 · version 4

Trifluridine/tipiracil (Lonsurf) monotherapy

Take the prescribed morning and evening tablets on Days 1–5, have no Lonsurf on Days 6–7, then take morning and evening tablets on Days 8–12. Days 13–28 are tablet-free. A new 28-day cycle starts only when the team authorises it.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Swallow the prescribed tablets with water within one hour after finishing breakfast and the evening meal on dosing days. Doses are based on body size and previous side effects; two tablet strengths may be combined. Some prescriptions have different morning and evening tablet quantities. Use the pharmacy calendar and do not calculate doses yourself.

Prescription and supply

Before collecting another course, confirm the dose, tablet strengths, calendar, blood-test date and authorisation. Bring the current packs and medicines list.

Meal routine

Swallow the prescribed tablets with water within one hour after finishing breakfast and the evening meal on dosing days. Doses are based on body size and previous side effects; two tablet strengths may be combined. Some prescriptions have different morning and evening tablet quantities. Use the pharmacy calendar and do not calculate doses yourself.

Missed or vomited doses

Do not make up a missed, held or vomited dose and do not double the next dose. Tell your team and follow the next scheduled dose on the agreed calendar. Repeated vomiting needs advice because of dehydration and missed medicines.

Appointments

Ask who books extra tests, who reviews results and whom to call if the plan is unclear. There is no infusion visit or take-home pump in monotherapy.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
