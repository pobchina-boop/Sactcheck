Additional phase-specific support

  • Agree oral preventive antiemetic and rescue instructions under NCCP oral guidance/local policy; this is not the NCIS parenteral moderate/high protocol.
  • No routine premedication is specified by NCCP 00382.
  • Assess need for granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF) or other infection support individually; no universal prophylaxis is prescribed here.
  • Use the exact dosing calendar and no catch-up doses.
  • Reconcile gastrointestinal (GI) symptoms, hydration and additional blood counts for renal impairment.
  • Confirm the body-surface-area prescription, actual morning and evening tablet counts and Days 1–5 and 8–12 calendar. Take within one hour after breakfast and the evening meal; do not replace missed or vomited doses.
  • Follow current NCCP cycle-start blood and urine checks and the additional EMA blood-count requirement before starting and as needed, at minimum before each cycle; increase monitoring with renal impairment or toxicity.
  • Prior reductions must be retained: no dose re-escalation. The severe-renal dosing pathway differs from the standard minimum dose and needs separate review.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
