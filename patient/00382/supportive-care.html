Trifluridine/tipiracil (Lonsurf) monotherapy

Treatment scope · NCCP 00382 This guide covers Lonsurf on its own for previously treated metastatic colorectal cancer under NCCP 00382a. It does not cover gastric cancer or Lonsurf with bevacizumab. Confirm the exact regimen on your prescription.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Agree oral preventive antiemetic and rescue instructions under NCCP oral guidance/local policy; this is not the NCIS parenteral moderate/high protocol.
  • No routine premedication is specified by NCCP 00382.
  • Assess need for G-CSF or other infection support individually; no universal prophylaxis is prescribed here.
  • Use the exact dosing calendar and no catch-up doses.
  • Reconcile GI symptoms, hydration and additional blood counts for renal impairment.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
