NCCP 00382 · version 4

Trifluridine/tipiracil (Lonsurf) monotherapy

Take the prescribed morning and evening tablets on Days 1–5, have no Lonsurf on Days 6–7, then take morning and evening tablets on Days 8–12. Days 13–28 are tablet-free. A new 28-day cycle starts only when the team authorises it.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

This oral chemotherapy aims to control colorectal cancer that has spread after earlier treatments, or when those treatments are unsuitable. It combines two ingredients in one medicine. Your team discusses the likely benefit, uncertainty, alternatives and your priorities. NCCP 00382a covers colorectal monotherapy. The medicine also has separate licensed uses for gastric cancer and with bevacizumab in colorectal cancer; those need their own treatment and safety plan.

Material risks and support

  • Low blood counts: infection, anaemia and bleeding
  • Serious infection or febrile neutropenia
  • Nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea and dehydration
  • Fatigue, reduced appetite and mouth soreness
  • Kidney impairment can increase treatment toxicity
  • Pregnancy harm and important contraception requirements

Discuss the disease-control aim, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including supportive care without this treatment, infection/bleeding risk, bowel symptoms and dehydration, oral dosing responsibilities, kidney/liver suitability, fertility, pregnancy prevention and your right to reconsider.

Monitoring and understanding

FBC, renal/liver profile and proteinuria dipstick. Confirm oral prescription, BSA/tablet band, previous treatment, symptoms and medicines. FBC, renal/liver profile and proteinuria dipstick before each cycle or as indicated. Review previous nadir, delay duration, current dose, GI exceptions, infection and oral adherence. Disease assessment as planned by Consultant; no fixed scan schedule.
