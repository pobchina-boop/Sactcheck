1. Treatment and purpose

Trifluridine/tipiracil (Lonsurf) monotherapy · NCCP 00382 v4

Take the prescribed morning and evening tablets on Days 1–5, have no Lonsurf on Days 6–7, then take morning and evening tablets on Days 8–12. Days 13–28 are tablet-free. A new 28-day cycle starts only when the team authorises it.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Trifluridine/tipiracil · Oral cytotoxic chemotherapy

Check the exact dosing and rest days. Report fever, sudden illness, vomiting, diarrhoea, bleeding or reduced urine promptly.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the disease-control aim, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including supportive care without this treatment, infection/bleeding risk, bowel symptoms and dehydration, oral dosing responsibilities, kidney/liver suitability, fertility, pregnancy prevention and your right to reconsider.
