Important medicine-specific effects

Trifluridine/tipiracil (Lonsurf)

  • Low neutrophils, anaemia and low platelets can require interruption or dose reduction.
  • Fever with neutropenia needs urgent assessment.
  • Nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea can cause dehydration.
Other / shared treatment effects: The anatomy page highlights important symptom patterns for this exact regimen, but it cannot show every possible effect. Fatigue, nausea, appetite change, mouth/bowel symptoms or blood-count changes may occur depending on the medicines used. Report new or worsening symptoms even if they are not listed here.

Monitoring / medicines

  • Take doses after the morning and evening meals exactly as prescribed.
  • Do not double a dose for a missed or vomited dose; follow your oncology/pharmacy instructions.
  • Blood counts are particularly important because neutropenia can be significant.

Bring / track

  • Your current medicines, including non-prescription products.
  • A note of new symptoms, when they started and what they stopped you doing.
  • Any temperature, blood-pressure or bowel-count readings your team asked you to record.
  • Questions about the purpose of treatment, scan/review timing or changes to the plan.

When to call - do not wait for the next appointment

Call oncology urgently for fever, shivers, sudden illness, uncontrolled vomiting, persistent diarrhoea, bleeding, inability to drink or marked weakness. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or severe chest pain: call 112/999. If your team has given you different local thresholds or emergency instructions, follow those instructions.
