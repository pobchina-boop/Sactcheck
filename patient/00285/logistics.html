Trastuzumab SC · early breast cancer

NCCP 00285 · Source reconciled; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

Trastuzumab 600 mg under the skin every 21 days, without a loading dose, to complete one year total. Previous IV or combination-treatment trastuzumab counts towards that year; a route switch does not start a new year.

STEP 1

Before your visit

Confirm the dose, symptoms, required blood results and echo due window.

STEP 2

Access and administration

SC trastuzumab needs no IV line for this medicine. Another medicine may still need a cannula or port.

STEP 3

Treatment and observation

Injection over 2–5 minutes into alternate thighs. Observation is 30 minutes after the first injection and 15 minutes after subsequent injections, subject to approved local policy.

STEP 4

After the visit

Contact the unit to rearrange a missed dose. SC doses remain at least 3 weeks apart; no loading dose. This medicine does not routinely require a take-home pump.

Injection site

Alternate thighs and avoid tender, bruised, red or hardened skin. Tell the nurse about a persistent or worsening local reaction.

Visit length

Injection over 2–5 minutes into alternate thighs. Observation is 30 minutes after the first injection and 15 minutes after subsequent injections, subject to approved local policy.

Missed or delayed visit

Contact the unit to rearrange a missed dose. SC doses remain at least 3 weeks apart; no loading dose.
