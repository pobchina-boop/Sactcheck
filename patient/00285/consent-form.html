Treatment context
Trastuzumab under the skin is part of treatment to reduce recurrence in HER2-positive early breast cancer. The wider plan can include chemotherapy, surgery, radiotherapy and endocrine treatment when indicated.
Schedule
Trastuzumab 600 mg under the skin every 21 days, without a loading dose, to complete one year total. Previous IV or combination-treatment trastuzumab counts towards that year; a route switch does not start a new year.
