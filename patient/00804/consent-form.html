Treatment context
Lomustine chemotherapy and bevacizumab targeted therapy are used together to help control recurrent glioblastoma. Your team discusses likely benefits, risks, symptoms and quality-of-life goals.
Schedule
Each 42-day cycle has lomustine capsules taken once on Day 1 and bevacizumab infusions on Days 1 and 22. Up to six cycles are planned, with review before each treatment.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Lomustine

Oral chemotherapy capsule
Delayed and cumulative low blood counts
Nausea / vomiting
Lung injury
Kidney and liver changes
Rare second cancers and fertility effects

Bevacizumab

Targeted antibody given into a vein
High blood pressure and rare PRES neurological syndrome
Protein in the urine and kidney injury
Bleeding, including serious internal bleeding
Blood clots, stroke or heart attack
Slow wound healing and wound complications
Gastrointestinal perforation or fistula
Infusion reactions and rare severe allergy
Fertility effects and harm to a pregnancy
