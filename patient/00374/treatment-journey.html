Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenAt baseline and before each cycle. Current product information also specifies weekly testing during the first two cycles, then at least once between doses.

Detect infection risk, anaemia and bleeding risk.

Liver tests and albumin

WhenAt baseline and before each cycle; liver parameters weekly for the first two cycles, then at least once between doses. Repeat sooner when symptoms or abnormal results require it.

Check eligibility, liver injury and protein levels relevant to treatment safety.

Kidney function

WhenAt baseline and before cycles; extra tests for reduced urine, dehydration, muscle injury or abnormal results.

Assess clearance and detect kidney injury, including injury associated with muscle breakdown.

Creatine phosphokinase: muscle-enzyme test

WhenAt baseline, weekly during the first two cycles and at least once between doses thereafter; urgent testing for marked muscle pain, weakness or dark urine.

Detect muscle injury before it becomes severe.

Heart pumping assessment

WhenHeart ultrasound (echocardiogram) or a multigated acquisition heart scan before treatment and every 2–3 months until stopping, as specified in the source complication section. Additional assessment follows symptoms and clinical risk.

Detect treatment-related heart dysfunction.

Infusion line and pump review

WhenCheck at connection and disconnection; report pain, swelling, leakage, fever or pump problems immediately.

Prevent or detect infection, leakage and incorrect infusion delivery.

If part of your plan
WhenThe specialist sets scan and clinical-review timing for the tumour type, treatment aim and symptoms.

The whole treatment journey

Trabectedin for advanced soft tissue sarcoma: treatment and checks

Adult advanced soft tissue sarcoma after anthracycline and ifosfamide treatment has failed, or when those medicines are unsuitable

Trabectedin 1.5 mg/m² is infused over 24 hours starting on day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment continues while benefit and tolerability justify it. The actual dose, start time and any delay require a new safety review.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewTrabectedin: one 24-hour infusion every 21 days

The aim is to control advanced sarcoma and help symptoms. The strongest trial evidence concerns previously treated liposarcoma and leiomyosarcoma; the team explains expected benefit for your particular subtype.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999. Marked muscle pain or weakness, dark or reduced urine, new swelling or faintness, jaundice, breathlessness or infusion-site pain requires urgent assessment.
