Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Review all medicines and supplements, especially strong liver-enzyme inhibitors or inducers, statins and other liver-toxic medicines. Avoid alcohol. Confirm steroid premedication, blood tests, pump training, line care, disconnection and verified contact numbers.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Central venous access is strongly recommended. Trained staff connect the prescribed 24-hour infusion and confirm its actual disconnection time. Dexamethasone 20 mg is given intravenously 30 minutes beforehand. The full visit includes checks, preparation and line care.

STEP 3

Intravenous access and line care

The team chooses appropriate intravenous access and explains its care. Report pain, burning, redness or swelling during treatment immediately. If a port or peripherally inserted central catheter is used, confirm dressing, flushing, infection precautions and eventual removal arrangements. Confirm spill or leak instructions and who to call if the pump alarms, becomes disconnected or appears to finish early. Never change settings or disconnect it yourself.

STEP 4

Home support

Dexamethasone 20 mg intravenously 30 minutes before each infusion is required for anti-sickness treatment and appears to help reduce liver toxicity. This regimen has moderate nausea/vomiting risk; agree the complete prevention and rescue prescription without duplicate steroids. Granulocyte colony-stimulating factor, a white-cell growth factor, may be used for low counts under local policy; it is not universally required.

STEP 5

Next appointment

Confirm the next treatment, tests and any scan before leaving.
