Weekly cetuximab + irinotecan

Cetuximab on Day 1 and Day 8; irinotecan on Day 1. Repeat the reference cycle every 14 days while beneficial and tolerable. There is no fluorouracil home pump.

NCCP 00330 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Cetuximab

  • Rash, dry skin, cracks and sore nails
  • Eye inflammation or corneal injury
  • Low magnesium, calcium or potassium
  • Diarrhoea, dehydration and kidney problems
  • Infusion reaction; rare serious lung or skin effects

Irinotecan

  • Early cramps, sweating and diarrhoea
  • Delayed diarrhoea and dehydration
  • Low blood counts / serious infection
  • Nausea, mouth soreness and fatigue
  • Hair thinning or loss
  • Rare serious lung or allergic reactions

Make the plan practical

Skin, nails and minerals

Start the prescribed skin-care plan early, use moisturiser and sun protection, and report painful or infected skin or nails. Keep mineral blood-test appointments during treatment and for two months after cetuximab.

Bowel symptoms and fluids

Irinotecan can cause early cramps/sweating during infusion and delayed diarrhoea after 24 hours. You need separate instructions for each. Follow your written rescue plan. Fever, blood, severe pain, reduced urine or inability to drink needs urgent advice.

Plan the visit

Allow for blood tests, review, pharmacy preparation, infusion and observation. A port is not automatically required; ask which access is suitable.

Comfort and daily life

Use prescribed anti-sickness medicines, pace activities and ask for nutritional, emotional or practical support.

The whole treatment plan

Ask how and when the team will assess benefit, which blood tests continue during a pause, and what happens after the final dose. Mineral checks continue monthly for two months after the last antibody treatment.

The cetuximab-only visit

Day 8 contains cetuximab only. Its premedicines and observation still matter, but the chemotherapy-day anti-sickness course is not automatically repeated.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
