SACTCheck · NCCP 00676 v3 · High-risk endometrial cancer after surgery

Your treatment journey

Cisplatin chemoradiation, then paclitaxel and carboplatin

Lower the chance of the cancer returning after surgery.

Your infusion schedule

During pelvic radiotherapy

Usually start 4–6 weeks after surgery. Cisplatin is given before radiation on two treatment days three weeks apart. Ring both teams if radiation is cancelled. Cisplatin is only given on a radiation day.

After radiotherapy

Within three weeks of finishing radiation, start paclitaxel then carboplatin AUC 5, every three weeks for four cycles. Bring your steroid premedication instructions.

At review visits

  • What happens at my next visit? Two cisplatin visits during radiotherapy, then four chemotherapy cycles.
  • Which symptoms should I phone about? Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Report persistent vomiting, reduced urine or severe diarrhoea promptly.
  • What should I plan before starting? Confirm the radiotherapy appointment before travelling.
  • My radiotherapy session was cancelled. Should I still attend for cisplatin? Contact both units before attending. The concurrent cisplatin dose is postponed until radiotherapy resumes. Your later paclitaxel and carboplatin visits follow a separate schedule.
