SACTCheck · NCCP 00676 v3 · High-risk endometrial cancer after surgery

Support during after radiotherapy

Cisplatin chemoradiation, then paclitaxel and carboplatin

Within three weeks of finishing radiation, start paclitaxel then carboplatin AUC 5, every three weeks for four cycles. Bring your steroid premedication instructions.

Paclitaxel

Report numbness and tell the nurse immediately if you flush or wheeze during treatment.

Carboplatin

Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Report persistent vomiting, reduced urine or severe diarrhoea promptly. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
