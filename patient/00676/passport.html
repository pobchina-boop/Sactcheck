My questions and next steps

  • What happens at my next visit? Two cisplatin visits during radiotherapy, then four chemotherapy cycles.
  • Which symptoms should I phone about? Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Report persistent vomiting, reduced urine or severe diarrhoea promptly.
  • What should I plan before starting? Confirm the radiotherapy appointment before travelling.
  • My radiotherapy session was cancelled. Should I still attend for cisplatin? Contact both units before attending. The concurrent cisplatin dose is postponed until radiotherapy resumes. Your later paclitaxel and carboplatin visits follow a separate schedule.
