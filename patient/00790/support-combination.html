Additional phase-specific support

  • First paclitaxel dose: dexamethasone 8 mg IV, chlorphenamine 10 mg IV and famotidine 20 mg IV, each 30 minutes before paclitaxel.
  • Day 8 and thereafter: no routine dexamethasone; chlorphenamine 10 mg IV and famotidine 20 mg IV 30 minutes before. Consultant may add steroid for risk/prior reaction or omit famotidine if appropriate; monitor closely.
  • Rechallenge steroid changes require consultant guidance; consider analgesia for taxane aches and G-CSF according to individual risk.
  • Day 1 antiemetic steroid is reconciled with paclitaxel premedication; do not add two steroid courses.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
