Your supportive care plan

Bevacizumab with weekly paclitaxel

Practical support

Days 1 and 15

Tell the nurse which steroid premedicines you have already taken. On this visit the unit checks blood pressure and urine protein. Tell them about bleeding or planned procedures.

Days 8 and 22

Tell the nurse which steroid premedicines you have already taken. Your chemotherapy team will review blood counts and how you have been since the last visit.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the oncology unit now for fever, chills, severe abdominal pain, unexpected bleeding or new breathlessness. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
