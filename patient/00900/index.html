When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Nivolumab

Nivolumab blocks PD-1, a brake on immune T cells. This can help immune cells attack cancer, but can also inflame healthy organs.

Ipilimumab

Ipilimumab blocks another immune brake, CTLA-4, helping activate T cells. Combining two immune medicines can increase anti-cancer activity and also the risk of serious immune inflammation.

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: Combination visit: cycles 1–4

Blood results and symptoms are reviewed. Nivolumab is given first, then ipilimumab. The team monitors you during treatment and afterwards.

What to do: Tell the nurse immediately if you become itchy, flushed, breathless, dizzy or suddenly unwell.

Day 2–21: Between combination visits

Immune inflammation can begin at any time; the calendar cannot predict a safe symptom-free week.

What to do: After cycle 4, confirm the nivolumab-only dose, route, interval and first maintenance date.

Support at home

Sickness prevention and premedication

Nivolumab has minimal and ipilimumab low emetogenic potential. Routine premedication is not usually required; the team individualises anti-sickness treatment and care after any previous reaction.

Steroids and other treatment for immune effects

Do not self-start or stop steroids. If prescribed, follow the exact taper and any infection-prevention plan. Do not restart cancer treatment until the team explicitly authorises it.

Pregnancy and feeding

Discuss fertility and effective contraception before treatment. Effective contraception continues for at least five months after the last nivolumab dose. Do not breastfeed during treatment; agree any later feeding plan with the team.
