Treatment context
These two immunotherapy medicines aim to control bowel cancer with a confirmed MSI-H/dMMR DNA-repair feature. This is a first-line treatment for unresectable or metastatic cancer.
Schedule
Cycles 1–4: nivolumab 240 mg, then ipilimumab 1 mg/kg, on Day 1 every 21 days. The first nivolumab-only dose is due three weeks after the final combination dose, subject to clinical review. Nivolumab alone: 240 mg IV every two weeks or 480 mg IV every four weeks.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Nivolumab

Immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver, kidneys, glands, skin, heart, nerves or muscles
Infusion reactions; injection-site effects if given under the skin
Some effects need hospital treatment, steroids or long-term hormone replacement
Effects can begin after the final dose

Ipilimumab

Immunotherapy
Immune colitis and diarrhoea
Immune liver, hormone, skin, nervous-system and other organ inflammation
Serious or potentially life-threatening reactions
Some adverse effects persist after the four-dose phase

Discuss the exact colorectal setting and two treatment phases, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives, greater immune-toxicity risk with the combination, possible hospital care, steroids, lasting hormone replacement and rare life-threatening effects. Explain late toxicity, pregnancy precautions, the contact plan and the option to reconsider.
