The whole treatment journey

Nivolumab + ipilimumab · first-line colorectal: treatment and checks

First-line unresectable or metastatic MSI-H/dMMR colorectal cancer

Cycles 1–4: nivolumab 240 mg, then ipilimumab 1 mg/kg, on Day 1 every 21 days. The first nivolumab-only dose is due three weeks after the final combination dose, subject to clinical review. Nivolumab alone: 240 mg intravenous (IV) every two weeks or 480 mg IV every four weeks.
