Active medicines: Nivolumab

Emetogenic risk: Minimal

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine antiemetic prophylaxis or infusion premedication; prescribe symptom relief individually.
  • Carry the immunotherapy alert card and report bowel, lung, liver, kidney, hormonal, skin, neurological or cardiac symptoms promptly during and after treatment.
  • FBC, renal/liver tests, glucose and thyroid function before each cycle; cortisol as clinically indicated. Maintain follow-up for delayed immune toxicity.
  • Management uses dose delay or discontinuation, not routine dose reduction. High-dose steroids for immune toxicity require a separately prescribed treatment and taper plan.
  • Cycles 1–4: nivolumab 240 mg, then ipilimumab 1 mg/kg, on Day 1 every 21 days. The first nivolumab-only dose is due three weeks after the final combination dose, subject to clinical review. Nivolumab alone: 240 mg IV every two weeks or 480 mg IV every four weeks.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
