The whole treatment journey

Fortnightly cetuximab + FOLFIRI: treatment and checks

Advanced / metastatic colorectal cancer

Cetuximab and FOLFIRI start on Day 1, followed by a 46-hour fluorouracil infusion with planned disconnection around Day 3. Repeat every 14 days while beneficial and tolerable. No weekly Day 8 cetuximab is scheduled.

What each check is for

1Before first treatmentBefore the first infusion

RAS-wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer; exact line and treatment goal are confirmed by the oncology team.

Ask / act: Confirm tests, medicines, infusion access and the written urgent contact plan.

Sources: NCCP 00585 · SMPC

2Each treatment decisionAt scheduled treatment reviews and whenever symptoms change

Benefit and toxicity determine the next treatment; the reference cycle is not an automatic clearance.

Ask / act: Report symptoms before the next dose, including at a separate antibody-only visit.

Sources: NCCP 00585

3Pause or completionWhen a component is held or the course ends

Blood tests, line care and side-effect follow-up may continue after dosing stops.

Ask / act: Agree the next appointment, remaining tests and who reviews unresolved symptoms.

Sources: NCCP 00585 · SMPC

At the next review, ask

  • Which test is expected next, who arranges it, and who explains the result?
  • Who should I contact if an expected appointment has not arrived?
