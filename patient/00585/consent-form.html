Treatment context
This combination is used for RAS wild-type bowel cancer that has spread. Your team explains the aim, alternatives and how benefit will be reviewed.
Schedule
Cetuximab on Day 1. Day 1: irinotecan, folinic acid and fluorouracil, then a 46-hour fluorouracil pump; planned disconnection around Day 3. A repeating 14-day cycle. The number of cycles depends on benefit, side effects and your treatment plan.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Cetuximab

EGFR-targeted antibody
Rash, dry skin, cracks and sore nails
Eye inflammation or corneal injury
Low magnesium, calcium or potassium
Diarrhoea, dehydration and kidney problems
Infusion reaction; rare serious lung or skin effects

Irinotecan

Topoisomerase I chemotherapy
Early cramps, sweating and diarrhoea
Delayed diarrhoea and dehydration
Low blood counts / serious infection
Nausea, mouth soreness and fatigue
Hair thinning or loss
Rare serious lung or allergic reactions

Folinic acid

Chemotherapy modulator
Enhances fluorouracil activity and toxicity
Rare allergic reaction
Diarrhoea / mouth soreness in combination

Fluorouracil

Chemotherapy
Diarrhoea, dehydration and mouth ulcers
Low blood counts / infection
Hand-foot skin soreness
Cardiotoxicity including chest pain / ischaemia
Severe early toxicity including DPD-related toxicity
Pump / central-line complications
