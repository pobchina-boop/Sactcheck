Bleomycin

Call urgently for new dry cough or breathlessness. Tell anaesthesia teams about past bleomycin.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Report new dry cough or breathlessness immediately, even after the last dose. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
