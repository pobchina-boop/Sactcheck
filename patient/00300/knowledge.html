Your complete treatment reference

BEP · bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Treat the germ-cell tumour with curative intent.

Bleomycin · Bleomycin

Antitumour antibiotic. It causes DNA strand breaks. Lung injury can develop during or after treatment. Call urgently for new dry cough or breathlessness. Tell anaesthesia teams about past bleomycin.

Etoposide · Etoposide

Topoisomerase II inhibitor. It prevents dividing cells from rejoining DNA. Low blood counts and hair loss can follow. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, faintness or breathing difficulty during an infusion.

Cisplatin · Cisplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. It damages DNA in dividing cells. It can also affect kidney function, hearing and nerves. Report ringing in the ears, hearing loss, reduced urine or new numbness.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · all three medicines

Bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin are given on day 1. IV fluids and an anti-sickness plan cover the five-day cisplatin block.

Days 2–5 · etoposide and cisplatin

Return on each of days 2, 3, 4 and 5 for etoposide, cisplatin and fluids. Keep using the same dated anti-sickness prescription for this block.

Days 8 and 15 · bleomycin

Bleomycin alone on days 8 and 15. The cycle repeats every 21 days. The consultant confirms your total number of cycles based on diagnosis and risk.

Common effects and what to do

How you feel between visits

Tiredness, nausea or appetite changes can make everyday activities harder. Ask your team for help if symptoms interfere with eating or drinking.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Report new dry cough or breathlessness immediately, even after the last dose. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

What the research found

The study supports the activity of BEP after complete resection of ovarian germ-cell tumours.

This was an adjuvant study. Its outcomes cannot be used to estimate an individual response in unresected or metastatic disease.
