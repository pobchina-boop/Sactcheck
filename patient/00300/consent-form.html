Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

BEP · bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Treat the germ-cell tumour with curative intent. Includes advanced or metastatic ovarian dysgerminoma (00300c). Testicular and extragonadal indications have separate NCCP codes under the same protocol.

Treatment schedule. Bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin are given on day 1. IV fluids and an anti-sickness plan cover the five-day cisplatin block. Return on each of days 2, 3, 4 and 5 for etoposide, cisplatin and fluids. Keep using the same dated anti-sickness prescription for this block. Bleomycin alone on days 8 and 15. The cycle repeats every 21 days. The consultant confirms your total number of cycles based on diagnosis and risk.

Regimen-specific side effects

Bleomycin

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Lung inflammation and scarringContact oncology urgently for new dry cough or breathlessness during or after treatment. Tell anaesthetists if you have ever had bleomycin.
Fever, skin or infusion reactionChills, rash and fever can occur. Call about any fever. Tell the nurse immediately about wheeze, swelling or faintness.

Etoposide

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Low counts and infectionPhone urgently for fever, chills, unexpected bleeding or marked breathlessness. Hair loss is common.
Infusion reactions and late effectsTell the nurse immediately about flushing, dizziness or breathlessness. There is a small long-term risk of treatment-related leukaemia.

Cisplatin

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Kidneys and hearingFluids and blood tests help protect the kidneys. Report reduced urine, ringing or hearing loss promptly. Hearing changes can persist.
Nerves, nausea and low countsReport numbness, persistent vomiting or bleeding. Call urgently for fever or chills.

Treatment discussion and decision

BEP · bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

The study supports the activity of BEP after complete resection of ovarian germ-cell tumours. This was an adjuvant study. Its outcomes cannot be used to estimate an individual response in unresected or metastatic disease.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Report new dry cough or breathlessness immediately, even after the last dose. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
