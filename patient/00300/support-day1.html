SACTCheck · NCCP 00300 v7b · Ovarian dysgerminoma and other germ-cell tumours

Support during day 1 · all three medicines

BEP · bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin

Bleomycin, etoposide and cisplatin are given on day 1. IV fluids and an anti-sickness plan cover the five-day cisplatin block.

Bleomycin

Call urgently for new dry cough or breathlessness. Tell anaesthesia teams about past bleomycin.

Etoposide

Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, faintness or breathing difficulty during an infusion.

Cisplatin

Report ringing in the ears, hearing loss, reduced urine or new numbness.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Report new dry cough or breathlessness immediately, even after the last dose. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
