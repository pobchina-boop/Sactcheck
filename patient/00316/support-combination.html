Additional phase-specific support

  • Fortnightly paclitaxel: dexamethasone 20 mg orally at 12 and 6 hours before treatment, OR the source IV option 30 minutes before; chlorphenamine 10 mg IV and famotidine 20 mg IV before treatment. The team selects the route / timing; do not substitute the weekly 8 mg first-dose schedule.
  • Growth-factor support is required for the dose-dense taxane phase. Consultant-directed steroid reduction / omission must account for reactions and interactions; if aprepitant is added, the source discusses 12 mg treatment-day dexamethasone.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
