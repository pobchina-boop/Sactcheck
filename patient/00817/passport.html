My questions and next steps

  • What will I receive at the next visit? Combination every 3 weeks, then pembrolizumab alone.
  • When should I call? Call oncology urgently for fever, chills, new breathlessness or severe diarrhoea.
  • What if a new symptom starts after my last pembrolizumab? Keep your immunotherapy card. Call the oncology team about new cough, diarrhoea, marked fatigue or thirst even after the last infusion.
