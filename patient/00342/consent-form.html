Treatment context
Temozolomide is used to help control a high-grade glioma that has returned or progressed after previous treatment. The team considers previous medicines, current symptoms, scans and your priorities when deciding whether it is suitable.
Schedule
Take temozolomide once daily on Days 1–5, then no temozolomide on Days 6–28. Repeat only when your team authorises the next 28-day cycle. The course is reviewed for benefit and toxicity rather than a fixed six-cycle finish.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Temozolomide

Oral chemotherapy capsule
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea / vomiting and appetite loss
Fatigue, headache and constipation
Liver injury, including delayed injury
PJP and other opportunistic infections
Rash and rare severe skin reactions
Fertility effects and harm to a pregnancy
Rare serious bone-marrow disorders
