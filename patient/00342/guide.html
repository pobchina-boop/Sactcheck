My overall treatment

28 days: temozolomide once daily D1–5; no temozolomide D6–28. Review each cycle.

  1. Course reviewed individuallyDoses, starts and stopping are confirmed by the team; a card stamp is a record, not approval.

A typical 28-day cycle

Day 1–5Five daily capsule doses
Day 6–21No temozolomide; recovery continues
Day 22–28Blood count and next-cycle decision

Timing may change after review. Side effects can occur on any day; there are no guaranteed safe days.

Between treatments

  • Blood counts can fall on rest days. Day 22 counts and after-cycle liver tests support safe recovery.
  • There is no automatic six-cycle finish or restart. Your team confirms the plan at each review.

Use the current pharmacy label and anti-sickness plan. Swallow capsules whole. Never double or replace a vomited dose.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Call 112 or 999 for a new seizure, sudden weakness or speech difficulty, loss of consciousness, severe breathing difficulty or uncontrolled bleeding. Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
