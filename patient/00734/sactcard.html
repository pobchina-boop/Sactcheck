weekly carboplatin AUC 2 / paclitaxel → dose-dense AC

NCCP 00734 v3 · 21-day cycle

Four 21-day cycles: paclitaxel on Days 1, 8 and 15; carboplatin AUC 2 on Days 1, 8 and 15. Four further cycles (cycles 5–8): doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide on Day 1 every 14 days. G-CSF support is required for every dose-dense AC cycle; the team prescribes its product and timing separately. Surgery and pathology review follow chemotherapy. Further systemic treatment, radiotherapy and follow-up depend on the findings and the MDT plan; no routine postoperative immunotherapy is specified by this regimen.
