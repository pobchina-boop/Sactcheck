Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Carboplatin

  • Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
  • Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
  • Allergic reaction, sometimes after previous exposure
  • Kidney or electrolyte changes

Paclitaxel

  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Numbness / tingling, sometimes lasting
  • Hair loss, muscle or joint pain
  • Infusion reaction
  • Mouth, bowel, skin and nail effects

Doxorubicin

  • Low counts, infection and mouth ulcers
  • Nausea, hair loss and fatigue
  • Heart damage, sometimes delayed
  • Severe tissue injury if the infusion leaks
  • Rare later blood cancers

Cyclophosphamide

  • Low counts and infection
  • Nausea, vomiting and hair loss
  • Bladder irritation / bleeding
  • Fertility effects and menopause
  • Rare later blood cancers

Make the plan practical

Separate anti-sickness plans

The platinum/taxane phase and AC need separate prescriptions. Carboplatin/paclitaxel is moderate emetic risk on each weekly treatment day. AC uses its own high-risk plan.

Growth-factor support

G-CSF is prescribed for every dose-dense AC cycle. Ask for the exact injection days and instructions.

Checks before the next dose

FBC, renal and liver tests weekly throughout chemotherapy, including review before each AC dose. Report new symptoms before the next dose.

After chemotherapy

Surgery and pathology review follow chemotherapy. Further systemic treatment, radiotherapy and follow-up depend on the findings and the MDT plan; no routine postoperative immunotherapy is specified by this regimen.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
