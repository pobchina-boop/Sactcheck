Active medicines: cetuximab

Emetogenic risk: low

MedicineDose / routeWhen
Chlorphenamine10 mg IV60 minutes before cetuximabNCCP suggested premedication.
Dexamethasone8 mg IV60 minutes before cetuximabNCCP suggested mandatory infusion premedication; reconcile low-risk antiemetic support to avoid duplicate steroid.
Rescue antiemeticAs individually prescribedIf requiredLow emetic risk; use current NCCP/local guidance and account for the infusion steroid.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Skin care and sun protection under local EGFR policy.
  • First two doses require post-infusion observation; delayed-reaction education.
  • Monthly electrolytes/Mg/Ca for two months after last dose.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
