When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. New breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, severe diarrhoea or inability to drink require prompt assessment. Report eye pain, blurred vision, blistering or infected skin promptly.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

Understand your treatment

How your medicines work

To control EGFR-expressing RAS wild-type bowel cancer that has spread after oxaliplatin- and irinotecan-based treatments have failed and irinotecan cannot be tolerated.

EGFR-targeted antibody

Cetuximab

Erbitux
Blocks growth messages at the cell surface

Cetuximab attaches to EGFR on the outside of cells and blocks growth messages. In bowel cancer, a validated RAS wild-type result helps identify whether this approach is suitable. Healthy skin also uses EGFR, so skin and nail effects need early care.

EGFR receives a growth signal↓Cetuximab blocks the receptor↓Some growth signals are reduced

What to watch for
Report rash or sore nails early. Tell the nurse immediately about infusion symptoms. Eye pain, new breathing problems or severe diarrhoea need prompt advice. Blood tests check minerals during treatment and for two months afterwards.

Read the whole cycle as text

Day 1: Treatment and checks

The team reviews blood tests, symptoms and your prescription before treatment. Premedicines and observation are part of cetuximab visits. Know the contact number and the next planned visit.

Day 2–3: Care between visits

Follow the skin-care or bowel-symptom plan provided. There is no pump to remove. Call early for symptoms; do not wait for the next appointment.

Day 4–7: Recovery and the next review

The team uses symptoms, tests and your priorities to decide what happens next. Bring questions and a symptom record if useful; this is optional.

Practical treatment guide

Infusion visits and care between treatments

Cetuximab on Day 1. No home chemotherapy pump. A repeating 7-day cycle. The number of cycles depends on benefit, side effects and your treatment plan.

STEP 1

Treatment phases and appointments

One larger first infusion is followed by weekly infusions. Bowel-cancer treatment continues while helping and tolerated.

STEP 2

Checks after treatment

Monthly electrolytes, magnesium and calcium for two months after the last cetuximab dose; assign responsibility before treatment ends.

STEP 3

Access and practical support

Day-unit IV access; no home fluorouracil pump.

Do I need a port?

A peripheral cannula or existing central line may be used. A port is not automatically needed; the team considers your veins and overall treatment plan.

If the schedule changes

Ask the unit to confirm the next visit and which medicine is being changed. Do not copy dates from another regimen.

Observation at every antibody visit

Premedicines and monitoring add time to every cetuximab visit. Your team will confirm the observation period before you leave; do not assume that later visits can omit observation.

Make the plan practical

Support around the medicines

First cetuximab dose

Skin care and sun protection under local EGFR policy. Premedicines and monitoring add time to every cetuximab visit. Your team will confirm the observation period before you leave; do not assume that later visits can omit observation. Report delayed reaction symptoms urgently. Monthly electrolytes/Mg/Ca for two months after last dose.

Subsequent weekly cetuximab

Premedication continues for subsequent infusions. Post-treatment monthly electrolytes/Mg/Ca for two months.

Previous infusion reactions

Tell the team about any previous severe antibody reaction, even if you feel well now. Premedication does not remove all reaction risk. Report breathing problems, swelling, faintness or rapidly worsening symptoms immediately.

Treatment reference · 01

Your treatment aim

The setting changes what benefit means.

Why this option?

Cetuximab alone is an option for RAS wild-type bowel cancer that has spread after oxaliplatin and irinotecan have failed, when irinotecan cannot be tolerated. Your team explains the aim, alternatives and how benefit will be reviewed.

A plan that can change

For bowel cancer, discuss control of cancer and symptoms, and whether further local treatment may become possible. Your preferences and priorities matter.

Treatment reference · 02

Before the first treatment

Checks reduce avoidable risk.

Blood tests and medicines

Review blood counts, kidney/liver tests, symptoms, allergies and all medicines and supplements. Tell the team about red-meat allergy or tick bites before cetuximab because these can be associated with infusion-reaction risk.

Preparing access

Allow for blood tests, review, pharmacy preparation, infusion and observation. A port is not automatically required; ask which access is suitable.

Treatment reference · 03

Symptoms in context

Report what changed and how it affects you.

When to call

New breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, severe diarrhoea or inability to drink require prompt assessment. Report eye pain, blurred vision, blistering or infected skin promptly.

Bowel symptoms

Follow your written rescue plan. Fever, blood, severe pain, reduced urine or inability to drink needs urgent advice.

Skin and nail changes

Start the prescribed skin-care plan early, use moisturiser and sun protection, and report painful or infected skin or nails. Keep mineral blood-test appointments during treatment and for two months after cetuximab.

Treatment reference · 04

Living with treatment

Use the support available.

Food, activity and practical help

Use prescribed anti-sickness medicines, pace activities and ask for nutritional, emotional or practical support.

Pregnancy and fertility

Discuss fertility preservation and reproductive plans before treatment. Ask for the contraception and breastfeeding instructions for every medicine; the whole regimen may require longer precautions than one medicine alone.

When treatment changes or ends

Follow-up continues.

Symptoms and monitoring

Continue the cetuximab skin plan and mineral checks for two months after the last dose. Ask which medicines to continue, when to seek help and how response will be assessed.

Next decisions

The team reviews benefit, side effects and your priorities. Supportive and palliative care can help alongside active treatment. Ask for a clear next-step and contact plan.

In selected previously treated bowel cancer, cetuximab improved disease control and survival compared with supportive care.

Questions worth asking

How is my treatment chosen?

Cetuximab alone is an option for RAS wild-type bowel cancer that has spread after oxaliplatin and irinotecan have failed, when irinotecan cannot be tolerated. Your team explains the aim, alternatives and how benefit will be reviewed.

Do I need to enter dates to understand the cycle?

No. The graph explains a general pattern. Your hospital arranges appointments; the guide and its safety information are available without entering anything.

Will I have a pump at home?

There is no home fluorouracil pump in this regimen. Your team chooses a cannula or central line based on your veins and wider treatment plan.

What happens if a dose is delayed?

The team checks the cause and may delay, reduce, change or stop one medicine. Ask which component changes and when you should next attend; do not restart yourself.

How will I know whether it is helping?

Scans, symptoms and clinical review assess benefit. A rash, side effect or good day alone cannot show whether the cancer is responding.

The graphic below shows the relationship.

What about fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding?

Discuss fertility preservation and reproductive plans before treatment. Ask for the contraception and breastfeeding instructions for every medicine; the whole regimen may require longer precautions than one medicine alone.

Why is RAS testing mentioned?

RAS testing selects cetuximab treatment in colorectal cancer. Confirm the cancer-specific plan with your team.

Which checks continue when treatment changes or finishes?

Monthly electrolytes, magnesium and calcium for two months after the last cetuximab dose; assign responsibility before treatment ends.
