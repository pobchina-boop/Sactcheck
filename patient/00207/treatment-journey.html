The whole treatment journey

Weekly cetuximab: treatment and checks

Selected metastatic colorectal cancer; or locally advanced head/neck cancer with radiotherapy when platinum is unsuitable

One larger first infusion is followed by weekly infusions. Bowel-cancer treatment continues while helping and tolerated. For head-and-neck treatment, cetuximab starts one week before radiotherapy and continues until radiation treatment finishes.
