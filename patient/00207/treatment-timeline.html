Head-and-neck indication: cetuximab before and during radiotherapy

This panel applies only to the head-and-neck radiotherapy indication of NCCP 00207. Cetuximab is a targeted antibody. Colorectal cetuximab alone has no routine radiotherapy component.

The axis shows order, not equal durations. Cetuximab starts one week before radiation and continues until radiation ends. The radiation team sets the number of sessions and confirms eligibility.

After the course: continue skin, mouth, swallowing and nutrition support. Mineral monitoring continues for two months after the final cetuximab dose.
