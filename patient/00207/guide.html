Weekly cetuximab

One larger first infusion is followed by weekly infusions. Bowel-cancer treatment continues while helping and tolerated. For head-and-neck treatment, cetuximab starts one week before radiotherapy and continues until radiation treatment finishes.

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Cetuximab

  • Rash, dry skin, cracks and sore nails
  • Eye inflammation or corneal injury
  • Low magnesium, calcium or potassium
  • Diarrhoea, dehydration and kidney problems
  • Infusion reaction; rare serious lung or skin effects

Head-and-neck indication only: cetuximab starts one week before radiation, then continues alongside it. See the course graphic.

Make the plan practical

First cetuximab dose

Skin care and sun protection under local EGFR policy. First two doses require post-infusion observation; delayed-reaction education. Monthly electrolytes/Mg/Ca for two months after last dose.

Subsequent weekly cetuximab

Premedication continues for subsequent infusions. Head/neck RT requires separate mucosal, skin, swallow and nutrition support. Post-treatment monthly electrolytes/Mg/Ca for two months.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
