The whole treatment journey

Weekly cetuximab + head and neck radiotherapy: treatment and checks

Locally advanced squamous head and neck cancer in people ineligible for platinum-based treatment.

Cetuximab starts one week before radiotherapy: a 400 mg/m² first infusion, then 250 mg/m² every week during radiation until radiotherapy ends. The number of weekly doses follows the actual radiotherapy plan.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.
