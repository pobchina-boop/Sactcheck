Weekly cetuximab + head and neck radiotherapy

Treatment context
Cetuximab is combined with radiotherapy for selected locally advanced head and neck cancer when platinum chemotherapy is unsuitable. The team discusses the reason for choosing this treatment and the alternatives.
Schedule
Cetuximab starts one week before radiotherapy: a 400 mg/m² first infusion, then 250 mg/m² every week during radiation until radiotherapy ends. The number of weekly doses follows the actual radiotherapy plan.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Cetuximab

EGFR-targeted antibody
Infusion reactions including anaphylaxis
Acne-like rash, dry skin and nail infection
Low magnesium and other mineral changes
Eye inflammation
Diarrhoea and dehydration
Rare serious lung inflammation

Radiotherapy

Local treatment
Sore mouth and throat
Dry mouth, taste and saliva changes
Swallowing or voice problems
Skin reaction in the treated area
Jaw stiffness and dental problems
Some swallowing, saliva or tissue effects can be lasting

Discuss the individual aim, alternatives and uncertainty; serious or lasting harms; treatment burden; fertility and pregnancy precautions; and the patient’s priorities. Explain infusion anaphylaxis, rash and nail/eye problems, mineral loss and post-treatment testing; the observation plan requires reconciliation of NCCP and product information. Discuss radiation mouth, skin, swallowing, saliva, dental and jaw effects, including lasting effects and potential nutritional support. Radiation has its own consent process. Confirm actual urgent contacts and teach-back; this draft does not authorise treatment.
