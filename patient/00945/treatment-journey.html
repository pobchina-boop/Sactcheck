Tests in your plan

Before treatment

WhenBlood count, kidney/liver profile, glucose, HbA1c, lipase and thyroid tests; assess skin and nerves. Eye examination and ECG are arranged when indicated. Review diabetes, immune conditions, medicines and previous treatment.

Confirm diagnosis, suitability and the required safety checks.

During treatment and follow-up

WhenNCCP specifies blood count, kidney/liver profile and glucose on days 1 and8 of cycle1 and day 1 of later cycles. Thyroid tests every 3–6 weeks; skin checks throughout. HbA1c, lipase, cortisol, nerve and eye reviews depend on symptoms and the plan. For the42-day option, the team must explicitly confirm tests for each enfortumab block.

Detect toxicity and assess response with the appropriate tests.

The whole treatment journey

Enfortumab vedotin + pembrolizumab: treatment and checks

First-line unresectable or metastatic urothelial cancer

Option 1: enfortumab on days 1 and8 plus pembrolizumab200 mg on day 1, repeating every 21 days. Option 2: enfortumab on days 1,8,22 and29 plus pembrolizumab400 mg on day 1, repeating every 42 days. Your team selects one option. The six-week reference shows two enfortumab blocks; pembrolizumab day 22 applies only to option 1.

Help control advanced urothelial cancer using a targeted chemotherapy-carrying antibody and immune treatment.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999.
