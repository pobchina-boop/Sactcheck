My tests and checks

WhenWhat is checkedTiming / reason
BeforeBloods and health reviewNCCP / product informationFull blood count, kidney and liver tests, blood glucose and a bone blood profile. Review heart disease, blood pressure, diabetes, mood, falls and fracture risk, sexual health, medicines and the treatment goal.
DuringSafety and responseNCCP / individual planRepeat blood count, kidney and liver tests, glucose and bone blood profile when clinically indicated. Review symptoms, PSA and testosterone; scans and the frequency of tests follow the individual plan.

Between treatments

  • Hormone effects can occur throughout treatment; there is no guaranteed symptom-free week.
  • The team assesses temporary anti-androgen protection against the initial hormone flare. Report worsening pain or trouble passing urine promptly. New weakness or changes in bladder/bowel control require emergency assessment. Keep the written injection dates even if you feel well.

Bring your medicine list and exact appointment dates.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.
