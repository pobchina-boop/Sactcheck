Treatment context
Shrink the cancer before surgery and contribute to reducing recurrence risk as part of a complete early-breast plan.
Schedule
Six 21-day cycles before surgery: Phesgo, docetaxel and carboplatin on Day 1. Surgery and pathology determine the postoperative HER2 medicine and remaining course.

Discuss the aim, alternatives, uncertain benefit, common and serious risks, heart function, diarrhoea, injection reaction, fertility and pregnancy precautions. Effective contraception is required during Phesgo and for 7 months after the last dose; review the full combination and breastfeeding restrictions with the team. After surgery, your team reviews the pathology and chooses the next HER2 medicine. The usual plan aims to complete about one year of HER2 treatment from the first dose. Remaining invasive cancer can change the medicine offered. The selected treatment has its own blood tests and supportive medicines.
