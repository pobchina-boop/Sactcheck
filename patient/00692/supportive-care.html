Additional phase-specific support

  • Antiemetic classification refers to the active treatment day. Reconcile duplicate steroid entries before prescribing.
  • No automatic granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF) schedule is added; use the patient’s neutropenia history, risk and consultant plan.
  • Cetuximab premedication and infusion observation remain necessary even on an antibody-only day.
  • Start local EGFR skin care; monitor magnesium, calcium and potassium before and periodically during therapy, then monthly for two months after the final dose.
  • Cold exposure can precipitate oxaliplatin neurotoxicity; do not recommend ice chips during fluorouracil in this combination. Review neuropathy every two cycles and sooner if symptoms interfere with function.
  • Confirm line care, pump contact details and disconnection arrangements; report chest pain, severe early fluorouracil toxicity or pump/line problems urgently.
  • National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00692 v2 p1 permits omitting observation after two tolerated cetuximab doses, whereas current EMA Erbitux SmPC sections 4.2/4.4 require monitoring during and for at least one hour after every infusion. Reconcile this conflict with oncology pharmacy/local policy; do not automatically waive monitoring. Chemotherapy must start no earlier than one hour after cetuximab ends.
  • FOLFOX4 uses two separate 22-hour fluorouracil infusions on Days 1 and 2, with a second treatment visit; never substitute a single 46-hour modified-FOLFOX6 pump schedule.
  • Cetuximab and FOLFOX may be delayed independently. Reconcile actual treatment days, component-specific recovery and previous dose changes before updating support prescriptions.
  • Intolerable cetuximab skin reactions require interruption even when graded below 3 under the EMA product information; review infection and supportive skin care promptly.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
