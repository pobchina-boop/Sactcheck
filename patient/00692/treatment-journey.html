The whole treatment journey

Weekly cetuximab + FOLFOX-4: treatment and checks

Advanced / metastatic colorectal cancer

Cetuximab on Day 1 and Day 8. FOLFOX4 starts on Day 1: oxaliplatin, folinic acid and fluorouracil bolus, followed by a 22-hour fluorouracil infusion. Day 2 brings another fluorouracil bolus and a second 22-hour infusion; finish around Day 3. Your team confirms the full Day 2 prescription. Repeat every 14 days while beneficial and tolerable.
