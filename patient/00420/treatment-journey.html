Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenBefore starting and before each 21-day cycle; repeat sooner for fever, bleeding or abnormal results.

Check infection-fighting cells, haemoglobin and platelets.

Kidney function and electrolytes

WhenBefore starting and before each cycle; additional checks accompany the prescribed hydration plan and reduced urine, dehydration or abnormal results.

Assess cisplatin kidney safety and identify magnesium or other electrolyte losses.

Liver tests

WhenBefore starting and before each cycle; repeat when results or symptoms warrant.

Assess doxorubicin handling and detect liver injury.

Heart tracing and heart imaging

If part of your plan
WhenHeart tracing before starting. Heart ultrasound or multigated acquisition scan if age above 65 or clinically indicated in the source; repeat imaging when indicated by symptoms or risk. The linked doxorubicin product information additionally recommends baseline pumping-function imaging and repeats after approximately each accumulated 100 mg/m². The specialist team reconciles this with the national source and writes the cardiac monitoring schedule.

Assess cardiac risk alongside lifetime anthracycline exposure.

Hearing assessment

If part of your plan
WhenReview symptoms before further cisplatin; formal audiology at baseline or later when clinically indicated. Report changes immediately.

Identify cisplatin-related hearing injury.

Nerve and symptom review

WhenAt each treatment review and whenever numbness, weakness or other symptoms change.

Detect neuropathy and other treatment effects before more dosing.

Surgery, pathology and response review

If part of your plan
WhenReview at the planned transition after three preoperative cycles and after surgery; scans and further reviews follow the consultant-set plan.

Coordinate cancer control and postoperative treatment.
