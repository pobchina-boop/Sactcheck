Further immune-related risks and lasting effects

Brain or nerve inflammation, severe weakness or paralysis (including encephalitis, Guillain–Barré or myasthenic syndromes)
Eye inflammation or changes in vision
Inflammation of the pancreas (pancreatitis)
New type 1 diabetes, which can present with dangerous blood-acid build-up (ketoacidosis)
Severe blistering or peeling skin reactions
Immune blood disorders, such as destruction of red blood cells; other rare inflammatory disorders can occur
Immune effects can start after treatment stops; some hormone deficiencies or other effects can be permanent
Treatment may need to be held or stopped; steroids, other immune-suppressing medicines, hospital care or lifelong hormone replacement may be needed
Immune reactions can become life-threatening or fatal; report new or persistent symptoms promptly

General treatment discussion

Possible short-term, long-term, unexpected and unlisted effects; benefits are not guaranteed.
Severe complications can require hospital admission and can rarely be fatal.
Infection / sepsis, allergic or infusion reactions and leakage outside a vein (extravasation) should be discussed where applicable to the medicines and route.
Pregnancy, contraception, breastfeeding, sexual health and fertility preservation should be discussed where relevant.
Treatment can be delayed, changed or stopped if risks outweigh benefit. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn.
