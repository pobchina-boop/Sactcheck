Your treatment journey

Pembrolizumab with cisplatin and radiotherapy for cervical cancer

The aim is to cure the cancer. Radiation treats the cervix and nearby areas. Cisplatin helps radiation work, and pembrolizumab helps the immune system recognise cancer cells.

Your infusion schedule

Cycles 1 and 2 · day 1

Both infusions, fluids and the booked external-beam fraction.

Cycle 1 days 8 and 15 · cycle 2 day 8

Weekly cisplatin and radiotherapy. Pembrolizumab is not scheduled on these days.

Cycle 2 day 15 · consultant decision

An extra cisplatin dose may be given only if the consultant requests it and radiation is being delivered.

Cycles 3–5 · every 21 days

Pembrolizumab alone on day 1, 200 mg over 30 minutes.

Cycles 6–20 · every 42 days

Pembrolizumab alone on day 1, 400 mg over 30 minutes. Confirm your first six-week appointment.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat your team checksWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count, kidney and liver tests, blood sugar and thyroid tests. Hearing assessment if indicated.Tell the team about hearing loss, immune disease, steroid use or problems drinking.
Before and during cyclesBlood count, kidney and liver tests and glucose before each cycle. Thyroid tests every 3–6 weeks, cortisol and hearing checks when indicated. Cisplatin visits need hydration and electrolyte review.Report less urine, diarrhoea, cough, marked fatigue or dizziness. Give the team the actual radiation dates.
After radiation and immunotherapyThe team reviews pelvic recovery, cancer response and delayed immune effects.Keep the alert card. Ask when to return for bleeding, bowel or bladder problems, vaginal changes or menopause symptoms.

The team individualises response assessments and extra tests according to symptoms and treatment aim.

At review visits

  • What if my radiation is cancelled? Phone both teams. Cisplatin waits until radiation resumes. Your team separately decides whether pembrolizumab is due.
  • What happens after the fifth week? The team checks whether a sixth cisplatin dose is appropriate. Pembrolizumab continues on its own. Ask for the dates for brachytherapy and the switch to six-weekly visits.
