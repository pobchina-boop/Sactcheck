Your complete treatment reference

Pembrolizumab with cisplatin and radiotherapy for cervical cancer

Your treatment and how the medicines work

The aim is to cure the cancer. Radiation treats the cervix and nearby areas. Cisplatin helps radiation work, and pembrolizumab helps the immune system recognise cancer cells.

Pembrolizumab · Keytruda

PD-1 immunotherapy. Blocks a signal that normally slows immune cells. This can help them attack the cancer. The same response can inflame healthy organs. Report a new cough, breathlessness, diarrhoea, rash, severe fatigue, thirst or dizziness, including after your last dose. Carry your immunotherapy alert card.

Cisplatin · Cisplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. Attaches to DNA so cells struggle to copy and repair it. During radiotherapy this can increase damage to the cancer cells. Call if you pass much less urine, cannot keep fluids down, or develop ringing ears, hearing changes or persistent tingling.

Your infusion schedule

Cycles 1 and 2 · day 1

Both infusions, fluids and the booked external-beam fraction.

Cycle 1 days 8 and 15 · cycle 2 day 8

Weekly cisplatin and radiotherapy. Pembrolizumab is not scheduled on these days.

Cycle 2 day 15 · consultant decision

An extra cisplatin dose may be given only if the consultant requests it and radiation is being delivered.

Cycles 3–5 · every 21 days

Pembrolizumab alone on day 1, 200 mg over 30 minutes.

Cycles 6–20 · every 42 days

Pembrolizumab alone on day 1, 400 mg over 30 minutes. Confirm your first six-week appointment.

Common effects and what to do

What you may notice

Fatigue, nausea or appetite changes can build during pelvic treatment. The team can help with food, fluids and bowel symptoms.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology line for fever or shaking chills, new breathlessness, frequent diarrhoea, very little urine, severe headache, heavy bleeding or severe weakness. If breathing becomes difficult at rest, seek emergency help. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Planning your visits

Appointments

Cycles 1–5 last 21 days. Cisplatin is planned weekly for five doses in cycles 1–2, with a possible sixth dose. Pembrolizumab continues alone from cycle 3 and changes to every 42 days for cycles 6–20. Treatment can end earlier and lasts up to 24 months.

On the day

When both drugs are due, pembrolizumab is infused over 30 minutes before cisplatin over 60 minutes. IV fluids protect the kidneys. Radiation follows cisplatin that day. External-beam radiation and the later internal treatment (brachytherapy) use a separately booked calendar.

Between visits

Radiation appointments are booked separately. Keep both calendars together. Tell the chemotherapy and radiation teams about any dose or appointment change.

What the research found

Among people with stage III–IVA disease, the group receiving immunotherapy with chemoradiation had fewer deaths during follow-up.

Subgroup findings are exploratory. The overall trial also included earlier node-positive stages. It did not test pembrolizumab alone against chemoradiotherapy or directly compare induction chemotherapy.
