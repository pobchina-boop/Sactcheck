When to call

Call the 24-hour oncology line for fever or shaking chills, new breathlessness, frequent diarrhoea, very little urine, severe headache, heavy bleeding or severe weakness. If breathing becomes difficult at rest, seek emergency help.

Contact your oncology service urgently.
