When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. New chest pain, fainting, severe breathlessness, fever or sudden illness needs urgent assessment. Report worsening numbness, severe constipation, mouth ulcers or inability to drink promptly.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

Your medicines and what they do

Control advanced cancer and symptoms while balancing side effects and treatment burden.

Chemotherapy

Eribulin

Halaven · eribulin base
Stops microtubules growing

Eribulin blocks growth of the tiny structures cells need to divide. It can slow cancer growth, but can also affect blood cells and nerves.

Cell-division structures↓Growth blocked↓Cell division disrupted

What to watch for
Report numbness, weakness or difficulty with walking or hand tasks before the next dose. Fever or sudden illness needs immediate advice; palpitations or fainting need urgent assessment.

Practical treatment guide

Your treatment and access plan

Eribulin on Days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Days 8 and 22 are planned treatment-free weeks. This alternative schedule requires a specific discussion and consent.

STEP 1

Before treatment

FBC, kidney and liver tests at baseline and the start of each cycle. Confirm count recovery for each administration. Correct low potassium, calcium and magnesium before treatment and monitor periodically.

STEP 2

Plan venous access

A cannula, PICC or implanted port may be used. A port is not automatically required; insertion and removal are separate procedures with individual instructions.

STEP 3

On the treatment day

Confirm the medicines due that day, any premedicines and the reviewed blood results. Tell staff immediately about pain, burning, rash or breathlessness.

STEP 4

After the visit

No routine take-home infusion pump is required for this regimen. Keep line-care, urgent-contact and next-review instructions.

Visit length

Eribulin is given over 5 minutes in this source. Preparation and blood review add to the visit; doses are on Days 1 and 15.

Line care

A PICC or port has its own care plan. Report fever, redness, swelling, pain, leakage or line damage; do not remove or adjust it yourself.

Treatment changes

A delayed, omitted or changed dose needs an updated plan from the treating team. Do not move doses or restart on your own.

Support for this visit

No routine hypersensitivity premedication is specified. Low-risk antiemetic support is reviewed separately, including QT interactions.
