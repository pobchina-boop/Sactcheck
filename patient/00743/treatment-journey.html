What each check is for

1Before treatmentBefore the first dose

Locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer after prior chemotherapy · alternative eribulin schedule. NCCP specifies FBC, kidney and liver tests at baseline and the start of each cycle. EMA Halaven information additionally requires FBC before every dose, including Day 15. Correct low potassium, calcium and magnesium before treatment; monitor periodically.

Ask / act: Confirm eligibility, test results and medicine list.

Sources: NCCP 00743

2Other checksAt the specified review points

ECG monitoring is indicated with heart failure, bradyarrhythmia, QT-prolonging medicines or electrolyte disturbance. Review QT risk when choosing antiemetics; monitor neuropathy.

Ask / act: Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.

Sources: NCCP 00743 · SMPC

3Support and practical planAt each treatment or phase change

No routine hypersensitivity premedication is specified. Low-risk antiemetic support is reviewed separately, including QT interactions.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support plan; confirm active medicines.

Sources: NCCP 00743

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

Agree the scan type and review interval for the known disease sites. Review response, symptoms and toxicity together; new or worsening symptoms may need an earlier assessment. Keep the agreed follow-up and report persistent or new symptoms between visits.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities.

Sources: METASTATIC · MARKERS
