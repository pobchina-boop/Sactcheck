Eribulin · Days 1 and 15 of 28

Eribulin on Days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Days 8 and 22 are planned treatment-free weeks. This alternative schedule requires a specific discussion and consent.

Source reconciled; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient review pending. Selected effects; not a complete medicine leaflet.

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Eribulin

  • Low blood counts, infection and sepsis
  • Numbness, tingling or weakness, sometimes lasting
  • Fatigue, hair loss, nausea and constipation
  • Changes to heart rhythm / QT in susceptible people

Surgery, radiation and monitoring: open the whole-treatment map or scan the QR.

Make the plan practical

Supportive medicines

No routine hypersensitivity premedication is specified. Low-risk antiemetic support is reviewed separately, including QT interactions.

Everyday care

Keep gently active as tolerated, pace rest and activity, and report nerve symptoms before the next dose.

Tests and appointments

Ask who books each test, when it is due and who reviews the result. Tell the team if an expected appointment has not arrived.

Correct medicine and schedule

This is not the licensed Day 1 / Day 8 schedule. The reason for the alternative schedule, evidence limits and off-label consent are part of the treatment discussion.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
