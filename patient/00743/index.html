Eribulin on Days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Days 8 and 22 are planned treatment-free weeks. This alternative schedule requires a specific discussion and consent.

Tests and checks

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) specifies full blood count (FBC), kidney and liver tests at baseline and the start of each cycle. EMA Halaven information additionally requires FBC before every dose, including Day 15. Correct low potassium, calcium and magnesium before treatment; monitor periodically. electrocardiogram (ECG) monitoring is indicated with heart failure, bradyarrhythmia, QT-prolonging medicines or electrolyte disturbance. Review QT risk when choosing antiemetics; monitor neuropathy.

Between treatment visits

Treatment markers show the planned days. Symptom bands show broad possibilities, not measured risk curves or guaranteed symptom-free days.

Fever, shivers or suddenly feeling unwell needs prompt advice on any day, including planned treatment-free weeks.
