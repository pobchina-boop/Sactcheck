Brigatinib monotherapy · ALK-positive advanced NSCLC Brigatinib on its own for adults with ALK-positive advanced non-small-cell lung cancer: either after crizotinib (00562a) or before any ALK inhibitor (00562b). This is not an adjuvant course or a general pathway after every ALK inhibitor.
NCCP 00562 · version 4

Brigatinib Therapy

The usual starting plan is 90 mg once daily for seven days, then 180 mg once daily if your team confirms the increase. Some people need a different dose. Treatment continues while helping and tolerable, with regular reviews and no fixed number of cycles.

What each check is for

1Check the exact indicationBefore the first dose

Treatment line and ALK result define this pathway.

Ask / act: Confirm written lead-in, monitoring and contact plan.

Sources: NCCP 00562

2Early pulmonary surveillanceFirst week and scheduled day 8 / day 15 review

Early pulmonary events can occur before the first follow-up.

Ask / act: Report immediately; review whether escalation is still appropriate.

Sources: NCCP 00562 · SMPC

3Ongoing testsEvery two weeks initially for selected tests; monthly for others

Liver, cardiac and metabolic monitoring have different cadences.

Ask / act: Confirm due window, booking team and result reviewer.

Sources: NCCP 00562

4Restart or stop reviewAny pause, dose change or final dose

Reason and duration of a break change the restart plan.

Ask / act: Obtain a new individual written plan; retain contraception and five-day sun precautions after stopping.

Sources: NCCP 00562 · SMPC
