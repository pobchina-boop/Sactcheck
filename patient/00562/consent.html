Brigatinib Therapy

The usual starting plan is 90 mg once daily for seven days, then 180 mg once daily if your team confirms the increase. Some people need a different dose. Treatment continues while helping and tolerable, with regular reviews and no fixed number of cycles.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Brigatinib is a targeted medicine intended to control ALK-positive advanced lung cancer. The choice depends on the cancer’s ALK result, previous treatment, your health and your priorities. Brigatinib on its own for adults with ALK-positive advanced non-small-cell lung cancer: either after crizotinib (00562a) or before any ALK inhibitor (00562b). This is not an adjuvant course or a general pathway after every ALK inhibitor.

Material risks and support

  • Lung inflammation or early breathing reactions, which can be serious or fatal
  • Raised blood pressure or a slow heartbeat
  • Liver injury and abnormal liver tests
  • High blood glucose
  • Raised muscle enzyme with muscle pain or weakness
  • Raised pancreatic enzymes; report severe abdominal pain
  • Visual disturbance
  • Diarrhoea, nausea, vomiting, reduced appetite and fatigue
  • Skin rash and sensitivity to sunlight
  • Low blood counts and infection risk
  • Allergic reactions; pregnancy and fertility risks

Discuss the exact indication and prior ALK treatment, likely benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, serious lung/heart/liver/glucose/vision risks, common symptoms, fertility and contraception, monitoring and the practical tablet plan. Agree who reviews a hold or restart. Consent can be reconsidered.

Monitoring and understanding

Before starting: ALK result, blood count, kidney/liver tests, breathing-function assessment, ECG, blood pressure, fasting glucose, muscle enzyme CPK and pancreatic enzymes. Review breathing symptoms on days 8 and 15, then monthly or sooner if needed. ECG and blood pressure are checked every two weeks initially, then monthly. Liver tests are every two weeks for the first three months, then as advised. Blood count, kidneys, fasting glucose, CPK and pancreatic enzymes are usually checked monthly. Scans follow your Consultant’s plan.
