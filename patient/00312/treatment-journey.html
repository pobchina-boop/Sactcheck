Your treatment journey

Weekly topotecan

Slow the cancer and help with symptoms after earlier treatment.

Your infusion schedule

Days 1, 8 and 15

Topotecan is given over 30 minutes on three weekly visits in a 28-day cycle. Day 22 is a rest day. Blood results determine whether each dose can go ahead.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count, kidney and liver function, prior marrow treatment and infection.Ask which blood results are needed before the first infusion.
Before each weekly doseNeutrophils, platelets and haemoglobin decide whether the infusion proceeds.Confirm your day 8 and day 15 blood appointments.
Between dosesReport fever, breathlessness, bruising or bleeding.Contact the unit urgently for fever or a new cough affecting your breathing.

Scan dates and tumour-marker testing are individualised by your oncology team.

At review visits

  • What happens at my next visit? Infusions on days 1, 8 and 15, followed by a week off.
  • Which symptoms should I phone about? Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. A new cough, shortness of breath or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent assessment.
  • What should I plan before starting? Ask which blood results are needed before the first infusion.
  • Do I come for topotecan in the fourth week? Treatment visits are days 1, 8 and 15. Day 22 is a planned break. If a dose is held, ask your team for the next confirmed blood test and treatment dates.
