My treatment handbook

Weekly topotecan

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Slow the cancer and help with symptoms after earlier treatment.

Topotecan · Hycamtin

Topoisomerase I inhibitor. It prevents dividing cells from repairing DNA breaks. It also affects bone marrow. Call urgently for fever, unexpected bleeding or new shortness of breath.

What the research found

Weekly dosing caused less severe blood toxicity than the five-day schedule in the randomised phase II study.

The study enrolled platinum-resistant disease. Choice of schedule also depends on prior treatment and the activity required.

Your infusion schedule

Days 1, 8 and 15

Topotecan is given over 30 minutes on three weekly visits in a 28-day cycle. Day 22 is a rest day. Blood results determine whether each dose can go ahead.

Common effects and what to do

How you feel between visits

Tiredness, nausea or appetite changes can make everyday activities harder. Ask your team for help if symptoms interfere with eating or drinking.

Serious effects: when to call

Low counts · Topotecan

Fever, bruising or breathlessness can signal low blood counts.

Lung inflammation · Topotecan

Call urgently for a new cough or difficulty breathing.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. A new cough, shortness of breath or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent assessment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
