Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Weekly topotecan

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Slow the cancer and help with symptoms after earlier treatment. Metastatic disease after prior therapy.

Treatment schedule. Topotecan is given over 30 minutes on three weekly visits in a 28-day cycle. Day 22 is a rest day. Blood results determine whether each dose can go ahead.

Regimen-specific side effects

Topotecan

Treatment discussion and decision

Weekly topotecan

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Weekly dosing caused less severe blood toxicity than the five-day schedule in the randomised phase II study. The study enrolled platinum-resistant disease. Choice of schedule also depends on prior treatment and the activity required.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. A new cough, shortness of breath or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent assessment. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
