When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Sunitinib

Sunitinib blocks several signalling proteins involved in tumour growth and blood-vessel formation. This can slow a pancreatic NET. Effects on healthy blood vessels and other tissues help explain high blood pressure, bleeding, wound-healing problems, thyroid changes and sore hands or feet.

Support at home

Skin and mouth care

Use the unit’s gentle skin and mouth-care plan. Report hand-foot pain before it limits walking or daily tasks; the team may adjust treatment.

Sickness and diarrhoea

This oral regimen is minimal-to-low emetogenic. Routine premedication is not required. Agree rescue anti-sickness and antidiarrhoeal medicines and when to call.

Blood pressure and glucose

Follow the agreed BP plan. If you use diabetes medicines, ask how to monitor glucose and manage low readings; sunitinib may lower blood sugar.

Nutrition and fatigue

Report poor intake, weight loss or troublesome fatigue. Thyroid changes, anaemia, dehydration or other causes may need treatment.
