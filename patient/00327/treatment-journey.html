The whole treatment journey

Sunitinib 37.5 mg · pancreatic NET: treatment and checks

Progressive unresectable or metastatic well-differentiated pancreatic neuroendocrine tumour under NCCP 00327a.

Take the prescribed sunitinib dose once daily continuously, at the same time, consistently with or without food. The 28-day cycle is a review period, with no planned capsule-free week.
