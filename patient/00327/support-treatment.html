No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Use the unit’s gentle skin and mouth-care plan. Report hand-foot pain before it limits walking or daily tasks; the team may adjust treatment.
  • This oral regimen is minimal-to-low emetogenic. Routine premedication is not required. Agree rescue anti-sickness and antidiarrhoeal medicines and when to call.
  • Follow the agreed blood pressure (BP) plan. If you use diabetes medicines, ask how to monitor glucose and manage low readings; sunitinib may lower blood sugar.
  • Report poor intake, weight loss or troublesome fatigue. Thyroid changes, anaemia, dehydration or other causes may need treatment.
  • No routine premedication is required by NCCP. Any antidiarrhoeal, rescue antiemetic or other support must follow the local prescription.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
