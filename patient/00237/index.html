Dabrafenib alone — advanced melanoma

To control advanced BRAF V600-mutated melanoma using dabrafenib alone, with regular review of benefit, fever and other side effects.

Advanced melanoma · NCCP 00237 v6 · Document revised 6 November 2026

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

This is dabrafenib monotherapy for advanced melanoma. Do not import the food, heart-testing, fertility or duration instructions of a different BRAF/MEK combination. The NCCP fever table states 38.5°C, while current EMA product information states interruption at 38°C for monotherapy; this guide uses the earlier current-product safety trigger and the team must reconcile the written plan.

My overall treatment

Dabrafenib twice daily, about 12 hours apart, every day. Take it on an empty stomach; there is no planned tablet-free week.

Between treatments

  • At 38°C or higher, stop and call under current product information. Take on an empty stomach. Skip missed dose if less than six hours until next; no extra after vomiting.

Bring medicine list, test dates and actual oncology/out-of-hours numbers. Example numbers must be replaced.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

What to watch for

You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

A picture of side effects

Match each symptom box to its numbered body location. Tell your team about new symptoms.

1Fever / head
Dabrafenib

Fever, chills and headache can occur. At 38°C or higher, stop and call under the current product information; severe weakness, dizziness or confusion is urgent.

3Skin
Dabrafenib

Rash, thickened skin and new skin cancers can occur. Report a changing lesion, a persistent sore or severe rash.

5Kidneys / hydration
Dabrafenib

Fever, vomiting or diarrhoea can cause dehydration and kidney injury. Report reduced urine or inability to drink.

7Joints / muscles
Dabrafenib

Joint pain, muscle pain or fatigue may affect activity. Report increasing pain or weakness and ask about suitable relief.

2Eyes
Dabrafenib

Pain, redness, light sensitivity or blurred vision may be inflammation. Prompt eye assessment may be needed.

4Liver
Dabrafenib

Routine blood tests look for liver changes. Yellow skin or eyes or dark urine need prompt review.

6Abdomen / pancreas
Dabrafenib

Nausea and bowel changes can occur. Severe or persistent abdominal pain needs urgent assessment because pancreatic inflammation is possible.

8Hands / feet
Dabrafenib

Sore thickened skin or redness on palms and soles can affect walking and grip. Report it early and ask for skin care tailored to your symptoms.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

If your temperature is 38°C or higher, stop dabrafenib and contact the oncology team immediately under the current product information; do not restart on your own. Also call for shaking chills, dizziness, reduced urine, severe abdominal pain, eye pain or changed vision, or a blistering or spreading rash. Infection and medicine-related fever both need consideration.
