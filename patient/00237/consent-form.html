General treatment discussion

Possible short-term, long-term, unexpected and unlisted effects; benefits are not guaranteed.
Severe complications can require hospital admission and can rarely be fatal.
Infection / sepsis, allergic or infusion reactions and leakage outside a vein (extravasation) should be discussed where applicable to the medicines and route.
Pregnancy, contraception, breastfeeding, sexual health and fertility preservation should be discussed where relevant.
Treatment can be delayed, changed or stopped if risks outweigh benefit. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn.
