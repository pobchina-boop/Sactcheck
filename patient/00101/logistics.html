Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests; review performance status, current medicines, infection, neuropathy and the treatment goal.

STEP 2

Your treatment visit

Cabazitaxel is given by drip over one hour on Day 1 every 21 days, with prednisolone 10 mg daily. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerable; six cycles is not a fixed maximum. The unit gives chlorphenamine 10 mg intravenous (IV), dexamethasone 8 mg IV and famotidine 20 mg IV at least 30 minutes before the infusion.

STEP 3

IV access

Treatment is given through a cannula or suitable established access. No take-home pump is part of this regimen. Report pain, burning, leakage or swelling immediately.

STEP 4

Between visits

Collect the prescribed diarrhoea medicine before the first cycle. The team considers white-cell growth-factor support (granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF)), particularly with higher infection risk. Contact the team early for diarrhoea, fever, abdominal pain or reduced urine. Take prednisolone continuously as prescribed, with food. Do not stop suddenly when chemotherapy is delayed. Ask for a steroid alert card, an illness plan and advice if vomiting prevents you keeping tablets down.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next blood-test and treatment dates, support medicines and verified daytime/out-of-hours numbers.
