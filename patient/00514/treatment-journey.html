The whole treatment journey

Gemcitabine alone: nasopharyngeal cancer: treatment and checks

Recurrent or metastatic nasopharyngeal cancer that cannot be treated with local treatment aimed at cure.

Gemcitabine 1,250 mg/m² on Days 1 and 8 every 21 days, for up to 6 cycles or until progression or unacceptable side effects.
