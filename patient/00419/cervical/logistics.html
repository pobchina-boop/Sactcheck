SACTCheck · NCCP 00419 v6 · Locally advanced cervical cancer when cisplatin is unsuitable

Preparing for treatment visits

Weekly carboplatin with radiotherapy for cervical cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

One carboplatin infusion each week during radiotherapy for up to six cycles. The team stops carboplatin if the radiation course ends early.

At the unit

The team calculates your dose from kidney function. Carboplatin runs into a vein over about 30 minutes on the scheduled chemotherapy day.

After this part of treatment

If radiation is interrupted, ask both teams to confirm the next infusion. There is no routine follow-on chemotherapy in this cervical protocol.

Write down your next appointments

  • Discuss the reason cisplatin is unsuitable. Hearing and nerve tests are considered when indicated.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Contact oncology urgently for fever, heavy bleeding, uncontrolled diarrhoea, very little urine or vomiting that prevents drinking. Tell both teams if you miss an appointment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
