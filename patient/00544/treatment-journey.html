The whole treatment journey

Atezolizumab 1200 mg · every 3 weeks · lung cancer: treatment and checks

Lung settings: previously treated NSCLC; first-line PD-L1-high metastatic NSCLC; adjuvant NSCLC after surgery and platinum chemotherapy; ES-SCLC maintenance after induction.

Atezolizumab 1200 mg is given by intravenous infusion on Day 1 every 3 weeks (21 days). The first infusion lasts about 60 minutes; later infusions may last 30 minutes if tolerated. The visit includes checks and preparation. This protocol also offers a separately prescribed 1875 mg injection under the skin of the thigh over about 7 minutes every 3 weeks. The IV and under-skin doses are not interchangeable milligram for milligram. Adjuvant: maximum 12 months. Advanced NSCLC and ES-SCLC maintenance: until progression or unacceptable toxicity. ES-SCLC requires completed induction; this suite contains no induction chemotherapy.
