Adjuvant trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1): the whole pathway

Adjuvant · residual invasive HER2-positive early breast cancer

T-DM1 by IV infusion every 21 days for 14 postoperative cycles, unless recurrence or unacceptable toxicity requires a change.

What each check is for

1Blood tests and treatment reviewPrior-treatment checks → Baseline FBC, liver and renal tests → Before every cycle: platelets, liver and renal tests → Review abnormal results → As required by continuing care

Results may change whether a dose is given or held.

Ask / act: Confirm the booking and who reviews the result before treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00659

2Heart testsBaseline / earlier HER2 monitoring → Review LVEF before switching → Every 12 weeks; sooner if abnormal / symptoms → Completion assessment → Risk-based surveillance

ECG measures rhythm; echo measures pumping function. They are different checks.

Ask / act: Keep the planned appointments and report symptoms between them.

Sources: NCCP 00659 · ESC

3Imaging and markersResponse imaging for surgery → Pathology establishes this indication → No routine CT series if well → Agree mammogram schedule → Annual mammography of remaining breast tissue

The purpose and frequency depend on early versus advanced disease.

Ask / act: Ask which test is next and what decision its result will inform.

Sources: EARLY · BONE

4Bone health with endocrine treatmentAt endocrine / ovarian-suppression planning; repeat by risk

Low oestrogen can affect bone strength.

Ask / act: Ask whether DEXA, fracture-risk assessment or bone protection is appropriate.

Sources: BONE · NOGG

5Who arranges and reviews each check?At initiation and every handover

A recommended test can be missed if responsibility is unclear.

Ask / act: Ask the team to document the booking owner, result reviewer, due window and escalation route.

Sources: NCCP 00659
