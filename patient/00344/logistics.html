Preparing for treatment visits

FLOT · chemotherapy before and after surgery

Planning your visits

Day before treatment

Check the steroid prescription and bring your medication list.

Connecting the pump

The day unit attaches your 24-hour fluorouracil pump to your line on day 1. Confirm where and when your trained team will disconnect it on day 2.

At home with the pump

Keep the line secure, dry and free of kinks. Follow your unit’s instructions for sleeping and washing. Leave disconnection and any change to the flow to your trained team.

Pump or line trouble

Phone the unit promptly for swelling, pain, a wet dressing, leakage or a pump that finishes much earlier or later than planned. Follow your unit’s spill instructions.

Fatigue and eating after surgery

Small frequent meals and drinks can be easier. Contact your dietitian if your weight falls or feeds are difficult. Ask about support for fatigue.

Write down your next appointments

  • Take your prescribed steroids from the day before.
  • Confirm your day 2 pump appointment.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Phone oncology immediately for fever, chills, sudden illness, severe diarrhoea, or a leaking pump. Call 112 or 999 for severe chest pain, collapse or severe breathing difficulty. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
