Carboplatin + fluorouracil after nasopharyngeal chemoradiotherapy

This chemotherapy is an additional treatment after chemoradiotherapy for nasopharyngeal cancer. It aims to treat cancer cells that may remain. The team explains the expected benefit and burden for you, including recovery from the earlier treatment.

Adjuvant chemotherapy after chemoradiotherapy for locally advanced stage III or intravenous (IV) nasopharyngeal cancer when cisplatin cannot be used or tolerated. · NCCP 00552 v6 · Document revised 10 November 2026

My overall treatment

Between treatments

  • Continuous fluorouracil over 96 hours, or the prescribed daily 22-hour option on Days 1–4; confirm actual pump visits.
  • Report fever or sudden illness promptly. Follow the written support plan; do not wait for the next visit if symptoms worsen.

Bring your medicines list, appointment calendar, test results and verified urgent contacts.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
