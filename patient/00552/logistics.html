Carboplatin + fluorouracil after nasopharyngeal chemoradiotherapy

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00552 · Source reconciled draft; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the first visit

Bring your medicines and allergy list, test dates, treatment calendar and actual emergency contacts. Tell the unit about swallowing, hearing, mobility, transport or communication needs.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Carboplatin is infused over 30 minutes on Day 1. Its dose is calculated from kidney function using a target exposure called AUC 5. Fluorouracil 1,000 mg/m² per day is given as a continuous 96-hour infusion, or as the source-listed daily 22-hour option on Days 1–4. Your unit confirms the actual prescription, pump changes and disconnection time.

STEP 3

Infusion and appointment planning

Continuous fluorouracil over 96 hours, or the prescribed daily 22-hour option on Days 1–4; confirm actual pump visits.

STEP 4

Before you leave

Check the next treatment and test dates, medicines, pump or line appointments, and who to contact if unwell. Have the team replace and verify every example phone number on the wallet card.

STEP 5

Practical support

Ask about transport, work, costs, caring responsibilities, an interpreter or a support person. Keep changes to the plan in one record.
