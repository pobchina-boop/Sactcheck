Treatment context
Control advanced cancer and symptoms while balancing side effects and treatment burden.
Schedule
Atezolizumab 840 mg is given on Days 1 and 15, before nab-paclitaxel 100 mg/m² on Days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Nab-paclitaxel is planned for up to six cycles; extension needs consultant review. Atezolizumab may continue after chemotherapy stops.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Atezolizumab

PD-L1 checkpoint immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver or kidneys
Thyroid, adrenal or pituitary problems and diabetes
Rare serious heart, nerve or muscle inflammation
Infusion reactions and severe skin reactions

Nab-paclitaxel

Chemotherapy
Numbness / tingling or weakness, sometimes lasting
Low blood counts, infection and sepsis
Hair loss, fatigue and muscle / joint aches
Infusion reactions; uncommon serious lung inflammation
