The whole treatment journey

Atezolizumab + nab-paclitaxel: treatment and checks

Previously untreated unresectable locally advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer with eligible PD-L1 expression

Atezolizumab 840 mg is given on Days 1 and 15, before nab-paclitaxel 100 mg/m² on Days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Nab-paclitaxel is planned for up to six cycles; extension needs consultant review. Atezolizumab may continue after chemotherapy stops.

What each check is for

1Before treatmentBefore the first dose

Previously untreated unresectable locally advanced or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer with eligible PD-L1 expression. Baseline FBC, kidney/liver function, glucose, thyroid function, hepatitis B/C testing and morning cortisol. FBC, kidney/liver tests and glucose before each cycle; check the count results needed for Day 8 and Day 15 decisions.

Ask / act: Confirm eligibility, test results and medicine list.

Sources: NCCP 00688

2Other checksAt the specified review points

Thyroid function at Cycle 1 Day 1 and every second cycle. Review immune symptoms throughout and after treatment. Cardiac imaging is based on history/prior anthracyclines or clinical concern; there is no routine HER2-style echo schedule.

Ask / act: Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.

Sources: NCCP 00688

3Support and practical planAt each treatment or phase change

Separate combination, Day 8 chemotherapy-only and immunotherapy-only support; maintain immune and blood-count monitoring.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support plan; confirm active medicines.

Sources: NCCP 00688

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

Agree the scan type and review interval for the known disease sites. Review response, symptoms and toxicity together; new or worsening symptoms may need an earlier assessment. Keep the agreed follow-up and report persistent or new symptoms between visits.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities.

Sources: METASTATIC · MARKERS
