What each check is for

1Baseline reviewBefore treatment

HER2-positive neoadjuvant or adjuvant high-risk breast cancer. Review lifetime anthracycline exposure and baseline cardiac risk before AC; reassess before starting trastuzumab. NCCP specifies cardiac tests as clinically indicated; EMA trastuzumab information specifies baseline and three-monthly cardiac assessment during treatment. Keep the monitoring clock through the change to trastuzumab alone; arrange earlier review for symptoms or falling function. EMA product information recommends cardiac checks every 6 months after treatment until 24 months; following anthracyclines in early disease, yearly up to 5 years from the last trastuzumab dose or longer if function falls. Your team individualises this plan.

Ask / act: Confirm source eligibility and results.

Sources: NCCP 00433 · SMPC

2Treatment-day checkBefore each planned dose

Baseline blood tests and review before each chemotherapy administration according to the source and local plan. Once chemotherapy ends, testing follows trastuzumab and individual clinical needs.

Ask / act: Confirm active medicines and whether a dose is held.

Sources: NCCP 00433

3Support planAt every phase or day change

Four AC cycles 14 days apart with growth-factor support, then 12 weekly paclitaxel doses with weekly trastuzumab. Continue trastuzumab to complete one year total; no concurrent AC / trastuzumab.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support script; do not merge phases.

Sources: NCCP 00433

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

After early-breast treatment, breast imaging and clinical review follow the agreed plan. Routine CT/PET scans or blood tumour markers are not used to look for distant recurrence in a person without symptoms or clinical concerns.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities together.

Sources: NCCP 00433 · EARLY

At the next review, ask
