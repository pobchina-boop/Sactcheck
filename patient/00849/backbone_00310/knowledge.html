When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Contact your oncology team urgently for fever, feeling suddenly unwell, new breathlessness, chest pain, persistent diarrhoea, severe weakness, confusion, reduced urine or a severe rash. Follow your local emergency instructions; do not wait for the next appointment.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

Understand your treatment

Three medicines. Different jobs.

This treatment is given before planned surgery for resectable non-small-cell lung cancer at high risk of returning. It aims to treat the cancer before the operation and reduce the chance of recurrence. Surgery and benefit are not guaranteed.

Immunotherapy

Nivolumab

Opdivo
Blocks the PD-1 immune checkpoint

Nivolumab binds PD-1 on T cells. It prevents signals that can reduce the immune attack on cancer. This can also let immune cells inflame healthy tissues. It is infused every 21 days in this plan.

T cell↓PD-1 blocked↓Immune response

What to watch for
New breathing, bowel, skin, hormone or other symptoms need prompt advice during or after treatment.

Sources: nccp · smpc

Chemotherapy

Gemcitabine

Antimetabolite chemotherapy
Interferes with deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA) building

Gemcitabine interferes with making DNA, which dividing cancer cells need. Healthy blood-forming cells and other tissues can also be affected.

DNA building↓Replication disrupted↓Dividing cells affected

What to watch for
Report fever, unusual bleeding, breathlessness, rash, swelling or reduced urine promptly.

Sources: companion

Chemotherapy

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Damages DNA

Carboplatin forms links in DNA that hinder copying and repair. Healthy tissues can also be harmed. Its dose depends on target exposure (AUC) and measured or estimated kidney function; AUC is not a fixed number of milligrams. There are up to three planned doses, one in each neoadjuvant cycle before surgical review.

DNA↓Platinum links↓Copying disrupted

What to watch for
Report fever, bleeding, nausea, hearing changes or numbness. Tell the nurse immediately about possible allergy during the infusion.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm blood tests, transport, medicine instructions and the oncology contact number.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Nivolumab is infused over 30 minutes before chemotherapy. Gemcitabine is infused over 30 minutes on Days 1 and 8. Carboplatin is infused over 30 minutes. Blood-result review, preparation, support medicines and observation add to the visit.

STEP 3

Access

Treatment uses suitable intravenous (IV) access. No routine take-home infusion pump is used.

STEP 4

Home support

Follow the written anti-sickness plan; Day 8 gemcitabine has its own review and support instructions. Carboplatin dosing uses a verified kidney-function measurement; report dehydration or a change in health.

STEP 5

Surgical review

Keep the oncology and surgical teams informed about new symptoms and other appointments.

Treatment reference · 01

Your treatment before surgery

This treatment is given before planned surgery for resectable non-small-cell lung cancer at high risk of returning. It aims to treat the cancer before the operation and reduce the chance of recurrence. Surgery and benefit are not guaranteed.

Why these medicines?

Nivolumab helps immune cells recognise cancer; chemotherapy damages dividing cells. Your prescribed backbone is gemcitabine and carboplatin.

Sources: nccp

Evidence and uncertainty

In a randomized study, adding nivolumab to chemotherapy before surgery improved survival compared with chemotherapy alone. Your team considers the exact cancer, test results, fitness and preferences.

Sources: trial

Alternatives

Discuss surgery and other suitable treatments, clinical trials and the option of not proceeding; choices depend on the multidisciplinary assessment.

Prepare for the conversation

Questions worth asking

Will I receive all five combinations?

No. This guide is for gemcitabine and carboplatin with nivolumab. The five backbones are alternatives; your team prescribes one.

Does nivolumab continue after surgery?

Not automatically under this NCCP regimen. It covers at most three cycles before surgery; any later treatment needs a separate plan.

What if a dose is delayed?

The team reviews blood tests, symptoms and recovery. Do not assume a delay means treatment has failed or that surgery must occur on a fixed date.

Try explaining the plan back: Which medicines are you having now? What changes after induction? What would make you call today? Ask your team to fill any gaps.
