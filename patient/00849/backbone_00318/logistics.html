Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm blood tests, transport, medicine instructions and the oncology contact number.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Nivolumab is infused over 30 minutes before chemotherapy. Pemetrexed is infused over 10 minutes. Carboplatin is infused over 30 minutes. Blood-result review, preparation, support medicines and observation add to the visit.

STEP 3

Access

Treatment uses suitable IV access. No routine take-home infusion pump is used.

STEP 4

Home support

Take the prescribed folic acid, vitamin B12 and rash-prevention steroid plan; continue folic acid for 21 days after the final pemetrexed dose. Carboplatin dosing uses a verified kidney-function measurement; report dehydration or a change in health.

STEP 5

Surgical review

Keep the oncology and surgical teams informed about new symptoms and other appointments.
