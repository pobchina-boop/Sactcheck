Nivolumab, pemetrexed and carboplatin before surgery

This treatment is given before planned surgery for resectable non-small-cell lung cancer at high risk of returning. It aims to treat the cancer before the operation and reduce the chance of recurrence. Surgery and benefit are not guaranteed.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Up to three 21-day cycles. Day 1: nivolumab, pemetrexed and carboplatin. Then surgical multidisciplinary team (MDT) reassessment. The five listed backbones are alternatives; this guide covers only your selected combination.

Tests and checks

Blood count, kidneys, liver and glucose before each cycle; thyroid tests every 3–6 weeks; cortisol if indicated. Assess symptoms before every dose.

Between treatment visits

Relative cycle days, not guaranteed symptom or recovery dates.

Contact your oncology team urgently for fever, feeling suddenly unwell, new breathlessness, chest pain, persistent diarrhoea, severe weakness, confusion, reduced urine or a severe rash. Follow your local emergency instructions; do not wait for the next appointment.
