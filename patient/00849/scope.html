NCCP 00849 · Before surgery

Choose the chemotherapy prescribed with nivolumab.

These are five alternative treatment plans. You receive the plan selected by your team, not all five. Check the medicine names on your prescription or ask your team before choosing.

This pathway covers up to three neoadjuvant cycles followed by surgical review. It does not prescribe nivolumab after surgery.

Nivolumab, paclitaxel and carboplatin before surgery

Up to three 21-day cycles. Day 1: nivolumab, paclitaxel and carboplatin. Then surgical MDT reassessment. The five listed backbones are alternatives; this guide covers only your selected combination.
