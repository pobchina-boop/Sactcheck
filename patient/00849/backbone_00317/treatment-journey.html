Nivolumab, pemetrexed and cisplatin before surgery: treatment and checks

Neoadjuvant NSCLC · selected pemetrexed / cisplatin backbone

Up to three 21-day cycles. Day 1: nivolumab, pemetrexed and cisplatin. Then surgical MDT reassessment. The five listed backbones are alternatives; this guide covers only your selected combination.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.
