1. Treatment and purpose

Nivolumab every 4 weeks for head and neck cancer · NCCP 00484 v13a

Nivolumab is given as 480 mg through a vein over 60 minutes OR 1200 mg as an injection under the skin over 3–5 minutes, on Day 1 every 4 weeks. Your team selects one formulation; these doses are not interchangeable milligram for milligram. Allow extra time for safety checks, preparation and observation. Treatment continues until the cancer progresses or side effects become unacceptable, with individual benefit and safety review. There is no fixed number of cycles in this head and neck treatment course.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Nivolumab · Immunotherapy

Report new breathing, bowel, skin, hormone, heart or nerve symptoms promptly; these may be immune inflammation.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss your diagnosis, intent, alternatives, expected benefit and uncertainty, treatment burden, serious or lasting harms, fertility and pregnancy precautions and your priorities. Include immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver, kidneys, skin, glands, heart, muscles, nerves and eyes; infusion or injection reactions; delayed effects; possible high-dose steroid treatment, permanent hormone replacement or permanent treatment stopping. Confirm actual urgent contacts and use teach-back. This draft does not authorise treatment.
