Active medicines: nivolumab

Emetogenic risk: Minimal

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Minimal emetic risk; no routine antiemetic prophylaxis or premedication. Prescribe rescue/support individually.
  • Immune alert card and immediate symptom reporting.
  • Blood count, kidney/liver tests and glucose before every 4-week cycle; thyroid once a month; cortisol if clinically indicated. Review cancer progression before cycle 4. Continue immune-toxicity monitoring for at least 5 months after the last dose.
  • Effective contraception during treatment and at least 5 months afterwards; specialist breastfeeding discussion.
  • Current NCIS medical-oncology guidance does not create a routine cytotoxic prescription for minimal-risk nivolumab monotherapy.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
