When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Nivolumab

Nivolumab blocks PD-1 on immune cells, helping them recognise and attack cancer. It can also cause inflammation in healthy organs.

Getting ready for treatment visits

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: Your treatment visit

The team checks blood results, symptoms and medicines before the prescribed infusion or injection. Tell the nurse immediately about itching, flushing, breathlessness, dizziness or feeling suddenly unwell.

What to do: Before leaving, confirm the next tests, treatment visit and urgent contact number.

Day 2–28: Between visits

You may feel well between infusions, but immune inflammation can begin at any time. Keep a short symptom record and take medicines exactly as prescribed. Contact your team about new symptoms instead of waiting for your next appointment.

What to do: Confirm monthly thyroid tests and who arranges them; report new symptoms without waiting for the next treatment.

Support at home

Sickness medicines

Nivolumab alone has minimal sickness risk. Routine preventive anti-sickness medicines or premedication are not usually required; the team may prescribe symptom relief.

Immune alert card

Carry your alert card during treatment and afterwards. Show it when seeking urgent care; symptoms can be mistaken for an infection or an ordinary stomach upset.

Hormones and steroids

Some immune effects need steroids or hormone replacement. Follow the prescribed plan and do not stop steroids suddenly. Ask for help if symptoms worsen or you cannot take essential medicines. Hormone replacement alone does not always mean nivolumab must stop. Your team checks symptoms and recovery and reviews any medicines that suppress the immune system before deciding whether treatment can continue or restart.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Discuss pregnancy, fertility and breastfeeding before treatment. Use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 5 months after the last dose. Breastfeeding requires a specialist decision; contact your team promptly if pregnancy occurs.
