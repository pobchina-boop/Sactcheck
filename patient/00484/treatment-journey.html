Nivolumab · every 4 weeks · previously treated non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC): treatment and checks

Previously treated locally advanced or metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

Nivolumab 480 mg intravenous (IV) over 60 minutes OR 1200 mg as a subcutaneous injection over 3–5 minutes on Day 1 every 4 weeks. Your team confirms one formulation; the whole visit includes checks and preparation. Continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity, with individual benefit and safety review.
