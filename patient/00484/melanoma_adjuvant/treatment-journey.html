The whole treatment journey

Nivolumab · every 4 weeks · adjuvant melanoma: treatment and checks

Completely resected melanoma with lymph-node involvement or metastatic disease; the team confirms the stage III or resected stage IV indication. Uveal melanoma is excluded from this adjuvant pathway.

Nivolumab every 4 weeks. Adjuvant treatment continues for up to 12 months, subject to safety review.

What each check is for

1Adjuvant treatment after surgeryEvery 4 weeks

Nivolumab helps immune cells act against melanoma. After complete surgery, its aim is to lower the risk of recurrence; this does not guarantee that melanoma will not return.

Ask / act: Confirm the current phase, prescription, tests and support plan.
