Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Appointments

Nivolumab 480 mg intravenous (IV) over 60 minutes OR 1200 mg as a subcutaneous injection over 3–5 minutes on Day 1 every 4 weeks. Your team confirms one formulation; the whole visit includes checks and preparation.

STEP 2

Course length

Continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity, with individual benefit and safety review.

STEP 3

Tests and review

Blood count, kidney/liver tests and glucose before every 4-week cycle; thyroid function before each cycle and when clinically indicated; cortisol if clinically indicated. Review cancer progression before cycle 4. Continue immune-toxicity monitoring for at least 5 months after the last dose.

STEP 4

Home records

Carry your immune alert card, medicine list and urgent contact details. Follow prescribed steroids or hormone replacement carefully; do not stop steroids suddenly.

STEP 5

After treatment

Keep reporting symptoms and attend agreed follow-up for at least 5 months after the final dose; some hormone replacement continues longer.
