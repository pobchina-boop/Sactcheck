The whole treatment journey

Nivolumab · every 4 weeks · advanced melanoma: treatment and checks

Unresectable or metastatic melanoma in adults; the team confirms the source-specific eligibility and previous treatment.

Nivolumab every 4 weeks. Treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity, with individual benefit and safety review.

What each check is for

1Ongoing treatment and reviewEvery 4 weeks

Nivolumab helps immune cells act against melanoma that cannot be completely removed or has spread. The aim is disease control; benefit and side effects are reviewed for you.

Ask / act: Confirm the current phase, prescription, tests and support plan.
