Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

Take letrozole once daily continuously. The prescriber confirms whether the aim is before surgery, after surgery, extended adjuvant treatment or advanced-disease control.

Take with or without food. Take a forgotten dose when remembered unless the next dose is within about two to three hours; then skip it. Do not take two doses together.

Checks and follow-up

FBC, renal and liver profile; confirm menopause with hormone tests when uncertain. Assess fracture risk and baseline bone density, particularly for adjuvant AI treatment. The NICE adjuvant baseline-DXA recommendation has an exception for patients already receiving adjuvant bisphosphonates. FBC, renal / liver profile and lipids when clinically indicated. Review bone density during treatment and after treatment according to bone risk. Neoadjuvant response and surgical planning need explicit review milestones.

The whole-treatment map distinguishes early-breast follow-up from advanced-disease response assessment.

Support between reviews

Symptoms and daily life

Tell the team if joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal dryness or sleep disturbance affects daily life. Bone thinning may cause no early symptoms, so keep the agreed bone-health reviews.

Support medicines

No routine antiemetic, steroid premedication or G-CSF is required by this endocrine monotherapy. Ask for individual symptom support.

Tests and appointments

Ask who books each check, when it is due and who reviews it. Tell the team if an expected appointment has not arrived.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
