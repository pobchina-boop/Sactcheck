What each check is for

1Confirm the planBefore starting or changing treatment

Letrozole is used for selected postmenopausal hormone-sensitive breast cancer before surgery, after surgery, as extended treatment or for advanced disease. Before-surgery treatment also depends on HER2 results, whether chemotherapy is suitable and whether surgery should happen immediately; the team checks these details.

Ask / act: Confirm treatment setting, medicines, menopause and the planned review.

Sources: NCCP 00371

2Checks and symptomsAt the agreed review points

FBC, renal and liver profile; confirm menopause with hormone tests when uncertain. Assess fracture risk and baseline bone density, particularly for adjuvant AI treatment. The NICE adjuvant baseline-DXA recommendation has an exception for patients already receiving adjuvant bisphosphonates. FBC, renal / liver profile and lipids when clinically indicated. Review bone density during treatment and after treatment according to bone risk. Neoadjuvant response and surgical planning need explicit review milestones.

Ask / act: Agree who books tests, reviews results and responds to new symptoms.

Sources: NCCP 00371

3Review ongoing benefitAt setting-specific decision points

After surgery, letrozole can reduce the risk of hormone-sensitive breast cancer returning. Before surgery, it can be used in a selected treatment plan to shrink the cancer and prepare for an operation. In advanced disease, the aim is cancer and symptom control. Each setting has its own review and duration plan.

Ask / act: Review duration, alternatives and patient priorities; do not stop or extend treatment automatically.

Sources: NCCP 00371
