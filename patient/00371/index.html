Know the plan before your next visit.

Letrozole · daily hormone therapy

After surgery, letrozole can reduce the risk of hormone-sensitive breast cancer returning. Before surgery, it can be used in a selected treatment plan to shrink the cancer and prepare for an operation. In advanced disease, the aim is cancer and symptom control. Each setting has its own review and duration plan.

My schedule at a glance

Your treatment pattern

Continuous daily tablets

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Take letrozole once daily continuously. The prescriber confirms whether the aim is before surgery, after surgery, extended adjuvant treatment or advanced-disease control.

Tests and checks

full blood count (FBC), renal and liver profile; confirm menopause with hormone tests when uncertain. Assess fracture risk and baseline bone density, particularly for adjuvant aromatase inhibitor (AI) treatment. The NICE adjuvant baseline-dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (bone density) (DXA) recommendation has an exception for patients already receiving adjuvant bisphosphonates. FBC, renal / liver profile and lipids when clinically indicated. Review bone density during treatment and after treatment according to bone risk. Neoadjuvant response and surgical planning need explicit review milestones.
