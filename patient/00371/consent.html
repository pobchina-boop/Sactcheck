NCCP 00371 · version 4

Letrozole · daily hormone therapy

Take letrozole once daily continuously. The prescriber confirms whether the aim is before surgery, after surgery, extended adjuvant treatment or advanced-disease control.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

After early breast cancer treatment, endocrine therapy reduces the risk of recurrence. In advanced disease, the aim is to control cancer and symptoms. Your setting determines the duration and follow-up. Postmenopausal hormone-sensitive breast cancer in the specific NCCP early, extended, advanced or neoadjuvant indications. Neoadjuvant use has additional HER2-negative and treatment-suitability criteria; it is not inferred for every ER-positive cancer.

Material risks and support

  • Joint and muscle pain or stiffness
  • Hot flushes, sleep disturbance and fatigue
  • Vaginal dryness and sexual symptoms
  • Bone thinning and fractures
  • Lipid / cardiovascular risk changes
  • Uncommon liver abnormalities or serious allergic reactions

NCCP Child-Pugh C dosing is 2.5 mg every other day; this is not the same hepatic advice as anastrozole. The daily schedule shown is the usual dose and must be checked against the prescription. AIs alone require a confirmed postmenopausal endocrine state. Ovarian suppression is a separate treatment plan and must not be assumed from this card.

Monitoring and understanding

FBC, renal and liver profile; confirm menopause with hormone tests when uncertain. Assess fracture risk and baseline bone density, particularly for adjuvant AI treatment. The NICE adjuvant baseline-DXA recommendation has an exception for patients already receiving adjuvant bisphosphonates. FBC, renal / liver profile and lipids when clinically indicated. Review bone density during treatment and after treatment according to bone risk. Neoadjuvant response and surgical planning need explicit review milestones.
