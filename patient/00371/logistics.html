NCCP 00371 · version 4

Letrozole · daily hormone therapy

Take letrozole once daily continuously. The prescriber confirms whether the aim is before surgery, after surgery, extended adjuvant treatment or advanced-disease control.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Take with or without food. Take a forgotten dose when remembered unless the next dose is within about two to three hours; then skip it. Do not take two doses together.

Check the prescribed frequency

The usual schedule is daily. In severe hepatic impairment NCCP specifies every other day; follow the individual prescription and do not make this change yourself.

If treatment is before surgery

Ask when response will be assessed, which team owns the surgical review and what would lead to earlier surgery or a different treatment.

After surgery or previous tamoxifen

Confirm whether this is the initial adjuvant course or an extension. Record the planned review date and previous endocrine-treatment duration.

Support during treatment

Keep the fracture-risk and DXA plan, discuss activity and joint symptoms, and check all medicines and supplements with the treating team.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
