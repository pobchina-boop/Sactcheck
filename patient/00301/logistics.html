Preparing for treatment visits

Five-day EP for good-risk metastatic germ-cell tumours

Planning your visits

Your appointments

Both medicines on days 1–5 of a 21-day cycle. Four cycles are planned if treatment remains suitable.

Taking treatment

Each treatment day starts with etoposide into a vein over about one hour. You also receive fluid before cisplatin, which runs over about one hour. Allow extra time for blood tests, hydration and review.

Planning the next step

Days 6–21 have no scheduled EP infusions. Your blood count can still fall. The next cycle starts only after review.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills, unexpected bleeding or sudden illness, including during the treatment break. Sudden chest pain, collapse or severe breathlessness needs emergency help. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
