Day 1 · begin five-day EP

Bring your medicines list. The team checks your treatment plan and dates one anti-sickness course for the whole block.

Etoposide

Tell the nurse at once if you feel faint, hot or breathless during the infusion.

Cisplatin

Report ringing ears, trouble hearing, tingling, reduced urine or vomiting that prevents drinking.

Fluids and nausea

Kidney-protecting fluid is given before cisplatin. Follow your dated anti-sickness plan and call if vomiting prevents drinking.

Gaps between treatment visits

Keep your 24-hour oncology number and treatment card with you. Report fever or sudden illness straight away, including at night and during a break.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills, unexpected bleeding or sudden illness, including during the treatment break. Sudden chest pain, collapse or severe breathlessness needs emergency help. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
