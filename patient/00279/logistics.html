Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Confirm your plan

Reference IV plan: etoposide first on Days 1–3, cisplatin after etoposide on Day 1, each infusion about one hour plus checks and fluids. If prescribed, Days 2–3 etoposide may instead be capsules twice daily. Confirm route, platinum, interval and hydration before treatment; never combine alternatives yourself.

STEP 2

Keep the contact card

The team supplies the daytime and out-of-hours numbers. Keep the card with you and show it when seeking medical care.

STEP 3

Two linked treatment teams

Confirm which team to contact for radiation or chemotherapy changes. Report swallowing problems early and ask about transport, nutrition and fatigue support.
