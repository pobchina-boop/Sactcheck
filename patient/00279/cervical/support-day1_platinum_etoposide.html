When to call

Call the 24-hour oncology number for fever, chills, unexplained bleeding, very little urine, uncontrolled vomiting or diarrhoea. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, breathlessness or faintness during an infusion.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology number for fever, chills, unexplained bleeding, very little urine, uncontrolled vomiting or diarrhoea. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, breathlessness or faintness during an infusion. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999.
