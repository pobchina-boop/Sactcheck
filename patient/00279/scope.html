This pack describes the reference IV cisplatin/etoposide21-day schedule for limited-stage lung cancer. Oral etoposide, split cisplatin, carboplatin substitution or a28-day interval need personalised treatment information from your team. They are not interchangeable schedules.

Cisplatin Day1 + IV etoposide Days1–3, every21days

Reference plan: four 21-day cycles. Day 1: cisplatin and etoposide. Days 2–3: etoposide. Radiotherapy usually starts with cycle 1; its appointments are planned separately. Capsules, split cisplatin, a 28-day interval or a different platinum are used only if explicitly prescribed.
