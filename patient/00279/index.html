When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Cisplatin

Cisplatin forms links in deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA) that interfere with cell copying. Fluids and kidney checks help reduce harm to healthy tissue.

Etoposide

Etoposide blocks an enzyme cells need while copying DNA. The route and prescribed amount differ between infusion and capsules; they are not interchangeable milligram for milligram.

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: Day 1: reference combination

Blood results, symptoms, anti-sickness medicines and hydration are checked before both drugs.

What to do: Tell the nurse about any hearing, nerve or fluid-intake problems.

Day 2–3: Days 2–3: etoposide

IV treatment each day or prescribed capsules twice daily. The split-cisplatin alternative changes these visits.

What to do: Use your written prescription; do not add IV and oral etoposide together.

Day 4–7: Support and recovery

No routine chemotherapy in the reference plan. Follow ongoing anti-sickness medicines and separate RT appointments.

What to do: Call early for poor intake, vomiting, swallowing pain or fever.

Day 8–21: Between chemotherapy cycles

Fatigue, mouth symptoms and infection risk can persist. RT may continue according to its own plan.

What to do: Report symptoms when they occur; do not wait for the next blood test.

Support at home

Fluids and sickness prevention

Cisplatin needs a planned fluid and anti-sickness course. Follow the labelled medicines for each treatment day and the days afterwards. Call if vomiting prevents drinking or taking medicines.

If capsules are prescribed

Etoposide capsules on Days 2–3 replace the IV doses on those days. Take the prescribed amount twice daily on an empty stomach. Follow pharmacy handling instructions and contact the team if a dose is missed or vomited; do not take an extra dose without advice.

Radiotherapy support

Ask about swallowing, eating, skin care and fatigue during radiation. Painful swallowing or poor intake needs early help; inability to drink needs urgent assessment.
