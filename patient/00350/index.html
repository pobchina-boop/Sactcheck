intravenous (IV) pertuzumab and IV trastuzumab every 21 days. Before surgery: 3–6 cycles alongside the selected chemotherapy. After surgery: up to 18 cycles overall under the agreed adjuvant plan. If an anthracycline is used first, finish it before starting these antibodies with the taxane phase. Confirm the exact companion regimen and local access.
