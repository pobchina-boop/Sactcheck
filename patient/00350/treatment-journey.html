The whole treatment journey

Pertuzumab + trastuzumab · with selected chemotherapy: the whole pathway

HER2-positive early breast cancer · neoadjuvant or adjuvant framework

IV pertuzumab and IV trastuzumab every 21 days. Before surgery: 3–6 cycles alongside the selected chemotherapy. After surgery: up to 18 cycles overall under the agreed adjuvant plan. If an anthracycline is used first, finish it before starting these antibodies with the taxane phase. Confirm the exact companion regimen and local access.

What each check is for

1Blood testsFBC, kidney and liver profile at baseline and before every 21-day cycle. The team reviews the necessary results before a planned dose. The selected chemotherapy can require extra or more frequent checks.

Check safety before the next dose.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP

2Heart testsCheck heart pumping function before starting HER2 treatment. Repeat echo or MUGA every 12 weeks: around cycle 5, then cycle 9 if 21-day cycles stay on time. Earlier checks are needed for symptoms or abnormal findings. Count elapsed time from the first HER2 dose; changing phase or route does not restart the clock.

Monitor pumping function and relevant rhythm risk.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP · HERCEPTIN · ESC

3Cancer imagingBaseline breast and nodal imaging, with staging tests appropriate to the diagnosis. Review response during preoperative treatment and before surgery. After local treatment, arrange breast surveillance; routine CT, PET or tumour-marker testing is not the usual screening plan for someone without symptoms.

Assess response or follow-up needs.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: EARLY

4Bone healthIf the cancer is also hormone-receptor positive, endocrine treatment may form part of the wider plan. Bone-density testing is linked to aromatase inhibitors, ovarian suppression and fracture risk; it is not automatically required because a HER2 medicine is prescribed. Agree baseline DEXA where indicated and individual follow-up.

Assess fracture risk where relevant.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: BONE
