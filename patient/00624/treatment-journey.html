Your treatment journey

Liposomal doxorubicin and carboplatin

The aim is to control recurrent cancer and review how it responds.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1

Both medicines every four weeks, usually for six cycles. Extra cycles may be considered if the cancer shrinks or stays stable.

Your tests and checks
