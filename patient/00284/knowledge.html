When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Call oncology immediately for fever or sudden illness, breathing difficulty, chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, bleeding, very little urine, severe headache or confusion, or a spreading/blistering rash. Tell the nurse about infusion-site pain.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

Understand your treatment

Your medicines

Gemcitabine aims to control lung cancer and its symptoms. Treatment is reviewed against side effects, your health and your priorities; benefit is not guaranteed.

Chemotherapy

Gemcitabine

Antimetabolite chemotherapy
Interferes with DNA building

Gemcitabine interferes with making DNA, which dividing cancer cells need. Healthy blood-forming cells and other tissues can also be affected.

DNA building↓Replication disrupted↓Dividing cells affected

What to watch for
Report fever, unusual bleeding, breathlessness, rash, swelling or reduced urine promptly.

Make the plan practical

Support around the medicines

Sickness medicines

Gemcitabine has low sickness risk. Follow the prescribed preventive or rescue medicine plan; do not take a platinum anti-sickness course for gemcitabine alone.

Feeling unwell

Do not dismiss fever as a flu-like drug effect. Call the oncology team immediately for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness.

Medicines and fluids

Follow your individual drinking advice. Report reduced urine, swelling, breathlessness or trouble drinking, and ask before new medicines or supplements.

Sources: nccp · ncis

Understanding the evidence

Gemcitabine has been studied in older adults with advanced lung cancer.

Small historical single-arm older-adult study cannot establish a comparative survival benefit or predict an individual outcome.

Sources: ricci-2000

Prepare for the conversation

Questions worth asking

Why are there three visits?

The same medicine is planned on Days 1, 8 and 15, followed by a week without a planned dose. Each visit has its own safety checks.

Is treatment always six cycles?

No. That fixed duration belongs to the separate pancreatic adjuvant indication. Your lung treatment continues only while appropriate.

When will I have scans?

Your consultant agrees timing from your disease and treatment goals; this regimen does not mandate one interval.
