This patient pack describes lung treatment only. The pancreatic adjuvant six-cycle plan is different and is not covered here.

Lung cancer

Gemcitabine is given on Days 1, 8 and 15. Week 4 has no planned dose. Each cycle lasts 28 days. For lung cancer there is no fixed six-cycle limit in this source: continue only while the team considers treatment helpful and tolerable.
