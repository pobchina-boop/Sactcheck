Gemcitabine alone · non-small-cell lung cancer

Gemcitabine aims to control lung cancer and its symptoms. Treatment is reviewed against side effects, your health and your priorities; benefit is not guaranteed.

This guide covers the lung indication only. The same NCCP number also covers pancreatic treatment after surgery with a separate six-cycle plan. Use the correct disease-specific plan.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

My treatment

My overall treatment

Gemcitabine is given on Days 1, 8 and 15. Week 4 has no planned dose. Each cycle lasts 28 days. For lung cancer there is no fixed six-cycle limit in this source: continue only while the team considers treatment helpful and tolerable.

  1. TreatmentDays 1, 8, 15; repeat every 28 days. Duration agreed with your team.
  2. ReviewYour team reviews benefit and side effects, then agrees whether to continue, pause or change treatment.

More about my medicines

Gemcitabine

Gemcitabine interferes with making DNA, which dividing cancer cells need. Healthy blood-forming cells and other tissues can also be affected.

Support at home

Sickness medicines

Gemcitabine has low sickness risk. Follow the prescribed preventive or rescue medicine plan; do not take a platinum anti-sickness course for gemcitabine alone.

Feeling unwell

Do not dismiss fever as a flu-like drug effect. Call the oncology team immediately for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness.

Medicines and fluids

Follow your individual drinking advice. Report reduced urine, swelling, breathlessness or trouble drinking, and ask before new medicines or supplements.
