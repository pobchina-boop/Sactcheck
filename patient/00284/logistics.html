Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before each visit

Bring your medicine list and report illness. The team reviews blood results before deciding the dose.

STEP 2

Three treatment visits

Day 1,Day 8 and Day 15 are planned infusion visits. Week 4 has no routine dose.

STEP 3

At home

Keep the prescribed support medicines and urgent contact plan available. Do not make up a missed dose yourself.
