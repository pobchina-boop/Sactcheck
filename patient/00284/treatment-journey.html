The whole treatment journey

Gemcitabine alone · non-small-cell lung cancer: treatment and checks

Locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC in older adults or people with ECOG2

Gemcitabine is given on Days 1, 8 and 15. Week 4 has no planned dose. Each cycle lasts 28 days. For lung cancer there is no fixed six-cycle limit in this source: continue only while the team considers treatment helpful and tolerable.

What each check is for

1PrepareBefore first dose

Gemcitabine aims to control lung cancer and its symptoms. Treatment is reviewed against side effects, your health and your priorities; benefit is not guaranteed.

Ask / act: Confirm aim, prescription, tests and contacts.

Sources: NCCP 00284

2ReviewBefore each dose

Check treatment remains appropriate

Ask / act: Report symptoms and confirm blood results before dosing.

Sources: NCCP 00284

Assess benefit and recovery
