The whole treatment journey

Nivolumab and ipilimumab for advanced melanoma: treatment and checks

NCCP 00431a: advanced unresectable or metastatic melanoma in adults. This patient guide is the melanoma context, not the separate liver-cancer indication.

The combination phase gives nivolumab followed by ipilimumab by IV infusion every three weeks for up to four doses. If appropriate, nivolumab alone then continues on the team-selected two-weekly or four-weekly schedule, by the prescribed IV or subcutaneous route. After the last combination dose, the first two-weekly maintenance dose starts three weeks later; the first four-weekly maintenance dose starts six weeks later in this melanoma schedule. Treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity, subject to review.

What each check is for

1Combination inductionUp to four visits

Nivolumab and ipilimumab help immune cells recognise and attack melanoma. The combination phase is followed by nivolumab alone when appropriate. The aim in this setting is to control advanced disease; benefit and toxicity are reviewed for the individual.

Ask / act: Confirm the current treatment phase, prescription, tests and support medicines.
