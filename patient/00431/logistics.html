Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking or receiving treatment

At each combination visit, nivolumab is given before ipilimumab, as separate IV infusions. The source schedule gives each over 30 minutes; allow extra time for assessment, preparation and observation. Maintenance may be IV or subcutaneous nivolumab with its own dose and appointment interval. The route and interval are recorded in your plan; they are not interchangeable on your own.

STEP 2

Missed, vomited or held treatment

If an appointment is missed or treatment is held, contact the unit for a new plan. Do not count a missed visit as a dose received. A move to maintenance does not erase previous immune toxicity or a permanent-stop decision.

STEP 3

Appointments and supply

Arrange dates, medicine supply, transport and any needed interpreter or carer support. Ask whether bloods are taken in the oncology unit or elsewhere and how the result reaches the prescriber.

STEP 4

Paper copies and contact information

Keep this complete handbook, the three-page summary and the wallet card together. The QR code is optional. Ask the team to add your actual contacts and print a revised plan when treatment changes.
