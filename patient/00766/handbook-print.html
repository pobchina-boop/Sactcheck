My treatment handbook

Paclitaxel, carboplatin AUC 6 and bevacizumab

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Control the cancer, relieve symptoms and delay progression.

Paclitaxel · Paclitaxel

Taxane chemotherapy. It disrupts cell division. Hair follicles, blood cells and nerves can also be affected. Report numbness and tell the nurse immediately if you flush or wheeze during treatment.

Carboplatin · Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. It damages DNA in dividing cells. Kidney function helps determine the prescribed amount. Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.

Bevacizumab · Avastin or biosimilar

Antibody targeting VEGF. It reduces signals that help tumours form blood vessels. This can also affect blood pressure, kidneys and wound healing. Call urgently for severe abdominal pain, significant bleeding, a new severe headache or sudden weakness.

What the research found

Continuing bevacizumab after chemotherapy delayed progression in the first-line study.

The final analysis did not show an overall survival improvement across the whole study population.

Your infusion schedule

First cycle after surgery

After surgery, the first cycle contains paclitaxel and carboplatin. Bevacizumab starts from cycle 2 if the wound has healed and the team considers it safe.

Combination · day 1

Paclitaxel, carboplatin AUC 6 and bevacizumab 15 mg/kg on day 1 every three weeks. Chemotherapy is given for up to six cycles.

Maintenance · bevacizumab

Bevacizumab alone every three weeks, up to 15 months (22 cycles) across treatment, or until progression or unacceptable side effects.

Common effects and what to do

Between visits

Fatigue, taste changes and nausea can affect meals and daily activities. Take the prescribed anti-sickness medicines and call if you cannot drink.

Numbness or tingling

Tell the team before your next infusion if tingling, numbness or loss of balance affects walking or using your hands.

Serious effects: when to call

Blood pressure · Bevacizumab

Blood pressure can rise without symptoms. Keep your checks. Call urgently for a severe headache or vision changes.

Bowel injury · Bevacizumab

Call urgently for severe new tummy pain, swelling or fever. A tear or abnormal connection needs immediate care.

Protein in urine · Bevacizumab

A urine test can find protein before symptoms. Report new swelling or frothy urine.

Bleeding and healing · Bevacizumab

Report wounds that reopen. Heavy bleeding, coughing blood or sudden breathlessness need urgent help.

Infection and low counts · Chemotherapy

Call the unit immediately for fever, chills, heavy bleeding or suddenly feeling unwell.

Numb hands or feet · Paclitaxel

Report tingling, difficulty fastening buttons or a change in balance before your next dose.

Hair loss · Paclitaxel

Discuss headwear or scalp cooling with the team before the first infusion.

Hearing · Carboplatin

Report ringing in your ears or a change in hearing.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call oncology urgently for fever, chills, new breathlessness or severe diarrhoea. Severe abdominal pain or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent review. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
