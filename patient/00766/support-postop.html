Support during first cycle after surgery

Paclitaxel, carboplatin AUC 6 and bevacizumab

After surgery, the first cycle contains paclitaxel and carboplatin. Bevacizumab starts from cycle 2 if the wound has healed and the team considers it safe.

Paclitaxel

Confirm which steroid premedicines to take before paclitaxel. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, wheeze or throat tightness.

Carboplatin

Kidney function helps determine carboplatin dosing. Report previous platinum allergy.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call oncology urgently for fever, chills, new breathlessness or severe diarrhoea. Severe abdominal pain or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent review. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
