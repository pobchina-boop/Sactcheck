The whole treatment journey

FOLFIRINOX followed by chemoradiotherapy · rectal cancer: treatment and checks

Locally advanced rectal cancer

Phase 1: six FOLFIRINOX cycles, one every 14 days, each with a 46-hour fluorouracil pump. Phase 2: capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–5 of each seven-day week for five weeks, with radiotherapy. Your team supplies the separate radiation calendar.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.
