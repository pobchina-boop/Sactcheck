Capecitabine with radiotherapy: separate five-week phase

Download this phase PDF

Active medicines: Capecitabine + Radiotherapy

Emetogenic risk: Oral minimal-to-low; radiation risk assessed separately

Use oral-SACT antiemetics as required under local policy. The radiation team assesses radiation-related emesis separately. The induction aprepitant, dexamethasone, olanzapine and atropine schedule does not continue automatically into this phase.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Confirm the capecitabine prescription and radiation-day calendar: planned Days 1–5 each week for five weeks; no catch-up doses without instructions.
  • Capecitabine is taken within 30 minutes after food. Use the written missed-dose, vomiting and diarrhoea plan.
  • Confirm weekly blood tests and bowel, skin, hydration and radiation review. Stop capecitabine and contact the team for significant toxicity as instructed.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
