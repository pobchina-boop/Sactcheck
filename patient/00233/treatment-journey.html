Tests in your plan

Baseline bloods and tests

WhenFull blood count, kidney and liver tests and blood pressure; ECG if at risk of a heart-rhythm change; INR if taking warfarin. Review seizures, falls, cognition, bone and cardiac health and interactions.

Checks organ safety and suitability before treatment.

Treatment safety monitoring

WhenBlood count, kidney and liver tests every 4 weeks as clinically indicated. Blood pressure, ECG and creatinine as clinically indicated. If taking warfarin, INR weekly until a stable dose is established.

Finds changes that may need supportive care, a dose hold or adjustment.

Specialist and cancer tests

If part of your plan
WhenPSA and testosterone help assess cancer activity and hormone suppression. Scans distinguish disease not seen on conventional imaging from metastatic disease; the treatment setting must be recorded.

Distinguishes treatment selection, hormone monitoring and cancer response.

The whole treatment journey

Enzalutamide: castration-resistant prostate cancer: treatment and checks

NCCP-listed metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer before or after docetaxel, or high-risk nonmetastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer. Your team records your exact setting.

Enzalutamide 160 mg once daily, continuously while helping and tolerated. Continue ADT unless surgically castrated. A lower dose may be prescribed for toxicity or interactions.
