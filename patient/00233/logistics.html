Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests and blood pressure; electrocardiogram (ECG) if at risk of a heart-rhythm change; international normalised ratio (blood-clotting test) (INR) if taking warfarin. Review seizures, falls, cognition, bone and cardiac health and interactions.

STEP 2

Taking or receiving treatment

Take at the same time daily with or without food. Swallow the prescribed capsules or tablets whole with water; do not open, chew or crush them. If missed, take as soon as remembered that day. If the entire day is missed, resume the usual dose the following day; do not double it. Ask pharmacy if swallowing is difficult.

STEP 3

Tests and reviews

Blood count, kidney and liver tests every 4 weeks as clinically indicated. Blood pressure, ECG and creatinine as clinically indicated. If taking warfarin, INR weekly until a stable dose is established.

STEP 4

Specific practical instructions

Report falls, new memory or concentration problems and troublesome fatigue. Seizure, severe headache with confusion or vision changes needs urgent help. Discuss driving safety and every new medicine with the team; interactions can change anticoagulant effects.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next appointment, dose, support medicines and verified daytime and out-of-hours phone numbers.
