Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

The usual adult starting dose is 600 mg by mouth once daily, every day without a planned rest week. Use your prescribed dose. Treatment continues while helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of cycles.

Take whole capsules with or without food; do not crush or chew. Avoid grapefruit, grapefruit products and Seville oranges. If a dose is missed, take it unless the next dose is due within 12 hours; confirm the exact boundary with your pharmacist. The leaflet permits repeating whole capsules after immediate vomiting: follow your written plan and call if uncertain. Suspension or tube administration requires separate pharmacy instructions.

Checks and follow-up

Blood count, kidney/liver tests, uric acid and ECG/electrolytes before starting. Repeat ECG/electrolytes after one month, then periodically as indicated; regular blood tests and symptom reviews. Heart-pumping tests depend on symptoms/risk. Scans follow your Consultant’s plan. Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.

Support between reviews

Symptom medicines

NCCP rates entrectinib minimal-to-low oral emetic risk. Agree individual rescue treatment and check heart-rhythm interactions; no routine infusion premedication is specified.

Daily life and medicines

Keep the medicine list current. Avoid driving or machinery while dizzy, faint, visually affected or cognitively affected. Review falls risk and report bone pain.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Use the agreed contraception plan during and after treatment. Do not breastfeed during treatment; ask about fertility and family plans before starting.
