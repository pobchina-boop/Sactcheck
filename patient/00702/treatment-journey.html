Adult treatment scope · NCCP 00702a / 00702b This adult suite covers entrectinib alone for ROS1-positive advanced NSCLC before any ROS1 inhibitor (00702a), or a separate NTRK gene-fusion solid-tumour indication with no previous NTRK inhibitor and no satisfactory treatment options (00702b). Molecular tests and Irish access criteria must be confirmed. The adult 600 mg schedule does not apply to children.
NCCP 00702 · version 2

Entrectinib monotherapy · adult

The usual adult starting dose is 600 mg by mouth once daily, every day without a planned rest week. Use your prescribed dose. Treatment continues while helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of cycles.
