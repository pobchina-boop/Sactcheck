NCCP 00702 · version 2

Entrectinib monotherapy · adult

The usual adult starting dose is 600 mg by mouth once daily, every day without a planned rest week. Use your prescribed dose. Treatment continues while helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of cycles.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Entrectinib aims to control a cancer with a matching gene fusion. It may help shrink or slow cancer, including some cancer in the brain. Your team explains the treatment aim, uncertainty and alternatives for your exact setting. This adult suite covers entrectinib alone for ROS1-positive advanced NSCLC before any ROS1 inhibitor (00702a), or a separate NTRK gene-fusion solid-tumour indication with no previous NTRK inhibitor and no satisfactory treatment options (00702b). Molecular tests and Irish access criteria must be confirmed. The adult 600 mg schedule does not apply to children.

Material risks and support

  • Dizziness, altered thinking or memory
  • Heart failure and heart-rhythm changes
  • Taste change, nausea, constipation or diarrhoea
  • Swelling, weight gain and tiredness
  • Liver injury and low blood counts
  • Raised uric acid and kidney-test changes
  • Visual disturbance, nerve symptoms and fractures
  • Potential harm in pregnancy

Discuss the exact ROS1 or NTRK indication, alternatives including not proceeding, expected benefit and uncertainty, cognitive/heart/liver risks, fractures, daily capsules, interactions, monitoring and reproductive precautions.

Monitoring and understanding

Blood count, kidney/liver tests, uric acid and ECG/electrolytes before starting. Repeat ECG/electrolytes after one month, then periodically as indicated; regular blood tests and symptom reviews. Heart-pumping tests depend on symptoms/risk. Scans follow your Consultant’s plan. Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.
