Adult treatment scope · NCCP 00702a / 00702b This adult suite covers entrectinib alone for ROS1-positive advanced NSCLC before any ROS1 inhibitor (00702a), or a separate NTRK gene-fusion solid-tumour indication with no previous NTRK inhibitor and no satisfactory treatment options (00702b). Molecular tests and Irish access criteria must be confirmed. The adult 600 mg schedule does not apply to children.
NCCP 00702 · version 2

Entrectinib monotherapy · adult

The usual adult starting dose is 600 mg by mouth once daily, every day without a planned rest week. Use your prescribed dose. Treatment continues while helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of cycles.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Take whole capsules with or without food; do not crush or chew. Avoid grapefruit, grapefruit products and Seville oranges. If a dose is missed, take it unless the next dose is due within 12 hours; confirm the exact boundary with your pharmacist. The leaflet permits repeating whole capsules after immediate vomiting: follow your written plan and call if uncertain. Suspension or tube administration requires separate pharmacy instructions.

Start with a clear plan

Confirm the gene fusion, indication, dose, supply route, baseline tests, next review and urgent contact numbers.

Whole capsules or another method

Swallow whole capsules. If swallowing is difficult, contact pharmacy: current product information allows a specifically prepared suspension, while the older NCCP text says not to open capsules. Do not improvise.

Supply and appointments

Arrange repeat prescriptions before capsules run out. Oral treatment needs blood tests and heart reviews but no routine infusion line or pump.

If a dose is missed or vomited

Use the written missed-dose and immediate-vomiting instructions for your formulation. Ask the team if timing is unclear; suspension vomiting needs professional advice.

If treatment changes

Ask what must recover, who authorises restarting and when the next review is due. Continue follow-up and contraception advice after stopping.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
