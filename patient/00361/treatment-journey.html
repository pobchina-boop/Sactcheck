What each check is for

1Bloods and symptomsBaseline FBC, renal and liver profile; later blood tests are clinically indicated, not automatic chemotherapy-cycle tests. At injection visits, review bleeding, bruising and injection / nerve symptoms.

Checks treatment safety.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking owner and result reviewer. Report new symptoms between appointments.

Sources: NCCP 00361

2Heart monitoringThere is no routine ECG or echo schedule for fulvestrant monotherapy. Monitoring required by a separately prescribed companion drug must be added from that regimen.

ECG and echo answer different questions.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking owner and result reviewer. Report new symptoms between appointments.

Sources: NCCP 00361 · SMPC

3Bone healthAssess personal fracture and osteoporosis risk. Fulvestrant does not carry an automatic AI DXA timetable; agree testing and repeat intervals when indicated.

Checks fracture risk and whether treatment is needed.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking owner and result reviewer. Report new symptoms between appointments.

Sources: NICE_NG101

4Cancer scansAdvanced: response assessment generally every 2–4 months, adapted to disease pace and symptoms; less often may suit indolent disease. The team selects imaging, not automatically CT chest/abdomen/pelvis. New symptoms can bring assessment forward.

The treatment setting determines surveillance or response assessment.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking owner and result reviewer. Report new symptoms between appointments.

Sources: ESMO_EBC2024 · ESMO_MBC2023

5MarkersTumour markers may supplement imaging and clinical assessment in advanced disease if they are informative, especially if elevated at baseline. They do not replace scans, and a marker change alone should not determine treatment failure. There is no universal fixed marker timetable.

A marker result alone does not establish treatment failure.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking owner and result reviewer. Report new symptoms between appointments.

Sources: ABC5_MARKERS
