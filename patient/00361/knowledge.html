How this medicine supports your treatment

The aim is to control advanced hormone-sensitive breast cancer and its symptoms, with response and tolerability reviewed before continuing.

Hormone therapy · oestrogen-receptor degrader

Fulvestrant

Blocks and reduces oestrogen receptors in cancer cells
ER-positive cells use oestrogen signals↓Fulvestrant binds the receptor↓Receptors are reduced and signalling falls

Fulvestrant is an endocrine medicine given into muscle. It blocks oestrogen receptors and promotes their breakdown. It is not an intravenous chemotherapy infusion; an accompanying targeted medicine, if prescribed, has its own safety plan.

Report injection pain with numbness or radiating leg pain, extensive bruising, jaundice or worsening symptoms.

The exact treatment being discussed

The aim is to control advanced hormone-sensitive breast cancer and its symptoms, with response and tolerability reviewed before continuing.

Who this card covers

The NCCP monotherapy card covers postmenopausal ER-positive locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer with relapse or progression in its specified prior anti-oestrogen setting. Companion CDK4/6 medicines require their own regimen.

NCCP 00361 v4 · official regimen

Dose and administration

500 mg intramuscularly; individual liver-dose modification may apply. Each usual 500 mg dose is given as two 5 mL injections, one in each buttock, slowly over one to two minutes per injection. This is not an intravenous infusion; no port or pump is needed for fulvestrant itself.

NCCP 00361 v4 · official regimen

Duration and decision points

Continue while the treatment is beneficial and tolerated, with planned response reviews. There is no fixed adjuvant five-year course on this card.

NCCP 00361 v4 · official regimen

Individual safety

NCCP specifies 250 mg for Child-Pugh B, whereas EMA Faslodex product information describes no mild/moderate reduction with caution. The clinician and pharmacist must reconcile this source difference. Severe hepatic impairment is excluded.

NCCP 00361 v4 · official regimen

Checks throughout treatment

Baseline, ongoing safety checks and cancer scans have different purposes.

Before treatment

FBC, renal and liver profile. Confirm the indication, menopausal state, liver category, pregnancy exclusion and any bleeding / anticoagulant risk before intramuscular injections.

During treatment

Liver profile as clinically indicated. At injection visits review symptoms, bleeding / bruising and injection-site or nerve symptoms; response imaging follows the cancer-control plan.

Your setting changes the pathway

Choose the pathway that your team has confirmed; these are not all extra treatments you must have.

Advanced-disease scans

For advanced breast cancer, response assessment is generally every two to four months, adapted to disease pace, sites, symptoms and treatment. Indolent disease may be reviewed less often; new symptoms can prompt earlier scans. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not a universal three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

Pan-Asian adapted ESMO metastatic breast cancer guidelines

Tumour markers

Tumour markers may supplement imaging and clinical assessment in advanced disease if they are informative, especially if elevated at baseline. They do not replace scans, and a marker change alone should not determine treatment failure. There is no universal fixed marker timetable.

ESO-ESMO ABC 5 consensus: tumour-marker interpretation

Menopause, pregnancy and contraception

Where relevant, effective contraception is needed during treatment and for two years after the final dose under the EMA product information. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are excluded.

NCCP 00361 v4 · official regimen · EMA Faslodex product information

Evidence for a different decision

Each study applies to its own population and treatment setting.

FALCON: separately labelled endocrine-naive context

462 postmenopausal endocrine-therapy-naive women with HR-positive/HER 2-negative locally advanced/metastatic breast cancer. Fulvestrant 500 mg versus anastrozole 1 mg: median PFS 16.6 versus 13.8 months, HR 0.797 (95% CI 0.637–0.999). Endocrine-naive population differs from NCCP 00361's listed post-adjuvant anti-oestrogen relapse/progression indications. This trial does not broaden the card's prescribing eligibility.

FALCON: fulvestrant versus anastrozole in endocrine-naive advanced breast cancer

FALCON final overall survival

Same randomized 462-patient FALCON population; final OS 68% mature. No significant OS difference:44.8 versus 42.7 months; HR 0.97 (95% CI 0.77–1.21),p=.7579. Nonvisceral subgroup signal was not statistically significant. Do not claim a proven OS advantage. Reviewed 2025 erratum corrects author qualifications, not outcomes.

What the evidence can tell us

CONFIRM: fulvestrant 500 mg versus 250 mg: population, findings and limits

736 postmenopausal women with ER-positive locally advanced/metastatic breast cancer after prior endocrine therapy.

Median OS 26.4 versus 22.3 months; HR 0.81 (95% CI 0.69–0.96), nominalp=.02, for 500 mg versus 250 mg.

Dose-comparison trial, not evidence against current CDK 4/6 combinations. Final OS testing had no multiplicity adjustment. The 250 mg comparator is a historical standard-dose arm, not evidence to override an individual hepatic dose adjustment.

Indolent disease may be reviewed less often; new symptoms can prompt earlier scans. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not a universal three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

What is my bone-health plan?
