NCCP 00361 · version 4

Fulvestrant · injections every 4 weeks

NCCP schedule: 500 mg on Days 1 and 15 of the first 28-day cycle, then Day 1 of each 28-day cycle. This means Day 29 next, then every 28 days.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four recording sheets are reusable review periods, not four prescribed cycles. For injections, use the dates given by your team. For daily tablets, treatment continues unless your prescriber changes the plan.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Tell the team about unusual bleeding or extensive bruising, injection-site pain with numbness or pain down the leg, jaundice or worsening symptoms. Severe chest pain, breathlessness or stroke symptoms require emergency help.
