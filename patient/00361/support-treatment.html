NCCP 00361 · version 4

Fulvestrant · injections every 4 weeks

NCCP schedule: 500 mg on Days 1 and 15 of the first 28-day cycle, then Day 1 of each 28-day cycle. This means Day 29 next, then every 28 days.

Clinician supportive-care discussion draft. This is not an issued prescription.

Minimal emetogenic risk

No routine prophylactic antiemetic, corticosteroid premedication or G-CSF order is populated for this endocrine monotherapy. Review persistent nausea or other symptoms and prescribe individually if needed.

Medicine-specific support

  • Report injection pain with numbness or radiating leg pain, extensive bruising, jaundice or worsening symptoms.
  • NCCP specifies 250 mg for Child-Pugh B, whereas EMA Faslodex product information describes no mild/moderate reduction with caution. The clinician and pharmacist must reconcile this source difference. Severe hepatic impairment is excluded.
  • Assess personal fracture and osteoporosis risk. Fulvestrant does not carry an automatic AI DXA timetable; agree testing and repeat intervals when indicated.
  • Assess persistent symptoms and prescribe individually; no automatic prophylactic antiemetic, steroid or G-CSF.
