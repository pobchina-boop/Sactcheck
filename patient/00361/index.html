Know the plan before your next visit.

Fulvestrant · injections every 4 weeks

The aim is to control advanced hormone-sensitive breast cancer and its symptoms, with response and tolerability reviewed before continuing.

My schedule at a glance

Your treatment pattern

Loading visits, then every 28 days

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) schedule: 500 mg on Days 1 and 15 of the first 28-day cycle, then Day 1 of each 28-day cycle. This means Day 29 next, then every 28 days.

Tests and checks

Before treatment, check blood count, kidney and liver function, the menopausal and treatment history, pregnancy exclusion and bleeding risk. Before injections, tell staff about anticoagulants, bruising, bleeding or previous nerve symptoms. Repeat liver tests when indicated; scans follow the agreed cancer-control plan. Tell anyone ordering an oestrogen blood test that you receive fulvestrant because some tests can give a misleadingly high result.
