Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenAt baseline and before each cycle. The 20 mg/m² and 50 mg/m² regimens have different source count decisions; additional checks follow symptoms and prior results.

Check infection, anaemia and bleeding risks.

Kidney and liver tests

WhenAt baseline and before each cycle; repeat sooner when indicated by symptoms or abnormal results.

Identify organ problems and review safe drug handling.

Heart function and cumulative exposure

WhenElectrocardiogram (heart tracing) and heart pumping assessment at baseline; regular heart tracings during treatment and further pumping tests as indicated. Measure left ventricular ejection fraction, the heart pumping measurement, before each further dose taking lifetime anthracycline exposure above 450 mg/m². This threshold does not itself mean automatic treatment stopping.

Assess cardiac risk despite the liposomal formulation.

Mouth, palms and soles

WhenAssess before each cycle and report symptoms between visits. Tell the team the worst symptoms and when they occurred; the team records their severity and any treatment delay.

Identify painful ulcers or hand–foot inflammation before they become disabling.

Lactate dehydrogenase

WhenAt baseline, then only when clinically indicated; it is not a universal every-cycle tumour-marker requirement.

Provide a baseline cell-turnover blood marker in the Kaposi pathway.

Coordinate antiretroviral treatment, immune status and infection prevention.

The whole treatment journey

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin for Kaposi sarcoma: treatment and checks

acquired immunodeficiency syndrome-related Kaposi sarcoma with a CD4 helper T-cell (infection-fighting immune-cell) count below 200 cells/mm³ and extensive disease of skin or mucous membranes, or internal organs

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin 20 mg/m² is given on day 1 every 21 days. National Cancer Control Programme specifies a maximum of 6 cycles or earlier stopping for progression or unacceptable toxicity. A 14-day interval is a separately selected alternative and must be written into the personal prescription.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewPegylated liposomal doxorubicin 20 mg/m² on day 1 every 21 days

Treatment aims to reduce Kaposi disease, symptoms and complications while care for human immunodeficiency virus continues. The oncology and human immunodeficiency virus teams coordinate the plan.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999. Chest or throat tightness during infusion needs immediate staff attention. New breathlessness, fever, painful blistered hands or feet, or mouth ulcers preventing drinking needs urgent advice.

Sources: NCCP
