What this treatment is doing

Regimen-specific point
Side effects overlap. The map below separates the main treatment/agent families to help you recognise what to report; it cannot diagnose the cause of a symptom.
02 / treatment journey

How the schedule fits together

Day 1

Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² by IV infusion.

Every 21 days

Treatment repeats every 21 days if review and blood tests allow.

During treatment

Take folic acid as prescribed; vitamin B12 support and steroid rash prophylaxis are part of pemetrexed care.

Duration

For maintenance/second-line use, treatment may continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity, according to the treatment plan.

A delay, dose change or omitted medicine can be appropriate. Your team should explain what changed and why.

04 / monitoring and support

What helps make treatment safer

Before and during treatment

  • FBC, kidney and liver profile are checked before each cycle.
  • Folic acid must be taken as prescribed during therapy and after the final pemetrexed dose.
  • Vitamin B12 is given according to the regimen schedule.

Bring to each review

  • A corticosteroid is used around pemetrexed to reduce skin reactions.
  • Bring a list of current medicines and report new symptoms before the next dose.
05 / when to call

Do not wait for the next appointment

Use your oncology SOS instructionsContact oncology urgently for fever or sudden illness, new significant breathlessness, uncontrolled vomiting or diarrhoea, severe mouth soreness preventing fluids, marked reduction in urine, significant bleeding or a rapidly worsening rash. For a medical emergency call 112/999.

If your team has given you different local thresholds or emergency instructions, follow those instructions.
