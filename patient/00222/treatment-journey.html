The whole treatment journey

Pemetrexed Monotherapy: treatment and checks

Non-squamous non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) · standalone maintenance or second-line chemotherapy

One pemetrexed infusion on Day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment is planned every three weeks while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable. There is no fixed total number of cycles in this regimen. Scans, symptoms, blood results, your priorities and the oncology review guide continuation, delays, changes or stopping.

What each check is for

1Vitamin preparation or carry-forwardBefore first pemetrexed or a phase change

Vitamins lower serious pemetrexed toxicity.

Ask / act: Confirm last B12 and next due date, daily folate and one steroid prescription.

Sources: NCCP 00222

2Review each doseBefore every 21-day cycle

Counts, organ function and toxicity can change.

Ask / act: Confirm whether to continue, delay, modify or stop.

Sources: NCCP 00222

3Benefit reviewAccording to Consultant plan

There is no fixed total cycle number.

Ask / act: Confirm ongoing benefit and the next plan.

Sources: NCCP 00222

4Final dose follow-upAfter the final pemetrexed

Protection and recovery continue after the infusion.

Ask / act: Agree medicine stop dates and review persistent symptoms.

Sources: NCCP 00222
