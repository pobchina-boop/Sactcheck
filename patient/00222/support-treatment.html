NCCP 00222 · clinician draft

Pemetrexed Monotherapy

Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers pemetrexed on its own for maintenance or second-line treatment of selected non-squamous non-small-cell lung cancer. If pembrolizumab or another immunotherapy is also prescribed, use the matching combination regimen and its additional consent, monitoring and urgent advice. Pemetrexed with pembrolizumab maintenance is a different treatment plan.
Supportive prescription draft for review. This is a regimen template. It is not an issued patient prescription; individual review and completion are required.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Standalone pemetrexed is low emetogenic risk; review and replace the prior platinum support plan when that phase ends.
  • Daily folate and B12 every three cycles continue while pemetrexed continues.
  • Rash steroid is one reconciled prescription; no second antiemetic dexamethasone course is added.
  • Review renal function and NSAID/aspirin interactions against the current SmPC.
  • Routine G-CSF or antimicrobial prophylaxis is not mandated by NCCP00222; individualise after clinical risk or toxicity review.
  • Pembrolizumab/pemetrexed maintenance requires the combination regimen and separate immune safety plan.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
