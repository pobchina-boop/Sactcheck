Pemetrexed Monotherapy

To help control non-squamous non-small-cell lung cancer, as maintenance or after earlier treatment.

My overall treatment

Pemetrexed alone by drip on Day 1, repeated every 21 days. There is no preset number of cycles: your team reviews benefit and side effects.

  1. PrepareConfirm blood tests, folic acid, vitamin B12 and the steroid plan.
  2. Every 21 daysOne treatment day followed by time at home; repeat only after review.
  3. Review or finishAgree the next plan. Continue folic acid for 21 days after the final dose.

Timing may change after review. Side effects can occur on any day; there are no guaranteed safe days.

Between treatments

  • Day 2: finish prescribed steroid. Continue daily folic acid; B12 is due every three cycles.
  • Between visits: watch for infection, mouth or bowel changes and reduced urine. Timing varies; report concerns promptly.

Take folic acid daily and attend for vitamin B12; tell the team if doses were missed. Take your prescribed steroid the day before, on treatment day and the following day. Check with your team before using ibuprofen, aspirin or other medicines, and before any vaccine.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials a cycle when it is complete, after the final planned dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
