Treatment context
To help control bowel cancer that has spread, alongside the chemotherapy chosen by your oncology team.
Schedule
Bevacizumab is given by drip on Day 1 every 14 days with the prescribed fluoropyrimidine-based chemotherapy. Treatment continues while helping and tolerable; there is no fixed total number of cycles. The partner chemotherapy has its own dose days, tests and support plan.
