Before treatment

Tell the team before dental surgery or an operation. Starting or restarting bevacizumab after major surgery requires adequate healing and at least 28 days.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call now for new severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, severe headache or a change in vision. Call 112 or 999 for sudden chest pain, severe breathlessness, seizure or stroke symptoms. With chemotherapy, fever, chills or suddenly feeling unwell also need urgent assessment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
