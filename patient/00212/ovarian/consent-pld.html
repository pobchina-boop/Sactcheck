Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Bevacizumab with liposomal doxorubicin

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Treatment to help control recurrent, platinum-resistant ovarian, tubal or peritoneal cancer.

Treatment schedule. Both medicines are given on day 1 of the partner cycle. Bevacizumab is given alone on day 15.

Treatment discussion and decision

Bevacizumab with liposomal doxorubicin

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Across the three chemotherapy choices, median time before progression was 6.7 months with bevacizumab and 3.4 months with chemotherapy alone. These are pooled results. The study did not compare the three chemotherapy partners against each other or establish an overall survival advantage.

Record the selected combination, its expected benefit and alternatives. Document prior treatment reactions and any personal risks discussed.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call now for new severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, severe headache or a change in vision. Call 112 or 999 for sudden chest pain, severe breathlessness, seizure or stroke symptoms. With chemotherapy, fever, chills or suddenly feeling unwell also need urgent assessment. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
