Your treatment journey

Bevacizumab every two weeks with chemotherapy for recurrent ovarian cancer

Bevacizumab is given with chemotherapy to help control ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer that has returned and is resistant to platinum. Your team chooses one partner medicine.

Your infusion schedule

With weekly paclitaxel

Bevacizumab and paclitaxel are both due on days 1 and 15 of the 28-day partner schedule. Paclitaxel also has separate weekly visits.

With weekly topotecan

Bevacizumab is due every two weeks. Use the weekly topotecan calendar for additional appointments.

With liposomal doxorubicin

Both medicines are given on day 1 of the partner cycle. Bevacizumab is given alone on day 15.

Bevacizumab alone · PLD day 15

Day 15 of the liposomal doxorubicin cycle is a bevacizumab-only visit.

At review visits

  • What do I need to check before my next visit? Tell the team before dental surgery or an operation. Starting or restarting bevacizumab after major surgery requires adequate healing and at least 28 days.
  • When is my next treatment due? Bevacizumab every 14 days alongside weekly paclitaxel, weekly topotecan or pegylated liposomal doxorubicin. Your other medicine has its own appointment dates.
