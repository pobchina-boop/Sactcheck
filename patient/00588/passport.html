Breast adjuvant treatment only · NCCP 00588h. These materials cover olaparib after treatment for high-risk early breast cancer. Other cancers use different eligibility, duration and follow-up; use the matching NCCP indication and your treating team’s plan.
NCCP 00588 · version 5b

Olaparib · adjuvant breast treatment

Take olaparib tablets morning and evening every day. There is no planned week off. Blood-review appointments are usually every four weeks during this one-year breast course.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact the oncology team urgently for fever or feeling acutely unwell, unusual bleeding, new or worsening breathlessness/cough, severe weakness, persistent vomiting or inability to drink.
