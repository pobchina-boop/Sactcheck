Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenBefore starting, then every four weeks throughout the one-year course, with extra tests when indicated.

Check anaemia, infection risk and platelets; persistent changes may need further investigation.

Kidney and liver tests

WhenBefore starting, then every four weeks in the first 12 months; earlier for concerning symptoms/results.

Check organ function and whether the tablet prescription needs review.

Germline BRCA1/2 result

WhenValidated result before treatment; genetics discussion as appropriate.

Confirm the molecular requirement and discuss inherited/family implications.

Pregnancy testing

If part of your plan
WhenBefore treatment if you could become pregnant; repeat testing as advised.

Avoid exposing an unborn baby to olaparib.

Mammography and breast follow-up

If part of your plan
WhenIndividual follow-up; annual mammography is generally recommended for remaining breast tissue. Other tests follow symptoms or individual risk.

Surveillance of remaining breast tissue and review of new symptoms.

Heart and bone-density checks

If part of your plan
WhenAs indicated by the separate cardiac or endocrine/bone-health plan.

Monitor relevant previous treatment, endocrine therapy or existing risk; these are not a routine olaparib-only panel.

The whole treatment journey

Olaparib after treatment for early breast cancer · the whole treatment journey

Germline BRCA1/2-associated, HER2-negative high-risk early breast cancer

Take your prescribed olaparib dose morning and evening every day, without a planned rest week. This breast course lasts up to one year, or stops earlier if the cancer returns or side effects make treatment unsuitable. Prescribed endocrine therapy can continue on its own timetable.

What each check is for

1Baseline checksBefore

Confirm the germline BRCA1/2 result and high-risk HER2-negative breast indication, recovery from chemotherapy/local treatment, full blood count, kidney/liver tests, medicines and pregnancy testing where relevant.

Ask / act: Ask when this check is due, who arranges it and who reviews the result. New symptoms need advice before the next appointment.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

2Safety blood tests and symptom reviewDuring

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests every four weeks through the first 12 months; extra tests for symptoms or abnormal results. Review nausea, intake, fatigue, infection, bruising, breathing, medicines and tablet supply.

Ask / act: Ask when this check is due, who arranges it and who reviews the result. New symptoms need advice before the next appointment.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

3Cancer assessmentResponse

Continue the agreed breast follow-up and mammography plan. New symptoms need assessment. Olaparib safety blood tests are separate from surveillance; asymptomatic follow-up does not automatically require CT scans or tumour markers.

Ask / act: Ask when this check is due, who arranges it and who reviews the result. New symptoms need advice before the next appointment.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC
