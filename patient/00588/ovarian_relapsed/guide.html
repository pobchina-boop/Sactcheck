Olaparib after treatment for relapsed ovarian cancer

Help prolong cancer control after platinum chemotherapy has worked again following a relapse, where a qualifying BRCA change is confirmed.

BRCA-mutated, platinum-sensitive relapsed ovarian, fallopian-tube or primary-peritoneal cancer · NCCP 00588 v6 · Document revised 24 November 2026

My overall treatment

Olaparib tablets morning and evening, every day

28day reference

Reference window · ongoing treatment

Daily prescribed olaparibDay 1–28
  1. Daily tabletsThe usual starting dose is 300 mg of olaparib tablets twice daily, with or without food. Treatment continues while the cancer remains controlled and side effects remain acceptable. There is no automatic two-year endpoint for this relapsed-maintenance pathway; the first-line ovarian two-year rule does not apply. Follow your own prescription if the dose changes.

Between treatments

  • Symptoms may occur on any day; there is no planned rest week. Report new problems promptly.
  • Use only your prescribed sickness/rescue medicines. Call if vomiting, diarrhoea or poor intake persists; do not wait for the next review.

Confirm tablet strength, supply, written support instructions and verified daytime/out-of-hours contact numbers.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

2Mouth, taste and appetite
Olaparib

Taste changes, soreness and poor appetite may make eating difficult. Ask early for symptom and nutrition support.

4Liver and kidney tests
Olaparib

Blood tests check liver and kidney function and help the team review the dose. Report yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or right-sided upper abdominal pain. A changed creatinine result needs interpretation, not a self-directed dose change.

6Blood cells and marrow
Olaparib

Low red cells can cause tiredness or breathlessness; low white cells can increase infection risk; low platelets can cause bruising or bleeding. Persistent unexplained changes may need specialist marrow tests.

8Leg swelling and blood clots
Olaparib

A new painful swollen leg needs urgent assessment. Sudden chest pain or severe breathlessness is an emergency.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

Follow your own temperature and contact plan; do not wait for the next appointment. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, seizures or stroke symptoms: call 112/999.
