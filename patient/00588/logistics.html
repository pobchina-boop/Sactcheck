Breast adjuvant treatment only · NCCP 00588h. These materials cover olaparib after treatment for high-risk early breast cancer. Other cancers use different eligibility, duration and follow-up; use the matching NCCP indication and your treating team’s plan.
NCCP 00588 · version 5b

Olaparib · adjuvant breast treatment

Take olaparib tablets morning and evening every day. There is no planned week off. Blood-review appointments are usually every four weeks during this one-year breast course.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Olaparib tablets 300 mg orally twice daily continuously, with source renal/toxicity/interaction adjustments. Tablets are not interchangeable with capsules. Breast duration:one year or earlier recurrence/unacceptable toxicity.

After chemotherapy and local treatment

Olaparib follows the chemotherapy and local-treatment plan. In OlympiA, surgery and any indicated radiotherapy were completed before olaparib, with at least a two-week gap after local treatment. NCCP advises starting ideally within twelve weeks of the last surgery, chemotherapy or radiation; your team sets the start.

Endocrine therapy may overlap

For hormone receptor-positive disease, the prescribed hormone treatment can continue alongside olaparib and often continues after the one-year olaparib course. Its injections, tablets and bone checks keep their own schedule.

A daily routine at home

Choose consistent morning and evening times and check the strength and number of tablets on each new prescription. Keep a sufficient supply before weekends or holidays. No infusion pump or port is needed for olaparib itself.

If treatment is paused

The oncology team decides when and at what dose to restart after blood recovery or symptom improvement. Do not calculate a catch-up dose or restart automatically because a calendar month has passed.

After the planned year

Agree the stop date and ongoing breast follow-up. Endocrine treatment and breast surveillance may continue. Report persistent unexplained fatigue, infections, bruising or breathlessness even after olaparib finishes.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
