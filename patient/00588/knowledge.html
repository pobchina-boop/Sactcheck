How this medicine supports your treatment

Reduce the chance of invasive recurrence after standard chemotherapy and local treatment. It may be taken alongside prescribed endocrine therapy for hormone receptor-positive disease.

PARP inhibitor

Olaparib (Lynparza)

Disrupts DNA repair in susceptible cancer cells
BRCA-related repair is impaired↓Olaparib blocks and traps PARP↓DNA damage accumulates in cancer cells

BRCA1 and BRCA2 help cells repair breaks in DNA. Some cancers with these changes rely more heavily on other repair routes. Olaparib inhibits PARP activity and traps PARP on DNA, making it harder for those cancer cells to survive DNA damage. Normal blood-forming cells can also be affected, which is why regular blood counts matter.

Keep the four-weekly blood reviews and report persistent nausea, marked fatigue, infections, bleeding or new respiratory symptoms. Severe blood abnormalities need review even if you feel well.

The exact treatment being discussed

Reduce the chance of invasive recurrence after standard chemotherapy and local treatment. It may be taken alongside prescribed endocrine therapy for hormone receptor-positive disease.

Who this covers

Adults with a confirmed pathogenic germline BRCA1/2 mutation, HER2-negative high-risk early breast cancer and previous neoadjuvant or adjuvant chemotherapy. The high-risk definition depends on hormone receptors, prior chemotherapy setting, surgical pathology and, for one pathway, CPS+EG score.

NCCP 00588 v5b · Olaparib tablets, pages 1–7 · EMA Lynparza product information · sections 4.2–4.8 and5.1

Dose and administration

Usually 300 mg twice daily; follow the individual prescription. Swallow tablets whole with water, with or without food. Do not chew, crush, dissolve or divide them. If a dose is missed, take the next normal dose at its usual time; do not double it. Check tablet strength after any dose change.

NCCP 00588 v5b · Olaparib tablets, pages 1–7 · EMA Lynparza product information · sections 4.2–4.8 and5.1

Duration and decisions

For breast indication 00588h: up to one year, or earlier if the cancer recurs or side effects make treatment unsuitable. This is different from olaparib durations in ovarian, prostate and pancreatic cancer.

NCCP 00588 v5b · Olaparib tablets, pages 1–7 · EMA Lynparza product information · sections 4.2–4.8 and5.1

Individual safety and source differences

This patient suite is only for breast adjuvant indication 00588h. Kidney/liver impairment, previous dose reductions and interacting medicines can change the dose. New blood abnormalities can occasionally signal a serious marrow problem and must be investigated.

Your wider treatment plan

Additional treatments depend on the confirmed setting and indication.

Early-breast follow-up

After early breast cancer treatment, annual mammography is generally used for remaining breast tissue: both breasts after conservation, or the opposite breast after unilateral mastectomy. Routine mammography of the mastectomy side is not required. Routine CT, bone scans and tumour markers are not recommended solely to look for recurrence in an asymptomatic person. Safety blood tests for ongoing olaparib are a separate requirement. New symptoms need assessment between appointments.

Pan-Asian adapted ESMO early breast cancer guidelines

Tumour markers

No routine tumour-marker surveillance for asymptomatic early breast cancer.

Pan-Asian adapted ESMO early breast cancer guidelines

Pregnancy, fertility and contraception

Olaparib can harm an unborn baby. Discuss effective contraception before treatment; EMA recommends two reliable complementary methods for women during treatment and for six months afterwards. Men and their partners need contraception during treatment and for three months afterwards. Do not breastfeed during treatment or for one month after the last dose.

Evidence depends on the population

Different studies answer different questions; do not compare their results as if populations and follow-up were identical.

OlympiA: four-year survival analysis

The same 1,836-patient randomised population; median follow-up 3.5 years. Updated invasive disease-free survival; prespecified second interim overall-survival analysis. Four-year overall survival was 89.8% with olaparib versus 86.4% with placebo; OS hazard ratio0.68 (98.5%CI 0.47–0.97). Four-year invasive disease-free survival was 82.7% versus 75.4%. Absolute survival estimates are time-specific group results, not an individual prognosis; avoid mixing the four-year values with later follow-up.

OlympiA: four-year survival analysis

OlympiA: sustained benefit at six years

Updated follow-up of the same high-risk germline BRCA1/2, HER2-negative trial population. Updated invasive disease-free survival, distant disease-free survival and overall survival. Six-year invasive disease-free survival was 79.6% versus 70.3%; the publication reports an absolute difference 9.4 percentage points (95%CI 5.1–12.7), HR 0.65 (95%CI 0.53–0.78). Overall-survival HR was 0.72 (95%CI 0.56–0.93). The reported difference uses unrounded survival estimates, so it need not equal subtraction of the rounded percentages. This remains one year of treatment with later follow-up, not six years of olaparib.

Practical support

Nausea support matched to oral treatment

NCCP classifies oral olaparib as moderate-to-high emetic risk. Agree an anti-sickness plan under local policy, and seek review if oral intake falls. The SmPC describes early/intermittent symptoms; do not automatically copy an intravenous chemotherapy antiemetic cycle.

Check the antiemetic interaction

Aprepitant is a moderate CYP3A inhibitor in NCCP 00588 and can increase olaparib exposure. The prescriber/pharmacist should choose and reconcile supportive medicines; never add an antiemetic from another regimen without checking.

Blood and infection support

Anaemia or other low counts can lead to interruption, dose adjustment, transfusion or additional investigation. There is no universal routine G-CSF course for every person on this regimen.

Medicines and supplements

Review all prescriptions, over-the-counter drugs and herbal products. Strong/moderate CYP3A inhibitors or inducers can change exposure. Avoid grapefruit products and seek advice before starting anything new.

Stomach symptoms

NCCP allows consideration of a proton pump inhibitor under local policy. This is symptom- and patient-dependent, rather than a compulsory medication for everyone.

What the evidence can tell us

OlympiA: adjuvant olaparib primary analysis: population, findings and limits

1,836 patients with high-risk HER2-negative early breast cancer and germline BRCA1/2 pathogenic/likely pathogenic variants, after chemotherapy and local treatment.

Invasive disease-free survival. One year of olaparib improved invasive disease-free survival compared with placebo. The trial establishes the adjuvant sequence: local therapy first, then olaparib; endocrine therapy was permitted concurrently.

Eligibility was selected by BRCA status and high-risk pathology. Results do not establish benefit for every HER2-negative tumour, a BRCA variant of uncertain significance or concurrent radiation.

Questions to ask

When do I take or receive treatment?

Take olaparib tablets morning and evening every day. There is no planned week off. Blood-review appointments are usually every four weeks during this one-year breast course. Swallow tablets whole with water, with or without food. Do not chew, crush, dissolve or divide them. If a dose is missed, take the next normal dose at its usual time; do not double it. Check tablet strength after any dose change.

When are bloods, ECGs or echoes needed?

Which scans apply to me?

Routine mammography of the mastectomy side is not required. Routine CT, bone scans and tumour markers are not recommended solely to look for recurrence in an asymptomatic person. How long will treatment continue?

For breast indication 00588h: up to one year, or earlier if the cancer recurs or side effects make treatment unsuitable. This is different from olaparib durations in ovarian, prostate and pancreatic cancer.

What should make me call today?

Contact the oncology team urgently for fever or feeling acutely unwell, unusual bleeding, new or worsening breathlessness/cough, severe weakness, persistent vomiting or inability to drink. Chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse require emergency help.

What is my bone-health plan?

There is no olaparib-specific DXA schedule. If an aromatase inhibitor or ovarian suppression is also prescribed, retain that treatment’s separate bone-density and bone-health plan.
