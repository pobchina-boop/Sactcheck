Olaparib for prostate cancer

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00588 · Source-reconciled feasibility draft; independent clinician, pharmacy and patient review pending.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Validated BRCA1/2 result, full blood count, kidney and liver tests. Confirm recovery from previous blood toxicity and review current medicines, previous hormone treatment and ongoing testosterone suppression.

STEP 2

Dose and timing

The usual starting dose is 300 mg of olaparib tablets twice daily, with or without food, continuously while helpful and tolerable. Kidney function, interactions or side effects can change the prescribed dose. This prostate course does not use the one-year breast or two-year ovarian limit.

STEP 3

Practical care

Swallow tablets whole; do not chew, crush, dissolve or divide them. Tablets and capsules are not interchangeable. If a dose is missed, take the next normal dose at its usual time; never double up. Avoid grapefruit and Seville oranges, and check all medicines with pharmacy.

STEP 4

Support medicines

Confirm the pathogenic germline or tumour BRCA1/2 result and previous treatment. Continue prescribed androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) unless you have had surgical castration. NCCP classes oral olaparib as moderate-to-high sickness risk. The team considers anti-sickness medicine and a proton pump inhibitor for stomach protection under local policy. Obtain a written prevention/rescue plan and report persistent nausea or inability to take tablets.

STEP 5

Before leaving

Confirm your next tests, dose, review date and verified daytime/out-of-hours contact numbers.
