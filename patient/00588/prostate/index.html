28day reference

Reference window · ongoing treatment

Daily prescribed treatmentDay 1–28
  1. TreatmentThe usual starting dose is 300 mg of olaparib tablets twice daily, with or without food, continuously while helpful and tolerable. Kidney function, interactions or side effects can change the prescribed dose. This prostate course does not use the one-year breast or two-year ovarian limit.

Between treatments

  • Side effects may occur on any day, including during treatment breaks. Report problems promptly.
  • NCCP classes oral olaparib as moderate-to-high sickness risk. The team considers anti-sickness medicine and a proton pump inhibitor for stomach protection under local policy. Obtain a written prevention/rescue plan and report persistent nausea or inability to take tablets.

Confirm the pathogenic germline or tumour BRCA1/2 result and previous treatment. Continue prescribed androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) unless you have had surgical castration.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
