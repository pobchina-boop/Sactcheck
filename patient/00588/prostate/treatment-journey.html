The whole treatment journey

Olaparib for prostate cancer: treatment and checks

BRCA1/2-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer after a new hormonal agent

The usual starting dose is 300 mg of olaparib tablets twice daily, with or without food, continuously while helpful and tolerable. Kidney function, interactions or side effects can change the prescribed dose. This prostate course does not use the one-year breast or two-year ovarian limit.

What each check is for

1Treatment review300 mg tablets twice daily, continuously

Help control prostate cancer that has progressed despite hormone treatment, where a BRCA1 or BRCA2 mutation is confirmed.

Ask / act: Call your oncology team promptly for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting/diarrhoea or new breathlessness. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, seizures or stroke symptoms: call 112/999.
