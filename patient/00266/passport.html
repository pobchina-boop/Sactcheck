My treatment record

Treat the trophoblastic tumour with the aim of cure. Serial blood hCG measures how the disease responds.

My questions and next steps

  • What happens at my next visit? Day 1 is paclitaxel and cisplatin. Day 15 is paclitaxel and etoposide. One full cycle lasts 28 days.
  • Which symptoms should I phone about? Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Report new tingling or hearing changes before your next infusion. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, chest tightness or breathing difficulty.
  • What should I plan before starting? Report any numbness, hearing changes and previous infusion reactions.
  • How should I plan follow-up? After remission your specialist arranges hCG every two weeks for six months, then monthly for six months, then every two months for two years.
  • Why should I report tingling or ringing in my ears before the next visit? Paclitaxel is given at both visits. Cisplatin is given on day 1 and can affect hearing and nerves. Tell your team about ringing, changes in hearing, numb fingers or difficulty fastening buttons so they can check which medicine needs review.
