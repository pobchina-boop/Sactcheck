Support during day 1 · paclitaxel and cisplatin

TP/TE for gestational trophoblastic neoplasia

Paclitaxel runs over three hours, followed by cisplatin and kidney-protecting fluids. Anti-sickness medicines are stronger at this visit.

Paclitaxel

Report numbness between visits and tell the nurse at once about chest tightness during treatment.

Cisplatin

Attend for fluids and blood tests. Report hearing changes or reduced urine.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Report new tingling or hearing changes before your next infusion. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, chest tightness or breathing difficulty. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
