Your complete treatment reference

TP/TE for gestational trophoblastic neoplasia

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Treat the trophoblastic tumour with the aim of cure. Serial blood hCG measures how the disease responds.

Paclitaxel · Paclitaxel

Taxane. Prevents tumour cells completing cell division. Report numbness between visits and tell the nurse at once about chest tightness during treatment.

Cisplatin · Cisplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. Links strands of DNA together so tumour cells cannot divide normally. Attend for fluids and blood tests. Report hearing changes or reduced urine.

Etoposide · Etoposide

Topoisomerase inhibitor. Prevents tumour cells repairing DNA during division. Tell the nurse if you feel faint or breathless during the infusion.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · paclitaxel and cisplatin

Paclitaxel runs over three hours, followed by cisplatin and kidney-protecting fluids. Anti-sickness medicines are stronger at this visit.

Day 15 · paclitaxel and etoposide

After paclitaxel, etoposide runs over an hour. Your team checks for infusion reactions. Treatment is usually planned for two to four complete 28-day cycles, guided by hCG and tolerance.
