Your treatment journey

EOX for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and relieve symptoms. Your team will discuss benefit and side effects at review.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · EOX

Day 1 epirubicin and oxaliplatin. Take capecitabine twice daily throughout days 1–21 of each 21-day cycle. There is no planned tablet break between continuing cycles.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingCheck blood count, kidney and liver function and DPD status before starting. Review cardiac function, prior anthracycline exposure and lifetime epirubicin dose.Bring your medicine list and oncology contact details.
Before the next cycleReview blood counts, kidney function, electrolytes and liver tests before the next cycle. Ask about cold sensitivity and persistent neuropathy. Record cumulative epirubicin and investigate cardiac symptoms.Tell the team about symptoms and missed doses.
At reviewReview symptoms, nutrition, weight and treatment response.Ask when scans or surgery will be discussed.

Plan six to eight cycles while treatment is helping and tolerable.

At review visits

  • When is the next treatment? Day 1 epirubicin and oxaliplatin. Take capecitabine twice daily throughout days 1–21 of each 21-day cycle. There is no planned tablet break between continuing cycles.
  • What should I do between visits? Swallow capecitabine with water within 30 minutes of a meal. Record your doses. Leave out a missed or vomited dose and call for advice if unwell.
