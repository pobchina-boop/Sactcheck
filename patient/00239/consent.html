EOX for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and relieve symptoms. Your team will discuss benefit and side effects at review. Locally advanced or metastatic gastric carcinoma. Locally advanced or metastatic oesophageal carcinoma. Locally advanced or metastatic gastro-oesophageal junction carcinoma.

Treatment schedule. Day 1 epirubicin and oxaliplatin. Take capecitabine twice daily throughout days 1–21 of each 21-day cycle. There is no planned tablet break between continuing cycles. Plan six to eight cycles while treatment is helping and tolerable.

Treatment discussion and decision

EOX for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

In advanced disease, the pooled comparisons supported substitution of capecitabine for infused fluorouracil and oxaliplatin for cisplatin. The trial did not compare these with modern immunotherapy combinations. These results describe selected trial populations. Your outlook depends on your disease, fitness and treatment setting.

Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and relieve symptoms. Your team will discuss benefit and side effects at review. Plan six to eight cycles while treatment is helping and tolerable. Discuss alternatives, fertility, and individual risks with the treating clinician.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
