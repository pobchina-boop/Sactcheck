Your treatment reference

Nab-paclitaxel and gemcitabine for metastatic pancreatic cancer

Your treatment and how the medicines work

This first treatment for pancreatic cancer that has spread aims to slow its growth and relieve symptoms.

Nab-paclitaxel · Albumin-bound paclitaxel

Taxane chemotherapy. Paclitaxel is carried in particles bound to albumin. It stabilises the fibres needed for cell division. Your team uses this specific preparation. Tell the nurse about tingling, numbness or problems walking. Call urgently for fever, breathlessness or a widespread rash.

Gemcitabine · gemcitabine

Chemotherapy. It interferes with copying DNA as cells divide. Blood-forming cells can also be affected. Phone promptly about fever, bleeding, a new cough or difficulty breathing.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · Both infusions

The albumin-bound paclitaxel infusion takes 30 minutes. Gemcitabine follows immediately over 30 minutes. This formulation must not be interchanged with conventional paclitaxel.

Day 8 · Both infusions

Review counts and symptoms before both infusions.

Common effects and what to do

Eating and drinking

Tell the team about nausea or reduced appetite. Call if you cannot keep fluids down.

Fatigue and low counts

Rest between activities. Report fever, breathlessness or unusual bleeding promptly.

Nerve symptoms

Report numbness or loss of balance before the next visit.

Serious effects: when to call

Infection

Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately.

Urgent symptoms

Seek urgent help for chest pain, trouble breathing or collapse.

Bowel symptoms

Ring promptly for persistent diarrhoea, dehydration or severe abdominal pain.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

At home

Check your fingers and toes for numbness. Tell the team if you drop things, feel unsteady or have difficulty walking before another infusion.

Pancreatic and biliary symptoms

Ask the team about a new fever with jaundice, dark urine or shivering, especially if you have a bile duct stent.

What the research found

In metastatic pancreatic adenocarcinoma, adding nab-paclitaxel to gemcitabine improved median overall survival (8.5 versus 6.7 months).

These are group results from the named setting. They cannot predict the benefit or side effects for an individual.
