Preparing for treatment visits

Nab-paclitaxel and gemcitabine for metastatic pancreatic cancer

Planning your visits

Day unit

The albumin-bound paclitaxel infusion takes 30 minutes. Gemcitabine follows immediately over 30 minutes. This formulation must not be interchanged with conventional paclitaxel.

Between visits

Check your fingers and toes for numbness. Tell the team if you drop things, feel unsteady or have difficulty walking before another infusion.

Write down your next appointments

  • Bring your medicine list and 24-hour oncology contact details.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
