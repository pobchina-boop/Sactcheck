Your treatment journey

Nab-paclitaxel and gemcitabine for metastatic pancreatic cancer

This first treatment for pancreatic cancer that has spread aims to slow its growth and relieve symptoms.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · Both infusions

The albumin-bound paclitaxel infusion takes 30 minutes. Gemcitabine follows immediately over 30 minutes. This formulation must not be interchanged with conventional paclitaxel.

Day 8 · Both infusions

Review counts and symptoms before both infusions.

Day 15 · Both infusions

Review counts and symptoms before both infusions.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count, kidney and liver function. Confirm the pancreatic tumour diagnosis and review any bile duct stent or active infection.Bring your medicine list and 24-hour oncology contact details.
During treatmentBlood count and kidney tests before treatment. Liver tests according to the protocol. Day 15 dose review takes account of blood counts and what happened on day 8.Report changes before the next dose.
Response or follow-upClinical review and imaging as arranged for your treatment setting.Ask when results will be discussed.

Ask the team about a new fever with jaundice, dark urine or shivering, especially if you have a bile duct stent.
