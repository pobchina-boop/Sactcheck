The whole treatment journey

Docetaxel 100 mg/m² · every 3 weeks: the whole pathway

Locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer

Docetaxel 100 mg/m² by IV infusion on Day 1 every 21 days, with dose changes when needed. The number of cycles follows benefit, tolerance and the agreed treatment plan.

What each check is for

1Blood checksFBC, renal and liver profile before each treatment dose

Results help the team judge treatment safety.

Ask / act: Confirm the next test and who reviews it.

Sources: NCCP 00202

2Heart assessmentNo routine fixed-cycle echo or ECG is mandated for this regimen alone; assess symptoms and individual cardiac risk.

Some heart changes occur without symptoms.

Ask / act: Keep the appointment and report new symptoms between scans.

Sources: NCCP 00202 · SMPC · ESC

3Imaging and markersFor metastatic breast disease, response assessment commonly every 2–4 months, adapted to sites, symptoms and treatment. Markers can supplement assessment; they do not replace imaging or alone determine a treatment change.

Each scan should answer a defined clinical question.

Ask / act: Ask when the result will be reviewed.

Sources: EARLY · METASTATIC

4Booking and handoverAt initiation, phase changes and completion

Expected tests can be missed without an owner.

Ask / act: Record booking owner, result reviewer, due window and escalation contact.

Sources: NCCP 00202

At the next review, ask

  • Which treatment phase am I in, and what happens next?
  • Which test is expected next, who arranges it, and who explains the result?
  • Who should I contact if an expected appointment has not arrived?

This map does not show whether a test has been ordered, booked or completed. Do not wait for a scheduled test if you develop new symptoms.
