How your medicines work

Taxane chemotherapy

Docetaxel

Disrupts the cell’s division machinery
Microtubules stabilised→Division disrupted→Cell growth affected

Docetaxel stabilises tiny structures called microtubules. Cells need these to separate their chromosomes during division; disrupting the process can stop cancer cells multiplying. Healthy dividing cells are also affected.

Take prescribed steroid premedication. Report fever, lasting numbness, swelling, sore nails or infusion symptoms.

Practical treatment guide

Your treatment and access plan

Docetaxel 100 mg/m² by IV infusion on Day 1 every 21 days, with dose changes when needed. The number of cycles follows benefit, tolerance and the agreed treatment plan.

STEP 1

Before treatment

FBC, kidney and liver tests before treatment and every cycle. Review infection, neuropathy, skin/nail toxicity and swelling before the next dose.

STEP 2

Plan venous access

A cannula, PICC or implanted port may be used. A port is not automatically required; insertion and removal are separate procedures with individual instructions.

STEP 3

On the treatment day

Confirm the medicines due that day, any premedicines and the reviewed blood results. Tell staff immediately about pain, burning, rash or breathlessness.

STEP 4

After the visit

No routine take-home infusion pump is required for this regimen. Keep line-care, urgent-contact and next-review instructions.

Visit length

Docetaxel infusion is about one hour. Blood review, preparation, premedication and observation add to the visit.

Line care

A PICC or port has its own care plan. Report fever, redness, swelling, pain, leakage or line damage; do not remove or adjust it yourself.

Treatment changes

A delayed, omitted or changed dose needs an updated plan from the treating team. Do not move doses or restart on your own.

Support for this visit

Docetaxel: dexamethasone 8 mg orally twice daily for 3 days starting the day before chemotherapy; at least 3 doses before the infusion. If missed, contact the team; any IV replacement is a consultant decision.
