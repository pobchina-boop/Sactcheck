NCCP 00449 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Bevacizumab

  • High blood pressure / urine protein
  • Bleeding or blood clots
  • Delayed wound healing
  • Rare bowel perforation / fistula
  • Rare serious neurological effects
  • Infusion reaction

Irinotecan

  • Early cramps, sweating and diarrhoea
  • Delayed diarrhoea and dehydration
  • Low blood counts / serious infection
  • Nausea, mouth soreness and fatigue
  • Hair thinning or loss
  • Rare serious lung or allergic reactions

Folinic acid

  • Enhances fluorouracil activity and toxicity
  • Rare allergic reaction
  • Diarrhoea / mouth soreness in combination

Fluorouracil

  • Diarrhoea, dehydration and mouth ulcers
  • Low blood counts / infection
  • Hand-foot skin soreness
  • Cardiotoxicity including chest pain / ischaemia
  • Severe early toxicity including DPD-related toxicity
  • Pump / central-line complications

Make the plan practical

DPD testing before fluorouracil

DPD helps the body break down fluorouracil. Deficiency can cause severe toxicity. Testing guides the plan but cannot exclude all serious reactions; unexpectedly severe early diarrhoea, mouth sores or illness needs immediate review.

Your diarrhoea plan

Have the prescribed anti-diarrhoeal medicine and written instructions before going home. Follow that plan as soon as diarrhoea starts. Call promptly for persistent symptoms, cramps, fever, blood in stools or reduced urine.

Pump and line care

See “Your port and treatment pump” for insertion, connection, daily care and disconnection. Before leaving, know who to call, where to return and how to use the supplied spill plan.

Blood pressure, urine and procedures

Keep blood pressure and urine checks. Tell the team about wounds, operations, dental work or port insertion so bevacizumab timing can be agreed.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
