FOLFIRI + bevacizumab

FOLFIRI + bevacizumab is used for cancer of the colon or rectum that has spread. Treatment aims to control the cancer; the likely benefit and review plan depend on your individual situation. Surgery may be reconsidered in selected cases after multidisciplinary review.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Bevacizumab

Bevacizumab binds VEGF, a signal that helps blood vessels grow. Blocking it can restrict the blood supply supporting a tumour. It also affects normal blood vessels and healing, which is why blood pressure, urine and planned procedures matter.

Irinotecan

The body converts irinotecan into SN-38. This traps an enzyme called topoisomerase I while deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA) is being copied, causing damage that can stop cancer cells dividing. Normal bowel and blood-forming cells can also be affected.

Folinic acid

Folinic acid helps fluorouracil remain bound to an enzyme needed to make DNA. In this regimen it increases fluorouracil activity and can also increase its unwanted effects. It is not a vitamin supplement to start yourself.

Fluorouracil

Fluorouracil interferes with the production and use of DNA and RNA, the instructions cells need to grow. The infusion extends exposure over approximately two days.

Getting ready for treatment visits

Consent discussion aid

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: Infusion and pump connection

The team reviews symptoms, blood results and your treatment plan, including blood pressure and urine protein. Day-ward infusions are followed by connection of the fluorouracil pump.

What to do: Before leaving, confirm the pump-care instructions, disconnection appointment and contact number.

Day 2–3: The pump is delivering treatment

The pump runs for approximately 46 hours from connection. Keep it and the line secure and dry as taught. The team confirms the actual disconnection appointment.

What to do: Call for a leaking, disconnected or unexpectedly stopped pump. Do not reconnect, alter settings or disconnect it yourself unless specifically trained and instructed.

Day 4–7: After disconnection

Delayed diarrhoea can begin after the first 24 hours and may continue after the pump is removed. Use the written plan and contact the team early. Mouth soreness, nausea and tiredness can overlap.

What to do: Report poor intake, persistent diarrhoea or mouth pain early. Chest pain or major breathing difficulty requires emergency help.

Day 8–14: Between visits and the next review

Blood counts can fall between visits. Tell the team about bowel symptoms, mouth pain and how you are managing daily activities. Treatment proceeds only after review.

What to do: Bring the symptom passport and check the next appointment. Day 1 usually repeats 14 days after this cycle started, only if confirmed.

Support at home

Nausea prevention and rescue

Take the prescribed prevention medicines at the planned times and keep rescue treatment available. Tell the team if nausea or constipation is not controlled; do not duplicate steroid or anti-sickness medicines.

Bowel symptoms

Irinotecan can cause immediate cramps or sweating during the infusion and delayed diarrhoea afterwards. These need different treatments. Ask for the written rescue and urgent-contact plan before starting.

Counts and recovery

White-cell growth-factor injections are prescribed according to individual risk and previous complications. They are not automatically required for every patient or every cycle.

Blood pressure and wound care

Keep the agreed blood-pressure and urine checks. Report bleeding, poorly healing wounds, new swelling or severe headache promptly.
