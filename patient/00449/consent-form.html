Treatment context
FOLFIRI + bevacizumab is used for cancer of the colon or rectum that has spread. Treatment aims to control the cancer; the likely benefit and review plan depend on your individual situation. Surgery may be reconsidered in selected cases after multidisciplinary review.
Schedule
Day 1: bevacizumab, irinotecan, folinic acid and fluorouracil, then a 46-hour fluorouracil pump. A cycle lasts 14 days; your team confirms the next treatment.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Anti-VEGF targeted antibody
High blood pressure / urine protein
Bleeding or blood clots
Delayed wound healing
Rare bowel perforation / fistula
Rare serious neurological effects

Delayed diarrhoea and dehydration
Low blood counts / serious infection
Nausea, mouth soreness and fatigue
Hair thinning or loss
Rare serious lung or allergic reactions

Folinic acid

Chemotherapy modulator
Enhances fluorouracil activity and toxicity
Rare allergic reaction
Diarrhoea / mouth soreness in combination

Fluorouracil

Chemotherapy
Diarrhoea, dehydration and mouth ulcers
Low blood counts / infection
Hand-foot skin soreness
Cardiotoxicity including chest pain / ischaemia
Severe early toxicity including DPD-related toxicity
Pump / central-line complications

Confirm DPD testing, anti-sickness medicines, port/PICC care, the 46-hour pump and disconnection arrangements. Explain early cholinergic symptoms and the separate delayed-diarrhoea plan. Discuss blood pressure, urine protein, bleeding/clotting and procedure timing with bevacizumab.
