When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Call your team promptly for new or worsening symptoms. New leg weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, collapse or a seizure needs emergency assessment: call 112/999. Blistering or peeling skin, mouth or eye sores, or a severe headache with vision changes needs urgent medical assessment.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

Understand your treatment

What each medicine does

Lower testosterone to help control prostate cancer. Your team explains whether the goal is cancer control or part of a plan that includes radiotherapy.

GnRH agonist

Leuprorelin 11.25 mg

Prostap 3 DCS
Leuprorelin initially stimulates the pituitary gland. Continued stimulation then reduces its signal to the testes, so testosterone falls. This explains both the early flare risk and sustained hormone suppression between injections.

Brief initial stimulation↓Pituitary signal switches down↓Testosterone falls

What to watch for
Ask whether you need temporary anti-androgen tablets to protect against the initial testosterone flare. Report worsening pain, difficulty passing urine or an injection-site abscess. A low PSA is not a reason to stop injections. Blistering or peeling skin, mouth or eye sores, or a severe headache with vision changes needs urgent medical assessment.

Why this treatment is used

Your treatment setting

Prostatic carcinoma for which testosterone suppression is indicated.

Sources: nccp · smPC

How it works

Leuprorelin initially stimulates the pituitary gland. Continued stimulation then reduces its signal to the testes, so testosterone falls. This explains both the early flare risk and sustained hormone suppression between injections.

Sources: nccp · smPC

How it fits the wider plan

Androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) lowers testosterone. Many prostate cancers use this hormone as a growth signal. Lowering it helps control disease. Additional androgen-pathway medicines, chemotherapy and radiotherapy are separate decisions based on the cancer setting, health and priorities.

Sources: nccp · smPC

Previous surgery

Tell the team if you have had both testes removed or have been told the cancer is insensitive to endocrine treatment. This product has specific restrictions; castration-resistant cancer alone does not mean ADT should stop.

Sources: nccp · smPC

What the evidence can tell you

Trial results describe groups of people; they cannot predict an individual outcome.

Hormone suppression and PSA response are different from survival. Choice of depot is based on formulation, interval, clinical circumstances and preferences; separate studies do not establish a survival ranking.
