What each check is for

1Treatment and reviewOne injection every 12 weeks

Lower testosterone to help control prostate cancer. Your team explains whether the goal is cancer control or part of a plan that includes radiotherapy.

Ask / act: Call your team promptly for new or worsening symptoms. New leg weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, collapse or a seizure needs emergency assessment: call 112/999. Blistering or peeling skin, mouth or eye sores, or a severe headache with vision changes needs urgent medical assessment.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC
