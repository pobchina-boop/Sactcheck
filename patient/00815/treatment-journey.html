Weekly paclitaxel + trastuzumab → trastuzumab continuation: treatment and checks

Paclitaxel weekly for 12 doses with trastuzumab every 3 weeks; continue trastuzumab to complete one year of HER2 treatment when appropriate. The team selects IV trastuzumab or locally approved subcutaneous trastuzumab; these are alternative formulations, not additional medicines.

What each check is for

1Baseline reviewBefore treatment

Adjuvant HER2-positive, node-negative breast cancer; tumour ≤3 cm. Assess cardiac function before trastuzumab and every 12 weeks during treatment. Keep the same monitoring clock when paclitaxel ends or trastuzumab changes schedule; arrange earlier review for symptoms or falling function. EMA product information recommends cardiac checks every 6 months after treatment until 24 months; following anthracyclines in early disease, yearly up to 5 years from the last trastuzumab dose or longer if function falls. Your team individualises this plan.

Ask / act: Confirm source eligibility and results.

Sources: NCCP 00815 · SMPC

2Treatment-day checkBefore each planned dose

Baseline blood tests and review before each chemotherapy administration according to the source and local plan. Once chemotherapy ends, testing follows trastuzumab and individual clinical needs.

Ask / act: Confirm active medicines and whether a dose is held.

Sources: NCCP 00815

3Support planAt every phase or day change

Paclitaxel weekly for 12 doses with trastuzumab every 3 weeks; continue trastuzumab to complete one year of HER2 treatment when appropriate. The team selects IV trastuzumab or locally approved subcutaneous trastuzumab; these are alternative formulations, not additional medicines.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support script; do not merge phases.

Sources: NCCP 00815

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

After early-breast treatment, breast imaging and clinical review follow the agreed plan. Routine CT/PET scans or blood tumour markers are not used to look for distant recurrence in a person without symptoms or clinical concerns.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities together.

Sources: NCCP 00815 · EARLY

At the next review, ask
