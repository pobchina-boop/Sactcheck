My treatment handbook

ECF for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Your treatment and how the medicines work

When the cancer is resectable, treatment before and after surgery aims to reduce recurrence. When the cancer is advanced, treatment aims to control it and ease symptoms. Confirm which plan applies.

Epirubicin · Pharmorubicin

Anthracycline. Interferes with the enzymes that help cells copy and repair DNA. Tell the nurse immediately if the IV site stings or swells.

Cisplatin · Cisplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. Makes links in DNA that interfere with cell division. Call the team for reduced urine, persistent vomiting or ringing in the ears.

Fluorouracil · 5-FU

Chemotherapy. Fluorouracil interferes with the production and use of DNA and RNA, the instructions cells need to grow. The infusion extends exposure over approximately two days. Diarrhoea, mouth sores and skin soreness may become serious. Chest pain during the pump infusion needs emergency assessment. Unexpected severe early toxicity can occur even after DPD testing.

What the research found

In advanced disease, the pooled comparisons supported substitution of capecitabine for infused fluorouracil and oxaliplatin for cisplatin. The trial did not compare these with modern immunotherapy combinations. For resectable disease, ECF before and after surgery improved overall survival versus surgery alone. The trial tested ECF specifically.

These results describe selected trial populations. Your outlook depends on your disease, fitness and treatment setting.

Common effects and what to do

Mouth and bowel

Ring for painful ulcers, persistent diarrhoea or inability to drink.

Fatigue and appetite

Tell the team about weight loss or difficulty swallowing so that nutrition support can be arranged.

Fluid and hearing changes

Report reduced urine or ringing in the ears.

Serious effects: when to call

Infection

Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999.

Chest pain

Fluoropyrimidines can affect blood flow to the heart. Seek emergency help for new severe chest pain.

Severe early toxicity

Contact oncology immediately for severe diarrhoea, mouth ulcers or fever early in a course.

Injection site

Tell the nurse straight away about burning, pain or swelling while epirubicin is being given.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Between treatments

Keep the pump and dressing dry. Call the day unit if the pump leaks, disconnects or empties early or late. A trained team changes it weekly.

Swallowing and nutrition

Call if swallowing becomes difficult or your weight falls. Ask about dietitian support.

Using your seven-day pump

Weekly exchanges

Your team connects a seven-day pump on days 1, 8 and 15. Fluorouracil continues through all 21 days.

At home

Keep the pump and dressing dry. Call the day unit if the pump leaks, disconnects or empties early or late. A trained team changes it weekly.

Leaks or line symptoms

Follow your pump emergency instructions and call immediately. Seek urgent help for fever, swelling, chest pain or breathing difficulty.
