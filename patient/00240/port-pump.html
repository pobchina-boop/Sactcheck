My line and pump

ECF for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Using your seven-day pump

Weekly exchanges

Your team connects a seven-day pump on days 1, 8 and 15. Fluorouracil continues through all 21 days.

At home

Keep the pump and dressing dry. Call the day unit if the pump leaks, disconnects or empties early or late. A trained team changes it weekly.

Leaks or line symptoms

Follow your pump emergency instructions and call immediately. Seek urgent help for fever, swelling, chest pain or breathing difficulty.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
