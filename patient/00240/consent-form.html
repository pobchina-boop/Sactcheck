Treatment and purpose

When the cancer is resectable, treatment before and after surgery aims to reduce recurrence. When the cancer is advanced, treatment aims to control it and ease symptoms. Confirm which plan applies. Unresectable locally advanced or metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma. Unresectable locally advanced or metastatic oesophagogastric junction adenocarcinoma. Unresectable locally advanced or metastatic oesophageal adenocarcinoma. Perioperative treatment of resectable gastric adenocarcinoma. Perioperative treatment of resectable oesophagogastric junction adenocarcinoma. Perioperative treatment of resectable lower oesophageal adenocarcinoma.

Treatment schedule. Day 1 epirubicin and cisplatin. Fluorouracil runs continuously for all 21 days. Start a fresh seven-day pump on days 1, 8 and 15. Up to six cycles. For surgery, plan three before and three after, with reassessment after the operation.

Treatment discussion and decision

ECF for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

In advanced disease, the pooled comparisons supported substitution of capecitabine for infused fluorouracil and oxaliplatin for cisplatin. The trial did not compare these with modern immunotherapy combinations. For resectable disease, ECF before and after surgery improved overall survival versus surgery alone. The trial tested ECF specifically. These results describe selected trial populations. Your outlook depends on your disease, fitness and treatment setting.

When the cancer is resectable, treatment before and after surgery aims to reduce recurrence. When the cancer is advanced, treatment aims to control it and ease symptoms. Confirm which plan applies. Up to six cycles. For surgery, plan three before and three after, with reassessment after the operation. Discuss alternatives, fertility, and individual risks with the treating clinician.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
