My treatment at a glance · NCCP 00568

Pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + carboplatin

Chemotherapy and immunotherapy to help control lung cancer. Your team explains the expected benefits and the choices for you.

Your treatment plan

Treatment is usually on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle: once every 3 weeks. Your team confirms each visit after checking your blood tests and symptoms.

  1. First part: up to 4 cyclesPembrolizumab, pemetrexed and carboplatin together. Each cycle lasts 3 weeks.
  2. Review and ongoing treatmentCarboplatin stops. Pembrolizumab and pemetrexed may continue. Your team reviews how treatment is working and how you are feeling.

Before and between visits

  • Take the prescribed folic acid and attend for vitamin B12 injections. These help protect you during pemetrexed treatment.
  • Follow your written anti-sickness and steroid plan. Ask the team to check the steroid instructions if you have more than one prescription.
  • Have the arranged blood tests and report new symptoms before each visit. Keep your immunotherapy alert card with you.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately for temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions.

Cannot reach the team? Seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.
