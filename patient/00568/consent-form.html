2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Pembrolizumab

Immunotherapy · immune-mediated toxicity
Pneumonitis - new cough / breathlessness
Colitis - diarrhoea / abdominal symptoms
Hepatitis - liver inflammation
Endocrine inflammation - thyroid, pituitary, adrenal or diabetes
Nephritis / kidney inflammation
Severe skin inflammation / rash
Neurological / muscle inflammation
Cardiac inflammation including myocarditis

Immune effects may occur during treatment or after treatment has stopped.

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts / infection
Fatigue
Mouth soreness / mucositis
Skin rash
Renal / kidney toxicity

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts / infection / bleeding
Nausea and vomiting
Hypersensitivity / allergic reaction
Individual organ-function / blood-test changes discussed where relevant
