Treatment context
The combination aims to control metastatic non-squamous lung cancer. Your team checks cancer markers, your health and your preferences. Chemotherapy and immunotherapy work in different ways.
Schedule
Induction: all three medicines on Day 1 every 21 days for up to four cycles. Maintenance: carboplatin stops; pembrolizumab and pemetrexed may continue every 21 days while appropriate.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Pembrolizumab

Immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver, kidneys, glands, skin and other organs
Serious infusion reactions
Effects can develop after treatment ends

Pemetrexed

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts and infection
Fatigue, nausea, mouth soreness and rash
Kidney toxicity; folate and B12 supplementation are required

Carboplatin

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection or bleeding
Nausea and vomiting
Allergic reactions
Kidney, hearing and nerve effects
