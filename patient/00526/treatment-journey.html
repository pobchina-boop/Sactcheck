The whole treatment journey

Pertuzumab + trastuzumab + vinorelbine: the whole pathway

HER2-positive advanced breast cancer · taxanes unsuitable

IV pertuzumab and IV trastuzumab on Day 1; vinorelbine on Days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle. Vinorelbine is prescribed by IV OR by mouth, not both. There is no planned Day 15 vinorelbine. Continue while beneficial and tolerated; treatment may change if one component causes toxicity.

What each check is for

1Blood testsFBC, kidney and liver profile at baseline and before every 21-day cycle. The team reviews the necessary results before a planned dose. Confirm the blood-count check needed before each additional vinorelbine or paclitaxel dose; a Day 1 result does not automatically authorise a later dose. For oral vinorelbine, the product information requires blood counts on the day of each administration, including Day 8; the regimen source lists full blood/renal/liver checks before each cycle.

Check safety before the next dose.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP · VINORELBINE-ORAL

2Heart testsCheck heart pumping function before starting HER2 treatment. Repeat echo or MUGA every 12 weeks: around cycle 5, then cycle 9 if 21-day cycles stay on time. Earlier checks are needed for symptoms or abnormal findings. Count elapsed time from the first HER2 dose; changing phase or route does not restart the clock. After the first year, ESC guidance allows six-monthly echo in selected asymptomatic low- or moderate-risk metastatic patients with normal assessments, only under an agreed team plan. Entering maintenance alone does not justify reducing checks.

Monitor pumping function and relevant rhythm risk.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP · HERCEPTIN · ESC

3Cancer imagingRecord baseline disease sites. Response imaging is generally reviewed every 2–4 months under international guidance, adapted to symptoms, disease pace and treatment. The team selects the scan; this is not an automatic CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order for every patient. Tumour markers may support assessment in selected cases, but do not alone decide progression or a treatment change.

Assess response or follow-up needs.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: METASTATIC

4Bone healthIf the cancer is also hormone-receptor positive, endocrine treatment may form part of the wider plan. Bone-density testing is linked to aromatase inhibitors, ovarian suppression and fracture risk; it is not automatically required because a HER2 medicine is prescribed. Agree baseline DEXA where indicated and individual follow-up.

Assess fracture risk where relevant.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: BONE
