Vinorelbine capsules · advanced breast cancer

Aim to slow or shrink advanced breast cancer and help symptoms while balancing side effects. Duration depends on benefit, tolerability and the agreed plan; a fixed number of cycles or a guaranteed survival improvement is not promised.

ONCE WEEKLY. First 3 administrations 60 mg/m², total maximum 120 mg. From administration 4,80 mg/m² (maximum 160 mg) only after the team reviews nadirs and toxicity.60 or 50 mg/m²/lower may be needed. Continue weekly while helpful and tolerated; no fixed total number of weeks.

Tests and checks

Blood counts are checked on the day of each weekly dose. Kidney and liver tests follow the weekly regimen and prescribed plan. The team reviews the lowest counts after earlier doses before any increase, alongside bowel, nerve, infection and medicine checks.

Between treatment visits

This is a relative reference, not an appointment calendar. Treatment days can change after clinical review. Symptom bands do not predict when an effect will occur.

A day without treatment is not automatically a safe day. Use the emergency contact when needed; do not move or restart doses yourself.

My side-effect map

Recognise changes and know when to call.

A picture of side effects

Numbered symptom boxes connect to the anatomical illustration. The medicine responsible may be uncertain; report the symptom.

1Blood counts and infection
Vinorelbine

White blood cells can fall, raising the risk of infection. Anaemia can cause tiredness or breathlessness; low platelets can cause bruising or bleeding. Blood tests guide delays and dose changes. Fever, shaking chills or feeling suddenly unwell need the oncology emergency contact immediately.

3Tingling, numbness or weakness
Vinorelbine

Nerve effects may change sensation, grip, walking or bowel movement. Report changes early because the next dose may need to wait or be reduced.

5Uncommon serious symptoms
Vinorelbine

New chest pain, sudden or worsening breathlessness, or severe allergic symptoms need urgent medical help. Previous heart disease and interacting medicines need review before treatment.

2Constipation, nausea and mouth problems
Vinorelbine

The bowel can slow down and nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea or sore mouth can occur. Use the prescribed bowel and anti-sickness plan. Severe abdominal pain, swelling, repeated vomiting or inability to pass stool/gas needs urgent assessment for ileus; do not simply take extra laxatives.

4Fatigue, hair and liver tests
Vinorelbine

Tiredness, hair thinning/loss and liver-test changes can occur. Pace activities and tell the team if eating, drinking or daily tasks become difficult.
