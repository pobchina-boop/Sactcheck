Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy

Vinorelbine

Disrupts the machinery of cell division
Binds cell-division structures↓Microtubules cannot function normally↓Cell division is disrupted

Vinorelbine disrupts the tiny structures a cell needs to divide. This can stop cancer cells multiplying. Healthy blood-forming cells, gut cells and nerves can also be affected.

How treatment is given

Take with some food; swallow capsules whole with water. Do not chew, suck or dissolve. The exact total dose may require more than one capsule; pharmacy provides the strengths and number. Verify the weekly date, dose and capsule count each time. Do not take another dose if vomiting occurs within a few hours. Contact the team and record the dose taken. Do not swallow damaged capsules. Return them to pharmacy; immediately wash any contact with contents using water or saline. Missed/late dose: contact the team for the next date. Do not double or move the weekly schedule yourself. This is pragmatic medication-safety advice, not an extra NCCP catch-up schedule.

Fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding

Purpose and alternatives for the person’s advanced breast cancer, including other systemic treatment or supportive care according to goals. Possible benefit is uncertain; response is reviewed and treatment can be paused, changed or stopped. Low blood counts, infection/sepsis, fatigue, nausea/vomiting, bowel problems, neuropathy, hair change and uncommon serious lung/heart/allergic toxicity. Fertility and pregnancy planning before treatment; discuss sperm/egg preservation where relevant. Do not breastfeed. HPRA supplements: contraception during treatment and for 7 months afterwards for women,4 months for men; confirm the exact product and individual advice.

Questions to ask

Can IV and oral vinorelbine be exchanged?

Oral 60/80 mg/m² and IV 25/30 mg/m² have pharmacokinetic equivalence references, but never convertmg-for-mg or swap routes yourself. Dose rules and schedules differ.

How long will treatment continue?

Continue weekly while helpful and tolerated; no fixed total number of weeks.

Do I need regular heart scans?

Review cardiovascular history and new symptoms. These monotherapy NCCP protocols do not mandate the serial LVEF/echo schedule used for anthracyclines or trastuzumab; arrange cardiac tests when clinically indicated.

What if I vomit, miss a dose or find damaged capsules?

Do not take another dose if vomiting occurs within a few hours. Contact the team and record the dose taken. Do not swallow damaged capsules. Return them to pharmacy; immediately wash any contact with contents using water or saline. Missed/late dose: contact the team for the next date. Do not double or move the weekly schedule yourself. This is pragmatic medication-safety advice, not an extra NCCP catch-up schedule.

How do I store the capsules?

Current Vinorelbine Accord SmPC: refrigerator 2–8°C. Confirm the dispensed brand/pack instructions; keep in original packaging and out of children’s reach. Return unused or damaged capsules to pharmacy; follow cytotoxic handling advice.

Does dose four always use more capsules?

No. The team reviews the lowest counts and side effects after the first three doses. Take only the current prescribed dose; severe or repeated low counts can prevent an increase.
