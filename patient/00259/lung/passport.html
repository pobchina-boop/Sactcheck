NCCP 00259 · version 6

Vinorelbine capsules · non-small-cell lung cancer

ONCE WEEKLY. First 3 administrations 60 mg/m², total maximum 120 mg. From administration 4, 80 mg/m² (maximum 160 mg) only after the team reviews nadirs and toxicity. 60 or 50 mg/m²/lower may be needed. Continue weekly while helpful and tolerated; no fixed total number of weeks.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Use the treating unit’s 24-hour emergency number and local fever threshold. Contact immediately for fever/chills, sudden illness, breathlessness/chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, severe abdominal pain/obstruction symptoms, bleeding or severe allergy. Do not wait until the next visit.
