Vinorelbine capsules · non-small-cell lung cancer

ONCE WEEKLY. First 3 administrations 60 mg/m², total maximum 120 mg. From administration 4, 80 mg/m² (maximum 160 mg) only after the team reviews nadirs and toxicity. 60 or 50 mg/m²/lower may be needed. Continue weekly while helpful and tolerated; no fixed total number of weeks.

What each check is for

1Treatment and dose reviewBefore every administration

Blood counts are checked on the day of each weekly dose. Kidney and liver tests follow the weekly regimen and prescribed plan. The team reviews the lowest counts after earlier doses before any increase, alongside bowel, nerve, infection and medicine checks.

Ask / act: The treating team confirms results, dose and suitability; this map does not authorise treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00259 · SMPC

2Support and symptomsThroughout treatment, including days at home

Use the treating unit’s 24-hour emergency number and local fever threshold. Contact immediately for fever/chills, sudden illness, breathlessness/chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, severe abdominal pain/obstruction symptoms, bleeding or severe allergy. Do not wait until the next visit.

Ask / act: Keep a written medicine plan and urgent-contact route.

Sources: NCCP 00259 · ORAL-ANTIEMETIC · NCIS

3Response and continuationAt agreed clinical review points

Vinorelbine chemotherapy can help control non-small-cell lung cancer and its symptoms. The team weighs possible benefit against side effects, previous treatments and your priorities, and reviews whether to continue.

Ask / act: Confirm the current plan with the team.

Sources: NCCP 00259 · TRIAL

At the next review, ask

  • Which treatment phase am I in, and what happens next?
  • Which test is expected next, who arranges it, and who explains the result?
  • Who should I contact if an expected appointment has not arrived?
