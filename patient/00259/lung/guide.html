Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

ONCE WEEKLY. First 3 administrations 60 mg/m², total maximum 120 mg. From administration 4, 80 mg/m² (maximum 160 mg) only after the team reviews nadirs and toxicity. 60 or 50 mg/m²/lower may be needed. Continue weekly while helpful and tolerated; no fixed total number of weeks.

Take with some food; swallow capsules whole with water. Do not chew, suck or dissolve. The exact total dose may require more than one capsule; pharmacy provides the strengths and number. Verify the weekly date, dose and capsule count each time. Do not take another dose if vomiting occurs within a few hours. Contact the team and record the dose taken. Do not swallow damaged capsules. Return them to pharmacy; immediately wash any contact with contents using water or saline. Missed/late dose: contact the team for the next date. Do not double or move the weekly schedule yourself. This is pragmatic medication-safety advice, not an extra NCCP catch-up schedule.

Checks and follow-up

Blood counts are checked on the day of each weekly dose. Kidney and liver tests follow the weekly regimen and prescribed plan. The team reviews the lowest counts after earlier doses before any increase, alongside bowel, nerve, infection and medicine checks.

Support between reviews

Sickness plan

Take the anti-sickness medicine exactly as prescribed before/around the weekly capsule dose; contact the team if it is not controlling symptoms.

Bowel care

Agree a constipation plan, particularly if using opioids or ondansetron. Severe abdominal pain, swelling, repeated vomiting or inability to pass stool/gas needs urgent assessment; do not simply add laxatives.

Medicines and vaccines

Check every new prescription, supplement and vaccine with the team; avoid grapefruit. Live vaccines and interacting medicines need review.

Weekly safety

Take capsules only once weekly after the team clears the blood results. Do not repeat a vomited dose or increase the capsules yourself.
