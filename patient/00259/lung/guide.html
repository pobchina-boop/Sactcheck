Vinorelbine capsules · non-small-cell lung cancer

Vinorelbine chemotherapy can help control non-small-cell lung cancer and its symptoms. The team weighs possible benefit against side effects, previous treatments and your priorities, and reviews whether to continue.

My overall treatment

ONCE WEEKLY. First 3 administrations 60 mg/m², total maximum 120 mg. From administration 4, 80 mg/m² (maximum 160 mg) only after the team reviews nadirs and toxicity. 60 or 50 mg/m²/lower may be needed. Continue weekly while helpful and tolerated; no fixed total number of weeks.

  1. TreatmentOnce every 7 days after blood-test clearance. Duration agreed with your team.
  2. ReviewYour team reviews benefit and side effects, then agrees whether to continue, pause or change treatment.

Timing may change after review. Side effects can occur on any day; there are no guaranteed safe days.

Between treatments

  • Take capsules only once on the prescribed weekly day, after blood-test clearance. They are not a daily medicine.
  • The first three administrations use an introductory dose; any later increase depends on counts and the prescription.

Bring your medicine list and report new symptoms before dosing. Confirm the prescription, test appointments and daytime / out-of-hours contact plan.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.

What to watch for

You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

1. Blood counts and infectionVinorelbine

Low blood counts can cause infection, tiredness, bruising or bleeding. Fever, chills or sudden illness: call oncology immediately.

3. Tingling, numbness or weaknessVinorelbine

Nerve effects may change sensation, grip, walking or bowel movement. Report changes early because the next dose may need to wait or be reduced.

5. Uncommon serious symptomsVinorelbine

New chest pain, sudden or worsening breathlessness, or severe allergic symptoms need urgent medical help. Previous heart disease and interacting medicines need review before treatment.

2. Bowel and mouth symptomsVinorelbine

Constipation, vomiting or sore mouth can occur. Severe pain, swelling, repeated vomiting or no stool/gas: urgent assessment; do not take extra laxatives.

4. Fatigue, hair and liver testsVinorelbine

Tiredness, hair thinning/loss and liver-test changes can occur. Pace activities and tell the team if eating, drinking or daily tasks become difficult.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials a cycle when it is complete, after the final planned dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
