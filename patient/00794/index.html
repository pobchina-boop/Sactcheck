Two treatment visits in each three-week cycle: Day 1, then Day 8. There is no planned infusion on Day 15. The next cycle starts after Day 21 if the team confirms it is appropriate.

Tests and checks

Before the first treatment: full blood count, kidney and liver blood tests, glucose and electrolytes including magnesium, calcium and phosphate. Pregnancy testing is needed where relevant. electrocardiogram (ECG) is added if clinically indicated. The team checks infection, medicines, previous low blood counts and whether preventive granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF) injections are appropriate. Blood count, kidney and liver tests are checked before each cycle; the neutrophil count must also meet the separate Day 8 threshold before that infusion. Glucose and the listed electrolytes are checked every second cycle. Pregnancy testing is repeated before each cycle where relevant. Review diarrhoea, hydration, nausea, oral intake, fever and infection before each infusion. Scans follow the individual response-assessment plan.

Between treatment visits

Treatment days are specified by the regimen. Symptom timing varies; the full-cycle symptom bands show that an effect can occur at any point, not a measured probability or severity.

Contact the oncology service immediately for fever, shivering or feeling acutely unwell; significant diarrhoea, inability to keep fluids down, dizziness, very little urine, or abdominal pain. Do not wait for the next treatment visit. During an infusion, tell staff immediately about breathing difficulty, swelling, rash, chest discomfort or faintness. Use the emergency service for severe breathing difficulty, collapse or other life-threatening symptoms.
