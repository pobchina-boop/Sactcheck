NCCP 00794 · version 3

Sacituzumab govitecan (Trodelvy)

Two treatment visits in each three-week cycle: Day 1, then Day 8. There is no planned infusion on Day 15. The next cycle starts after Day 21 if the team confirms it is appropriate.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact the oncology service immediately for fever, shivering or feeling acutely unwell; significant diarrhoea, inability to keep fluids down, dizziness, very little urine, or abdominal pain. Do not wait for the next treatment visit. During an infusion, tell staff immediately about breathing difficulty, swelling, rash, chest discomfort or faintness. Use the emergency service for severe breathing difficulty, collapse or other life-threatening symptoms.
