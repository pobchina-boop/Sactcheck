What each check is for

1Blood testsBefore the first treatment: full blood count, kidney and liver blood tests, glucose and electrolytes including magnesium, calcium and phosphate. Pregnancy testing is needed where relevant. ECG is added if clinically indicated. The team checks infection, medicines, previous low blood counts and whether preventive G-CSF injections are appropriate. Blood count, kidney and liver tests are checked before each cycle; the neutrophil count must also meet the separate Day 8 threshold before that infusion. Glucose and the listed electrolytes are checked every second cycle. Pregnancy testing is repeated before each cycle where relevant. Review diarrhoea, hydration, nausea, oral intake, fever and infection before each infusion. Scans follow the individual response-assessment plan.

Checks safety before continuation.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP 00794 · SMPC

2Heart checksAn ECG is used when clinically indicated. Sacituzumab alone does not require a routine echocardiogram schedule. Cardiac symptoms or other medicines may change the plan.

Checks cardiac safety when the agent or clinical risk requires it.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP 00794 · SMPC

3ImagingResponse assessment in advanced breast cancer is generally every two to four months, adapted to disease dynamics, sites, symptoms and treatment. Less frequent monitoring may suit indolent disease. New symptoms or suspected progression need earlier assessment. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not an automatic three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

Uses the correct surveillance or response-assessment pathway.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: ESMO_MBC2023

4Bone healthA routine DXA scan is not a sacituzumab-specific requirement. Bone health, bone metastases and any bone-strengthening treatment need their own individual plan.

Companion endocrine therapy and individual fracture risk may change the plan.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.
