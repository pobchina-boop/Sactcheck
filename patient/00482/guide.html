My tests and checks

WhenWhat is checkedTiming / reason
BeforeBloods and health reviewNCCP / product informationFull blood count, kidney and liver tests and blood glucose. Review liver disease, heart-rhythm risks, medicines, fertility and contraception.
DuringSafety and responseNCCP / product informationRepeat blood count, kidney and liver tests and glucose when clinically indicated. Periodic liver tests are particularly relevant in the first six months. Warfarin or other coumarin treatment needs close international normalised ratio (blood-clotting test) (INR) monitoring.

Between treatments

  • Effects can occur on any day; there is no guaranteed symptom-free period.
  • Tell the team about yellow eyes or skin, dark urine, worsening breathlessness or unusual bleeding. Use effective contraception during treatment and for 130 days afterwards. Do not stop or alter blood thinners yourself.

Bring your medicine list and exact prescription / appointment dates.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.
