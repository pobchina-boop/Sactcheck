Additional phase-specific support

  • Use the dedicated carboplatin AUC≥4 high-risk reference (no routine olanzapine): aprepitant125mg Day1 then80mg Days2–3, ondansetron16mg Day1, dexamethasone12mg Day1 then8mg Days2–4; subsequent metoclopramide10mg TDS with duration/suitability prescribed.
  • Etoposide is given on Days1–3. Reconcile support for each active treatment day and for two days afterwards; a fixed Day1 script alone is not a complete multi-day prescription.
  • No routine infusion premedication is usually required unless there has been a previous hypersensitivity reaction. Have anaphylaxis facilities available.
  • If oral etoposide is chosen, confirm capsule strengths, empty-stomach dosing, adherence and handling; no replacement dose after vomiting/missed dose without advice.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.

Maintenance: serplulimab alone

Active medicines: serplulimab

Emetogenic risk: minimal

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Serplulimab alone has minimal emetogenic potential: no routine continuation of the induction chemotherapy antiemetic course.
  • Provide symptom-directed rescue if appropriate; investigate new nausea/vomiting for endocrine, immune and other causes.
  • Continue cycle bloods, thyroid tests every3–6weeks and immune symptom vigilance; keep the alert card.
