Consent discussion aid

Gemcitabine and carboplatin

The aim is to shrink or control the cancer after it has returned.

Treatment discussion

This combination is used when one of these cancers returns after at least six months without recurrence following first-line platinum treatment.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Day 1Day 1Gemcitabine then carboplatin, both into a vein.
Day 8Day 8Gemcitabine alone. The next cycle is planned three weeks after day 1 while treatment remains helpful and tolerable.

Possible benefit, alternatives and your priorities

In a randomised trial in platinum-sensitive recurrent ovarian cancer, median time before progression was 8.6 months with the combination and 5.8 months with carboplatin alone.

The trial did not establish an overall survival advantage. Group medians cannot predict an individual outcome.

Discuss the expected benefit, side effects, fertility, alternatives and symptom-directed care with the treating clinician. You may ask for more time or change your decision.

Other options discussed and what matters most to me

Important risks we discussed

  • Infusion reaction · Infused medicines — Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, wheeze or chest tightness during treatment.
  • Infection or bleeding · Chemotherapy — Fever, shivering, unexplained bleeding or suddenly feeling unwell? Call the oncology team now.
  • Kidneys · Carboplatin — Tell the team if you pass much less urine or cannot keep fluids down. Kidney checks guide treatment.
  • Nausea and vomiting · Chemotherapy — Use your prescribed anti-sickness plan. Call if vomiting continues or you cannot drink.
  • Skin · Gemcitabine — Blisters, peeling or a painful spreading rash need urgent assessment.
  • New breathlessness · Gemcitabine — New cough or shortness of breath needs urgent assessment, including between visits.

Risks to discuss for each medicine

Gemcitabine

Infection or bleeding, Fatigue, Breathing problems, Severe skin reaction. Phone promptly about fever, bleeding, a new cough or difficulty breathing.

Carboplatin

Low blood counts, Nausea, Infusion reaction. Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.
