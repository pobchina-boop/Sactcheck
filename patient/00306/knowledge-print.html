Your treatment reference

Gemcitabine and carboplatin

Your treatment and how the medicines work

The aim is to shrink or control the cancer after it has returned.

Gemcitabine · Gemcitabine

Chemotherapy. It interferes with copying DNA as cells divide. Blood-forming cells can also be affected. Phone promptly about fever, bleeding, a new cough or difficulty breathing.

Carboplatin · Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. It damages DNA in dividing cells. Kidney function helps determine the prescribed amount. Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1

Gemcitabine then carboplatin, both into a vein.

Day 8

Gemcitabine alone. The next cycle is planned three weeks after day 1 while treatment remains helpful and tolerable.

Common effects and what to do

Nausea and vomiting · Chemotherapy

Use your prescribed anti-sickness plan. Call if vomiting continues or you cannot drink.

Skin · Gemcitabine

Blisters, peeling or a painful spreading rash need urgent assessment.

Serious effects: when to call

Infusion reaction · Infused medicines

Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, wheeze or chest tightness during treatment.

Infection or bleeding · Chemotherapy

Fever, shivering, unexplained bleeding or suddenly feeling unwell? Call the oncology team now.

Kidneys · Carboplatin

Tell the team if you pass much less urine or cannot keep fluids down. Kidney checks guide treatment.

Nausea and vomiting · Chemotherapy

Use your prescribed anti-sickness plan. Call if vomiting continues or you cannot drink.

Skin · Gemcitabine

Blisters, peeling or a painful spreading rash need urgent assessment.

New breathlessness · Gemcitabine

New cough or shortness of breath needs urgent assessment, including between visits.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shaking chills, unusual bleeding or sudden illness? Call the oncology unit now. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Day 1 sickness prevention

Follow your written carboplatin anti-sickness prescription, including any medicines you take after leaving the unit.

Day 8 sickness and fever

Gemcitabine alone has a lower sickness risk. It can cause flu-like symptoms. Call about fever or chills so infection can be assessed.

Planning your visits

At the day unit

Each infusion usually takes about half an hour. Blood counts on day 8 can lead to a smaller gemcitabine dose or an omitted visit. An omitted dose is not made up in that cycle.

If a visit changes

Your team will tell you if a visit moves after a delay. Do not work out a new date yourself.

Before starting treatment

Discuss fertility, contraception and breastfeeding with your team. Bring your current medicines and details of previous infusion reactions.

What the research found

In a randomised trial in platinum-sensitive recurrent ovarian cancer, median time before progression was 8.6 months with the combination and 5.8 months with carboplatin alone.

The trial did not establish an overall survival advantage. Group medians cannot predict an individual outcome.
