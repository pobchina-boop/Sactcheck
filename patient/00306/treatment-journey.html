SACTCheck · NCCP 00306 v7 · Relapsed ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer

Your treatment journey

Gemcitabine and carboplatin

The aim is to shrink or control the cancer after it has returned.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1

Gemcitabine then carboplatin, both into a vein.

Day 8

Gemcitabine alone. The next cycle is planned three weeks after day 1 while treatment remains helpful and tolerable.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhy it matters
Before startingBlood counts, kidney and liver tests, and a kidney filtration assessment for carboplatin dosing. A hearing test may be needed.Confirm which checks are booked before your first infusion.
Day 1Blood count, kidney and liver tests.These help your team decide whether the next cycle can begin.
Day 8Blood count and kidney tests.The day 8 decision concerns gemcitabine alone.

Scans and any tumour-marker tests follow your individual treatment plan.

At review visits

  • Which tests do you need before day 8, and who would you call if you felt shivery afterwards? Talk through this with your nurse and write the answer on your appointment sheet.
  • Who can I contact outside clinic hours? Ask the team to fill in your wallet card with local numbers.
