Preparing for treatment visits

Jemperli with paclitaxel and carboplatin

Planning your visits

Day-unit visits

On combination days, dostarlimab goes into a vein first, then paclitaxel, then carboplatin. The paclitaxel infusion usually takes about three hours. Allow extra time for checks, premedicines and observation.

After cycle six

Confirm the actual date of cycle seven with the unit. Delays can move subsequent appointments.

Family planning

Discuss fertility, contraception and breastfeeding with the oncology team before treatment.

Write down your next appointments

  • Bring your medicine list and immunotherapy alert card. Confirm home premedicines and the phase of your next visit.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, new breathlessness, diarrhoea or unusual bleeding? Call oncology now. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
