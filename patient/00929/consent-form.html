Jemperli with paclitaxel and carboplatin

The aim is to control womb cancer that is advanced or has returned, and delay it getting worse.

Treatment discussion

This Irish regimen requires a tumour test showing mismatch repair deficiency (dMMR) or high microsatellite instability (MSI-H). Your team explains what the result means for you.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
CombinationDay 1 every 21 daysThree infusions every three weeks if your team confirms you are ready.
MaintenanceFirst visit 3 weeks after cycle 6Jemperli alone, then every six weeks. Your team reviews benefit and side effects. NCCP allows up to three years of monotherapy.

Possible benefit, alternatives and your priorities

In RUBY, adding dostarlimab to chemotherapy and then continuing dostarlimab reduced the hazard of progression or death in the dMMR/MSI-H group (HR 0.28, 95% CI 0.16–0.50).

These results compare groups over time. They cannot predict the duration of benefit for one person. The NCCP indication shown here is biomarker-specific.

Discuss the expected benefit, side effects, fertility, alternatives and symptom-directed care with the treating clinician. You may ask for more time or change your decision.

Other options discussed and what matters most to me

Important risks we discussed

  • Dostarlimab and immune inflammation — Lung, bowel, liver, kidney, skin and hormone problems can begin after treatment stops. Call urgently for new breathlessness, diarrhoea, jaundice or marked weakness.
  • Paclitaxel and carboplatin — Fever, chills, bleeding or sudden illness need an immediate call. Report flushing, dizziness or wheeze during any infusion immediately.
