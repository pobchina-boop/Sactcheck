Support during combination

Jemperli with paclitaxel and carboplatin

Three infusions every three weeks if your team confirms you are ready.

Combination phase

Carboplatin carries high sickness risk. Follow the scheduled anti-sickness medicines and the rescue plan given by your team.

Paclitaxel premedicines

Before paclitaxel the unit provides medicines to reduce allergic reactions. Confirm any doses you must take at home before attending.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, new breathlessness, diarrhoea or unusual bleeding? Call oncology now. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
