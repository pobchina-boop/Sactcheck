Carboplatin · 28-day breast schedule

Aim to slow or shrink metastatic breast cancer and help symptoms, balancing possible benefit, side effects and treatment burden.

My schedule at a glance

intravenous (IV) treatment · Day 1Day 1

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Carboplatin is infused on Day 1 of each 28-day cycle. The team selects the dose using the prescribed AUC and kidney function. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerated; there is no fixed breast course length.

Tests and checks

Blood count, kidney and liver tests are checked before each cycle. The team confirms the kidney-function method, current AUC and previous dose changes. Report numbness, hearing changes, infection and bleeding. Extra blood-count checks on Days 13–15 and Day 21 in the first cycles check the lowest counts.

Between treatment visits

This is a relative reference, not an appointment calendar. Treatment days can change after clinical review. Symptom bands do not predict when an effect will occur.

A day without treatment is not automatically a safe day. Use the emergency contact when needed; do not move or restart doses yourself.
