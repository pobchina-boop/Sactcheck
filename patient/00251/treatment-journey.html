The whole treatment journey

Carboplatin · 28-day breast schedule: treatment and checks

Advanced breast cancer

Carboplatin is infused on Day 1 of each 28-day cycle. The team selects the dose using the prescribed AUC and kidney function. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerated; there is no fixed breast course length.

What each check is for

1Treatment and dose reviewBefore every administration

Blood count, kidney and liver tests are checked before each cycle. The team confirms the kidney-function method, current AUC and previous dose changes. Report numbness, hearing changes, infection and bleeding. Extra blood-count checks on Days 13–15 and Day 21 in the first cycles check the lowest counts.

Ask / act: The treating team confirms results, dose and suitability; this map does not authorise treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00251 · SMPC

2Support and symptomsThroughout treatment, including days at home

Contact the oncology emergency service immediately for fever or shaking chills, sudden illness, bleeding, new breathlessness, chest pain or severe allergy. Report reduced urine, hearing changes and new numbness promptly. Do not wait for the next visit.

Ask / act: Keep a written medicine plan and urgent-contact route.

Sources: NCCP 00251 · ORAL-ANTIEMETIC · NCIS

3Response and continuationAt agreed clinical review points

Aim to slow or shrink metastatic breast cancer and help symptoms, balancing possible benefit, side effects and treatment burden.

Ask / act: Discuss benefit, toxicity and priorities before continuing or changing treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00251 · TRIAL

At the next review, ask

  • Which test is expected next, who arranges it, and who explains the result?
  • Who should I contact if an expected appointment has not arrived?
