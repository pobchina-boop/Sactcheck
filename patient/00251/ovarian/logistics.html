Preparing for treatment visits

Carboplatin every four weeks for ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

One infusion on day 1 every 28 days (four weeks). Bring your written next appointment date. Your team plans the number of cycles and reviews response, blood results and treatment tolerance.

On the day

Carboplatin is given into a vein over about 30 minutes on day 1 of each 28-day cycle. Blood checks and anti-sickness medicine add time to the visit. The team works out your dose from kidney function and a prescribed target exposure.

Between visits

Use the recovery days to take prescribed medicines and note symptoms. Contact the 24-hour number for urgent problems.

Write down your next appointments

  • Mention previous platinum treatment or allergy, bleeding, infections and medicines that may affect the kidneys.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology line for fever, chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting or markedly reduced urine. Tell the nurse immediately about itching, wheeze, throat tightness or faintness during treatment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
