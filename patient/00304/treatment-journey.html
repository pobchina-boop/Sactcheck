The whole treatment journey

Carboplatin and paclitaxel · lung cancer: treatment and checks

Standalone chemotherapy · adjuvant or advanced non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC)

Both medicines on Day 1, repeated every 21 days for four planned cycles, unless cancer progresses or side effects require a change. This standalone plan does not include nivolumab or radiotherapy.
