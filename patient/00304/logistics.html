Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm blood tests, premedication instructions, transport and urgent contacts.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Paclitaxel is given into a vein over three hours, followed by carboplatin over 30 minutes. Blood review, preparation, prevention medicines and observation make the visit longer.

STEP 3

Access

Treatment uses a suitable IV line. A port may be considered individually; no take-home infusion pump is part of this regimen.

STEP 4

At home

Take allergy-prevention and anti-sickness medicines exactly as prescribed. The team combines steroid instructions into one plan; do not add leftover tablets or miss a premedication without telling the team.

STEP 5

Review

Confirm the next appointment and individual scan plan before leaving.
