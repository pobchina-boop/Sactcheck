Treatment context
After surgery, treatment aims to reduce the chance of lung cancer returning. For advanced lung cancer, it aims to control cancer and help symptoms. Your team confirms which aim applies to you.
Schedule
Both medicines on Day 1, repeated every 21 days for four planned cycles, unless cancer progresses or side effects require a change. This standalone plan does not include nivolumab or radiotherapy.
