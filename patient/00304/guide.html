My treatment at a glance · National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00304

Carboplatin and paclitaxel · lung cancer

After surgery, treatment aims to reduce the chance of lung cancer returning. For advanced lung cancer, it aims to control cancer and help symptoms. Your team confirms which aim applies to you.

Your treatment plan

Both medicines on Day 1, every 21 days (three weeks), for four planned cycles.

  1. Before startingConfirm why treatment is being offered, blood tests, medicines and whom to call.
  2. Four treatment cyclesOne treatment visit every three weeks, with time at home between visits. The team may adjust or delay doses.
  3. After the courseReview recovery and agree the next steps. Other treatment or scans follow your own plan.

Before and between visits

  • Bring your medicine list and report new symptoms.
  • Confirm blood tests and your allergy-prevention medicines.
  • Keep the daytime and out-of-hours numbers with you.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately for temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions.

Cannot reach the team? Seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.
