Carboplatin and paclitaxel · lung cancer

After surgery, treatment aims to reduce the chance of lung cancer returning. For advanced lung cancer, it aims to control cancer and help symptoms. Your team confirms which aim applies to you.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Both medicines on Day 1, repeated every 21 days for four planned cycles, unless cancer progresses or side effects require a change. This standalone plan does not include nivolumab or radiotherapy.

Tests and checks

Blood count, kidney and liver tests before starting and before each cycle. Tell the team about numbness, tingling, hearing change, infection or bleeding before every treatment. Scan timing follows your individual plan.

Between treatment visits

Relative cycle days, not guaranteed symptom or recovery dates.

Call your oncology team immediately for fever, shivering, sudden illness, new breathing difficulty, chest pain, unusual bleeding, severe abdominal pain or inability to drink. Report new numbness, weakness or hearing change promptly. Follow the unit’s emergency instructions and do not wait for the next visit.
