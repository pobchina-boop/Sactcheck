Patient portalComplete reference
Advanced MET exon 14 skipping lung cancer NCCP 00823a covers tepotinib on its own for adults with advanced non-small cell lung cancer carrying a confirmed MET exon 14 skipping change, after immunotherapy and/or platinum chemotherapy. It is not a first-line or adjuvant treatment guide.
NCCP 00823 · version 2

Tepotinib monotherapy

The usual starting dose is 450 mg once daily: two 225 mg tablets taken together with food. Treatment is continuous, without a planned rest week, while it is helping. Follow your prescribed dose; the team may use one 225 mg tablet or pause treatment for side effects.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact the oncology team urgently for new or worsening cough, breathlessness or fever; marked swelling or sudden weight gain; severe diarrhoea, repeated vomiting or inability to drink; yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or severe abdominal pain; or palpitations or fainting. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or swelling of the mouth or throat needs emergency help. Use your unit’s temperature and 24-hour contact instructions.
