Advanced MET exon 14 skipping lung cancer National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00823a covers tepotinib on its own for adults with advanced non-small cell lung cancer carrying a confirmed MET exon 14 skipping change, after immunotherapy and/or platinum chemotherapy. It is not a first-line or adjuvant treatment guide.
NCCP 00823 · version 2

Tepotinib monotherapy

The usual starting dose is 450 mg once daily: two 225 mg tablets taken together with food. Treatment is continuous, without a planned rest week, while it is helping. Follow your prescribed dose; the team may use one 225 mg tablet or pause treatment for side effects.

What each check is for

1Confirm eligibilityBefore the first dose

Treatment depends on a specific tumour alteration and previous treatment.

Ask / act: Document the NCCP 00823a indication and agreed prescription.

Sources: NCCP 00823

2Keep tests and symptoms connectedAt scheduled reviews and between visits

Blood tests can change before symptoms and symptoms can occur between appointments.

Ask / act: Confirm who orders tests and reviews results; give an urgent contact plan.

Sources: NCCP 00823 · SMPC

3Possible interstitial lung disease (ILD) or liver injuryAt any time

Suspected lung inflammation and significant liver abnormalities can require an immediate hold.

Ask / act: Urgent clinical assessment and an authorised treatment decision.

Sources: NCCP 00823 · SMPC

4Benefit or toxicity changesAt treatment reviews

There is no fixed chemotherapy course length.

Ask / act: Agree continuation, an authorised hold/restart, or the next treatment plan.

Sources: NCCP 00823 · SMPC

At the next review, ask
