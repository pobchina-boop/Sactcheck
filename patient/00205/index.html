Treatment · Day 1Day 1

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin on Day 1 every 28 days, typically for six cycles or until progression or unacceptable toxicity. The team confirms the intended duration and reviews benefit.

Tests and checks

full blood count (FBC), renal and liver profile before every 28-day cycle; assess hands/feet, mouth, infection and symptoms. Perform regular electrocardiogram (ECG) monitoring and left ventricular ejection fraction (heart pumping measurement) (LVEF) assessment according to risk and cumulative exposure. Baseline ECG and echo/multigated acquisition (heart pumping) scan (MUGA), regular ECG and risk-based LVEF checks throughout treatment; mandatory LVEF before further administration beyond the source cumulative threshold.

Between treatment visits

Treatment markers show the planned days. Symptom bands show broad possibilities, not measured risk curves or guaranteed symptom-free days.

Fever, shivers or suddenly feeling unwell needs prompt advice on any day, including planned treatment-free weeks.
