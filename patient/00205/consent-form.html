Treatment context
The aim is to control the cancer and its symptoms. The team reviews benefit, side effects and your priorities before continuing.
Schedule
Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin on Day 1 every 28 days, typically for six cycles or until progression or unacceptable toxicity. The team confirms the intended duration and reviews benefit.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin

Liposomal chemotherapy
Painful red palms / soles (hand-foot syndrome)
Mouth soreness or ulcers affecting eating and drinking
Low blood counts, infection, fatigue and nausea
Heart damage and infusion reactions; risk depends partly on previous treatment

Discuss the exact indication, expected benefit, alternatives, uncertainty, serious and lasting risks, contraception and fertility, venous access, monitoring and the urgent contact plan. This is the pegylated liposomal formulation (for example Caelyx), not conventional doxorubicin or non-pegylated Myocet. These formulations and doses must not be interchanged.
