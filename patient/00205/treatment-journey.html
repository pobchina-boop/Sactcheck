The whole treatment journey

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin · every 4 weeks: the treatment pathway

Source-defined advanced breast, ovarian, endometrial cancer or soft-tissue sarcoma

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin on Day 1 every 28 days, typically for six cycles or until progression or unacceptable toxicity. The team confirms the intended duration and reviews benefit.

What each check is for

1BloodsFBC, renal and liver profile before starting and before each 28-day cycle. Check mouth soreness, palms / soles and recovery from earlier toxicity before each dose.

Results and symptoms guide treatment safety.

Sources: NCCP 00205

2Heart checksECG is part of baseline and regular monitoring. The NCCP source advises frequent ECG monitoring; the team should specify when it is due rather than assuming an arbitrary cycle interval. Baseline echo / MUGA is required. Repeat during treatment as clinically indicated, and before each additional administration that exceeds a lifetime cumulative anthracycline exposure of 450 mg/m². Prior anthracyclines count; this threshold is not an automatic treatment stop.

ECG and echo answer different questions.

Sources: NCCP 00205 · SMPC

3Response reviewFor metastatic breast cancer, ESMO recommends response evaluation generally every 2–4 months, adjusted to disease, treatment and symptoms. Agree the scan type, due window and result-review appointment; symptoms can prompt earlier assessment. For the other cancer indications on this card, use the cancer-specific monitoring plan; the breast interval is not transferred automatically.
