The whole treatment journey

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin · every 4 weeks: treatment and checks

Source-defined advanced breast, ovarian, endometrial cancer or soft-tissue sarcoma

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin on Day 1 every 28 days, typically for six cycles or until progression or unacceptable toxicity. The team confirms the intended duration and reviews benefit.

2Benefit and continuationAt agreed clinical review points

In a study in metastatic breast cancer, this liposomal formulation controlled cancer about as well as conventional doxorubicin and caused fewer heart problems, but more hand–foot and mouth problems. It can still affect the heart, and personal benefit is uncertain.

Ask / act: Discuss benefit, toxicity and priorities before continuing or changing treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00205 · RETROSPECTIVE-TRIAL
