Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine premedicines are specified. Low-risk antiemetic support is separate. Infusion reactions may require a different supervised plan for future doses.
  • This is the pegylated liposomal formulation (for example Caelyx), not conventional doxorubicin or non-pegylated Myocet. These formulations and doses must not be interchanged.
  • Assess G-CSF according to individual risk and the exact source; it is not automatically prescribed by this template.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
