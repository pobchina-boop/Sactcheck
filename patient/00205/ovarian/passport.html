My questions and next steps

  • What should I check before treatment? Bring your medicines list and details of earlier anthracycline treatment. Tell the team about heart disease or breathlessness.
  • What should I watch for at home? Avoid friction, heat and tight shoes if your palms or soles become tender. Call early for peeling skin or mouth pain that affects eating.
  • Why is this being offered? Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin is being offered to slow the growth of advanced ovarian cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue.
