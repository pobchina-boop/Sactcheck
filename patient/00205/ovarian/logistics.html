Preparing for treatment visits

PLD after platinum treatment for advanced ovarian cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

One infusion on day 1 of each 28-day cycle. Six cycles are typical. Your consultant may continue treatment while it helps and side effects remain manageable.

At the day unit

The team uses the pegylated liposomal formulation and starts the first infusion slowly. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, chest tightness, back pain or breathing difficulty.

What to bring

Bring your medicines list and details of earlier anthracycline treatment. Tell the team about heart disease or breathlessness.

Write down your next appointments

  • Bring your medicines list and details of earlier anthracycline treatment. Tell the team about heart disease or breathlessness.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, chills or sudden illness need an immediate call. Report new breathlessness, ankle swelling or chest pain promptly. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
