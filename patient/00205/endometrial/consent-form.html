Treatment and purpose

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin is being offered to slow the growth of recurrent or advanced endometrial cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue. Recurrent or advanced endometrial cancer.

Treatment schedule. One infusion on day 1 of each 28-day cycle. Six cycles are typical. Your consultant may continue treatment while it helps and side effects remain manageable.

Treatment discussion and decision

PLD for recurrent or advanced endometrial cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Some participants with persistent or recurrent endometrial cancer had a partial response to PLD. This small single-arm study cannot establish a survival advantage or predict an individual response.

Recurrent or advanced endometrial cancer. Discuss expected benefit, alternatives, side effects and the option of symptom-directed care. Record the individual decision and any off-label discussion. Endometrial cancer is listed by NCCP and falls outside the licensed indications for Caelyx. Explain and document this according to local policy.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Fever, chills or sudden illness need an immediate call. Report new breathlessness, ankle swelling or chest pain promptly. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
