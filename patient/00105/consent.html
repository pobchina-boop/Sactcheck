1. Treatment and purpose

Ipilimumab alone — advanced melanoma · NCCP 00105 v7

Ipilimumab by IV infusion once every 21 days, up to four doses. Complete within 16 weeks of the first dose, whichever limit comes first.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Ipilimumab · CTLA-4 checkpoint immunotherapy

Report diarrhoea, blood in stools, severe headache, unusual weakness or spreading rash promptly.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the exact diagnosis and treatment intent, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives, regimen-specific risks, tests, fertility, practical burdens and your preferences. This guide supports the consent conversation and does not replace it. This guide describes ipilimumab monotherapy at 3 mg/kg. It is not the nivolumab combination and does not automatically lead to nivolumab maintenance. A four-dose treatment course does not mean immune risks end after dose four.
