Your testing and review plan

A reference cycle is not a personalised calendar. Tests listed as symptom-triggered or individual are not presented as mandatory tests at every visit. No universal imaging or tumour-marker interval is invented.

The whole treatment journey

Ipilimumab alone — advanced melanoma: treatment and checks

Advanced melanoma

Ipilimumab by intravenous (IV) infusion once every 21 days, up to four doses. Complete within 16 weeks of the first dose, whichever limit comes first.
