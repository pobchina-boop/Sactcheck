Ipilimumab alone — advanced melanoma

To help immune cells control advanced melanoma using ipilimumab alone; the planned infusion course is limited, but immune effects and follow-up continue beyond it.

Advanced melanoma · NCCP 00105 v7 · Document revised 6 November 2026

This guide describes ipilimumab monotherapy at 3 mg/kg. It is not the nivolumab combination and does not automatically lead to nivolumab maintenance. A four-dose treatment course does not mean immune risks end after dose four.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Ipilimumab

Ipilimumab blocks CTLA-4, one of the signals that normally restrain T cells. This may help immune cells attack melanoma. The same immune activity can inflame healthy organs, sometimes after the last infusion; some hormone problems need lasting treatment.

Getting ready for treatment visits

Consent discussion aid

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: Treatment day

The source dose is ipilimumab 3 mg/kg over 90 minutes on day 1 of each 21-day cycle, with observation for one hour after the infusion. Allow additional time for symptom review, blood results and preparation. Report any previous infusion reaction before treatment.

What to do: Report symptoms and previous reactions before treatment.

Day 2–21: Between treatment visits

Immunotherapy can inflame healthy organs during treatment or after it stops. Contact the oncology team promptly for new or worsening diarrhoea, blood or mucus in stools, abdominal pain, cough or breathlessness, yellow skin or eyes, severe headache, vision changes, marked thirst, unusual weakness or a spreading rash. Tell every treating clinician that you are receiving or have received immunotherapy.

What to do: Do not wait for the next appointment to report urgent symptoms.

Support at home

Supportive medicines and symptom plan

NCCP classifies ipilimumab as low emetogenic risk; anti-sickness treatment is individual. Routine infusion premedication is not usually required, but previous reactions may change the plan. Do not start steroids or alter prescribed steroid tapers or hormone replacement without the team.

Skin and daily care

Use gentle skin care and sun protection. Report rash and itching early. Spreading rash, skin pain, blisters, peeling or sores affecting the mouth, eyes or genitals need urgent assessment. Continue melanoma skin self-checks and planned skin reviews.

Fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding

Discuss pregnancy prevention, fertility and breastfeeding before treatment. Effective contraception and the duration after the last dose must be confirmed against the current ipilimumab product information and your written plan; this guide does not borrow the nivolumab five-month interval. Tell the team promptly if pregnancy is possible.
