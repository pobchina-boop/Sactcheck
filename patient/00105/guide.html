Ipilimumab alone — advanced melanoma

To help immune cells control advanced melanoma using ipilimumab alone; the planned infusion course is limited, but immune effects and follow-up continue beyond it.

Advanced melanoma · NCCP 00105 v7 · Document revised 6 November 2026

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

This guide describes ipilimumab monotherapy at 3 mg/kg. It is not the nivolumab combination and does not automatically lead to nivolumab maintenance. A four-dose treatment course does not mean immune risks end after dose four.

My overall treatment

Ipilimumab by IV infusion once every 21 days, up to four doses. Complete within 16 weeks of the first dose, whichever limit comes first.

  1. Treatment courseUp to four infusions, every 21 days; maximum 16 weeks from first dose.

What to watch for

You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

A picture of side effects

Match each symptom box to its numbered body location. Tell your team about new symptoms.

1Head / hormones
Immunotherapy

Severe headache, dizziness, profound tiredness, thirst or altered vision can signal pituitary, adrenal, thyroid or glucose problems. Symptoms may need urgent hormone tests and treatment.

3Heart
Immunotherapy

Rare heart inflammation can cause chest pain, palpitations, faintness or breathlessness. Severe chest pain, collapse or major breathing difficulty is an emergency.

5Liver
Immunotherapy

Liver inflammation may first appear in blood tests. Yellow eyes or skin, dark urine, severe nausea or right-sided abdominal pain also need prompt review.

7Nerves / muscles
Immunotherapy

New weakness, drooping eyelids, double vision, numbness or difficulty walking, swallowing or breathing may be a rare nerve or muscle effect. Seek urgent help; do not wait for the next visit.

9Eyes
Immunotherapy

Eye pain, redness, light sensitivity or changed vision can indicate inflammation. Get prompt assessment; do not assume it is ordinary tiredness.

Immunotherapy

New diarrhoea, blood or mucus, severe cramps or abdominal pain can mean bowel inflammation. Record changes from your usual pattern and call early; severe pain or bleeding needs urgent assessment.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

Immunotherapy can inflame healthy organs during treatment or after it stops. Contact the oncology team promptly for new or worsening diarrhoea, blood or mucus in stools, abdominal pain, cough or breathlessness, yellow skin or eyes, severe headache, vision changes, marked thirst, unusual weakness or a spreading rash. Tell every treating clinician that you are receiving or have received immunotherapy.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
