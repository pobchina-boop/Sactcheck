Cabozantinib for kidney cancer

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00518 · Source-reconciled feasibility draft; independent clinician, pharmacy and patient review pending.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney, liver and bone profile, blood glucose, urine protein, thyroid tests, blood pressure, heart tracing (electrocardiogram (ECG)) and dental examination.

STEP 2

Dose and timing

The usual monotherapy starting dose is 60 mg once daily. Take no food for two hours before and one hour after the dose. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerable, with no planned week off. Combination treatment with nivolumab uses a different regimen.

STEP 3

Practical care

Swallow the tablet whole. If the next dose is less than 12 hours away, skip the missed dose; never double up. Cabometyx tablets and Cometriq capsules are not interchangeable. Avoid grapefruit and check all medicines, supplements and herbal products.

STEP 4

Support medicines

Arrange the baseline dental assessment and tell your dentist about treatment. No routine infusion premedication is required. Report diarrhoea and painful palms/soles early. Confirm your prescribed symptom medicines and blood-pressure plan. Tell the team before surgery or dental procedures: treatment usually needs withholding at least 28 days beforehand, with restart only after review of healing.

STEP 5

Before leaving

Confirm your next tests, dose, review date and verified daytime/out-of-hours contact numbers.
