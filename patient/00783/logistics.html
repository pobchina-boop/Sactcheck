Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Confirm the aim, DPD result, test appointments, prescribed supportive medicines and working urgent contact numbers. Bring your full medicines list, including supplements.

STEP 2

Line, pump and disconnection

Agree the access device and care training. The pump runs for 48 hours; confirm the disconnection appointment from its connection time. Do not alter settings, disconnect or reconnect it unless specifically trained and instructed.

STEP 3

If the plan changes

The team confirms the next treatment, blood-test and supportive-medicine dates after a delay or dose change. Do not use a previous calendar as a prescription.

STEP 4

Contacts, paper copies and QR

The example telephone numbers on the wallet card are placeholders. Your team must replace them with working daytime and out-of-hours numbers before patient use. Keep the card with you; the QR opens the regimen information, not your confidential medical record.
