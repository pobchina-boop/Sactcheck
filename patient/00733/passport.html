Fortnightly cetuximab + modified FOLFOX-6

A record of what you experience

One cycle per page. Each medicine has its own symptom table. Mark the worst symptoms since your last review, when they occurred and what they stopped you doing. Overlapping symptoms can be recorded under more than one medicine; you do not have to identify the cause.

These are patient-language severity prompts, not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE grading tool. Your team assigns the clinical grade. Serious symptoms override the columns: call without waiting to decide a severity level.

Cetuximab on Day 1. Day 1: oxaliplatin, folinic acid and fluorouracil, then a 46-hour fluorouracil pump; planned disconnection around Day 3. A repeating 14-day cycle. The number of cycles depends on benefit, side effects and your treatment plan.

The four pages are reusable recording sheets, not a prescribed four-cycle course. Ask for additional sheets as needed.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
