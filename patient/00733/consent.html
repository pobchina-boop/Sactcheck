1. Treatment and purpose

Fortnightly cetuximab + modified FOLFOX-6 · NCCP 00733 v2a

Cetuximab on Day 1. Day 1: oxaliplatin, folinic acid and fluorouracil, then a 46-hour fluorouracil pump; planned disconnection around Day 3. A repeating 14-day cycle. The number of cycles depends on benefit, side effects and your treatment plan.

2. Risks and supportive care

Cetuximab · EGFR-targeted antibody

Report rash or sore nails early. Tell the nurse immediately about infusion symptoms. Eye pain, new breathing problems or severe diarrhoea need prompt advice. Blood tests check minerals during treatment and for two months afterwards.

Oxaliplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Cold can trigger tingling or throat discomfort. Tell the team about persistent numbness, difficulty with buttons or walking, or infusion symptoms. New breathing difficulty always needs assessment.

Folinic acid · Chemotherapy modulator

The combined treatment can increase mouth soreness and diarrhoea. Report these promptly. Rare allergic reactions can occur.

Fluorouracil · Chemotherapy

Diarrhoea, mouth sores and skin soreness may become serious. Chest pain during the pump infusion needs emergency assessment. Unexpected severe early toxicity can occur even after DPD testing.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the treatment aim, alternatives, expected benefit, serious and lasting risks, fertility/pregnancy precautions and the contact plan. Confirm RAS selection for colorectal treatment, infusion premedication, skin care and post-treatment mineral checks. Explain DPD testing and the exact pump schedule.
