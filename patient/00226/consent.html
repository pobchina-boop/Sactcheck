1. Treatment and purpose

Weekly paclitaxel · NCCP 00226 v9

Four weekly infusion visits within each 28-day cycle. The team reviews safety before each planned dose. Day 22 is omitted only if the three-visit schedule is prescribed. Follow your confirmed appointments and prescription.

2. Risks and supportive care

Paclitaxel · Taxane chemotherapy

Report tingling or numbness before the next weekly dose, especially if it affects walking or hand use. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, chest or throat tightness, itching, dizziness or breathing changes during infusion.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Confirm the indication, weekly schedule, hypersensitivity premedication, neuropathy assessment and any required off-label-use discussion.

3. Understanding and next steps

Consent is a conversation and can be reconsidered. Tick boxes record discussion topics only; they do not establish capacity, consent or authorisation.
