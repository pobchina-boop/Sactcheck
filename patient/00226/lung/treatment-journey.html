Weekly paclitaxel · relapsed or refractory SCLC: treatment and checks

Lung · relapsed / refractory SCLC

Paclitaxel 80 mg/m² is infused on Days 1, 8, 15 and 22 of a 28-day cycle. The consultant may instead prescribe Days 1, 8 and 15. Each planned weekly dose needs review. Continue while helping and tolerated; NCCP does not set a fixed cycle cap.

What each check is for

1Safety reviewBefore each weekly treatment

FBC, kidney and liver profile at baseline and before each treatment; NCCP also explicitly lists Day 8 FBC. Check neuropathy, daily function, infection and previous infusion reactions before every dose. Confirm whether Day 22 is prescribed.

Ask / act: The team reviews results, current symptoms and the prescription.

Sources: NCCP 00226 · SMPC

2Benefit and treatment choiceAt consultant-agreed response reviews

A small uncontrolled study found some tumour responses to weekly paclitaxel in previously treated SCLC. The study used six weekly doses followed by two weeks without treatment, which differs from this NCCP schedule. It cannot establish a survival advantage over other treatment or supportive care.

Ask / act: Discuss continuing, changing or stopping treatment according to benefit, toxicity and priorities.

Sources: NCCP 00226 · TRIAL
