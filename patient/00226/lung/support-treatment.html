Additional phase-specific support

  • NCCP first-dose suggested premedication: dexamethasone 8 mg IV, chlorphenamine 10 mg IV and famotidine 20 mg IV 30 minutes before infusion.
  • From Day 8, NCCP permits omission of routine dexamethasone; previous reaction or higher risk requires consultant-directed adjustment. Famotidine omission is also a consultant/local-policy decision with close observation.
  • The actual conventional product SmPC uses a different generic premedication schedule. Reconcile the exact product and local protocol; no automatic patient-led omission or switch to nab-paclitaxel.
  • Consider analgesia for muscle/joint aches; review neuropathy before every dose and growth-factor support when indicated.
  • Low emetic risk: prevention and rescue medicines remain separate from reaction premedication. Confirm individual suitability, interactions and duration; this reference is not a prescription.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
