Choose your prescribed oral etoposide schedule

These are alternative schedules, not consecutive phases. Do not choose by convenience: confirm the exact prescribed schedule with your team.

Oral etoposide · Days 1–7 twice daily

Take your prescribed etoposide capsules twice daily on Days 1–7. Days 8–21 have no planned etoposide. The source cycle is 21 days; continue only while helping and tolerable, after team review.
