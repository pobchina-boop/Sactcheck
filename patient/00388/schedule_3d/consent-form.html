Treatment context
This treatment aims to slow or shrink extensive small-cell lung cancer when intravenous or combination chemotherapy is unsuitable. Benefit varies; your team explains the aim for you.
Schedule
Take your prescribed etoposide capsules twice daily on Days 1–3. Days 4–21 have no planned etoposide. The source cycle is 21 days; continue only while helping and tolerable, after team review.
