The whole treatment journey

Nivolumab + ipilimumab · colorectal after chemotherapy: treatment and checks

MSI-H/dMMR metastatic colorectal cancer after fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy

Cycles 1–4: nivolumab 3 mg/kg, then ipilimumab 1 mg/kg, on Day 1 every 21 days. The first nivolumab-only dose is due three weeks after the final combination dose, subject to clinical review. Nivolumab alone: 240 mg IV or 600 mg under the skin every two weeks, or 1200 mg under the skin every four weeks.
