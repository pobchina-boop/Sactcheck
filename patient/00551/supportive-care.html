Nivolumab + ipilimumab · colorectal after chemotherapy

Colorectal treatment setting MSI-H/dMMR metastatic colorectal cancer after fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy. Other nivolumab/ipilimumab regimens have different doses, routes and timing.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Routine infusion premedication is not usually required. Antiemetics above are a reference requiring individual/local prescription; do not add prophylactic corticosteroids automatically.
  • Carry the immunotherapy alert card and report bowel, lung, liver, kidney, hormonal, skin, neurological or cardiac symptoms promptly during and after treatment.
  • full blood count (FBC), renal/liver tests, glucose and thyroid function before each cycle; cortisol as clinically indicated. Maintain follow-up for delayed immune toxicity.
  • Management uses dose delay or discontinuation, not routine dose reduction. High-dose steroids for immune toxicity require a separately prescribed treatment and taper plan.
  • Confirm nivolumab first, separate infusion sets/filters and source-specific observation. The weight-based 00551 regimen and flat-dose 00900 regimen are not interchangeable.
  • If the team changes the interval between nivolumab-only doses, they will give you a new appointment plan: the first four-weekly dose is two weeks after the last two-weekly dose, or the first two-weekly dose is four weeks after the last four-weekly dose. Do not change the schedule yourself. This does not change which formulations are permitted for your colorectal treatment.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.

Cycle 5 onwards: prescribed nivolumab monotherapy schedule

Download this phase PDF · Open printable script

Active medicines: Nivolumab

Emetogenic risk: Minimal

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine antiemetic prophylaxis or infusion premedication; prescribe symptom relief individually.
  • Carry the immunotherapy alert card and report bowel, lung, liver, kidney, hormonal, skin, neurological or cardiac symptoms promptly during and after treatment.
  • full blood count (FBC), renal/liver tests, glucose and thyroid function before each cycle; cortisol as clinically indicated. Maintain follow-up for delayed immune toxicity.
  • Management uses dose delay or discontinuation, not routine dose reduction. High-dose steroids for immune toxicity require a separately prescribed treatment and taper plan.
  • Cycles 1–4: nivolumab 3 mg/kg, then ipilimumab 1 mg/kg, on Day 1 every 21 days. The first nivolumab-only dose is due three weeks after the final combination dose, subject to clinical review. Nivolumab alone: 240 mg intravenous (IV) or 600 mg under the skin every two weeks, or 1200 mg under the skin every four weeks.
  • If the team changes the interval between nivolumab-only doses, they will give you a new appointment plan: the first four-weekly dose is two weeks after the last two-weekly dose, or the first two-weekly dose is four weeks after the last four-weekly dose. Do not change the schedule yourself. This does not change which formulations are permitted for your colorectal treatment.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
