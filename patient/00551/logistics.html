Nivolumab + ipilimumab · colorectal after chemotherapy

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00551 · Source reconciled; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Combination appointments

During the first four cycles, nivolumab is infused first over about 30 minutes, then ipilimumab over about 30 minutes. Allow extra time for checks and observation. Nivolumab alone: 240 mg intravenous (IV) or 600 mg under the skin every two weeks, or 1200 mg under the skin every four weeks.

STEP 2

Changing phase

The first nivolumab-only dose is due three weeks after the final combination dose, subject to clinical review. Nivolumab alone: 240 mg IV or 600 mg under the skin every two weeks, or 1200 mg under the skin every four weeks.

STEP 3

Tests and reviews

Before starting and before every cycle: blood count, kidney and liver profile, glucose and thyroid function. Cortisol is checked if clinically indicated. Disease assessment follows the consultant plan. Continue monitoring for late immune effects for at least five months after the final dose.

STEP 4

Alert card and home care

There is no take-home chemotherapy pump. Keep the card, medicine list, symptom record and urgent contact numbers accessible.

STEP 5

Treatment duration

Continue while helping and tolerable, until progression or unacceptable toxicity. This colorectal regimen does not set a fixed overall two-year limit.
