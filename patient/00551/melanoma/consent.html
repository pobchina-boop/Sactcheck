1. Treatment and purpose

Nivolumab 3 mg/kg + ipilimumab 1 mg/kg — advanced melanoma · NCCP 00551 v7a

NCCP identifies nivolumab 3 mg/kg plus ipilimumab 1 mg/kg for melanoma as an unlicensed dosing posology in Ireland, despite reimbursement. Your clinician should explain this, the available alternatives and uncertainties, document informed consent under hospital policy, and follow the hospital Drug and Therapeutics Committee or equivalent acknowledgement process. CheckMate 511 primarily compared safety; it did not prove that this schedule has equal survival benefit to nivolumab 1 mg/kg plus ipilimumab 3 mg/kg. If a dose is delayed or held, obtain a revised written plan. The first two-weekly maintenance dose starts three weeks after the last combination dose; the first four-weekly dose starts six weeks afterwards. Do not calculate a different start from the maintenance interval alone. Earlier immune toxicity and permanent-stop decisions still apply.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Nivolumab · PD-1 checkpoint immunotherapy

Carry the immunotherapy alert card and report new symptoms during treatment and after it stops.

Ipilimumab · CTLA-4 checkpoint immunotherapy

Report diarrhoea, blood in stools, severe headache, unusual weakness or spreading rash promptly.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list.
