The whole treatment journey

Nivolumab 3 mg/kg + ipilimumab 1 mg/kg — advanced melanoma: treatment and checks

Advanced melanoma

Up to four combination infusions, 21 days apart, then nivolumab alone every two or four weeks as prescribed; four stamps record combination doses only.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.
