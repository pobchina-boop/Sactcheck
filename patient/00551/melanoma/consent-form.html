Treatment context
To help immune cells control advanced melanoma with two checkpoint medicines, followed when appropriate by nivolumab alone. The chosen dose combination has specific licensing and evidence limitations to discuss during consent.
Schedule
Up to four combination infusions, 21 days apart, then nivolumab alone every two or four weeks as prescribed; four stamps record combination doses only.

This guide supports the consent conversation and does not replace it. NCCP identifies nivolumab 3 mg/kg plus ipilimumab 1 mg/kg for melanoma as an unlicensed dosing posology in Ireland, despite reimbursement. Your clinician should explain this, the available alternatives and uncertainties, document informed consent under hospital policy, and follow the hospital Drug and Therapeutics Committee or equivalent acknowledgement process. CheckMate 511 primarily compared safety; it did not prove that this schedule has equal survival benefit to nivolumab 1 mg/kg plus ipilimumab 3 mg/kg.
