When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately for temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions.

Cannot reach the team? Seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Every 3 weeks: gemcitabine and cisplatin on Day 1, then gemcitabine alone on Day 8. Usually 4–6 cycles, with a review before each dose.

  1. PrepareAgree the treatment aim, planned number of cycles, blood tests and contact numbers.
  2. TreatmentDay 1: both medicines. Day 8: gemcitabine only. Then time at home before the next cycle.
  3. ReviewYour team checks symptoms, scans and side effects to agree what happens next.

Before and between visits

  • Bring your medicines list and report any new symptoms.
  • Confirm blood tests and the anti-sickness tablets to take at home.
  • Ask for the daytime and out-of-hours oncology numbers before leaving.
