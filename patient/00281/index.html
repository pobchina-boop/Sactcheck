Know the plan before your next visit.

Gemcitabine and cisplatin · lung cancer

These two chemotherapy medicines aim to slow or shrink advanced non-small-cell lung cancer and help control symptoms. Benefit is not guaranteed. Your team explains the aim for your own situation.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

A cycle lasts 21 days (3 weeks). Day 1: gemcitabine and cisplatin. Day 8: gemcitabine alone after separate blood tests and review. Days 9–21: no scheduled chemotherapy. The usual planned course is 4–6 cycles, with earlier changes for progression or unacceptable effects.

Tests and checks

Blood count, kidney and liver tests before treatment and on Day 1; blood count and creatinine again before Day 8. Symptoms are reviewed before each dose. Hearing tests are arranged when needed. Scans follow your consultant’s individual plan, not a fixed universal interval.

Between treatment visits

Relative cycle days, not guaranteed symptom or recovery dates.

Call your oncology team immediately for fever or suddenly feeling unwell, shaking chills, new breathlessness, chest pain, unusual bleeding, very little urine, severe rash or blistering, or vomiting that stops you keeping fluids down. Use your unit’s temperature threshold and emergency instructions. Do not wait until the next visit.
