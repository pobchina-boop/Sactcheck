How this medicine supports your treatment

Ceritinib blocks abnormal ALK signals that help some lung cancers grow. In advanced cancer after crizotinib, the aim is to control cancer and delay progression. The choice depends on your tumour test, previous treatment, other options and priorities.

ALK targeted therapy

Ceritinib

Blocks abnormal ALK growth signalling
ALK-positive tumour confirmed↓Growth signal blocked↓Cancer growth slowed

Ceritinib inhibits ALK signalling in a tumour with a confirmed ALK rearrangement. It can affect healthy tissues too, so blood tests and symptom checks continue even when treatment is taken at home.

Report breathing changes, fainting, jaundice, severe abdominal pain, persistent diarrhoea/vomiting or marked thirst promptly.

Taking ceritinib at home

Food changes absorption, so the dose and food instructions belong together.

Daily, missed or vomited doses

Take at the same time daily with food. Swallow capsules whole with water; do not chew or crush. Take a missed dose unless the next is due within 12 hours; ask your pharmacist if timing is unclear. Never double a dose. If you vomit, do not replace the dose; take the next at its usual time.

NCCP 00340 v4 · official regimen · EMA Zykadia product information, retrieved 29 October 2026

If you cannot eat

If illness prevents you eating with the dose, contact the team. Only the prescriber can arrange a different fasting dose. Do not alternate between fed and fasting schedules or change the dose yourself.

Tests and reviews

Baseline tests and ongoing checks do different jobs.

Your monitoring plan

Before starting: ALK test, blood count, kidney and liver tests, pancreatic enzymes, fasting glucose, ECG and symptom/medicine review. Blood count, kidneys and fasting glucose every 4 weeks; liver tests every 2 weeks for the first 3 months, then monthly. ECG after 1 month, then as needed. Extra heart, electrolyte, glucose or pancreatic tests may be needed for risks, symptoms or abnormal results; scans follow the consultant plan.

Support during treatment

Ask for help with symptoms, daily routines and practical concerns.

Prevent and treat nausea

Ceritinib has moderate-to-high oral sickness risk. Ask for daily prevention and rescue instructions. Your team must check heart-rhythm and medicine interactions when choosing anti-sickness treatment; do not add medicines or a steroid course yourself.

Check interactions

Avoid grapefruit or grapefruit juice and St John’s wort. Check prescription, pharmacy and herbal medicines before use, including heart medicines, antibiotics, antifungals, seizure treatments and acid-reducing medicines. Do not stop a prescribed medicine without advice.

EMA Zykadia product information, retrieved 29 October 2026

Pregnancy, fertility and feeding

Discuss pregnancy, fertility and breastfeeding before treatment. Women who could become pregnant need highly effective contraception during treatment and for 3 months afterwards; hormonal contraception may be less effective, so agree a suitable method. Do not breastfeed during this treatment. Fertility effects are uncertain; ask about preservation if relevant.

NCCP 00340 v4 · official regimen · EMA Zykadia product information, retrieved 29 October 2026

Diarrhoea, fluids and daily life

Use the agreed diarrhoea treatment promptly. Report persistent symptoms, poor intake or dehydration. Ask for help with appetite, fatigue and practical support; avoid driving or machinery if fatigue or visual symptoms affect safety.

Benefit and choices

A study after crizotinib and platinum chemotherapy found longer disease control with ceritinib than the chemotherapy used at that time. It tested an older fasting dose. A separate dose study supports taking the current lower dose with food; its main efficacy group had not had previous treatment. These studies cannot predict your outcome or rank all current treatment options.

Match evidence to your situation

ASCEND-5 involved previous crizotinib and platinum chemotherapy and an older fasting dose. ASCEND-8 supports the fed schedule but its main efficacy analysis was treatment-naive. Ask how these results fit your previous treatments and current alternatives.

ASCEND-5: post-chemotherapy/crizotinib phase III trial, Shaw 2017 · ASCEND-8: pharmacokinetic and food-effect study, Cho 2017 · ASCEND-8: primary efficacy and safety analysis, Cho 2019
