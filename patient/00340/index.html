Know the plan before your next visit.

Ceritinib monotherapy

Ceritinib blocks abnormal ALK signals that help some lung cancers grow. In advanced cancer after crizotinib, the aim is to control cancer and delay progression. The choice depends on your tumour test, previous treatment, other options and priorities.

My schedule at a glance

Your treatment pattern

Once daily with food; no rest week

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

The usual starting dose is 450 mg once daily with food, every day without a planned rest week. Follow your own prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed course length.

Tests and checks

Before starting: ALK test, blood count, kidney and liver tests, pancreatic enzymes, fasting glucose, electrocardiogram (ECG) and symptom/medicine review. Blood count, kidneys and fasting glucose every 4 weeks; liver tests every 2 weeks for the first 3 months, then monthly. ECG after 1 month, then as needed. Extra heart, electrolyte, glucose or pancreatic tests may be needed for risks, symptoms or abnormal results; scans follow the consultant plan.

Between treatment visits
