Advanced ALK-positive NSCLC after crizotinib Adults with ALK-positive advanced non-small-cell lung cancer previously treated with crizotinib: NCCP 00340a. This suite does not turn the broader first-line licence into an NCCP indication or funding approval. Confirm your treatment setting with the team.
NCCP 00340 · version 4

Ceritinib monotherapy

The usual starting dose is 450 mg once daily with food, every day without a planned rest week. Follow your own prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed course length.

Discussion aid: review with the patient and complete the approved local consent process.

Purpose and plan

□ Exact indication, treatment aim and uncertainty explained
□ Expected duration and review / switch points discussed
□ Alternatives and the option not to proceed discussed
□ Patient priorities, capacity and support needs explored

Practical safety

□ Medicine, route, schedule and missed-dose instructions
□ Food, interactions and safe handling reviewed
□ Pregnancy, contraception / fertility and breastfeeding
□ Monitoring, symptom reporting and contact plan

Selected material risks

□ Nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea
□ Liver injury
□ Lung inflammation
□ Heart rhythm changes and slow pulse
□ High blood glucose
□ Pancreatic inflammation
□ Poor appetite, fatigue and weight loss
□ Vision and kidney-test changes

Discuss the post-crizotinib setting, aim and uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, material risks, daily treatment and food burden, interactions, pregnancy/fertility/feeding, monitoring and your right to reconsider. This aid is not treatment authorisation.

Individual benefit, alternatives and concerns

Agreed checks and next review

Before starting: ALK test, blood count, kidney and liver tests, pancreatic enzymes, fasting glucose, ECG and symptom/medicine review. Blood count, kidneys and fasting glucose every 4 weeks; liver tests every 2 weeks for the first 3 months, then monthly. ECG after 1 month, then as needed. Extra heart, electrolyte, glucose or pancreatic tests may be needed for risks, symptoms or abnormal results; scans follow the consultant plan.
