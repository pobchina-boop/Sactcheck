Docetaxel + prednisolone every 21 days

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00546 · Source-reconciled feasibility draft; independent clinician, pharmacy and patient review pending.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests; review performance status, current medicines, infection, neuropathy and the treatment goal.

STEP 2

Your treatment visit

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² is infused over one hour on Day 1 every 21 days, with prednisolone 10 mg daily or 5 mg twice daily continuously. Continue while helpful and tolerable; there is no fixed course maximum in this source. Dexamethasone 8 mg by mouth at 12 hours, 3 hours and 1 hour before docetaxel. Tell the team if a dose is missed; an intravenous (IV) alternative is a consultant decision.

STEP 3

IV access

Treatment is given through a cannula or suitable established access. No take-home pump is part of this regimen. Report pain, burning, leakage or swelling immediately.

STEP 4

Between visits

Keep the exact prescribed steroid schedule. This castration-resistant regimen includes continuous prednisolone, unlike the separate 00203 hormone-sensitive service. Take prednisolone continuously as prescribed, with food. Do not stop suddenly when chemotherapy is delayed. Ask for a steroid alert card, an illness plan and advice if vomiting prevents you keeping tablets down.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next blood-test and treatment dates, support medicines and verified daytime/out-of-hours numbers.
