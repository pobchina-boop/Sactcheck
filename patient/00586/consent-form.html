Treatment context
Capecitabine tablets are given with pelvic radiotherapy for locally advanced rectal cancer. The tablets help the radiation treatment work; your team confirms how this fits with surgery and other treatment.
Schedule
Capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–5 of each 7-day cycle, alongside radiotherapy, for up to 5–6 cycles. Follow the written plan for treatment days and interruptions.
