Treatment context
Capecitabine tablets help pelvic radiotherapy work against locally advanced rectal cancer. Your team explains how this course fits with surgery and any other chemotherapy.
Schedule
Take capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–5 of each 7-day cycle with radiotherapy, for a maximum of 5–6 cycles. Days 6–7 are not routine tablet days. Follow the team’s written instructions if radiotherapy or tablets are interrupted.
