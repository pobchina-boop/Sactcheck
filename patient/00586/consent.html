1. Treatment and purpose

Capecitabine + rectal radiotherapy · NCCP 00586 v3

Capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–5 of each 7-day cycle, alongside radiotherapy, for up to 5–6 cycles. Follow the written plan for treatment days and interruptions.

Capecitabine is taken twice daily on Days 1–5. The weekly tablet break is different from finishing the whole course. Radiation appointments follow the centre’s plan. If a session changes or either treatment is paused, contact the team for the revised tablet instructions.

*The NCCP course is a maximum of 5–6 weekly cycles. The sixth week is shown as optional. Your radiation team confirms the actual number of sessions and any bank-holiday changes.

After the course: recovery and reassessment. Surgery or other chemotherapy, if planned, follows your individual multidisciplinary plan; no universal interval is implied.

2. Risks and supportive care

Capecitabine · Oral chemotherapy

Stop tablets and contact the team for significant diarrhoea, painful mouth ulcers, hand-foot soreness affecting activities, fever or dehydration. Chest pain needs urgent assessment. Do not restart until instructed.

Pelvic radiotherapy · Local radiation treatment

Tell the radiotherapy team about diarrhoea, pain, skin soreness or urinary changes. Discuss possible longer-term bowel, bladder, sexual and fertility effects before treatment.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Confirm DPD testing, tablet directions, missed-dose and stop-and-call instructions, kidney/liver and blood monitoring, interactions, fertility and contraception. Discuss radiotherapy-specific early and late effects and the separate radiation consent process.
