The whole treatment journey

FOLFOXIRI: treatment and checks

Metastatic colorectal cancer

Day 1: irinotecan, oxaliplatin and folinic acid, followed by a 48-hour fluorouracil pump. Repeat every 14 days while treatment is helping and side effects remain acceptable.

What each check is for

114-day treatment cycleDay ward → home pump → review

FOLFOXIRI combines three chemotherapy medicines with folinic acid to treat colorectal cancer that has spread. The aim is cancer control, symptom relief and, for selected people, a response that allows further local treatment after specialist review.

Ask / act: Confirm the phase, prescription, tests and supportive medicines before starting.
