Tests in your plan

Before treatment

WhenCystoscopy with biopsy or resection confirms diagnosis. Confirm healing after surgery, no recent bleeding or catheter trauma, and assess urine for infection. Tell the urologist about pregnancy, breastfeeding, allergies and bladder capacity problems.

Confirm diagnosis, suitability and the required safety checks.

During treatment and follow-up

WhenBefore treatment, urine dipstick and symptom assessment help check for infection; symptoms require urology review. Response assessment determines maintenance. If disease-free, cystoscopy with cytology is usually every three months for two years.

Detect toxicity and assess response with the appropriate tests.

The whole treatment journey

Gemcitabine + docetaxel bladder treatment: treatment and checks

High-risk non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer

Both medicines are given at each weekly induction visit for six weeks. Selected patients then receive treatment monthly for 18 months, beginning six weeks after the final induction dose. Maintenance depends on response and tolerability; it is not automatic.

Treat selected high-risk bladder cancer when BCG has not worked, cannot be tolerated or is unsuitable.

Ask / act: Contact your urology or oncology treatment team immediately for fever, chills, sudden illness, severe bladder pain, persistent bleeding or inability to pass urine. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or chest pain: call112/999.
