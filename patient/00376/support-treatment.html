NCCP 00376 · version 4

Exemestane · daily hormone therapy

Take exemestane once daily continuously, preferably after a meal. There is no planned week off. Confirm the prior-treatment and duration plan.

Clinician supportive-care discussion draft. This is not an issued prescription.

Minimal emetogenic risk

No routine prophylactic antiemetic, corticosteroid premedication or G-CSF order is populated for this endocrine monotherapy. Review persistent nausea or other symptoms and prescribe individually if needed.

Medicine-specific support

  • Tell the team if joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal dryness or sleep disturbance affects daily life. Bone thinning may cause no early symptoms, so keep the agreed bone-health reviews.
  • NCCP does not require a routine renal or hepatic dose reduction. This does not remove the need to assess organ dysfunction, interactions and tolerability.
  • Offer baseline DXA when starting an adjuvant aromatase inhibitor if not already receiving adjuvant bisphosphonates; assess fracture risk and relevant previous results. Under the 2025 joint guidance, patients with a T-score above −2.0 and no additional risk factors generally have risk and bone density reassessed every one to two years. Patients on bone-protective treatment generally have BMD reassessed about every two years. Fracture, substantial bone loss or a change in risk can require earlier review. The clinician selects the interval and any bone treatment; these are not automatic yearly scans for everyone.
  • Assess persistent symptoms and prescribe individually; no automatic prophylactic antiemetic, steroid or G-CSF.
