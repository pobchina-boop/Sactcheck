What each check is for

1Confirm the planBefore starting or changing treatment

Exemestane is used for selected postmenopausal hormone-sensitive breast cancer after surgery, as extended endocrine treatment or for advanced disease after earlier treatment. The team checks which previous endocrine medicine you received and why a switch or extension is appropriate. If switching after tamoxifen, the earlier years count towards the intended total treatment plan.

Ask / act: Confirm treatment setting, medicines, menopause and the planned review.

Sources: NCCP 00376

2Checks and symptomsAt the agreed review points

FBC, renal and liver profile, confirmation of menopause when uncertain, and bone / fracture assessment. Consider baseline vitamin D as specified by the product information. The NICE adjuvant baseline-DXA recommendation has an exception for patients already receiving adjuvant bisphosphonates. Review symptoms, adherence and bone health. Repeat bone density and lipids when indicated; assess other blood tests according to the clinical plan.

Ask / act: Agree who books tests, reviews results and responds to new symptoms.

Sources: NCCP 00376

3Review ongoing benefitAt setting-specific decision points

After early breast cancer treatment, endocrine therapy reduces the risk of recurrence. In advanced disease, the aim is to control cancer and symptoms. Your setting determines the duration and follow-up.

Ask / act: Review duration, alternatives and patient priorities; do not stop or extend treatment automatically.

Sources: NCCP 00376
