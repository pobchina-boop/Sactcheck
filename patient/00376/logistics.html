NCCP 00376 · version 4

Exemestane · daily hormone therapy

Take exemestane once daily continuously, preferably after a meal. There is no planned week off. Confirm the prior-treatment and duration plan.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Take the tablet after a meal. Skip a missed dose and continue at the next scheduled time; do not double the dose.

Take after food

A regular meal can anchor the daily dose. Do not compensate for a missed tablet with a double dose.

Count the whole endocrine course

In an adjuvant switch, the earlier tamoxifen years count towards the intended total. Switching medicines does not automatically restart a five-year clock.

Bone and vitamin D

Ask about baseline DXA, vitamin D assessment if appropriate, and the result-review plan. Supplements or antiresorptive drugs should follow the individual plan.

Medicine interactions

Strong enzyme-inducing medicines can reduce exposure. Ask the pharmacy to check all medicines and supplements before starting or changing them.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
