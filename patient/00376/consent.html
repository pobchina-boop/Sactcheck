Exemestane · daily hormone therapy

Take exemestane once daily continuously, preferably after a meal. There is no planned week off. Confirm the prior-treatment and duration plan.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

After early breast cancer treatment, endocrine therapy reduces the risk of recurrence. In advanced disease, the aim is to control cancer and symptoms. Your setting determines the duration and follow-up. Exemestane is used for selected postmenopausal hormone-sensitive breast cancer after surgery, as extended endocrine treatment or for advanced disease after earlier treatment. The team checks which previous endocrine medicine you received and why a switch or extension is appropriate. If switching after tamoxifen, the earlier years count towards the intended total treatment plan.

Material risks and support

  • Joint and muscle pain or stiffness
  • Hot flushes, sleep disturbance and fatigue
  • Vaginal dryness and sexual symptoms
  • Bone thinning and fractures
  • Lipid / cardiovascular risk changes
  • Uncommon liver abnormalities or serious allergic reactions

The kidney and liver advice for exemestane differs from some other aromatase inhibitors: a routine dose reduction is not specified, but organ problems still need clinical review. Tell the pharmacist about all medicines and supplements because some can make exemestane less effective. Do not increase the dose yourself. Confirm menopause and any separate ovarian-suppression plan.

Monitoring and understanding

FBC, renal and liver profile, confirmation of menopause when uncertain, and bone / fracture assessment. Consider baseline vitamin D as specified by the product information. The NICE adjuvant baseline-DXA recommendation has an exception for patients already receiving adjuvant bisphosphonates. Review symptoms, adherence and bone health. Repeat bone density and lipids when indicated; assess other blood tests according to the clinical plan.
