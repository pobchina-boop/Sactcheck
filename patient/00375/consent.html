Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin and trabectedin

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Control relapsed disease and delay further progression. Relapsed platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer.

Treatment schedule. You receive a steroid before pegylated liposomal doxorubicin, followed by trabectedin over three hours. Your team normally recommends a central line. Treatment repeats every 21 days while it helps and remains tolerable.

Regimen-specific side effects

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Hands and feetReport burning pain, redness, peeling or blisters early. Your team may delay or adjust treatment.
Mouth and blood countsReport mouth ulcers or difficulty eating. Fever or chills require an urgent call for possible infection.
Heart function and infusion reactionsReport ankle swelling, new breathlessness or difficulty lying flat. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, chest tightness or back pain during infusion.

Trabectedin

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Muscles and kidneysSevere muscle pain, weakness or dark urine can signal muscle breakdown. Call the oncology unit urgently.
Liver and blood countsAttend blood tests. Call urgently for jaundice, fever or chills. The prescribed steroid also helps protect your liver.
Heart and infusion siteReport swelling or new breathlessness. Tell the nurse at once if the line stings, hurts or leaks.

Treatment discussion and decision

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin and trabectedin

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Adding trabectedin to liposomal doxorubicin delayed progression compared with liposomal doxorubicin alone. The trial does not compare this combination directly with retreatment using platinum.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Call urgently for severe muscle pain, weakness or dark urine. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
