SACTCheck · NCCP 00375 v6a · Relapsed ovarian cancer

Your treatment journey

Pegylated liposomal doxorubicin and trabectedin

Control relapsed disease and delay further progression.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · two infusions

You receive a steroid before pegylated liposomal doxorubicin, followed by trabectedin over three hours. Your team normally recommends a central line. Treatment repeats every 21 days while it helps and remains tolerable.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count, bilirubin, liver enzymes, albumin, kidney function, creatine kinase and heart function.Bring details of earlier anthracyclines and heart problems.
First two cyclesCheck creatine kinase weekly for the first two cycles. Blood count, kidney and liver tests are checked before each cycle.Ask for the dates of blood tests between infusions.
Before later cyclesCheck creatine kinase at least once between later cycles. ECG and heart assessment by ECHO or MUGA are part of monitoring. ECHO or MUGA is due before every cycle.Report muscle pain with weakness or dark urine urgently.

Scan dates and tumour-marker testing are individualised by your oncology team.

At review visits

  • What happens at my next visit? Two infusions on day 1 every three weeks.
  • Which symptoms should I phone about? Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Call urgently for severe muscle pain, weakness or dark urine.
  • What should I plan before starting? Bring details of earlier anthracyclines and heart problems.
