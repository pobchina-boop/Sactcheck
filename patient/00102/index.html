When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Vemurafenib

Vemurafenib blocks the altered BRAF V600 protein that can drive melanoma-cell growth. A confirmed tumour BRAF V600 result is needed. Healthy tissues can also be affected; tablets need the same careful monitoring as treatment given in hospital.

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1–28: At home each day

The usual starting dose is 960 mg twice daily, about 12 hours apart; the prescribed dose may differ after review. Swallow tablets whole with water. Vemurafenib can be taken with or without food, but do not regularly take both daily doses on an empty stomach. Keep a consistent routine and follow the pharmacy label.

What to do: Take a missed dose only if at least four hours remain before the next dose. Otherwise skip it. Do not double a dose. If you vomit after taking it, do not take an extra dose; take the next dose at the usual time. Contact the team if vomiting continues or treatment has been held.

Support at home

Supportive medicines and symptom plan

This is a minimal-emetic-risk oral regimen in the NCCP plan. Anti-sickness medicine is chosen to match symptoms and interactions; do not add over-the-counter medicines without checking. Agree a hydration and diarrhoea plan, and report inability to drink or persistent symptoms.

Skin and daily care

Use broad-spectrum UVA/UVB sun protection, protective clothing and lip protection; the product information specifies SPF 30 or higher. Sun sensitivity can be marked. Check skin regularly for new growths or changes, and keep scheduled dermatology reviews.

Fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding

Use effective contraception during treatment and for at least six months after the final dose. Vemurafenib may reduce the effectiveness of hormonal contraception, so ask for an appropriate alternative or additional method. Discuss fertility and breastfeeding before treatment; tell the team immediately if pregnancy is possible.
