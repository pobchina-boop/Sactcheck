Vemurafenib alone — advanced melanoma

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking or receiving treatment

The usual starting dose is 960 mg twice daily, about 12 hours apart; the prescribed dose may differ after review. Swallow tablets whole with water. Vemurafenib can be taken with or without food, but do not regularly take both daily doses on an empty stomach. Keep a consistent routine and follow the pharmacy label.

STEP 2

Missed, vomited or held treatment

Take a missed dose only if at least four hours remain before the next dose. Otherwise skip it. Do not double a dose. If you vomit after taking it, do not take an extra dose; take the next dose at the usual time. Contact the team if vomiting continues or treatment has been held.

STEP 3

Appointments and supply

Before each visit, check four things: the blood-test booking, how you will get there, your medicine supply and any help you need. Ask who will review the results and tell you whether the next dose can go ahead.

STEP 4

Paper copies and contacts

Keep the handbook, short guide and treatment card in one place. Ask your team to fill in the real daytime and emergency numbers and print a replacement when the plan changes. A phone is optional.
