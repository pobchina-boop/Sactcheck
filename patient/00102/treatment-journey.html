Your testing and review plan

A reference cycle is not a personalised calendar. Tests listed as symptom-triggered or individual are not presented as mandatory tests at every visit. No universal imaging or tumour-marker interval is invented.

Report new or changing skin growths even when the melanoma is responding.Current Zelboraf product information calls for monthly skin checks during treatment and for up to six months after stopping, or until another anticancer therapy starts. Head-and-neck checks every three months and chest CT every six months during treatment screen for other cancers; these are distinct from the individual melanoma-response scan plan. Anal/pelvic checks are considered at baseline/end and when indicated. The whole treatment journey

Vemurafenib alone — advanced melanoma: treatment and checks

Advanced melanoma

Vemurafenib twice daily, about 12 hours apart, every day. A 28-day review cycle does not mean a planned tablet-free week.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.
