Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

Phesgo is one combined injection of pertuzumab and trastuzumab under the skin of the thigh. It is not two separate injections and is never given into a vein.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm blood results, cardiac checks, active phase, previous dual HER2 dose and the consent discussion.

STEP 2

The thigh injection

Loading/reloading 1200 mg/600 mg over 8 minutes with 30 minutes observation; maintenance 600 mg/600 mg over 5 minutes with 15 minutes observation. Alternate thighs; avoid sore, bruised or damaged skin.

STEP 3

No IV line for Phesgo itself

Phesgo is given under the skin and does not itself require a port, cannula or take-home pump. Other treatment may still require access.

STEP 4

Leaving the unit

Confirm supportive medicines, next visits and due tests. Injection time is shorter than the whole appointment; preparation, assessment and observation still take time.

Switching / a missed appointment

The team checks the last dual HER2 dose, including IV treatment: a gap of less than 6 weeks uses the maintenance dose; 6 weeks or longer needs reloading. A first Phesgo visit after IV treatment does not automatically require a loading dose.

An existing port

Ask the line team whether the port is still needed and how it will be cared for or removed. Removal is a separate procedure; do not adjust the device yourself.
