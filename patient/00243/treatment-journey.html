NCCP 00243a is previously treated ALK disease; 00243b is ROS1 disease; 00243c is first-line ALK disease. Confirm the exact indication and local access; a listed indication, licence and reimbursement are different facts. This guide does not cover paediatric lymphoma or inflammatory myofibroblastic tumour.
NCCP 00243 · version 7

Crizotinib monotherapy

The usual adult starting dose is 250 mg twice daily, every day, with no planned rest week. Use the strength and frequency on your own prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed course length.

What each check is for

1Eligibility and safe startBefore first dose

Ask / act: Confirm prescription, contraception/feeding plan, interaction review and contacts.

Sources: NCCP 00243 · SMPC

2Early liver surveillanceWeekly for first 2 months

Clinically important liver injury can develop early.

Ask / act: Arrange tests, identify reviewer and repeat sooner for abnormalities.

Sources: NCCP 00243 · SMPC

3Monthly and symptom-led checksThroughout treatment

Continuous tablets still require continuing clinical review.

Ask / act: Review benefit, adverse events, interactions and supply.

Sources: NCCP 00243 · SMPC

4Hold or stop safelyToxicity, progression or changed priorities

Dose changes can alter both capsule strength and frequency.

Ask / act: Give explicit new instructions and follow-up; only the prescriber authorises restart.

Sources: NCCP 00243 · SMPC
