Treatment context
Control the cancer and symptoms, with repeated review of benefit and tolerance.
Schedule
Lapatinib once daily on Days 1–21, continuously. Capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14, then no capecitabine on Days 15–21. Repeat every 21 days while beneficial and tolerated. The capecitabine rest week is not a lapatinib rest week.
