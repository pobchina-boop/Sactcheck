Pemetrexed + Carboplatin

Pemetrexed + Carboplatin is chemotherapy used for selected people with unresectable pleural mesothelioma or non-squamous non-small-cell lung cancer. The aim may be disease control or, within a separate combined preoperative plan, support for surgery. Ask your team to explain the expected benefit for your own diagnosis.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Both chemotherapy medicines are planned on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle. For pleural mesothelioma, the source describes up to 4–6 cycles. For advanced non-squamous non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC), it describes 4–6 combination cycles followed, when appropriate, by a separate pemetrexed maintenance plan (National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00222). The selected preoperative nivolumab combination uses three cycles and a separate immunotherapy plan (NCCP 00849). Your team confirms which course applies and may stop or change it earlier.

Between treatment visits

This is one reference cycle, not a prediction of when side effects will happen. Risks overlap and symptoms can persist after the last dose.

There are no guaranteed safe days. Follow the temperature threshold and emergency instructions given by your unit. Sudden illness, shaking chills or feeling very unwell need urgent advice even without a high temperature.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

Call urgently for fever or shaking chills, sudden illness, new or worsening breathlessness, chest pain, uncontrolled bleeding, severe or persistent diarrhoea, inability to drink, very little urine, a spreading or blistering rash, or swelling/wheeze during an infusion. Tell the team promptly about new tinnitus, hearing change, numbness, weakness or difficulty walking.
