NCCP 00619 · version 4a

Adjuvant abemaciclib + endocrine therapy

The usual abemaciclib starting dose is 150 mg twice daily; a lower dose or altered schedule may be prescribed. Abemaciclib is taken twice daily continuously for up to two years with endocrine therapy, unless recurrence or unacceptable toxicity requires an earlier change. A four-week dispensing block is not a rest cycle. Endocrine treatment normally continues beyond abemaciclib.

CDK4/6 inhibitor

Abemaciclib

Slows the cell-division checkpoint
CDK4/6 checkpoint↓Checkpoint inhibited↓Cell cycling slows

Abemaciclib inhibits CDK4 and CDK6 proteins. These proteins help cells move towards division. It is combined with endocrine treatment to reduce recurrence risk in eligible high-risk early breast cancer.

Start the prescribed diarrhoea rescue plan at the first loose stool and contact the team. Breathlessness, a swollen painful leg, fever or jaundice need prompt assessment.

Hormone-directed treatment

Your endocrine treatment

Reduces the hormone signal
Hormone signal↓Signal reduced or blocked↓HR-positive growth reduced

The chosen endocrine medicine either reduces oestrogen production or blocks its action. An aromatase inhibitor may need ovarian suppression before menopause. The exact drug and duration are prescribed separately.

Tell the team about joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal symptoms and mood changes. The bone-health and clot-risk discussion depends on the drug chosen.

Support throughout treatment

Diarrhoea rescue from the first loose stool

Have the prescribed antidiarrhoeal treatment available before starting. Begin the agreed plan at the first loose stool, increase fluids as advised and contact the team early; persistent symptoms or dehydration may require a treatment hold.

Nausea support

NCCP classifies abemaciclib as oral moderate–high emetic risk. Agree preventive or rescue antiemetics using individual risk and local oral-SACT policy.

Blood, liver and symptom checks

Attend early two-weekly then monthly checks even when tablets are taken at home. Seek prompt advice for infection, jaundice, new breathing symptoms or possible blood clots.

Endocrine and bone support

Discuss symptoms and bone protection for the actual endocrine partner. Ovarian suppression is required with an AI before menopause. Endocrine treatment normally continues after the two-year abemaciclib course.

Medicines and supply

Take tablets whole at the prescribed regular times. There is no routine week off. Check grapefruit/CYP3A interactions and do not double a missed or vomited dose.

Your practical treatment guide

When to get help

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Report new breathlessness, chest symptoms, severe diarrhoea, inability to drink, jaundice, a painful swollen leg or a severe rash promptly. You do not need to identify which medicine caused the symptom.
