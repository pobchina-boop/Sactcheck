NCCP 00619 · version 4a

Adjuvant abemaciclib + endocrine therapy

The usual abemaciclib starting dose is 150 mg twice daily; a lower dose or altered schedule may be prescribed. Abemaciclib is taken twice daily continuously for up to two years with endocrine therapy, unless recurrence or unacceptable toxicity requires an earlier change. A four-week dispensing block is not a rest cycle. Endocrine treatment normally continues beyond abemaciclib.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Take abemaciclib twice daily at regular times with or without food. Swallow whole; do not split, chew or crush. Avoid grapefruit. Do not replace a missed or vomited dose. A clinical hold and restart require the treating team’s instructions.

Before starting

Confirm the exact medicine, strength, endocrine partner, baseline tests and first review appointments. Ask who arranges tests and who reviews the results.

Taking treatment

Take abemaciclib twice daily at regular times with or without food. Swallow whole; do not split, chew or crush. Avoid grapefruit. Do not replace a missed or vomited dose. A clinical hold and restart require the treating team’s instructions.

Treatment duration

At completion of the planned two-year abemaciclib course, continue the separately agreed endocrine and follow-up plan.

Supply and appointments

Request the next supply before tablets run out. Blood tests and any ECG or injections need their own appointments. This oral treatment does not itself require a central line or infusion pump.

If treatment is held

Ask which medicine is held, whether the endocrine partner continues, what needs to recover, when the next review is due and who will authorise restart.

Support between appointments

Keep the oncology contact details and prescribed symptom medicines accessible. New severe symptoms need prompt assessment rather than waiting for the next blood test.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
