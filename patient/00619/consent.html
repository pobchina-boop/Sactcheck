1. Treatment and purpose

Adjuvant abemaciclib + endocrine therapy · NCCP 00619 v4a

Abemaciclib is taken twice daily continuously, with endocrine treatment, for up to two years if appropriate. A 28-day block is shown for orientation; there is no planned tablet-free week. Endocrine treatment usually continues longer.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Abemaciclib · CDK4/6 inhibitor

Start the prescribed diarrhoea rescue plan at the first loose stool and contact the team. Breathlessness, a swollen painful leg, fever or jaundice need prompt assessment.

Your endocrine treatment · Hormone-directed treatment

Tell the team about joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal symptoms and mood changes. The bone-health and clot-risk discussion depends on the drug chosen.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the treatment aim, alternatives, expected benefit, serious and lasting risks, fertility/pregnancy precautions, the planned monitoring and who to contact. Review the full longitudinal pathway and confirm which conditional treatments apply.
