Abemaciclib: baseline checks and the first four months

NCCP 00619 specifies baseline ECG as clinically indicated. It does not mandate the ribociclib Cycle 1 Day 14 ECG schedule. Abemaciclib is continuous twice daily: there is no routine seven-day break.

The whole treatment journey

Adjuvant abemaciclib: two years within a longer plan

Adjuvant · high-risk node-positive HR-positive / HER2-negative early breast cancer

Abemaciclib is taken with endocrine treatment after surgery and other indicated local/systemic treatment. Abemaciclib is planned for two years; endocrine treatment commonly continues beyond that.

What each check is for

1Early blood monitoringBaseline; every 2 weeks for 2 months; monthly for the next 2 months

Checks blood counts and kidney/liver/bone-profile results.

Ask / act: Confirm blood-test appointments even though the medicine is taken at home.

Sources: NCCP 00619

2Early diarrhoea supportFrom the first loose stool and throughout treatment

Diarrhoea can develop early and cause dehydration.

Ask / act: Use the prescribed rescue plan and contact the team; do not wait for the next review.

Sources: NCCP 00619 · ABEMA

3Bone density and fracture riskAt AI/OFS initiation and then by risk

Assess fracture risk and whether a baseline DEXA bone-density scan is needed when starting an aromatase inhibitor or ovarian suppression/treatment-induced menopause. It may already have been done before this regimen.

Ask / act: Ask when bone health was last assessed and whether a repeat scan or bone medicine is needed.

Sources: BONE · NOGG

4Breast surveillanceAfter early breast treatment

Annual mammography of remaining breast tissue after treatment; the team sets the first date. The mastectomy side is not routinely mammographed. New symptoms need assessment between visits.

Ask / act: Report symptoms promptly; a normal scheduled mammogram does not explain every new symptom.

Sources: EARLY · BONE

5Two-year transitionAt planned abemaciclib completion

The CDK4/6 drug ends while endocrine treatment may continue.

Ask / act: Ask for the continuing endocrine, bone-health and follow-up plan.

Sources: NCCP 00619 · EARLY

6Baseline ECGBefore starting, as clinically indicated

Cardiac symptoms, history or other medicines may make an ECG appropriate.

Ask / act: Ask whether a baseline ECG is needed for your situation.

Sources: NCCP 00619
