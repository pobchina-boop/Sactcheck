NCCP 00619 · version 4a

Adjuvant abemaciclib + endocrine therapy

The usual abemaciclib starting dose is 150 mg twice daily; a lower dose or altered schedule may be prescribed. Abemaciclib is taken twice daily continuously for up to two years with endocrine therapy, unless recurrence or unacceptable toxicity requires an earlier change. A four-week dispensing block is not a rest cycle. Endocrine treatment normally continues beyond abemaciclib.
