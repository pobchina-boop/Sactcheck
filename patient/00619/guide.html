Your treatment journey

Every day

Abemaciclib 150 mg twice daily, with or without food, at approximately the same times each day unless your dose has been changed.

Alongside it

Continue the endocrine treatment prescribed for you.

First 2 months

Blood count, kidney/liver and bone profile are checked every 2 weeks.

Next 2 months

These tests are usually monthly, then as clinically indicated.

Treatment duration

Abemaciclib is taken continuously for 2 years or until progression/unacceptable toxicity, according to the NCCP source and your treatment plan.

Monitoring and support

  • Start anti-diarrhoeal treatment at the first sign of loose stools exactly as advised by your oncology team.
  • Swallow tablets whole; do not chew, split or crush.
  • Avoid grapefruit/grapefruit juice. If a dose is missed or vomited, take the next dose at the scheduled time rather than an extra dose.
  • Your current medicines, including non-prescription products.
  • A note of new symptoms, when they started and what they stopped you doing.
  • Any temperature, blood-pressure or bowel-count readings your team asked you to record.
  • Questions about the purpose of treatment, scan/review timing or changes to the plan.
