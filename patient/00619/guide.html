Adjuvant abemaciclib + endocrine therapy

Abemaciclib is taken twice daily continuously, with endocrine treatment, for up to two years if appropriate. A 28-day block is shown for orientation; there is no planned tablet-free week. Endocrine treatment usually continues longer.

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Abemaciclib

  • Diarrhoea, dehydration and nausea
  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Liver-test abnormalities
  • Blood clots
  • Rare serious lung inflammation

Your endocrine treatment

  • Hot flushes, joint or muscle symptoms
  • Vaginal dryness and sexual effects
  • Bone loss with AI / ovarian suppression
  • Clot and uterine risks with tamoxifen

Make the plan practical

Diarrhoea plan

Have the prescribed antidiarrhoeal medicine available. Start the agreed plan at the first loose stool and call early.

Daily tablets

Take the prescribed dose at regular times. Do not double up after a missed or vomited dose.

Bloods and interactions

Keep early blood-test appointments. Check every new medicine or supplement with oncology/pharmacy; avoid grapefruit.

Endocrine and bone care

Confirm endocrine/OFS treatment, bone-health assessment and the longer-term follow-up plan.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
