When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

My treatment

My overall treatment

Capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14, followed by seven days off. Adjuvant treatment is usually eight cycles; advanced treatment continues according to benefit and tolerance.

  1. Days 1–14Take tablets with food or within 30 minutes afterwards.
  2. Days 15–21Planned tablet break; symptoms may continue.

More about my medicines

Capecitabine

After you swallow capecitabine, your body changes it into fluorouracil. This interferes with the DNA and RNA processes cells use to grow. Normal cells can also be affected, which helps explain mouth soreness, bowel symptoms and changes in blood counts.

Consent discussion aid

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1–14: Treatment days

capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14, then no capecitabine on Days 15–21. Follow your individual prescription.

What to do: Use the current tablet label. Swallow whole with water with or within 30 minutes of food; do not crush or cut tablets.

Day 15–21: Planned tablet-free days

Do not take leftover tablets to replace doses missed earlier. Side effects may continue during this interval.

What to do: Keep the oncology contact plan available and confirm the next review before restarting.

Support at home

Nausea prevention and rescue

The team agrees a rescue medicine if needed; routine daily anti-sickness prevention is not automatically required for this oral low-risk schedule.

Bowel symptoms

Report diarrhoea promptly and follow the written rescue plan. Severe symptoms, fever, dehydration or inability to drink need urgent advice.

Counts and recovery

White-cell growth-factor injections are prescribed according to individual risk and previous complications. They are not automatically required for every patient or every cycle.

Skin and mouth care

Use gentle skin and mouth care. Painful hands and feet or mouth sores can require a tablet hold and team review; do not continue simply to finish the pack.
