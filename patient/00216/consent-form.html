Treatment context
Capecitabine is chemotherapy taken as tablets. NCCP 00216 covers several colorectal and breast cancer settings, including treatment after surgery and treatment of advanced disease. Ask which aim and duration apply to you.
Schedule
capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14, then no capecitabine on Days 15–21. Follow your individual prescription.
