Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

My overall treatment

Twice daily on Days 1–14, then seven days off; each cycle is 21 days. Adjuvant colon or residual triple-negative breast treatment is eight cycles (about six months). Advanced-disease treatment continues while helping and tolerated.

Between treatments

  • Stop tablets and call for significant diarrhoea, painful mouth or hand-foot symptoms, vomiting that prevents drinking, fever or sudden illness.
  • Symptoms can continue in the rest week. Chest pain needs emergency assessment.

Swallow tablets whole with water and food or within 30 minutes afterwards. Never double a missed dose or restart after a toxicity hold without instructions.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
