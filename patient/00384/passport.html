My questions and next steps

  • Which treatment setting applies to me? For locally advanced or metastatic pancreatic cancer, or locally advanced bile duct cancer, the aim is to control disease and symptoms.
  • What needs urgent attention at home? Keep a dated tablet diary. If you miss or vomit a dose, do not take an extra dose. Phone the team if unsure. Hold tablets and call for significant diarrhoea or painful mouth, hands or feet.
  • Why is this called off-label? The NCCP lists this treatment, but the Irish product licence does not cover these uses. Your consultant will discuss the evidence, alternatives and your choice.
