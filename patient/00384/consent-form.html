Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

For locally advanced or metastatic pancreatic cancer, or locally advanced bile duct cancer, the aim is to control disease and symptoms. Advanced pancreatic cancer or locally advanced biliary cancer

Treatment schedule. Gemcitabine on days 1, 8. Take capecitabine twice daily on days 1–14, then seven tablet-free days. Repeat every 21 days while helping and tolerable.

Gemcitabine and capecitabine · 21-day treatment

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

In the overall pancreatic trial population, median overall survival was 8.4 versus 7.2 months. The difference was statistically inconclusive. These figures do not describe biliary cancer. These are group results from the named setting. They cannot predict the benefit or side effects for an individual.

For locally advanced or metastatic pancreatic cancer, or locally advanced bile duct cancer, the aim is to control disease and symptoms. Discuss alternatives, fertility, individual risks and supportive care with the treating team. Both NCCP indications are off-label in Ireland. Discuss this and document consent under hospital policy.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
