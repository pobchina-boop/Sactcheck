Your complete treatment reference

Gemcitabine and capecitabine · 21-day treatment

Your treatment and how the medicines work

For locally advanced or metastatic pancreatic cancer, or locally advanced bile duct cancer, the aim is to control disease and symptoms.

Gemcitabine · gemcitabine

Chemotherapy. It interferes with copying DNA as cells divide. Blood-forming cells can also be affected. Phone promptly about fever, bleeding, a new cough or difficulty breathing.

Capecitabine · Xeloda

Oral chemotherapy. The body converts capecitabine into fluorouracil, which disrupts DNA and RNA processes needed for cell growth. Tablets are active chemotherapy; taking them at home still requires a clear monitoring and interruption plan. Stop tablets and contact the team for significant diarrhoea, painful mouth ulcers, hand-foot soreness affecting activities, fever or dehydration. Chest pain needs urgent assessment. Do not restart until instructed.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · Gemcitabine infusion

Gemcitabine runs over 30 minutes. Swallow prescribed capecitabine tablets whole with water, with food or within 30 minutes of a meal. Follow the dated tablet calendar.

Common effects and what to do

Eating and drinking

Tell the team about nausea or reduced appetite. Call if you cannot keep fluids down.

Fatigue and low counts

Rest between activities. Report fever, breathlessness or unusual bleeding promptly.

Hands and feet

Report sore, red or peeling skin before it limits walking or using your hands.

Serious effects: when to call

Infection

Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately.

Urgent symptoms

Seek urgent help for chest pain, trouble breathing or collapse.

Bowel symptoms

Ring promptly for persistent diarrhoea, dehydration or severe abdominal pain.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

At home

Keep a dated tablet diary. If you miss or vomit a dose, do not take an extra dose. Phone the team if unsure. Hold tablets and call for significant diarrhoea or painful mouth, hands or feet.

Pancreatic and biliary symptoms

Ask the team about a new fever with jaundice, dark urine or shivering, especially if you have a bile duct stent.

What the research found

In the overall pancreatic trial population, median overall survival was 8.4 versus 7.2 months. The difference was statistically inconclusive. These figures do not describe biliary cancer.

These are group results from the named setting. They cannot predict the benefit or side effects for an individual.
