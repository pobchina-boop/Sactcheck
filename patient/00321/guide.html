NCCP 00321 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Capecitabine

  • Diarrhoea / dehydration
  • Hand-foot syndrome
  • Mouth ulcers / reduced intake
  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Cardiotoxicity including chest pain / ischaemia
  • Severe early toxicity including DPD-related toxicity
  • Renal impairment and medicine interactions

Oxaliplatin

  • Cold-triggered tingling / throat discomfort
  • Persistent sensory neuropathy, sometimes lasting after treatment
  • Allergic / infusion reaction
  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Nausea, diarrhoea and fatigue
  • Rare serious lung or blood complications

Make the plan practical

Nausea prevention and rescue

Take the prescribed prevention medicines at the planned times and keep rescue treatment available. Tell the team if nausea or constipation is not controlled; do not duplicate steroid or anti-sickness medicines.

Bowel symptoms

Report diarrhoea promptly and follow the written rescue plan. Severe symptoms, fever, dehydration or inability to drink need urgent advice.

Counts and recovery

White-cell growth-factor injections are prescribed according to individual risk and previous complications. They are not automatically required for every patient or every cycle.

Cold and nerve symptoms

Avoid cold exposure as advised after oxaliplatin. Report persistent numbness or difficulty with buttons, walking or everyday tasks before the next dose.

Skin and mouth care

Use gentle skin and mouth care. Painful hands and feet or mouth sores can require a tablet hold and team review; do not continue simply to finish the pack.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
