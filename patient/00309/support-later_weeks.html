Additional phase-specific support

  • Reconcile paclitaxel premedication and antiemetic dexamethasone into ONE prescription; do not add duplicate steroids.
  • NCCP allows consultant-led famotidine omission without prior hypersensitivity, while noting uncertain reaction risk; close monitoring and reaction facilities remain necessary.
  • Muscle/joint pain: consider prescribed analgesia. Assess swallowing, nutrition and respiratory symptoms during and after RT.
  • No routine G-CSF, antibiotic, antiviral or PJP prophylaxis is mandated in this source; assess individual risk/local policy.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
