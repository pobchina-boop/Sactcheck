The whole treatment journey

Weekly carboplatin + paclitaxel with radiotherapy: treatment and checks

Stage III non-small-cell lung cancer · concurrent chemoradiation

Both medicines are given by drip once every 7 days for seven weeks, alongside a separate radiotherapy course. The plan may change for side effects or test results. Further chemotherapy after radiotherapy is a separate decision.
