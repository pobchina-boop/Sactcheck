The whole treatment journey

TCHP: docetaxel, carboplatin, trastuzumab and pertuzumab: the whole pathway

HER2-positive early breast cancer · before surgery

All four medicines are given by infusion on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle, for six cycles before surgery. Surgery findings guide the postoperative treatment. There is no routine take-home chemotherapy pump.

What each check is for

1Blood testsFBC, kidney and liver profile at baseline and before every 21-day cycle. The team reviews the necessary results before a planned dose.

Check safety before the next dose.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP

2Heart testsCheck heart pumping function before starting HER2 treatment. Repeat echo or multigated acquisition (heart pumping) scan (MUGA) every 12 weeks: around cycle 5, then cycle 9 if 21-day cycles stay on time. Earlier checks are needed for symptoms or abnormal findings. Count elapsed time from the first HER2 dose; changing phase or route does not restart the clock.

Monitor pumping function and relevant rhythm risk.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP · HERCEPTIN · ESC

3Cancer imagingBaseline breast and nodal imaging, with staging tests appropriate to the diagnosis. Review response during preoperative treatment and before surgery. After local treatment, arrange breast surveillance; routine computed tomography (CT), positron emission tomography (PET) or tumour-marker testing is not the usual screening plan for someone without symptoms.

Assess response or follow-up needs.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: EARLY

4Bone healthIf the cancer is also hormone-receptor positive, endocrine treatment may form part of the wider plan. Bone-density testing is linked to aromatase inhibitors, ovarian suppression and fracture risk; it is not automatically required because a HER2 medicine is prescribed. Agree baseline DEXA where indicated and individual follow-up.

Assess fracture risk where relevant.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: BONE
