TCHP: docetaxel, carboplatin, trastuzumab and pertuzumab

All four medicines are given by infusion on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle, for six cycles before surgery. Surgery findings guide the postoperative treatment. There is no routine take-home chemotherapy pump.

NCCP 00722 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Docetaxel

  • Low blood counts, infection and sepsis
  • Hair loss, mouth soreness and fatigue
  • Numbness / tingling; sometimes lasting
  • Fluid retention and nail changes
  • Allergic infusion reaction

Carboplatin

  • Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
  • Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
  • Allergic reaction, sometimes after previous exposure
  • Kidney or electrolyte changes

Trastuzumab

  • Heart pumping-function reduction / heart failure
  • Infusion or allergic reaction
  • Diarrhoea and fatigue
  • Rare serious lung effects

Pertuzumab

  • Diarrhoea and dehydration
  • Infusion / allergic reaction
  • Heart pumping-function reduction
  • Rash and fatigue

Make the plan practical

Before each infusion

Confirm steroid and anti-sickness instructions; ask whether growth-factor injections are prescribed.

Baseline and ongoing checks

Echo before Cycle 1, then every 12 weeks (around Cycle 5 if on schedule), continuing through HER2 treatment and at completion. Bloods before each cycle. Breast imaging is arranged for response / surgery planning; there is no routine CT each cycle.

Line and day-ward plan

A cannula, PICC or port may be used. Ask about insertion, wound care and whom to call for line symptoms.

Recovery and support

Report diarrhoea or numbness early. Ask about fertility, scalp cooling, nutrition and practical support.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
