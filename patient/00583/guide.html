21day reference

Reference window · ongoing treatment

Axitinib tabletsDay 1–21
Pembrolizumab infusionDay 1
  1. TreatmentPembrolizumab 200 mg is given intravenously every 21 days. Axitinib starts at 5 mg twice daily, about 12 hours apart, continuously, with or without food. Only your team may change the dose. Do not take an extra axitinib dose after a missed or vomited dose. Duration depends on benefit, tolerance and the agreed plan.

Between treatments

  • Side effects may occur on any day, including during a break. Report problems promptly.
  • Routine anti-sickness prophylaxis is not usually required; use the individual symptom plan. Bring an immunotherapy alert card. Follow blood-pressure treatment, skin/foot care and the diarrhoea plan. Steroids for suspected immune toxicity require medical direction. Review CYP3A-interacting medicines, grapefruit and herbal products with pharmacy.

Confirm infusion appointments, daily tablet dose, home blood-pressure instructions and urgent contact numbers.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
