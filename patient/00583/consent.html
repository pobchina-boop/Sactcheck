1. Treatment and purpose

Pembrolizumab + axitinib · NCCP 00583 v4

Pembrolizumab 200 mg is given intravenously every 21 days. Axitinib starts at 5 mg twice daily, about 12 hours apart, continuously, with or without food. Only your team may change the dose. Do not take an extra axitinib dose after a missed or vomited dose. Duration depends on benefit, tolerance and the agreed plan.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Pembrolizumab · PD-1 checkpoint immunotherapy

Report new diarrhoea, cough, breathlessness, rash, headache, unusual weakness, thirst or changes in urine promptly.

Axitinib · Targeted therapy: VEGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor

Collect a prescription for diarrhoea medicine, usually loperamide, before starting. Follow the supplied instructions and contact the team for persistent diarrhoea, dizziness, poor intake or reduced urine.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the intended benefit, alternatives, common and serious risks, lasting effects, fertility and individual priorities. Record the patient’s questions and the agreed plan. Pembrolizumab is held or stopped for toxicity, never dose-reduced. Axitinib has separate dose levels and holding rules. Liver injury or diarrhoea can involve either medicine, so do not attribute symptoms or change tablets without advice.
