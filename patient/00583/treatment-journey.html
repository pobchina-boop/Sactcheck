The whole treatment journey

Pembrolizumab + axitinib: treatment and checks

Advanced clear-cell-component kidney cancer · first line

Pembrolizumab 200 mg is given intravenously every 21 days. Axitinib starts at 5 mg twice daily, about 12 hours apart, continuously, with or without food. Only your team may change the dose. Do not take an extra axitinib dose after a missed or vomited dose. Duration depends on benefit, tolerance and the agreed plan.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewPembrolizumab every 21 days · axitinib twice daily

Help control advanced kidney cancer using immunotherapy and a blood-vessel-growth inhibitor.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999.
