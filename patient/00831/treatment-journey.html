Atezolizumab + bevacizumab for HCC: treatment and checks

First-line advanced or unresectable liver cancer (HCC)

Atezolizumab is given first on Day 1 as an IV infusion or a subcutaneous injection into the thigh, followed by bevacizumab through a vein. Treatment is planned every 21 days while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable.

What each check is for

1Varices review before bevacizumabBefore first dose

Liver disease and anti-VEGF therapy can combine to increase bleeding risk.

Ask / act: Confirm the screening result and management plan.

Sources: NCCP 00831

2Review each medicineBefore every cycle

Immune and anti-VEGF toxicities have different actions.

Ask / act: Record which medicine is held, continued or stopped.

Sources: NCCP 00831

3Six-week thyroid monitoringDuring treatment

Thyroid dysfunction can develop without obvious early symptoms.

Ask / act: Confirm who books and reviews results.

Sources: NCCP 00831

4After treatmentAfter last dose

Immune effects may be delayed and other risks persist.

Ask / act: Agree contact, contraception and medicine follow-up.

Sources: NCCP 00831
