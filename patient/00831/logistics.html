Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before travelling

Confirm blood/urine test arrangements, blood-pressure checks, transport and the 24-hour number. Tell the team if you are unwell before attending.

STEP 2

Time at the unit

IV atezolizumab is usually infused over 60 minutes initially and may take 30 minutes later if tolerated. The alternative 1875 mg thigh injection takes about seven minutes. Bevacizumab is still an IV infusion: usually 90 minutes first, then 60 and later 30 minutes if tolerated. Checks, preparation and observation make the whole visit longer.

STEP 3

At home

Keep a medicines list, symptom notes and the immune alert card. Follow your own hospital contact plan; the pocket card complements the medicine alert card.

STEP 4

Procedures and collections

Tell the team about planned surgery or dental procedures and ask for clear timed urine-collection instructions if needed.
