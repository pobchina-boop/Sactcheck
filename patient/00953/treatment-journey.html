Your treatment journey

Durvalumab, paclitaxel and carboplatin

The aim is to control the cancer, ease or prevent symptoms and delay worsening. Your team can explain the benefit they expect for you and your other options.

Your infusion schedule

Three-drug combination every 21 days

Day 1: durvalumab, then paclitaxel, then carboplatin. Repeat every 21 days for 4–6 cycles unless the team pauses or stops earlier.

Durvalumab alone every 28 days

The first durvalumab-only dose is 3 weeks after the final combination infusion. It then repeats every 4 weeks while helping and tolerated.

At review visits

  • When does maintenance start? Three weeks after the final combination dose; later visits are four weeks apart.
  • When should I call today? Call urgently for new cough, breathlessness, fever, diarrhoea or marked weakness.
