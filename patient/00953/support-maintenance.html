Support during maintenance

Durvalumab, paclitaxel and carboplatin

The first durvalumab-only dose is 3 weeks after the final combination infusion. It then repeats every 4 weeks while helping and tolerated.

Change of medicines

Only durvalumab is planned. Its sickness risk is minimal, and the team reviews whether you still need rescue medicines. The taxane premedicines used during combination treatment are no longer part of a routine maintenance visit.

Immune symptoms may be delayed

Call urgently for new cough or breathlessness, diarrhoea or bloody stool, severe weakness, dizziness or a blistering rash during or after maintenance. Carry your immunotherapy alert card. Severe breathing difficulty or collapse needs 112 or 999.

Current appointments

The first maintenance infusion is planned three weeks after the last combination infusion; following visits are every four weeks. Confirm the next blood test, infusion and 24-hour contact before leaving.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the oncology team immediately for a fever or feeling suddenly unwell, new cough or breathlessness, diarrhoea or bloody stool, blistering rash, marked weakness or dizziness. Do this even after treatment ends. Call 112 or 999 for severe breathing trouble, severe chest pain, collapse or confusion. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
