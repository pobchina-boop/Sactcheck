My treatment passport

Durvalumab, paclitaxel and carboplatin

My treatment record

The aim is to control the cancer, ease or prevent symptoms and delay worsening. Your team can explain the benefit they expect for you and your other options.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
CombinationDay 1 of each cycle · 4–6 cycles if toleratedDay 1: durvalumab, then paclitaxel, then carboplatin. Repeat every 21 days for 4–6 cycles unless the team pauses or stops earlier.
MaintenanceFirst dose 3 weeks after last combination · Then every 4 weeksThe first durvalumab-only dose is 3 weeks after the final combination infusion. It then repeats every 4 weeks while helping and tolerated.

Hospital __________________ Who to call if I cannot reach the team __________________

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the oncology team immediately for a fever or feeling suddenly unwell, new cough or breathlessness, diarrhoea or bloody stool, blistering rash, marked weakness or dizziness. Do this even after treatment ends. Call 112 or 999 for severe breathing trouble, severe chest pain, collapse or confusion. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

My questions and next steps
