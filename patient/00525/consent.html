NCCP 00525 · version 6

Ribociclib + endocrine therapy · metastatic

The usual metastatic starting dose is ribociclib 600 mg once daily on Days 1–21 followed by Days 22–28 off. Continue the prescribed AI daily or fulvestrant on its own injection schedule; add ovarian suppression when indicated. Continue while beneficial and tolerated.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Ribociclib with an aromatase inhibitor or fulvestrant aims to control advanced HR-positive/HER2-negative breast cancer. The exact endocrine partner depends on previous treatment and menopausal status. HR-positive/HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic disease with an AI or fulvestrant in the exact NCCP indication. NCCP page 1 and page 2 do not fully align on menopause and previous advanced-disease treatment; preserve consultant reconciliation.

Material risks and support

  • Low white cells and infection
  • Liver injury / abnormal blood tests
  • QT prolongation / heart-rhythm problems
  • Nausea, fatigue and hair thinning
  • Rare serious lung or skin reactions
  • Hot flushes, joint pain and fatigue
  • Bone loss with AI / ovarian suppression
  • Injection-site discomfort with fulvestrant
  • Drug-specific liver and other effects

Discuss the treatment aim, alternatives, benefit and uncertainty, important medicine-specific risks, reproductive precautions, symptom support and planned duration. Confirm the endocrine partner and any ovarian-suppression plan. The timeline does not authorise a restart or certify clinical review.

Monitoring and understanding

FBC, renal and liver profile, potassium, calcium, magnesium and phosphorus. ECG before initiation; QTcF must be below 450 ms. Correct electrolyte abnormalities and review QT/CYP3A interactions. Confirm endocrine partner, menopause/ovarian suppression, prior treatment and the NCCP indication/eligibility discrepancy. FBC, renal and liver profile every two weeks for cycles 1–2, then before cycles 3–6, then as clinically indicated. Electrolytes before each of the first six cycles, then as clinically indicated; correct abnormalities. Repeat ECG around Day 14 of cycle 1, then as clinically indicated. Increase liver monitoring for grade 2 or worse abnormalities. Review fever, jaundice, palpitations/fainting, pulmonary and severe skin symptoms; response imaging follows the advanced-disease plan.
