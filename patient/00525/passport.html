Ribociclib (Kisqali) + endocrine therapy · metastatic

Your symptoms, cycle by cycle

Keep this separate passport and bring it to each review. Use one record sheet for each cycle; print extra copies for as long as treatment continues. Four blank sheets do not mean a four-cycle course.

The usual metastatic starting dose is ribociclib 600 mg once daily on Days 1–21 followed by Days 22–28 off. Continue the prescribed aromatase inhibitor (AI) daily or fulvestrant on its own injection schedule; add ovarian suppression when indicated. Continue while beneficial and tolerated.

How to record symptoms

Tick the worst level since your last review, write the date and explain what the symptom stopped you doing. Mild: noticeable but usual activities remain possible. Moderate: affects usual activities or sleep; contact your team. Severe: substantially limits daily activities, self-care or intake; seek urgent advice.

These patient ratings are not formal Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) grades. The clinician confirms the symptom, assigns its event-specific grade and records the plan in the last column. Symptoms are not automatically attributed to either medicine.

Do not wait to fill in this passport: new breathlessness, fever or shivering, palpitations or fainting, jaundice, or a blistering rash need prompt clinical advice. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or severe chest pain: 112 / 999.

Store completed sheets securely. This printed passport does not send your information to SACTCheck.
