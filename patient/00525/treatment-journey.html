Ribociclib + endocrine therapy · metastatic

The usual metastatic starting dose is ribociclib 600 mg once daily on Days 1–21 followed by Days 22–28 off. Continue the prescribed aromatase inhibitor (AI) daily or fulvestrant on its own injection schedule; add ovarian suppression when indicated. Continue while beneficial and tolerated.
