Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

More about my medicines

Ribociclib

Ribociclib blocks CDK4 and CDK6, proteins that help cells move through the cycle of division. Endocrine therapy reduces the hormone signal that can drive HR-positive breast cancer.

aromatase inhibitor (AI) or fulvestrant

An aromatase inhibitor (AI) lowers oestrogen production; fulvestrant blocks and reduces oestrogen receptors. These are alternative endocrine partners, selected by the team. Ovarian suppression is needed in pre/perimenopausal patients.

Day 1–28: Follow the phase and prescribed schedule

Ribociclib is taken once daily on Days 1–21, followed by seven days off, in a 28-day cycle. The endocrine partner continues according to its own prescription. Treatment continues while it is helping and tolerable.

What to do: Keep planned tests and call for symptoms at any time.

Support at home

Nausea support

NCCP classifies ribociclib as oral moderate–high emetic risk. The team chooses preventive or rescue antiemetics using local policy, symptoms, QT risk and drug interactions; do not reuse another regimen’s prescription.

Electrolytes and heart rhythm

Correct abnormal potassium, calcium, magnesium and phosphate as directed. Attend the baseline and cycle 1 Day-14 ECG; fainting or palpitations need urgent advice.

Counts and liver tests

Keep the early two-weekly blood tests and subsequent monitoring. Report fever, shivers, jaundice or dark urine promptly.

Endocrine treatment

The AI or fulvestrant, and ovarian suppression when prescribed, continue on their own timetable. Discuss joint, menopausal, bone and injection-site symptoms.

Daily administration

Avoid grapefruit and check interactions. Do not replace a missed or vomited dose. A clinical hold requires explicit instructions about which medicine stops and who authorises restart.
