Ribociclib + endocrine therapy · metastatic

Ribociclib is taken once daily on Days 1–21, followed by seven days off, in a 28-day cycle. The endocrine partner continues according to its own prescription. Treatment continues while it is helping and tolerable.

Important medicine-specific effects

Ribociclib

  • Low white cells and infection
  • Liver injury / abnormal blood tests
  • QT prolongation / heart-rhythm problems
  • Nausea, fatigue and hair thinning
  • Rare serious lung or skin reactions

AI or fulvestrant

  • Hot flushes, joint pain and fatigue
  • Bone loss with AI / ovarian suppression
  • Injection-site discomfort with fulvestrant
  • Drug-specific liver and other effects

Make the plan practical

Three weeks on, one off

The ribociclib break is Days 22–28. Your endocrine partner usually continues.

Bloods and ECG

Early blood tests and a Day-14 ECG are important even if you feel well.

Medicines check

Avoid grapefruit; check new prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines and supplements for interactions.

Missed or vomited tablets

Do not take an extra dose. Take the next prescribed dose at the usual time and contact the team if uncertain.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
