1. Treatment and purpose

Lomustine + bevacizumab 5 mg/kg · NCCP 00742 v3

Each 42-day cycle has lomustine capsules taken once on Day 1 and bevacizumab infusions on Days 1, 15 and 29. Up to six cycles are planned, with review before each treatment.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Lomustine · Oral chemotherapy capsule

This is one dose on Day 1, not a daily medicine. If an extra dose is taken, get urgent advice even if you feel well. Keep blood tests after the dose and report fever, bruising, bleeding, new cough or breathlessness.

Bevacizumab · Targeted antibody given into a vein

Report bleeding, severe abdominal pain, new chest or breathing symptoms, sudden neurological changes, headaches with visual changes, and any operation or wound problem. Keep blood-pressure and urine checks.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the exact diagnosis, treatment goal, evidence limits, alternatives and burdens; medicine-specific risks, monitoring, fertility and pregnancy precautions; any unlicensed supply or off-label use; and practical support. Keep steroid and seizure plans separate from anticancer treatment.
