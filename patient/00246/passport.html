My treatment passport

Methotrexate with folinic acid rescue

My treatment record

Cure low-risk disease and use serial hCG blood tests to confirm the response and detect a recurrence.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Days 1, 3, 5 and 7 · injectionDays 1, 3, 5 and 7 · injectionMethotrexate 50 mg is given into a muscle on days 1, 3, 5 and 7. Write down the actual time of each injection. A new cycle starts every 14 days.
Days 2, 4, 6 and 8 · rescue tabletDays 2, 4, 6 and 8 · rescue tabletTake folinic acid 15 mg by mouth exactly 24 hours after each injection. Contact the unit immediately if a tablet is late, missed or vomited. Ask the team what to do before taking another dose.

My questions and next steps

  • What happens at my next visit? Four injections on alternate days, each followed 24 hours later by a rescue tablet.
  • Which symptoms should I phone about? Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Call immediately if a rescue tablet is missed, vomited or late.
  • What should I plan before starting? Bring all medicines including painkillers, supplements and antibiotics for an interaction check.
  • What if my spinal and muscle injections fall in the same week? Bring both schedules to the GTN team. Record the route and actual time for each methotrexate dose and have the team check each folinic acid time before going home.
