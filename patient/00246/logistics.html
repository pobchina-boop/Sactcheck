Preparing for treatment visits

Planning your visits

Appointments

Four injections on alternate days, each followed 24 hours later by a rescue tablet.

Bring to your visit

Bring all medicines including painkillers, supplements and antibiotics for an interaction check.

Between visits

Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Call immediately if a rescue tablet is missed, vomited or late.
