Your complete treatment reference

Methotrexate with folinic acid rescue

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Cure low-risk disease and use serial hCG blood tests to confirm the response and detect a recurrence.

Methotrexate · Methotrexate

Antifolate chemotherapy. It blocks the supply of folate that rapidly growing trophoblastic cells need. Report mouth ulcers, fever, vomiting or difficulty drinking before the next injection.

Folinic acid · Calcium folinate

Rescue medicine. It provides usable folate to healthy cells after methotrexate. The 24-hour interval matters. Confirm the tablet and time with the unit if a dose is late, missed or vomited.

Your infusion schedule

Days 1, 3, 5 and 7 · injection

Methotrexate 50 mg is given into a muscle on days 1, 3, 5 and 7. Write down the actual time of each injection. A new cycle starts every 14 days.

Days 2, 4, 6 and 8 · rescue tablet

Take folinic acid 15 mg by mouth exactly 24 hours after each injection. Contact the unit immediately if a tablet is late, missed or vomited. Ask the team what to do before taking another dose.

Common effects and what to do

How you feel between visits

Tiredness, nausea or appetite changes can make everyday activities harder. Ask your team for help if symptoms interfere with eating or drinking.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Call immediately if a rescue tablet is missed, vomited or late. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

What if my spinal and muscle injections fall in the same week?

Bring both schedules to the GTN team. Record the route and actual time for each methotrexate dose and have the team check each folinic acid time before going home.

What the research found

Low-dose methotrexate and folinic acid achieved remission for many patients. A rising or plateauing hCG led to reassessment and treatment changes.

This specialist-centre cohort was observational. Ongoing hCG testing is essential even after a response.
