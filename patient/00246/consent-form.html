Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Methotrexate with folinic acid rescue

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Cure low-risk disease and use serial hCG blood tests to confirm the response and detect a recurrence. Low-risk gestational trophoblastic neoplasia (FIGO score 6 or less).

Treatment schedule. Methotrexate 50 mg is given into a muscle on days 1, 3, 5 and 7. Write down the actual time of each injection. A new cycle starts every 14 days. Take folinic acid 15 mg by mouth exactly 24 hours after each injection. Contact the unit immediately if a tablet is late, missed or vomited. Ask the team what to do before taking another dose.

Treatment discussion and decision

Methotrexate with folinic acid rescue

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Low-dose methotrexate and folinic acid achieved remission for many patients. A rising or plateauing hCG led to reassessment and treatment changes. This specialist-centre cohort was observational. Ongoing hCG testing is essential even after a response.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shivering or sudden illness. Call immediately if a rescue tablet is missed, vomited or late. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
