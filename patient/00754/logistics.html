Ifosfamide, vincristine, dactinomycin and doxorubicin

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm whether this is cycle 1–2, 3–4 or 5–9, and which vincristine appointments and doxorubicin doses apply. Check the full mesna/hydration prescription and the growth-factor calendar. Ask about individual infection prevention, bowel support and medicines that may interact.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Vincristine 1.5 mg/m², maximum 2 mg, is given by an intravenous minibag over 15 minutes. Dactinomycin 1.5 mg/m², maximum 2 mg, and doxorubicin 30 mg/m² on each of days 1 and 2 are administered by trained staff as intravenous boluses. Ifosfamide 3000 mg/m² on each of days 1 and 2 is infused over three hours. Mesna, fluids, safety review and observation add to the attendance or admission time.

STEP 3

Intravenous access and practical care

The treatment service chooses suitable intravenous access. Report burning, pain, swelling or redness during administration immediately. If a port or central catheter is used, agree dressing, flushing, infection precautions and eventual removal arrangements. Do not alter infusion or mesna-pump settings yourself.

STEP 4

Home support

Check the support section and your prescription for current protective medicines, timing and contact instructions.

STEP 5

Next appointment

Confirm the next treatment, tests and any scan before leaving.
