Tests and care through treatment

Safety monitoring is separate from scans and tumour-response review. The exact sequence can change after toxicity or local treatment.

Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenBefore treatment and each cycle; additional samples are directed by symptoms, treatment admission and count recovery.

Identify infection-fighting cell, haemoglobin and platelet changes.

Kidney and liver tests

WhenBefore treatment and each cycle; repeat sooner for symptoms, fluid changes or abnormal results.

Check safe drug handling and identify organ toxicity.

Heart pumping and previous anthracyclines

WhenHeart ultrasound or pumping scan before treatment; repeat when clinically indicated. Review symptoms and total previous anthracycline exposure before further doxorubicin.

Assess risk from doxorubicin.

Neurological function

WhenCheck neurological function before every ifosfamide dose; review peripheral neuropathy before every cycle and before further vincristine. Report new weakness, sensory changes or altered alertness immediately.

Detect ifosfamide effects on alertness and vincristine nerve toxicity.

Urine and fluid balance

WhenCheck urine for blood before each ifosfamide dose and every 8 hours on treatment days. Monitor urine output and fluid balance; record daily weight during prescribed hydration.

Detect bladder bleeding, kidney effects or fluid retention.

Bowel, mouth and infection review

WhenReview before further dosing and report symptoms between visits. The team confirms prophylaxis and growth-factor timing for the current phase.

Detect constipation, ulcers and infection requiring a change in care.

Tumour response and local-treatment review

If part of your plan
WhenThe specialist sets scans and clinical reviews according to tumour type, disease extent, symptoms and any surgery or radiotherapy plan.

Assess benefit separately from treatment safety.
