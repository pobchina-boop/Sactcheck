Treatment context
This treatment combines daily temozolomide with radiotherapy, followed by a break and further temozolomide cycles, to help control newly diagnosed glioblastoma. Your team discusses the expected benefit and how it fits after surgery or biopsy.
Schedule
Take temozolomide daily throughout six weeks of radiotherapy, including weekends as prescribed. After a four-week break, take temozolomide on Days 1–5 of each 28-day cycle for up to six adjuvant cycles.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Temozolomide

Oral chemotherapy capsule
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea / vomiting and appetite loss
Fatigue, headache and constipation
Liver injury, including delayed injury
PJP and other opportunistic infections
Rash and rare severe skin reactions
Fertility effects and harm to a pregnancy
Rare serious bone-marrow disorders

Radiotherapy

Treatment focused on the brain tumour area
Fatigue and scalp reaction
Hair loss in the treated area
Swelling and neurological symptoms
Possible delayed effects on thinking and function
