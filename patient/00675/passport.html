Alternating ifosfamide/etoposide and vincristine/doxorubicin/cyclophosphamide · every 2 weeks

A record of what you experience

One cycle per page. Each medicine has its own symptom table. Mark the worst symptoms since your last review, when they occurred and what they stopped you doing. Overlapping symptoms can be recorded under more than one medicine; you do not have to identify the cause.

These are patient-language severity prompts, not a validated questionnaire or a formal Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) grading tool. Your team assigns the clinical grade. Serious symptoms override the columns: call without waiting to decide a severity level.

There are 14 alternating administrations, planned every 14 days after recovery, not 14 pairs. Ifosfamide and etoposide are given on days 1–5 in cycles 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 and 13. Vincristine, doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide are given on day 1 in cycles 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10. Cycles 12 and 14 contain vincristine and cyclophosphamide with no doxorubicin; dactinomycin may be added only if the consultant selects it. Surgery, radiotherapy, toxicity or delayed recovery may change the sequence or dates.

These pages are reusable recording sheets; the prescribed course length is stated above. Ask for additional sheets as needed.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
