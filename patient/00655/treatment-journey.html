The whole treatment journey

Durvalumab 1500 mg every 28 days: treatment and checks

Stage III NSCLC · consolidation after concurrent chemoradiotherapy

Durvalumab is infused on Day 1 of each 28-day cycle. The course lasts up to 12 months (13 cycles), and can finish earlier if the cancer progresses or side effects make continuation unsuitable. The first dose should start within 42 days after chemoradiotherapy finishes.

2Recognise lung and endocrine symptomsThroughout the course, holds and follow-up

Immune inflammation and radiation effects can overlap; hormone problems can develop with subtle symptoms.

Ask / act: Report changes promptly and keep the alert card available.

Sources: NCCP 00655 · SMPC

3Complete, pause or stop safelyAt toxicity review or the maximum course

The course is capped at12 months (13 cycles), with earlier stopping if needed.

Ask / act: Agree follow-up, hormone replacement if needed and how to obtain urgent advice after completion.

Sources: NCCP 00655 · SMPC
