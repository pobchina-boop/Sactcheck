The horizontal axis shows order, not equal time intervals. NCCP specifies starting durvalumab within 42 days after chemoradiotherapy and continuing for up to 12 months (13 cycles), if suitable. The team confirms recovery, no progression and all eligibility requirements.

Chemotherapy and radiotherapy overlap in the earlier phase. Durvalumab follows; it is not shown as concurrent with them.
