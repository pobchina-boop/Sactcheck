1. Treatment and purpose

Durvalumab 1500 mg every 28 days · NCCP 00655 v3a

Durvalumab is infused on Day 1 of each 28-day cycle. The course lasts up to 12 months (13 cycles), and can finish earlier if the cancer progresses or side effects make continuation unsuitable. The first dose should start within 42 days after chemoradiotherapy finishes.

The whole treatment course

Chemoradiotherapy first, then durvalumab

For this lung-cancer pathway, chemotherapy and radiotherapy are given together. Durvalumab is a separate later phase, after treatment review.

2. Risks and supportive care

Durvalumab · PD-L1 immune checkpoint inhibitor

Report new symptoms early and carry the immunotherapy alert card. Do not wait for the next infusion.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the consolidation aim and possible benefit after chemoradiotherapy, alternatives and the option to decline; lung inflammation after chest radiation; immune inflammation of other organs; possible long-term hormone replacement; infusion reactions; reproductive precautions; tests, visits, the course cap and possible holds or permanent stopping. Trial results do not guarantee cure or individual benefit.
