When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Lomustine

Lomustine is an oral nitrosourea chemotherapy that can reach the brain. It damages DNA and interferes with cell division. Its effects on healthy blood-forming cells can appear several weeks after the dose and can build up over successive cycles. This explains the long gap between doses and the need for continuing blood tests.

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: One authorised capsule dose

Check the correct total dose and capsule strengths with the pharmacist. Take the prescribed anti-sickness plan.

What to do: Lomustine is not taken daily.

Day 2–42: No scheduled lomustine; continue monitoring

Blood counts may fall weeks after the dose. Attend weekly blood-count checks and other planned reviews.

What to do: Do not take another dose until the team has reviewed you and issued instructions.

Support at home

Lomustine nausea prevention

Lomustine has moderate-to-high oral emetic potential. Take the prescribed prevention and rescue plan around the single dose. Contact the team if nausea or vomiting prevents fluids or essential medicines; do not automatically repeat a vomited chemotherapy dose.

Separate steroid, seizure and bowel plans

Keep steroids and seizure medicines on their own written schedules if prescribed. Ask what to do after a missed or vomited dose. Other medicines can worsen constipation; agree a bowel plan and have all new medicines checked for interactions.
