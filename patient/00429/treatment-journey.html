Your treatment journey

EOF for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and relieve symptoms. Your team will discuss benefit and side effects at review.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · EOF

Day 1 epirubicin and oxaliplatin. Fluorouracil runs continuously for all 21 days. Start a fresh seven-day pump on days 1, 8 and 15.

Day 8 · fluorouracil pump exchange

A trained team replaces the seven-day pump. Report line problems or mouth, bowel or heart symptoms.

Day 15 · fluorouracil pump exchange

A trained team replaces the seven-day pump. Report line problems or mouth, bowel or heart symptoms.

At review visits

  • When is the next treatment? Day 1 epirubicin and oxaliplatin. Fluorouracil runs continuously for all 21 days. Start a fresh seven-day pump on days 1, 8 and 15.
  • What should I do between visits? Keep the pump and dressing dry. Call the day unit if the pump leaks, disconnects or empties early or late. A trained team changes it weekly.
