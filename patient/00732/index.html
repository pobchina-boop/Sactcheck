How your medicines work

EGFR-targeted antibody

Cetuximab

Blocks growth messages at the cell surface
EGFR receives a growth signal→Cetuximab blocks the receptor→Some growth signals are reduced

Cetuximab attaches to EGFR on the outside of cells and blocks some growth messages. In this bowel-cancer plan, validated extended RAS testing helps identify whether treatment is suitable. Healthy skin also uses EGFR, so skin and nail effects need early care.

Report rash or sore nails early. Tell the nurse immediately about infusion symptoms. Eye pain, new breathing problems or severe diarrhoea need prompt advice. Blood tests check minerals during treatment and for two months afterwards.

Practical treatment guide

Visits, access and care between treatments

Cetuximab on Day 1 every 14 days while beneficial and tolerable. No Day 8 cetuximab or home chemotherapy pump is scheduled.

STEP 1

Before the first visit

Confirm the exact regimen, tests, medicines, emergency number and treatment goal. Ask whether a peripheral cannula or an existing line is appropriate; a port is not automatically required.

STEP 2

At the treatment unit

Fortnightly cetuximab infusion time is set from the dose and safe infusion rate; the first dose may take longer. Blood review, preparation, premedicines and observation add time; infusion time is not the total appointment length.

STEP 3

After the infusion

There is no fluorouracil home pump and no disconnection visit in this regimen.

STEP 4

Between doses

Use the written symptom and support plan; do not wait for the next appointment if urgent symptoms appear.

STEP 5

The next decision

Confirm the next visit after any delay or dose change. Treatment continues only while the oncology team judges benefit and tolerability acceptable. Follow-up mineral checks continue after the final EGFR-antibody dose where applicable.

Transport and daily life

Plan transport if antihistamines, symptoms or other medicines affect driving. Ask for practical, work, nutrition or emotional support.

If treatment changes

Ask which component has been held and which visits remain. A treatment delay does not automatically cancel blood tests, line care or symptom follow-up.

Support around treatment

Skin, nails and minerals

Start the prescribed skin-care plan early, use moisturiser and sun protection, and report painful or infected skin or nails. Keep mineral blood-test appointments during treatment and for two months after cetuximab.

Bowel symptoms and fluids

Follow your written rescue plan. Fever, blood, severe pain, reduced urine or inability to drink needs urgent advice.

Plan the visit

Allow for blood tests, review, pharmacy preparation, infusion and observation. A port is not automatically required; ask which access is suitable.

Comfort and daily life

Use prescribed anti-sickness medicines, pace activities and ask for nutritional, emotional or practical support.

The whole treatment plan

Ask how and when the team will assess benefit, which blood tests continue during a pause, and what happens after the final dose. Mineral checks continue monthly for two months after the last antibody treatment.
