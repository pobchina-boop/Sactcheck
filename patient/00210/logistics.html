Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the first treatment

Confirm the aim, likely course length, blood-test arrangements and a working urgent contact number. dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (a medicine-processing enzyme) (DPD) testing is required before starting the fluoropyrimidine unless already completed.

STEP 2

Line and pump arrangements

FOLFOX4 has separate fluorouracil infusions over 22 hours on Day 1 and again on Day 2. Confirm both attendance/pump arrangements and the final disconnection; this is not the single 46-hour modified FOLFOX6 schedule.

STEP 3

If treatment is delayed

Ask the unit to confirm the new treatment and blood-test dates. Re-anchor supportive medicines to the treatment actually given; do not take an extra or catch-up anticancer dose yourself.

STEP 4

Getting help between visits

Follow your oncology unit’s urgent advice for fever, infection, severe diarrhoea, chest pain or difficulty breathing. Bring your treatment card and current medicines list.

STEP 5

Paper copies, wallet QR and contacts

The example telephone numbers on the wallet card are placeholders. Your team must replace them with working daytime and out-of-hours numbers before patient use. Keep the card with you; the QR opens the regimen information, not your confidential medical record.
