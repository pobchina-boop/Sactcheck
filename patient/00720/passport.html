Neratinib · extended adjuvant treatment

Neratinib is taken once daily with food for one year. The usual starting dose is 240 mg; the team may interrupt or reduce it for toxicity. Treatment starts less than one year after completing adjuvant trastuzumab.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Report diarrhoea early. Poor intake, dizziness, reduced urine, blood in stool, fever, severe pain or jaundice needs prompt clinical advice.
