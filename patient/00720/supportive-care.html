Weeks 1–2 · early diarrhoea prevention

Download this phase PDF · Open printable script

Active medicines: Neratinib

Emetogenic risk: Oral minimal-to-low

MedicineDose / routeWhen
Loperamide4 mg orally three times dailyWeeks 1–2 from the first neratinib doseNCCP-specific oncology prevention regimen; individual review is required.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Start with the first neratinib dose. Titrate under the prescribed plan toward 1–2 bowel movements/day.
  • Review constipation, hydration, electrolyte disturbance and diarrhoea severity promptly.
  • No generic PPI order: review acid-suppressing medicines and interactions.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.

Loperamide4 mg orally twice dailyWeeks 3–8Review bowel pattern, hydration and constipation.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Titrate under the prescribed plan toward 1–2 bowel movements/day.
  • New or worsening diarrhoea may require investigation, fluids and a neratinib hold; do not simply escalate loperamide indefinitely.
