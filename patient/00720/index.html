Oral HER-family kinase inhibitor

Neratinib

Blocks growth signalling inside HER2-positive cancer cells
Enters the cell↓HER signalling inhibited↓Growth signals reduced

Neratinib binds irreversibly to signalling enzymes in the HER family, including HER2. It is used after trastuzumab-based adjuvant therapy in a specific hormone-receptor-positive, HER2-positive early-breast setting. Its effects on the bowel make early diarrhoea prevention important.

Start the prescribed diarrhoea-prevention plan with the first dose. Report diarrhoea early, especially poor intake, dizziness or reduced urine.

What each check is for

1Before treatmentBefore the first dose

Hormone-receptor-positive, HER2-positive early breast cancer after adjuvant trastuzumab. FBC monthly for the first six months, then every three months. Liver tests at week 1, monthly for the first three months, then every six weeks; test earlier for liver symptoms or significant diarrhoea.

Ask / act: Confirm eligibility, test results and medicine list.

Sources: NCCP 00720

2Other checksAt the specified review points

Confirm normal baseline LVEF eligibility; cardiac/LVEF assessment is clinically indicated. Review bowel symptoms, hydration, weight and interacting medicines. Continue any follow-up already required after prior trastuzumab; neratinib does not reset that plan.

Ask / act: Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.

Sources: NCCP 00720 · SMPC

3Support and practical planAt each treatment or phase change

Prescribed loperamide from the first dose, early hydration and diarrhoea review, liver monitoring and an acid-suppression/interactions plan.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support plan; confirm active medicines.

Sources: NCCP 00720

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

After early-breast treatment, breast imaging and clinical review follow the agreed plan. Routine CT/PET scans or blood tumour markers are not used to search for distant recurrence in someone without symptoms or clinical concerns. Keep the agreed follow-up and report persistent or new symptoms between visits.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities.

Sources: EARLY

Support throughout treatment

Diarrhoea prevention

Have the prescribed loperamide plan ready before the first tablet. Call early if diarrhoea starts or worsens.

Hydration

Follow the fluid plan and report dizziness, poor intake or reduced urine.

Liver checks

Keep the week-1 and subsequent liver tests; report yellow skin, dark urine or severe nausea.

Other medicines

Check acid-suppressing medicines and all new prescriptions with the pharmacist.
