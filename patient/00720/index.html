My schedule at a glance

Your treatment pattern

Daily treatment for one year

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Neratinib is taken once daily with food for one year. The usual starting dose is 240 mg; the team may interrupt or reduce it for toxicity. Treatment starts less than one year after completing adjuvant trastuzumab.

Tests and checks

full blood count (FBC) monthly for the first six months, then every three months. Liver tests at week 1, monthly for the first three months, then every six weeks; test earlier for liver symptoms or significant diarrhoea. Confirm normal baseline left ventricular ejection fraction (heart pumping measurement) (LVEF) eligibility; cardiac/LVEF assessment is clinically indicated. Review bowel symptoms, hydration, weight and interacting medicines. Continue any follow-up already required after prior trastuzumab; neratinib does not reset that plan.
