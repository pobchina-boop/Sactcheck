Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

Neratinib is taken once daily with food for one year. The usual starting dose is 240 mg; the team may interrupt or reduce it for toxicity. Treatment starts less than one year after completing adjuvant trastuzumab.

Take tablets whole with food, preferably in the morning. Do not crush or dissolve them. Do not replace a missed dose. Start the prescribed loperamide prevention plan with the first neratinib dose.

Checks and follow-up

FBC monthly for the first six months, then every three months. Liver tests at week 1, monthly for the first three months, then every six weeks; test earlier for liver symptoms or significant diarrhoea. Confirm normal baseline LVEF eligibility; cardiac/LVEF assessment is clinically indicated. Review bowel symptoms, hydration, weight and interacting medicines. Continue any follow-up already required after prior trastuzumab; neratinib does not reset that plan.

Support between reviews

Diarrhoea prevention

Have the prescribed loperamide plan ready before the first tablet. Call early if diarrhoea starts or worsens.

Hydration

Follow the fluid plan and report dizziness, poor intake or reduced urine.

Liver checks

Keep the week-1 and subsequent liver tests; report yellow skin, dark urine or severe nausea.

Other medicines

Check acid-suppressing medicines and all new prescriptions with the pharmacist.
