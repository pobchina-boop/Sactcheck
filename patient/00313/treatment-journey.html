The whole treatment journey

Docetaxel + prednisolone every 14 days: treatment and checks

Metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer

Docetaxel 50 mg/m² is infused over one hour on Day 1 every 14 days, with prednisolone 10 mg once daily or 5 mg twice daily continuously. Treatment duration depends on benefit and side effects.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewDay 1 every 14 days

Help control advanced prostate cancer and relieve symptoms.

Ask / act: Call your oncology team immediately for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding or persistent vomiting/diarrhoea. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or new leg weakness with bladder/bowel changes: call 112/999.
