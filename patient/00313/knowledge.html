When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Call your oncology team immediately for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding or persistent vomiting/diarrhoea. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or new leg weakness with bladder/bowel changes: call 112/999.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

What each medicine does

Cytotoxic chemotherapy

Docetaxel

Docetaxel
Docetaxel stabilises microtubules, preventing normal cell division. It can slow cancer growth but also affects blood-forming cells, hair follicles and the mouth and bowel lining.

Planned course confirmed by your team

Docetaxel 50 mg/m² is infused over one hour on Day 1 every 14 days, with prednisolone 10 mg once daily or 5 mg twice daily continuously. Treatment duration depends on benefit and side effects.

Continue prescribed androgen deprivation therapy (ADT), unless both testes have been surgically removed. ADT lowers testosterone; chemotherapy or a bone-targeted radiopharmaceutical has a different action. Additional androgen-pathway agents and radiotherapy need their own disease-specific plan.

Read the whole cycle as text

Day 1: Treatment day

Dexamethasone 8 mg by mouth at 12 hours, 3 hours and 1 hour before docetaxel. Tell the team if a dose is missed; an IV alternative is a consultant decision. Confirm blood results, symptoms and the prescribed dose.

Day 2–14: Between visits

Take prednisolone continuously as prescribed, with food. Do not stop suddenly when chemotherapy is delayed. Ask for a steroid alert card, an illness plan and advice if vomiting prevents you keeping tablets down. This two-week schedule uses a lower dose per visit than the three-week regimen. Do not transfer the dose or steroid timing from another docetaxel schedule.

Treatment reference · 04

Benefits, uncertainty and your choices

Evidence in this setting

A randomised trial compared docetaxel 50 mg/m² every two weeks with 75 mg/m² every three weeks in castration-resistant prostate cancer. Time to treatment failure was 5.6 versus 4.9 months; febrile neutropenia occurred in 4% versus 14%. Time to treatment failure is not progression-free or overall survival. The comparison supports a schedule discussion for tolerability, not a guarantee of fewer complications for an individual.

Sources: nccp · smpc · trial23294853

Consent discussion

This 14-day posology is unlicensed in Ireland according to the NCCP footnote; explain and document the prescribing and consent decision. Discuss alternatives, likely benefit and uncertainty, possible lasting or fatal effects, fertility, contraception and your priorities.

Sources: nccp · smpc

Discuss fertility preservation before starting. Men should use effective contraception during treatment and for four months afterwards, according to the current product information.
