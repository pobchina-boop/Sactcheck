Dactinomycin and etoposide are followed by methotrexate over 12 hours. Write down the infusion start time so you know when rescue begins.

Dactinomycin

Etoposide

Tell the nurse if you feel faint or breathless during the infusion.

Methotrexate

Check new medicines with oncology pharmacy and follow the timed rescue plan.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Call for severe constipation, abdominal pain, weakness, or a missed folinic acid dose. Report stinging at an IV site immediately. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
