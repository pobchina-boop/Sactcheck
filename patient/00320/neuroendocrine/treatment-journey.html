The whole treatment journey

Everolimus · neuroendocrine tumours: treatment and checks

Progressive unresectable or metastatic pancreatic NET, or well-differentiated G1/G2 nonfunctional gastrointestinal NET under NCCP 00320b/c.

Take the prescribed everolimus dose once daily every day, at the same time and consistently with or without food. There is no planned tablet-free week.

What each check is for

1Daily everolimusContinuous

Everolimus is a daily targeted tablet used to help control particular advanced NETs. It aims to slow growth; the team balances disease control, side effects and your priorities. This NET guide does not cover the separate kidney-cancer indication.

Ask / act: Confirm the current prescription, tests and support medicines.
