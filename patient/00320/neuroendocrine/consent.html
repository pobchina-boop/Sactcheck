2. Risks and supportive care

Everolimus · Targeted therapy tablet

Report mouth ulcers and new breathing symptoms early; keep blood and metabolic checks even if you feel well.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the exact neuroendocrine tumour (NET) indication, expected control and uncertainty, alternatives, mouth ulcers, infection and lung inflammation, metabolic and kidney/liver effects, interactions, pregnancy precautions and the monitoring plan.
