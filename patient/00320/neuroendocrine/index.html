When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Everolimus

Everolimus blocks a growth-control pathway called mTOR inside cells. This can slow NET growth. Healthy tissues and immune responses also depend on these pathways, helping explain mouth ulcers, infections, lung inflammation and changes in blood sugar or fats.

Support at home

Mouth care

Use gentle mouth care and any prescribed alcohol-free mouthwash. Ask before buying a rinse; avoid products containing alcohol, peroxide, iodine or thyme. Report ulcers early.

Sickness and bowel support

Everolimus has minimal-to-low emetogenic potential. Routine premedication is not required by this regimen; agree any rescue anti-sickness or diarrhoea medicine with the team.

Infection prevention

Existing infection must be assessed and resolved before starting. Hepatitis B testing informs any specialist prevention plan. Vaccination and any antimicrobial prevention, including Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia (PJP) prevention with certain steroid plans, are individual decisions.

Eating and energy

Report weight loss, poor intake or persistent fatigue. Ask for nutritional and symptom support; do not continue through severe symptoms simply to finish a pack.

If part of your plan
WhenOnly if requested for your hormone syndrome; the team sets the dates.

Measures a breakdown product of serotonin to help assess selected neuroendocrine tumours.

Often a timed urine collection. Follow the laboratory’s food and medicine instructions.

Chromogranin A

If part of your plan
WhenOnly when the team considers it informative for your tumour.

A selected tumour-marker result, interpreted with symptoms and scans.

Acid-suppressing medicines and other conditions can alter the result. It is not a routine test before every treatment; do not stop medicines yourself.
