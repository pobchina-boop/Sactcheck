Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking tablets

The usual starting dose is 10 mg once daily, but follow your own prescription. Swallow whole with a glass of water; do not chew or crush. Take consistently with or without food. If a dose is missed, do not take an extra dose: take the next prescribed dose at the usual time.

STEP 2

Medicines, vaccines and food

Avoid grapefruit and St John’s wort. Ask the pharmacist to check all prescribed, over-the-counter and herbal products. Live vaccines should be avoided during treatment; agree vaccination with your team.

STEP 3

Procedures and radiotherapy

Discuss planned surgery, wounds and current or recent radiotherapy. Everolimus may affect healing and can worsen or reactivate radiation-related inflammation. The teams agree any interruption.

STEP 4

Safe storage and supply

Keep tablets in their original labelled packaging, out of children’s reach, and follow the pack storage instructions. Return unused tablets to the pharmacy as advised. Plan repeat prescriptions before your supply runs out.

STEP 5

Pregnancy, feeding and fertility

Tell the team before starting if pregnant, planning pregnancy or breastfeeding. Ask for the medicine-specific contraception duration and fertility advice, and record it in your plan.

STEP 6

Review visits

Baseline blood count, kidneys/liver, BUN, urine protein, lipids, fasting glucose and hepatitis B screening. Blood count, kidney and liver tests every four weeks. Lipids regularly and glucose as clinically indicated; scans and NET markers follow the consultant plan.
