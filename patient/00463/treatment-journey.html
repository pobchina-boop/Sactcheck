Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenBefore starting and before each treatment, with repeat checks as indicated. Methotrexate and doxorubicin/cisplatin use different source treatment thresholds.

Check infection, anaemia and bleeding risk for the selected drug phase.

Kidney function and daily creatinine

WhenBefore each treatment; creatinine daily during methotrexate administration and clearance monitoring. Further testing follows a rise in creatinine or delayed clearance.

Assess high-dose methotrexate clearance and cisplatin injury.

Methotrexate blood concentration

WhenAt 24, 48, 72, 96 and 120 hours as appropriate from the actual infusion START; continue later measurements if the specialist clearance plan requires them.

Guide safe continuation and adjustment of folinic acid rescue.

Urine acidity

WhenConfirm urine pH at least 7 before methotrexate, then check every 6 hours during methotrexate and rescue while prescribed alkalinisation continues.

Reduce methotrexate precipitation in the kidneys.

Fluid balance and hydration

WhenBegin prescribed prehydration at least 6 hours before methotrexate; record fluid balance every 4 hours during treatment. Cisplatin has a separate pre/post-hydration prescription.

Maintain clearance while identifying fluid retention or reduced output.

Liver tests

WhenBefore each treatment; repeat after abnormal results or symptoms. The team distinguishes expected transient methotrexate-related changes from persistent injury.

Detect hepatic effects and interpret persistent abnormalities.

Coagulation and urine screening

WhenCoagulation tests and urine dipstick for blood, protein and glucose before starting; repeats are directed by findings and the clinical plan.

Identify baseline bleeding and urinary abnormalities.

Heart function

If part of your plan
WhenHeart tracing before starting; imaging when clinically indicated. Symptoms or abnormal pumping measurements trigger a specific reassessment before further doxorubicin. The linked doxorubicin product information additionally recommends baseline pumping-function imaging and repeats after approximately each accumulated 100 mg/m². The specialist team reconciles this with the national source and writes the cardiac monitoring schedule.

Detect anthracycline injury.

Hearing and nerve assessment

If part of your plan
WhenAssess hearing symptoms and sensory nerve damage before each course; audiology when clinically indicated.

Identify cisplatin injury that may change future doses.

Surgery, pathology and tumour review

If part of your plan
WhenPlan surgery at reference week 11 after induction, review resection/pathology afterwards, and individualise imaging and follow-up.

Connect local treatment, response and postoperative planning.
