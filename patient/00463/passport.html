Methotrexate, doxorubicin and cisplatin for osteosarcoma

A record of what you experience

One treatment visit per page. Each medicine has its own symptom table. Mark the worst symptoms since your last review, when they occurred and what they stopped you doing. Overlapping symptoms can be recorded under more than one medicine; you do not have to identify the cause.

These are patient-language severity prompts, not a validated questionnaire or a formal Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) grading tool. Your team assigns the clinical grade. Serious symptoms override the columns: call without waiting to decide a severity level.

The reference plan is not a repeating 21-day cycle. Before surgery, doxorubicin 37.5 mg/m² and cisplatin 60 mg/m² are each given on days 1 and 2 of weeks 1 and 6; methotrexate 12 g/m² is given on day 1 of weeks 4, 5, 9 and 10. Surgery is planned for reference week 11. Afterwards, doxorubicin is given on days 1 and 2 of weeks 12, 17, 22 and 26; cisplatin on days 1 and 2 of weeks 12 and 17; methotrexate on day 1 of weeks 15, 16, 20, 21, 24, 25, 28 and 29. Actual dates depend on surgery, recovery, drug clearance and safety review.

These are reusable treatment records; their number does not define the course length. Ask for additional sheets as needed.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
