1. Treatment and purpose

Eribulin · Days 1 and 8 of 21 · NCCP 00228 v8

Eribulin on Days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle. Day 15 is a planned treatment-free week. Treatment continues while beneficial and tolerated.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Eribulin · Chemotherapy

Report numbness, weakness or difficulty with walking or hand tasks before the next dose. Fever or sudden illness needs immediate advice; palpitations or fainting need urgent assessment.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the exact indication, expected benefit, alternatives, uncertainty, serious and lasting risks, contraception and fertility, venous access, monitoring and the urgent contact plan. EU dose labels use eribulin base. 1.23 mg/m² base corresponds to 1.4 mg/m² mesylate in some trial reports; these are not two alternative doses.

3. Understanding and next steps
