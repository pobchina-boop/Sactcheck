Treatment context
The aim is to control the cancer and its symptoms. The team reviews benefit, side effects and your priorities before continuing.
Schedule
Eribulin on Days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle. Day 15 is a planned treatment-free week. Treatment continues while beneficial and tolerated.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Eribulin

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and sepsis
Numbness, tingling or weakness, sometimes lasting
Fatigue, hair loss, nausea and constipation
Changes to heart rhythm / QT in susceptible people

Discuss the exact indication, expected benefit, alternatives, uncertainty, serious and lasting risks, contraception and fertility, venous access, monitoring and the urgent contact plan. EU dose labels use eribulin base. 1.23 mg/m² base corresponds to 1.4 mg/m² mesylate in some trial reports; these are not two alternative doses.
