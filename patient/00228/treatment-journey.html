2Benefit and continuationAt agreed clinical review points

In heavily pretreated advanced breast cancer, eribulin helped people live longer on average than the comparison treatments chosen by their doctors. This does not predict an individual result or establish the same benefit in every treatment line or another cancer.

Ask / act: Discuss benefit, toxicity and priorities before continuing or changing treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00228 · RETROSPECTIVE-TRIAL
