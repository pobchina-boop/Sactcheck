Your complete treatment reference

Pembrolizumab, paclitaxel, carboplatin and bevacizumab

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Control the cancer, relieve symptoms and delay progression.

Pembrolizumab · Keytruda

PD-1 immunotherapy. It helps immune cells recognise cancer. The immune system can also inflame healthy organs. Call about new symptoms during treatment or months after it ends. Carry your immunotherapy alert card.

Paclitaxel · Paclitaxel

Taxane chemotherapy. It disrupts cell division. Hair follicles, blood cells and nerves can also be affected. Report numbness and tell the nurse immediately if you flush or wheeze during treatment.

Carboplatin · Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. It damages DNA in dividing cells. Kidney function helps determine the prescribed amount. Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.

Bevacizumab · Avastin or biosimilar

Antibody targeting VEGF. It reduces signals that help tumours form blood vessels. This can also affect blood pressure, kidneys and wound healing. Call urgently for severe abdominal pain, significant bleeding, a new severe headache or sudden weakness.

Your infusion schedule

Combination · day 1

Four infusions every three weeks for 6–8 cycles. Your team reviews the benefit and side effects before continuing chemotherapy.

Maintenance · two medicines

Pembrolizumab and bevacizumab every three weeks while the treatment helps and side effects are manageable.

Common effects and what to do

Between visits

Fatigue, taste changes and nausea can affect meals and daily activities. Take the prescribed anti-sickness medicines and call if you cannot drink.

Numbness or tingling

Tell the team before your next infusion if tingling, numbness or loss of balance affects walking or using your hands.

Practical support

Pembrolizumab

Carry your immunotherapy alert card. New cough, diarrhoea, severe fatigue, excessive thirst or weakness need urgent review, including after treatment ends.

Paclitaxel

Confirm which steroid premedicines to take before paclitaxel. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, wheeze or throat tightness.

Carboplatin

Kidney function helps determine carboplatin dosing. Report previous platinum allergy.

Bevacizumab

Your team checks blood pressure, urine protein and healing. Report recent procedures, bleeding or new abdominal pain.

Which pelvic symptoms should I report on bevacizumab?

Tell the team about previous pelvic radiotherapy. Call urgently for new pelvic pain, bleeding, or leakage of urine or stool through the vagina.

Planning your visits

Arrange your visit

Combination every 3 weeks, then pembrolizumab and bevacizumab.

Before attending

Carry your immunotherapy alert card. New cough, diarrhoea, severe fatigue, excessive thirst or weakness need urgent review, including after treatment ends.

At home

Keep the hospital contact numbers beside your treatment card. Call promptly if you feel unwell.

What the research found

Adding pembrolizumab improved survival in people whose cervical cancer expressed PD-L1.

Bevacizumab use was selected by the treating team. The trial does not compare the two bevacizumab strategies.
