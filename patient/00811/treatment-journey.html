SACTCheck · NCCP 00811 v3 · Cervical cancer

Your treatment journey

Pembrolizumab, paclitaxel, carboplatin and bevacizumab

Control the cancer, relieve symptoms and delay progression.

Your infusion schedule

Combination · day 1

Four infusions every three weeks for 6–8 cycles. Your team reviews the benefit and side effects before continuing chemotherapy.

Maintenance · two medicines

Pembrolizumab and bevacizumab every three weeks while the treatment helps and side effects are manageable.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count, kidney and liver function. Review medicines, allergies and current symptoms.Bring a medicine list and details of previous treatment.
Before each cycleBlood counts and organ function are reviewed with symptoms and fitness for treatment.Tell your team if infection, tiredness or side effects limit normal activity.
Before bevacizumabBlood pressure, urine protein, bleeding, bowel symptoms and wound healing.Tell your team about surgery or dental procedures.
During immunotherapyBlood sugar, liver and kidney tests, and thyroid function every 3–6 weeks. Other hormone tests depend on symptoms.Report new headache, thirst, weakness, cough or diarrhoea even between visits.

Scan dates and tumour-marker testing are individualised by your oncology team.

At review visits

  • What will I receive at the next visit? Combination every 3 weeks, then pembrolizumab and bevacizumab.
  • When should I call? Call oncology urgently for fever, chills, new breathlessness or severe diarrhoea. Severe abdominal pain or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent review.
