Dacarbazine — metastatic melanoma

To control metastatic melanoma with chemotherapy, with benefit, blood counts and tolerability reviewed before each treatment.

Advanced melanoma · NCCP 00464 v3 · Document revised 6 November 2026

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

This is single-agent dacarbazine at the NCCP 850 mg/m² dose. It is not an immune-checkpoint or BRAF-targeted regimen. Historical studies often used 1000 mg/m² and different treatment settings; their response or survival figures are not a precise forecast for this 850 mg/m² course.

My overall treatment

Dacarbazine by IV infusion on day 1, repeated every 21 days for up to six cycles, or stopped earlier for progression or unacceptable toxicity.

  1. Treatment courseDay 1 every 21 days, up to 6 cycles; may stop earlier.

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

What to watch for

You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

A picture of side effects

Match each symptom box to its numbered body location. Tell your team about new symptoms.

1Energy / concentration
Dacarbazine

Tiredness, anaemia, poor sleep and nausea can affect concentration. Report severe dizziness, confusion or a major change in function; do not drive if unwell.

3Breathlessness / anaemia
Dacarbazine

Low red cells can cause tiredness or breathlessness. New or worsening breathlessness needs assessment; severe breathlessness or chest pain is an emergency.

5Liver
Dacarbazine

Rare severe liver injury can cause abdominal pain, fever and jaundice. Seek urgent assessment; routine liver tests do not replace symptom reporting.

7Stomach / appetite
Dacarbazine

Nausea and vomiting need preventive medicines and a take-home plan. Report persistent symptoms, severe abdominal pain or failure of rescue treatment.

9Bruising / bleeding
Dacarbazine

Low platelets can cause bruising, nosebleeds or bleeding gums. Persistent bleeding, black stools or blood in urine require prompt assessment.

2Blood / infection
Dacarbazine

Low white cells increase infection risk and may occur late in the cycle. Fever or suddenly feeling unwell needs urgent contact, even near the next planned dose.

4Skin / hair
Dacarbazine

Hair loss or sun sensitivity can occur. New severe rash, swelling or breathing difficulty may indicate allergy and needs urgent help.

6Kidneys / fluids
Dacarbazine

Vomiting and reduced intake can lead to dehydration. Report reduced urine, persistent vomiting or inability to drink; kidney tests also help guide dosing.

8Infusion site
Dacarbazine

Pain, burning, swelling or redness during infusion can mean leakage outside the vein. Tell the nurse immediately because tissue injury is possible.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
