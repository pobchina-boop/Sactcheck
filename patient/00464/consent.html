1. Treatment and purpose

Dacarbazine — metastatic melanoma · NCCP 00464 v3

Dacarbazine by IV infusion on day 1, repeated every 21 days for up to six cycles, or stopped earlier for progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Dacarbazine · Cytotoxic chemotherapy

Report fever or feeling acutely unwell, bleeding, persistent vomiting, severe abdominal pain or burning at the infusion site.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the exact diagnosis and treatment intent, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives, regimen-specific risks, tests, fertility, practical burdens and your preferences. This guide supports the consent conversation and does not replace it. This is single-agent dacarbazine at the NCCP 850 mg/m² dose. It is not an immune-checkpoint or BRAF-targeted regimen. Historical studies often used 1000 mg/m² and different treatment settings; their response or survival figures are not a precise forecast for this 850 mg/m² course.
