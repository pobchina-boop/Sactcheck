2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Dacarbazine

Cytotoxic chemotherapy
Nausea and vomiting
Low white cells, platelets or red cells
Tiredness and appetite loss
Infusion-site injury if medicine leaks
Rare severe liver injury or allergic reaction

Discuss the exact diagnosis and treatment intent, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives, regimen-specific risks, tests, fertility, practical burdens and your preferences. This guide supports the consent conversation and does not replace it. This is single-agent dacarbazine at the NCCP 850 mg/m² dose. It is not an immune-checkpoint or BRAF-targeted regimen. Historical studies often used 1000 mg/m² and different treatment settings; their response or survival figures are not a precise forecast for this 850 mg/m² course.
