Head and neck: chemoradiotherapy · NCCP 00591 · version 4 · 21-day cycle

Treatment context
Chemotherapy is given with radiation to improve control of cancer in the head and neck. Your team confirms the treatment aim and why carboplatin is suitable for your circumstances.
Schedule
Carboplatin AUC 4 on Day 1, with fluorouracil 600 mg/m²/day on Days 1–4, every 21 days for 3 cycles, only concurrently with radiotherapy.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts and serious infection
Low platelets and bleeding
Anaemia and fatigue
Nausea and vomiting
Infusion or allergic reactions
Kidney, nerve or hearing effects

Fluorouracil

Antimetabolite chemotherapy
Low blood counts and serious infection
Mouth and throat soreness
Diarrhoea and dehydration
Painful palms or soles
Heart problems including chest pain
Rare severe neurological or early excessive toxicity
