The whole treatment journey

Bevacizumab 5 mg/kg every 14 days: treatment and checks

NCCP 00813: recurrent malignant glioblastoma; off-label indication.

Bevacizumab is given into a vein on Day 1 of each 14-day cycle. Treatment continues while benefit and tolerance support it, with review before each infusion. No fixed total number of cycles is specified.
