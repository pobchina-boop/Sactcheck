When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Bevacizumab

Bevacizumab binds VEGF, a signal involved in making and maintaining blood vessels. Blocking this signal can change the blood supply and leakiness of tumour vessels. It can also affect normal vessels, which helps explain high blood pressure, protein in the urine, bleeding, clots and slower wound healing. A scan change does not by itself prove that the tumour has stopped growing.

Support at home

Bevacizumab support is separate

Bevacizumab alone has minimal emetic potential. Routine anti-sickness drugs or steroid premedication are not automatically required for every infusion; the team may prescribe specific medicines after a previous reaction or for another reason.

Blood pressure, urine and wound care

Keep prescribed blood-pressure medicine and urine checks. Follow the team’s plan for any home measurements. Report wounds and upcoming surgery or dental treatment early, so timing and healing can be assessed before another infusion.

Separate steroid, seizure and bowel plans

Keep prescribed steroids and seizure medicines on their own schedules. Ask about missed or vomited doses. Agree a bowel plan if other medicines cause constipation and check interactions before adding a medicine.
