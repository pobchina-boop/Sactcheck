Check your treatment setting

This patient pack covers bevacizumab 7.5 mg/kg for lung cancer. It is used with a separately prescribed chemotherapy plan; confirm that partner plan with your team. Colorectal and other settings need their own treatment information.

Lung cancer

Bevacizumab is given by drip on Day 1 every 21 days. In this lung setting it starts with platinum chemotherapy for up to 6 cycles, then may continue alone while helping and tolerable. Your team must supply the partner chemotherapy schedule.
