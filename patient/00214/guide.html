Bevacizumab 7.5 · lung

To help control advanced non-squamous lung cancer alongside the prescribed chemotherapy, then possibly as continuation treatment.

My overall treatment

Bevacizumab is given by drip on Day 1 every 21 days. In this lung setting it starts with platinum chemotherapy for up to 6 cycles, then may continue alone while helping and tolerable. Your team must supply the partner chemotherapy schedule.

  1. With chemotherapyUp to 6 three-week cycles with the named platinum regimen. Bring its schedule and safety plan too.
  2. Possible continuationBevacizumab alone every 3 weeks if the team confirms ongoing benefit and acceptable side effects. No fixed total number of cycles.

A typical 21-day cycle

Day 1Bevacizumab infusion
Day 2–21Between bevacizumab doses

Timing may change after review. Side effects can occur on any day; there are no guaranteed safe days.

Between treatments

  • Symptoms and blood-pressure changes can occur between appointments; attend the agreed checks.
  • During combination treatment, additional chemotherapy visits and side effects follow the partner plan.

Bring your medicine list and report new symptoms before dosing. Confirm test appointments, the current phase and daytime / out-of-hours contact plan.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
