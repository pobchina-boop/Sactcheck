When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

My treatment

My overall treatment

Bevacizumab is given by drip on Day 1 every 21 days. In this lung setting it starts with platinum chemotherapy for up to 6 cycles, then may continue alone while helping and tolerable. Your team must supply the partner chemotherapy schedule.

  1. With chemotherapyUp to 6 three-week cycles with the named platinum regimen. Bring its schedule and safety plan too.
  2. Possible continuationBevacizumab alone every 3 weeks if the team confirms ongoing benefit and acceptable side effects. No fixed total number of cycles.

More about my medicines

Bevacizumab

Bevacizumab binds VEGF, a signal that helps new blood vessels grow. It can slow tumour growth but also affect normal blood vessels and wound healing.

Support at home

Partner chemotherapy

This covers bevacizumab only. Bring the separate chemotherapy schedule, anti-sickness plan and urgent-contact instructions.

Blood pressure and urine

These checks can find problems before you feel unwell. Take blood-pressure medicines as prescribed; do not adjust them yourself.

Surgery and dental procedures

Tell oncology before any operation or invasive procedure. The team must agree treatment pauses and assess wound healing.

Support medicines

Bevacizumab alone does not usually need routine anti-sickness prevention. Chemotherapy partners may need a different plan.
