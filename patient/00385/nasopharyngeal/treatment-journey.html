The whole treatment journey

Weekly cisplatin + nasopharyngeal radiotherapy: treatment and checks

Chemoradiation for locally advanced nasopharyngeal carcinoma.

Cisplatin 40 mg/m² once a week for six planned doses during radiotherapy. Cisplatin is given before radiation on the same day. If radiotherapy is cancelled, cisplatin is held until radiation resumes; the team updates both calendars.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.
