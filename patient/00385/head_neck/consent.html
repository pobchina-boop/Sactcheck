1. Treatment and purpose

Weekly cisplatin + head and neck radiotherapy · NCCP 00385 v5

Cisplatin 40 mg/m² once a week for six planned doses during radiotherapy. Cisplatin is given before radiation on the same day. If radiotherapy is cancelled, cisplatin is held until radiation resumes; the team updates both calendars. Mouth, skin, swallowing, energy and hydration may need support after radiation ends. The team arranges response assessment and follows hearing, kidney or nerve problems. Any further chemotherapy is a separate prescription.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Cisplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report reduced urine, persistent vomiting, tinnitus, hearing change or new tingling.

Radiotherapy · Local treatment

Report difficulty swallowing, weight loss, mouth pain, skin breakdown or problems managing saliva.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the individual aim, alternatives and uncertainty; serious or lasting harms; treatment burden; fertility and pregnancy precautions; and the patient’s priorities. Explain blood-count suppression/infection, nausea, kidney/mineral injury, hearing loss and neuropathy that can persist, hydration and same-day radiation coordination. Discuss radiation mouth, skin, swallowing, saliva, dental and jaw effects, including lasting effects and potential nutritional support. Radiation has its own consent process. Confirm actual urgent contacts and teach-back; this draft does not authorise treatment.
