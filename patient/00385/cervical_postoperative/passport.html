My treatment passport

Cisplatin with radiotherapy after cervical cancer surgery

My treatment record

A lymph node removed during surgery contained cancer. Radiation and weekly cisplatin aim to reduce the chance of cancer returning.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Weekly cisplatin during postoperative radiotherapyWeekly cisplatin during postoperative radiotherapyCisplatin runs into a vein over about one hour. Fluids before and after it protect the kidneys. Your visit also includes blood review and anti-sickness treatment.

My questions and next steps

  • Why do I need treatment after surgery? The lymph node result suggests that microscopic cancer cells may remain. Radiation treats the area at risk, and cisplatin helps the radiation damage these cells.
  • How will the team decide what happens next? Your radiation plan is based on the surgical pathology. Ask whether a radiation boost is needed and who will review bladder, bowel or pelvic symptoms after treatment.
