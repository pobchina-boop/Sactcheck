The whole treatment journey

CMV · before definitive bladder treatment: treatment and checks

Bladder cancer · neoadjuvant CMV

Methotrexate and vinblastine are given on days 1 and 8; cisplatin on day 2. Up to three 21-day cycles are planned before definitive local treatment. Folinic acid 15 mg is prescribed every six hours for four doses, starting 24 hours after each methotrexate dose (days 2 and 9). Confirm the written prescription.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewDays 1, 2 and 8 · every 21 days · up to three cycles

Treat possible microscopic spread before definitive local treatment for muscle-invasive bladder cancer.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999.
