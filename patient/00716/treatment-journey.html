SACTCheck · NCCP 00716 v2 · Advanced cervical cancer

Your treatment journey

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg with carboplatin and paclitaxel

Control the cancer, help relieve symptoms and potentially prolong life.

Your infusion schedule

Combination · day 1

Paclitaxel, carboplatin and bevacizumab 15 mg/kg every 21 days for up to six cycles. Omit bevacizumab in cycle 1 if starting within four weeks of surgery.

Bevacizumab alone

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg alone every 21 days. Continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

At review visits

  • Which medicines will I receive at my next visit? Day 1 every 21 days, then bevacizumab alone.
  • When should I call immediately? Call the oncology unit now for fever, chills, severe abdominal pain, unexpected bleeding or new breathlessness.
