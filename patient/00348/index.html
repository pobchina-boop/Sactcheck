This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Four 21-day cycles: paclitaxel on Days 1, 8 and 15; carboplatin AUC 6 on Day 1. Four further cycles (cycles 5–8): doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide on Day 1 every 14 days. granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF) support is required for every dose-dense AC cycle; the team prescribes its product and timing separately. Surgery and pathology review follow chemotherapy. Further systemic treatment, radiotherapy and follow-up depend on the findings and the multidisciplinary team (MDT) plan; no routine postoperative immunotherapy is specified by this regimen.

Relative sequence, not booked dates or equal time intervals. Baseline ECG is distinct from risk-based echo. No routine three-monthly CT or echo is implied. The team books and reviews results.

Between treatment visits

Treatment markers show the planned days. Symptom bands are broad possibilities, not measured risk curves; serious symptoms can occur at any time.
