1. Treatment and purpose

Gemcitabine + split-dose cisplatin · NCCP 00622 v3

Gemcitabine and cisplatin are both given on days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle, usually for four to six cycles. Cisplatin is split into two 35 mg/m² doses; do not use another GemCis calendar.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Gemcitabine · Chemotherapy

Report fever, unusual bleeding, breathlessness, rash, swelling or reduced urine promptly.

Cisplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report ringing in the ears, hearing changes, numbness or loss of function. Tell the team if you cannot drink, are passing less urine or become swollen or breathless during fluids.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the intended benefit, alternatives, common and serious risks, lasting effects, fertility and individual priorities. Record the patient’s questions and the agreed plan. NCCP 00622 uses cisplatin 35 mg/m² on both days 1 and 8. Its impaired-renal-function indication and creatinine clearance (CrCl) <60 exclusion need clinical reconciliation; a split dose does not itself prove cisplatin is safe.
