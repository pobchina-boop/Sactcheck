Your treatment journey

Paclitaxel and carboplatin every three weeks

After surgery the aim may be to reduce the risk of recurrence. With advanced or recurrent disease the aim may be to shrink or control the cancer.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1

Both infusions every 21 days, for six planned cycles unless treatment ends earlier.
