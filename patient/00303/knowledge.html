Your complete treatment reference

Paclitaxel and carboplatin every three weeks

Your treatment and how the medicines work

After surgery the aim may be to reduce the risk of recurrence. With advanced or recurrent disease the aim may be to shrink or control the cancer.

Paclitaxel · Paclitaxel

Taxane chemotherapy. It disrupts cell division. Hair follicles, blood cells and nerves can also be affected. Report numbness and tell the nurse immediately if you flush or wheeze during treatment.

Carboplatin · Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. It damages DNA in dividing cells. Kidney function helps determine the prescribed amount. Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1

Both infusions every 21 days, for six planned cycles unless treatment ends earlier.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shaking chills, unusual bleeding or sudden illness? Call the oncology unit now. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Infusion premedicines

Confirm whether to take dexamethasone at home before your visit. The unit gives anti-allergy medicines and checks for reactions.

Nausea prevention

Use the written carboplatin anti-sickness plan and have the prescribed rescue medicine available. Phone if you cannot keep fluids down.

Planning your visits

At the day unit

Paclitaxel runs first over about three hours, then carboplatin over about half an hour. Allow time for premedicines and checks.

If a visit changes

Your team will tell you if a visit moves after a delay. Do not work out a new date yourself.

Before starting treatment

Discuss fertility, contraception and breastfeeding with your team. Bring your current medicines and details of previous infusion reactions.

What the research found

In patients with optimally resected stage III ovarian cancer, the carboplatin combination was easier to tolerate and achieved comparable cancer control to cisplatin with paclitaxel.

The trial population was specific to ovarian cancer. Other indications have their own evidence and treatment goals.
