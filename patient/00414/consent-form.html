Palbociclib with endocrine therapy

The usual palbociclib starting dose is 125 mg once daily; a lower dose may be prescribed. Palbociclib is taken once daily on Days 1–21, then paused on Days 22–28 of each 28-day cycle. The aromatase inhibitor continues daily, or fulvestrant follows its injection schedule. Ovarian suppression has its own plan when required.

Discussion aid: review with the patient and complete the approved local consent process.

Purpose and plan

□ Exact indication, treatment aim and uncertainty explained
□ Expected duration and review / switch points discussed
□ Alternatives and the option not to proceed discussed
□ Patient priorities, capacity and support needs explored

Practical safety

□ Medicine, route, schedule and missed-dose instructions
□ Menopause and ovarian-suppression requirements if relevant
□ Pregnancy, contraception / fertility and breastfeeding
□ Monitoring, symptom reporting and contact plan

Selected material risks

□ Low neutrophils and infection risk
□ Fatigue
□ Mouth soreness
□ Nausea or diarrhoea
□ Hair thinning
□ Rare but serious interstitial lung disease / pneumonitis
□ Hot flushes and other hormone-related symptoms
□ AI: joint symptoms and bone loss
□ Fulvestrant: injection-site pain and bruising
□ Partner-specific liver, interaction and pregnancy considerations

Discuss the treatment aim, alternatives, benefit and uncertainty, important medicine-specific risks, reproductive precautions, symptom support and planned duration. Confirm the endocrine partner and any ovarian-suppression plan. The timeline does not authorise a restart or certify clinical review.

Individual benefit, alternatives and concerns

Agreed checks and next review

FBC, renal and liver profile. Confirm receptor status, exact endocrine partner, previous endocrine therapy, menopause/ovarian suppression, pregnancy precautions and interacting medicines. FBC on Day 1 and Day 15 of cycles 1–2; Day 1 from cycle 3 and extra checks as indicated. Only after at most grade 1–2 neutropenia throughout the first six cycles may FBC monitoring reduce to every three months, before the start of a cycle and as indicated. Renal and liver profile before each cycle; review fever/infection, mouth/bowel symptoms, fatigue, pulmonary symptoms and adherence. No routine palbociclib-specific Day-14 ECG or echo timetable; investigate when clinically indicated.
