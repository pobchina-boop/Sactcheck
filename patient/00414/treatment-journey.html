Palbociclib with endocrine therapy

Take palbociclib for 21 days, then have 7 days without palbociclib. The aromatase-inhibitor tablet continues every day, or fulvestrant injections follow their separate schedule. The week off applies to palbociclib, not automatically to the hormone treatment.

What each check is for

1Blood testsBefore the first dose: FBC, kidney and liver bloods; review other medicines, pregnancy possibility, receptor results, menopausal status and the exact endocrine partner. Confirm ovarian suppression when relevant. FBC on Day 1 and Day 15 of cycles 1–2, then Day 1 from cycle 3. Kidney/liver bloods before each cycle. If counts are low, extra blood tests and a pause may be needed. Only after no worse than grade 1/2 neutropenia in the first 6 cycles may FBC frequency reduce to every 3 months before a cycle, with additional tests when clinically indicated.

Checks safety before continuation.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

2Heart checksPalbociclib does not have the routine ECG timetable used for ribociclib. ECG or echo is arranged if symptoms, another medicine or individual cardiac risk make it appropriate.

Checks cardiac safety when the agent or clinical risk requires it.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

3ImagingResponse assessment in advanced breast cancer is generally every two to four months, adapted to disease dynamics, sites, symptoms and treatment. Less frequent monitoring may suit indolent disease. New symptoms or suspected progression need earlier assessment. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not an automatic three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

Uses the correct surveillance or response-assessment pathway.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: ESMO_MBC2023

4Bone healthBone-health monitoring belongs to the endocrine partner, ovarian suppression and personal fracture risk. If taking an aromatase inhibitor, agree the baseline bone-density assessment and follow-up plan with the team; palbociclib itself has no fixed DXA interval.

Companion endocrine therapy and individual fracture risk may change the plan.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC
