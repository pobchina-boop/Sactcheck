Palbociclib with endocrine therapy

The usual palbociclib starting dose is 125 mg once daily; a lower dose may be prescribed. Palbociclib is taken once daily on Days 1–21, then paused on Days 22–28 of each 28-day cycle. The aromatase inhibitor continues daily, or fulvestrant follows its injection schedule. Ovarian suppression has its own plan when required.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact the treatment team promptly if you feel feverish or unwell, have shaking chills or infection symptoms, or develop new cough or breathlessness. Follow the local emergency temperature instructions; do not wait for a planned blood test or for the 38.5°C value used in one clinician dose-adjustment table. Severe breathing difficulty needs emergency care.
