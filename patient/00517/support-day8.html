Additional phase-specific support

  • Gemcitabine runs over 30 minutes, followed on Day 1 by cisplatin over 2 hours. Cisplatin requires prescribed fluids before and after treatment. Day 8 is gemcitabine alone.
  • Day 1: full blood count, kidney and liver tests. Day 8: full blood count and creatinine. Hearing assessment and measured kidney clearance are arranged when indicated.
  • Confirm the full treatment sequence and any separate radiotherapy plan. The linked NCCP document gives 4–6 cycles; this is not the three-cycle 00903 induction schedule.
  • The local CUH source is retained as a reference list, not as the national antiemetic standard. Nystatin/chlorhexidine are displayed as local mouth-care adjuncts, not universal antiemetic or mucositis prophylaxis. Verify current local oral-care policy.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
