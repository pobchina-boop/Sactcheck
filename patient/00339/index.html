My treatment at a glance · NCCP 00339

Vinorelbine and cisplatin · 21-day cycle

After surgery, treatment aims to lower the chance of cancer returning. For advanced or recurrent lung cancer, it aims to control disease and symptoms. Your team should mark which goal applies to you; benefit is not guaranteed.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately for temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions.

Cannot reach the team? Seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Your treatment plan

A cycle lasts 21 days (3 weeks). Vinorelbine is given on Days 1, 8, 15. Cisplatin is given on Day 1. Four cycles are planned, with review before doses. Delays or omitted doses may be needed for safety.
