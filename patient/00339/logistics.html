Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm weekly blood tests, transport, medicines and your daytime/out-of-hours oncology numbers.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Vinorelbine is infused over15 minutes, followed by a saline flush. Cisplatin is infused over about one hour on its treatment days, with fluids before and afterwards. Checks, preparation and support medicines make the appointment longer than the infusion itself.

STEP 3

intravenous (IV) access

Suitable IV access is required. No routine take-home pump. Tell the nurse promptly about pain or burning.

STEP 4

Home support

Follow your prescribed anti-sickness and constipation-prevention plan. Cisplatin days need kidney-protecting fluids and electrolyte checks. Do not repeat the full cisplatin anti-sickness course on a vinorelbine-only day unless prescribed.

STEP 5

After the course

Agree follow-up, scans and how to report persistent problems.
