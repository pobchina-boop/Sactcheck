The whole treatment journey

Vinorelbine and cisplatin · 21-day cycle: treatment and checks

Postoperative adjuvant or advanced/recurrent NSCLC: confirm your treatment goal

A cycle lasts 21 days (3 weeks). Vinorelbine is given on Days 1, 8, 15. Cisplatin is given on Day 1. Four cycles are planned, with review before doses. Delays or omitted doses may be needed for safety.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.
