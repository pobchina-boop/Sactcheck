1. Treatment and purpose

Carboplatin + Etoposide · NCCP 00271 v6

Carboplatin is planned on Day 1. Etoposide is planned on Days 1–3: usually all three days by infusion, or a prescribed alternative with an infusion on Day 1 and capsules twice daily on Days 2–3. The source describes 4–6 cycles every 21 days, or stopping for cancer progression or unacceptable side effects. Your team confirms the intended course and may change it earlier.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Carboplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report changes in urine, hearing, ringing in the ears, numbness or daily function. Tell the team about infection, unusual bruising or bleeding and any symptoms during the infusion.

Etoposide · Topoisomerase II chemotherapy

Report fever, bleeding, mouth sores or diarrhoea. Tell the nurse about faintness, rash or breathing difficulty during an infusion. If capsules are prescribed, use the exact pharmacy instructions and contact the team about a missed or vomited dose.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the individual aim, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, serious and lasting risks, kidney/hearing/nerve injury, infection/bleeding, fertility and pregnancy advice, infusion versus capsule logistics, delays and dose changes, and support at home. Confirm that any additional immunotherapy or radiotherapy has its own consent discussion.
