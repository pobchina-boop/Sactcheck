Practical treatment guide

Infusion visits and care at home

One infusion visit per planned 14-day cycle; no home chemotherapy pump.

STEP 1

Before the first visit

Confirm RAS testing, blood tests, minerals and the treatment discussion.

STEP 2

Prepare your skin plan

Collect prescribed creams or medicines and ask how to use them.

STEP 3

At the day ward

Blood results and symptoms are reviewed. A cannula or existing line is checked.

STEP 4

Receive the infusion

The first infusion usually takes about an hour; the whole visit is longer. Tell the nurse about any reaction.

STEP 5

At home

Continue skin care and report eye, breathing, bowel or skin problems early.

STEP 6

Next review and after treatment

Confirm the next visit and mineral monitoring for about eight weeks after the final dose.

Do I need a port?

Panitumumab alone does not require a home pump. A peripheral cannula or existing central line may be used; your team chooses access based on your veins and wider plan.

Infusion time can change

Large doses or reactions can require a longer infusion. Later infusions may be shorter if the first was tolerated. Blood tests, review and preparation add to your time in the unit.
