Dactinomycin for low-risk GTN

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Treat the trophoblastic tumour with the aim of cure. Serial blood hCG measures how the disease responds. An alternative after methotrexate resistance in low-risk GTN (FIGO score 6 or below) when hCG is below 1,000 IU/L.

Treatment schedule. One injection into a vein on day 1 of a 14-day cycle.

Regimen-specific side effects

Dactinomycin

Treatment discussion and decision

Dactinomycin for low-risk GTN

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

A specialist-centre series supports activity after methotrexate failure. The study used a five-day regimen. The Irish pulse dose and schedule come from NCCP 00247.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. A burning or painful IV site must be checked immediately, including redness or blistering after going home. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
