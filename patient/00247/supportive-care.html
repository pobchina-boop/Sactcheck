Dactinomycin

Tell the nurse immediately if the injection site burns or stings.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. A burning or painful IV site must be checked immediately, including redness or blistering after going home. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
