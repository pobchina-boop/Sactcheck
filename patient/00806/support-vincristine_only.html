Additional phase-specific support

  • NCCP recommends preventive laxatives with vincristine. Report constipation, new tingling, weakness, balance or hand-function changes before each visit. Severe abdominal pain, vomiting or inability to pass stool or wind needs urgent assessment.
  • Have all medicines checked, including seizure drugs, steroids, antibiotics, antifungals, non-prescription products and supplements. The team will confirm the local antifungal prevention plan and choose medicines that avoid vincristine interactions. Other infection prevention follows the individual specialist plan.
  • Do not automatically repeat cisplatin hydration or its high-risk antiemetic course on vincristine-only days. Prior symptoms and the local individual plan can require additional support.
  • Draft for clinician/pharmacist completion, not an issued prescription. Confirm agents, doses, timing, duration, interactions and rescue instructions with the current written plan.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
