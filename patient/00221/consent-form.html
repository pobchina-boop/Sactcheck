Which treatment this guide covers NCCP 00221a covers adults with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC with an activating EGFR mutation who have not received an EGFR TKI. The separate licensed squamous-cancer indication after platinum treatment is not included in this NCCP regimen or its evidence card.
NCCP 00221 · version 5

Afatinib monotherapy

The usual starting dose is 40 mg by mouth once daily, continuously without a planned rest week. Use the dose on your prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; any dose change is agreed with your team.

General treatment discussion

Possible short-term, long-term, unexpected and unlisted effects; benefits are not guaranteed.
Severe complications can require hospital admission and can rarely be fatal.
Infection / sepsis, allergic or infusion reactions and leakage outside a vein (extravasation) should be discussed where applicable to the medicines and route.
Pregnancy, contraception, breastfeeding, sexual health and fertility preservation should be discussed where relevant.
Treatment can be delayed, changed or stopped if risks outweigh benefit. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn.

Individual benefit, alternatives and concerns

Agreed checks and next review

Confirm the activating EGFR test, previous treatments and prescription. Check blood count, kidneys and liver. A heart-pumping test (ECHO or MUGA) is used if clinically indicated. Review lungs, bowel, eyes, skin, all medicines and pregnancy precautions. NCCP specifies blood count, kidney and liver tests monthly. Heart-function tests are repeated if clinically indicated. Report diarrhoea and skin changes early, especially in the first weeks. Scans and cancer reviews follow your Consultant’s plan.
