NCCP 00221 · version 5

Afatinib monotherapy

The usual starting dose is 40 mg by mouth once daily, continuously without a planned rest week. Use the dose on your prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; any dose change is agreed with your team.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Afatinib blocks abnormal EGFR-family growth signals in selected lung cancers. Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and delay progression. NCCP 00221a covers adults with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC with an activating EGFR mutation who have not received an EGFR TKI. The separate licensed squamous-cancer indication after platinum treatment is not included in this NCCP regimen or its evidence card.

Material risks and support

  • Diarrhoea, dehydration and kidney problems
  • Rash, dry skin and nail changes
  • Mouth soreness and reduced appetite
  • Serious lung inflammation
  • Liver injury
  • Rare serious skin reactions and bowel perforation
  • Eye inflammation

Discuss the aim of advanced cancer control, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, bowel and skin effects, serious lung/liver problems, fertility and pregnancy, daily treatment, monitoring and your right to reconsider.

Monitoring and understanding

Confirm the activating EGFR test, previous treatments and prescription. Check blood count, kidneys and liver. A heart-pumping test (ECHO or MUGA) is used if clinically indicated. Review lungs, bowel, eyes, skin, all medicines and pregnancy precautions. NCCP specifies blood count, kidney and liver tests monthly. Heart-function tests are repeated if clinically indicated. Report diarrhoea and skin changes early, especially in the first weeks. Scans and cancer reviews follow your Consultant’s plan.
