The whole treatment journey

Akeega and prednisolone: BRCA-selected prostate treatment: treatment and checks

BRCA1/2-mutated metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer when chemotherapy is not clinically indicated.

Usual starting dose: two Akeega 100/500 mg tablets once daily, plus prednisolone 10 mg daily. Continue your hormone injections unless you have had surgical castration. Treatment is continuous, with no routine rest week.
