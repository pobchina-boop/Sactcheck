Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Confirm BRCA1/2 result and cancer setting. Check full blood count, kidney/liver tests, potassium, glucose and blood pressure; review heart disease, medicines and bone health. electrocardiogram (ECG) or echocardiogram is arranged when indicated.

STEP 2

Taking or receiving treatment

Take Akeega whole with water at least two hours after food, then wait at least one hour before eating. Take prednisolone with food. If a dose is missed, take it as soon as possible on the same day; do not take extra tablets. Check with the team after vomiting. The two Akeega strengths are not interchangeable.

STEP 3

Tests and reviews

Blood count: weekly in month 1, fortnightly in months 2-3, then monthly to year 1 and every two months thereafter. Liver tests: fortnightly for months 1-3, then monthly to year 1 and every two months thereafter. Blood pressure: weekly for two months, then monthly to year 1 and every two months thereafter. Potassium: monthly to year 1, then every two months.

STEP 4

Specific instructions

Do not stop prednisolone suddenly, including when Akeega is held. Ask about steroid cover during illness. Dose reductions differ for low blood counts and liver injury; always follow the exact tablet strength and number on the revised prescription. Use condoms with a pregnant partner, or condoms plus another effective method with a partner who could become pregnant, during treatment and for four months after the last dose.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the exact dose, next dates, duration and verified daytime/out-of-hours contact numbers.
