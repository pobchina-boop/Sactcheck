Vinorelbine and cisplatin · 28-day cycle

After surgery, treatment aims to lower the chance of cancer returning. For advanced or recurrent lung cancer, it aims to control disease and symptoms. Your team should mark which goal applies to you; benefit is not guaranteed.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

A cycle lasts 28 days (4 weeks). Vinorelbine is given on Days 1, 8, 15, 22. Cisplatin is given on Days 1 and 8. Four cycles are planned, with review before doses. Delays or omitted doses may be needed for safety.

Tests and checks

Blood count every week; kidney and liver tests before each cycle. Report hearing changes, tingling, bowel problems and poor fluid intake before every dose. Extra tests and scans follow your individual plan.

Between treatment visits

Calendar shows planned visits only. No patient dates are required.

Call your oncology team immediately for fever or sudden illness, shaking chills, breathing difficulty, chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, very little urine, severe abdominal pain or constipation with vomiting. Tell the nurse immediately about burning or pain at the infusion site. Do not wait until the next visit.
