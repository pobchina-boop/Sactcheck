Additional phase-specific support

  • Cisplatin requires high-risk antiemetics and individual hydration/electrolyte review.
  • Folic acid and B12 must be in place before pemetrexed and continue into maintenance.
  • Combine rash and antiemetic dexamethasone directions into one reconciled prescription; do not stack courses.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
