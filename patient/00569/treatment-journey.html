The whole treatment journey

Pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + cisplatin: treatment and checks

First-line metastatic non-squamous NSCLC without EGFR or ALK alterations

Induction: all three medicines on Day 1 every 21 days for up to four cycles. Maintenance: cisplatin stops; pembrolizumab and pemetrexed may continue every 21 days while appropriate. The team agrees duration and review points.
