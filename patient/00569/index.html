02 / treatment journey

How the schedule fits together

Cycles 1-4 · day 1

Pembrolizumab 200 mg + pemetrexed 500 mg/m² + cisplatin 75 mg/m² every 21 days.

Cisplatin phase

Cisplatin is given in cycles 1-4 with hydration and electrolyte support.

Maintenance

After the platinum phase, pembrolizumab + pemetrexed continue every 21 days when appropriate.

Throughout

Immune toxicities can occur during treatment or after treatment stops.

A delay, dose change or omitted medicine can be appropriate. Your team should explain what changed and why.

04 / monitoring and support

What helps make treatment safer

Before and during treatment

  • Folic acid and vitamin B12 support are required with pemetrexed.
  • Cisplatin requires hydration/electrolyte management and high-emetogenic-risk antiemetic support.

Bring to each review

  • Blood count, kidney/liver profile and glucose are checked; thyroid monitoring continues during pembrolizumab.
  • Report immune symptoms early rather than waiting for the next cycle.
05 / when to call

Do not wait for the next appointment

Use your oncology SOS instructionsContact oncology urgently for fever, new breathlessness, persistent diarrhoea, jaundice, severe rash, chest pain, marked weakness, inability to maintain fluids, reduced urine or new neurological symptoms. For severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain call 112/999.

If your team has given you different local thresholds or emergency instructions, follow those instructions.
