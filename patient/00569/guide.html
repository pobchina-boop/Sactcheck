Pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + cisplatin

Induction: all three medicines on Day 1 every 21 days for up to four cycles. Maintenance: cisplatin stops; pembrolizumab and pemetrexed may continue every 21 days while appropriate. The team agrees duration and review points.

NCCP 00569 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Pembrolizumab

  • Immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver, kidneys, glands, skin and other organs
  • Serious infusion reactions
  • Effects can develop after treatment ends

Pemetrexed

  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Fatigue, nausea, mouth soreness and rash
  • Kidney toxicity; folate and B12 supplementation are required

Cisplatin

  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Kidney injury and electrolyte changes
  • Hearing loss or tinnitus
  • Numbness/tingling and other nerve effects
  • Low blood counts and infection

Make the plan practical

Induction support

You receive anti-sickness medicines and a fluid plan around cisplatin. Take prescribed folic acid, B12 injections and rash-prevention steroids. Ask for one clear steroid schedule.

Maintenance support

Folic acid, B12 and rash-prevention medicines continue with pemetrexed; cisplatin fluids and some anti-sickness medicines may stop. Immune symptom vigilance continues.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
