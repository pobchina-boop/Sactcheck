1. Treatment and purpose

Pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + cisplatin · NCCP 00569 v5

Induction: all three medicines on Day 1 every 21 days for up to four cycles. Maintenance: cisplatin stops; pembrolizumab and pemetrexed may continue every 21 days while appropriate. The team agrees duration and review points.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Pembrolizumab · Immunotherapy

The immune system can also inflame healthy organs. New cough, diarrhoea, unusual weakness, severe headache, thirst or a spreading rash need prompt advice. Effects can start during treatment or after it finishes.

Pemetrexed · Chemotherapy

Healthy tissues can be affected too: low blood counts, a sore mouth, rash and kidney problems are important. Folic acid, vitamin B12 and prescribed steroids help reduce particular risks.

Cisplatin · Chemotherapy

Report reduced urine, ringing in the ears, hearing changes or tingling before the next treatment. Tell the nurse immediately about swelling or pain around the drip, rash, chest tightness or feeling suddenly unwell.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss expected benefit, alternatives and important short- and longer-term risks. Tell the team about all medicines, existing illnesses, pregnancy possibility and symptoms that matter to you.
