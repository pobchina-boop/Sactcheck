The whole treatment journey

Tivozanib for kidney cancer: treatment and checks

Advanced clear-cell renal cell carcinoma: first line or after one cytokine, without previous VEGF/mTOR-targeted treatment

Take one 1340 microgram capsule daily on Days 1–21, then no tivozanib on Days 22–28. A new cycle starts only when your team confirms it. A reduced dose or alternate-day liver-impairment plan needs its own written calendar.

What each check is for

1Treatment review1340 microgram daily: 21 days on, 7 days off

Help control advanced kidney cancer in the treatment setting agreed with your consultant.

Ask / act: Call your oncology team promptly for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting/diarrhoea or new breathlessness. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, seizures or stroke symptoms: call 112/999.
