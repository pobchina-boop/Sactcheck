Tivozanib for kidney cancer

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00564 · Source-reconciled feasibility draft; independent clinician, pharmacy and patient review pending.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Confirm the clear-cell diagnosis and previous treatments. Full blood count, kidney and liver tests, calcium, magnesium, potassium, thyroid tests, blood pressure, urine protein and a heart tracing (electrocardiogram (ECG)).

STEP 2

Dose and timing

Take one 1340 microgram capsule daily on Days 1–21, then no tivozanib on Days 22–28. A new cycle starts only when your team confirms it. A reduced dose or alternate-day liver-impairment plan needs its own written calendar.

STEP 3

Practical care

Swallow the capsule whole with water, with or without food. If a dose is missed or vomited, do not take a replacement; take the next scheduled dose normally. Do not use St John’s wort. Check every other medicine and supplement with pharmacy.

STEP 4

Support medicines

No routine premedication is required. Confirm that you understand the seven-day break and have the correct capsule strength. Keep a dated dosing calendar and blood-pressure record if requested. Contact the team for persistent diarrhoea, reduced intake, severe tiredness or painful palms/soles. Tell the team before surgery; restart after a break for surgery requires a healing review.

STEP 5

Before leaving

Confirm your next tests, dose, review date and verified daytime/out-of-hours contact numbers.
