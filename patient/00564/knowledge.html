When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Call your oncology team promptly for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting/diarrhoea or new breathlessness. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, seizures or stroke symptoms: call 112/999.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

Understand your treatment

How your medicine works

Help control advanced kidney cancer in the treatment setting agreed with your consultant.

Targeted therapy: VEGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor

Tivozanib

Fotivda
Tivozanib blocks VEGFR signals involved in growing blood vessels. This reduces the support available to the tumour. Effects on healthy blood vessels explain why blood pressure, bleeding, wounds and urine protein need monitoring.

Make the plan practical

Support around the medicines

Preparation and symptom support

No routine premedication is required. Confirm that you understand the seven-day break and have the correct capsule strength. Keep a dated dosing calendar and blood-pressure record if requested. Contact the team for persistent diarrhoea, reduced intake, severe tiredness or painful palms/soles. Tell the team before surgery; restart after a break for surgery requires a healing review.

Safe medicine use

Swallow the capsule whole with water, with or without food. If a dose is missed or vomited, do not take a replacement; take the next scheduled dose normally. Do not use St John’s wort. Check every other medicine and supplement with pharmacy.

Fertility and contraception

Discuss fertility and avoid pregnancy. Use effective contraception during treatment and for at least one month afterwards; hormonal contraception should be combined with a barrier method. Do not breastfeed during treatment; agree the post-treatment interval with the team.

Your wider treatment

Treatment options depend on previous medicines, cancer behaviour, general health and your priorities. Surgery or focused radiotherapy may be appropriate for selected problems; they do not replace this systemic medicine automatically.

Treatment reference · 04

Benefits, uncertainty and your choices

Evidence in this setting

TIVO-1 compared tivozanib with sorafenib in 517 patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma and no prior VEGF/mTOR-targeted therapy. Median progression-free survival was 11.9 versus 9.1 months (hazard ratio 0.797; 95% confidence interval 0.639–0.993). This supports delayed progression in that population; it does not establish an overall-survival advantage or compare modern immunotherapy combinations.

Sources: nccp · smpc
