Pemetrexed + Cisplatin

Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers pemetrexed and cisplatin chemotherapy. It does not include an immunotherapy medicine. If nivolumab, pembrolizumab or another medicine is also prescribed, obtain its separate regimen, consent, monitoring and emergency instructions. Mesothelioma and NSCLC have different aims and evidence; ask which indication is on your prescription.

Additional phase-specific support

  • High emetic risk driven by cisplatin. Use general high-risk NCIS V8 row.
  • Reconcile pemetrexed rash and antiemetic dexamethasone into one prescription.
  • Folic acid daily and B12 every three cycles; continue folate21days beyond the last pemetrexed.
  • Cisplatin requires pre/post hydration and electrolyte review under local policy.
  • An additional nivolumab or other drug needs its own regimen and support plan.
  • When platinum stops or another phase begins, review the antiemetic plan; do not carry the entire high-risk course into pemetrexed-only maintenance automatically.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
