When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately for temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions.

Cannot reach the team? Seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Pemetrexed and cisplatin are given by drip on Day 1, then repeated every 21 days (3 weeks), if your team confirms it is safe.

  1. Before startingBlood tests and a review of your health. Start folic acid and arrange your vitamin B12 injection.
  2. Treatment and time at homeBoth medicines on Day 1. Extra intravenous (IV) fluids help protect your kidneys. Follow your medicines plan at home.
  3. Review and next stepsUsually 4–6 cycles for the chemotherapy-only course. Your team confirms the number and the next step. Pemetrexed maintenance, if planned, is a separate phase.

Before and between visits

  • Start folic acid and arrange vitamin B12 before treatment; follow your prescription.
  • Ask for one clear steroid and anti-sickness schedule; do not add duplicate steroid courses.
  • Tell your team about hearing problems, numbness, other medicines and any infection.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

Call urgently for fever or shaking chills, sudden illness, new or worsening breathlessness, chest pain, uncontrolled bleeding, severe or persistent diarrhoea, inability to drink, very little urine, a spreading or blistering rash, or swelling/wheeze during an infusion. Tell the team promptly about new tinnitus, hearing change, numbness, weakness or difficulty walking.
