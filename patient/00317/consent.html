1. Treatment and purpose

Pemetrexed + Cisplatin · NCCP 00317 v7

Both chemotherapy medicines are planned on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle. For pleural mesothelioma, the source describes up to 4–6 cycles. For advanced non-squamous NSCLC, it describes 4–6 combination cycles followed, when appropriate, by a separate pemetrexed maintenance plan (NCCP00222). The selected preoperative nivolumab combination uses three cycles and a separate immunotherapy plan (NCCP00849). Your team confirms which course applies and may stop or change it earlier.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Pemetrexed · Antifolate chemotherapy

Take the prescribed folic acid and attend for B12. Report mouth sores, diarrhoea, a rash, breathlessness or reduced urine; check before taking ibuprofen or other non-prescription medicines.

Cisplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report ringing in the ears, hearing changes, numbness or loss of function. Tell the team if you cannot drink, are passing less urine or become swollen or breathless during fluids.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Confirm the treatment aim and indication, likely benefit and uncertainty, reasonable alternatives including not proceeding, serious and lasting risks, kidney/hearing/nerve problems, fertility and pregnancy advice, vitamin and steroid plans, and the chance of delays or dose changes.
