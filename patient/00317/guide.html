Pemetrexed + cisplatin

Lung · NCCP 00317 · version 7
Treatment journey
Day 1

Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² IV, followed by cisplatin 75 mg/m² IV with pre- and post-hydration.

Every 21 days

Treatment repeats every 21 days.

Mesothelioma

Usually up to 4-6 cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

NSCLC

Usually 4-6 cycles, then maintenance pemetrexed (NCCP 00222) where appropriate.

Important medicine-specific effects

Pemetrexed

  • Low blood counts can increase infection, anaemia or bruising risk.
  • Mouth/gut inflammation and skin rash can occur.
  • Kidney function can deteriorate and is monitored.

Cisplatin

  • Kidney injury and low magnesium/potassium can occur.
  • Hearing loss or ringing in the ears may be permanent.
  • Numbness/tingling and marked nausea/vomiting can occur.
Other / shared treatment effects: The anatomy page highlights important symptom patterns for this exact regimen, but it cannot show every possible effect. Fatigue, nausea, appetite change, mouth/bowel symptoms or blood-count changes may occur depending on the medicines used. Report new or worsening symptoms even if they are not listed here.

Monitoring / medicines

  • Folic acid and vitamin B12 support are required with pemetrexed.
  • Cisplatin requires hydration and electrolyte management according to local policy.
  • Blood count, kidney and liver profile are checked before each cycle.

Bring / track

  • Audiology may be considered where clinically indicated.
  • Use the regimen-specific high-emetogenic-risk antiemetic prescription.

When to call - do not wait for the next appointment

Contact oncology urgently for fever, inability to keep fluids down, marked reduction in urine, severe weakness, new hearing change, severe numbness/weakness, significant bleeding or rapidly worsening symptoms. For a medical emergency call 112/999.
