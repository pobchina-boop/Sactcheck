Treatment context
Pemetrexed + Cisplatin is chemotherapy used for selected people with unresectable pleural mesothelioma or non-squamous non-small-cell lung cancer. The aim may be disease control or, within a separate combined preoperative plan, support for surgery. Ask your team to explain the expected benefit for your own diagnosis.
Schedule
Both chemotherapy medicines are planned on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle. For pleural mesothelioma, the source describes up to 4–6 cycles. For advanced non-squamous NSCLC, it describes 4–6 combination cycles followed, when appropriate, by a separate pemetrexed maintenance plan (NCCP00222). The selected preoperative nivolumab combination uses three cycles and a separate immunotherapy plan (NCCP00849). Your team confirms which course applies and may stop or change it earlier.

Nausea, vomiting and fatigue

Kidney problems
Allergic or infusion reactions

Rare serious lung inflammation

Mouth soreness and diarrhoea
Kidney problems
Allergic or infusion reactions
Hearing changes or tinnitus, which can persist
Numbness or tingling, which can persist
Electrolyte changes and need for hydration
