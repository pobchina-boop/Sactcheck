The whole treatment journey

Roswell Park modified fluorouracil + folinic acid: treatment and checks

Adjuvant or metastatic colorectal cancer

Fluorouracil and folinic acid are given once weekly for six weeks, then two weeks off: Days 1, 8, 15, 22, 29 and 36 of a 56-day cycle. Adjuvant treatment is three cycles (24 weeks). A four-dose/six-week variant requires a separate consultant plan.
