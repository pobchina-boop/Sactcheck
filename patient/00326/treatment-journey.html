The whole treatment journey

Temsirolimus for kidney cancer: treatment and checks

First-line advanced renal cell carcinoma with at least three of six source prognostic risk factors

The usual dose is 25 mg by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes once a week. The NCCP calendar lists Days 1, 8, 15 and 22 of each 28-day cycle. Continue while helpful and tolerable; there is no routine week off.

What each check is for

1Treatment review25 mg IV once weekly

Help control advanced kidney cancer in the source-defined higher-risk setting.

Ask / act: Call your oncology team promptly for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting/diarrhoea or new breathlessness. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, seizures or stroke symptoms: call 112/999.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC
