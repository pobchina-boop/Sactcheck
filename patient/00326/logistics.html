Temsirolimus for kidney cancer

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00326 · Source-reconciled feasibility draft; independent clinician, pharmacy and patient review pending.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests, total cholesterol, triglycerides, fasting glucose and chest X-ray. Review infection, breathing symptoms, medicines, recent surgery and the source prognostic factors.

STEP 2

Dose and timing

The usual dose is 25 mg by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes once a week. The NCCP calendar lists Days 1, 8, 15 and 22 of each 28-day cycle. Continue while helpful and tolerable; there is no routine week off.

STEP 3

Practical care

An intravenous antihistamine is given about 30 minutes before each infusion to reduce reaction risk. Report flushing, chest pain, throat tightness, dizziness or breathing difficulty immediately. There is no take-home infusion pump in this regimen.

STEP 4

Support medicines

NCCP specifies diphenhydramine 25–50 mg intravenous (IV), or a similar antihistamine, about 30 minutes before each dose. Your unit confirms its prescribed equivalent. Use gentle mouth care and report ulcers early. Review infection prevention, diabetes and lipid management. Preventive medicine against Pneumocystis pneumonia may be considered if you also take corticosteroids or other immune-suppressing medicines; it is not automatically required for everyone.

STEP 5

Before leaving

Confirm your next tests, dose, review date and verified daytime/out-of-hours contact numbers.
