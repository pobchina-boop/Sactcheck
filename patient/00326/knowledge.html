When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Call your oncology team promptly for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding, persistent vomiting/diarrhoea or new breathlessness. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse, seizures or stroke symptoms: call 112/999.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

Understand your treatment

How your medicine works

Help control advanced kidney cancer in the source-defined higher-risk setting.

Targeted therapy: mTOR inhibitor

Temsirolimus

Torisel
Temsirolimus inhibits a protein called mTOR, part of the signalling system that controls cell growth, protein production and blood-vessel development. This can slow tumour growth. mTOR also supports normal immune and metabolic functions, explaining infection risk and changes in glucose or cholesterol.

Safe medicine use

An intravenous antihistamine is given about 30 minutes before each infusion to reduce reaction risk. Report flushing, chest pain, throat tightness, dizziness or breathing difficulty immediately. There is no take-home infusion pump in this regimen.

Fertility and contraception

Discuss fertility preservation. Men with partners who could become pregnant should use effective contraception during treatment; women should also avoid pregnancy. Ask the team to document the product-specific post-treatment plan. Do not breastfeed during treatment.

Your wider treatment

Treatment options depend on previous medicines, cancer behaviour, general health and your priorities. Surgery or focused radiotherapy may be appropriate for selected problems; they do not replace this systemic medicine automatically.

Treatment reference · 04

Benefits, uncertainty and your choices

Evidence in this setting

The pivotal poor-prognosis renal cancer trial compared temsirolimus with interferon alfa. Median overall survival was 10.9 versus 7.3 months. The current EMA table distinguishes the prespecified interim hazard ratio of 0.73 (95% confidence interval 0.58–0.92) from the final analysis of 0.78 (0.63–0.97). This historical comparison does not establish superiority over modern first-line combinations.

Sources: nccp · smpc
