What each check is for

1Before first doseBaseline

Baseline FBC, kidney and liver tests; regular tests are required by NCCP. The unit should confirm pre-cycle timing and review results before prescribing.

Ask / act: Confirm tests and consent before prescribing.

Sources: NCCP 00423

2Heart-monitoring planBaseline → cycles → after treatment

ESC supplementary guidance: baseline echo before anthracycline treatment. High/very-high cardiac risk: echo every two anthracycline cycles (for example before cycles 3 and 5 when continuing), and within 3 months after completion. All adults: echo within 12 months after completion. Other repeat tests depend on risk, cumulative exposure and symptoms.

Ask / act: Name the team responsible for booking and reviewing results.

Sources: NCCP 00423 · ESC

3Treatment-phase reviewBefore each transition

Doxorubicin followed by docetaxel on Day 1 every 21 days, for up to six cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity changes the plan.

Ask / act: Confirm active medicines, support script and the next test checkpoint.

Sources: NCCP 00423

4Response and follow-upPlanned review points

Record disease sites before treatment. Response imaging is generally considered every 2–4 months under ESMO guidance, adjusted to symptoms and disease pace. For a 21-day course this is approximately after 3–6 cycles; the team chooses the scan and exact checkpoint. New symptoms may need earlier assessment.

Ask / act: Review symptoms and imaging together; do not change treatment on tumour markers alone.

Sources: NCCP 00423

At the next review, ask

  • Which treatment phase am I in, and what happens next?
  • Which test is expected next, who arranges it, and who explains the result?
  • Who should I contact if an expected appointment has not arrived?

This map does not show whether a test has been ordered, booked or completed. Do not wait for a scheduled test if you develop new symptoms.
