After cycle 1

Continue nivolumab on days 1 and 22 and ipilimumab on day 1 of each 42-day cycle.

Duration

Immunotherapy continues until progression/unacceptable toxicity or up to 24 months in patients without progression, according to the NCCP source.

Important medicine-specific effects

Nivolumab + ipilimumab - immune effects

  • Immune inflammation can affect lungs, bowel, liver, hormone glands, kidneys, skin, heart, nerves or muscles.
  • New symptoms can begin during treatment or after treatment has stopped.

Pemetrexed

  • Low blood counts can increase infection, anaemia or bruising risk.
  • Mouth/gut inflammation and skin rash can occur.
  • Kidney function can deteriorate and is monitored.

Carboplatin

  • Low blood counts, especially neutropenia and low platelets, can occur.
  • Allergic infusion reactions can occur, particularly after repeated exposure.
  • Nausea and vomiting may occur despite preventative medicines.
Other / shared treatment effects: The anatomy page highlights important symptom patterns for this exact regimen, but it cannot show every possible effect. Fatigue, nausea, appetite change, mouth/bowel symptoms or blood-count changes may occur depending on the medicines used. Report new or worsening symptoms even if they are not listed here.

Monitoring / medicines

  • Folic acid and vitamin B12 support are required with pemetrexed.
  • Blood count, kidney/liver profile and endocrine monitoring support treatment safety.
  • Use the regimen-specific antiemetic prescription for the chemotherapy phase.

Bring / track

  • Immune adverse events can begin during treatment or after it stops; report new symptoms early.

When to call - do not wait for the next appointment

Contact oncology urgently for fever, new breathlessness, persistent diarrhoea, jaundice, severe rash, chest pain, marked weakness, confusion, reduced urine or rapidly worsening symptoms. For severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain call 112/999.

Consent discussion support

Use the approved consent process to document treatment aim, expected benefit, material risks, alternatives, questions and the patient's decision.
