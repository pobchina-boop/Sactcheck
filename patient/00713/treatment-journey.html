The whole treatment journey

Nivolumab, ipilimumab, pemetrexed and carboplatin: treatment and checks

First-line metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) · confirm pemetrexed-compatible histology

The first cycle lasts 42 days: all four medicines on Day 1, then nivolumab, pemetrexed and carboplatin on Day 22. Chemotherapy ends after those two visits. From Cycle 2, nivolumab continues on Days 1 and 22 and ipilimumab on Day 1 of each 42-day cycle. Immune treatment continues while appropriate until the cancer progresses, side effects require stopping, or up to 24 months without progression. Plans can change after review.

2Final chemotherapy visitCycle 1 Day 22 (21 days after first visit)

Ask / act: Check next phase and stop/review chemotherapy support; continue folate for 21 days after final pemetrexed.

Sources: NCCP 00713

3Immune continuationCycle 2 onwards, Days 1 and 22

Different medicines and support at alternating visits

Ask / act: Review joint immune hold rules and duration; maintain alert-card education.

Sources: NCCP 00713

4End of treatmentAt progression, unacceptable toxicity or up to 24 months without progression

Delayed immune effects and unresolved toxicities still need care

Ask / act: Provide responsible-team, monitoring and urgent-contact arrangements.
