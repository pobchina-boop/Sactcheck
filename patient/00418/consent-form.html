Treatment context
Cetuximab with carboplatin and fluorouracil is used to treat squamous head and neck cancer when cisplatin is unsuitable. Your team explains the treatment aim, possible benefit and alternatives for your situation.
Schedule
Carboplatin is given on Day 1, fluorouracil on Days 1–4, and cetuximab on Days 1, 8 and 15, for up to 6 cycles every 21 days. Review determines what happens afterwards. Your team confirms any treatment after the combination phase; continuing cetuximab needs an agreed prescription and calendar.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Cetuximab

EGFR-targeted antibody
Infusion reactions, including anaphylaxis
Acne-like rash, dry or cracked skin and nail infections
Low magnesium and other mineral changes
Eye inflammation
Diarrhoea and dehydration
Rare serious lung inflammation

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts and serious infection
Low platelets and bleeding
Anaemia and fatigue
Nausea and vomiting
Infusion or allergic reactions
Kidney, nerve or hearing effects

Fluorouracil

Antimetabolite chemotherapy
Low blood counts and serious infection
Mouth and throat soreness
Diarrhoea and dehydration
Painful palms or soles
Heart problems including chest pain
Rare severe neurological or early excessive toxicity
