1. Treatment and purpose

Vinorelbine IV · advanced breast cancer · NCCP 00232 v5

30 mg/m² onDays 1 and 8 every 21 days. Consultant may consider 25 mg/m² for poor marrow reserve; the dose is individualised after toxicity. Repeated 21-day cycles until progression/unacceptable toxicity; team review determines continuation.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Vinorelbine · Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy

Use the treating unit’s 24-hour emergency number and local fever threshold. Contact immediately for fever/chills, sudden illness, breathlessness/chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, severe abdominal pain/obstruction symptoms, bleeding or severe allergy. Do not wait until the next visit.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Purpose and alternatives for the person’s advanced breast cancer, including other systemic treatment or supportive care according to goals. Possible benefit is uncertain; response is reviewed and treatment can be paused, changed or stopped. Low blood counts, infection/sepsis, fatigue, nausea/vomiting, bowel problems, neuropathy, hair change and uncommon serious lung/heart/allergic toxicity. Fertility and pregnancy planning before treatment; discuss sperm/egg preservation where relevant. Do not breastfeed. HPRA supplements: contraception during treatment and for 7 months afterwards for women,4 months for men; confirm the exact product and individual advice.
