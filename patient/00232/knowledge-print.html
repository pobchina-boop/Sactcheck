When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Use the treating unit’s 24-hour emergency number and local fever threshold. Contact immediately for fever/chills, sudden illness, breathlessness/chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, severe abdominal pain/obstruction symptoms, bleeding or severe allergy. Do not wait until the next visit.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

How the medicines work

Aim to slow or shrink advanced breast cancer and help symptoms while balancing side effects. Duration depends on benefit, tolerability and the agreed plan; a fixed number of cycles or a guaranteed survival improvement is not promised.

Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy

Vinorelbine

IV infusion
Disrupts the machinery of cell division

Vinorelbine disrupts the tiny structures a cell needs to divide. This can stop cancer cells multiplying. Healthy blood-forming cells, gut cells and nerves can also be affected.

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

30 mg/m² onDays 1 and 8 every 21 days. Consultant may consider 25 mg/m² for poor marrow reserve; the dose is individualised after toxicity. Repeated 21-day cycles until progression/unacceptable toxicity; team review determines continuation.

STEP 1

Visits and tests

Two infusion visits per 21-day cycle, with blood tests before doses. Peripheral cannula or existing access according to the treating service; a port is not automatically required.

STEP 2

Treatment preparation

Wear clothing allowing IV-site observation; report discomfort immediately.

STEP 3

At home

No home vinorelbine capsules are part of this regimen. Keep an up-to-date medication list and emergency contact.

Individual instructions

NCCP specifies 50 mL sodium chloride 0.9% over 15 minutes followed by 250 mL saline. Current Navelbine SmPC says 6–10 minutes: product/local-pharmacy reconciliation is required. Do not promise a total visit length; preparation, tests and observation add time.

Make the plan practical

Support around the medicines

Sickness plan

No routine preventive anti-sickness medicine is usually required for this IV regimen. Ask for a rescue plan and report uncontrolled symptoms.

Bowel care

Agree a constipation plan, particularly if using opioids or ondansetron. Severe abdominal pain, swelling, repeated vomiting or inability to pass stool/gas needs urgent assessment; do not simply add laxatives.

Medicines and vaccines

Check every new prescription, supplement and vaccine with the team; avoid grapefruit. Live vaccines and interacting medicines need review.

Checks before and during treatment

Every dose depends on the reviewed treatment plan and results.

Blood and symptom checks

Check blood count, kidney and liver function before each cycle, and blood counts on the day of every administration including Day 8. Review neuropathy, bowel symptoms and infection each cycle; abnormal results may postpone or change treatment.

Sources: nccp

Heart and response checks

Review cardiovascular history and new symptoms. These monotherapy NCCP protocols do not mandate the serial left ventricular ejection fraction (heart pumping measurement) (LVEF)/echo schedule used for anthracyclines or trastuzumab; arrange cardiac tests when clinically indicated.

Sources: nccp

Fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding

Purpose and alternatives for the person’s advanced breast cancer, including other systemic treatment or supportive care according to goals. Possible benefit is uncertain; response is reviewed and treatment can be paused, changed or stopped. Low blood counts, infection/sepsis, fatigue, nausea/vomiting, bowel problems, neuropathy, hair change and uncommon serious lung/heart/allergic toxicity. Fertility and pregnancy planning before treatment; discuss sperm/egg preservation where relevant. Do not breastfeed. HPRA supplements: contraception during treatment and for 7 months afterwards for women,4 months for men; confirm the exact product and individual advice.

Why this treatment may be chosen

Vinorelbine has shown activity in advanced breast cancer. Expected benefit depends on cancer biology, earlier treatment and health; historical trial averages do not predict an individual result.

Prepare for the conversation

Questions worth asking

Can IV and oral vinorelbine be exchanged?

For IV use only; intrathecal administration may be fatal. Oral and IV vinorelbine are not interchangeablemg-for-mg. Stop infusion and tell staff immediately about pain, burning, swelling or leakage at the IV site; extravasation can damage tissue.

How long will treatment continue?

Repeated 21-day cycles until progression/unacceptable toxicity; team review determines continuation.

Do I need regular heart scans?

Review cardiovascular history and new symptoms. These monotherapy NCCP protocols do not mandate the serial LVEF/echo schedule used for anthracyclines or trastuzumab; arrange cardiac tests when clinically indicated.

How long is an infusion visit?

The source describes a short infusion followed by a saline flush. Your unit confirms the product-specific infusion instructions and complete visit length, including blood results, preparation and observation.

Try explaining the plan back: Which treatment days and doses are prescribed, which blood tests must happen first, how do you take the support medicines, and which symptoms need urgent help?
