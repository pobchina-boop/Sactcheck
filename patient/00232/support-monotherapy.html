Active medicines: Vinorelbine

Emetogenic risk: Minimal IV risk

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Individual antiemetic plan differs by route; list routine and rescue medicines separately.
  • Discuss constipation prevention, hydration/diet where appropriate and prescribed laxatives; regular opioid use increases the need for a bowel plan.
  • No routine steroid premedication, G-CSF, antibacterial, antiviral or PJP prophylaxis is mandated by these NCCP monotherapy documents. Consider additional prevention based on individual risk/local policy; do not create a generic automatic prescription.
  • Gastric protection is a consideration for high gastrointestinal-bleed risk, not a universal drug.
  • Confirm strong CYP3 A4 inhibitors/inducers, P-gp modulators, phenytoin, itraconazole, warfarin and live vaccines. Yellow fever vaccine is contraindicated. Avoid grapefruit per NCCP; do not self-start supplements.
  • No routine prophylaxis required by NCCP minimal-risk pathway.
  • Prescriber selects an as-needed medicine according to symptoms, constipation, QT/interactions and local policy. Reassess before future doses if nausea/vomiting occurs.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
