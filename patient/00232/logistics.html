Vinorelbine IV · advanced breast cancer

NCCP 00232 · Source reconciled; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

30 mg/m² onDays 1 and 8 every 21 days. Consultant may consider 25 mg/m² for poor marrow reserve; the dose is individualised after toxicity. Repeated 21-day cycles until progression/unacceptable toxicity; team review determines continuation.

STEP 1

Visits and tests

Two infusion visits per 21-day cycle, with blood tests before doses. Peripheral cannula or existing access according to the treating service; a port is not automatically required.

STEP 2

Treatment preparation

Wear clothing allowing IV-site observation; report discomfort immediately.

STEP 3

At home

No home vinorelbine capsules are part of this regimen. Keep an up-to-date medication list and emergency contact.

Individual instructions

NCCP specifies 50 mL sodium chloride 0.9% over 15 minutes followed by 250 mL saline. Current Navelbine SmPC says 6–10 minutes: product/local-pharmacy reconciliation is required. Do not promise a total visit length; preparation, tests and observation add time.
