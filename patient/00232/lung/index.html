How your medicines work

Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy

Vinorelbine

Disrupts the machinery of cell division
Binds cell-division structures→Microtubules cannot function normally→Cell division is disrupted

Vinorelbine disrupts the tiny structures a cell needs to divide. This can stop cancer cells multiplying. Healthy blood-forming cells, gut cells and nerves can also be affected.

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

30 mg/m² on Days 1 and 8 every 21 days. Consultant may consider 25 mg/m² for poor marrow reserve; the dose is individualised after toxicity. Repeated 21-day cycles until progression/unacceptable toxicity; team review determines continuation.

STEP 1

Visits and tests

Two infusion visits per 21-day cycle, with blood tests before doses. Peripheral cannula or existing access according to the treating service; a port is not automatically required.

STEP 2

Treatment preparation

Wear clothing allowing IV-site observation; report discomfort immediately.

STEP 3

At home

No home vinorelbine capsules are part of this regimen. Keep an up-to-date medication list and emergency contact.

Individual instructions

NCCP specifies 50 mL sodium chloride 0.9% over 15 minutes followed by 250 mL saline. Current Navelbine SmPC says 6–10 minutes: product/local-pharmacy reconciliation is required. Do not promise a total visit length; preparation, tests and observation add time.

Regimen-specific symptoms

Recognise symptoms and act early.

A picture of side effects

Numbered symptom boxes connect to the anatomical illustration. The medicine responsible may be uncertain; report the symptom.

1Blood counts and infection
Vinorelbine

Low blood counts can cause infection, tiredness, bruising or bleeding. Fever, chills or sudden illness: call oncology immediately.

3Tingling, numbness or weakness
Vinorelbine

Nerve effects may change sensation, grip, walking or bowel movement. Report changes early because the next dose may need to wait or be reduced.

5Uncommon serious symptoms
Vinorelbine

New chest pain, sudden or worsening breathlessness, or severe allergic symptoms need urgent medical help. Previous heart disease and interacting medicines need review before treatment.

2Bowel and mouth symptoms
Vinorelbine

Constipation, vomiting or sore mouth can occur. Severe pain, swelling, repeated vomiting or no stool/gas: urgent assessment; do not take extra laxatives.

4Fatigue, hair and liver tests
Vinorelbine

Tiredness, hair thinning/loss and liver-test changes can occur. Pace activities and tell the team if eating, drinking or daily tasks become difficult.

6Infusion site
Vinorelbine

Tell staff immediately about burning, pain, swelling or leakage.

Support around treatment

Sickness plan

No routine preventive anti-sickness medicine is usually required for this IV regimen. Ask for a rescue plan and report uncontrolled symptoms.

Bowel care

Agree a constipation plan, particularly if using opioids or ondansetron. Severe abdominal pain, swelling, repeated vomiting or inability to pass stool/gas needs urgent assessment; do not simply add laxatives.

Medicines and vaccines

Check every new prescription, supplement and vaccine with the team; avoid grapefruit. Live vaccines and interacting medicines need review.

Do not wait for the next appointment

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
