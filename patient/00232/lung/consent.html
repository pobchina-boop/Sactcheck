1. Treatment and purpose

Vinorelbine IV · non-small-cell lung cancer · NCCP 00232 v5

30 mg/m² on Days 1 and 8 every 21 days. Consultant may consider 25 mg/m² for poor marrow reserve; the dose is individualised after toxicity. Repeated 21-day cycles until progression/unacceptable toxicity; team review determines continuation.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Vinorelbine · Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy

Use the treating unit’s 24-hour emergency number and local fever threshold. Contact immediately for fever/chills, sudden illness, breathlessness/chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, severe abdominal pain/obstruction symptoms, bleeding or severe allergy. Do not wait until the next visit.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the disease-control aim, likely benefit and uncertainty, other lung-cancer treatments or symptom-focused care, and the burden of visits and tests. Review low blood counts and infection, nausea/vomiting, fatigue, constipation/ileus, nerve symptoms, hair changes and uncommon serious lung/heart/allergic effects. Discuss IV-site injury and the different IV and capsule doses; the product-specific infusion plan needs pharmacy confirmation. Discuss fertility preservation and pregnancy planning. Do not breastfeed. Confirm the exact product contraception advice: HPRA recommends contraception during treatment and for 7 months afterwards for women, and 4 months for men.
