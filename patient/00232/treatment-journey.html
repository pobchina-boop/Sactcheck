Vinorelbine IV · advanced breast cancer: treatment and checks

Advanced breast cancer

30 mg/m² onDays 1 and 8 every 21 days. Consultant may consider 25 mg/m² for poor marrow reserve; the dose is individualised after toxicity. Repeated 21-day cycles until progression/unacceptable toxicity; team review determines continuation.

What each check is for

1Treatment and dose reviewBefore every administration

Check blood count, kidney and liver function before each cycle, and blood counts on the day of every administration including Day 8. Review neuropathy, bowel symptoms and infection each cycle; abnormal results may postpone or change treatment.

Ask / act: The treating team confirms results, dose and suitability; this map does not authorise treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00232 · SMPC

2Support and symptomsThroughout treatment, including days at home

Use the treating unit’s 24-hour emergency number and local fever threshold. Contact immediately for fever/chills, sudden illness, breathlessness/chest pain, uncontrolled vomiting, severe abdominal pain/obstruction symptoms, bleeding or severe allergy. Do not wait until the next visit.

Ask / act: Keep a written medicine plan and urgent-contact route.

Sources: NCCP 00232 · ORAL-ANTIEMETIC · NCIS

3Response and continuationAt agreed clinical review points

Aim to slow or shrink advanced breast cancer and help symptoms while balancing side effects. Duration depends on benefit, tolerability and the agreed plan; a fixed number of cycles or a guaranteed survival improvement is not promised.

Ask / act: Discuss benefit, toxicity and priorities before continuing or changing treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00232 · TRIAL

At the next review, ask
