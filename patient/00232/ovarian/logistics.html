Preparing for treatment visits

IV vinorelbine for platinum-refractory ovarian cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

IV vinorelbine on days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle. Week three is a break. Your team reviews benefit and tolerability before repeating the cycle.

At the day unit

The nurse gives vinorelbine into a vein over 15 minutes and then flushes the line with saline. Tell them immediately if the site stings, burns or swells.

What to bring

Tell the team about constipation, numbness and any infection. Confirm your blood test and day-8 appointment before leaving.

Write down your next appointments

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call immediately for fever, chills or feeling suddenly unwell. During the infusion, report any pain or leakage around the IV line straight away. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
