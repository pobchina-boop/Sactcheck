IV vinorelbine for platinum-refractory ovarian cancer

Treatment and purpose

Vinorelbine is being offered to slow the growth of platinum-refractory advanced ovarian cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue. Advanced ovarian carcinoma that is refractory to platinum. This use of IV vinorelbine is unlicensed in Ireland.

Treatment schedule. IV vinorelbine on days 1 and 8 of a 21-day cycle. Week three is a break. Your team reviews benefit and tolerability before repeating the cycle.

Treatment discussion and decision

IV vinorelbine for platinum-refractory ovarian cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Investigators observed tumour responses with single-agent vinorelbine on days 1 and 8. The study included a broader platinum-resistant population and had no control group. It cannot estimate benefit specifically for an individual with platinum-refractory disease.

Advanced ovarian carcinoma that is refractory to platinum. This use of IV vinorelbine is unlicensed in Ireland. Discuss expected benefit, alternatives, side effects and the option of symptom-directed care. Record the individual decision and any off-label discussion.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call immediately for fever, chills or feeling suddenly unwell. During the infusion, report any pain or leakage around the IV line straight away. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
