My questions and next steps

  • What should I check before treatment? Tell the team about constipation, numbness and any infection. Confirm your blood test and day-8 appointment before leaving.
  • What should I watch for at home? Record bowel movements and any new tingling. Call promptly for severe constipation, abdominal swelling, vomiting or inability to pass wind.
  • Why is this being offered? Vinorelbine is being offered to slow the growth of platinum-refractory advanced ovarian cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue.
