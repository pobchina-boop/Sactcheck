SACTCheck · NCCP 00772 v3 · Platinum-resistant ovarian cancer

Your treatment journey

Bevacizumab with pegylated liposomal doxorubicin

Slow the growth of recurrent cancer and help control symptoms.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1

Bevacizumab 10 mg/kg with pegylated liposomal doxorubicin 40 mg/m² on day 1 of each 28-day cycle.

Day 15

Bevacizumab 10 mg/kg alone on day 15. The liposomal chemotherapy is given only on day 1.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood counts, kidney and liver tests, blood pressure and urine protein. Review bowel symptoms, bleeding, recent surgery and wound healing.Bring your medicines and the dates of operations.
During treatmentBlood counts before chemotherapy. Your team checks blood pressure, urine protein and organ function.Ask when to have blood tests and urine samples before each visit.
Heart and skinHeart function, prior anthracycline exposure, mouth ulcers and hand–foot symptoms.Report symptoms even if they started between visits.

Scan dates and tumour-marker testing are individualised by your oncology team.

At review visits

  • Which medicines will I receive at my next visit? Both on day 1; bevacizumab alone on day 15.
  • When should I call immediately? Call the oncology unit now for fever, chills, severe abdominal pain, unexpected bleeding or new breathlessness.
