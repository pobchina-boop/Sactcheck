Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenAt baseline and before every induction cycle.

Checks white cells for infection protection, red cells for oxygen carriage and platelets for clotting.

Kidney function and fluid balance

WhenAt baseline and before each cycle; extra checks or measured clearance when indicated.

Cisplatin can injure kidneys; results and hydration guide safe prescribing.

Liver function

WhenAt baseline and before each cycle.

Helps assess whether the medicines, particularly docetaxel, can be given safely.

DPD assessment before fluorouracil

WhenBefore first exposure unless already tested. Moderate or severe kidney impairment can make a blood-uracil phenotype harder to interpret; the team considers genotype testing where appropriate.

Reduced ability to break down fluorouracil can cause severe or life-threatening toxicity. Normal testing does not exclude every severe reaction.

Hearing assessment

If part of your plan
WhenAudiology when indicated; report hearing or tinnitus changes before each dose.

Checks hearing changes or ringing in the ears that cisplatin may cause.

Heart assessment / ECG

If part of your plan
WhenECG for compromised cardiac function; additional assessment for symptoms or clinician concern.

Assesses existing heart problems and helps plan safe treatment; fluorouracil-related chest symptoms need urgent review.

Response assessment

If part of your plan
WhenScans and examinations according to the individual treatment plan.

Helps decide the next treatment stage after induction.

The whole treatment journey

Docetaxel 75 / cisplatin 75 / fluorouracil 1,000 · induction TPF: treatment and checks

Induction treatment of stage III/intravenous (IV) non-metastatic squamous cell carcinoma of the head and neck, with a separately agreed definitive chemoradiation and/or surgery plan.

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² and cisplatin 75 mg/m² on Day 1, with fluorouracil 1,000 mg/m²/day on Days 1–4. Repeat every 21 days for 3 cycles, unless progression or unacceptable toxicity changes the plan.
