High-dose carboplatin + etoposide with stem-cell rescue

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm admission, stored stem-cell availability, fertility discussion and the complete transplant-support prescription before conditioning.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Chemotherapy and stem-cell rescue take place in a specialist transplant service. Carboplatin runs over 30 minutes and etoposide over two hours on each treatment day. Preparation, fluid management and monitoring add time. Staff give stem-cell infusion premedication under the local plan.

STEP 3

Access and pump care

The transplant team arranges suitable intravenous access and line care. Record that you require irradiated cellular blood components and tell every treating service.

STEP 4

Home support

Prescribed anti-sickness treatment is needed across the high-dose days. The local transplant plan includes antiviral and antifungal prevention, mouth care, a proton-pump inhibitor and Pneumocystis pneumonia prevention. Co-trimoxazole starts only after engraftment; the team specifies the stop date. Irradiated cellular blood components are required from one week before conditioning until 12 months after stem-cell infusion.

STEP 5

Next appointment

Confirm the next treatment, tests and any scan before leaving.
