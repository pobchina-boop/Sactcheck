Your treatment journey

ECX for upper gastrointestinal cancer

When the cancer is resectable, treatment before and after surgery aims to reduce recurrence. When the cancer is advanced, treatment aims to control it and ease symptoms. Confirm which plan applies.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · ECX

Day 1 epirubicin and cisplatin. Take capecitabine twice daily throughout days 1–21 of each 21-day cycle. There is no planned tablet break between continuing cycles.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingCheck blood count, kidney and liver function and DPD status before starting. Review cardiac function, prior anthracycline exposure and lifetime epirubicin dose.Bring your medicine list and oncology contact details.
Before the next cycleReview blood counts, kidney function, electrolytes and liver tests before the next cycle. Check hearing, hydration and urine output. Record cumulative epirubicin and investigate cardiac symptoms.Tell the team about symptoms and missed doses.
At reviewReview symptoms, nutrition, weight and treatment response.Ask when scans or surgery will be discussed.
Around surgeryThe protocol schedules surgery 3–6 weeks after cycle 3 and cycle 4 at 6–12 weeks after surgery.Restart depends on recovery and the treating team.

For surgery, plan three cycles before and three after. In advanced disease continue while helping and tolerable.

At review visits

  • When is the next treatment? Day 1 epirubicin and cisplatin. Take capecitabine twice daily throughout days 1–21 of each 21-day cycle. There is no planned tablet break between continuing cycles.
  • What should I do between visits? Swallow capecitabine with water within 30 minutes of a meal. Record your doses. Leave out a missed or vomited dose and call for advice if unwell.
