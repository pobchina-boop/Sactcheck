ECX for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Treatment and purpose

When the cancer is resectable, treatment before and after surgery aims to reduce recurrence. When the cancer is advanced, treatment aims to control it and ease symptoms. Confirm which plan applies. Perioperative treatment of resectable gastric adenocarcinoma. Perioperative treatment of resectable gastro-oesophageal junction adenocarcinoma. Perioperative treatment of resectable lower oesophageal adenocarcinoma. Palliative treatment of locally advanced or metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma. Palliative treatment of locally advanced or metastatic gastro-oesophageal junction adenocarcinoma.

Treatment schedule. Day 1 epirubicin and cisplatin. Take capecitabine twice daily throughout days 1–21 of each 21-day cycle. There is no planned tablet break between continuing cycles. For surgery, plan three cycles before and three after. In advanced disease continue while helping and tolerable.

Treatment discussion and decision

ECX for upper gastrointestinal cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

In advanced disease, the pooled comparisons supported substitution of capecitabine for infused fluorouracil and oxaliplatin for cisplatin. The trial did not compare these with modern immunotherapy combinations. In resectable gastric or junctional adenocarcinoma, perioperative FLOT improved median survival versus the ECF/ECX comparator group. The trial did not independently compare ECX with surgery alone. These results describe selected trial populations. Your outlook depends on your disease, fitness and treatment setting.

When the cancer is resectable, treatment before and after surgery aims to reduce recurrence. When the cancer is advanced, treatment aims to control it and ease symptoms. Confirm which plan applies. For surgery, plan three cycles before and three after. In advanced disease continue while helping and tolerable. Discuss alternatives, fertility, and individual risks with the treating clinician.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
