The whole treatment journey

Gemcitabine + cisplatin · 28-day schedule: treatment and checks

Advanced urothelial cancer

Gemcitabine is given on days 1, 8 and 15. Cisplatin is usually given after gemcitabine on day 1; the source also permits day 2. The cycle repeats every 28 days, usually for four to six cycles. Your unit confirms your actual cisplatin day and appointments.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewDays 1, 8 and 15 · every 28 days

Help control advanced urothelial cancer and its symptoms.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999.

Sources: NCCP

At the next review, ask

  • Which treatment phase am I in, and what happens next?
  • Which test is expected next, who arranges it, and who explains the result?
