Your supportive care plan

Niraparib (Zejula) tablets

Practical support

At home

Follow your prescribed anti-sickness plan. Bedtime dosing may help nausea. Speak to the team before changing the dose or stopping.

Other medicines

Show your team all medicines, including blood thinners, aspirin and supplements. Do not stop prescribed medicines on your own.

Family planning

Niraparib can harm a developing baby. Discuss contraception, fertility and breastfeeding before starting.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness or a severe headache? Call oncology now. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
