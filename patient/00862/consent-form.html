Consent discussion aid

Niraparib (Zejula) tablets

These tablets help delay the return or growth of ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer after it has responded to platinum chemotherapy.

Treatment discussion

Your team will explain whether this is maintenance after first treatment for advanced disease or after platinum-sensitive relapse.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Daily tabletsEvery day, including weekendsSwallow the tablets whole with water at the same time each day, either with a light meal or at least one hour before or two hours after food. If you miss a dose or vomit after it, take the next scheduled dose. Do not take an extra tablet.

Possible benefit, alternatives and your priorities

PRIMA studied first-line maintenance after platinum response. The risk of progression or death was lower with niraparib than placebo in the homologous-recombination-deficient group (HR 0.43, 95% CI 0.31–0.59). NOVA separately studied maintenance after platinum-sensitive relapse.

The first-line and relapse studies enrolled different populations. Delayed progression is a group result and does not establish a survival benefit for an individual.

Discuss the expected benefit, side effects, fertility, alternatives and symptom-directed care with the treating clinician. You may ask for more time or change your decision.

Other options discussed and what matters most to me

Important risks we discussed

  • Low blood counts — Unusual bleeding, infection or new breathlessness needs urgent assessment. Persistent abnormalities may need assessment for rare bone marrow disorders including MDS or AML.
  • High blood pressure — Report severe headache, confusion or changes in vision urgently. Call 112 or 999 for a seizure, collapse, chest pain or severe breathing difficulty.

Understanding and next steps
