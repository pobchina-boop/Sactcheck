My treatment passport

Niraparib (Zejula) tablets

My treatment record

These tablets help delay the return or growth of ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer after it has responded to platinum chemotherapy.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Daily tabletsEvery day, including weekendsSwallow the tablets whole with water at the same time each day, either with a light meal or at least one hour before or two hours after food. If you miss a dose or vomit after it, take the next scheduled dose. Do not take an extra tablet.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness or a severe headache? Call oncology now. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Appointments, tests and results

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhen and why
Before startingWeight, blood count, blood pressure, kidney and liver testsWeight and platelets help the team select your starting dose.
First monthBlood count weekly. Check blood pressure with the team.Have the next test dates written down before starting.
OngoingBlood count monthly for the next 10 months, then periodically. Kidney and liver tests monthly.Continue blood pressure checks. Your team may ask for more frequent home readings.

The NCCP lists blood pressure at week 2, monthly for 6 months and then every 3 months. The European product information calls for at least weekly readings for the first 2 months, monthly during the first year and periodically thereafter. Agree a written monitoring plan with your team before starting.
