Planning your visits

Prescription and supply

Check tablet strength and dose on the latest label. Arrange repeat supplies before running out. Ask what to do if the team pauses treatment.

Home monitoring

Bring your blood pressure readings, tablet list, symptoms and dates of any missed doses to each review.

Write down your next appointments

  • Bring your medicines and blood pressure record. Confirm your tablet strength and next blood tests.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness or a severe headache? Call oncology now. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
