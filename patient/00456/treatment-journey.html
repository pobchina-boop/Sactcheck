The whole treatment journey

Cisplatin + etoposide + chest radiotherapy: treatment and checks

Stage III non-small-cell lung cancer

Cisplatin on Days 1 and 8; etoposide on Days 1–5. Each cycle lasts 28 days. Two cycles first; two more may be offered after review. Radiotherapy usually starts within 24 hours of the first chemotherapy dose; follow your separate radiation appointment plan.

What each check is for

1Prepare both plansBefore treatment

Ask / act: Confirm chemotherapy, radiation, tests and contacts.
