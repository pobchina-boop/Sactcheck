Tests in your plan

Baseline bloods and tests

WhenFull blood count, kidney and liver tests and thyroid function; ECG if at risk of heart-rhythm changes; INR if taking warfarin. Review cardiovascular and seizure history, falls, bone health and all medicines.

Checks organ safety and suitability before treatment.

Treatment safety monitoring

WhenRepeat blood count, kidney and liver tests on the schedule agreed with your team. Thyroid function and ECG as clinically indicated. If warfarin is unavoidable, INR weekly until the dose is stable. The NCCP regimen does not set one fixed interval for every routine blood test.

Finds changes that may need supportive care, a dose hold or adjustment.

Specialist and cancer tests

If part of your plan
WhenPSA and testosterone monitor cancer activity and ADT suppression. PSA doubling time helps select high-risk nonmetastatic castration-resistant disease. Thyroid function is a safety test because apalutamide can cause an underactive thyroid.

Distinguishes treatment selection, hormone monitoring and cancer response.

The whole treatment journey

Apalutamide: prostate cancer with ongoing ADT: treatment and checks

High-risk nonmetastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer, or metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer, together with ADT. Your team confirms the indication and its separate eligibility criteria.

Apalutamide 240 mg once daily, continuously while helping and tolerated, with ongoing ADT unless surgically castrated. Lower prescribed doses are 180 mg or 120 mg daily when indicated.
