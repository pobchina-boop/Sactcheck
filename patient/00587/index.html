When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Topotecan capsules

Topotecan blocks topoisomerase I, an enzyme that relieves strain while DNA is copied. Unrepaired DNA breaks can stop dividing cancer cells; healthy blood-forming and gut cells can also be affected.

Support at home

Sickness and bowel symptoms

Use your prescribed prevention or rescue medicine. Report persistent vomiting, diarrhoea or inability to drink; fever with severe tummy pain needs urgent assessment.

Blood tests and review

Blood count, kidney and liver tests are checked before treatment and before each cycle. Extra tests depend on symptoms and results.

Pregnancy, fertility and medicines

Discuss fertility and contraception before treatment. Tell the team about pregnancy, breastfeeding, all medicines, supplements and vaccines. Check before changing any medicine.

Missed or vomited capsules

Do not double a missed dose or take replacement capsules after vomiting without team advice. Record what happened, contact the oncology pharmacist/team, and follow the next scheduled dose in the agreed plan.

Safe handling

Swallow capsules whole with water; do not open, chew or crush them. Topotecan can be taken with or without food. Keep it refrigerated at 2–8°C; do not freeze. The pharmacy label gives the exact strengths and number of capsules. Keep away from children; ask the pharmacy how to return unused or damaged capsules.
