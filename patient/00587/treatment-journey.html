Topotecan capsules · small-cell lung cancer: treatment and checks

Relapsed small-cell lung cancer

Take the prescribed topotecan capsules once daily on Days 1–5 of a 21-day cycle. Days 6–21 have no planned topotecan. Up to 6 cycles are planned, with earlier changes if needed.
