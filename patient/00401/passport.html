NCCP 00401 · version 6

Alectinib monotherapy

Take alectinib twice daily with food, every day, with no planned rest week. After surgery, treatment lasts up to 24 months, stopping sooner for recurrence or unacceptable side effects. For advanced cancer, treatment continues until progression or unacceptable side effects under the oncology plan.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact the oncology team promptly for new or worsening cough or breathlessness, unexplained muscle pain or weakness, yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, marked tiredness or pallor, dizziness or fainting, severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting or diarrhoea, or reduced urine. Sudden serious breathing difficulty, severe chest pain or collapse needs emergency help. Do not wait for the next blood test.
