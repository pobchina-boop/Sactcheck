1. Treatment and purpose

Weekly gemcitabine with radiotherapy · NCCP 00759 v1

Gemcitabine 100 mg/m² is given weekly on days 1, 8, 15 and 22, two to four hours before radiotherapy. There are four planned gemcitabine doses in one course. This low-dose schedule is different from gemcitabine 1000 mg/m² chemotherapy.

The whole treatment course

2. Risks and supportive care

Gemcitabine · Chemotherapy

Report fever, unusual bleeding, breathlessness, rash, swelling or reduced urine promptly.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the intended benefit, alternatives, common and serious risks, lasting effects, fertility and individual priorities. Record the patient’s questions and the agreed plan. NCCP 00759 is four weekly 100 mg/m² gemcitabine doses with radiotherapy. Do not import a 1000 mg/m² gemcitabine dose, repeated chemotherapy cycles or another radiotherapy prescription.
