Your complete treatment reference

Gemcitabine, carboplatin and bevacizumab

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Control the cancer, relieve symptoms and delay progression.

Bevacizumab · Avastin or biosimilar

Antibody targeting VEGF. It reduces signals that help tumours form blood vessels. This can also affect blood pressure, kidneys and wound healing. Call urgently for severe abdominal pain, significant bleeding, a new severe headache or sudden weakness.

Gemcitabine · Gemcitabine

Chemotherapy. It interferes with copying DNA as cells divide. Blood-forming cells can also be affected. Phone promptly about fever, bleeding, a new cough or difficulty breathing.

Carboplatin · Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy. It damages DNA in dividing cells. Kidney function helps determine the prescribed amount. Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · combination

Bevacizumab, gemcitabine and carboplatin on day 1 of each three-week cycle. Six cycles are usual and up to ten may be given.

Day 8 · gemcitabine

Gemcitabine alone after a blood count and kidney check. If omitted for low counts, this dose is skipped for the rest of the cycle.

Maintenance · bevacizumab

After chemotherapy finishes, bevacizumab alone every three weeks while it helps and side effects are manageable.

Common effects and what to do

Between visits

Fatigue, taste changes and nausea can affect meals and daily activities. Take the prescribed anti-sickness medicines and call if you cannot drink.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call oncology urgently for fever, chills, new breathlessness or severe diarrhoea. Severe abdominal pain or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent review. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Bevacizumab

Your team checks blood pressure, urine protein and healing. Report recent procedures, bleeding or new abdominal pain.

Gemcitabine

Contact the unit for fever, unusual bleeding or new breathlessness.

Carboplatin

Kidney function helps determine carboplatin dosing. Report previous platinum allergy.

What happens if my day 8 blood count is too low?

Day 8 is gemcitabine alone. If your team omits this dose for low counts, it is skipped for this cycle. Ask for your next blood-test date before leaving.

Planning your visits

Days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks, then bevacizumab alone.

At home

Keep the hospital contact numbers beside your treatment card. Call promptly if you feel unwell.

What the research found

Adding bevacizumab delayed progression in platinum-sensitive recurrent disease.

The final analysis did not demonstrate a significant overall survival benefit.
