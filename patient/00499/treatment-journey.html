Your treatment journey

Gemcitabine, carboplatin and bevacizumab

Control the cancer, relieve symptoms and delay progression.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · combination

Bevacizumab, gemcitabine and carboplatin on day 1 of each three-week cycle. Six cycles are usual and up to ten may be given.

Day 8 · gemcitabine

Gemcitabine alone after a blood count and kidney check. If omitted for low counts, this dose is skipped for the rest of the cycle.

Maintenance · bevacizumab

After chemotherapy finishes, bevacizumab alone every three weeks while it helps and side effects are manageable.

At review visits

  • What will I receive at the next visit? Days 1 and 8 every 3 weeks, then bevacizumab alone.
  • When should I call? Call oncology urgently for fever, chills, new breathlessness or severe diarrhoea. Severe abdominal pain or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent review.
  • What happens if my day 8 blood count is too low? Day 8 is gemcitabine alone. If your team omits this dose for low counts, it is skipped for this cycle. Ask for your next blood-test date before leaving.
