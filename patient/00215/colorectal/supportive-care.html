Active medicines: Bevacizumab

Emetogenic risk: Minimal (bevacizumab component only)

No routine antiemetic prophylaxis for the bevacizumab component. The prescribed fluoropyrimidine-based chemotherapy requires its own antiemetic and supportive-care plan; minimal risk here does not classify the whole combination.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Premedication is not usually required unless there has been previous hypersensitivity; the treating team specifies any individual plan.
  • Review blood pressure, urine protein, bleeding, thrombosis, wounds and planned procedures before dosing.
  • Provide a prescribed diarrhoea plan appropriate to the chemotherapy partner; bevacizumab does not define a universal loperamide schedule.
  • Urgent assessment for significant bleeding, severe abdominal pain, chest pain, sudden breathlessness, severe headache, confusion, seizure or visual disturbance.
  • Use the separate chemotherapy schedule and support prescription. No lung six-cycle limit or ovarian course cap applies to this colorectal context.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
