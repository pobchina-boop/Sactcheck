Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Partner chemotherapy

This covers bevacizumab only. Bring the separate chemotherapy schedule, anti-sickness plan and urgent-contact instructions.

STEP 2

Blood pressure and urine

These checks can find problems before you feel unwell. Take blood-pressure medicines as prescribed; do not adjust them yourself.

STEP 3

Surgery and dental procedures

Tell oncology before any operation or invasive procedure. The team must agree treatment pauses and assess wound healing.

STEP 4

Support medicines

Bevacizumab alone does not usually need routine anti-sickness prevention. Chemotherapy partners may need a different plan.
