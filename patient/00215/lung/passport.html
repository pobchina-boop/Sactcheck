Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg · lung treatment component

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg is infused on Day 1 every 21 days. With platinum chemotherapy, the combined phase lasts up to six cycles, then bevacizumab may continue alone. With erlotinib, the combination continues while helpful and tolerated. Progression or unacceptable toxicity can stop either plan earlier; no fixed overall lung-treatment duration is promised.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Seek urgent help for sudden severe abdominal pain, significant bleeding or coughing blood, chest pain, breathlessness, a swollen painful leg, a severe headache, confusion, seizures, a change in vision, or a rapidly painful/red spreading wound. Follow the chemotherapy emergency plan for fever or feeling acutely unwell.
