NCCP 00215 · version 7

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg · lung treatment component

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg is infused on Day 1 every 21 days. With platinum chemotherapy, the combined phase lasts up to six cycles, then bevacizumab may continue alone. With erlotinib, the combination continues while helpful and tolerated. Progression or unacceptable toxicity can stop either plan earlier; no fixed overall lung-treatment duration is promised.

What each check is for

1Confirm treatment scopeBefore starting and whenever the partner changes

NCCP 00215f: first-line unresectable advanced, recurrent or metastatic non-squamous NSCLC with platinum-based chemotherapy. NCCP 00215g: the corresponding first-line EGFR-activating-mutation setting with erlotinib. This is the bevacizumab component; every partner needs its own prescription, assessment and consent.

Ask / act: Confirm indication, partner, phase and separate assessment.

Sources: NCCP 00215

2Blood pressure and proteinuriaBaseline, each cycle and extra checks when indicated

These effects can be silent, so attend planned tests even when well.

Ask / act: Agree who books, reviews and acts on each test.

Sources: NCCP 00215

3Procedures and urgent symptomsThroughout treatment and treatment breaks

Seek urgent help for sudden severe abdominal pain, significant bleeding or coughing blood, chest pain, breathlessness, a swollen painful leg, a severe headache, confusion, seizures, a change in vision, or a rapidly painful/red spreading wound. Follow the chemotherapy emergency plan for fever or feeling acutely unwell.

Ask / act: Contact the team before procedures or for urgent symptoms.

Sources: NCCP 00215 · SMPC
