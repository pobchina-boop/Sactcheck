NCCP 00215 · version 7

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg · lung treatment component

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg is infused on Day 1 every 21 days. With platinum chemotherapy, the combined phase lasts up to six cycles, then bevacizumab may continue alone. With erlotinib, the combination continues while helpful and tolerated. Progression or unacceptable toxicity can stop either plan earlier; no fixed overall lung-treatment duration is promised.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Bevacizumab blocks VEGF, a signal that supports blood-vessel growth. In selected non-squamous lung cancers it is added to another treatment to help control cancer; the balance of benefit and risk depends on the partner and tumour features. NCCP 00215f: first-line unresectable advanced, recurrent or metastatic non-squamous NSCLC with platinum-based chemotherapy. NCCP 00215g: the corresponding first-line EGFR-activating-mutation setting with erlotinib. This is the bevacizumab component; every partner needs its own prescription, assessment and consent.

Material risks and support

  • Raised blood pressure and protein in the urine
  • Bleeding, blood clots and problems with wound healing
  • Rare bowel perforation, abnormal internal connections called fistulae, or serious wound infection
  • Rare severe headache, confusion, seizures or vision changes associated with a brain syndrome
  • Infusion reactions and fatigue; the partner treatment adds its own risks

Confirm the chosen lung pathway, disease-control aim, alternatives and the evidence for the specific partner. Discuss blood-pressure and urine monitoring, pulmonary and other bleeding, clots, bowel perforation/fistula, wound healing, infusion reactions, fertility/pregnancy precautions and all partner risks. The chemotherapy and erlotinib trials showed different outcomes; neither predicts an individual result.

Monitoring and understanding

Before starting: blood count, kidney and liver tests, urine protein, blood pressure and a heart history/examination. Repeat blood count, kidney/liver tests and urine testing before each cycle; check blood pressure before treatment and after it. Extra checks depend on symptoms and results.
