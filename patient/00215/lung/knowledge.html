Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg · lung treatment component

Bevacizumab 15 mg/kg is infused on Day 1 every 21 days. With platinum chemotherapy, the combined phase lasts up to six cycles, then bevacizumab may continue alone. With erlotinib, the combination continues while helpful and tolerated. Progression or unacceptable toxicity can stop either plan earlier; no fixed overall lung-treatment duration is promised.

How this medicine supports your treatment

Bevacizumab blocks VEGF, a signal that supports blood-vessel growth. In selected non-squamous lung cancers it is added to another treatment to help control cancer; the balance of benefit and risk depends on the partner and tumour features.

Binds VEGF, a signal that promotes blood-vessel growth. Blocking VEGF can reduce the blood supply that supports tumour growth; it also affects normal blood vessels and wound healing.

Report bleeding, severe pain, neurological symptoms or wound problems promptly, and attend blood-pressure and urine checks.

Evidence and personal choice

Adding bevacizumab to carboplatin/paclitaxel improved survival in one historical trial. Adding it to erlotinib delayed progression in another trial without a demonstrated overall-survival improvement. These findings concern complete combinations, not bevacizumab alone. They do not rank modern treatment options or predict an individual outcome.

Sandler et al., NEJM 2006: ECOG 4599 carboplatin/paclitaxel with or without bevacizumab · Yamamoto et al., Lung Cancer 2021: JO25567 updated PFS and final OS

Tests and review

Before starting: blood count, kidney and liver tests, urine protein, blood pressure and a heart history/examination. Repeat blood count, kidney/liver tests and urine testing before each cycle; check blood pressure before treatment and after it. Extra checks depend on symptoms and results.

Urine results

Protein in the urine can require a 24-hour collection and additional checks. Your team explains how and when to collect it, and reviews whether treatment can continue.

NCCP 00215 v7 · official regimen

Heart and blood-thinning medicines

An ultrasound of the heart may be considered if you have had certain earlier chemotherapy or chest-wall radiotherapy. If you take warfarin, INR tests are usually weekly until stable and then before each cycle; follow your individual plan.

Surgery, wounds and teeth

Tell the team about planned operations, dental procedures and any wound that is not healing. Bevacizumab can require a treatment break around procedures; the team sets the timing.

Separate partner support

Bevacizumab itself usually needs no routine anti-sickness premedication. Platinum chemotherapy needs its own anti-sickness and other support plan. Erlotinib needs its own tablet, interaction, skin and diarrhoea plan. Keep the complete plan and emergency numbers.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Discuss fertility and effective contraception before treatment and continue contraception during treatment and for at least six months after the last bevacizumab dose. Do not breastfeed during treatment or for at least six months after the final dose. The partner treatment may require additional precautions.

Questions to ask

Which lung pathway am I following?

Ask whether you are receiving bevacizumab with platinum chemotherapy or with erlotinib for an EGFR-mutated cancer. Their partner schedules, checks and phase changes differ.

When does treatment change?

The platinum combination is given for up to six cycles, after which bevacizumab can continue alone if appropriate. The erlotinib pathway has no automatic six-cycle switch. The team reviews benefit, side effects and your priorities.

What happens if urine protein or blood pressure rises?

The team may repeat tests, treat blood pressure or pause bevacizumab. Severe problems may mean stopping it. Do not restart after a pause without an agreed plan.

What if I need an operation or dental work?

Tell the oncology team before the procedure. Bevacizumab can slow healing, and the team must coordinate any treatment break and check healing before restarting.

Explain the plan back: Which lung pathway applies, when are bevacizumab and partner treatments due, when are blood-pressure and urine checks, and which symptoms need urgent help?
