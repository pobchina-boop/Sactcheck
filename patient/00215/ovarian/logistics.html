Preparing for treatment visits

Bevacizumab every three weeks for ovarian cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

First-line treatment starts with chemotherapy. Bevacizumab starts in cycle 2 and is given every 21 days. After up to six chemotherapy cycles it can continue alone, up to 15 months (22 cycles) in total. In platinum-resistant recurrence it is given every 21 days with five-day topotecan until progression or unacceptable side effects.

What to bring

Bring the appointment calendar, medicines list, oncology contact card and any record of new symptoms.

Before the infusion

Tell the team before dental surgery or an operation. Starting or restarting bevacizumab after major surgery requires adequate healing and at least 28 days.
