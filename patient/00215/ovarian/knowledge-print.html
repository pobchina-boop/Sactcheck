Your treatment reference

Bevacizumab every three weeks for ovarian cancer

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Your team may add bevacizumab to initial chemotherapy for advanced ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer and then continue it alone. A different use is with five-day topotecan if the cancer has returned and is resistant to platinum.

Bevacizumab · Avastin or the prescribed biosimilar

Targeted anti-VEGF antibody. Binds VEGF, a signal that promotes blood-vessel growth. Blocking VEGF can reduce the blood supply that supports tumour growth; it also affects normal blood vessels and wound healing. Report bleeding, severe pain, neurological symptoms or wound problems promptly, and attend blood-pressure and urine checks.

Your infusion schedule

First-line · with carboplatin and paclitaxel

Bevacizumab starts with cycle 2. Confirm wound healing and the chemotherapy premedicines for this visit.

First-line · bevacizumab alone

After the chemotherapy phase, bevacizumab can continue every three weeks within the first-line course limit.

Recurrence · with five-day topotecan

Bevacizumab is due on day 1. Topotecan is due on days 1 to 5. Confirm the written anti-sickness plan for each return visit.

Common effects and what to do

Blood pressure and urine protein

Blood pressure can rise and protein can leak into urine without causing symptoms. Attend the scheduled checks.

Nosebleeds and healing

Tell the team about repeated nosebleeds, new bruising or a wound that is slow to heal.

Serious effects: when to call

Head / vision

Severe headache, confusion, seizures or a change in vision need urgent help.

Heart / blood vessels

Attend blood-pressure checks; chest pain, breathlessness or a swollen painful leg need urgent help.

Kidneys / urine

Urine checks look for protein. Report new swelling, foamy urine or reduced urine.

Abdomen / bowel

Sudden severe abdominal pain needs urgent assessment.

Skin / wounds

Report bleeding or wounds that are painful, red, spreading or slow to heal.

Leg / clots

A new swollen painful leg or sudden breathlessness needs urgent help.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call now for new severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, severe headache or a change in vision. Call 112 or 999 for sudden chest pain, severe breathlessness, seizure or stroke symptoms. With chemotherapy, fever, chills or suddenly feeling unwell also need urgent assessment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Before treatment

Tell the team before dental surgery or an operation. Starting or restarting bevacizumab after major surgery requires adequate healing and at least 28 days.

Between visits

Call now for new severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, severe headache or a change in vision. Call 112 or 999 for sudden chest pain, severe breathlessness, seizure or stroke symptoms. With chemotherapy, fever, chills or suddenly feeling unwell also need urgent assessment.

Planning your visits

Appointments

First-line treatment starts with chemotherapy. Bevacizumab starts in cycle 2 and is given every 21 days. After up to six chemotherapy cycles it can continue alone, up to 15 months (22 cycles) in total. In platinum-resistant recurrence it is given every 21 days with five-day topotecan until progression or unacceptable side effects.

What to bring

Bring the appointment calendar, medicines list, oncology contact card and any record of new symptoms.

Before the infusion

Tell the team before dental surgery or an operation. Starting or restarting bevacizumab after major surgery requires adequate healing and at least 28 days.

What the research found

Continuing bevacizumab after chemotherapy delayed progression in the first-line study.

The final analysis did not show an overall survival improvement across the whole study population. The first-line results do not estimate outcomes with five-day topotecan in recurrent disease.
