Treatment and purpose

Your team may add bevacizumab to initial chemotherapy for advanced ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer and then continue it alone. A different use is with five-day topotecan if the cancer has returned and is resistant to platinum. 00215a covers first-line FIGO IIIB, IIIC or IV epithelial ovarian, tubal or primary peritoneal cancer with carboplatin and paclitaxel. 00215c covers platinum-resistant recurrence with five-day topotecan after no more than two prior chemotherapy regimens and no prior VEGF-directed treatment.

Treatment schedule. First-line treatment starts with chemotherapy. Bevacizumab starts in cycle 2 and is given every 21 days. After up to six chemotherapy cycles it can continue alone, up to 15 months (22 cycles) in total. In platinum-resistant recurrence it is given every 21 days with five-day topotecan until progression or unacceptable side effects.

Treatment discussion and decision

Bevacizumab every three weeks for ovarian cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Continuing bevacizumab after chemotherapy delayed progression in the first-line study. The final analysis did not show an overall survival improvement across the whole study population. The first-line results do not estimate outcomes with five-day topotecan in recurrent disease.

The prescriber will discuss the medicines on your individual plan. This sheet covers bevacizumab. Complete the separate consent for the selected chemotherapy partner before treatment.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call now for new severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, severe headache or a change in vision. Call 112 or 999 for sudden chest pain, severe breathlessness, seizure or stroke symptoms. With chemotherapy, fever, chills or suddenly feeling unwell also need urgent assessment. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
