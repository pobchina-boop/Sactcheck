Treatment context
This chemotherapy aims to control lung cancer and symptoms when cisplatin is unsuitable. The team reviews benefit, side effects and your priorities.
Schedule
Four 21 day cycles are planned, unless treatment stops or changes earlier. Day 1: carboplatin plus vinorelbine. Day 8: vinorelbine only. Your prescription specifies vinorelbine by drip OR capsules; these are alternatives, not extra doses.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Vinorelbine

Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy
Low blood counts and serious infection
Constipation; rarely bowel obstruction
Numbness or tingling
Infusion-site injury if medicine leaks
Fatigue, nausea and hair thinning

Carboplatin

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts and infection
Nausea and vomiting
Kidney, hearing and nerve effects
Allergic reactions

Discuss serious infection and low blood counts, allergy, constipation/ileus, neuropathy that can persist, mouth sores, extravasation if IV, oral-handling issues if capsules, fertility and individual material risks.
