The whole treatment journey

PCV chemotherapy · 56-day cycle: treatment and checks

NCCP 00658: adjuvant treatment of grade II glioma after radiotherapy.

Lomustine once on Day 1; procarbazine daily on Days 8–21; vincristine into a vein on Days 8 and 29. The cycle lasts 56 days. Six cycles are planned, with review before treatment.

Read from left to right. Columns show stages, not equal lengths of time. This is a general plan: no dates or personal information are needed. Your team confirms which steps apply and arranges appointments.

What each check is for

1PCV · 56-day cycleThree medicines on different days

This eight-week PCV regimen combines procarbazine, lomustine and vincristine after radiotherapy for grade II glioma. It aims to improve disease control. NCCP describes this indication as unlicensed; the treating team explains the evidence, alternatives and local consent process.

Ask / act: Confirm the current phase, prescription, blood tests and support medicines.
