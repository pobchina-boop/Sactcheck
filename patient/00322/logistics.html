Everolimus + exemestane

Both tablets are taken every day continuously. There is no planned week off. A four-week blood-test review is a monitoring interval, not a stop-and-restart treatment cycle.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Take everolimus at the same time daily, consistently with food or consistently without food. Take exemestane after a meal. Swallow tablets whole with water; do not crush or chew. If a dose is missed, take the next scheduled dose without adding an extra dose.

Two daily tablets, two different jobs

Keep a simple routine for the oral medicines and request your next supply before tablets run out. No port or pump is needed for this regimen itself.

Four-week review, continuous treatment

A blood-test or pharmacy review every four weeks does not mean a rest week. Follow the prescribed daily doses unless the team explicitly tells you to pause.

The first eight weeks: mouth care

Agree a mouth-care plan before the first dose. The team may prescribe an alcohol-free steroid mouthwash. Report soreness early, before it affects eating or drinking; use the specific prescribed directions.

When one medicine is held

Ask the team to state clearly which tablet to pause, whether exemestane should continue, what needs to recover and who will authorise restarting everolimus.

Radiotherapy or surgery

Tell both teams that you take everolimus. Treatment given during or soon after radiotherapy may increase toxicity, and previous irradiated areas can develop recall inflammation. The teams decide spacing or interruption; there is no universal interval on this card.

Response scans

Ask which scan will assess benefit and when it is due. Routine response imaging follows your individual plan. New breathing symptoms need earlier assessment rather than waiting for that scan.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
