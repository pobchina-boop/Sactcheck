What each check is for

1Blood testsBefore starting: FBC, renal and liver profile, urea/BUN, urinary protein, blood glucose, cholesterol and triglycerides. Confirm the endocrine indication, review lung symptoms and infections, and check all medicines. Assess bone/fracture risk for the exemestane component. FBC, renal/liver profile, cholesterol and triglycerides every four weeks or as clinically indicated. Check fasting glucose as clinically indicated, with closer monitoring if diabetes or another medicine increases risk. Ask about mouth soreness, infection, cough/breathlessness and daily function at reviews.

Checks safety before continuation.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

2Heart checksNCCP 00322 does not mandate routine ECG or echocardiography. Cardiac tests should follow symptoms, comorbidities and other treatments, rather than a copied HER2-treatment schedule.

Checks cardiac safety when the agent or clinical risk requires it.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

3ImagingResponse assessment in advanced breast cancer is generally every two to four months, adapted to disease dynamics, sites, symptoms and treatment. Less frequent monitoring may suit indolent disease. New symptoms or suspected progression need earlier assessment. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not an automatic three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

Uses the correct surveillance or response-assessment pathway.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: ESMO_MBC2023

4Bone healthExemestane can reduce bone mineral density. Review fracture risk and arrange DXA and bone protection when indicated. This regimen does not specify a single repeat-DXA interval for everyone.

Companion endocrine therapy and individual fracture risk may change the plan.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC
