Everolimus + exemestane

Usual starting doses are everolimus 10 mg once daily and exemestane 25 mg once daily; use the doses on your prescription. Both tablets are taken every day continuously. There is no planned rest week. Review benefit and tolerability over time; there is no fixed adjuvant completion date.

Understand the treatment. Keep the plan manageable.

Exemestane lowers oestrogen production while everolimus blocks an intracellular growth pathway that can contribute to endocrine resistance. The aim in this setting is to control cancer and delay progression.

Open your complete treatment reference
mTOR-targeted tablet

Everolimus

Inhibits mTORC1 growth signalling
Growth signals reach mTOR↓Everolimus blocks mTORC1↓Cancer-cell growth signalling decreases

Everolimus binds the intracellular protein FKBP12; this complex inhibits mTORC1, affecting protein synthesis and growth signalling. This pathway can support endocrine resistance, which explains pairing it with an oestrogen-lowering medicine. The same pathway operates in normal tissues, contributing to mouth ulcers, infection risk and metabolic changes.

Report new mouth soreness, cough/breathlessness, fever/infection symptoms, diarrhoea or excessive thirst promptly. A new cough must not simply be labelled drug pneumonitis without checking other causes.

Steroidal aromatase-inhibitor tablet

Exemestane

Irreversibly inhibits aromatase and lowers oestrogen production
Aromatase makes oestrogen↓Exemestane blocks aromatase↓Less oestrogen drives cancer cells

After menopause, aromatase in tissues converts androgen precursors into oestrogen. Exemestane is an irreversible aromatase inhibitor that reduces this supply to hormone-sensitive cancer cells. It does not directly switch off functioning ovaries; the menopausal indication must be confirmed.

Tell the team if joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal dryness or sleep disturbance affects daily life. Bone thinning can be silent, so keep the agreed bone-health reviews.

Support throughout treatment

Nausea support

Everolimus is oral minimal–low emetic risk. No routine infusion premedication is needed; agree individual rescue treatment and local oral-SACT advice.

Mouth care from the start

Agree non-alcoholic mouth care and whether a prescribed alcohol-free corticosteroid mouthwash is appropriate during the first eight weeks. Report mouth ulcers early and check for thrush rather than adding an antifungal automatically.

Lungs and infection

New cough, breathlessness or fever needs prompt review. If systemic steroids or other immunosuppression are used, the team considers PJP prevention according to risk.

Metabolic and bone health

Keep glucose, cholesterol, kidney and liver checks. Discuss diabetes/lipid care, exemestane bone risk, exercise, diet and any indicated bone-protection treatment.

Surgery, radiotherapy and other medicines

Tell both oncology and procedure teams about everolimus before surgery or radiotherapy. Check all new medicines and supplements; avoid grapefruit and St John’s wort.
