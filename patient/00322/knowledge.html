How this medicine supports your treatment

Exemestane lowers oestrogen production while everolimus blocks an intracellular growth pathway that can contribute to endocrine resistance. The aim in this setting is to control cancer and delay progression.

mTOR-targeted tablet

Everolimus

Inhibits mTORC1 growth signalling
Growth signals reach mTOR↓Everolimus blocks mTORC1↓Cancer-cell growth signalling decreases

Everolimus binds the intracellular protein FKBP12; this complex inhibits mTORC1, affecting protein synthesis and growth signalling. This pathway can support endocrine resistance, which explains pairing it with an oestrogen-lowering medicine. The same pathway operates in normal tissues, contributing to mouth ulcers, infection risk and metabolic changes.

Report new mouth soreness, cough/breathlessness, fever/infection symptoms, diarrhoea or excessive thirst promptly. A new cough must not simply be labelled drug pneumonitis without checking other causes.

Steroidal aromatase-inhibitor tablet

Exemestane

Irreversibly inhibits aromatase and lowers oestrogen production
Aromatase makes oestrogen↓Exemestane blocks aromatase↓Less oestrogen drives cancer cells

After menopause, aromatase in tissues converts androgen precursors into oestrogen. Exemestane is an irreversible aromatase inhibitor that reduces this supply to hormone-sensitive cancer cells. It does not directly switch off functioning ovaries; the menopausal indication must be confirmed.

Tell the team if joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal dryness or sleep disturbance affects daily life. Bone thinning can be silent, so keep the agreed bone-health reviews.

Your treatment aim

Exemestane lowers oestrogen production while everolimus blocks an intracellular growth pathway that can contribute to endocrine resistance. The aim in this setting is to control cancer and delay progression.

Who this treatment is for

Postmenopausal HR-positive/HER2-negative advanced disease after recurrence/progression following an NSAI, without symptomatic visceral disease. The intended endocrine partner is exemestane; do not automatically substitute another endocrine drug.

NCCP 00322 v4 · official regimen

Schedule and duration

Usual starting doses are everolimus 10 mg once daily and exemestane 25 mg once daily; use the doses on your prescription. Both tablets are taken every day continuously. There is no planned rest week. Review benefit and tolerability over time; there is no fixed adjuvant completion date. Continue while beneficial and tolerated; review each medicine separately if everolimus is held.

FBC, renal and liver profile; blood urea nitrogen and urinary protein. Blood glucose, total cholesterol and triglycerides; optimise pre-existing metabolic abnormalities. Review lung symptoms, infection, mouth health, all medicines, planned surgery/radiotherapy and exemestane bone risk.

Living with treatment

What the studies tell us

Adding everolimus to exemestane delayed cancer growth compared with exemestane alone in the study. The trial did not show a clear improvement in how long people lived overall. This older study cannot give a precise estimate for someone previously treated with modern CDK4/6 combinations.

How to interpret this

Study results describe groups with defined treatment and eligibility. Ask about your own likely benefits, important harms, alternatives and remaining uncertainty.

BOLERO-2 final PFS, Yardley et al 2013 · BOLERO-2 final OS, Piccart et al 2014

How long does treatment last?

Do I need to enter dates?

No. This map shows general timing without personal information. Your hospital arranges actual appointments; ask who books each test and who reviews its result.

What if a dose is missed or treatment is held?

Take everolimus at the same time daily, consistently with food or consistently without food. Take exemestane after a meal. Swallow tablets whole with water; do not crush or chew. If a dose is missed, take the next scheduled dose without adding an extra dose. A hold may affect one medicine rather than the whole combination; confirm each component.

What does the evidence mean for me?

Adding everolimus to exemestane delayed cancer growth compared with exemestane alone in the study. The trial did not show a clear improvement in how long people lived overall. This older study cannot give a precise estimate for someone previously treated with modern CDK4/6 combinations.

What happens after treatment changes or ends?

Continue while beneficial and tolerated; review each medicine separately if everolimus is held. Keep the separately agreed disease follow-up, endocrine and bone-health plan.
