How this medicine supports your treatment

Exemestane lowers oestrogen production while everolimus blocks an intracellular growth pathway that can contribute to endocrine resistance. The aim in this setting is to control cancer and delay progression.

mTOR-targeted tablet

Everolimus

Inhibits mTORC1 growth signalling
Growth signals reach mTOR↓Everolimus blocks mTORC1↓Cancer-cell growth signalling decreases

Everolimus binds the intracellular protein FKBP12; this complex inhibits mTORC1, affecting protein synthesis and growth signalling. This pathway can support endocrine resistance, which explains pairing it with an oestrogen-lowering medicine. The same pathway operates in normal tissues, contributing to mouth ulcers, infection risk and metabolic changes.

Report new mouth soreness, cough/breathlessness, fever/infection symptoms, diarrhoea or excessive thirst promptly. A new cough must not simply be labelled drug pneumonitis without checking other causes.

Steroidal aromatase-inhibitor tablet

Exemestane

Irreversibly inhibits aromatase and lowers oestrogen production
Aromatase makes oestrogen↓Exemestane blocks aromatase↓Less oestrogen drives cancer cells

After menopause, aromatase in tissues converts androgen precursors into oestrogen. Exemestane is an irreversible aromatase inhibitor that reduces this supply to hormone-sensitive cancer cells. It does not directly switch off functioning ovaries; the menopausal indication must be confirmed.

Tell the team if joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal dryness or sleep disturbance affects daily life. Bone thinning can be silent, so keep the agreed bone-health reviews.

The exact treatment being discussed

Exemestane lowers oestrogen production while everolimus blocks an intracellular growth pathway that can contribute to endocrine resistance. The aim in this setting is to control cancer and delay progression.

Who this covers

NCCP 00322a: postmenopausal hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer without symptomatic visceral disease after recurrence or progression following a non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor.

Dose and administration

Usual starting doses: everolimus 10 mg once daily and exemestane 25 mg once daily; the prescribed everolimus dose may be lower.. Take everolimus at the same time daily, consistently with food or consistently without food. Take exemestane after a meal. Swallow tablets whole with water; do not crush or chew. If a dose is missed, take the next scheduled dose without adding an extra dose.

Individual safety and source differences

If everolimus is interrupted or reduced for toxicity, NCCP says exemestane may continue; the treating team must confirm the plan for each medicine. Do not stop both automatically or restart everolimus yourself. Everolimus can increase toxicity around radiotherapy and impair wound healing: agree a specific plan before radiotherapy or surgery.

Your wider treatment plan

Additional treatments depend on the confirmed setting and indication.

Advanced-disease scans

Response assessment in advanced breast cancer is generally every two to four months, adapted to disease dynamics, sites, symptoms and treatment. Less frequent monitoring may suit indolent disease. New symptoms or suspected progression need earlier assessment. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not an automatic three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

Pan-Asian adapted ESMO metastatic breast cancer guidelines

Tumour markers

If informative, particularly when elevated at baseline or disease is not readily measurable, tumour markers may supplement clinical assessment and imaging. A marker rise alone should not trigger a treatment change. There is no universal fixed marker timetable.

ESO-ESMO ABC 5 consensus: tumour-marker interpretation

Pregnancy, fertility and contraception

This NCCP indication is postmenopausal. If pregnancy remains possible or menopause is uncertain, ask the team to confirm endocrine status and suitable contraception. Everolimus product information advises highly effective contraception during treatment and for eight weeks after it ends; pregnancy and breastfeeding are NCCP exclusions.

Evidence depends on the population

Different studies answer different questions; do not compare their results as if populations and follow-up were identical.

BOLERO-2 overall survival analysis

Same randomised BOLERO-2 population; overall survival was a key secondary endpoint. Overall survival: median 31.0 versus 26.6 months; HR 0.89 (95% CI 0.73–1.10), p=0.14. No statistically significant overall-survival advantage was demonstrated. Do not present the PFS benefit as proven longer survival. Subsequent therapies were common; OS was secondary, and the confidence interval includes no difference.

SWISH stomatitis-prevention study

Single-arm prevention study in 92 postmenopausal women starting everolimus+exemestane; 85 evaluable for the week 8 stomatitis endpoint in the EU SmPC report. EU SmPC reports Grade ≥2 stomatitis at 8 weeks in 2/85 (2.4%); no Grade 3/4 cases. Supports discussing a prescribed alcohol-free dexamethasone mouthwash early in treatment. Study regimen: 0.5 mg/5 mL solution, 10 mL swished for 2 minutes and spat four times daily for 8 weeks; local formulation/prescribing review is needed. Single-arm study with historical comparison, not a randomised concurrent control; oral candidiasis occurred and the mouthwash is a separate prescription.

Practical support

Nausea support

NCCP classifies everolimus as minimal-to-low emetic risk. There are no routine infusion premedications. Any as-needed antiemetic follows the individual prescription and local oral-SACT policy.

Prevent and treat mouth soreness

Discuss non-alcoholic mouth care and a prescribed alcohol-free corticosteroid mouthwash during the first eight weeks. Avoid irritating products containing alcohol, hydrogen peroxide, iodine or thyme derivatives. Check for oral thrush; do not automatically add an antifungal.

Steroids and infection prevention

Systemic steroids may be needed for pneumonitis. When steroids or other immunosuppressive medicines are used, the team considers PJP prevention; it is not an automatic prescription for every patient.

Glucose, lipids and bone health

Manage diabetes and lipid abnormalities alongside treatment. Bone-health advice and medicines depend on fracture risk, DXA findings, diet and vitamin-D status.

Medicine and supplement review

Check prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines and supplements with oncology pharmacy. Avoid grapefruit and St John’s wort; some antibiotics and antifungals can markedly change everolimus exposure.

What the evidence can tell us

BOLERO-2 final progression-free survival analysis: population, findings and limits

724 postmenopausal patients with HR-positive/HER2-negative advanced breast cancer after non-steroidal aromatase inhibitors; everolimus+exemestane versus placebo+exemestane.

Investigator-assessed PFS: median 7.8 versus 3.2 months; HR 0.45 (95% CI 0.38–0.54), p<0.0001. Central review: 11.0 versus 4.1 months. Adding mTOR inhibition delayed progression compared with exemestane alone in this trial population.

Conducted before routine CDK4/6-inhibitor use; the observed benefit is not a guaranteed individual outcome and is not direct randomised evidence for current post-CDK4/6 sequencing.
