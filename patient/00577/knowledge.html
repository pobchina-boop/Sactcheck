When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Call oncology urgently for sudden illness, persistent vomiting, inability to drink, jaundice, new breathlessness or severe rash. Call 112/999 for severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure, or new leg weakness with loss of bladder or bowel control.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

Understand your treatment

How the medicine works

This treatment aims to control prostate cancer and delay progression. The expected benefit depends on your disease setting, previous treatment and general health.

Androgen pathway treatment

Abiraterone

Oral targeted treatment
Adds deeper androgen suppression to ADT

ADT lowers testosterone from the testes. Abiraterone blocks CYP17 to reduce androgen production elsewhere too. Prednisolone limits hormonal side effects. This combination can be offered early, before ADT stops controlling the cancer.

ADT lowers testosterone↓Abiraterone reduces more androgen↓Cancer growth signals reduced

What to watch for
Never stop prednisolone suddenly or when abiraterone is held unless the team gives a steroid plan. Ask about a steroid alert card and illness instructions. Contact the team if vomiting prevents you keeping tablets down. Abiraterone with prednisolone must not be combined with radium-223.

Sources: nccp · ema

Corticosteroid

Prednisolone

Prescribed oral steroid
Reduces excess hormonal drive

Prednisolone suppresses ACTH drive during abiraterone treatment, helping control blood pressure, fluid retention and potassium. It needs its own illness and withdrawal plan.

ACTH drive reduced↓Hormonal side effects limited↓Abiraterone supported

What to watch for
Never stop prednisolone suddenly or when abiraterone is held unless the team gives a steroid plan. Ask about a steroid alert card and illness instructions. Contact the team if vomiting prevents you keeping tablets down. Abiraterone with prednisolone must not be combined with radium-223.

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests, glucose and blood pressure; ECG if clinically indicated or if you have a cardiac history. Review potassium, swelling, heart health and all medicines.

STEP 2

Taking or receiving treatment

Take abiraterone on an empty stomach: at least 2 hours after food, then wait at least 1 hour before eating. Swallow tablets whole with water. Take prednisolone with food. If a daily dose of either medicine is missed, take the usual dose the next day; do not double it.

STEP 3

Tests and reviews

Blood count, kidney and liver tests, glucose and blood pressure every 2 weeks for the first 3 treatment months, then every 4 weeks. Potassium may need closer checks if low; assess swelling and cardiac symptoms. ECG if clinically indicated or with cardiac history.

STEP 4

Specific practical instructions

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next appointment, dose, support medicines and verified daytime and out-of-hours phone numbers.

Support around the medicines

Preventing and treating sickness

This is minimal-to-low oral sickness risk; the team agrees rescue medicine if needed.

Medicine-specific support

Never stop prednisolone suddenly or when abiraterone is held unless the team gives a steroid plan. Ask about a steroid alert card and illness instructions. Contact the team if vomiting prevents you keeping tablets down. Abiraterone with prednisolone must not be combined with radium-223.

Living well during hormone treatment

Discuss hot flushes, fatigue, mood, sexual function and fertility. Ask for an individual activity and bone-health plan, including appropriate strength exercise, falls prevention, calcium intake and vitamin D advice. These treatments can change glucose, cholesterol and muscle strength.

Urgent symptoms

Call oncology urgently for sudden illness, persistent vomiting, inability to drink, jaundice, new breathlessness or severe rash. Call 112/999 for severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure, or new leg weakness with loss of bladder or bowel control.

Medicine and interaction review

Bring all prescription, non-prescription and herbal medicines. Check with the oncology pharmacist before starting or stopping anything.

Your diagnosis and treatment setting

Who this treatment is for

Newly diagnosed prostate cancer that has spread, remains hormone-sensitive and meets the NCCP high-risk definition, treated together with ADT.

Sources: nccp

Specialist tests

PSA and testosterone help assess cancer response and ADT suppression. High risk here requires at least two of Gleason score 8 or more, at least three bone lesions and measurable spread to an internal organ; your team explains which criteria apply.

Sources: nccp

Why ADT continues

Androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) lowers testosterone from the testes. This medicine adds another form of hormone-pathway blockade. Even when cancer becomes castration-resistant, continued testosterone suppression remains part of this treatment unless you have had surgical castration.

Treatment aims and choices

Deciding together

This treatment aims to slow cancer growth and help control symptoms. Your team explains the expected benefit, uncertainty and alternatives for your situation.

Sources: nccp

Review and alternatives

Continue your prescribed androgen deprivation therapy (ADT), unless you have had surgery to remove both testes. The team reviews symptoms, blood tests, PSA and scans according to your own plan; there is no universal scan interval. Discuss suitable alternatives, supportive care and the option of declining treatment.

Sources: nccp

Where radiotherapy fits

Radiotherapy may treat the prostate, a recurrence or a troublesome site such as a painful bone lesion. Its purpose depends on your disease setting. Ask the team whether it belongs in your plan; starting these tablets does not itself schedule or replace radiotherapy.

Sources: adt

What the evidence can tell you

Research supports a treatment discussion; it cannot predict one person’s outcome.

In the LATITUDE trial’s 2017 analysis of newly diagnosed high-risk metastatic hormone-sensitive cancer, 66% receiving abiraterone plus prednisone and ADT were alive at 3 years versus 49% receiving ADT and placebos (hazard ratio 0.62; 95% confidence interval 0.51–0.76). The difference is a trial average, not an individual forecast.
