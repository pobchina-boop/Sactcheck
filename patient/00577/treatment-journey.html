Tests in your plan

Baseline bloods and tests

WhenFull blood count, kidney and liver tests, glucose and blood pressure; ECG if clinically indicated or if you have a cardiac history. Review potassium, swelling, heart health and all medicines.

Checks organ safety and suitability before treatment.

Treatment safety monitoring

WhenBlood count, kidney and liver tests, glucose and blood pressure every 2 weeks for the first 3 treatment months, then every 4 weeks. Potassium may need closer checks if low; assess swelling and cardiac symptoms. ECG if clinically indicated or with cardiac history.

Finds changes that may need supportive care, a dose hold or adjustment.

Specialist and cancer tests

If part of your plan
WhenPSA and testosterone help assess cancer response and ADT suppression. High risk here requires at least two of Gleason score 8 or more, at least three bone lesions and measurable spread to an internal organ; your team explains which criteria apply.

Distinguishes treatment selection, hormone monitoring and cancer response.

The whole treatment journey

Abiraterone and prednisolone: metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer: treatment and checks

Newly diagnosed prostate cancer that has spread, remains hormone-sensitive and meets the NCCP high-risk definition, treated together with ADT.

Abiraterone 1000 mg once daily plus prednisolone 5 mg once daily, continuously while helping and tolerated. Continue your ADT.

What each check is for

1Treatment and reviewDaily tablets plus ongoing ADT

This treatment aims to slow cancer growth and help control symptoms. Your team explains the expected benefit, uncertainty and alternatives for your situation.

Ask / act: Blood count, kidney and liver tests, glucose and blood pressure every 2 weeks for the first 3 treatment months, then every 4 weeks. Potassium may need closer checks if low; assess swelling and cardiac symptoms. ECG if clinically indicated or with cardiac history.
