When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Dabrafenib

A BRAF V600 change can keep the cell-growth pathway switched on. Dabrafenib blocks the altered BRAF protein. It is paired with a MEK inhibitor to act on the same pathway at another point. The BRAF result comes from tumour testing; it does not automatically mean a hereditary condition.

Trametinib

MEK carries growth signals further along the pathway below BRAF. Trametinib blocks MEK alongside dabrafenib. Normal heart, eye, lung and other tissues can also be affected, which is why scheduled tests and prompt symptom reporting matter.

Support at home

Fever action plan

If temperature reaches 38°C or higher, withhold both dabrafenib and trametinib and contact your oncology team promptly for assessment. For recurrent episodes, the team may advise withholding at the first symptom. Restarting needs the team’s plan after at least 24 hours without symptoms; do not use this as an automatic self-restart rule.

An individual symptom plan

Ask which anti-sickness or other symptom medicines are prescribed, when to take them and when to call if they do not help. Do not start a borrowed medicine or alter treatment to manage a side effect yourself.

Your urgent contacts

Keep your actual oncology, out-of-hours and pharmacy contacts on the wallet card. Example numbers on a template must be replaced before you use it.

Pregnancy and fertility precautions

Discuss fertility and pregnancy precautions before starting. Use an effective alternative to hormonal contraception during treatment and for two weeks after dabrafenib and 16 weeks after trametinib; dabrafenib can make hormonal contraception less effective. The team must confirm the method and the later applicable end date for the combination. Do not breastfeed during treatment; ask when it is safe to resume. Report a possible pregnancy promptly.

Eating, activity and practical help

Report falling intake, weight loss, pain or fatigue early. The team can tailor nutrition, activity, wound, psychological and practical support to your needs.
