The whole treatment journey

Adjuvant dabrafenib and trametinib after removal of BRAF-mutated stage III melanoma: treatment and checks

NCCP 00415b: adjuvant treatment after complete resection of stage III melanoma with a BRAF V600 mutation. This route is distinct from treatment of unresectable or metastatic melanoma.

Take dabrafenib twice daily, around 12 hours apart, and trametinib once daily, every day unless the team instructs a hold. The adjuvant course is planned for 12 months, unless stopped earlier for recurrence, side effects or another clinical reason. A four-week review is not a drug holiday or one of 12 chemotherapy cycles.

What each check is for

1Adjuvant oral treatment12-month planned course

After eligible stage III melanoma has been completely removed, dabrafenib and trametinib are offered to reduce the chance of recurrence. The expected benefit, alternatives and treatment burden need an individual discussion; treatment cannot guarantee that melanoma will not return.
