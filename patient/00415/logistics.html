Dabrafenib and trametinib for advanced BRAF-mutated melanoma

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00415 · Draft source reconciliation; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking or receiving treatment

Swallow the prescribed capsules/tablet whole with water. Take each dose at least one hour before food or at least two hours after food. Take the once-daily trametinib dose at the same time each day, alongside either the morning or evening dabrafenib dose according to your written plan. Keep the prescribed storage instructions.

STEP 2

Missed, vomited or held treatment

The missed-dose windows differ: take missed dabrafenib only when more than six hours remain before the next dose; take missed trametinib only when more than 12 hours remain. Otherwise skip it. Do not double up. Do not take an extra trametinib dose after vomiting; contact the team or pharmacist about any vomited or uncertain dose.

STEP 3

Appointments and supply

Plan blood tests, travel and medicine collection in advance. If you need an interpreter or someone to help at home, tell the team before the appointment.

STEP 4

Paper copies and contact information

Ask for your latest schedule and contact details on paper. Replace old versions when treatment changes so everyone helping you uses the same plan.
