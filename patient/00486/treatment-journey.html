The whole treatment journey

FLOX: fluorouracil, folinic acid and oxaliplatin: treatment and checks

Adjuvant stage II or III colon cancer after resection

Fluorouracil and folinic acid on Days 1, 8, 15, 22, 29 and 36; oxaliplatin also on Days 1, 15 and 29. Two weeks off follow. Three 56-day cycles make a planned 24-week course.

What each check is for

1Confirm your personal planBefore starting

FLOX is given after surgery for selected stage II or III colon cancer to reduce the chance of recurrence. It combines weekly fluorouracil/folinic acid with oxaliplatin on alternate treatment weeks.

Ask / act: Agree medicines, treatment days, duration and monitoring.
