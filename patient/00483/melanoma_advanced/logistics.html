Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking or receiving treatment

Nivolumab is given as 240 mg by intravenous (IV) infusion over 30 minutes OR 600 mg by subcutaneous injection over 3–5 minutes, on Day 1 every 2 weeks. Allow extra time for assessment, preparation and observation. The team selects one formulation. A subcutaneous injection is given into the thigh or abdomen, alternating sites and avoiding damaged skin, scars or moles. IV and subcutaneous doses are not interchangeable milligram for milligram.

STEP 2

Missed, vomited or held treatment

If a visit is missed or treatment is held, contact the unit for a new plan. Do not count a missed appointment as a dose received. An interval change, operation or new phase does not erase earlier immune toxicity or a permanent-stop decision.

STEP 3

Appointments and supply

Plan blood tests, travel and medicine collection in advance. If you need an interpreter or someone to help at home, tell the team before the appointment.

STEP 4

Paper copies and contact information

Ask for your latest schedule and contact details on paper. Replace old versions when treatment changes so everyone helping you uses the same plan.
