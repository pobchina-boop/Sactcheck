Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking or receiving treatment

Nivolumab is given as 240 mg by IV infusion over 30 minutes OR 600 mg by subcutaneous injection over 3–5 minutes, on Day 1 every 2 weeks. Allow extra time for assessment, preparation and observation. The team selects one formulation. A subcutaneous injection is given into the thigh or abdomen, alternating sites and avoiding damaged skin, scars or moles. IV and subcutaneous doses are not interchangeable milligram for milligram.

STEP 2

Missed, vomited or held treatment

If a visit is missed or treatment is held, contact the unit for a new plan. Do not count a missed appointment as a dose received. An interval change, operation or new phase does not erase earlier immune toxicity or a permanent-stop decision.

STEP 3

Appointments and supply

Arrange dates, medicine supply, transport and any needed interpreter or carer support. Ask whether bloods are taken in the oncology unit or elsewhere and how the result reaches the prescriber.

STEP 4

Paper copies and contact information

Keep this complete handbook, the three-page summary and the wallet card together. The QR code is optional. Ask the team to add your actual contacts and print a revised plan when treatment changes.
