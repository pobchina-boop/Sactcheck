Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

EMA/EP for gestational trophoblastic neoplasia

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Treat the trophoblastic tumour with the aim of cure. Serial blood hCG measures how the disease responds. For high-risk GTN resistant to or recurring after EMA/CO, or GTN with liver metastases at presentation.

Treatment schedule. EMA on day 1, folinic acid on days 2 and 3, then EP with fluids on day 8.

Regimen-specific side effects

Dactinomycin

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Low counts, mouth sores and IV leakagePhone urgently for fever or bleeding. Report painful ulcers. Tell the nurse immediately if the line burns or stings.

Etoposide

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Low counts and infusion reactionPhone for fever or bleeding. Report flushing or faintness during infusion. Hair loss and a small later leukaemia risk are possible.

Methotrexate

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Mouth, blood counts, liver and kidneysReport mouth ulcers, fever or poor fluid intake. Check new medicines and take folinic acid at the written times.

Folinic acid

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Rescue and allergyPhone immediately if a tablet is missed, late or vomited. Seek emergency help for swelling or wheeze.

Cisplatin

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Kidneys, hearing, nerves and sicknessReport reduced urine, persistent vomiting, tinnitus, hearing loss or numbness. Attend for fluids and electrolyte checks.

Treatment discussion and decision

EMA/EP for gestational trophoblastic neoplasia

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

A specialist series supports platinum-containing salvage treatment after EMA/CO failure. Non-randomised series with differing prior treatments. Follow the Irish sequence for administration.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Call promptly for reduced urine, ringing in the ears, new numbness, or missed rescue tablets. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
