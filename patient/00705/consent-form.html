Pembrolizumab + carboplatin + fluorouracil

First-line treatment of metastatic or unresectable recurrent head and neck squamous cell carcinoma in adults with tumour PD-L1 CPS ≥1. · NCCP 00705 · version 2d · 21-day cycle

Treatment context
Pembrolizumab and chemotherapy aim to control head and neck cancer that has spread or returned and cannot be removed. Your team explains the expected benefit, uncertainty and alternatives for you.
Schedule
Every 21 days: pembrolizumab and carboplatin on Day 1; fluorouracil over Days 1–4. Up to 6 combination cycles, then pembrolizumab alone while appropriate. Every dose depends on safety and benefit review.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Pembrolizumab

PD-1 checkpoint immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of bowel, lungs, liver or kidneys
Thyroid, pituitary, adrenal or glucose problems
Severe skin reactions
Rare heart, muscle, nerve and eye inflammation
Infusion reactions
Some immune effects need long-term treatment

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts and serious infection
Low platelets and bleeding
Anaemia and fatigue
Nausea and vomiting
Infusion or allergic reactions
Kidney, nerve or hearing effects

Fluorouracil

Antimetabolite chemotherapy
Low blood counts and serious infection
Mouth and throat soreness
Diarrhoea and dehydration
Painful palms or soles
Heart problems including chest pain
Rare severe neurological or early excessive toxicity

Discuss the individual aim, alternatives and uncertainty; serious or lasting harms; treatment burden; fertility and pregnancy precautions; and the patient’s priorities. Explain low blood counts/infection, bleeding, nausea, mucositis, diarrhoea, fluorouracil cardiac and early severe toxicity, platinum kidney/hearing/nerve effects, DPD testing limits, pump/line complications and immune organ inflammation including permanent hormone replacement. Separate the up-to-six-cycle combination phase from ongoing pembrolizumab maintenance. Confirm actual urgent contacts and teach-back; this draft does not authorise treatment.
