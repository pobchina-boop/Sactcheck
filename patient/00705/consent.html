1. Treatment and purpose

Pembrolizumab + carboplatin + fluorouracil · NCCP 00705 v2d

Pembrolizumab 200 mg is infused over 30 minutes, followed by carboplatin AUC 5 over 30 minutes on Day 1. Fluorouracil is prescribed as 1,000 mg/m² each day by 22-hour infusion on Days 1–4, or 2,000 mg/m² over 48 hours twice. Staff confirm pump changes and disconnection from the actual connection time. The planned combination limit is six cycles; toxicity may change the plan earlier. Pembrolizumab 200 mg over 30 minutes every 21 days continues after the combination phase when appropriate, until progression or unacceptable toxicity under this regimen. A six-cycle chemotherapy record is not a six-cycle total course; the protocol does not impose an automatic 24-month stop.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Pembrolizumab · PD-1 checkpoint immunotherapy

Report new diarrhoea, cough, breathlessness, rash, headache, unusual weakness, thirst or changes in urine promptly.

Carboplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report fever, unusual bruising or bleeding, marked tiredness or an infusion reaction.

Fluorouracil · Antimetabolite chemotherapy

Report severe diarrhoea, mouth sores, chest pain, sudden confusion, sore palms or soles, or unexpectedly early severe symptoms.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the individual aim, alternatives and uncertainty; serious or lasting harms; treatment burden; fertility and pregnancy precautions; and the patient’s priorities. Explain low blood counts/infection, bleeding, nausea, mucositis, diarrhoea, fluorouracil cardiac and early severe toxicity, platinum kidney/hearing/nerve effects, dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (a medicine-processing enzyme) (DPD) testing limits, pump/line complications and immune organ inflammation including permanent hormone replacement. Separate the up-to-six-cycle combination phase from ongoing pembrolizumab maintenance. Confirm actual urgent contacts and teach-back; this draft does not authorise treatment.
