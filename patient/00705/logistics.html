Pembrolizumab + carboplatin + fluorouracil

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00705 · Source reconciled draft; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the first visit

Confirm the exact regimen, treatment aim, baseline tests, transport, support medicines and actual urgent contact numbers. Bring any hearing concerns, swallowing problems and medicine list.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Pembrolizumab 200 mg is infused over 30 minutes, followed by carboplatin AUC 5 over 30 minutes on Day 1. Fluorouracil is prescribed as 1,000 mg/m² each day by 22-hour infusion on Days 1–4, or 2,000 mg/m² over 48 hours twice. Staff confirm pump changes and disconnection from the actual connection time.

STEP 3

Pump visits and line care

Record every pump change and disconnection appointment. Device problems need the unit’s instructions; do not reconnect, adjust or remove the line yourself.

STEP 4

Practical help

Tell the team about travel, cost, work, caring responsibilities, communication or reading needs. Ask whether a support person or interpreter can attend and how treatment changes will be explained.

STEP 5

Leaving the unit

Check the next visit and blood-test dates, support medicines and who to call if unwell. Have the team replace example phone numbers before using the wallet card.
