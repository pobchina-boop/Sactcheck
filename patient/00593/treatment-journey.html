The whole treatment journey

Atezolizumab 1680 mg · every 4 weeks · lung cancer: treatment and checks

Lung settings: previously treated non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC); first-line PD-L1-high metastatic NSCLC; adjuvant NSCLC after surgery and platinum chemotherapy; extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer (ES-SCLC) maintenance after induction.

Atezolizumab 1680 mg is given by intravenous infusion on Day 1 every 4 weeks (28 days). The first infusion lasts about 60 minutes; later infusions may last 30 minutes if tolerated. The visit includes checks and preparation. Adjuvant: maximum 12 months. Advanced NSCLC and ES-SCLC maintenance: until progression or unacceptable toxicity. ES-SCLC requires completed induction; this suite contains no induction chemotherapy.
