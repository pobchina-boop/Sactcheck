Additional phase-specific support

  • Carboplatin AUC 1.5 intravenous (IV) every 7 days for 7 cycles, only while radiotherapy is being delivered. Discontinue carboplatin if radiotherapy is truncated.
  • The treatment paragraph describes a start 3–8 weeks (Days 22–56) after the START of cycle 3 of induction; the indication paragraph says 3–8 weeks after COMPLETION of induction. The multidisciplinary team (MDT) must resolve the reference point and set the actual radiotherapy and chemotherapy dates. Confirm the induction route and adequate recovery.
  • National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) gives no routine granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF) or additional supportive-care prescription for this weekly phase. Do not carry over induction G-CSF, docetaxel premedication or cisplatin hydration automatically.
  • Arrange dental/oral assessment, mouth care, dietetic and swallowing support according to need. Review weight, oral intake, pain and hydration throughout chemotherapy and radiotherapy; no automatic feeding-tube decision is generated.
  • Preserve the CUH high/moderate and docetaxel proforma adjuncts where applicable: omeprazole, local nystatin/chlorhexidine mouth care, breakthrough antiemetics and loperamide. These are historical local reference items requiring current-policy and patient-specific review, not universal prophylaxis or an additional duplicate prescription.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
