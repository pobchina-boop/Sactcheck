Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenAt baseline and before every weekly carboplatin cycle.

Checks infection-fighting white cells, red cells and platelets; low counts may require a delay or another change.

Kidney function and filtration assessment

WhenBefore starting and before each weekly cycle. The team determines the required recency of creatinine results for the calculation method.

Carboplatin dosing depends on kidney function. Measured GFR is preferred where feasible, with an appropriate estimate used when needed.

Liver function

WhenAt baseline and before every weekly cycle.

Supports safe treatment review and investigation of changes.

Hearing and nerve review

If part of your plan
WhenReview symptoms before dosing; hearing tests or further examination when indicated.

Identifies new problems and effects remaining from earlier platinum chemotherapy.

Dental, nutrition and swallowing assessment

WhenPrepare before head and neck radiation; reassess as symptoms or intake change.

Helps plan safe intake, mouth care and prevention of avoidable radiation-related complications.

Imaging and cancer assessment

If part of your plan
WhenExaminations and scans on the individual oncology and radiation plan.

Assesses the cancer and informs further treatment.

The whole treatment journey

Carboplatin AUC 1.5 · weekly chemoradiation after induction: treatment and checks

Concurrent carboplatin and radiotherapy after induction for stage III/intravenous (IV) non-metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma, subject to response and recovery.

Carboplatin AUC 1.5 IV every 7 days for 7 cycles, only while radiotherapy is being delivered. Discontinue carboplatin if radiotherapy is truncated.
