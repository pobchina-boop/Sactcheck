Preparing for treatment visits

Docetaxel every three weeks for recurrent ovarian, tubal or peritoneal cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

One docetaxel infusion over one hour on day 1 of a 21-day cycle. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain manageable.

What to bring

Bring the appointment calendar, medicines list, oncology contact card and any record of new symptoms.

Before the infusion

Take the prescribed steroid starting the day before docetaxel. Ring before attending if you missed a dose. Steroids help prevent allergy and fluid retention.

Write down your next appointments

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology number immediately for fever, chills, bleeding or feeling suddenly unwell. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, breathlessness, dizziness, throat tightness or pain at the infusion site. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
