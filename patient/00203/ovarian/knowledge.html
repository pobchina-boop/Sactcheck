Your complete treatment reference

Docetaxel every three weeks for recurrent ovarian, tubal or peritoneal cancer

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Docetaxel can slow the growth of epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer that has returned or is progressing. Your team reviews symptoms and scans with you to decide whether to continue.

Docetaxel · Docetaxel

Taxane chemotherapy. Docetaxel stabilises tiny structures called microtubules. Cells need these to separate their chromosomes during division; disrupting the process can stop cancer cells multiplying. Healthy dividing cells are also affected. Take prescribed steroid premedication. Report fever, lasting numbness, swelling, sore nails or infusion symptoms.

Common effects and what to do

Hair and energy

Hair loss and tiredness are common. Plan rest around visits and tell the team if fatigue interferes with daily activities.

Nerves and sensation

Report tingling, burning or numbness before the next dose. Trouble buttoning clothes or walking can mean the dose needs review.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology number immediately for fever, chills, bleeding or feeling suddenly unwell. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, breathlessness, dizziness, throat tightness or pain at the infusion site. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Before treatment

Take the prescribed steroid starting the day before docetaxel. Ring before attending if you missed a dose. Steroids help prevent allergy and fluid retention.

Between visits

Call the 24-hour oncology number immediately for fever, chills, bleeding or feeling suddenly unwell. Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, breathlessness, dizziness, throat tightness or pain at the infusion site.

Planning your visits

Appointments

One docetaxel infusion over one hour on day 1 of a 21-day cycle. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain manageable.

What to bring

What the research found

Some participants with previously treated ovarian cancer had tumour shrinkage with docetaxel.

This was a small, single-arm study. It cannot show a survival advantage or establish the best dose or schedule for you.
