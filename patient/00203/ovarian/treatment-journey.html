Your treatment journey

Docetaxel every three weeks for recurrent ovarian, tubal or peritoneal cancer

Docetaxel can slow the growth of epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer that has returned or is progressing. Your team reviews symptoms and scans with you to decide whether to continue.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · Docetaxel

One docetaxel infusion today. Confirm the date of your next cycle.

At review visits

  • What do I need to check before my next visit? Take the prescribed steroid starting the day before docetaxel. Ring before attending if you missed a dose. Steroids help prevent allergy and fluid retention.
  • When is my next treatment due? One docetaxel infusion over one hour on day 1 of a 21-day cycle. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain manageable.
