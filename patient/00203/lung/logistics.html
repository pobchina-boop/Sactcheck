Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² is infused on Day 1 every 21 days. For this NSCLC indication, the NCCP plan has a maximum of six cycles; treatment stops earlier for progression or unacceptable side effects. Your team confirms your prescribed dose and course.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Check the oral steroid schedule, blood-test appointment and transport. Contact the unit before attending if premedication was missed or you are unwell.

STEP 2

At the unit

Docetaxel is given through a cannula or suitable existing IV line over about one hour. The appointment also includes checks, preparation, premedication and observation and is longer than the infusion itself. This regimen has no routine take-home pump.

STEP 3

At home

Keep the contact number, medicine list and symptom record available. Ask who will arrange your next blood test, scan and review. Sedating medicines may affect driving.
