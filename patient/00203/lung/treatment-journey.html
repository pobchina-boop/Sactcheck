Docetaxel 75 mg/m² · previously treated NSCLC: treatment and checks

Lung · previously treated advanced NSCLC

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² is infused on Day 1 every 21 days. For this NSCLC indication, the NCCP plan has a maximum of six cycles; treatment stops earlier for progression or unacceptable side effects. Your team confirms your prescribed dose and course.

What each check is for

1Safety reviewBefore each 21-day cycle

FBC, kidney and liver profile at baseline and before every 21-day cycle; review symptoms, neuropathy, fluid retention and the steroid plan. Confirm remaining cycles against the six-cycle NSCLC maximum.

Ask / act: The team reviews results, current symptoms and the prescription.

Sources: NCCP 00203 · SMPC

2Benefit and treatment choiceAt consultant-agreed response reviews

A historical randomised trial found that docetaxel 75 mg/m² every three weeks prolonged survival compared with supportive care alone in selected people whose NSCLC had progressed after platinum chemotherapy. Modern previous treatment and available alternatives differ, so the trial cannot predict an individual outcome.

Ask / act: Discuss continuing, changing or stopping treatment according to benefit, toxicity and priorities.

Sources: NCCP 00203 · TRIAL
