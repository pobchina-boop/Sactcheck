When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Call the oncology contact number immediately for fever, shivers or sudden illness, severe or new breathing difficulty, chest pain, fainting, severe abdominal pain or persistent diarrhoea. Tell staff immediately about infusion symptoms. Report new numbness, weakness, painful skin changes, swelling or visual changes promptly; do not wait for the next cycle.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

How the medicines work

Treatment aims to slow or shrink lung cancer and relieve symptoms after previous chemotherapy. Expected benefit, side effects, other options and your priorities guide the decision.

Taxane chemotherapy

Docetaxel

Disrupts the cell’s division machinery

Docetaxel stabilises tiny structures called microtubules. Cells need these to separate their chromosomes during division; disrupting the process can stop cancer cells multiplying. Healthy dividing cells are also affected.

Microtubules stabilised↓Division disrupted↓Cell growth affected

What to watch for
Take prescribed steroid premedication. Report fever, lasting numbness, swelling, sore nails or infusion symptoms.

Read the whole cycle as text

Day 1–21: Your current reference cycle

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² by IV infusion on Day 1 every 21 days. Duration depends on the cancer and treatment aim: NSCLC has a source maximum of six cycles; other indications require an explicitly agreed course, including prostate treatment. This differs from the 100 mg/m² card. Follow the team’s appointment and medicine instructions; contact them about missed doses or new symptoms.

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² is infused on Day 1 every 21 days. For this NSCLC indication, the NCCP plan has a maximum of six cycles; treatment stops earlier for progression or unacceptable side effects. Your team confirms your prescribed dose and course.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Check the oral steroid schedule, blood-test appointment and transport. Contact the unit before attending if premedication was missed or you are unwell.

STEP 2

At the unit

Docetaxel is given through a cannula or suitable existing IV line over about one hour. The appointment also includes checks, preparation, premedication and observation and is longer than the infusion itself. This regimen has no routine take-home pump.

STEP 3

At home

Keep the contact number, medicine list and symptom record available. Ask who will arrange your next blood test, scan and review. Sedating medicines may affect driving.

Make the plan practical

Support around the medicines

Premedication

Take dexamethasone exactly as prescribed before and after docetaxel. Tell the unit about missed doses before infusion; any alternative needs the prescriber’s decision. Do not double a dose or add another steroid course yourself.

Nausea, bowel symptoms and pain

Use your individual anti-sickness and pain plan. Contact the team if symptoms are uncontrolled or you cannot drink. Antihistamines and other support medicines may cause drowsiness; arrange transport if advised.

Nerves and daily activities

Report tingling, numbness or weakness early, including effects on buttons, writing, walking or balance. Protect hands and feet and take care with hot surfaces if sensation changes.

Infection and blood counts

Each treatment needs its own blood and symptom review. A growth-factor injection may be prescribed after individual risk assessment; follow its separate instructions. Fever or sudden illness needs immediate contact on any day.
