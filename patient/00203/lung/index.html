When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

My treatment

My overall treatment

Docetaxel by drip on Day 1, repeated every 21 days, for up to six cycles. Treatment may stop earlier if it is not helping or side effects are unacceptable. Your team confirms the prescribed course.

  1. Before startingConfirm your blood tests, current medicines and steroid plan.
  2. Treatment every 21 daysDay 1 drip, then time at home. Up to six cycles, with a review before each.
  3. After treatmentReview benefit, recovery and the next care plan with your team.

More about my medicines

Docetaxel

Docetaxel stabilises tiny structures called microtubules. Cells need these to separate their chromosomes during division; disrupting the process can stop cancer cells multiplying. Healthy dividing cells are also affected.

Getting ready for treatment visits

Consent discussion aid

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: Treatment day

Your blood results, symptoms and steroid preparation are checked before docetaxel. The infusion takes about one hour; the full visit is longer.

What to do: Tell the nurse immediately about discomfort at the drip, flushing, breathing symptoms or feeling faint.

Day 2: Day after treatment

Finish the prescribed three-day steroid course and use anti-sickness medicine as directed.

What to do: Ask the team if a steroid dose was missed; do not invent a replacement dose.

Day 3–20: Between visits

Watch for infection, mouth soreness, fatigue, bowel changes, numbness or swelling. Symptoms and recovery vary; no day is guaranteed to be risk-free.

What to do: Contact the team promptly about worrying symptoms, even if your temperature is normal.

Day 21: Prepare for the next cycle

The team arranges blood tests and reviews recovery and benefit before another dose. Your next treatment day depends on this review.

What to do: Check the next appointment and the steroid start date. Do not use a passport stamp as permission for another dose.

Support at home

Premedication

Take dexamethasone exactly as prescribed before and after docetaxel. Tell the unit about missed doses before infusion; any alternative needs the prescriber’s decision. Do not double a dose or add another steroid course yourself.

Nausea, bowel symptoms and pain

Use your individual anti-sickness and pain plan. Contact the team if symptoms are uncontrolled or you cannot drink. Antihistamines and other support medicines may cause drowsiness; arrange transport if advised.

Nerves and daily activities

Report tingling, numbness or weakness early, including effects on buttons, writing, walking or balance. Protect hands and feet and take care with hot surfaces if sensation changes.

Infection and blood counts

Each treatment needs its own blood and symptom review. A growth-factor injection may be prescribed after individual risk assessment; follow its separate instructions. Fever or sudden illness needs immediate contact on any day.
