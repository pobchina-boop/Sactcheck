The whole treatment journey

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² · every 3 weeks: treatment and checks

Source-defined breast, lung, gastric, gynaecological and castration-sensitive prostate cancer (including high-risk nonmetastatic prostate disease)

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² by IV infusion on Day 1 every 21 days. For the source breast indication, continue until progression or unacceptable toxicity; the team reviews benefit and tolerability. The six-cycle source maximum applies to NSCLC.

2Benefit and continuationAt agreed clinical review points

Docetaxel may slow or shrink advanced breast cancer and help symptoms. The dose is selected to balance benefit and side effects; results from a different dose or another cancer do not predict your outcome.

Ask / act: Discuss benefit, toxicity and priorities before continuing or changing treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00203 · RETROSPECTIVE-TRIAL
