Docetaxel for hormone-sensitive prostate cancer

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00203 · Source-reconciled feasibility draft; independent clinician, pharmacy and patient review pending.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests; review performance status, current medicines, infection, neuropathy and the treatment goal.

STEP 2

Your treatment visit

Docetaxel 75 mg/m² is infused over one hour on Day 1 every 21 days. The team writes the planned course: NCCP 00203 does not set a six-cycle prostate cap, while CHAARTED used six cycles. Continuous prednisolone is not specified in this source. Dexamethasone 8 mg twice daily for three days, starting the day before docetaxel; take at least three doses before the infusion. Tell the team if any dose is missed.

STEP 3

intravenous (IV) access

Treatment is given through a cannula or suitable established access. No take-home pump is part of this regimen. Report pain, burning, leakage or swelling immediately.

STEP 4

Between visits

Confirm whether your cancer is metastatic or nonmetastatic, the intended number of cycles, continued ADT and any separately prescribed androgen-pathway medicine or radiotherapy. Do not copy the continuous prednisolone plan from 00546.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next blood-test and treatment dates, support medicines and verified daytime/out-of-hours numbers.
