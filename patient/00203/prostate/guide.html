Treatment timing in this window

Treatment visitDay 1
  1. TreatmentDocetaxel 75 mg/m² is infused over one hour on Day 1 every 21 days. The team writes the planned course: NCCP 00203 does not set a six-cycle prostate cap, while CHAARTED used six cycles. Continuous prednisolone is not specified in this source.
  2. Hormone backboneContinue prescribed ADT unless surgically castrated; additional treatments need their own plan.

Between treatments

  • Side effects may occur on any day. Report problems promptly.
  • Confirm whether your cancer is metastatic or nonmetastatic, the intended number of cycles, continued ADT and any separately prescribed androgen-pathway medicine or radiotherapy. Do not copy the continuous prednisolone plan from 00546.

Dexamethasone 8 mg twice daily for three days, starting the day before docetaxel; take at least three doses before the infusion. Tell the team if any dose is missed.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
