Schedule
Docetaxel 75 mg/m² by IV infusion on Day 1 every 21 days. Duration depends on the cancer and treatment aim: NSCLC has a source maximum of six cycles; other indications require an explicitly agreed course, including prostate treatment. This differs from the 100 mg/m² card.
