FOLFIRINOX for metastatic pancreatic cancer

My treatment record

The aim is to control cancer that has spread and help you live longer. Your team will review symptoms and scans with you.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Day 1Day 1Oxaliplatin is followed by folinic acid and irinotecan. A short fluorouracil injection is given before the 46-hour pump.

My questions and next steps

  • Which treatment setting applies to me? The aim is to control cancer that has spread and help you live longer. Your team will review symptoms and scans with you.
  • What needs urgent attention at home? Keep the pump line dry and unkinked. Phone for leaks, disconnection, swelling or a pump that empties early or late. Leave adjustments to your trained team.
