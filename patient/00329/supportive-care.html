Your supportive care plan

FOLFIRINOX for metastatic pancreatic cancer

Practical support

At home

Keep the pump line dry and unkinked. Phone for leaks, disconnection, swelling or a pump that empties early or late. Leave adjustments to your trained team.

Pancreatic and biliary symptoms

Ask the team about a new fever with jaundice, dark urine or shivering, especially if you have a bile duct stent.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
