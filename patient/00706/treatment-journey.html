The whole treatment journey

Pembrolizumab + cisplatin + fluorouracil: treatment and checks

First-line treatment of metastatic or unresectable recurrent head and neck squamous cell carcinoma in adults with tumour PD-L1 CPS ≥1.

Every 21 days: pembrolizumab and cisplatin on Day 1; fluorouracil over Days 1–4. Up to 6 combination cycles, then pembrolizumab alone while appropriate. Every dose depends on safety and benefit review.
