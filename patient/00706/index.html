SACTCheck · National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00706 · My treatment at a glance

Pembrolizumab + cisplatin + fluorouracil

Pembrolizumab and chemotherapy aim to control head and neck cancer that has spread or returned and cannot be removed. Your team explains the expected benefit, uncertainty and alternatives for you.

First-line treatment of metastatic or unresectable recurrent head and neck squamous cell carcinoma in adults with tumour PD-L1 CPS ≥1. · NCCP 00706 v3 · Document revised 7 November 2026

Between treatments

  • Pump changes/disconnection follow the prescribed option. Call early for diarrhoea, mouth pain or chest symptoms.
  • Immune symptoms can arise during maintenance or after stopping.

Bring your medicine list, test dates, treatment calendar and actual urgent contacts.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Selected effects, not every possible effect or a prediction. Blood-count, skin and line markers are schematic. Number and text carry meaning; colour is decorative.

Call the oncology team promptly for fever, sudden illness, diarrhoea, painful mouth, reduced drinking, new breathlessness or immune symptoms. Chest pain during or after fluorouracil needs urgent assessment. Severe chest pain, breathing difficulty, collapse or a seizure is an emergency. Early severe fluorouracil symptoms can occur despite DPD testing.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
