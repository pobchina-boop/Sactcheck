The whole treatment journey

Relugolix: daily hormone treatment: treatment and checks

Adults with advanced hormone-sensitive prostate cancer, following specialist assessment.

Relugolix 360 mg on the first day, then 120 mg once daily without planned breaks. Your specialist sets the duration and any prescribed changes.

What each check is for

1Treatment and reviewLoading dose, then once daily

This is androgen deprivation therapy (ADT): it lowers testosterone to help control prostate cancer. With radiotherapy, the aim may be cure; in advanced disease, it is part of ongoing cancer control.

Ask / act: Repeat blood count, kidney and liver tests and blood glucose as arranged. Bone blood tests and ECG are repeated when clinically indicated. PSA, testosterone and scans follow the individual cancer plan.
