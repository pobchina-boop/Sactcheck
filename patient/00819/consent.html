Consent discussion aid

Dostarlimab (Jemperli) alone

The aim is to help your immune system recognise and control the cancer.

Treatment discussion

This immunotherapy is for advanced or recurrent womb cancer that progressed during or after platinum treatment and has a tumour test showing mismatch repair deficiency or high microsatellite instability.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
First four dosesFirst four dosesOne infusion every three weeks for four doses.
From dose 5From dose 5Dose 5 is three weeks after dose 4. After dose 5 the interval is six weeks, while treatment remains suitable.

Possible benefit, alternatives and your priorities

In the GARNET trial, measurable tumour shrinkage was seen in about 46 in 100 evaluable people in the mismatch repair deficient or MSI-high endometrial cancer group.

This was a single-arm trial. A response rate does not tell us how long someone will live or how the medicine compares directly with another treatment.

Discuss the expected benefit, side effects, fertility, alternatives and symptom-directed care with the treating clinician. You may ask for more time or change your decision.

Important risks we discussed

  • Lungs · Dostarlimab — Call urgently for new cough or breathlessness.
  • Hormone glands · Dostarlimab — Report severe tiredness, headache, unusual thirst or dizziness.
  • Heart or muscles · Dostarlimab — New weakness needs urgent advice. Severe chest pain or collapse needs 112 or 999.
  • Bowel · Dostarlimab — Call urgently for new diarrhoea, pain or blood in stools.
  • Liver · Dostarlimab — Call for yellow eyes or dark urine. Blood tests can also detect inflammation.
  • Skin · Dostarlimab — Blistering, peeling skin or sores around your mouth or eyes need urgent assessment.
  • Kidneys · Dostarlimab — Contact the team for reduced urine or new swelling. Blood tests may show changes before symptoms start.
