Dostarlimab (Jemperli) alone

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

The aim is to help your immune system recognise and control the cancer. This immunotherapy is for advanced or recurrent womb cancer that progressed during or after platinum treatment and has a tumour test showing mismatch repair deficiency or high microsatellite instability.

Treatment schedule. One infusion every three weeks for four doses. Dose 5 is three weeks after dose 4. After dose 5 the interval is six weeks, while treatment remains suitable.

Treatment discussion and decision

Dostarlimab (Jemperli) alone

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

In the GARNET trial, measurable tumour shrinkage was seen in about 46 in 100 evaluable people in the mismatch repair deficient or MSI-high endometrial cancer group. This was a single-arm trial. A response rate does not tell us how long someone will live or how the medicine compares directly with another treatment.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call urgently for new cough, breathlessness, diarrhoea, severe weakness or a blistering rash. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
