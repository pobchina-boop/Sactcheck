1. Treatment and purpose

CAP: advanced salivary gland cancer · NCCP 00615 v2

Doxorubicin 50 mg/m², cisplatin 50 mg/m² and cyclophosphamide 500 mg/m² on Day 1, repeated every 21 days for up to 6 cycles, stopping earlier for progression or unacceptable side effects.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Cyclophosphamide · Cytotoxic chemotherapy

Follow the unit’s cisplatin hydration plan and individual fluid target. The source advises 2–3 litres on Day 1 to reduce bladder irritation from cyclophosphamide; the team must adapt this if heart or kidney problems restrict fluids. Report blood in urine or painful urination promptly.
