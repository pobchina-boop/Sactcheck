Your treatment journey

Mirvetuximab soravtansine for FRα-positive ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer

The treatment aims to shrink or slow the cancer and help with symptoms. A test on the tumour shows whether enough cells have the target for this medicine.

Your infusion schedule

First infusion · cycle 1 day 1

After blood and eye checks, receive four premedicines. The nurse starts the drip slowly and monitors for reactions.

Later infusions · day 1 every 21 days

Tell the team about eye or nerve symptoms before each dose. Premedicines continue. The infusion can run at the highest previously tolerated rate.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat your team checksWhat you can do
Before startingTumour receptor test, blood count, kidney and liver tests, pregnancy test where relevant, and an eye appointment for vision and a slit-lamp examination. The team assesses nerve symptoms as indicated.Take your glasses, eye-drop list and contact-lens details. Explain any eye disease, cough or numbness.
Before each infusionBlood counts and kidney and liver tests. Review vision, breathing and sensation. New or worsening eye symptoms need an eye exam and review of the report before dosing.Call promptly about blurred vision, pain, dryness or light sensitivity. The team may delay a dose to protect your eyes.
If eye injury developsAfter grade 2 or worse eye toxicity, the eye team repeats exams at least every other cycle and as needed until recovery to baseline or resolution.Use the agreed eye-drop calendar. Avoid contact lenses. Avoid driving if vision, sensation, alertness or balance is affected.

The team individualises response assessments and extra tests according to symptoms and treatment aim.

At review visits

  • Which drops will I need? Use the lubricating drops as advised. An eye specialist may prescribe steroid drops for subsequent cycles if the cornea shows grade 2 or worse injury. Use them only as prescribed, from infusion day through the next seven days, then wait at least 15 minutes after a steroid drop before a lubricant.
  • Can I wear my contact lenses or drive home? Avoid contact lenses during treatment. Arrange a lift if vision changes, dizziness, numbness or tiredness affect safe driving. You can resume when symptoms have fully resolved and the team confirms it is safe.
