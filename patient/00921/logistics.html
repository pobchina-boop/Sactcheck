Preparing for treatment visits

Mirvetuximab soravtansine for FRα-positive ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

One infusion on day 1 of each 21-day cycle. The team reviews benefit and side effects each cycle. Treatment continues until the cancer progresses or side effects become unacceptable.

On the day

You receive medicines to reduce sickness and infusion reactions before the drip. The first infusion starts slowly and may speed up in steps while the nurse checks you. The dose is based on height and weight. Subsequent infusions may use the rate you previously tolerated.

Between visits

Continue your prescribed lubricating drops. Avoid contact lenses and call about new eye symptoms before your next infusion.

Write down your next appointments

  • Take your glasses, eye-drop list and contact-lens details. Explain any eye disease, cough or numbness.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Contact oncology promptly for new eye pain, light sensitivity or blurred vision, new cough, breathlessness, fever or shaking chills. Sudden visual loss or severe breathing difficulty needs emergency assessment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
