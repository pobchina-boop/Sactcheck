Rucaparib (Rubraca) maintenance tablets

Chemotherapy has reduced the cancer. These tablets aim to keep it under control for longer. Your team will explain how your response and tumour test results inform your options.

First-line maintenance after a platinum response · NCCP 00904 v2 · Source checked 2026-11-27

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

My overall treatment

Take the prescribed dose twice daily, about 12 hours apart, every day. There is no planned week off.

  1. After this part of treatmentStart within eight weeks of the final platinum dose. Treatment lasts up to two years and may stop earlier if the cancer grows or side effects make continued treatment unsuitable.

Between treatments

  • Call the oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills, unexpected bleeding, worsening breathlessness or vomiting that prevents drinking. Report unusual bruising or persistent fatigue promptly.

Swallow the prescribed tablets whole with a large glass of water, with or without food. Leave a missed or vomited dose out and take only your next scheduled dose. Never crush the tablets or take extra to catch up. Confirm your last platinum date and ask your pharmacist to check prescribed medicines, painkillers, supplements and herbal products.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.
