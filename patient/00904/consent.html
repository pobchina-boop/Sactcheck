Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Rucaparib (Rubraca) maintenance tablets

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Chemotherapy has reduced the cancer. These tablets aim to keep it under control for longer. Your team will explain how your response and tumour test results inform your options. First-line maintenance for adults whose stage III or IV high-grade epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer has responded completely or partially to platinum chemotherapy.

Treatment schedule. Take the prescribed dose twice daily, about 12 hours apart, every day. There is no planned week off. Start within eight weeks of the final platinum dose. Treatment lasts up to two years and may stop earlier if the cancer grows or side effects make continued treatment unsuitable.

Treatment discussion and decision

Rucaparib (Rubraca) maintenance tablets

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

In the ATHENA-MONO trial, the time before the cancer grew or a participant died was about 20 months with rucaparib and 9 months with placebo at the middle of each group. Some people had shorter or longer periods. The study has not established whether people live longer overall. Ask how your tumour results and the side effects you wish to avoid affect your choice.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call the oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills, unexpected bleeding, worsening breathlessness or vomiting that prevents drinking. Report unusual bruising or persistent fatigue promptly. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
