Call about persistent fatigue, breathlessness, infections or unusual bruising. Regular blood tests can detect problems before you feel unwell.

At home

Use the morning and evening tablet plan. Ask about any new medicine, including over-the-counter medicines, before starting it. Protect exposed skin from sunlight.

Your anti-sickness plan

Rucaparib can cause nausea. Your team may prescribe a daily anti-sickness tablet and separate rescue treatment. Ask which one to take and when.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Use effective contraception during treatment and for six months after the last dose if you could become pregnant. Avoid breastfeeding during treatment and for two weeks afterwards.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills, unexpected bleeding, worsening breathlessness or vomiting that prevents drinking. Report unusual bruising or persistent fatigue promptly. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
