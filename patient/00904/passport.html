My questions and next steps

  • What if I am sick after a tablet or miss a dose? Leave that dose out. Take the next scheduled dose only. Contact your team if vomiting continues or you cannot keep fluids down.
  • What happens after this part of treatment? Start within eight weeks of the final platinum dose. Treatment lasts up to two years and may stop earlier if the cancer grows or side effects make continued treatment unsuitable.
  • Which symptoms need urgent help? Call the oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills, unexpected bleeding, worsening breathlessness or vomiting that prevents drinking. Report unusual bruising or persistent fatigue promptly.
  • What does maintenance mean? The platinum treatment has already reduced the cancer. Maintenance is a different treatment that aims to prolong that response. Your team can discuss the expected benefit, side effects and alternatives in your situation.
