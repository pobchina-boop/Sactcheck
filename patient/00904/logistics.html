SACTCheck · NCCP 00904 v2 · First-line maintenance after a platinum response

Preparing for treatment visits

Rucaparib (Rubraca) maintenance tablets

Planning your visits

Your appointments

Take the prescribed dose twice daily, about 12 hours apart, every day. There is no planned week off.

Taking treatment

Swallow the prescribed tablets whole with a large glass of water, with or without food. Leave a missed or vomited dose out and take only your next scheduled dose. Never crush the tablets or take extra to catch up.

Planning the next step

Start within eight weeks of the final platinum dose. Treatment lasts up to two years and may stop earlier if the cancer grows or side effects make continued treatment unsuitable.

Write down your next appointments

  • Swallow the prescribed tablets whole with a large glass of water, with or without food. Leave a missed or vomited dose out and take only your next scheduled dose. Never crush the tablets or take extra to catch up.
  • Confirm your last platinum date and ask your pharmacist to check prescribed medicines, painkillers, supplements and herbal products.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills, unexpected bleeding, worsening breathlessness or vomiting that prevents drinking. Report unusual bruising or persistent fatigue promptly. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
