Lorlatinib therapy

Lorlatinib aims to control ALK-positive advanced lung cancer, including disease in the brain where present. Your oncology team explains the likely benefit, treatment line and alternatives for your situation.

My schedule at a glance

Your treatment pattern

Once daily, continuously

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Lorlatinib is taken once daily continuously, with no planned rest week. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of treatment cycles.

Tests and checks

Before starting: ALK testing, blood count, kidney and liver tests, clotting tests, cholesterol and triglycerides, glucose, pancreatic enzymes, blood pressure and an electrocardiogram (ECG). Lipids are checked at weeks 2, 4 and 8, then as needed. Blood count, kidney/liver tests, ECG and pancreatic enzymes are normally checked monthly. Blood pressure is checked after two weeks and at least monthly; glucose as needed. Scans follow your oncology plan.

Carry your treatment details

Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers lorlatinib alone for ALK-positive advanced non-small-cell lung cancer. The available Irish National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) v3 document lists use after particular earlier ALK medicines. The current European licence also includes people who have not had an ALK inhibitor. The team must confirm your treatment setting and the current Irish protocol and access arrangements before prescribing; the source versions currently disagree.

Print, cut out and fold. Follow your treating hospital’s contact plan.
