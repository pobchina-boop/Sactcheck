How this medicine supports your treatment

ALK tyrosine kinase inhibitor

Lorlatinib

Blocks abnormal ALK growth signals
ALK-positive cancer identified↓Abnormal ALK signalling blocked↓Cancer growth may be controlled, including in the brain

Lorlatinib blocks the abnormal ALK protein that helps some lung cancers grow. It can reach the brain, an important site of disease in ALK-positive lung cancer. It is selected using an ALK test. It can also affect healthy tissues, so blood tests and symptom checks remain important.

Taking lorlatinib

Use your own prescription and hold/restart instructions.

Daily treatment

The usual starting dose is 100 mg once daily; use the dose on your prescription. Take it at about the same time each day, with or without food. Swallow whole; do not crush, chew or split tablets, or use damaged tablets. Avoid grapefruit and grapefruit juice.

NCCP 00570 v3 · official regimen · EMA Lorviqua current product information and patient leaflet

Missed or vomited dose

If a dose is missed, take it when remembered if the next dose is at least four hours away; otherwise skip it. Do not take two doses together. If you vomit after a dose, do not take another tablet; take the next dose at the usual time. These instructions follow the current product leaflet.

Interactions

Avoid grapefruit and St John’s wort. Check all prescriptions, supplements and herbal products before any change; interactions can alter lorlatinib or other medicines.

Tests and important effects

Effects may occur between appointments.

Expected monitoring

Before starting: ALK testing, blood count, kidney and liver tests, clotting tests, cholesterol and triglycerides, glucose, pancreatic enzymes, blood pressure and an ECG. Lipids are checked at weeks 2, 4 and 8, then as needed. Blood count, kidney/liver tests, ECG and pancreatic enzymes are normally checked monthly. Blood pressure is checked after two weeks and at least monthly; glucose as needed. Scans follow your oncology plan.

Thinking and mood

Ask someone you trust to help notice changes in thinking, mood, speech or behaviour, if you wish. Report changes early. Lorlatinib can affect driving and machine use, especially when CNS effects are present; discuss whether it is safe for you.

EMA Lorviqua current product information and patient leaflet

Urgent changes

Contact your oncology team promptly for changes in memory, thinking, mood, speech or behaviour, hallucinations, new or worsening breathlessness, cough, fainting, a slow or irregular heartbeat, severe abdominal pain, marked thirst or frequent urination. Severe confusion, unsafe behaviour, severe breathing difficulty or collapse needs emergency help. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Support and sickness

This oral regimen has minimal to low sickness risk. Use anti-sickness medicine only according to your own plan and report persistent vomiting or poor intake. A standard chemotherapy steroid or anti-sickness course is not automatically needed.

NCCP 00570 v3 · official regimen

Pregnancy and fertility

Lorlatinib may harm an unborn baby and make hormonal contraception less effective. Women who could become pregnant need highly effective non-hormonal contraception during treatment and for at least 35 days afterwards; discuss any unavoidable hormonal method and barrier protection. Men with partners who could become pregnant need effective contraception including a condom during treatment and for at least 14 weeks afterwards; condoms are also needed with a pregnant partner. Discuss fertility preservation before treatment. Do not breastfeed during treatment; agree when it can safely resume with the team.

Practical support

Taking and replacing tablets

If a dose is missed, take it when remembered if the next dose is at least four hours away; otherwise skip it. Do not take two doses together. If you vomit after a dose, do not take another tablet; take the next dose at the usual time. These instructions follow the current product leaflet.

Cholesterol and other medicines

Cholesterol or triglycerides may rise without symptoms. Attend blood tests and take any prescribed lipid-lowering medicine. Ask the pharmacist before starting or stopping any medicine, supplement or herbal product, including St John’s wort. Do not change the lorlatinib dose yourself.

Questions to ask

Why is an ALK test needed?

Lorlatinib is intended for cancers with a specific ALK change. Your team checks the result and whether the treatment fits your previous medicines.

How long will I take it?

Lorlatinib is taken once daily continuously, with no planned rest week. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of treatment cycles.

What if I miss a dose or vomit?

If a dose is missed, take it when remembered if the next dose is at least four hours away; otherwise skip it. Do not take two doses together. If you vomit after a dose, do not take another tablet; take the next dose at the usual time. These instructions follow the current product leaflet.

Why are cholesterol tests needed if I feel well?

Blood fats can rise without symptoms. Regular tests let the team treat this early. Raised cholesterol does not always mean lorlatinib must stop; your clinician uses the actual results and your overall condition.

What changes should my family or friends notice?

Changes in memory, concentration, mood, speech, behaviour or perceptions should be reported promptly. Agree with the team and someone you trust how changes will be noticed, if helpful.

Can I drive?

Lorlatinib can affect thinking and other CNS functions. Use caution and discuss driving or operating machinery, particularly if symptoms occur.

Does a dose reduction mean treatment has failed?

No. A hold or reduction may allow side effects to recover and reduce harm. The team reviews cancer control separately and explains the restart plan.

What belongs in the consent discussion?

Discuss your aim, expected benefits and uncertainty, reasonable alternatives including not proceeding, serious and lasting risks, daily practicalities, contraception, fertility, support needs and your right to reconsider.
