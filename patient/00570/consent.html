Lorlatinib therapy

Lorlatinib is taken once daily continuously, with no planned rest week. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of treatment cycles.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Lorlatinib aims to control ALK-positive advanced lung cancer, including disease in the brain where present. Your oncology team explains the likely benefit, treatment line and alternatives for your situation. This guide covers lorlatinib alone for ALK-positive advanced non-small-cell lung cancer. The available Irish NCCP v3 document lists use after particular earlier ALK medicines. The current European licence also includes people who have not had an ALK inhibitor. The team must confirm your treatment setting and the current Irish protocol and access arrangements before prescribing; the source versions currently disagree.

Material risks and support

  • Raised cholesterol and triglycerides; pancreatic inflammation may occur
  • Changes in memory, thinking, mood, speech or perception, including serious psychiatric effects
  • Numbness, tingling or nerve symptoms
  • Swelling, weight gain and fatigue
  • Heart conduction problems, fainting and reduced heart function
  • High blood pressure and raised blood sugar
  • Lung inflammation that can be serious
  • Diarrhoea, nausea and skin effects
  • Harm to an unborn baby, contraception interactions and possible impaired male fertility

Discuss the individual treatment setting, likely benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, brain/mood, metabolic, cardiac and lung risks, possible lasting effects, daily tablets, interactions, pregnancy/fertility and the monitoring/contact plan. Confirm the current Irish source/access discrepancy with the clinical team. This aid supports an ongoing consent conversation.

Monitoring and understanding

Before starting: ALK testing, blood count, kidney and liver tests, clotting tests, cholesterol and triglycerides, glucose, pancreatic enzymes, blood pressure and an ECG. Lipids are checked at weeks 2, 4 and 8, then as needed. Blood count, kidney/liver tests, ECG and pancreatic enzymes are normally checked monthly. Blood pressure is checked after two weeks and at least monthly; glucose as needed. Scans follow your oncology plan.
