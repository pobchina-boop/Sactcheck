Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers lorlatinib alone for ALK-positive advanced non-small-cell lung cancer. The available Irish NCCP v3 document lists use after particular earlier ALK medicines. The current European licence also includes people who have not had an ALK inhibitor. The team must confirm your treatment setting and the current Irish protocol and access arrangements before prescribing; the source versions currently disagree.
NCCP 00570 · version 3

Lorlatinib therapy

Lorlatinib is taken once daily continuously, with no planned rest week. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of treatment cycles.

What each check is for

1Confirm source and settingBefore starting

Previous therapy changes the applicable indication.

Ask / act: Confirm ALK test, treatment line, prescription and baseline tests.

Sources: NCCP 00570 · SMPC

2Early metabolic checksWeeks 2, 4 and 8

Lipids can rise without symptoms.

Ask / act: Confirm who books, reviews and acts on results.

Sources: NCCP 00570 · SMPC

3Monthly reviewDuring continuous therapy

Organ function, conduction and symptoms may change.

Ask / act: Review dose, medicines, CNS/lung/cardiac symptoms and treatment benefit.

Sources: NCCP 00570 · SMPC

4Hold or changeWhenever toxicity or progression requires

Restart criteria differ by toxicity.

Ask / act: Follow a written clinician plan; continue reporting symptoms and contraception advice.

Sources: NCCP 00570 · SMPC
