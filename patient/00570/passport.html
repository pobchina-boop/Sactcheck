Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers lorlatinib alone for ALK-positive advanced non-small-cell lung cancer. The available Irish NCCP v3 document lists use after particular earlier ALK medicines. The current European licence also includes people who have not had an ALK inhibitor. The team must confirm your treatment setting and the current Irish protocol and access arrangements before prescribing; the source versions currently disagree.
NCCP 00570 · version 3

Lorlatinib therapy

Lorlatinib is taken once daily continuously, with no planned rest week. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of treatment cycles.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact your oncology team promptly for changes in memory, thinking, mood, speech or behaviour, hallucinations, new or worsening breathlessness, cough, fainting, a slow or irregular heartbeat, severe abdominal pain, marked thirst or frequent urination. Severe confusion, unsafe behaviour, severe breathing difficulty or collapse needs emergency help. Do not wait for the next appointment.
