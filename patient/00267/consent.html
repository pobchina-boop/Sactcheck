Two-day EP for emergency treatment of GTN

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

This short first treatment aims to bring the disease under control while your hospital team stabilises you and prepares the next treatment. Emergency induction for gestational trophoblastic neoplasia (GTN) with acute illness from liver or central nervous system involvement. Large lung metastases can put breathing at particular risk.

Treatment schedule. Both medicines on days 1 and 2. The specialist may repeat the block weekly for 1–3 weeks. The GTN team decides when to switch to EMA/CO or EMA/EP, combinations of chemotherapy medicines used after induction.

Regimen-specific side effects

Etoposide

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Low counts and infectionPhone urgently for fever, chills, unexpected bleeding or marked breathlessness. Hair loss is common.
Infusion reactions and late effectsTell the nurse immediately about flushing, dizziness or breathlessness. There is a small long-term risk of treatment-related leukaemia.

Treatment discussion and decision

Two-day EP for emergency treatment of GTN

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

A specialist centre reported fewer early deaths in a later group of people treated for high-risk disease. In that group, doctors selected some of the people most seriously ill for short induction chemotherapy. Hospital care also changed over time. The study cannot tell us exactly how much of the improvement came from the two-day treatment.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Tell ward staff immediately about new breathlessness, coughing blood, a severe headache, confusion or a seizure. At home, call 112 or 999 for severe breathing difficulty, collapse or a seizure. Call the oncology number urgently for fever or shaking chills. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
