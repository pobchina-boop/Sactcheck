Pregnancy tests are supervised before treatment and monthly, even with absent periods. The whole treatment journey

Vismodegib for advanced basal cell skin cancer: treatment and checks

NCCP 00236: symptomatic metastatic basal cell carcinoma, or locally advanced basal cell carcinoma unsuitable for surgery or radiotherapy.

Take vismodegib once every day at the same time, continuously while the team considers it beneficial and tolerable. A 28-day calendar organises the four-week review; it is not a planned seven-day break or a fixed number of courses.

Vismodegib is used to help control advanced basal cell skin cancer when the source-specific criteria are met. Discuss whether the aim is shrinkage, symptom relief, longer disease control or another agreed goal, and how surgery or radiotherapy has been considered.
