Tests in your plan

Full blood count

WhenAt baseline and regularly; your team specifies visit-by-visit timing

Checks infection-fighting cells, oxygen-carrying cells and platelets.

Kidney and liver tests

WhenAt baseline and regularly; your team specifies visit-by-visit timing

Guide safe treatment and identify organ problems.

Electrolytes, including magnesium

WhenBefore and periodically during cetuximab under product information; no exact universal interval is stated in this NCCP protocol

Cetuximab can lower minerals; the team agrees which tests and replacement are needed.

Imaging and tumour-specific tests

If part of your plan
WhenAs individually arranged for the disease and symptoms; no universal marker panel or imaging interval

Assesses the cancer rather than whether a treatment-day blood count is safe.

The whole treatment journey

Paclitaxel Days 1, 8, 15 + cetuximab Days 1, 15 · 28-day cycle: treatment and checks

Recurrent and/or metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma under NCCP 00696.

Paclitaxel is given on Days 1, 8 and 15, with cetuximab on Days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. There is no scheduled treatment dose on Day 22. Both medicines continue while appropriate, until progression or unacceptable side effects; the protocol does not set a fixed total number of cycles.
