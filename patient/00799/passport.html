My treatment record

Control the cancer, relieve symptoms and delay progression.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Combination · day 1Combination · day 1Paclitaxel, cisplatin and bevacizumab on day 1 every three weeks. IV fluid before and after cisplatin helps protect the kidneys. Treatment continues while it helps and is tolerated.

Hospital __________________ Who to call if I cannot reach the team __________________

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call oncology urgently for fever, chills, new breathlessness or severe diarrhoea. Severe abdominal pain or unexpected bleeding also needs urgent review. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count, kidney and liver function. Review medicines, allergies and current symptoms.Bring a medicine list and details of previous treatment.
Before each cycleBlood counts and organ function are reviewed with symptoms and fitness for treatment.Tell your team if infection, tiredness or side effects limit normal activity.
Before bevacizumabBlood pressure, urine protein, bleeding, bowel symptoms and wound healing.Tell your team about surgery or dental procedures.
Before cisplatinKidney function and electrolytes, including magnesium. Review hearing and fluid balance.Tell the team if hearing, sensation or urine output has changed.
