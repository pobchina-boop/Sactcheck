How this medicine supports your treatment

Osimertinib blocks abnormal EGFR growth signals in selected lung cancers. After complete surgery, treatment aims to lower the risk of cancer returning. In advanced cancer, it aims to control cancer and delay progression. Confirm which aim applies to you.

EGFR targeted therapy

Osimertinib

Blocks abnormal EGFR growth signals
EGFR mutation identified↓Growth signal blocked↓Cancer growth slowed

Osimertinib is an EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor. It blocks signalling from sensitising EGFR mutations and the T790M resistance mutation, reducing cancer-cell growth signals. The tumour test and treatment setting determine whether it is appropriate.

Report new breathlessness, severe rash, diarrhoea, fainting, unusual bleeding or eye pain promptly.

Checks before and during treatment

Tests and symptoms guide safe treatment decisions.

Before starting

Confirm the relevant EGFR test, treatment setting and prescription. Adjuvant treatment needs complete resection and the specific stage/mutation criteria. Blood count, kidney, liver and bone profile; ECG before treatment. Consider an echocardiogram or MUGA LVEF assessment for cardiac risks or symptoms; review all medicines, pregnancy, contraception and breastfeeding.

NCCP 00353 v5 · official regimen · EMA Tagrisso product information (updated 18/08/2026)

During treatment

Repeat blood count, kidney, liver and bone profile at the agreed reviews; extra checks may be needed for symptoms or abnormal results. NCCP specifies ECG at baseline and cycle 2, then cycle 3 onwards if clinically indicated. The team translates cycle 2 into a booked review; the tablets remain daily, without a treatment break. LVEF assessment if clinically indicated; ECG and electrolytes with cardiac/QT risks. Scans and disease checks follow the Consultant plan.

Taking osimertinib

Follow your own prescription.

Daily treatment

Take at the same time each day, with or without food. Swallow whole with water; do not crush, split or chew. If you forget a dose, take it when remembered unless less than 12 hours remain before the next dose, when it should be skipped. Confirm the instruction with your pharmacist if the next dose is exactly 12 hours away. Do not take an extra dose without advice if you vomit. Ask for the approved water-dispersion instructions if swallowing is difficult.

Interactions

Tell the team about all medicines and supplements. Do not take St John’s wort. Strong enzyme-inducing medicines and medicines that affect heart rhythm require pharmacy review before changes.

Nausea medicines

Osimertinib has minimal-to-low oral emetic risk. Routine infusion premedication is not usually needed. Agree rescue medicines individually, including QT and interaction review; do not copy a chemotherapy antiemetic course.

Pregnancy, fertility and breastfeeding

Avoid pregnancy during treatment. Effective contraception continues for at least two months after the final dose for women and four months for men. Osimertinib may reduce hormonal contraceptive effectiveness; discuss a suitable method. Do not breastfeed during treatment. Discuss fertility and plans for pregnancy before starting.

When to get urgent help

Do not wait until the next appointment.

Call urgently

Contact the oncology team urgently for new or worsening breathlessness, cough or fever; fainting, palpitations or chest pain; blistering/peeling skin or mouth/eye/genital sores; unusual bruising, bleeding or marked pallor; severe diarrhoea, dehydration or inability to drink. New eye pain, light sensitivity or blurred vision needs prompt advice. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or swelling of the tongue/throat needs emergency help. Use your unit’s temperature and 24-hour contact instructions.

What studies tell us

The evidence differs by setting. In a study after complete surgery, adjuvant osimertinib improved survival compared with placebo. In untreated advanced EGFR-mutated cancer, it improved survival compared with older EGFR tablets. After resistance with T790M, it delayed progression compared with platinum/pemetrexed, but the final overall-survival comparison was not statistically significant. These results describe selected study groups and cannot predict your own outcome.

Your own decision

Ask how closely the study population matches your situation and discuss alternatives, harms and remaining uncertainty.

Do I need dates to use the timeline?

No. It shows relative steps; your team arranges real appointments and reviews results.

Does a rash show treatment is working?

Side effects do not reliably show benefit. Report them; scans and clinical reviews assess cancer control.

Is this the same as osimertinib plus chemotherapy?

This guide covers NCCP 00353 osimertinib on its own: T790M-positive advanced disease, first-line activating-EGFR advanced disease, or completely resected stage IB–IIIA Ex19del/L858R adjuvant treatment. What should I ask at consent?

Discuss adjuvant versus advanced intent, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, common skin/bowel effects, serious lung/heart/skin/blood problems, fertility and pregnancy, daily tablets, monitoring and your right to reconsider.

Explain the plan back: Can you explain your treatment aim and duration, how you take the tablet, which heart/blood tests are next, and which symptoms mean you should call today?
