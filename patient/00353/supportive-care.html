Osimertinib monotherapy

Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers NCCP 00353 osimertinib on its own: T790M-positive advanced disease, first-line activating-EGFR advanced disease, or completely resected stage IB–IIIA Ex19del/L858R adjuvant treatment. The newer licensed post-chemoradiation setting and osimertinib with platinum/pemetrexed need their own applicable plan; their study results and support medicines are not added to this monotherapy suite.

Osimertinib alone · daily oral treatment

Download this phase PDF · Open printable script

Active medicines: osimertinib

Emetogenic risk: Minimal to low (oral)

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Agree an EGFR-inhibitor skin-care plan from the start and report rash or painful nail changes early. Blistering, peeling or mucosal sores need urgent assessment.
  • Report diarrhoea early, follow the prescribed symptom plan and maintain fluids if able. Severe diarrhoea, weakness or reduced urine needs urgent advice.
  • Osimertinib has minimal-to-low oral emetic risk. Routine infusion premedication is not usually needed. Agree rescue medicines individually, including QT and interaction review; do not copy a chemotherapy antiemetic course.
  • Tell the team about all medicines and supplements. Do not take St John’s wort. Strong enzyme-inducing medicines and medicines that affect heart rhythm require pharmacy review before changes.
  • Avoid pregnancy during treatment. Effective contraception continues for at least two months after the final dose for women and four months for men. Osimertinib may reduce hormonal contraceptive effectiveness; discuss a suitable method. Do not breastfeed during treatment. Discuss fertility and plans for pregnancy before starting.
  • NCCP V6 Table 5 supports as-required oral antiemetics for minimal-low risk; select the agent, dose and duration individually after QT, renal and interaction review.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
