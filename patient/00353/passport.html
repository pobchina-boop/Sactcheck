Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers NCCP 00353 osimertinib on its own: T790M-positive advanced disease, first-line activating-EGFR advanced disease, or completely resected stage IB–IIIA Ex19del/L858R adjuvant treatment. The newer licensed post-chemoradiation setting and osimertinib with platinum/pemetrexed need their own applicable plan; their study results and support medicines are not added to this monotherapy suite.
NCCP 00353 · version 5

Osimertinib monotherapy

The usual starting dose is 80 mg by mouth once daily, every day without a planned rest week. Use the dose on your prescription. Advanced treatment continues while helping and tolerated; adjuvant treatment is planned for up to three years, with earlier stopping if cancer returns or side effects become unacceptable.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact the oncology team urgently for new or worsening breathlessness, cough or fever; fainting, palpitations or chest pain; blistering/peeling skin or mouth/eye/genital sores; unusual bruising, bleeding or marked pallor; severe diarrhoea, dehydration or inability to drink. New eye pain, light sensitivity or blurred vision needs prompt advice. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or swelling of the tongue/throat needs emergency help. Use your unit’s temperature and 24-hour contact instructions.
