Nivolumab, ipilimumab, pemetrexed and cisplatin

The first cycle lasts 42 days: all four medicines on Day 1, then nivolumab, pemetrexed and cisplatin on Day 22. Chemotherapy ends after those two visits. From Cycle 2, nivolumab continues on Days 1 and 22 and ipilimumab on Day 1 of each 42-day cycle. Immune treatment continues while appropriate until the cancer progresses, side effects require stopping, or up to 24 months without progression. Plans can change after review.

Important medicine-specific effects

Nivolumab

  • Immune inflammation in multiple organs
  • Infusion reactions
  • Delayed effects after treatment stops

Ipilimumab

  • Immune bowel, liver, skin and hormone inflammation
  • Other serious organ inflammation
  • Infusion reactions

Pemetrexed

  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Mouth soreness, rash and fatigue
  • Kidney toxicity

Cisplatin

  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Kidney, hearing and nerve effects
  • Allergic reactions

Make the plan practical

Cycle 1 Days 1 and 22 · chemotherapy-containing visits

Use generic high-risk NCIS row including olanzapine. Cisplatin requires pre/post hydration and electrolyte review; follow individual/local fluid policy. Folic acid starts before pemetrexed, continues through both chemotherapy visits and for 21 days after the last pemetrexed dose. B12 is given in the preceding week. The source repeats B12 every three cycles if pemetrexed continues; this planned course has only two pemetrexed administrations, so do not automatically continue B12 in immune-only cycles. Your team reconciles the steroid used to prevent pemetrexed rash with the anti-sickness steroid into one clear prescription. Do not add the two schedules together or use leftover steroids yourself. Nivolumab and ipilimumab can inflame healthy organs. Report new symptoms promptly, including after treatment stops; follow-up vigilance is required for at least five months after the last dose.

Cycle 2 onwards Day 1 · nivolumab + ipilimumab every 42 days

Ipilimumab is low risk and nivolumab minimal; this combined visit is low risk. No further pemetrexed or platinum: stop/review induction antiemetics and rash steroid; complete the folate tail only. Continue immune symptom education, bloods each treatment and glucose/TFT each 42-day cycle.

Cycle 2 onwards Day 22 · nivolumab alone, between ipilimumab visits

No routine prophylactic antiemetic course for nivolumab-only minimal-risk treatment; prescribe symptom-directed rescue under local policy if needed. Ipilimumab is due at the next 42-day cycle Day 1, not every nivolumab visit. Immune toxicity can still occur between ipilimumab visits; the joint hold rule remains relevant.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
