Nivolumab, ipilimumab, pemetrexed and cisplatin

This treatment aims to control metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer, help symptoms and extend life. It is usually given without an expectation of cure. The team checks the cancer subtype, molecular results, your health and your preferences before recommending this particular chemotherapy backbone.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

The first cycle lasts 42 days: all four medicines on Day 1, then nivolumab, pemetrexed and cisplatin on Day 22. Chemotherapy ends after those two visits. From Cycle 2, nivolumab continues on Days 1 and 22 and ipilimumab on Day 1 of each 42-day cycle. Immune treatment continues while appropriate until the cancer progresses, side effects require stopping, or up to 24 months without progression. Plans can change after review.

Tests and checks

Blood count/kidney/liver before every treatment; glucose/thyroid before each 42-day cycle; symptoms assessed throughout and after treatment.

Between treatment visits

This is a 42-day reference cycle. Only Cycle 1 has chemotherapy; Day 22 never includes ipilimumab. Symptom bands illustrate possible periods, not daily risk or a prediction of recovery.

Nivolumab and ipilimumab can inflame healthy organs. Report new symptoms promptly, including after treatment stops; follow-up vigilance is required for at least five months after the last dose. Do not wait for the next appointment if new or serious symptoms develop.
