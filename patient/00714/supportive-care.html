Additional phase-specific support

  • Cycle 1: nivolumab, pemetrexed and cisplatin on Days 1 and 22; ipilimumab on Day 1 ONLY. Day 22 has no ipilimumab.
  • Use generic high-risk NCIS row including olanzapine. Cisplatin requires pre/post hydration and electrolyte review; follow individual/local fluid policy.
  • Folic acid starts before pemetrexed, continues through both chemotherapy visits and for 21 days after the last pemetrexed dose. B12 is given in the preceding week. The source repeats B12 every three cycles if pemetrexed continues; this planned course has only two pemetrexed administrations, so do not automatically continue B12 in immune-only cycles.
  • Your team reconciles the steroid used to prevent pemetrexed rash with the anti-sickness steroid into one clear prescription. Do not add the two schedules together or use leftover steroids yourself.
  • Nivolumab and ipilimumab can inflame healthy organs. Report new symptoms promptly, including after treatment stops; follow-up vigilance is required for at least five months after the last dose.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.

Cycle 2 onwards Day 22 · nivolumab alone, between ipilimumab visits

Active medicines: nivolumab

Emetogenic risk: minimal

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine prophylactic antiemetic course for nivolumab-only minimal-risk treatment; prescribe symptom-directed rescue under local policy if needed.
  • Ipilimumab is due at the next 42-day cycle Day 1, not every nivolumab visit.
  • Immune toxicity can still occur between ipilimumab visits; the joint hold rule remains relevant.
