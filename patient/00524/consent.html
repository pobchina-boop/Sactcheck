Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Gemcitabine and capecitabine after pancreatic surgery

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

After surgery has removed all visible pancreatic cancer, treatment aims to reduce the risk of the cancer returning. Pancreatic adenocarcinoma after macroscopically complete resection

Treatment schedule. Gemcitabine on days 1, 8, 15. Take capecitabine twice daily on days 1–21, then seven tablet-free days. Up to six 28-day cycles.

Regimen-specific side effects

Gemcitabine

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help

Lungs, kidneys and skinContact oncology urgently for new breathlessness, reduced urine or a painful blistering rash.

Capecitabine

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help
Bowel, mouth, hands and feetHold tablets and call for significant diarrhoea, painful mouth ulcers or hands and feet painful enough to limit activity.
Chest pain or severe early symptomsChest pain needs emergency assessment. Severe early diarrhoea, dehydration or fever needs urgent oncology review.

Treatment discussion and decision

Gemcitabine and capecitabine after pancreatic surgery

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

After resection, gemcitabine plus capecitabine improved median overall survival compared with gemcitabine alone (28.0 versus 25.5 months in the original analysis). These are group results from the named setting. They cannot predict the benefit or side effects for an individual.

After surgery has removed all visible pancreatic cancer, treatment aims to reduce the risk of the cancer returning. Discuss alternatives, fertility, individual risks and supportive care with the treating team.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
