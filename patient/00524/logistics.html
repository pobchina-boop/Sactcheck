Preparing for treatment visits

Gemcitabine and capecitabine after pancreatic surgery

Planning your visits

Day unit

Gemcitabine runs over 30 minutes. Swallow prescribed capecitabine tablets whole with water, with food or within 30 minutes of a meal. Follow the dated tablet calendar.

Between visits

Keep a dated tablet diary. If you miss or vomit a dose, do not take an extra dose. Phone the team if unsure. Hold tablets and call for significant diarrhoea or painful mouth, hands or feet.

Write down your next appointments

  • Bring your medicine list and 24-hour oncology contact details.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
