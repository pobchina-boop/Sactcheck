Your treatment journey

Gemcitabine and capecitabine after pancreatic surgery

After surgery has removed all visible pancreatic cancer, treatment aims to reduce the risk of the cancer returning.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · Gemcitabine infusion

Gemcitabine runs over 30 minutes. Swallow prescribed capecitabine tablets whole with water, with food or within 30 minutes of a meal. Follow the dated tablet calendar.

Day 8 · Gemcitabine infusion

Gemcitabine infusion and prescribed capecitabine tablets.

Day 15 · Gemcitabine infusion

Gemcitabine infusion and prescribed capecitabine tablets.

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do

Response or follow-upClinical review and imaging as arranged for your treatment setting.Ask when results will be discussed.

Ask the team about a new fever with jaundice, dark urine or shivering, especially if you have a bile duct stent.

At review visits

  • Which treatment setting applies to me? After surgery has removed all visible pancreatic cancer, treatment aims to reduce the risk of the cancer returning.
  • What needs urgent attention at home? Keep a dated tablet diary. If you miss or vomit a dose, do not take an extra dose. Phone the team if unsure. Hold tablets and call for significant diarrhoea or painful mouth, hands or feet.
