Bevacizumab + weekly paclitaxel

Bevacizumab 10 mg/kg is given on Days 1 and 15, with paclitaxel 80 mg/m² on Days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Continue according to benefit, toxicity and the treating team’s plan.

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Bevacizumab

  • High blood pressure / urine protein
  • Bleeding or blood clots
  • Delayed wound healing
  • Rare bowel perforation / fistula
  • Rare serious neurological effects
  • Infusion reaction

Paclitaxel

  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Numbness / tingling, sometimes lasting
  • Hair loss, muscle or joint pain
  • Infusion reaction
  • Mouth, bowel, skin and nail effects

Make the plan practical

Blood pressure and urine

Attend the required checks even if you feel well. Report severe headache, visual change or swelling.

Operations and dental work

Tell oncology about planned procedures; bevacizumab timing and wound healing need review.

Paclitaxel premedicines

The first dose and later doses have different plans. A previous reaction changes the assessment.

Nerves and bleeding

Report numbness, unusual bleeding or bruising before the next visit.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
