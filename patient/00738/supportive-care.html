First paclitaxel dose · combination Day 1

Download this phase PDF · Open printable script

Active medicines: Bevacizumab + Paclitaxel

Emetogenic risk: Low

MedicineDose / routeWhen
Ondansetron8 mg orally onceBefore each administered chemotherapy doseReview QT risk, constipation, liver function and any steroid already prescribed.
Metoclopramide10 mg orally up to three times daily as neededRescue duration to be confirmedReview contraindications, renal function and interactions.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Before the first paclitaxel dose: dexamethasone 8 mg IV, chlorphenamine 10 mg IV and famotidine 20 mg IV, each 30 minutes before treatment.
  • The first and subsequent infusion-reaction plans are different; do not automatically repeat the initial steroid schedule.
  • Check BP before each bevacizumab dose and reconcile urine-protein results, bleeding, thrombosis and wound healing.
  • NCCP: withhold bevacizumab 28 days before major or 7 days before minor elective surgery. Do not restart for at least 28 days after major surgery and until the wound is fully healed; the team individualises this.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
