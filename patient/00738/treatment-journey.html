The whole treatment journey

Bevacizumab + weekly paclitaxel: treatment and checks

First-line metastatic breast cancer · source-defined eligibility

Bevacizumab 10 mg/kg is given on Days 1 and 15, with paclitaxel 80 mg/m² on Days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Continue according to benefit, toxicity and the treating team’s plan.

What each check is for

1Before treatmentBefore the first dose

First-line metastatic breast cancer · source-defined eligibility. Baseline FBC, kidney/liver tests and urine protein. Review blood counts, renal/liver function and urine protein before cycles; Day 8 FBC is explicitly required. Check current counts for each paclitaxel decision.

Ask / act: Confirm eligibility, test results and medicine list.

Sources: NCCP 00738

2Other checksAt the specified review points

Measure blood pressure before each bevacizumab dose and follow the agreed urine-protein plan. Review bleeding, clotting, wounds and planned procedures. Cardiac imaging is considered for prior anthracycline/chest radiotherapy or clinical concern; warfarin requires additional INR monitoring.

Ask / act: Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.

Sources: NCCP 00738

3Support and practical planAt each treatment or phase change

Separate the first paclitaxel premedication from later weekly doses; review blood pressure, urine protein, wounds and surgery timing for bevacizumab.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support plan; confirm active medicines.

Sources: NCCP 00738

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

Agree the scan type and review interval for the known disease sites. Review response, symptoms and toxicity together; new or worsening symptoms may need an earlier assessment. Keep the agreed follow-up and report persistent or new symptoms between visits.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities.

Sources: METASTATIC · MARKERS
