NCCP 00254 · version 5

Anastrozole · daily hormone therapy

Take anastrozole once daily continuously. There is no planned week off. The treatment setting determines the intended duration.

Clinician supportive-care discussion draft. This is not an issued prescription.

Minimal emetogenic risk

No routine prophylactic antiemetic, corticosteroid premedication or G-CSF order is populated for this endocrine monotherapy. Review persistent nausea or other symptoms and prescribe individually if needed.

Medicine-specific support

  • Tell the team if joint pain, hot flushes, vaginal dryness or sleep disturbance affects daily life. Bone thinning may cause no early symptoms, so keep the agreed bone-health reviews.
  • No routine antiemetic, steroid premedication or G-CSF is required by this endocrine monotherapy. Ask for individual symptom support.
  • Ask who books each check, when it is due and who reviews it. Tell the team if an expected appointment has not arrived.
  • A functioning premenopausal ovarian state is not compatible with AI monotherapy. Severe hepatic impairment is not recommended by NCCP; moderate impairment requires caution. Confirm menopausal status rather than assuming that absent periods after chemotherapy mean permanent menopause. Any ovarian-suppression treatment must have its own prescribed schedule.
  • NCCP no renal adjustment including haemodialysis; severe renal impairment requires caution. No mild/moderate hepatic reduction; linked SmPC cautions moderate/severe hepatic dysfunction. NCCP severe hepatic disease not recommended: withhold pending review rather than invent a reduction.
