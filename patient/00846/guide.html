Treatment timing in this window

Enfortumab vedotinDay 1, 8, 15
  1. TreatmentEnfortumab vedotin is given on days 1, 8 and 15 of a 28-day cycle. The starting dose is 1.25 mg/kg, capped at 125 mg per dose. The team may hold, reduce or stop treatment for side effects. Treatment continues while appropriate according to benefit and tolerance.

Between treatments

  • Side effects may occur on any day, including during a break. Report problems promptly.
  • Follow the prescribed low-risk anti-sickness plan. Ask about moisturiser, skin care and artificial tears for dry eyes. Check glucose as directed, including if not previously diabetic. Premedication with an antihistamine, antipyretic and intravenous steroid is considered for subsequent infusions after a prior infusion reaction; it is not an automatic routine prescription for everyone.

Confirm diabetes/glucose advice, a skin and eye-care plan, baseline nerve symptoms and contact numbers.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
