The whole treatment journey

Enfortumab vedotin · monotherapy: treatment and checks

Advanced urothelial cancer · after platinum and immunotherapy

Enfortumab vedotin is given on days 1, 8 and 15 of a 28-day cycle. The starting dose is 1.25 mg/kg, capped at 125 mg per dose. The team may hold, reduce or stop treatment for side effects. Treatment continues while appropriate according to benefit and tolerance.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewDays 1, 8 and 15 · every 28 days

Help control advanced urothelial cancer previously treated with platinum chemotherapy and a PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999.
