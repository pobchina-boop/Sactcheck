Adjuvant ribociclib + endocrine therapy

Download this phase PDF

Active medicines: Ribociclib + Non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor ± ovarian suppression

Emetogenic risk: Oral minimal to low

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • No default QT-prolonging antiemetic is generated. Pharmacy must choose symptom treatment after reviewing ECG, electrolytes and interacting medicines.
  • Ribociclib Days 1–21; Days 22–28 off. Endocrine therapy continues separately.
  • Bloods every 2 weeks for cycles 1–2, then before the next four cycles; ECG baseline and around C1D14; further checks as indicated.
  • The optional local D25–26 review window does not replace early checks or authorise restarting.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
