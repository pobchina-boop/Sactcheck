When to get help

Emergency: call 112 / 999

Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call emergency services. Do not wait for an online answer.

Contact your oncology service immediately

Temperature 37.5°C or higher, 36°C or lower, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell — even with a normal temperature. Report new breathlessness, jaundice, marked bleeding, severe diarrhoea, inability to drink, chest symptoms or a severe rash promptly.

Follow the urgent contact instructions from your treating hospital. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Outside clinic hours, use your instructed out-of-hours pathway or the emergency department.

This page does not diagnose symptoms or decide whether it is safe to stay at home. You do not need to know which medicine caused a symptom before calling.

Understand your treatment

Your medicines and their different roles

For eligible higher-risk HR-positive/HER2-negative early breast cancer, ribociclib is added to endocrine therapy to reduce recurrence risk.

CDK4/6 inhibitor

Ribociclib

Kisqali
Slows the cell-division checkpoint

Ribociclib blocks CDK4 and CDK6, proteins that help cells move through the cycle of division. Endocrine therapy reduces the hormone signal that can drive HR-positive breast cancer.

CDK4/6 checkpoint↓Checkpoint inhibited↓Cell cycling slows

What to watch for
Keep blood and ECG appointments. Fever, jaundice, fainting, palpitations, severe rash or new breathlessness need prompt advice.

Sources: nccp

Endocrine partner

Non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor ± ovarian suppression

Letrozole or anastrozole; ovarian suppression when indicated
Reduces oestrogen-driven growth

An aromatase inhibitor lowers oestrogen production. Premenopausal patients need an agreed ovarian-suppression plan; men also require the appropriate hormonal partner plan. The endocrine schedule continues separately from the ribociclib breaks.

Oestrogen pathway↓AI or receptor blockade↓Growth signal reduced

What to watch for
The endocrine medicine continues during the ribociclib week off. Report troublesome joint, menopausal or injection-site symptoms.

Make the plan practical

Support around the medicines

Support for this phase

Agree symptom medicines with the oncology pharmacist because many anti-sickness and other medicines can affect QT or CYP3A interactions. Do not automatically reuse a chemotherapy antiemetic prescription.

Bloods and cardiac checks

Review prior toxicity → FBC, renal, liver and electrolytes → FBC / renal / liver every 2 weeks. Repeat ECG around Cycle 1 Day 14.

Visits and practical support

Ask about travel, work, fertility, menopause, exercise and help at home. The team arranges and reviews clinical checks; the patient should not have to calculate the monitoring timetable.

Follow-up and bone health

If an aromatase inhibitor or ovarian suppression is prescribed, assess fracture risk and baseline DEXA as indicated. Repeat timing depends on the result, risk and bone treatment; it is not an automatic scan for everyone.

Read the study population and endpoint alongside the numbers.

Show trial results and what the numbers mean

Randomised phase III study of ribociclib plus a non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor versus the aromatase inhibitor alone in eligible stage II–III disease.

Ribociclib + aromatase inhibitorAromatase inhibitor alone

Four-year invasive disease-free survival was 88.5% versus 83.6%, an absolute difference of 4.9 percentage points. Overall-survival data were immature in this analysis. This is a dated analysis, not a claim that it is the latest follow-up.
