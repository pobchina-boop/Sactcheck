The whole treatment journey

Adjuvant ribociclib + endocrine therapy: the whole pathway

Adjuvant · HR-positive / HER2-negative early breast cancer

Ribociclib 400 mg once daily on Days 1–21, followed by Days 22–28 off, for up to 3 years. The aromatase inhibitor continues daily on its own schedule; ovarian suppression is added when indicated.

5Bone health with endocrine treatmentAt endocrine / ovarian-suppression planning; repeat by risk

Low oestrogen can affect bone strength.

Ask / act: Ask whether DEXA, fracture-risk assessment or bone protection is appropriate.

Sources: BONE · NOGG
