Preparing for treatment visits

Durvalumab + gemcitabine + cisplatin

Planning your visits

Your first visit

On day 1 you receive durvalumab, gemcitabine and cisplatin. On day 8 you receive gemcitabine and cisplatin only. Blood results or symptoms may change dates.

Later visits

Chemotherapy finishes after up to eight cycles. If treatment continues as planned, only durvalumab is given on day 1 of a 28-day cycle. Your team reviews benefit and side effects.

Before leaving the unit

Write down the next infusion, blood tests and the 24-hour oncology number. Ask which appointments change if a dose is delayed. Bring the wallet and passport to your next review.

Write down your next appointments

  • Bring your medicines list and your immunotherapy alert card.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shaking chills or jaundice may signal serious infection, especially with a bile duct stent. Contact oncology immediately. New cough, breathlessness or diarrhoea is also urgent. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
