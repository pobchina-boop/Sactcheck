My treatment passport

Durvalumab + gemcitabine + cisplatin

My treatment record

First-line treatment to slow advanced bile duct or gallbladder cancer and help people live longer.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Initial combinationCycles 1–8 at most · Day 1: all three · Day 8: chemotherapy onlyOn day 1 you receive durvalumab, gemcitabine and cisplatin. On day 8 you receive gemcitabine and cisplatin only. Blood results or symptoms may change dates.
MaintenanceCycle 9 onward · Every 28 days · Day 1: durvalumab aloneChemotherapy finishes after up to eight cycles. If treatment continues as planned, only durvalumab is given on day 1 of a 28-day cycle. Your team reviews benefit and side effects.

Hospital __________________ Who to call if I cannot reach the team __________________

Contact your oncology service urgently. Fever, shaking chills or jaundice may signal serious infection, especially with a bile duct stent. Contact oncology immediately. New cough, breathlessness or diarrhoea is also urgent. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
