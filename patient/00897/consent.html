Possible benefit, alternatives and your priorities

The TOPAZ-1 trial compared adding durvalumab to gemcitabine and cisplatin with adding a placebo. At the three-year analysis, about 15 in 100 people in the durvalumab group and 7 in 100 in the placebo group were alive after three years.

These are results in selected trial groups. They cannot predict an individual response; ask how the findings apply to you.

Discuss the aim, alternatives including supportive care alone, possible benefit, immune effects that may be serious or permanent, possible need for steroids or long-term hormone treatment, and what matters most to the patient. Include infection, kidney, hearing and nerve risks from chemotherapy.

Other options discussed and what matters most to me

Important risks we discussed

  • Lung inflammation (pneumonitis) — Contact oncology urgently for a new cough or breathlessness. Severe trouble breathing or chest pain needs 112 or 999.
  • Bowel inflammation (colitis) — Call oncology urgently for new diarrhoea, blood in stools or increasing abdominal pain.
  • Liver inflammation or bile drainage problems — Call urgently for yellow eyes or skin, dark urine or worsening upper abdominal pain.
  • Hormone problems — Unusual thirst, frequent urination, dizziness, vomiting, marked weakness or severe headache need urgent assessment. Collapse or confusion with severe illness needs emergency help.
  • Infection or blocked bile duct — Fever, shivering, jaundice or upper abdominal pain need urgent assessment, especially with a biliary stent.
  • Kidneys, hearing or nerves — Report much less urine, ringing in the ears, hearing changes or tingling promptly.
