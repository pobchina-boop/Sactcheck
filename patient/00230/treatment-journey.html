Nab-paclitaxel · every 3 weeks: treatment and checks

Metastatic breast cancer after first-line failure; standard anthracycline therapy unsuitable

Nab-paclitaxel on Day 1 every 21 days. Review response after two cycles; the NCCP protocol says to discontinue if there is no response. Further treatment depends on benefit and tolerance.

What each check is for

1Before treatmentBefore the first dose

Metastatic breast cancer after first-line failure; standard anthracycline therapy unsuitable. FBC, kidney and liver tests before treatment and each cycle. Review response after two cycles; the source specifies stopping if there is no response.

Ask / act: Confirm eligibility, test results and medicine list.

Sources: NCCP 00230

2Other checksAt the specified review points

Baseline cardiac assessment for a significant cardiac history or prior anthracycline exposure; further assessment as clinically indicated. Review neuropathy before doses.

Ask / act: Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.

Sources: NCCP 00230

3Support and practical planAt each treatment or phase change

Routine allergy premedicines are usually not required for this albumin-bound formulation. Low-risk antiemetic support is separate; previous reactions may change the plan.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support plan; confirm active medicines.

Sources: NCCP 00230

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

Agree the scan type and review interval for the known disease sites. Review response, symptoms and toxicity together; new or worsening symptoms may need an earlier assessment. Keep the agreed follow-up and report persistent or new symptoms between visits.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities.

Sources: METASTATIC · MARKERS
