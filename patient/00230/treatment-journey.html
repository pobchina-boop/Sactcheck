The whole treatment journey

Nab-paclitaxel · every 3 weeks: the treatment pathway

Metastatic breast cancer after first-line treatment

Nab-paclitaxel on Day 1 every 21 days. Review response after two cycles; the NCCP protocol says to discontinue if there is no response. Further treatment depends on benefit and tolerance.

2Heart checksBaseline cardiac assessment, which may include ECG, echo or MUGA, if significant cardiac history or previous anthracycline therapy. Repeat cardiac assessment if clinically indicated. Baseline echo / MUGA when cardiac history or previous anthracycline treatment makes it necessary; ongoing testing is driven by symptoms and risk, with no universal 3-month schedule for nab-paclitaxel alone.

3Response reviewAfter two cycles, around week 6: assess response before proceeding. The source says to discontinue if no response; the team decides which examinations or scans establish response. This earlier source-specific review takes priority over the general 2–4-month metastatic breast interval.
