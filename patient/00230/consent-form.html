Treatment context
The aim is to control the cancer and its symptoms. The team reviews benefit, side effects and your priorities before continuing.
Schedule
Nab-paclitaxel on Day 1 every 21 days. Review response after two cycles; the NCCP protocol says to discontinue if there is no response. Further treatment depends on benefit and tolerance.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Nab-paclitaxel

Chemotherapy
Numbness / tingling or weakness, sometimes lasting
Low blood counts, infection and sepsis
Hair loss, fatigue and muscle / joint aches
Infusion reactions; uncommon serious lung inflammation

Discuss the exact indication, expected benefit, alternatives, uncertainty, serious and lasting risks, contraception and fertility, venous access, monitoring and the urgent contact plan. Nab-paclitaxel is an albumin-bound formulation. It is not interchangeable with ordinary paclitaxel or with the weekly nab-paclitaxel schedule.
