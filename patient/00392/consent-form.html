Doxorubicin 75 mg/m² and ifosfamide for sarcoma

High-risk soft tissue sarcoma before surgery, or advanced/metastatic soft tissue sarcoma · NCCP 00392 · version 5 · 21-day cycle

Treatment context
Your team may choose a combination when reducing tumour size is an important goal and you are fit for more intensive treatment. For high-risk disease before surgery, the goal and local-treatment plan require specialist sarcoma review; for advanced disease, treatment generally aims at control and symptom relief.
Schedule
NCCP 00392: doxorubicin 25 mg/m² and ifosfamide 3000 mg/m² on each of days 1–3, repeated every 21 days for up to 6 cycles. Per-cycle totals are doxorubicin 75 mg/m² and ifosfamide 9000 mg/m². Mesna, hydration and other support continue for their prescribed periods.

General treatment discussion

Possible short-term, long-term, unexpected and unlisted effects; benefits are not guaranteed.
Severe complications can require hospital admission and can rarely be fatal.
Infection / sepsis, allergic or infusion reactions and leakage outside a vein (extravasation) should be discussed where applicable to the medicines and route.
Pregnancy, contraception, breastfeeding, sexual health and fertility preservation should be discussed where relevant.
Treatment can be delayed, changed or stopped if risks outweigh benefit. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Doxorubicin

Anthracycline chemotherapy
Low blood counts, serious infection or sepsis, anaemia and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting, mouth ulcers, hair loss and fatigue
Heart muscle damage or rhythm problems; heart damage may appear later and can be permanent
Severe tissue injury if the medicine leaks outside the vein
Fertility and pregnancy harm; rare later leukaemia or other blood cancers

Ifosfamide

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts, serious infection or sepsis, anaemia and bleeding
Bladder inflammation or bleeding; kidney damage and electrolyte loss, sometimes lasting
Brain effects causing confusion, unusual sleepiness, hallucinations or seizures
Nausea, vomiting, mouth soreness, hair loss and fatigue
Fertility and pregnancy harm; rare serious heart or lung injury and later cancers

Discuss the treatment goal, alternatives including supportive care, uncertainty of benefit, common and serious risks, lasting effects and individual priorities. Record questions and the agreed plan. This is NCCP 00392: doxorubicin 75 mg/m² and ifosfamide 9 g/m² per cycle. The two NCCP combination regimens have different doxorubicin doses and eligibility details. They must not be substituted for each other or for a published 10 g/m² ifosfamide regimen.
