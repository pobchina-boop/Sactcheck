Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Modified FOLFIRINOX for pancreatic cancer

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

After surgery the aim is to reduce the chance of pancreatic cancer returning. If the cancer has spread, the aim is to control it. Ask which aim applies to you. Resected pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma or metastatic pancreatic cancer

Treatment schedule. Day 1 infusions, then a 46-hour fluorouracil pump. Disconnect around day 3. Repeat every 14 days for up to 12 cycles.

Treatment discussion and decision

Modified FOLFIRINOX for pancreatic cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Following resection, modified FOLFIRINOX improved disease-free survival compared with gemcitabine. In metastatic disease the pivotal comparison tested standard FOLFIRINOX. It does not establish identical outcomes for the modified doses. These are group results from the named setting. They cannot predict the benefit or side effects for an individual.

After surgery the aim is to reduce the chance of pancreatic cancer returning. If the cancer has spread, the aim is to control it. Ask which aim applies to you. Discuss alternatives, fertility, individual risks and supportive care with the treating team.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Fever, shivering or sudden illness: phone oncology immediately. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
