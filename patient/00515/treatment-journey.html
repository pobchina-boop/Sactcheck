Your treatment journey

Modified FOLFIRINOX for pancreatic cancer

After surgery the aim is to reduce the chance of pancreatic cancer returning. If the cancer has spread, the aim is to control it. Ask which aim applies to you.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · FOLFIRINOX infusion

Oxaliplatin is followed by folinic acid and irinotecan. Fluorouracil runs by pump over 46 hours. This modified schedule omits the short fluorouracil injection.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count, kidney and liver function. A DPD test checks how your body handles fluorouracil.Bring your medicine list and 24-hour oncology contact details.

Response or follow-upClinical review and imaging as arranged for your treatment setting.Ask when results will be discussed.

Ask the team about a new fever with jaundice, dark urine or shivering, especially if you have a bile duct stent.

At review visits

  • Which treatment setting applies to me? After surgery the aim is to reduce the chance of pancreatic cancer returning. If the cancer has spread, the aim is to control it. Ask which aim applies to you.
  • What needs urgent attention at home? Keep the pump line dry and unkinked. Phone for leaks, disconnection, swelling or a pump that empties early or late. Leave adjustments to your trained team.
