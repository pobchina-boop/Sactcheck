Which treatment this guide covers NCCP 00219a is first-line EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC; 00219b is switch-maintenance after stable disease following first-line chemotherapy; 00219c is after failure of at least one previous chemotherapy regimen. Global NCCP eligibility requires a validated activating EGFR mutation in each setting. These are advanced-disease settings, not adjuvant treatment.

Understand the treatment. Keep the plan manageable.

Erlotinib blocks abnormal EGFR growth signals in selected lung cancers. Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and delay progression.

EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor

Erlotinib

Blocks abnormal EGFR growth signals
Activating EGFR mutation identified↓EGFR signalling blocked↓Cancer growth slowed

Erlotinib blocks the EGFR tyrosine kinase signal that can drive growth in cancers with activating EGFR mutations. Effects on normal skin and bowel cells also contribute to side effects.

Report diarrhoea, rash, breathing changes and eye symptoms promptly.

Support throughout treatment

Diarrhoea and fluids

Keep the prescribed anti-diarrhoea plan available from the first dose. Report loose stools early and keep drinking if able. Persistent or severe diarrhoea, weakness, inability to drink or much less urine requires urgent advice. The team decides whether to hold treatment and when it is safe to restart.

Skin, nails and mouth

Use the agreed skin, nail and mouth-care plan. Report rash, painful nails or mouth sores early. Emollients and sun protection may help; the team decides whether creams, antibiotics or other treatment are needed. Blistering or peeling skin needs urgent assessment.

Nausea medicines

Erlotinib has minimal-to-low oral emetic risk. Agree any as-needed anti-sickness medicine with the team. Routine infusion premedication is not required.

Medicine interactions

Give pharmacy all medicines and supplements. Strong CYP3A4 inducers or inhibitors, grapefruit and medicines for stomach acid can change exposure. Avoid proton pump inhibitor combinations unless the specialist resolves the plan. Necessary antacids require source-specific separation; ask for written timing. Warfarin needs extra blood-clotting checks. Smoking reduces exposure; get cessation support and do not change your cancer dose yourself.

Pregnancy, fertility and breastfeeding

Avoid pregnancy. Use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 2 weeks after the final dose. Do not breastfeed during treatment or for at least 2 weeks after the final dose. Discuss fertility and future pregnancy plans before starting.

Your practical treatment guide

When to get help

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Call urgently for new or worsening breathlessness, cough or fever; severe diarrhoea, inability to drink or much less urine; severe or persistent abdominal pain; blistering or peeling skin; or yellow skin/eyes. Eye pain, light sensitivity or blurred vision needs prompt assessment. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or swelling of the tongue/throat needs emergency help. Use your unit’s temperature and 24-hour contact instructions.
