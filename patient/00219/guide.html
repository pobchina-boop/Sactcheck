Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

The usual dose is 150 mg by mouth once daily, continuously without a planned rest week. Use the dose on your prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; any change is agreed with your team.

Take at least 1 hour before or 2 hours after food. If a dose is missed, do not replace it; take the usual next dose. If you vomit within a few hours after taking it, do not repeat the dose. Ask pharmacy before changing smoking habits or adding medicines for stomach acid.

Checks and follow-up

NCCP specifies blood count, kidney and liver tests every 60 days. Diarrhoea or dehydration needs extra kidney and electrolyte tests, including potassium. Liver problems or interacting medicines can require closer tests. Scans follow the Consultant’s plan.

Support between reviews

Diarrhoea and fluids

Keep the prescribed anti-diarrhoea plan available from the first dose. Report loose stools early and keep drinking if able. Persistent or severe diarrhoea, weakness, inability to drink or much less urine requires urgent advice. The team decides whether to hold treatment and when it is safe to restart.

Skin, nails and mouth

Use the agreed skin, nail and mouth-care plan. Report rash, painful nails or mouth sores early. Emollients and sun protection may help; the team decides whether creams, antibiotics or other treatment are needed. Blistering or peeling skin needs urgent assessment.

Nausea medicines

Erlotinib has minimal-to-low oral emetic risk. Agree any as-needed anti-sickness medicine with the team. Routine infusion premedication is not required.

Medicine interactions

Give pharmacy all medicines and supplements. Strong CYP3A4 inducers or inhibitors, grapefruit and medicines for stomach acid can change exposure. Avoid proton pump inhibitor combinations unless the specialist resolves the plan. Necessary antacids require source-specific separation; ask for written timing. Warfarin needs extra blood-clotting checks. Smoking reduces exposure; get cessation support and do not change your cancer dose yourself.

Pregnancy, fertility and breastfeeding

Avoid pregnancy. Use effective contraception during treatment and for at least 2 weeks after the final dose. Do not breastfeed during treatment or for at least 2 weeks after the final dose. Discuss fertility and future pregnancy plans before starting.
