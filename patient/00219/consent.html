NCCP 00219 · version 6

Erlotinib monotherapy

The usual dose is 150 mg by mouth once daily, continuously without a planned rest week. Use the dose on your prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; any change is agreed with your team.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Erlotinib blocks abnormal EGFR growth signals in selected lung cancers. Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and delay progression. NCCP 00219a is first-line EGFR-mutated advanced NSCLC; 00219b is switch-maintenance after stable disease following first-line chemotherapy; 00219c is after failure of at least one previous chemotherapy regimen. Global NCCP eligibility requires a validated activating EGFR mutation in each setting. These are advanced-disease settings, not adjuvant treatment.

Material risks and support

  • Diarrhoea and dehydration
  • Skin, nail and mouth changes
  • Serious lung inflammation
  • Liver injury
  • Eye inflammation
  • Rare bowel perforation and serious skin reactions

Discuss the aim of advanced cancer control, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, bowel and skin effects, serious lung/liver problems, fertility and pregnancy, daily treatment, monitoring and your right to reconsider.

Monitoring and understanding

NCCP specifies blood count, kidney and liver tests every 60 days. Diarrhoea or dehydration needs extra kidney and electrolyte tests, including potassium. Liver problems or interacting medicines can require closer tests. Scans follow the Consultant’s plan.
