Which treatment this guide covers National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00219a is first-line EGFR-mutated advanced non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC); 00219b is switch-maintenance after stable disease following first-line chemotherapy; 00219c is after failure of at least one previous chemotherapy regimen. Global NCCP eligibility requires a validated activating EGFR mutation in each setting. These are advanced-disease settings, not adjuvant treatment.
NCCP 00219 · version 6

Erlotinib monotherapy

The usual dose is 150 mg by mouth once daily, continuously without a planned rest week. Use the dose on your prescription. Treatment continues while it helps and side effects remain acceptable; any change is agreed with your team.

1Confirm eligibility and the prescriptionBefore the first dose

2Early bowel and skin reviewFrom the first dose and early reviews

Early support can prevent dehydration and uncontrolled skin toxicity.

Ask / act: Record grade, duration, supportive treatment and recovery; apply medicine-specific thresholds.

Sources: NCCP 00219 · SMPC

3New respiratory symptomsAt any time

Possible interstitial lung disease (ILD) requires urgent investigation.

Ask / act: Arrange urgent assessment and an authorised medicine plan.
