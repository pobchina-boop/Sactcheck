Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before each visit

Confirm transport for Days 1–3, blood-test arrangements, prescriptions and the 24-hour contact details. Tell the team if you are ill before travelling.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Etoposide is infused over about one hour before cisplatin, which is infused over about one hour on Day 1. Extra IV fluid and electrolytes before/after cisplatin make this a longer visit. Etoposide alone is given on Days 2 and 3 unless the prescribed capsule alternative is selected. The whole visit also includes review, preparation, prevention medicines and observation.

STEP 3

Infusion access

Treatment may use a cannula or an existing suitable central line. Ask for line-care instructions if you have a line. There is no routine take-home infusion pump.

STEP 4

Capsules and home medicines

If prescribed, confirm your etoposide capsule doses, empty-stomach timing, safe handling and what to do if a dose is missed or vomited. Keep prevention and rescue medicines clearly labelled.

STEP 5

Support between visits

Arrange practical help if needed and keep symptom notes. Bring questions and a current medication list to review.
