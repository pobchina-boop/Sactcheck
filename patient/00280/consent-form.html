Treatment context
Cisplatin + Etoposide is chemotherapy used for extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer. It aims to control the cancer and its symptoms. Ask your oncology team what benefit is realistic for you and how this option compares with other suitable treatments.
Schedule
Cisplatin is planned on Day 1. Etoposide is planned on Days 1–3: usually all three days by infusion, or a prescribed alternative with an infusion on Day 1 and capsules twice daily on Days 2–3. The source plans 4–6 cycles, usually every 21 days. The team may delay, reduce or stop treatment according to your health and response.

Topoisomerase II chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
Hair loss and mouth soreness
Allergic or infusion reactions
Effects on fertility and harm to an unborn baby
Diarrhoea and liver-test changes
Low blood pressure during infusion
A later blood cancer (leukaemia)

Cisplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
Hair loss and mouth soreness
Allergic or infusion reactions
Effects on fertility and harm to an unborn baby
Kidney injury and electrolyte loss
Hearing changes or tinnitus, which may persist
Nerve symptoms, which may persist
