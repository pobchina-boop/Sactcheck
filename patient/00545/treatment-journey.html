What each check is for

1Before treatmentBefore the first dose

Adjuvant breast cancer · postmenopausal (natural or treatment-induced). Before starting: renal profile, magnesium, calcium and phosphate. Check creatinine before every infusion; compare it with baseline as well as reviewing creatinine clearance. Correct low calcium and review vitamin D.

Ask / act: Confirm eligibility, test results and medicine list.

Sources: NCCP 00545

2Other checksAt the specified review points

Complete a dental assessment and preventive care before treatment where appropriate. Report dental symptoms; discuss invasive dental work with oncology and dental teams. Hydration is individualised, especially with heart failure.

Ask / act: Confirm who arranges and reviews each check.

Sources: NCCP 00545

3Support and practical planAt each treatment or phase change

Review renal function, calcium, vitamin D, dental health and hydration before each infusion.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support plan; confirm active medicines.

Sources: NCCP 00545

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

After early-breast treatment, breast imaging and clinical review follow the agreed plan. Routine CT/PET scans or blood tumour markers are not used to search for distant recurrence in someone without symptoms or clinical concerns. Keep the agreed follow-up and report persistent or new symptoms between visits.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities.

Sources: EARLY
