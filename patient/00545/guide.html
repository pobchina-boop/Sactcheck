Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

Zoledronic acid by IV infusion once every six months for up to five years, unless progression or unacceptable toxicity changes the plan. The usual source dose is 4 mg, with renal review before prescribing.

The infusion itself runs over at least 15 minutes. Preparation, blood review and observation make the visit longer. A port or take-home infusion pump is not routinely required for this medicine alone.

Checks and follow-up

Before starting: renal profile, magnesium, calcium and phosphate. Check creatinine before every infusion; compare it with baseline as well as reviewing creatinine clearance. Correct low calcium and review vitamin D. Complete a dental assessment and preventive care before treatment where appropriate. Report dental symptoms; discuss invasive dental work with oncology and dental teams. Hydration is individualised, especially with heart failure.

Support between reviews

Calcium and vitamin D

Follow the prescribed daily supplements; the team checks calcium and adjusts them if it is high or low.

Dental care

Keep routine dental care and tell the dentist about treatment. Report jaw pain or a wound that does not heal.

Flu-like symptoms

Aches or fever can occur after infusion. Use the agreed symptom plan; seek advice if severe or persistent.

Hydration

Follow the team’s fluid advice; heart or kidney problems may change it.
