NCCP 00545 · version 3

Zoledronic acid · adjuvant breast treatment

Zoledronic acid by IV infusion once every six months for up to five years, unless progression or unacceptable toxicity changes the plan. The usual source dose is 4 mg, with renal review before prescribing.

Make treatment fit into daily life

The infusion itself runs over at least 15 minutes. Preparation, blood review and observation make the visit longer. A port or take-home infusion pump is not routinely required for this medicine alone.

Before an infusion

Blood results, dental issues, supplements and current medicines are reviewed. The clinic confirms whether treatment can proceed.

In the unit

A peripheral cannula is often sufficient. The infusion is at least 15 minutes; ask about the full visit length.

Between infusions

Continue the prescribed supplements and dental care. Six-month spacing does not mean new symptoms should wait until the next visit.

Planning completion

The source allows up to five years. Confirm the individual duration and continuing endocrine/bone-health plan.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
