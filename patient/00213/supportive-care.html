Additional phase-specific support

  • Antiemetic classification refers to the active treatment day. Reconcile duplicate steroid entries before prescribing.
  • No automatic G-CSF schedule is added; use the patient’s neutropenia history, risk and consultant plan.
  • Provide a written irinotecan delayed-diarrhoea rescue and escalation plan before treatment; distinguish it from immediate infusion-related cramps/sweating. Fever, severe pain, dehydration or diarrhoea not responding to the prescribed plan needs urgent review.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
