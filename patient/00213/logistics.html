Practical treatment guide

Infusion visits and care between treatments

Irinotecan is given on Day 1; no home fluorouracil pump. A repeating 21-day cycle. The number of cycles depends on benefit, side effects and your treatment plan.

STEP 1

Before treatment

Confirm tests, medicines and the exact schedule.

STEP 2

Prepare for the visit

Arrange transport if premedicines or symptoms affect driving.

STEP 3

At the day ward

Blood results, symptoms and venous access are checked.

STEP 4

Infusion and observation

Irinotecan usually runs over 90 minutes.

STEP 5

At home

Follow the skin-care and/or bowel plan. There is no pump to disconnect.

STEP 6

Next review

Confirm the next appointment and follow-up tests.

Do I need a port?

A peripheral cannula or existing central line may be used. A port is not automatically needed; the team considers your veins and overall treatment plan.

If the schedule changes

Ask the unit to confirm the next visit and which medicine is being changed. Do not copy dates from another regimen.
