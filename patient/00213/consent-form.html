Treatment context
Irinotecan alone is used for advanced bowel cancer after a fluorouracil-containing treatment has failed. Your team explains the aim, alternatives and how benefit will be reviewed.
Schedule
Irinotecan is given on Day 1; no home fluorouracil pump. A repeating 21-day cycle. The number of cycles depends on benefit, side effects and your treatment plan.
