1. Treatment and purpose

FOLFIRI · NCCP 00227 v9

Day 1: irinotecan, folinic acid and fluorouracil, then a 46-hour fluorouracil pump. A cycle lasts 14 days; your team confirms the next treatment.

2. Risks and supportive care

Irinotecan · Topoisomerase I chemotherapy

Tell the nurse immediately about cramps, sweating or diarrhoea during the infusion. Diarrhoea starting later needs the separate written treatment and contact plan.

Folinic acid · Chemotherapy modulator

The combined treatment can increase mouth soreness and diarrhoea. Report these promptly. Rare allergic reactions can occur.

Fluorouracil · Chemotherapy

Diarrhoea, mouth sores and skin soreness may become serious. Chest pain during the pump infusion needs emergency assessment. Unexpected severe early toxicity can occur even after DPD testing.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Confirm DPD testing, anti-sickness medicines, port/PICC care, the 46-hour pump and disconnection arrangements. Explain early cholinergic symptoms and the separate delayed-diarrhoea plan.

3. Understanding and next steps

Consent is a conversation and can be reconsidered. Tick boxes record discussion topics only; they do not establish capacity, consent or authorisation.
