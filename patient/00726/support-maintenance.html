Active medicines: Pertuzumab + Trastuzumab

Emetogenic risk: Minimal

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine prophylactic antiemetic, corticosteroid or G-CSF order is added for these antibodies alone. Review individual symptoms and previous infusion reactions.
  • Confirm the last administration date. A gap of 6 weeks or longer requires review of both antibody loading doses under the NCCP schedule.
  • Keep the existing cardiac-monitoring clock when moving to maintenance; review new breathlessness, swelling, fatigue or rapid weight gain.
  • Report diarrhoea early; agree hydration, symptom treatment and when to contact the unit.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
