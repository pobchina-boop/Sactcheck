What each check is for

1Heart ultrasoundNCCP: baseline and every 12 weeks during HER2 treatment

Checks heart function while treatment continues.

Ask / act: Ask when the next echo is due; a move to maintenance does not restart the clock.

Sources: NCCP 00726 · ESC

2Response imagingUsually every 2–4 months; earlier if clinically needed

A baseline scan records where the cancer is. Response is usually reviewed every 2–4 months, adjusted for the treatment, disease pace, symptoms and previous results. computed tomography (CT) chest/abdomen/pelvis is common when appropriate; the team chooses the imaging needed for the known sites.

Ask / act: Ask what scan is planned, when results will be discussed and who to contact for new symptoms.

Sources: METASTATIC

3Tumour markers, if usefulOnly if included in the individual monitoring plan

CA 15-3 or carcinoembryonic antigen (CEA) may be helpful in selected metastatic cases, especially if previously raised. They support the assessment with symptoms and imaging; a marker alone does not decide whether treatment is working or should change.

Ask / act: Ask what a change means alongside the scan and clinical review.

Sources: MARKERS

4Missed or delayed infusionWhenever a treatment gap occurs

A long gap can change the required antibody loading doses.

Ask / act: Contact the team to confirm the new prescription; do not assume the old visit plan still applies.

Sources: NCCP 00726

5Separate early-breast pathwaysAt every transition

This particular NCCP code is for metastatic/unresectable recurrent maintenance.

Ask / act: Ask the team to confirm the setting and aim on the written plan.

Sources: NCCP 00726
