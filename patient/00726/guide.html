Pertuzumab + trastuzumab maintenance

Pertuzumab and trastuzumab are given by infusion every 21 days after the chemotherapy phase of the metastatic treatment plan. Duration depends on benefit, tolerance and the agreed plan; this is not a fixed one-year adjuvant course.

NCCP 00726 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Pertuzumab

  • Diarrhoea and dehydration
  • Infusion / allergic reaction
  • Heart pumping-function reduction
  • Rash and fatigue

Trastuzumab

  • Heart pumping-function reduction / heart failure
  • Infusion or allergic reaction
  • Diarrhoea and fatigue
  • Rare serious lung effects

Make the plan practical

Infusion planning

Confirm the last treatment date with the team; long gaps can change the loading-dose plan.

Heart checks

Echoes continue on maintenance. Report new breathlessness, ankle swelling or rapid weight gain.

Bowel symptoms

Report diarrhoea early and follow the agreed hydration/support plan.

Response and daily life

Keep response-review appointments. Ask for support with fatigue, activity, sexual wellbeing and practical concerns.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
