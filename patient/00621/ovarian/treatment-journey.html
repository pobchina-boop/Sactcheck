Your treatment journey

Weekly paclitaxel for ovarian cancer · three visits per cycle

After platinum chemotherapy has stopped controlling metastatic ovarian cancer, weekly paclitaxel can shrink or slow the cancer and help relieve symptoms.

At review visits

  • What do I need to check before my next visit? Premedicines reduce the risk of an infusion reaction. Tell your nurse about any previous reaction and which medicines you took. Your team prescribes any changes.
  • When is my next treatment due? Paclitaxel over one hour on days 1, 8 and 15 of a 28-day cycle. Day 22 is a planned week off. The next cycle begins on day 29 if your team confirms it.
