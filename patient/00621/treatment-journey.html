The whole treatment journey

Paclitaxel · Days 1, 8 and 15 of 28: treatment and checks

Source-defined advanced gastric, breast, ovarian, small-cell lung or bladder indications

Paclitaxel 80 mg/m² on Days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Day 22 is a planned week off, not a fourth routine dose. Continue according to benefit and tolerance. The NCCP indications are off-label in Ireland and require the local consent / approval process.

2Benefit and continuationAt agreed clinical review points

Paclitaxel may slow or shrink metastatic breast cancer and help symptoms. This prescribed pattern includes a week without paclitaxel. There is no verified numerical survival estimate for this exact schedule in this review; results from a different weekly pattern do not predict your outcome.

Ask / act: Discuss benefit, toxicity and priorities before continuing or changing treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00621 · RETROSPECTIVE-TRIAL
