My treatment at a glance · NCCP 00621

Paclitaxel Days 1, 8 and 15 · relapsed or refractorySCLC

This treatment aims to control small-cell lung cancer that has returned or has not responded to previous treatment, and may help symptoms.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately for temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions.

Cannot reach the team? Seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Your treatment plan

One infusion each week for three weeks, then one week without treatment. This 28-day cycle repeats while helping and tolerated.

  1. Before startingAgree the treatment aim, alternatives, blood tests and support medicines.
  2. Three treatment visitsPaclitaxel on Days 1, 8 and 15, with blood tests and symptom review before each dose.
  3. One week offNo Day 22 infusion. Keep watching for symptoms and confirm the next cycle.

Before and between visits

  • Bring your medicine list and symptom record.
  • Report tingling, numbness or weakness before each dose.
  • Confirm premedication, blood tests and urgent contact numbers.
