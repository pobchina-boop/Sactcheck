Cemiplimab for cervical cancer after platinum chemotherapy

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Cemiplimab helps the immune system recognise cancer that has grown during or after platinum chemotherapy. The aim is to slow cancer growth and help you live longer.

Cemiplimab · Libtayo

PD-1 checkpoint immunotherapy. Helps immune cells recognise and attack cancer by blocking the PD-1 signal. This can also cause inflammation in healthy organs. Carry your immunotherapy alert card and contact oncology for new symptoms, even after treatment has stopped.

What the research found

In a study of 608 people, those assigned cemiplimab lived longer as a group than those assigned another chemotherapy medicine. The figures describe groups and cannot predict one person’s outcome.

Evidence applies to the post-platinum trial population. Benefit after progression on a prior PD-1 inhibitor is unestablished in this comparison.

Your infusion schedule

Cemiplimab · every 3 weeks

Cemiplimab (Libtayo) 350 mg is given into a vein over about 30 minutes.

Common effects and what to do

What you may notice

Tiredness, itchy skin or changes in bowel habit may occur. A persistent or worsening symptom needs review, even if it seems mild.

Serious effects: when to call

Lung inflammation · Cemiplimab

Phone urgently for new breathlessness or a persistent cough.

Bowel inflammation · Cemiplimab

Phone about new diarrhoea, cramps or blood in stools the day they occur.

Liver inflammation · Cemiplimab

Report yellow skin, dark urine or pain under the right ribs.

Kidney inflammation · Cemiplimab

Tell the team promptly about reduced urine or new swelling.

Hormone gland changes · Cemiplimab

Phone about unusual weakness, headache, dizziness, marked thirst or passing much more urine.

Skin inflammation · Cemiplimab

Report a spreading rash. Painful rash, blistering or peeling needs urgent help.

Heart, muscles or nerves

New chest pain, palpitations with weakness, difficulty swallowing or trouble lifting the eyelids need urgent assessment. Severe chest pain, collapse or severe breathing difficulty needs 112 or 999.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology team immediately about new breathlessness, persistent cough, new diarrhoea, blood in stools, severe weakness, yellow eyes or a severe headache. Symptoms can start after treatment ends. Carry your immunotherapy alert card. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Cemiplimab

Carry your immunotherapy alert card and contact oncology for new symptoms, even after treatment has stopped.

Immune alert card

Keep the hospital’s immunotherapy alert card with you. Show it at emergency visits, during admissions and after treatment has ended.

Early contact

Some new symptoms need blood or stool tests, scans or steroids. Contact oncology before starting leftover steroids or treating diarrhoea yourself.

Nausea support

There is usually no preventive anti-sickness treatment for this infusion alone. Contact your team about persistent vomiting.

Planning your visits

Appointments

One infusion every three weeks (21 days), while it is helping and side effects remain manageable.

At the unit

Cemiplimab (Libtayo) 350 mg is given into a vein over about 30 minutes.

After this part of treatment

Your specialist compares scans and symptoms over time. If you need a treatment pause for immune inflammation, you may need tests, steroids or hormone replacement.

Preparing for your oncology discussion

  • Do I need a positive PD-L1 tumour test for this treatment? The Irish cemiplimab indication after platinum does not require a minimum PD-L1 score. Your team checks your previous treatments and whether immunotherapy is suitable.
  • How will the team decide what happens next? Your specialist compares scans and symptoms over time. If you need a treatment pause for immune inflammation, you may need tests, steroids or hormone replacement.
  • I already had pembrolizumab. Can I change to cemiplimab? Tell your team why pembrolizumab stopped. If cancer grew on it for the same advanced disease, switching to cemiplimab is generally outside the Irish pathway. Intolerance can be assessed separately.
  • Could a new symptom still be from immunotherapy after my last infusion? Yes. Immune inflammation can start later. Keep your alert card and tell any doctor or nurse the medicine name and date of your last dose.

Can you show your immunotherapy alert card, name your next infusion date and say which new symptoms to phone about?
