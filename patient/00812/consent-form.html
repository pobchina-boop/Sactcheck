Treatment context
Cemiplimab helps immune cells act against advanced cutaneous squamous cell cancer when curative surgery or radiotherapy is not suitable. The team reviews cancer control, symptoms, side effects and your priorities to decide whether to continue.
Schedule
Cemiplimab is given by IV infusion on Day 1 every 21 days. The usual source dose is 350 mg over 30 minutes; the prescription and visit checks confirm your treatment. There is no fixed cycle total in this regimen: treatment continues while the team considers it beneficial and tolerable.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Cemiplimab

PD-1 checkpoint immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of bowel, lungs, liver or kidneys
Thyroid, pituitary, adrenal and glucose problems
Skin reactions and rare severe skin injury
Rare heart, muscle, neurological and eye inflammation
Infusion reactions
Some immune effects need long-term treatment
