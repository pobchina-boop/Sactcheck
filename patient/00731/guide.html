NCCP 00731 · version 2

TCHP · subcutaneous trastuzumab

Six 21-day cycles before surgery: IV pertuzumab, fixed-dose trastuzumab under the skin, IV docetaxel and IV carboplatin. Surgery and pathology guide the postoperative HER2 plan. This is not the combined Phesgo injection.

Source reconciled; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient review pending. Selected effects; not a complete medicine leaflet.

NCCP 00731 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Docetaxel

  • Low blood counts, infection and sepsis
  • Hair loss, mouth soreness and fatigue
  • Numbness / tingling; sometimes lasting
  • Fluid retention and nail changes
  • Allergic infusion reaction

Carboplatin

  • Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
  • Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
  • Allergic reaction, sometimes after previous exposure
  • Kidney or electrolyte changes

Trastuzumab

  • Heart pumping-function reduction / heart failure
  • Infusion or allergic reaction
  • Diarrhoea and fatigue
  • Rare serious lung effects

Pertuzumab

  • Diarrhoea and dehydration
  • Infusion / allergic reaction
  • Heart pumping-function reduction
  • Rash and fatigue

Make the plan practical

The correct phase prescription

Carboplatin AUC 6 uses the NCCP high-risk carboplatin row.

Premedicines and growth factors

Docetaxel dexamethasone is taken for three days starting the day before treatment. Ask the team to reconcile this with antiemetic steroids.

Tests and appointments

The team should name the booking owner, due window and person reviewing each required test. Tell them if an expected appointment has not arrived.

After chemotherapy

HER2-only continuation has its own minimal-emetic-risk plan; do not automatically continue chemotherapy steroids, antiemetics or growth factors.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
