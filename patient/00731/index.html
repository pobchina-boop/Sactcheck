Treat HER2-positive early breast cancer as part of a plan intended to reduce the risk of it returning.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Six 21-day cycles before surgery: intravenous (IV) pertuzumab, fixed-dose trastuzumab under the skin, IV docetaxel and IV carboplatin. Surgery and pathology guide the postoperative HER2 plan. This is not the combined Phesgo injection.
