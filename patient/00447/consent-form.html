Treatment context
Panitumumab + modified FOLFOX-6 is used for bowel cancer that has spread and has a confirmed wild-type RAS result. This NCCP regimen is a first-line option. Your team explains the aim, alternatives and how benefit will be reviewed.
Schedule
Day 1: panitumumab, oxaliplatin, folinic acid and fluorouracil, followed by a fluorouracil pump for about 46 hours; disconnection is arranged around Day 3. A cycle lasts 14 days. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable.
