Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Treatment phases and appointments

A 14-day cycle: panitumumab, oxaliplatin, folinic acid and fluorouracil start on Day 1, followed by a fluorouracil pump lasting about 46 hours. Arrange disconnection around Day 3. Continue according to benefit and tolerance.

STEP 2

Checks after treatment

Monthly electrolytes, magnesium and calcium for two months after the last panitumumab dose. Assess cumulative oxaliplatin neuropathy and cold sensitivity throughout treatment and after stopping; disease scans follow the consultant plan.

STEP 3

Access and practical support

Central venous access and a take-home fluorouracil pump with disconnect and emergency contacts. Cold precautions and persistent neuropathy review are specific to the oxaliplatin component.
