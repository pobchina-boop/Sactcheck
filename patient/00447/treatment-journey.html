The whole treatment journey

Panitumumab + modified FOLFOX-6: treatment and checks

First-line RAS wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer

A 14-day cycle: panitumumab, oxaliplatin, folinic acid and fluorouracil start on Day 1, followed by a fluorouracil pump lasting about 46 hours. Arrange disconnection around Day 3. Continue according to benefit and tolerance.
