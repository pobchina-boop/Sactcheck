Additional phase-specific support

  • NCIS multi-day principle: match prophylaxis to the drugs on each treatment day and continue appropriate cover for two days after.
  • Etoposide Days2–3 may be IV or an explicitly prescribed oral twice-daily alternative; no silent conversion.
  • Issue a single reconciled day-by-day plan; do not start a fresh carboplatin high-risk course on each etoposide-only day.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
