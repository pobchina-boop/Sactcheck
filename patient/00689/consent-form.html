Treatment context
To control extensive-stage small-cell lung cancer, relieve symptoms and help people live longer. The balance of benefit, side effects and time in treatment should be reviewed with you.
Schedule
Induction: atezolizumab and carboplatin on Day 1, with etoposide on Days 1–3, repeated every 21 days for up to four cycles. Maintenance: carboplatin and etoposide stop; atezolizumab alone continues every 21 days while appropriate. The fourth cycle ends about 12 weeks after starting if there are no delays; maintenance has no fixed total duration in this regimen.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Atezolizumab

Immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver, kidneys, hormone glands, skin, heart, muscles or nerves
Serious infusion or SC-related reactions
Some effects require prolonged treatment or lasting hormone replacement

Carboplatin

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection, anaemia and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
Allergic reactions; renal function affects dosing

Etoposide

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts and infection
Hair loss, mouth soreness, nausea and bowel changes
Infusion reactions or low blood pressure during administration
Possible later treatment-related leukaemia; discuss longer-term risks
