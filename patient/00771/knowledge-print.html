SACTCheck · NCCP 00771 v2 · Platinum-resistant ovarian cancer

Your treatment reference

Bevacizumab with weekly topotecan

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Slow the growth of recurrent cancer and help control symptoms.

Bevacizumab · Avastin or biosimilar

Antibody targeting VEGF. It reduces signals that help tumours form blood vessels. This can also affect blood pressure, kidneys and wound healing. Call urgently for severe abdominal pain, significant bleeding, a new severe headache or sudden weakness.

Topotecan · Hycamtin or generic

Chemotherapy. It blocks an enzyme cells need to copy DNA. Blood cells made in the bone marrow can also be affected. Call the oncology unit immediately for fever, chills, bleeding or new breathlessness.

Your infusion schedule

Days 1 and 15

Bevacizumab 10 mg/kg and topotecan 4 mg/m² on days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle.

Day 8

Topotecan 4 mg/m² alone on day 8. No planned infusion on day 22.

Common effects and what to do

Symptoms to record

Tiredness, appetite changes and nausea can affect everyday activities. Tell the team if these limit eating, drinking or walking.

Blood pressure

High blood pressure may have no symptoms. Keep your blood pressure and urine checks.

Serious effects: when to call

Blood pressure · Bevacizumab

Blood pressure can rise without symptoms. Keep your checks. Call urgently for a severe headache or vision changes.

Bowel injury · Bevacizumab

Call urgently for severe new tummy pain, swelling or fever. A tear or abnormal connection needs immediate care.

Protein in urine · Bevacizumab

A urine test can find protein before symptoms. Report new swelling or frothy urine.

Bleeding and healing · Bevacizumab

Report wounds that reopen. Heavy bleeding, coughing blood or sudden breathlessness need urgent help.

Infection and low counts · Chemotherapy

Call the unit immediately for fever, chills, heavy bleeding or suddenly feeling unwell.

New breathlessness · Topotecan

Phone urgently about new cough or breathlessness. Infection or inflammation needs assessment.

Sore mouth · Topotecan

Use a soft toothbrush. Contact the unit if swallowing or drinking becomes difficult.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the oncology unit now for fever, chills, severe abdominal pain, unexpected bleeding or new breathlessness. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Days 1 and 15

On this visit the unit checks blood pressure and urine protein. Tell them about bleeding or planned procedures.

Day 8

Your chemotherapy team will review blood counts and how you have been since the last visit.

Planning your visits

Plan your visits

Both on days 1 and 15; topotecan alone on day 8.

Before surgery or dental procedures

Tell your oncology team before arranging any procedure. Bevacizumab can affect healing.

Between visits

Keep your hospital number on your phone and card. Discuss fertility, contraception and breastfeeding before starting.

What the research found

Across the three chemotherapy choices, median time before progression was 6.7 months with bevacizumab and 3.4 months with chemotherapy alone.

These are pooled results. The study did not compare the three chemotherapy partners against each other or establish an overall survival advantage.
