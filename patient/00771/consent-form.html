Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Bevacizumab with weekly topotecan

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Slow the growth of recurrent cancer and help control symptoms. Platinum-resistant recurrent epithelial ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer. The NCCP lists up to two previous chemotherapy regimens and no prior bevacizumab or other VEGF-targeted treatment.

Treatment schedule. Bevacizumab 10 mg/kg and topotecan 4 mg/m² on days 1 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Topotecan 4 mg/m² alone on day 8. No planned infusion on day 22.

Treatment discussion and decision

Bevacizumab with weekly topotecan

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Across the three chemotherapy choices, median time before progression was 6.7 months with bevacizumab and 3.4 months with chemotherapy alone. These are pooled results. The study did not compare the three chemotherapy partners against each other or establish an overall survival advantage.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call the oncology unit now for fever, chills, severe abdominal pain, unexpected bleeding or new breathlessness. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
