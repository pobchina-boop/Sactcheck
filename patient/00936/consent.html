1. Treatment and purpose

Tucatinib + capecitabine + trastuzumab · NCCP 00936 v1

The capecitabine break does not mean a tucatinib break. Your team may hold or adjust one medicine without applying the same change to all three.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Tucatinib · HER2 tyrosine kinase inhibitor

Report diarrhoea early, especially with poor intake. Liver tests and a full medicines review are important.

Capecitabine · Oral chemotherapy

Stop tablets and contact the team for significant diarrhoea, painful mouth ulcers, hand-foot soreness affecting activities, fever or dehydration. Chest pain needs urgent assessment. Do not restart until instructed.

Trastuzumab · HER2-targeted antibody

Attend planned echoes. Report breathlessness, ankle swelling, rapid weight gain or infusion symptoms.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the active phase, supportive medicines, fertility / menopause, treatment alternatives, monitoring, urgent contacts and when the plan will be reviewed.
