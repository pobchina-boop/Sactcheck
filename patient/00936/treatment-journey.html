Tucatinib + capecitabine + trastuzumab: the whole pathway

Advanced / metastatic · previously treated HER2-positive breast cancer

Tucatinib twice daily every day; capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14 then off on Days 15–21; IV trastuzumab every 21 days. Continue while beneficial and tolerated, with clinician-approved changes.

What each check is for

1Blood tests and treatment reviewFBC, renal, liver; DPD testing before capecitabine → Extra tests if symptoms / toxicity → Pre-cycle bloods and symptom review → FBC / renal per cycle; liver at least every 3 weeks → Check dehydration, hepatic toxicity and interactions

Results may change whether a dose is given or held.

Ask / act: Confirm the booking and who reviews the result before treatment.

Sources: NCCP 00936

2Heart testsBaseline echo / LVEF → Report chest pain or breathlessness → Symptoms need assessment between visits → Repeat echo according to HER2 plan, commonly 12-weekly → Risk / abnormalities may shorten interval

ECG measures rhythm; echo measures pumping function. They are different checks.

Ask / act: Keep the planned appointments and report symptoms between them.

Sources: NCCP 00936 · ESC

3Imaging and markersBaseline disease sites; brain assessment as indicated → No scan required simply because Day 14 is reached → Imaging schedule independent of tablet break → Usually review response about every 2–4 months; individualise → Use consistent modalities; earlier imaging if concern

The purpose and frequency depend on early versus advanced disease.

Ask / act: Ask which test is next and what decision its result will inform.

Sources: METASTATIC · MARKERS

4Bone health with endocrine treatmentAt endocrine / ovarian-suppression planning; repeat by risk

Low oestrogen can affect bone strength.

Ask / act: Ask whether DEXA, fracture-risk assessment or bone protection is appropriate.

Sources: BONE · NOGG

5Who arranges and reviews each check?At initiation and every handover

A recommended test can be missed if responsibility is unclear.

Ask / act: Ask the team to document the booking owner, result reviewer, due window and escalation route.

Sources: NCCP 00936
