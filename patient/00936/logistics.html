Tucatinib + capecitabine + trastuzumab

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

Tucatinib twice daily every day; capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14 then off on Days 15–21; IV trastuzumab every 21 days. Continue while beneficial and tolerated, with clinician-approved changes.

STEP 1

Before dispensing

Confirm blood tests, interactions, the exact tablet strengths and the plan for missed doses.

STEP 2

Taking tablets

Tucatinib twice daily every day; capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14 then off on Days 15–21; IV trastuzumab every 21 days. Continue while beneficial and tolerated, with clinician-approved changes.

STEP 3

Review before restarting

The team confirms blood results, side effects and the next prescription. Never double a missed dose or restart held treatment without advice.

STEP 4

Trastuzumab visit

IV treatment every three weeks with staff observation. A cannula, PICC or port may be used; no routine take-home infusion pump.

Missed or vomited dose

Do not take an extra dose. Follow the medicine-specific instructions and ask the team if uncertain.

Safe handling

Swallow tablets whole, keep them in their original packaging away from children, and return unused tablets to pharmacy.

Practical arrangements

Confirm who supplies the tablets, who arranges tests, who reviews results and your urgent contact route.

Port insertion and removal

The line team explains placement, dressing care and when normal activities can resume. A port is accessed with a special needle during treatment. Removal of the implanted device is a separate planned procedure when no longer needed; do not remove or adjust it yourself.

PICC care and urgent line symptoms

A PICC usually needs regular dressing and flushing appointments even between treatments. Fever, redness, swelling, pain, leakage or damage requires prompt advice. Your hospital supplies the exact care instructions.
