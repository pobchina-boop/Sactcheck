Tucatinib + capecitabine + trastuzumab

The three medicines work together to control previously treated HER2-positive advanced breast cancer. Treatment is reviewed regularly for benefit, symptoms and side effects.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

Tucatinib twice daily every day; capecitabine twice daily on Days 1–14 then off on Days 15–21; trastuzumab every 21 days by intravenous (IV) infusion or a locally approved under-the-skin injection. Continue while beneficial and tolerated, with clinician-approved changes.

Elapsed-time tests remain due if a cycle is delayed. The treatment team books, reviews and acts on results. Calendar timing alone does not authorise a dose.

Between treatment visits

Planned treatment days are distinct from broad possible symptom patterns. Symptoms can occur outside these bands.

There are no guaranteed safe days. Fever, shivers, sudden illness, new breathing problems or severe symptoms need prompt advice on any day, including a treatment break.
