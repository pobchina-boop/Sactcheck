Dose-dense AC → weekly paclitaxel + 3-weekly trastuzumab: treatment and checks

HER2-positive neoadjuvant or adjuvant high-risk breast cancer

Four AC cycles 14 days apart, followed by paclitaxel weekly for 12 doses and intravenous (IV) trastuzumab every 21 days. Trastuzumab then continues to complete one year total.

What each check is for

1Baseline reviewBefore treatment

HER2-positive neoadjuvant or adjuvant high-risk breast cancer. Review lifetime anthracycline exposure and baseline cardiac risk before AC; reassess before starting trastuzumab. Assess cardiac function before trastuzumab and every 12 weeks during treatment. Keep the same monitoring clock when paclitaxel ends or trastuzumab changes schedule; arrange earlier review for symptoms or falling function. EMA product information recommends cardiac checks every 6 months after treatment until 24 months; following anthracyclines in early disease, yearly up to 5 years from the last trastuzumab dose or longer if function falls. Your team individualises this plan.

Ask / act: Confirm source eligibility and results.

Sources: NCCP 00745 · SMPC

2Treatment-day checkBefore each planned dose

Baseline blood tests and review before each chemotherapy administration according to the source and local plan. Once chemotherapy ends, testing follows trastuzumab and individual clinical needs.

Ask / act: Confirm active medicines and whether a dose is held.

Sources: NCCP 00745

3Support planAt every phase or day change

Four AC cycles 14 days apart, followed by paclitaxel weekly for 12 doses and IV trastuzumab every 21 days. Trastuzumab then continues to complete one year total.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support script; do not merge phases.

Sources: NCCP 00745

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

After early-breast treatment, breast imaging and clinical review follow the agreed plan. Routine computed tomography (CT)/positron emission tomography (PET) scans or blood tumour markers are not used to look for distant recurrence in a person without symptoms or clinical concerns.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities together.

Sources: NCCP 00745 · EARLY

At the next review, ask
