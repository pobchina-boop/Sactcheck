The whole treatment journey

Pembrolizumab + paclitaxel + carboplatin AUC6: treatment and checks

First-line metastatic squamous non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC)

Induction: all three medicines on Day 1 every 21 days for up to four cycles. Maintenance: paclitaxel and carboplatin stop; pembrolizumab alone may continue every 21 days while appropriate. The team agrees duration and review points.
