Additional phase-specific support

  • Use the dedicated carboplatin AUC≥4 high-risk NCIS row; it has no routine olanzapine.
  • Paclitaxel premedication prevents infusion reactions and must be reconciled with antiemetic dexamethasone.
  • Manage muscle/joint aches according to a prescribed analgesic plan; assess neuropathy before each taxane cycle.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
