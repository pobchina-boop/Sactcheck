Weekly paclitaxel with three-weekly carboplatin

The team chooses this weekly schedule to reduce recurrence risk after surgery or to treat advanced disease.

Treatment discussion

This schedule can be used after surgery for selected high-risk early ovarian cancers, or for advanced ovarian, fallopian tube or primary peritoneal cancer.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
Day 1Day 1Paclitaxel and carboplatin together.
Days 8 and 15Days 8 and 15Paclitaxel alone on both visits. Repeat the cycle every 21 days. The planned course is 3–6 cycles after surgery or six in advanced disease, if suitable.

Possible benefit, alternatives and your priorities

In the US randomised study, weekly paclitaxel did not significantly prolong time before cancer progression in the overall study population compared with three-weekly paclitaxel.

Bevacizumab use varied. This result does not predict which schedule will suit an individual.

Discuss the expected benefit, side effects, fertility, alternatives and symptom-directed care with the treating clinician. You may ask for more time or change your decision.

Other options discussed and what matters most to me

Important risks we discussed

  • Infusion reaction · Infused medicines — Tell the nurse immediately about flushing, wheeze or chest tightness during treatment.
  • Nerves · Paclitaxel — Report tingling, numb toes, poor balance or difficulty with buttons before the next dose.
  • Infection or bleeding · Chemotherapy — Fever, shivering, unexplained bleeding or suddenly feeling unwell? Call the oncology team now.
  • Kidneys · Carboplatin — Tell the team if you pass much less urine or cannot keep fluids down. Kidney checks guide treatment.
  • Nausea and vomiting · Chemotherapy — Use your prescribed anti-sickness plan. Call if vomiting continues or you cannot drink.
  • Hearing · Carboplatin — Report new ringing in your ears or reduced hearing.

Risks to discuss for each medicine

Paclitaxel

Nerve symptoms, Hair loss, Allergic reaction. Report numbness and tell the nurse immediately if you flush or wheeze during treatment.

Carboplatin

Low blood counts, Nausea, Infusion reaction. Report fever, bleeding, hearing changes or an infusion reaction.
