Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

Talazoparib is taken once daily continuously until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. The usual starting dose is 1 mg; kidney function, interactions and side effects can require a different prescription.

Swallow capsules whole with water, with or without food. Do not open or dissolve them. If a dose is missed or vomited, take the next prescribed dose at its usual time; do not take a replacement or double dose.

Checks and follow-up

Check FBC, kidney and liver function before starting and every four weeks. More frequent blood review may be needed for low counts; prolonged abnormalities require specialist assessment. Confirm the germline BRCA result and previous treatment. Review pregnancy status and contraception, kidney function and interacting medicines, particularly P-glycoprotein inhibitors.

Support between reviews

Blood counts

Keep four-weekly blood reviews and any additional tests. Fever or unusual bleeding needs prompt advice.

Taking capsules

Use the strength and number on your current prescription. Do not replace a missed or vomited dose.

Nausea

Use the prescribed rescue plan; call if vomiting affects fluids or tablets.
