NCCP 00605 · version 2

Talazoparib · breast monotherapy

Talazoparib is taken once daily continuously until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. The usual starting dose is 1 mg; kidney function, interactions and side effects can require a different prescription.

Make treatment fit into daily life

Swallow capsules whole with water, with or without food. Do not open or dissolve them. If a dose is missed or vomited, take the next prescribed dose at its usual time; do not take a replacement or double dose.

A daily routine

Choose a consistent daily time. Check capsule strength after every dose change and arrange supplies before they run out.

Reviews without routine infusion visits

No port or infusion pump is needed for talazoparib itself. Blood tests, clinic review and scans still need appointments.

A treatment hold

Do not restart automatically when a calendar month has passed. The team reviews recovery and the next dose.

After treatment

Report persistent unexplained fatigue, infections or bruising; rare bone-marrow disorders can require investigation.

Who arranges each check?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and whom to contact if an appointment or result is missing. This reference page does not book or record a test.
