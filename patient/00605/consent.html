Talazoparib · breast monotherapy

Talazoparib is taken once daily continuously until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. The usual starting dose is 1 mg; kidney function, interactions and side effects can require a different prescription.

Purpose, setting and alternatives

Control advanced cancer and symptoms while balancing side effects and treatment burden. Germline BRCA1/2-mutated, HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer

Material risks and support

  • Anaemia, low white cells and platelets
  • Fatigue, nausea and vomiting
  • Rare serious bone-marrow disorders including MDS/AML
  • Harm to an unborn baby

Discuss the exact setting, expected benefit, alternatives, medicine-specific risks, monitoring, contraception and urgent contact plan.

Monitoring and understanding

Check FBC, kidney and liver function before starting and every four weeks. More frequent blood review may be needed for low counts; prolonged abnormalities require specialist assessment. Confirm the germline BRCA result and previous treatment. Review pregnancy status and contraception, kidney function and interacting medicines, particularly P-glycoprotein inhibitors.
