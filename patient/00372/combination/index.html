My treatment

My overall treatment

Day 1: docetaxel drip, with no nintedanib. Days 2–21: nintedanib capsules twice daily. Repeat every 21 days for up to 6 cycles (about 18 weeks without delays); treatment may stop earlier. A later tablet-only phase needs a new team-confirmed plan.

  1. Combination phaseDay 1 docetaxel; Days 2–21 nintedanib twice daily. Up to 6 cycles.
  2. Possible tablet-only phaseOnly after at least 4 completed combination cycles and team approval: nintedanib every day in 28-day cycles. This change is not automatic.

More about my medicines

Docetaxel

Docetaxel prevents normal rearrangement of the cell structures needed for division. It affects cancer cells and can also suppress blood cells, irritate nerves and cause fluid retention.

Nintedanib

Nintedanib blocks several signals involved in the growth of blood vessels that support a tumour. Its effects can also cause diarrhoea, liver changes, bleeding and wound-healing problems.

Support at home

Dexamethasone before docetaxel

Take the prescribed steroid course from the day before docetaxel. If missed, contact the unit before attending; do not invent a catch-up dose.

Infection prevention

The team should consider preventive G-CSF injections and arrange blood counts around the low-count period. Fever needs immediate advice.

Capsules

Take nintedanib about 12 hours apart with food; swallow whole. Do not open, chew or crush. If missed, take the next scheduled dose only; never double up.

Diarrhoea plan

Have the prescribed anti-diarrhoeal and fluid plan ready. Contact oncology early for diarrhoea, poor intake or vomiting; do not wait 7 days to seek help.

Medicine review

Check all medicines and supplements with oncology/pharmacy. Nintedanib has P-gp interactions; docetaxel has CYP 3 A interactions, including grapefruit concerns.

Pregnancy prevention

Discuss fertility and effective contraception. Nintedanib requires contraception during treatment and for at least 3 months after; follow the longest advice for your complete regimen. Vomiting/diarrhoea can reduce pill absorption.
