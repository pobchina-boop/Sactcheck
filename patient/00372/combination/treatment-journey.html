The whole treatment journey

Nintedanib + docetaxel: treatment and checks

Adenocarcinoma NSCLC · combination phase

Day 1: docetaxel drip, with no nintedanib. Days 2–21: nintedanib capsules twice daily. Repeat every 21 days for up to 6 cycles (about 18 weeks without delays); treatment may stop earlier. A later tablet-only phase needs a new team-confirmed plan.
