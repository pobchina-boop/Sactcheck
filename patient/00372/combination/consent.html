1. Treatment and purpose

Nintedanib + docetaxel · NCCP 00372 v6

Day 1 docetaxel; Days 2–21 nintedanib twice daily. Up to 6 cycles. Only after at least 4 completed combination cycles and team approval: nintedanib every day in 28-day cycles. This change is not automatic.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Docetaxel · Taxane chemotherapy

Fever or feeling suddenly unwell needs urgent advice. Tell the nurse immediately about infusion symptoms or pain.

Nintedanib · Oral angiokinase inhibitor

Call early for diarrhoea or poor fluid intake; report bleeding, yellow eyes, dark urine, severe tummy pain or new neurological symptoms urgently.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the goal, alternatives, important risks for you, fertility/pregnancy prevention, practical support and what would prompt changing or stopping treatment.
