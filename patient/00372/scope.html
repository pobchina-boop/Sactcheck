Nintedanib + docetaxel

Day 1: docetaxel drip, with no nintedanib. Days 2–21: nintedanib capsules twice daily. Repeat every 21 days for up to 6 cycles (about 18 weeks without delays); treatment may stop earlier. A later tablet-only phase needs a new team-confirmed plan.

Nintedanib · continuation

Take the prescribed nintedanib dose twice daily every day. Reviews are organised in 28-day cycles; there is no planned tablet-free week. This phase starts only after at least 4 combination cycles and team approval.
