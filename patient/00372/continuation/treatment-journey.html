The whole treatment journey

Nintedanib · continuation: treatment and checks

Adenocarcinoma NSCLC · nintedanib-only continuation

Take the prescribed nintedanib dose twice daily every day. Reviews are organised in 28-day cycles; there is no planned tablet-free week. This phase starts only after at least 4 combination cycles and team approval.
