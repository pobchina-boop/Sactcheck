Additional phase-specific support

  • First paclitaxel dose: dexamethasone 8 mg IV, chlorphenamine 10 mg IV and famotidine 20 mg IV 30 minutes before treatment.
  • Day 8 and later: no routine dexamethasone unless the consultant specifies it for reaction/risk; chlorphenamine continues and any famotidine omission requires consultant/local-policy agreement.
  • Reconcile antiemetic and taxane dexamethasone into one prescription; never add duplicate steroid courses.
  • Four 21-day cycles: paclitaxel on Days 1, 8 and 15; carboplatin AUC 1.5 on Days 1, 8 and 15. Pembrolizumab 400 mg on Day 1 of cycles 1 and 3 only (every six weeks).
  • This is a weekly combination visit, not paclitaxel alone.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
