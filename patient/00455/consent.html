1. Treatment and purpose

Pembrolizumab 200 mg · every 3 weeks · lung cancer · NCCP 00455 v16

After complete surgery and platinum chemotherapy, the adjuvant course lasts up to 12 months. For metastatic cancer, treatment continues while it remains helpful and tolerable; the team may consider stopping after 24 months of sustained response without progression, or continuing when appropriate.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Pembrolizumab · Immunotherapy

Report new breathing, bowel, skin, hormone, heart or nerve symptoms promptly; these may be immune inflammation.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the aim for your exact lung setting, likely benefit, alternatives, important immune risks and how treatment may be paused or stopped. Some side effects need long-term medicines. Ask about fertility, pregnancy, breastfeeding and effects on daily life.
