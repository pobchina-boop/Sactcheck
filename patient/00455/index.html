SACTCheck · National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00455 · My treatment at a glance

Pembrolizumab 200 mg · every 3 weeks · lung cancer

For metastatic lung cancer, the aim is to control cancer and help people live longer. After surgery and platinum chemotherapy, the adjuvant aim is to reduce the chance of cancer returning. Ask which aim applies to you.

Lung cancer only: first-line metastatic non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) or adjuvant NSCLC after surgery and platinum chemotherapy · NCCP 00455 v16 · Document revised 9 November 2026

Between treatments

  • Report new symptoms promptly between visits and after treatment stops.
  • Follow the written steroid or hormone-replacement plan. Do not restart a held medicine yourself.
  • A permanent-stop decision still applies after symptoms improve; your team decides whether restarting is safe.

Bring your medicine list, alert card, test dates and actual urgent contacts.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
