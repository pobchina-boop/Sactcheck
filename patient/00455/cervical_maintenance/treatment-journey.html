Your treatment journey

Pembrolizumab maintenance every 3 weeks for cervical cancer

You have finished the chemotherapy part of your treatment. Continuing pembrolizumab helps your immune system keep the cervical cancer under control.

Your infusion schedule

Pembrolizumab maintenance · every 3 weeks

Pembrolizumab (Keytruda) 200 mg runs into a vein over about 30 minutes. There is no platinum or paclitaxel in this maintenance infusion.

At review visits

  • Will I still receive bevacizumab after chemotherapy? If bevacizumab was part of the earlier treatment, it may also continue. Ask the team to write down whether your maintenance prescription contains one medicine or two.
  • How will the team decide what happens next? Your team reviews scans, symptoms and side effects. At 24 months they may discuss stopping if benefit is sustained and there is no growth. Continuing is also possible when clinically appropriate.
  • Could a new symptom still be from immunotherapy after my last infusion? Yes. Immune inflammation can start later. Keep your alert card and tell any doctor or nurse the medicine name and date of your last dose.
  • Do I repeat the chemotherapy or start counting treatment months again? You are continuing pembrolizumab from the earlier combination. Tell the team the date of your last infusion and ask them to record the next date and review plan.
