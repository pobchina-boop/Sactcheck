Lung cancer

For non-small-cell lung cancer indications only. This patient suite covers the two lung indications described here. The shared clinical protocol also lists other cancers, which need their own patient information.

Colorectal cancer

Pembrolizumab 200 mg · colorectal

First-line metastatic MSI-H/dMMR colorectal cancer only. Other cancer types and treatment settings require their own plan.

For other indications, ask your team for matching information. Public reference; independent clinical review pending.

Advanced melanoma

Pembrolizumab · every 3 weeks · advanced melanoma

Unresectable or metastatic melanoma in adults; the team confirms the source-specific eligibility and previous treatment.

Melanoma after complete surgery

Pembrolizumab · every 3 weeks · adjuvant melanoma

Completely resected stage IIB/IIC or stage III cutaneous melanoma; the team confirms the applicable NCCP indication and timing.

Melanoma before and after surgery

Pembrolizumab · every 3 weeks · melanoma before and after surgery

Selected stage IIIB melanoma eligible for the NCCP adjuvant indication: the treating clinician may choose pembrolizumab 200 mg before and after surgery. This is a distinct, discretionary perioperative plan.
