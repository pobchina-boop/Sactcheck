Treatment context
Pembrolizumab helps immune cells act against melanoma. After complete surgery, its aim is to lower the risk of recurrence; this does not guarantee that melanoma will not return.
Schedule
Pembrolizumab every 3 weeks. Adjuvant treatment continues for up to 12 months, subject to safety review.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Pembrolizumab

PD-1 checkpoint immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of bowel, lungs, liver or kidneys
Thyroid, pituitary, adrenal and glucose problems
Skin reactions and rare severe skin injury
Rare heart, muscle, neurological and eye inflammation
Infusion reactions
Some immune effects need long-term treatment
