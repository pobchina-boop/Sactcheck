When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

More about my medicines

Pembrolizumab

Pembrolizumab is an antibody that blocks PD-1, a control switch on immune T cells. Cancer cells can use this switch to reduce immune attack. Blocking it can help the immune system recognise and attack cancer. The same increased immune activity can inflame healthy organs, which is why new symptoms need attention even after treatment ends.

Between treatments

These are possible patterns, not guaranteed symptom dates.

Day 1: Infusion and assessment

The team reviews blood results, symptoms and medicines. Tell the nurse immediately about itching, flushing, tight chest, dizziness or suddenly feeling unwell.

What to do: Confirm the next tests, treatment visit and contact plan.

Day 2–21: Between infusions

Immune effects may start at any time. A long interval between treatment visits is not a break from symptom reporting.

What to do: Keep thyroid tests every 3–6 weeks, including separate appointments if required.

Support at home

Routine medicines

Routine premedication is not usually required. Pembrolizumab has minimal emetogenic potential; the team individualises anti-sickness treatment if needed.

Steroids and hormone replacement

If immune effects develop, the team may prescribe steroids or hormones. Follow the exact plan; do not stop steroids abruptly or restart pembrolizumab yourself. If treatment is paused because of an immune side effect, your team checks recovery and any steroid or hormone plan before deciding whether it can restart. Do not change steroids or restart treatment yourself.

Medicines and pregnancy

Bring prescription, non-prescription and herbal medicines. Discuss pregnancy prevention; effective contraception is required during pembrolizumab and for at least four months after the last dose. Do not breastfeed during treatment.
