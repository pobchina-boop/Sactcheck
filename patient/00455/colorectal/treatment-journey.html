Pembrolizumab 200 mg · colorectal: treatment and checks

First-line metastatic MSI-H / dMMR colorectal cancer

Pembrolizumab 200 mg is given over about 30 minutes on Day 1 every 3 weeks (21 days). The visit takes longer for assessment and preparation.

What each check is for

1Pembrolizumab every 3 weeksOne immunotherapy medicine

Pembrolizumab aims to control bowel cancer that has spread and has a particular deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA)-repair feature, called MSI-H or dMMR. Your team discusses likely benefit, uncertainty and alternatives for you.

Ask / act: Confirm the current phase, prescription, tests and support medicines.
