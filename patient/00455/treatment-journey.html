The whole treatment journey

Pembrolizumab 200 mg · every 3 weeks · lung cancer: treatment and checks

Lung cancer only: first-line metastatic NSCLC or adjuvant NSCLC after surgery and platinum chemotherapy

Pembrolizumab 200 mg is given as a drip over about 30 minutes on Day 1 every 3 weeks (21 days). The hospital visit is longer because of checks and preparation. After complete surgery and platinum chemotherapy, the adjuvant course lasts up to 12 months. For metastatic cancer, treatment continues while it remains helpful and tolerable; the team may consider stopping after 24 months of sustained response without progression, or continuing when appropriate.
