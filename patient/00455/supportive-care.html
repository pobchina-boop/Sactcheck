Active medicines: pembrolizumab

Emetogenic risk: minimal

No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine antiemetic prophylaxis or routine infusion premedication is usually required. Use an individual/local symptom-relief prescription if needed.
  • Carry the immunotherapy alert card and report possible immune symptoms promptly during and after treatment.
  • Blood count, renal/liver profile and glucose before each cycle; thyroid every 3–6 weeks, cortisol if clinically indicated.
  • Effective contraception during treatment and at least 4 months after the last dose.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
