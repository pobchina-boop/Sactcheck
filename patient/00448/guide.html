Panitumumab + FOLFIRI

This combination aims to control bowel cancer that has spread and has a confirmed RAS wild-type result. The choice depends on previous treatment and your health.

RAS wild-type metastatic colorectal cancer; first-line or selected second-line · NCCP 00448 v5 · Document revised 4 November 2026

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

My overall treatment

A 14-day cycle: panitumumab, irinotecan, folinic acid and fluorouracil start on Day 1, followed by a fluorouracil pump for about 46 hours. Arrange disconnection around Day 3. Continue according to benefit and tolerance.

  1. A repeating 14-day cycleDay 1: panitumumab, irinotecan, folinic acid and fluorouracil, followed by a fluorouracil pump for about 46 hours; disconnection is arranged around Day 3. A cycle lasts 14 days. Treatment continues while it is helping and side effects remain acceptable.

Your treatment schedule

Day 1Day-ward medicines; pump connected.
Days 2–3Complete 46-hour pump; disconnect as arranged.
Days 4–14Recovery, symptoms and tests; next cycle only after team review.

Your team confirms the active phase and dates. Symptoms may occur during or after treatment; there are no guaranteed safe days.

Between treatments

  • No guaranteed safe days: report fever, sudden illness, diarrhoea or poor intake promptly.
  • Symptoms can continue after pump removal and after treatment ends.
  • Skin/eye symptoms and low magnesium can persist; keep the post-treatment blood-test appointments.

Confirm supportive medicines, tests, working urgent contacts and pump disconnection.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; a weekly-treatment stamp records that weekly dose. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
