Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine panitumumab premedication unless previous hypersensitivity/local policy indicates.
  • Written delayed-diarrhoea plan and urgent fever/dehydration advice; do not confuse with acute cholinergic management.
  • Skin care and electrolytes; monthly Mg/Ca/electrolytes for two months after last panitumumab.
  • Supply a written irinotecan delayed-diarrhoea rescue prescription: NCCP reference loperamide 4 mg initially, then 2 mg every 2 hours; continue for 12 hours after the last liquid stool, never longer than 48 hours at this high-dose schedule. Start advised electrolyte-containing fluids at the first liquid stool and contact the team early. Fever, dehydration, severe pain or persistent symptoms require urgent oncology review.
  • Irinotecan treatment-related diarrhoea must fully resolve before restart; a table-grade threshold alone does not clear the next dose.
  • If treatment is delayed for cytopenia, follow the source-specific weekly blood-count checks and maximum delay; assess the antibody and chemotherapy components separately when indicated.
  • Medication review: NCCP contraindicates concomitant brivudine, sorivudine and analogues with fluorouracil; review dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (a medicine-processing enzyme) (DPD)-affecting medicines, phenytoin toxicity/levels and warfarin international normalised ratio (blood-clotting test) (INR).
  • Review CYP3A inducers/inhibitors and grapefruit; avoid prochlorperazine on the same day as irinotecan. Gilbert syndrome warrants consideration of a reduced starting irinotecan dose; no universal percentage is supplied.
  • EMA Vectibix also requires interruption for intolerable dermatologic reactions; a grade below 3 does not automatically clear treatment.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
