Treatment context
Desmoid tumours do not spread to distant organs but can grow locally, cause pain and impair function. This treatment aims to control troublesome or progressive local disease and preserve daily function; it is not a metastatic-cancer survival treatment.
Schedule
Vinblastine 6 mg/m² and methotrexate 30 mg/m² are given on days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Day 22 is a planned break. National Cancer Control Programme describes 12 cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity requires a change.

Complete general risk discussion

Discuss low blood counts with serious infection or sepsis, anaemia and bleeding; nausea or vomiting; mouth soreness, appetite or taste changes, diarrhoea or constipation; fatigue, hair, nail and skin changes; changes in sexual wellbeing; allergic reactions and intravenous-line complications; blood clots, including clots in the legs or lungs; fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding effects; and the possibility of lasting organ injury, rare later cancers or death from serious treatment complications. The likelihood and severity differ between medicines and people. Use this general discussion together with every medicine-specific risk below.

General treatment discussion

Possible short-term, long-term, unexpected and unlisted effects; benefits are not guaranteed.
Severe complications can require hospital admission and can rarely be fatal.
Infection / sepsis, allergic or infusion reactions and leakage outside a vein (extravasation) should be discussed where applicable to the medicines and route.
Pregnancy, contraception, breastfeeding, sexual health and fertility preservation should be discussed where relevant.
Treatment can be delayed, changed or stopped if risks outweigh benefit. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Vinblastine

Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy
Low blood counts with serious infection, anaemia or bleeding
Constipation, sometimes severe bowel slowing or blockage; mouth soreness, nausea and abdominal or jaw pain
Numbness, weakness and nerve injury; hearing or balance injury can occur and may persist
Hair loss, fatigue and fertility effects; severe tissue injury if the medicine leaks outside the vein
Allergic reactions and pregnancy harm; incorrect non-intravenous administration can be fatal

Methotrexate

Antimetabolite chemotherapy
Low blood counts, serious infection or sepsis, anaemia and bleeding
Mouth or bowel inflammation and ulceration; nausea, vomiting and diarrhoea can cause dehydration
Liver injury, including longer-term damage; kidney injury or accumulation when clearance is impaired
Lung inflammation can occur at this dose; a persistent dry cough or breathlessness requires urgent review
Rare severe skin or neurological effects and abnormal growth of immune cells (lymphoproliferative disorders); fertility and pregnancy harm
Serious medicine interactions, including with some antibiotics and painkillers; the injected oncology schedule is not a daily take-home dose

Discuss realistic benefit, uncertainty, alternatives including observation or symptom-focused care when appropriate, serious and lasting risks, personal priorities and the option to decline or reconsider treatment. This injected regimen gives three weekly doses followed by a one-week break, for up to 12 cycles. Follow the written injection calendar; never substitute daily oral methotrexate. Kidney or liver problems, ulcers or new breathing symptoms require individual oncology/pharmacy review before further treatment. Desmoid treatment is outside the listed licensed indications of the examined vinblastine and methotrexate products. The national regimen supports this use; your team should explain the product-specific off-label rationale and document consent under local policy.
