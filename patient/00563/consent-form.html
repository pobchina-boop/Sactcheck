Treatment context
Encorafenib and binimetinib block two points in the BRAF-MEK growth pathway to help control eligible advanced melanoma. The team reviews response, symptoms and side effects to decide whether the treatment continues.
Schedule
Take encorafenib once daily and binimetinib twice daily, around 12 hours apart, continuously unless the team tells you to hold them. Treatment continues while beneficial and tolerable. A 28-day review calendar does not mean a routine break or a fixed total number of cycles.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Encorafenib

Oral BRAF inhibitor
Skin reactions and new skin tumours
Nausea, vomiting, fatigue and joint pain
Bleeding and eye inflammation
Heart-rhythm and liver changes
Harm to pregnancy and interactions

Binimetinib

Oral MEK inhibitor
Reduced heart pumping function
Retinal/eye changes
Raised CK and muscle injury
Liver changes, diarrhoea and rash
Lung inflammation, clots and bleeding
Harm to pregnancy
