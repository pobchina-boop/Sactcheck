Encorafenib and binimetinib for advanced BRAF-mutated melanoma

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Taking or receiving treatment

The usual method is to swallow the prescribed capsules/tablets whole with water, with or without food. If swallowing is difficult, ask the pharmacist for the source-permitted preparation: Braftovi capsules may be opened into about 20 mL of apple puree for immediate use, and Mektovi 15 mg tablets may be dispersed in about 10 mL of water, orange juice or apple juice, taken immediately, followed by a further 10 mL rinse. Do not assume this applies to other tablet strengths or improvise a preparation. Keep doses at consistent times. Avoid grapefruit and grapefruit juice, and have all medicines and supplements checked.

STEP 2

Missed, vomited or held treatment

For a missed encorafenib dose, take it only if more than 12 hours remain before the next dose. For binimetinib, do not take a missed dose if fewer than six hours remain before the next; ask the pharmacist if timing is uncertain. Do not double doses. If either medicine is vomited, do not take an extra dose; take the next scheduled dose unless the team advises otherwise.

STEP 3

Appointments and supply

Plan blood tests, travel and medicine collection in advance. If you need an interpreter or someone to help at home, tell the team before the appointment.

STEP 4

Paper copies and contact information

Ask for your latest schedule and contact details on paper. Replace old versions when treatment changes so everyone helping you uses the same plan.
