Treatment context
This chemotherapy combination aims to control extensive-disease small-cell lung cancer, ease symptoms and help people live longer. Expected benefit depends on the cancer, general health and treatment choices. Ask what the team hopes it will achieve for you.
Schedule
Carboplatin is planned into a vein on Day 1, before oral etoposide. Etoposide capsules are prescribed twice daily on Days 1, 2 and 3 only, on an empty stomach. The cycle repeats every 21 days while treatment is helping and side effects remain acceptable. Your team decides when to delay, change or stop; the source does not set a fixed number of cycles.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
Mouth soreness and diarrhoea
Kidney problems
Allergic or infusion reactions
Hearing changes or tinnitus, which can persist
Numbness or tingling, which can persist

Oral etoposide

Topoisomerase-II inhibitor chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea and mouth soreness
Hair loss and fatigue
Allergic reactions
Fertility effects
Rare serious skin reactions
Secondary leukaemia can occur
