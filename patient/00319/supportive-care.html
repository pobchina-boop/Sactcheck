NCCP 00319 · clinician draft

Carboplatin + Oral Etoposide

Which treatment this guide covers This guide covers carboplatin plus oral etoposide for extensive-disease small-cell lung cancer in people unsuitable for cisplatin-based treatment. It does not cover an intravenous etoposide schedule or an added immunotherapy medicine. If the prescription differs, ask for its matching guide and consent discussion.

Additional phase-specific support

  • Prescribe one clear multi-day schedule with stop days.
  • No automatic routine olanzapine, prolonged aprepitant, or copied five-day cisplatin schedule.

  • Regular premedication is not usually required unless previous hypersensitivity; no additional routine supportive medicines specified by00319.
