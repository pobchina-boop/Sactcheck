The whole treatment journey

Carboplatin + Oral Etoposide: treatment and checks

Extensive-disease small-cell lung cancer · carboplatin + oral etoposide

Carboplatin is planned into a vein on Day 1, before oral etoposide. Etoposide capsules are prescribed twice daily on Days 1, 2 and 3 only, on an empty stomach. The cycle repeats every 21 days while treatment is helping and side effects remain acceptable. Your team decides when to delay, change or stop; the source does not set a fixed number of cycles.

What each check is for

1Before the first cycleBefore treatment authorisation

Confirm disease, organ function and safe home dosing.

Ask / act: Confirm consent, capsule teaching and prevention plan.

Sources: NCCP 00319

2After Day 3After the last prescribed capsule doses

Avoid unintended continuation or replacement.

Ask / act: Record doses and follow support plan; contact team about omissions/vomiting.

Sources: NCCP 00319 · LEAFLET · ORALCARE

3Before each new cycleAfter recovery and review

Counts, organ function and tolerability may change.

Ask / act: Start another capsule course only after authorisation.

Sources: NCCP 00319

4Course reviewAccording to Consultant plan

Treatment should continue only while appropriate.

Ask / act: Discuss benefit, toxicity and next plan; return unused capsules.

Sources: NCCP 00319 · LEAFLET
