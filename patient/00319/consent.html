1. Treatment and purpose

Carboplatin + Oral Etoposide · NCCP 00319 v5

Carboplatin is planned into a vein on Day 1, before oral etoposide. Etoposide capsules are prescribed twice daily on Days 1, 2 and 3 only, on an empty stomach. The cycle repeats every 21 days while treatment is helping and side effects remain acceptable. Your team decides when to delay, change or stop; the source does not set a fixed number of cycles.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Carboplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report ringing in the ears, hearing changes, numbness or loss of function. Report infection or unusual bruising/bleeding promptly and describe any symptoms during the infusion.

Oral etoposide · Topoisomerase-II inhibitor chemotherapy

Follow the exact capsule schedule and record doses. Report missed/vomited doses, infection, bruising, mouth ulcers or severe bowel symptoms. Do not double or convert doses yourself.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the individual treatment aim and likely benefit, uncertainty, alternatives including not proceeding, common and serious risks, lasting hearing/nerve/kidney effects, fertility and pregnancy advice, safe capsule use, practical support and the possibility of dose delays or changes. Oral treatment is chemotherapy and needs the same careful safety review as an infusion.
