Treatment context
These two chemotherapy medicines aim to slow or shrink advanced non-small-cell lung cancer and help control symptoms. Benefit is not guaranteed. Your team explains the aim for your own situation.
Schedule
A cycle lasts 21 days (3 weeks). Day 1: gemcitabine and carboplatin. Day 8: gemcitabine alone after separate blood tests and review. Days 9–21: no scheduled chemotherapy. The usual planned course is 4–6 cycles, with earlier changes for progression or unacceptable effects.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Gemcitabine

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts and infection
Nausea, fatigue and flu-like symptoms
Skin rash and swelling
Rare serious lung or kidney injury

Carboplatin

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts and infection
Nausea and vomiting
Kidney, hearing and nerve effects
Allergic reactions

Follow one written anti-sickness prescription, including any tablets for home and rescue treatment. Day 8 gemcitabine has a separate lower-risk anti-sickness plan.Carboplatin needs a reliable kidney-function result for dosing. Report unusual bruising or bleeding promptly. Discuss potentially serious or fatal infection, organ injury, fertility effects and lasting nerve/hearing changes, as applicable. This guide covers standalone chemotherapy for advanced NSCLC. If nivolumab is part of your treatment before surgery, use the separate 00849 guide for gemcitabine/carboplatin; that is a different three-cycle plan. The urothelial cancer indication is separate and is not explained by this lung guide. The standalone lung indication is off-label in Ireland; your team should discuss this and follow local approval and consent policy.
