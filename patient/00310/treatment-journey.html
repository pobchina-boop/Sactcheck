The whole treatment journey

Gemcitabine and carboplatin · lung cancer: treatment and checks

Advanced non-small-cell lung cancer · standalone chemotherapy

A cycle lasts 21 days (3 weeks). Day 1: gemcitabine and carboplatin. Day 8: gemcitabine alone after separate blood tests and review. Days 9–21: no scheduled chemotherapy. The usual planned course is 4–6 cycles, with earlier changes for progression or unacceptable effects.
