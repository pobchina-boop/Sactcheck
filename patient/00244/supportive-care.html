Regorafenib (Stivarga) monotherapy

Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine premedication is usually required. Provide local hand–foot skin reaction prevention and treatment; examine pressure areas and report pain, blisters or impaired walking early.
  • Take the prescribed dose once daily after a light meal containing less than 30% fat, as clarified by current Stivarga SmPC; do not replace a vomited dose or double the next day.
  • Check liver tests every two weeks for the first two months, then monthly or as clinically indicated; abnormalities require the separate source-specific closer monitoring plan.
  • Check blood pressure weekly for the first six weeks, then before each cycle or as clinically indicated. Baseline thyroid testing; subsequent thyroid, lipase and amylase checks as clinically indicated.
  • Review strong CYP3A4/UGT interactions, bleeding, infection, ischaemia, wounds and planned surgery; close international normalised ratio (blood-clotting test) (INR) monitoring if taking warfarin.
  • Dose changes require consultant review. HFSR recovery, occurrence count and previous dose determine the pathway; permitted re-escalation is not a patient-led action.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
