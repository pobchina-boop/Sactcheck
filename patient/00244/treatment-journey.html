Some tests are required for everyone; electrocardiogram (ECG)/QT, pancreatic enzymes, international normalised ratio (blood-clotting test) (INR) and extra checks depend on risk, symptoms or other medicines. There is no single mandatory imaging or tumour-marker interval for all patients.

The whole treatment journey

Regorafenib (Stivarga) monotherapy: treatment and checks

Previously treated metastatic colorectal cancer

Take the prescribed dose once daily on Days 1–21, then take no regorafenib on Days 22–28. Each cycle lasts 28 days. Take tablets at the same time after a light, low-fat meal. Start another cycle only when the team authorises it.

What each check is for

121 tablet days, then seven rest days28-day cycle

Regorafenib is a targeted tablet treatment for colorectal cancer that has spread, after previous treatments or when those treatments are unsuitable. The aim is to control the cancer. Your team discusses the likely benefit, side effects, alternatives and your priorities.
