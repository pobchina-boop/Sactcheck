NCCP 00209 · practical support

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Oxaliplatin

  • Cold-triggered tingling / throat discomfort
  • Persistent sensory neuropathy, sometimes lasting after treatment
  • Allergic / infusion reaction
  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Nausea, diarrhoea and fatigue
  • Rare serious lung or blood complications

Folinic acid

  • Enhances fluorouracil activity and toxicity
  • Rare allergic reaction
  • Diarrhoea / mouth soreness in combination

Fluorouracil

  • Diarrhoea, dehydration and mouth ulcers
  • Low blood counts / infection
  • Hand-foot skin soreness
  • Cardiotoxicity including chest pain / ischaemia
  • Severe early toxicity including DPD-related toxicity
  • Pump / central-line complications

Make the plan practical

DPD testing before fluorouracil

DPD helps the body break down fluorouracil. Deficiency can cause severe toxicity. Testing guides the plan but cannot exclude all serious reactions; unexpectedly severe early diarrhoea, mouth sores or illness needs immediate review.

Cold precautions

Oxaliplatin can trigger cold-related nerve symptoms. Avoid cold drinks and direct cold exposure if advised. Do not use ice chips during this regimen. Throat tightness or breathing difficulty needs assessment; do not assume it is harmless cold sensitivity.

Pump and line care

Check the pump and line as taught. Keep the disconnection appointment and written spill instructions. Pain, swelling, redness, leakage, a wet dressing or a change in pump delivery needs contact with the team.

Anti-sickness support

Take prescribed preventive medicines and keep the breakthrough plan available. Some anti-sickness medicines cause constipation. Ask for review if symptoms are poorly controlled.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
