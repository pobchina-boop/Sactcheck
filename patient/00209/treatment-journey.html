What each check is for

1Confirm your personal planBefore starting

Modified FOLFOX-6 combines oxaliplatin, folinic acid and fluorouracil. It can be used after colon-cancer surgery or to treat advanced colorectal, stomach or gastro-oesophageal-junction cancer. The aim and duration depend on the indication; ask which applies to you.

Ask / act: Agree medicines, treatment days, duration and monitoring.
