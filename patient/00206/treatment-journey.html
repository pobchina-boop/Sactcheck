When your checks happen

Cycle examples assume no delays and count from the first dose of this schedule. If switching route or continuing previous HER2 treatment, preserve the existing echo due date. Tests are scheduled by elapsed time and clinical need; the team owns booking and result review. NCCP remains 12-weekly during treatment, including maintenance; a longer interval needs an explicit individual specialist plan. Metastatic T-DM1 continues according to benefit and tolerance; the early-breast 14-cycle course does not apply.

The whole treatment journey

Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1) · metastatic: the whole pathway

Previously treated HER2-positive unresectable locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer

T-DM1 3.6 mg/kg IV every 21 days, adjusted if required, until progression or unacceptable toxicity. This metastatic course has no fixed 14-cycle endpoint; the postoperative early-breast regimen is a different card.
