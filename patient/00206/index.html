How your medicines work

HER2-directed antibody with a chemotherapy payload

Trastuzumab emtansine (T-DM1)

HER2 targeting carries DM1 into cancer cells.
Attach to HER2→Enter the cell→Release DM1 to disrupt division

The antibody attaches to HER2. After the complex enters a cell, its DM1 payload disrupts structures needed for cell division. This is a single linked medicine, with its own dosing and safety rules.

Report bleeding, numbness, jaundice, new cough or breathlessness. Keep blood and heart checks.

Practical treatment guide

Your practical treatment plan

T-DM1 3.6 mg/kg IV every 21 days, adjusted if required, until progression or unacceptable toxicity. This metastatic course has no fixed 14-cycle endpoint; the postoperative early-breast regimen is a different card.

STEP 1

Before your visit

Confirm the dose, symptoms, required blood results and echo due window.

STEP 2

Access and administration

A cannula, PICC or implanted port may be used according to the treatment plan. A port insertion is a separate procedure.

STEP 3

Treatment and observation

First infusion over 90 minutes, then observe for at least 90 minutes. Later infusions may take 30 minutes if tolerated, with at least 30 minutes observation.

STEP 4

After the visit

Contact the unit to rearrange a missed dose; the next schedule preserves a 3-week interval. This medicine does not routinely require a take-home pump.

Port insertion and removal

The line team explains placement, dressing care and when normal activities can resume. A port is accessed with a special needle during treatment. Removal of the implanted device is a separate planned procedure when no longer needed; do not remove or adjust it yourself.

PICC care and urgent line symptoms

A PICC usually needs regular dressing and flushing appointments even between treatments. Fever, redness, swelling, pain, leakage or damage requires prompt advice. Your hospital supplies the exact care instructions.

Visit length

First infusion over 90 minutes, then observe for at least 90 minutes. Later infusions may take 30 minutes if tolerated, with at least 30 minutes observation.

Missed or delayed visit

Contact the unit to rearrange a missed dose; the next schedule preserves a 3-week interval.

Support around treatment

Support for this medicine

T-DM1 has low emetogenic risk; review the low-risk antiemetic draft and individual contraindications.

Blood and heart appointments

FBC, renal and liver profile before every cycle. Echo / MUGA every 12 weeks; around C5 for uninterrupted 21-day cycles.

Local treatment and endocrine therapy

Surgery and radiotherapy are coordinated by the MDT. When endocrine therapy is indicated, it has its own schedule and bone-health plan.

Pregnancy / contraception

Discuss pregnancy, fertility and contraception before treatment. Report pregnancy promptly; do not breastfeed during treatment or for 7 months afterwards. The team confirms the medicine-specific contraception duration.

Do not wait for the next appointment

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
