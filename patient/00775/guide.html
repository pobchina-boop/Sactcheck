TRAIN-2 · IV pertuzumab / trastuzumab

Six 21-day cycles before surgery: IV pertuzumab, trastuzumab, carboplatin and paclitaxel on Day 1; paclitaxel alone on Day 8. There is no planned Day 15 paclitaxel. Surgery and pathology guide the postoperative HER2 medicine.

Source reconciled; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending. Selected effects; not a complete medicine leaflet.

Your treatment, at home.

Important medicine-specific effects

Paclitaxel

  • Low blood counts and infection
  • Numbness / tingling, sometimes lasting
  • Hair loss, muscle or joint pain
  • Infusion reaction
  • Mouth, bowel, skin and nail effects

Carboplatin

  • Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
  • Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
  • Allergic reaction, sometimes after previous exposure
  • Kidney or electrolyte changes

Pertuzumab

  • Heart pumping-function reduction
  • Treatment reaction
  • Diarrhoea and rash
  • Fatigue

Trastuzumab

  • Heart pumping-function reduction
  • Treatment reaction
  • Diarrhoea and rash
  • Fatigue

Make the plan practical

The correct supportive prescription

Follow the script for the active medicine, route and treatment day. Do not carry chemotherapy anti-sickness medicines automatically into an antibody-only phase.

Tests before treatment

FBC, kidney and liver profile at baseline and before every 21-day cycle. The team reviews the necessary results before a planned dose. Confirm the blood-count check needed before each additional vinorelbine or paclitaxel dose; a Day 1 result does not automatically authorise a later dose.

Keep heart checks on the plan

An echo every 12 weeks is separate from blood tests or cancer imaging. Ask who books and reviews it.

Plan for symptoms

Keep your agreed contact numbers and instructions. New symptoms can need assessment before a routine appointment.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain needs emergency help.
Contact oncology immediately for temperature ≥37.5°C or ≤36°C, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your own hospital’s instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment.
