1. Treatment and purpose

MVAC · 28-day schedule · NCCP 00338 v5

Methotrexate is given on days 1, 15 and 22; vinblastine on days 2, 15 and 22; doxorubicin and cisplatin on day 2. The cycle repeats every 28 days. NCCP specifies three cycles before surgery, four after surgery, or up to six in metastatic disease; your team records which applies.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Methotrexate · Cytotoxic chemotherapy

Tell the pharmacist about all painkillers, antibiotics, supplements and other medicines because interactions can increase methotrexate toxicity. Report new cough or breathlessness, mouth ulcers, unusual bleeding, jaundice, reduced urine or severe rash promptly.

Vinblastine · Cytotoxic chemotherapy

Report constipation, abdominal swelling, numbness or pain around the drip.

Doxorubicin · Cytotoxic chemotherapy

Follow the unit’s cisplatin hydration plan and individual fluid target. The source advises 2–3 litres on Day 1 to reduce bladder irritation from cyclophosphamide; the team must adapt this if heart or kidney problems restrict fluids. Report blood in urine or painful urination promptly.

Cisplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Report ringing in the ears, hearing changes, numbness or loss of function. Tell the team if you cannot drink, are passing less urine or become swollen or breathless during fluids.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the intended benefit, alternatives, common and serious risks, lasting effects, fertility and individual priorities. Record the patient’s questions and the agreed plan. This is conventional 28-day MVAC, not the 14-day granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF)-supported schedule. Do not copy its cycle count, G-CSF timing or day-15/day-22 appointments into another regimen.
