The whole treatment journey

Fluorouracil + folinic acid every 14 days: treatment and checks

Adjuvant or advanced colorectal cancer

Day 1: folinic acid and fluorouracil injection, followed by a 46-hour fluorouracil pump. Repeat every 14 days. Adjuvant treatment: up to 12 cycles; advanced treatment: continue only while appropriate.
