Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before treatment

Confirm the treatment aim, duration, dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (a medicine-processing enzyme) (DPD) result, blood tests, prescription and contact numbers.

STEP 2

Visits and access

Day 1: folinic acid and fluorouracil injection, followed by a 46-hour fluorouracil pump. Repeat every 14 days. Adjuvant treatment: up to 12 cycles; advanced treatment: continue only while appropriate. Confirm line access, pump changes and disconnection arrangements.

STEP 3

If treatment is delayed

Ask for revised written treatment and support-medicine instructions. Do not make up missed doses or carry an old plan into a new phase.

STEP 4

Getting help

Keep the wallet card and medicine list available. Contact the team promptly for infection, dehydration, chest symptoms or significant toxicity.
