Treatment context
Control advanced cancer and symptoms, with regular review of benefit and side effects.
Schedule
Carboplatin AUC2 is given first, followed by gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m², on BOTH Days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Carboplatin

Platinum chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting and fatigue
Allergic reaction, sometimes after previous exposure
Kidney or electrolyte changes

Gemcitabine

Antimetabolite chemotherapy
Low blood counts, infection and bleeding
Flu-like symptoms, fatigue, rash and swelling
Uncommon serious lung or liver injury
Rare haemolytic uraemic syndrome and kidney injury
