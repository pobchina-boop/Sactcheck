Gemcitabine + carboplatin AUC2: treatment and checks

Locally recurrent or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer

Carboplatin AUC2 is given first, followed by gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m², on BOTH Days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

What each check is for

1Baseline reviewBefore treatment

Locally recurrent or metastatic triple-negative breast cancer. There is no routine three-month echo schedule for CMF or gemcitabine alone. Review prior treatment and cardiac risk; new chest pain or breathing symptoms need urgent assessment.

Ask / act: Confirm source eligibility and results.

Sources: NCCP 00430

2Treatment-day checkBefore each planned dose

Baseline and Day 1: full blood count, kidney and liver tests. Day 8: full blood count. Review kidney-function dosing and whether either medicine is held before each administration.

Ask / act: Confirm active medicines and whether a dose is held.

Sources: NCCP 00430

3Support planAt every phase or day change

Carboplatin AUC2 is given first, followed by gemcitabine 1,000 mg/m², on BOTH Days 1 and 8 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Ask / act: Choose the matching support script; do not merge phases.

Sources: NCCP 00430

4Response and follow-upAgreed review points

For metastatic disease, record the disease sites and agree the scan type and review interval before treatment. Review response, symptoms and toxicity together; worsening symptoms may need earlier assessment.

Ask / act: Review symptoms, results and patient priorities together.

Sources: NCCP 00430 · METASTATIC · MARKERS

At the next review, ask

  • Which treatment phase am I in, and what happens next?
  • Which test is expected next, who arranges it, and who explains the result?
  • Who should I contact if an expected appointment has not arrived?
