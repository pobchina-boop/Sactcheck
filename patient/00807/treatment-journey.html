The whole treatment journey

BCG bladder treatment: treatment and checks

Non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer

BCG is placed in the bladder once weekly for six weeks. Maintenance uses three weekly instillations at months 3,6 and12 for intermediate-risk disease; high/very-high-risk plans may add months 18,24,30 and36. Your urologist confirms the course according to risk, response, tolerance and supply.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewSix weekly induction treatments · then risk-specific maintenance

Reduce the risk of bladder cancer returning or worsening, and treat suitable carcinoma in situ, using local immune treatment.

Ask / act: Contact your urology or oncology treatment team immediately for fever, chills, sudden illness, severe bladder pain, persistent bleeding or inability to pass urine. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse or chest pain: call112/999.
