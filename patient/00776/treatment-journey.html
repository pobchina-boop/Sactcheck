What each check is for

1Blood testsBefore the first dose: full blood count, kidney and liver tests, an echocardiogram or MUGA scan to measure heart pumping function, and a pregnancy test where relevant. The team checks the HER2 result, previous treatment, lung history and current breathing symptoms, and agrees how disease response will be assessed. Blood count, kidney and liver tests before each 21-day cycle. Review breathing, fatigue, nausea, intake and other symptoms at each visit. Repeat cardiac imaging every 12 weeks; assess new breathing symptoms immediately rather than waiting for the next planned scan.

Checks safety before continuation.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

2Heart checksNCCP specifies echo/MUGA before treatment and every 12 weeks. With uninterrupted three-week cycles, week 12 is approximately before cycle 5 and week 24 before cycle 9. Delays do not reset the elapsed-time monitoring clock. Signs of cardiac impairment can require 4–8-weekly checks; an abnormal result meeting the treatment-hold criteria requires repeat assessment within three weeks.

Checks cardiac safety when the agent or clinical risk requires it.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC

3ImagingResponse assessment in advanced breast cancer is generally every two to four months, adapted to disease dynamics, sites, symptoms and treatment. Less frequent monitoring may suit indolent disease. New symptoms or suspected progression need earlier assessment. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not an automatic three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

Uses the correct surveillance or response-assessment pathway.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: ESMO_MBC2023

4Bone healthEnhertu alone does not require a routine bone-density scan schedule. DEXA, bone medicines or bone scans are added for another treatment, fracture risk, symptoms or disease assessment when indicated.

Companion endocrine therapy and individual fracture risk may change the plan.

Ask / act: Agree the due window, booking team and result reviewer. Contact the team if a test is overdue.

Sources: NCCP · SMPC
