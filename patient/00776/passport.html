NCCP 00776 · version 3b

Trastuzumab deruxtecan (Enhertu) · every 3 weeks

One infusion on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle. The next infusion is normally three weeks later. A delayed appointment is rescheduled by the team; patients do not calculate catch-up doses.

Your treatment and review passport

Bring this to reviews. Record symptoms, treatment changes and questions when helpful. You do not need to complete a daily diary or calculate a toxicity grade.

The four sheets are reusable review periods, not a prescribed number of cycles. Follow the treatment and rest days agreed with your team; do not calculate a restart from this sheet.

These patient-language prompts are not a validated questionnaire or a formal CTCAE assessment. Urgent symptoms override the form.

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Contact the oncology team urgently for any new or worsening cough, breathlessness or fever: lung inflammation can start at any point and can become serious. Do not wait until the next infusion or scan. Also report fever or feeling acutely unwell, unusual bleeding, inability to keep fluids down, chest pain or new ankle swelling. Severe breathing difficulty or collapse needs emergency care.
