Enhertu (trastuzumab deruxtecan)

Your symptoms, cycle by cycle

Use one sheet for each prescribed 21-day cycle and bring it to review. Print extra copies as needed. Four blank sheets do not define a four-cycle course.

One infusion on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle. The next infusion is normally three weeks later. A delayed appointment is rescheduled by the team; patients do not calculate catch-up doses.

How to record symptoms

Tick the worst level since the previous review, add dates and explain what the symptom stopped you doing. Mild: noticeable but usual activities remain possible. Moderate: affects activities or sleep; contact your team. Severe: substantially limits daily activities, self-care or intake; seek urgent advice.

These patient ratings are not formal Common Terminology Criteria for Adverse Events (CTCAE) grades. The clinician confirms the problem and its event-specific grade, then records the decision. Red flags override every rating box.

Do not wait for a diary or appointment: new cough, breathlessness or fever needs immediate oncology advice. Report unusual bleeding, ongoing vomiting or poor fluid intake. Severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain or collapse: 112 / 999.

Keep your official Enhertu alert card too. Store completed sheets securely. This printed passport does not send your information to SACTCheck.
