How this medicine supports your treatment

Aim to control cancer growth, reduce cancer-related symptoms and prolong disease control. Individual benefit and duration vary; trial averages cannot predict one person's outcome.

HER2-directed antibody–drug conjugate

Trastuzumab deruxtecan (Enhertu)

Uses HER2 as a docking target to deliver a topoisomerase-I inhibitor payload
Antibody attaches to HER2↓Cancer cell takes the linked drug inside↓Released payload damages deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA) during cell division

Enhertu links an antibody that recognises HER2 with deruxtecan, a cell-killing medicine. After uptake into the cell, the payload inhibits topoisomerase I and interferes with DNA replication. The released payload can also affect nearby tumour cells. This targeting does not spare every healthy cell, which is why blood counts, nausea, lungs and heart function still need attention.

New cough, breathlessness or fever needs urgent contact even between cycles. Keep the 12-weekly heart-scan plan and use the anti-sickness medicines as prescribed.

The exact treatment being discussed

Aim to control cancer growth, reduce cancer-related symptoms and prolong disease control. Individual benefit and duration vary; trial averages cannot predict one person's outcome.

Who this covers

NCCP00776a: unresectable/metastatic HER2-positive breast cancer after at least one anti-HER2 regimen. NCCP00776b: unresectable/metastatic HER2-low breast cancer after chemotherapy for metastatic disease, or recurrence during or within six months after adjuvant chemotherapy.

NCCP 00776 v3b · official regimen · EMA Enhertu product information · retrieved 25 October 2026

Dose and administration

Usual starting dose 5.4 mg/kg intravenously on Day 1 every 21 days; prescribed reductions are 4.4 then 3.2 mg/kg. The first infusion takes 90 minutes. Later infusions may take 30 minutes if the previous infusion was tolerated. Allow extra time for assessment, anti-sickness medicines and any observation. A cannula or an existing central line can be used according to the clinical plan; there is no home pump.

NCCP 00776 v3b · official regimen · EMA Enhertu product information · retrieved 25 October 2026

Duration and decisions

Treatment continues while it controls the cancer and side effects remain acceptable. This advanced-disease regimen has no automatic one-year completion date. A good scan does not by itself instruct a treatment break.

NCCP 00776 v3b · official regimen · EMA Enhertu product information · retrieved 25 October 2026

Individual safety and source differences

Enhertu is different from trastuzumab and trastuzumab emtansine (Kadcyla); they are not interchangeable. Carry the supplied Enhertu lung-safety alert card. The team may pause treatment while investigating suspected lung inflammation; symptomatic drug-related ILD requires permanent discontinuation under the protocol.

NCCP specifies echo/MUGA before treatment and every 12 weeks. With uninterrupted three-week cycles, week 12 is approximately before cycle 5 and week 24 before cycle 9. Your wider treatment plan

Additional treatments depend on the confirmed setting and indication.

Advanced-disease scans

Response assessment in advanced breast cancer is generally every two to four months, adapted to disease dynamics, sites, symptoms and treatment. Less frequent monitoring may suit indolent disease. New symptoms or suspected progression need earlier assessment. The team chooses the imaging method; this is not an automatic three-month CT chest/abdomen/pelvis order.

Pan-Asian adapted ESMO metastatic breast cancer guidelines

Tumour markers

If informative, particularly when elevated at baseline or disease is not readily measurable, tumour markers may supplement clinical assessment and imaging. A marker rise alone should not trigger a treatment change. There is no universal fixed marker timetable.

ESO-ESMO ABC 5 consensus: tumour-marker interpretation

Pregnancy, fertility and contraception

Where relevant, use effective contraception during treatment and for at least seven months after the last dose for women, or four months for men with a partner who could become pregnant. Do not breastfeed during treatment and for seven months after the last dose. Discuss fertility before starting.

Evidence depends on the population

Different studies answer different questions; do not compare their results as if populations and follow-up were identical.

DESTINY-Breast04: previously treated HER2-low advanced breast cancer

557 patients with HER2-low metastatic breast cancer after one or two previous chemotherapy lines; 494 had hormone-receptor-positive disease. T-DXd versus physician's-choice chemotherapy. Blinded-central-review PFS in the hormone-receptor-positive cohort; PFS and OS in all patients were key secondary endpoints. In the HR-positive cohort, median PFS was 10.1 versus 5.4 months (HR 0.51). In all patients, median PFS was 9.9 versus 5.1 months (HR 0.50) and median OS 23.4 versus 16.8 months (HR 0.64). These are the original 2022 analysis, also reproduced in EMA SmPC §5.1. The HR-negative subgroup was much smaller. The trial does not establish benefit for HER2 IHC0 tumours or every earlier treatment setting. Drug-related ILD/pneumonitis occurred, including fatal cases; benefit must be discussed alongside pulmonary risk.

Practical support

Prevent nausea before it starts

This regimen has high emetic risk. An antiemetic combination is prescribed before every infusion, with an agreed plan for delayed symptoms and breakthrough nausea. The dedicated NCCP trastuzumab-deruxtecan pathway should be checked by the prescriber and pharmacist.

Lung-safety plan

Carry the patient alert card and report new cough, fever or breathlessness promptly. Steroids used for suspected lung inflammation are prescribed and monitored by the clinical team; do not start them yourself from this information.

Blood-count support

Low blood counts can cause infection, anaemia or bleeding. The need for granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF) or transfusion support depends on results, symptoms and previous complications; it is not automatic for every cycle.

Eating and energy

Small frequent meals and early treatment of nausea can help maintain intake. Tell the team about weight loss, constipation or diarrhoea so that support can be tailored.
