Trastuzumab deruxtecan (Enhertu) · every 3 weeks

Aim to control cancer growth, reduce cancer-related symptoms and prolong disease control. Individual benefit and duration vary; trial averages cannot predict one person's outcome.

This shows treatment timing, not guaranteed symptom-free days. Your team confirms the active phase and dates.

One infusion on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle. The next infusion is normally three weeks later. A delayed appointment is rescheduled by the team; patients do not calculate catch-up doses.

Tests and checks

Before the first dose: full blood count, kidney and liver tests, an echocardiogram or multigated acquisition (heart pumping) scan (MUGA) scan to measure heart pumping function, and a pregnancy test where relevant. The team checks the HER2 result, previous treatment, lung history and current breathing symptoms, and agrees how disease response will be assessed. Blood count, kidney and liver tests before each 21-day cycle. Review breathing, fatigue, nausea, intake and other symptoms at each visit. Repeat cardiac imaging every 12 weeks; assess new breathing symptoms immediately rather than waiting for the next planned scan.

Between treatment visits

Treatment days are specified by the regimen. Symptom timing varies; the full-cycle symptom bands show that an effect can occur at any point, not a measured probability or severity.

Contact the oncology team urgently for any new or worsening cough, breathlessness or fever: lung inflammation can start at any point and can become serious. Do not wait until the next infusion or scan. Also report fever or feeling acutely unwell, unusual bleeding, inability to keep fluids down, chest pain or new ankle swelling. Severe breathing difficulty or collapse needs emergency care.
