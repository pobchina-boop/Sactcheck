Additional phase-specific support

  • This regimen has high emetic risk. An antiemetic combination is prescribed before every infusion, with an agreed plan for delayed symptoms and breakthrough nausea. The dedicated NCCP trastuzumab-deruxtecan pathway should be checked by the prescriber and pharmacist.
  • Carry the patient alert card and report new cough, fever or breathlessness promptly. Steroids used for suspected lung inflammation are prescribed and monitored by the clinical team; do not start them yourself from this information.
  • Low blood counts can cause infection, anaemia or bleeding. The need for G-CSF or transfusion support depends on results, symptoms and previous complications; it is not automatic for every cycle.
  • Small frequent meals and early treatment of nausea can help maintain intake. Tell the team about weight loss, constipation or diarrhoea so that support can be tailored.
  • NCCP NCIS v8 Table1 has a dedicated trastuzumab-deruxtecan row: NK1 + 5-HT3 + dexamethasone. Olanzapine can be considered by the prescriber, but is not silently added to that national default row.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
