The whole treatment journey

Radium-223 (Xofigo): treatment and checks

Symptomatic bone-metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer without known visceral metastases

Radium-223 is given as a slow injection into a vein every 28 days, for a maximum of six injections. It is administered by a specialist service authorised to use radioactive medicines.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewInjection every 28 days; maximum six

Treat prostate cancer in affected bones, help control symptoms and delay complications in the appropriate disease setting.

Ask / act: Call your oncology team immediately for fever, shaking chills, feeling suddenly unwell, unusual bleeding or persistent vomiting/diarrhoea. Follow your own temperature and contact plan. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or new leg weakness with bladder/bowel changes: call 112/999.
