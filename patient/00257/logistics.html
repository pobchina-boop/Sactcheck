Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Confirm symptoms, bone-metastatic disease without visceral metastases, previous treatments, blood count, kidney/liver tests, bone scan/computed tomography (CT) and bone health.

STEP 2

Your treatment visit

Radium-223 is given as a slow injection into a vein every 28 days, for a maximum of six injections. It is administered by a specialist service authorised to use radioactive medicines. No routine premedication is usually required. The team prescribes sickness or other symptom medicines if needed.

STEP 3

intravenous (IV) access

Treatment is given through a cannula or suitable established access. No take-home pump is part of this regimen. Report pain, burning, leakage or swelling immediately.

STEP 4

Between visits

Do not combine with abiraterone plus prednisone/prednisolone. Bone-protective treatment is assessed before starting. Obtain the nuclear-medicine team’s written toilet, body-fluid, laundry and travel instructions; your treating service will explain which precautions to follow at home and for how long.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next blood-test and treatment dates, support medicines and verified daytime/out-of-hours numbers.
