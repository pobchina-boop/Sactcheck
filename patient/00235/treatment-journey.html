Your treatment journey

Teysuno and cisplatin for advanced gastric cancer

Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and relieve symptoms. Your team will discuss benefit and side effects at review.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · Teysuno and cisplatin

Teysuno twice daily on days 1–21, then seven days off. Cisplatin on day 1 every 28 days.

Teysuno alone · only if separately prescribed

Cisplatin has stopped. The team must confirm a new prescription and that restart criteria are met.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingCheck blood count, kidney and liver function and DPD status before starting. Review kidney function, other medicines and the prescribed capsule strengths.Bring your medicine list and oncology contact details.
Before the next cycleReview blood counts, kidney function, electrolytes and liver tests before the next cycle. Check hearing, hydration and urine output.Tell the team about symptoms and missed doses.
At reviewReview symptoms, nutrition, weight and treatment response.Ask when scans or surgery will be discussed.

Cisplatin is planned for up to six cycles. If it stops early while the cancer is responding, the team may restart Teysuno alone once restart criteria are met.

At review visits

  • When is the next treatment? Teysuno twice daily on days 1–21, then seven days off. Cisplatin on day 1 every 28 days.
  • What should I do between visits? Take Teysuno with water at least one hour before or one hour after food. Keep a capsule diary and skip a vomited or missed dose.
