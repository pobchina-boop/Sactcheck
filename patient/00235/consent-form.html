Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Teysuno and cisplatin for advanced gastric cancer

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and relieve symptoms. Your team will discuss benefit and side effects at review. Advanced gastric cancer.

Treatment schedule. Teysuno twice daily on days 1–21, then seven days off. Cisplatin on day 1 every 28 days. Cisplatin is planned for up to six cycles. If it stops early while the cancer is responding, the team may restart Teysuno alone once restart criteria are met.

Regimen-specific side effects

Teysuno (S-1)

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help

Chest painSeek emergency help for chest pain, severe breathlessness or collapse.
Capsule safetyUse only the prescribed strengths. Leave a vomited or missed dose out and record it.

Cisplatin

Effect to discussWhat to look for and when to get help

Hearing and nervesReport ringing in the ears, hearing loss or numbness before the next treatment.
Nausea and infectionTake the prescribed anti-sickness cover. Call urgently if unable to drink or if you feel feverish.

Treatment discussion and decision

Teysuno and cisplatin for advanced gastric cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

In previously untreated advanced gastric or junctional adenocarcinoma, median survival was 8.6 months with S-1/cisplatin and 7.9 months with infused fluorouracil/cisplatin. The trial did not demonstrate superiority (HR 0.92, 95% CI 0.80–1.05). These results describe selected trial populations. Your outlook depends on your disease, fitness and treatment setting.

Treatment aims to control advanced cancer and relieve symptoms. Your team will discuss benefit and side effects at review. Cisplatin is planned for up to six cycles. If it stops early while the cancer is responding, the team may restart Teysuno alone once restart criteria are met. Discuss alternatives, fertility, and individual risks with the treating clinician.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
