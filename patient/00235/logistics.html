Preparing for treatment visits

Teysuno and cisplatin for advanced gastric cancer

Planning your visits

Treatment visit

Teysuno twice daily on days 1–21, then seven days off. Cisplatin on day 1 every 28 days.

At home

Take Teysuno with water at least one hour before or one hour after food. Keep a capsule diary and skip a vomited or missed dose.

Write down your next appointments

  • Bring your medication record and oncology telephone number.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Phone oncology immediately for fever, shivering or sudden illness. For severe chest pain, severe breathing difficulty or collapse call 112 or 999. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
