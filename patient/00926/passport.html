My treatment passport

Tremelimumab + durvalumab

My treatment record

First-line immunotherapy to control advanced liver cancer and help people live longer.

PhaseWhenWhat changes
First visitCycle 1 day 1 · Two separate infusionsTremelimumab is given once over about one hour. Durvalumab begins 60–120 minutes after the first infusion finishes and is given over about one hour. Allow extra time for checks.
Later visitsCycle 2 onward · Day 1 every 28 daysOnly durvalumab is repeated, every four weeks if your team advises continuing. Tremelimumab is not repeated.

Hospital __________________ Who to call if I cannot reach the team __________________

Contact your oncology service urgently. New cough, breathlessness, diarrhoea, jaundice, severe headache or marked weakness: contact oncology urgently, even after treatment ends. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
