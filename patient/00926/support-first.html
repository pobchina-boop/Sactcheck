SACTCheck · NCCP 00926 v1 · First-line advanced or unresectable hepatocellular carcinoma

Support during first visit

Tremelimumab + durvalumab

Tremelimumab is given once over about one hour. Durvalumab begins 60–120 minutes after the first infusion finishes and is given over about one hour. Allow extra time for checks.

First infusion visit

Tremelimumab is given once. Durvalumab follows 60–120 minutes after the first infusion ends. Tell your nurse immediately about wheeze, rash, chills or faintness. Your team decides what supportive treatment is needed.

Before leaving

Write your day-unit and out-of-hours numbers on both cards. Bring them to appointments and urgent assessments. Ask what to do about diarrhoea before taking a medicine to suppress it.

Contact your oncology service urgently. New cough, breathlessness, diarrhoea, jaundice, severe headache or marked weakness: contact oncology urgently, even after treatment ends. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
