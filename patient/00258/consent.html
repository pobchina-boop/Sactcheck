1. Treatment and purpose

TCH → trastuzumab continuation · NCCP 00258 v9

Default: six 21-day TCH cycles with IV trastuzumab, then trastuzumab alone to complete one year total. The source also permits weekly IV or fixed-dose SC trastuzumab alternatives; the team confirms the formulation and visit schedule. Complete the selected chemotherapy and continue trastuzumab to the agreed total course, usually one year from the first dose; this is not an additional year after chemotherapy.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Docetaxel · Taxane chemotherapy

Take prescribed steroid premedication. Report fever, lasting numbness, swelling, sore nails or infusion symptoms.

Carboplatin · Platinum chemotherapy

Keep blood-test appointments. Fever, unusual bleeding, repeated vomiting or infusion symptoms need prompt advice.

Trastuzumab · HER2-targeted antibody

Keep echo appointments throughout HER2 treatment. Report breathlessness, swelling, palpitations or a reaction; chemotherapy side effects are tracked separately.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the treatment aim, alternatives, expected benefit, common and serious effects, fertility and pregnancy precautions, route, monitoring, practical support and the agreed contact plan. The treating team completes the approved individual consent process.

3. Understanding and next steps

Consent is a conversation and can be reconsidered. Tick boxes record discussion topics only; they do not establish capacity, consent or authorisation.
