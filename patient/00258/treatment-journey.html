The whole treatment journey

TCH → trastuzumab continuation: the whole pathway

HER2-positive adjuvant breast cancer

Default: six 21-day TCH cycles with IV trastuzumab, then trastuzumab alone to complete one year total. The source also permits weekly IV or fixed-dose SC trastuzumab alternatives; the team confirms the formulation and visit schedule.

What each check is for

1Blood testsNCCP specifies baseline and regular FBC, liver and renal profiles; the team confirms the pre-dose checks for the active chemotherapy or trastuzumab-only phase. Verify kidney function for carboplatin dosing.

Check safety before the next dose.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP

2Heart testsNCCP lists baseline echo/MUGA if clinically indicated, but requires cardiac eligibility assessment; the trastuzumab product information calls for baseline cardiac assessment. Repeat every 12 weeks during trastuzumab and at completion. Earlier checks follow symptoms or cardiac concern. Around cycle 5 is the first 12-week check if treatment stays on time.

Monitor pumping function and relevant rhythm risk.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP · HERCEPTIN · ESC

3Cancer imagingBaseline breast and nodal imaging, with staging tests appropriate to the diagnosis. Review response during preoperative treatment and before surgery. After local treatment, arrange breast surveillance; routine CT, PET or tumour-marker testing is not the usual screening plan for someone without symptoms.

Assess response or follow-up needs.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: EARLY

4Bone healthIf the cancer is also hormone-receptor positive, endocrine treatment may form part of the wider plan. Bone-density testing is linked to aromatase inhibitors, ovarian suppression and fracture risk; it is not automatically required because a HER2 medicine is prescribed. Agree baseline DEXA where indicated and individual follow-up.

Assess fracture risk where relevant.

Ask / act: Agree the booking owner, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: BONE
