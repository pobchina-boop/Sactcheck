Consent to systemic anti-cancer treatment

Intrathecal methotrexate for GTN

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

Reduce the risk of GTN involving the brain or the fluid surrounding the brain and spinal cord, alongside the systemic treatment plan. Prevention of central nervous system involvement in high-risk GTN or low-risk GTN with lung metastases.

Treatment schedule. One spinal treatment every 14 days for three doses, with an oral rescue tablet the following day.

Regimen-specific side effects

Treatment discussion and decision

Intrathecal methotrexate for GTN

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

The Irish guideline describes prophylaxis alongside systemic therapy for the defined risk groups. This is specialist guideline evidence. The benefit for an individual patient cannot be quantified from these recommendations.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Seek urgent assessment for severe headache, fever, a stiff neck, new weakness, confusion or a seizure after spinal treatment. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
