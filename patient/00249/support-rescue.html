Support during day 2 · oral rescue

Intrathecal methotrexate for GTN

Take the single prescribed oral folinic acid tablet 24 hours after the spinal dose. If you also have a separate methotrexate course, ask the team to write down both rescue schedules. Call immediately about a missed or vomited tablet.

Folinic acid

Ring the unit for any missed, late or vomited rescue dose.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Seek urgent assessment for severe headache, fever, a stiff neck, new weakness, confusion or a seizure after spinal treatment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
