Your treatment journey

Intrathecal methotrexate for GTN

Reduce the risk of GTN involving the brain or the fluid surrounding the brain and spinal cord, alongside the systemic treatment plan.

Your infusion schedule

Day 1 · spinal treatment

The specialist team gives methotrexate into the fluid around the spinal cord. There are three treatments two weeks apart. The team coordinates appointments with your systemic chemotherapy.

Day 2 · oral rescue

Take the single prescribed oral folinic acid tablet 24 hours after the spinal dose. If you also have a separate methotrexate course, ask the team to write down both rescue schedules. Call immediately about a missed or vomited tablet.

At review visits

  • What happens at my next visit? One spinal treatment every 14 days for three doses, with an oral rescue tablet the following day.
  • Which symptoms should I phone about? Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Seek urgent assessment for severe headache, fever, a stiff neck, new weakness, confusion or a seizure after spinal treatment.
  • What should I plan before starting? Bring your medicines list and your GTN centre contact details.
  • How should I plan follow-up? Bring both schedules. Ask which symptoms need an urgent call after the procedure.
  • Who gives the spinal treatment? Only the specialist team authorised to give intrathecal chemotherapy gives the spinal dose. Your oral rescue tablet and systemic medicines have separate routes and prescriptions.
