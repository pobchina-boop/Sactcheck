Preparing for treatment visits

Intrathecal methotrexate for GTN

Planning your visits

Appointments

One spinal treatment every 14 days for three doses, with an oral rescue tablet the following day.

Bring to your visit

Bring your medicines list and your GTN centre contact details.

Between visits

Call your oncology unit urgently for fever, shaking chills or sudden illness. Seek urgent assessment for severe headache, fever, a stiff neck, new weakness, confusion or a seizure after spinal treatment.
