Capivasertib + fulvestrant

Capivasertib is taken twice daily for four days, then stopped for three days, each week. Fulvestrant injections are given on Days 1 and 15 of the first 28-day cycle, then Day 1 of later cycles. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerated.

How this medicine supports your treatment

This combination aims to control advanced breast cancer. Capivasertib blocks a growth signal inside cancer cells and fulvestrant reduces signalling through the oestrogen receptor. Tumour testing helps identify people whose cancer has a relevant pathway alteration.

Targeted AKT inhibitor

Capivasertib

Blocks an internal growth signal called AKT
The tumour has a relevant pathway alteration↓Capivasertib blocks AKT signalling↓Growth and survival signals are reduced

Blocks AKT1, AKT2 and AKT3, proteins that help carry growth and survival signals inside cells. PIK3CA/AKT1/PTEN alterations can activate this pathway. AKT also helps the body respond to insulin. Blocking it can raise blood sugar, which is why glucose checks start early.

Keep the glucose plan and report diarrhoea, rash or high-glucose symptoms early.

Oestrogen-receptor antagonist and degrader

Fulvestrant

Blocks and reduces oestrogen receptors
ER-positive cancer uses oestrogen signals↓Fulvestrant binds and reduces ER↓Less oestrogen-driven signalling reaches the cell

Attaches to oestrogen receptors and reduces their number, making it harder for oestrogen to stimulate hormone-sensitive breast cancer.

Report unusual injection-site bleeding, severe pain, numbness or pain travelling down the leg.

The treatment being discussed

This combination aims to control advanced breast cancer. Capivasertib blocks a growth signal inside cancer cells and fulvestrant reduces signalling through the oestrogen receptor. Tumour testing helps identify people whose cancer has a relevant pathway alteration.

Who this regimen covers

For adults whose ER-positive, HER2-negative advanced breast cancer has a PIK3CA, AKT1 or PTEN change and has grown during or after hormone treatment. The team also checks general health and earlier treatment.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Taking the tablets

Take capivasertib with or without food and swallow the tablets whole with water. Do not chew, crush, split or dissolve them. Avoid grapefruit and grapefruit juice.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Injection visits

500 mg intramuscularly as two 5 mL injections, one in each buttock, slowly over 1–2 minutes per injection. Cycle 1: days 1 and 15. Cycle 2 onward: day 1 of each 28-day cycle.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Ovarian suppression and pregnancy

If you are pre- or perimenopausal, a separately prescribed medicine must suppress ovarian hormone production with this combination. The team may also consider this for men. Age alone does not confirm suppression. Discuss fertility, pregnancy prevention and breastfeeding. Follow the longer fulvestrant contraception advice: two years after its last dose for women; capivasertib advice for men extends sixteen weeks after stopping. Your team confirms the individual plan.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen · EMA Faslodex product information

Glucose and other checks

Some checks happen between injection visits. Agree who books them and who reviews the results.

Starting and continuing checks

Check blood count, kidney and liver function before treatment and each cycle. Check fasting glucose and HbA1c before starting. Fasting glucose is checked at weeks 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8, then monthly; ideally before the tablet dose on day 3 or 4 of a dosing week. HbA1c is checked every three months, with an extra week-4 check for diabetes, prediabetes or high baseline glucose. Diabetes, new high glucose or metformin initiation needs extra checks under a written plan.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

If you already have diabetes

Check fasting glucose daily for the first two weeks, then as often as the team advises. An extra HbA1c is recommended at week 4 when diabetes, prediabetes or high baseline glucose is present.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

If glucose rises

The team may arrange at least twice-weekly checks, including tablet-taking and tablet-free days, and ketone tests or specialist review. Follow the written plan; symptoms can need urgent assessment even without a very high reading.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

If metformin is started

The team checks kidney function weekly for up to three weeks after metformin starts with capivasertib, then on Day 1 of each cycle. Existing diabetes treatment needs individual reconciliation.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Support between treatment visits

Early reporting helps the team treat symptoms and review doses safely.

Diarrhoea plan

Have the agreed anti-diarrhoea plan available before starting. NCCP gives loperamide 4 mg at first onset, then 2 mg after each loose stool, up to 16 mg in a day. Increase oral fluids and contact the team promptly for severe or persistent diarrhoea, inability to drink, dizziness, reduced urine or palpitations. Secondary prevention can be considered for recurrent diarrhoea.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Skin plan

Report a new rash promptly. Use moisturisers and any antihistamine or cream prescribed for your symptoms. Do not start steroids or change doses without review.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Medicine review

Tell the team about every prescription, non-prescription or herbal product. New medicines can change capivasertib exposure and require a reviewed dose.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Cancer response

Your team agrees which scans or other tests you need and when, and reviews symptoms, treatment benefit and your priorities.

NCCP 00947 v1 · official regimen

Practical support

Glucose plan

Agree when to check glucose and when to call. Use your written diabetes-medicine plan on tablet-taking and tablet-free days; do not change existing diabetes medicines yourself.

Diarrhoea and fluids

Have the prescribed diarrhoea medicine available. Follow the agreed early-treatment instructions and contact the team for persistent symptoms, dizziness or reduced urine.

Skin and injections

Report rash early and use skin treatments as advised. Tell the injector about anticoagulants, bruising and any previous leg nerve symptoms.

Taking tablets / attending treatment

Why this combination may be chosen

The medicines work on different signals involved in hormone-sensitive cancer. Your team chooses the combination using the cancer tests, earlier treatment and your health, and checks both benefit and side effects as treatment continues.

Discuss your own expected benefit, alternatives and treatment burden with the team. Trial averages cannot predict an individual outcome.

When to get help

Emergency: 112 / 999. Severe chest pain, major breathing difficulty, collapse or stroke symptoms need emergency help.
Contact your treating team promptly: Seek urgent assessment for unusual thirst or urination, severe diarrhoea, inability to drink, or a new rash. Vomiting with abdominal pain, breathing difficulty, fruity breath, confusion or marked sleepiness can signal ketoacidosis: seek emergency help without waiting for a high glucose reading. Blistering or peeling skin also needs urgent help.

Questions to ask

What if I miss or vomit a capivasertib dose?

If a capivasertib dose is late, it may be taken within 4 hours of its usual time, leaving at least 8 hours before the next dose. If more than 4 hours late, skip it and take the next dose at the usual time. Do not double a dose. If you vomit after a dose, do not take an extra dose; take the next scheduled dose at its usual time. Contact the team if vomiting continues or prevents fluids or medicines.

Do the three tablet-free days also change my diabetes medicines?

Follow the individual written plan from the oncology and diabetes teams. Glucose can fall on tablet-free days. Do not automatically stop or continue a changed diabetes dose without the agreed instructions.

Do I need ovarian suppression?

Pre- or perimenopausal women need a separately prescribed LHRH medicine with this combination. The team may also consider this for men. Age alone does not confirm suppression.

What if the tablets are paused?

Contact the team for a revised plan. Do not restart or increase capivasertib yourself, and do not assume a tablet pause cancels or resets the fulvestrant injection schedule.

How long is treatment and how will benefit be assessed?

There is no fixed number of cycles. The team reviews cancer symptoms, scans, tolerability and priorities, and continues or changes treatment accordingly.
