Your practical treatment plan

Know the next step.

Taking treatment

Capivasertib is taken twice daily for four days, then stopped for three days, each week. Fulvestrant injections are given on Days 1 and 15 of the first 28-day cycle, then Day 1 of later cycles. Treatment continues while helpful and tolerated.

The usual starting capivasertib dose is 400 mg twice daily, about 12 hours apart, on the prescribed dosing days. Swallow tablets whole, with or without food; avoid grapefruit. Your prescription may use a lower dose. Each usual 500 mg fulvestrant dose is two slow injections into muscle, one in each buttock; no IV port or pump is required.

Checks and follow-up

Check blood count, kidney and liver function before treatment and each cycle. Check fasting glucose and HbA1c before starting. Fasting glucose is checked at weeks 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8, then monthly; ideally before the tablet dose on day 3 or 4 of a dosing week. HbA1c is checked every three months, with an extra week-4 check for diabetes, prediabetes or high baseline glucose. Diabetes, new high glucose or metformin initiation needs extra checks under a written plan.

Support between reviews

Glucose plan

Agree when to check glucose and when to call. Use your written diabetes-medicine plan on tablet-taking and tablet-free days; do not change existing diabetes medicines yourself.

Diarrhoea and fluids

Have the prescribed diarrhoea medicine available. Follow the agreed early-treatment instructions and contact the team for persistent symptoms, dizziness or reduced urine.

Skin and injections

Report rash early and use skin treatments as advised. Tell the injector about anticoagulants, bruising and any previous leg nerve symptoms.
