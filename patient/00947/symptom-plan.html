Capivasertib (Truqap) + fulvestrant

When to call

Keep your 24-hour oncology number with you, including on tablet-free days. If you feel seriously unwell, seek help even if your symptom is missing from this sheet.

Get emergency help now

Vomiting, tummy pain, deep or difficult breathing, fruity-smelling breath, confusion or unusual sleepiness can signal diabetic ketoacidosis. This can happen early or later in treatment. Seek emergency assessment if you suspect it, even without a high glucose reading. Call 112 or 999 in Ireland if seriously unwell.

Very thirsty or passing more urine

Capivasertib can raise blood sugar. Contact oncology immediately and report your readings, if available. Your team may check ketones. Follow the emergency advice above if unwell.

Loose or watery stools

Start your prescribed diarrhoea medicine at the first loose stool and drink fluids as advised. Contact oncology promptly. Seek urgent help if diarrhoea is severe or persistent, you cannot drink, or you feel faint.

New rash or sore skin

Contact oncology promptly when a rash starts. Blisters, peeling skin, painful sores around the eyes or mouth, or a rash with fever need urgent assessment. Use skin treatments agreed with the team.

Pain after the injections

Fulvestrant can cause local soreness. Call promptly for pain travelling down the leg, numbness, weakness, or unusual bleeding. Tell the injector beforehand if you take blood thinners.

Fever or sudden illness

Use your hospital’s fever and urgent-care instructions. Call the 24-hour oncology line if feverish, shivering or suddenly unwell. Infection can also upset blood sugar.
