Symptoms can matter before a reading is available

Call immediately for unusual thirst or passing much more urine. Vomiting with tummy pain, fast or difficult breathing, confusion or marked sleepiness can signal ketoacidosis. Seek emergency care immediately. Call 112 or 999 in Ireland if seriously unwell.
