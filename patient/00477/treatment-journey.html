The whole treatment journey

Goserelin 10.8 mg: hormone implant every 12 weeks: treatment and checks

Locally advanced or metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer under NCCP 00477.

Goserelin 10.8 mg is placed under the skin of the front of the abdomen every 12 weeks (84 days). Your team writes the next date and the intended treatment duration.

What each check is for

1Treatment and reviewOne implant every 12 weeks

This is androgen deprivation therapy (ADT): it lowers testosterone to help control prostate cancer. With radiotherapy, the aim may be cure; in advanced disease, it is part of ongoing cancer control.

Ask / act: Regular blood count, kidney and liver tests, blood glucose and bone blood profile. NCCP does not specify one interval for these tests. Review symptoms, PSA and testosterone, with scans according to the individual plan.
