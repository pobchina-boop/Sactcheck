The whole treatment journey

Lutetium-177 oxodotreotide (Lutathera / PRRT): treatment and checks

Progressive, unresectable or metastatic, well-differentiated G1/G2 somatostatin-receptor-positive gastroenteropancreatic NET under NCCP 00642a.

Four administrations are planned, usually eight weeks apart. Lutathera is infused over about 30–40 minutes, with a four-hour kidney-protective amino-acid infusion and anti-sickness treatment. The visit is much longer than the Lutathera infusion; an overnight stay may be needed.

What each check is for

1Four planned PRRT administrationsT1–T4

PRRT carries radiation to somatostatin receptors on NET cells to help control disease that cannot be removed or has spread. A specialist NET and nuclear-medicine team checks receptor imaging, organ function and your priorities before recommending it.

Ask / act: Confirm the current prescription, tests and support medicines.
