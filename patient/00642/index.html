Lutetium-177 oxodotreotide (Lutathera / peptide receptor radionuclide therapy (PRRT))

Specialist receptor-targeted radiation for selected advanced neuroendocrine tumours (NETs).

Progressive, unresectable or metastatic, well-differentiated G1/G2 somatostatin-receptor-positive gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumour (NET) under NCCP 00642a. · NCCP 00642 v2 · Document revised 5 November 2026

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, cycles, treatment changes and appointments in your written plan.

Specialist G1/G2 somatostatin receptor (SSTR)-positive gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine tumour (GEP-NET) PRRT. Use the service’s written appointment, somatostatin analogue (SSA), radiation-safety and emergency instructions.

Four planned administrations, usually 8 weeks apart; each needs specialist clearance.

Between treatments

  • Follow the written SSA injection and radiation-safety plans; some contact restrictions last up to 3 weeks.
  • Urgent help for severe flushing, faintness, wheeze or diarrhoea, especially first 48 hours; report fever, bleeding or reduced urine.

Arrange home/work precautions and actual emergency contacts before admission. Scans for tumour response follow your specialist plan.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

2Appetite / sickness
PRRT / amino acids

Nausea and vomiting can occur, especially around treatment. Tell staff early and follow the anti-sickness and fluid plan.

4Bone marrow / blood
PRRT

Counts may fall during or after the course. Report fever, bruising or bleeding. Rare late marrow disorders require ongoing follow-up.

6Kidneys / urine
PRRT

Amino acids reduce kidney exposure but do not remove risk. Keep renal blood tests and report much less urine or dehydration.

8Fertility / pregnancy
PRRT

Discuss fertility before treatment. Avoid pregnancy using the written contraception plan; follow individual radiation precautions for close contacts.

Keep the record accurate

The team dates and initials each recorded treatment unit when complete. A cycle is complete after its final planned dose; an administration stamp records that administration. Delays, omitted doses or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A stamp does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
