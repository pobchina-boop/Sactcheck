The whole treatment journey

Temozolomide with short-course radiotherapy, then adjuvant treatment: treatment and checks

Adults older than 65 with newly diagnosed glioblastoma who are not suitable for the standard radiotherapy regimen with temozolomide, under NCCP 00461.

Take temozolomide daily for 21 days during short-course radiotherapy, including weekends as prescribed. After a four-week break, take temozolomide on Days 1–5 of each 28-day cycle for up to six adjuvant cycles.
