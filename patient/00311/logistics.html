Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before treatment

Confirm the exact treatment plan and contact numbers before starting.

STEP 2

Treatment visit or home course

Each infusion takes about 30 minutes. Blood review, preparation and observation add to appointment time. The consultant may prescribe only 3 or 4 treatment days in selected circumstances; follow the confirmed appointment plan.

STEP 3

Bring to review

Bring your medicine list, dose record and questions; report new symptoms before starting another course.
