Treatment and purpose

Topotecan is being offered to slow the growth of metastatic ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue. Metastatic ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal carcinoma after earlier treatment has failed. The fallopian tube and peritoneal indications are unlicensed in Ireland.

Treatment schedule. A 30-minute infusion on each of five consecutive days, then a break through day 21. The standard plan is up to six cycles, stopping earlier for progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

Among 474 participants with recurrent epithelial ovarian cancer, tumour response occurred in 19.7% with PLD and 17.0% with topotecan. The response-rate difference was not statistically significant. Results from ovarian cancer cannot give a separate response estimate for fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer.

Metastatic ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal carcinoma after earlier treatment has failed. The fallopian tube and peritoneal indications are unlicensed in Ireland. Discuss expected benefit, alternatives, side effects and the option of symptom-directed care. Record the individual decision and any off-label discussion.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call immediately for fever, chills, sudden illness, uncontrolled bleeding or severe abdominal pain. New cough or breathlessness needs prompt assessment. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
