Your treatment journey

Five-day topotecan for ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer

Topotecan is being offered to slow the growth of metastatic ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue.

Your tests and checks

CheckWhat is checkedWhat you can do
Before startingBlood count and kidney and liver tests before starting. Kidney function helps determine the starting dose.Confirm all five visit dates and bring your anti-sickness medicines list. Tell your team about previous low blood counts, infection or kidney problems.
Before and during treatmentRepeat blood count and kidney and liver tests before each cycle. Low neutrophils, platelets or haemoglobin may require delay, dose change or supportive treatment.Keep your lab and clinic appointments. Report symptoms before the next infusion.
Response reviewSymptoms and imaging according to your consultant’s plan.Ask when you will discuss whether the treatment is helping.

At review visits

  • What should I check before treatment? Confirm all five visit dates and bring your anti-sickness medicines list. Tell your team about previous low blood counts, infection or kidney problems.
  • What should I watch for at home? Keep taking fluids if able. Record diarrhoea, mouth soreness, bruising or breathlessness. Ring urgently for fever with abdominal pain or diarrhoea.
  • Why is this being offered? Topotecan is being offered to slow the growth of metastatic ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue.
