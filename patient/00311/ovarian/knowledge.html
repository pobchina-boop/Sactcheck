Your complete treatment reference

Five-day topotecan for ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer

Your treatment and how the medicines work

Topotecan is being offered to slow the growth of metastatic ovarian, fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer. Your team will review symptoms and scan results with you to decide whether to continue.

Topotecan · Hycamtin; supplied brand may vary

Topoisomerase-I inhibitor chemotherapy. Topotecan blocks topoisomerase I, an enzyme that relieves strain while DNA is copied. Unrepaired DNA breaks can stop dividing cancer cells; healthy blood-forming and gut cells can also be affected. Call oncology immediately for fever or suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, severe tummy pain, persistent vomiting or diarrhoea, or new cough or breathlessness. Do not wait for the next cycle.

Common effects and what to do

Blood counts and fatigue

Anaemia can cause tiredness or breathlessness. Low platelets can cause bruising, nosebleeds or bleeding gums.

Mouth and gut

Nausea, diarrhoea and mouth ulcers can make drinking difficult. Call if you cannot keep fluids down.

Serious effects: when to call

Serious infection

Low neutrophils can lead to sepsis. Fever, chills or sudden illness require an urgent call.

Bleeding

Tell the team about unexpected bruising or bleeding. Seek urgent help if bleeding continues.

Bowel and lungs

Fever with abdominal pain or diarrhoea needs urgent review. Report new or worsening cough or breathlessness.

Contact your oncology service urgently. Call immediately for fever, chills, sudden illness, uncontrolled bleeding or severe abdominal pain. New cough or breathlessness needs prompt assessment. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.

Practical support

Before treatment

Confirm all five visit dates and bring your anti-sickness medicines list. Tell your team about previous low blood counts, infection or kidney problems.

Between visits

Keep taking fluids if able. Record diarrhoea, mouth soreness, bruising or breathlessness. Ring urgently for fever with abdominal pain or diarrhoea.

Urgent contact

Call immediately for fever, chills, sudden illness, uncontrolled bleeding or severe abdominal pain. New cough or breathlessness needs prompt assessment.

Planning your visits

Appointments

A 30-minute infusion on each of five consecutive days, then a break through day 21. The standard plan is up to six cycles, stopping earlier for progression or unacceptable toxicity.

At the day unit

You attend the unit for each prescribed daily infusion. A consultant may shorten a course to three or four days for toxicity or after extensive previous treatment. Follow your written calendar.

What to bring

Confirm all five visit dates and bring your anti-sickness medicines list. Tell your team about previous low blood counts, infection or kidney problems.

What the research found

Among 474 participants with recurrent epithelial ovarian cancer, tumour response occurred in 19.7% with PLD and 17.0% with topotecan.

The response-rate difference was not statistically significant. Results from ovarian cancer cannot give a separate response estimate for fallopian tube or peritoneal cancer.
