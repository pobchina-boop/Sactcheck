This pack describes relapsed small-cell lung cancer. Ovarian, fallopian tube and peritoneal cancer indications require their own treatment discussion.

Lung cancer

Topotecan is given by infusion on Days 1–5 of a 21-day cycle. Up to 6 cycles are planned, with earlier changes if treatment stops helping or side effects become unacceptable.
