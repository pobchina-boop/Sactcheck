1. Treatment and purpose

Phesgo + docetaxel · NCCP 00796 v4

Phesgo and docetaxel on Day 1 every 21 days. Docetaxel is planned for at least six cycles; it may stop after 6–8 cycles or toxicity. Phesgo may continue alone while beneficial and tolerated. After chemotherapy stops, use the agreed Phesgo maintenance plan (NCCP 00785 when eligible). Keep cardiac and response checks; chemotherapy steroids, antiemetics and growth factors do not automatically continue.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Docetaxel · Taxane chemotherapy

Take prescribed steroid premedication. Report fever, lasting numbness, swelling, sore nails or infusion symptoms.

Pertuzumab · HER2-targeted antibody

Keep echo appointments. Report diarrhoea, new breathlessness, swelling, palpitations or an injection reaction.

Trastuzumab · HER2-targeted antibody

Keep echo appointments. Report diarrhoea, new breathlessness, swelling, palpitations or an injection reaction.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the aim, alternatives, uncertain benefit, common and serious risks, heart function, diarrhoea, injection reaction, fertility and pregnancy precautions. Effective contraception is required during Phesgo and for 7 months after the last dose; review the full combination and breastfeeding restrictions with the team. After chemotherapy stops, use the agreed Phesgo maintenance plan (NCCP 00785 when eligible). Keep cardiac and response checks; chemotherapy steroids, antiemetics and growth factors do not automatically continue.
