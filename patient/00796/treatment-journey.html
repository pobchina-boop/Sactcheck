The whole treatment journey

Phesgo + docetaxel: the whole pathway

HER2-positive metastatic / unresectable locally recurrent breast cancer

Phesgo and docetaxel on Day 1 every 21 days. Docetaxel is planned for at least six cycles; it may stop after 6–8 cycles or toxicity. Phesgo may continue alone while beneficial and tolerated.

What each check is for

1Blood testsFBC, renal and liver profile before every cycle.

Check treatment safety.

Ask / act: The treating team records the booking owner, due window, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP 00796

2Echo / MUGAEcho or MUGA before starting and every 12 weeks during treatment, including maintenance. At an uninterrupted 21-day schedule, the first 12-week check is around cycle 5; count elapsed time from the first HER2 dose. A delay or route/phase change does not restart the clock. Cardiac concern may require 4–8-week checks or urgent earlier assessment.

Monitor heart pumping function.

Ask / act: The treating team records the booking owner, due window, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: NCCP 00796

3Cancer imagingBaseline imaging records the disease sites. ESMO recommends response review generally every 2–4 months, adjusted for symptoms, disease pace, sites and treatment. computed tomography (CT) chest/abdomen/pelvis is used when appropriate; the team selects the actual scan and review date. Tumour markers may supplement assessment, not replace scans or determine a treatment change alone.

Assess disease response or surveillance.

Ask / act: The treating team records the booking owner, due window, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: METASTATIC

4Bone healthIf hormone-receptor-positive disease leads to an aromatase inhibitor or ovarian suppression, assess fracture risk and baseline DEXA when indicated. Repeat according to results, risk and the endocrine plan; DEXA is not a scan at each Phesgo cycle.

Reduce fracture risk when endocrine therapy affects bone.

Ask / act: The treating team records the booking owner, due window, result reviewer and action if overdue.

Sources: BONE
