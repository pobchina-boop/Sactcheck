No routine prophylactic antiemetic order is populated for this phase. Agree an individual symptom-treatment plan where needed.

Additional phase-specific support

  • No routine prophylactic antiemetic or cytotoxic premedication is carried over. Previous reactions or symptoms need an individual plan.
  • Phesgo premedication is not usually required; consider paracetamol/antihistamine for the individual reaction history.
  • After chemotherapy stops, use the agreed Phesgo maintenance plan (NCCP 00785 when eligible). Keep cardiac and response checks; chemotherapy steroids, antiemetics and growth factors do not automatically continue.
  • Continue cardiac monitoring; maintenance does not restart the echo clock.

Before signing: verify allergies, current medicines, interactions, renal / hepatic function, QT risk, sedation, prior response, total steroid exposure and the rescue-medicine duration. Do not duplicate medicines from another phase.
