Preparing for treatment visits

Pembrolizumab maintenance every 6 weeks for cervical cancer

Planning your visits

Appointments

One infusion every 6 weeks (42 days). The team confirms the first maintenance date from your actual last treatment.

At the unit

Pembrolizumab (Keytruda) 400 mg runs into a vein over about 30 minutes. There is no platinum or paclitaxel in this maintenance infusion.

After this part of treatment

Your team reviews scans, symptoms and side effects. At 24 months they may discuss stopping if benefit is sustained and there is no growth. Continuing is also possible when clinically appropriate.

Write down your next appointments

  • List any steroids or hormone replacements and tell the team about autoimmune illness, diabetes or previous immunotherapy reactions.
Contact your oncology service urgently. Call the 24-hour oncology team immediately about new breathlessness, persistent cough, new diarrhoea, blood in stools, severe weakness, yellow eyes or a severe headache. Symptoms can start after treatment ends. Carry your immunotherapy alert card. If you have severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure, call 112 or 999. Follow the 24-hour contact instructions from your treating hospital.
