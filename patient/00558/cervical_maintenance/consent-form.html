Pembrolizumab maintenance every 6 weeks for cervical cancer

Patient nameDate of birthHospital / MRN

Treatment and purpose

You have finished the chemotherapy part of your treatment. Continuing pembrolizumab helps your immune system keep the cervical cancer under control. Adult persistent, recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer, validated PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) ≥1, ECOG 0–2 and completion of the induction chemotherapy component. Select monotherapy only when a pembrolizumab-alone maintenance plan has been confirmed.

Treatment schedule. One infusion every 6 weeks (42 days). The team confirms the first maintenance date from your actual last treatment.

Treatment discussion and decision

Pembrolizumab maintenance every 6 weeks for cervical cancer

Benefits, alternatives and individual risks

People who received pembrolizumab along with their first chemotherapy and then continued it lived longer as a group than those who received chemotherapy with placebo. The study assessed the whole treatment plan. The trial randomised the full first-line strategy using 200 mg every three weeks. It did not isolate the maintenance benefit or compare 200 mg every three weeks with 400 mg every six weeks.

Discuss other treatment options, symptom-focused care, fertility, contraception and the effects that matter most to you. You can ask for more time and change your decision.

Options discussed, personal risks and questions

Urgent contact Call the 24-hour oncology team immediately about new breathlessness, persistent cough, new diarrhoea, blood in stools, severe weakness, yellow eyes or a severe headache. Symptoms can start after treatment ends. Carry your immunotherapy alert card. Emergency services: 112 or 999.
