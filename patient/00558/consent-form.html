Treatment context
For metastatic lung cancer, the aim is to control cancer and help people live longer. After surgery and platinum chemotherapy, the adjuvant aim is to reduce the chance of cancer returning. Ask which aim applies to you.
Schedule
Pembrolizumab 400 mg is given as a drip over about 30 minutes on Day 1 every 6 weeks (42 days). The hospital visit is longer because of checks and preparation. After complete surgery and platinum chemotherapy, the adjuvant course lasts up to 12 months. For metastatic cancer, treatment continues while it remains helpful and tolerable; the team may consider stopping after 24 months of sustained response without progression, or continuing when appropriate.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Pembrolizumab

Immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver, kidneys, hormone glands, skin, heart and nervous system
Infusion reactions
Some effects need steroids, hospital care or long-term hormone replacement
Effects may start after the final dose

Discuss the aim for your exact lung setting, likely benefit, alternatives, important immune risks and how treatment may be paused or stopped. Some side effects need long-term medicines. Ask about fertility, pregnancy, breastfeeding and effects on daily life. For treatment after surgery, distinguish delaying recurrence from an overall-survival benefit, which was not established in the final trial analysis.
