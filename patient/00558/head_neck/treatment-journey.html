The whole treatment journey

Pembrolizumab every 6 weeks for head and neck cancer: treatment and checks

First-line treatment of metastatic or unresectable recurrent head and neck squamous cell carcinoma with a tumour PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) of at least 1. This indication excludes nasopharyngeal cancer and cancer progressing within six months after curatively intended systemic treatment for locoregionally advanced disease.

Pembrolizumab 400 mg is given through a vein over about 30 minutes on Day 1 every 6 weeks. The whole visit includes safety checks, preparation and observation. Treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity. After 24 months of sustained response without progression, the team may consider stopping; stopping at 24 months is not automatic.
