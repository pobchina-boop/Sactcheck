SACTCHECK · REGIMEN-SPECIFIC SACT CONSENT

Pembrolizumab every 6 weeks for head and neck cancer

First-line treatment of metastatic or unresectable recurrent head and neck squamous cell carcinoma with a tumour PD-L1 combined positive score (CPS) of at least 1. This indication excludes nasopharyngeal cancer and cancer progressing within six months after curatively intended systemic treatment for locoregionally advanced disease. · NCCP 00558 · version 13 · 42-day cycle

Treatment context
Pembrolizumab aims to control head and neck cancer that has returned or spread, relieve symptoms and help some people live longer. Benefit and side effects are reviewed for you.
Schedule
Pembrolizumab 400 mg is given through a vein over about 30 minutes on Day 1 every 6 weeks. The whole visit includes safety checks, preparation and observation. Treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity. After 24 months of sustained response without progression, the team may consider stopping; stopping at 24 months is not automatic.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Pembrolizumab

Immunotherapy
Immune inflammation of lungs, bowel, liver, kidneys, hormone glands, skin, heart and nervous system
Infusion reactions
Some effects need steroids, hospital care or long-term hormone replacement
Effects may start after the final dose
Heart, muscle and nerve immune effects can occur together and require urgent assessment

Discuss your diagnosis, intent, alternatives, expected benefit and uncertainty, treatment burden, serious or lasting harms, fertility and pregnancy precautions and your priorities.
