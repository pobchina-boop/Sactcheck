Pembrolizumab · NCCP 00558

Match the information to your treatment.

Pembrolizumab is used for several cancers. These patient materials cover non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC) only. Your team confirms which setting applies.

Lung cancer: advanced disease or treatment after surgery

The materials distinguish treatment for advanced lung cancer from adjuvant treatment after surgery and chemotherapy. The eligibility, aim and planned duration are different. An adjuvant time limit must not be applied to advanced disease.

Another cancer or a combination regimen

These lung monotherapy materials do not cover other cancers or pembrolizumab combined with chemotherapy. Use the matching regimen and indication and ask the treating team for the individual plan.
