Treatment context
This treatment aims to control prostate cancer and delay progression. The expected benefit depends on your disease setting, previous treatment and general health.
Schedule
Darolutamide 600 mg twice daily with food, continuously while helping and tolerated. Continue ADT unless surgically castrated. The team may prescribe 300 mg twice daily for selected organ impairment or toxicity.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Darolutamide

Androgen pathway treatment
Tiredness and weakness
Rash, limb pain and changes in blood counts or liver blood tests
Ischaemic heart disease or heart failure
Rare serious liver injury
Interactions, including increased exposure to some cholesterol medicines
Hot flushes, sexual or fertility effects and bone loss with ongoing ADT

Discuss the treatment aim, setting-specific evidence, alternatives and declining treatment; oral medicine and ADT risks, bone and cardiovascular health, sexual function, fertility, contraception, interactions, required tests and urgent contacts. Ask pharmacy to check all medicines, especially cholesterol medicines such as rosuvastatin and enzyme-inducing medicines or herbal products. Report jaundice, dark urine, severe tiredness, chest symptoms or a marked change in wellbeing promptly.
