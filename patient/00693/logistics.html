Darolutamide: high-risk nonmetastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00693 · Source reconciled draft; independent clinical, pharmacy and patient/PPI review pending

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests, blood pressure and an electrocardiogram (ECG) if at risk of heart-rhythm changes. Review recent cardiovascular disease, all medicines, bone health and falls risk.

STEP 2

Taking or receiving treatment

Take the morning and evening doses with food; swallow tablets whole with water. Take a missed dose when remembered before the next scheduled dose, then return to the normal schedule. Do not take two doses together or double the dose. Confirm any prescribed reduction with your team.

STEP 3

Tests and reviews

Repeat blood count, kidney and liver tests on your agreed schedule; blood pressure and ECG as clinically indicated. The NCCP regimen does not give a universal blood-test or scan interval. Monitor more closely with kidney or liver impairment.

STEP 4

Specific practical instructions

Ask pharmacy to check all medicines, especially cholesterol medicines such as rosuvastatin and enzyme-inducing medicines or herbal products. Report jaundice, dark urine, severe tiredness, chest symptoms or a marked change in wellbeing promptly.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next appointment, dose, support medicines and verified daytime and out-of-hours phone numbers.
