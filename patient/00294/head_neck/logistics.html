Sorafenib: differentiated thyroid cancer

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Full blood count, kidney and liver tests, blood pressure, thyroid tests and assessment of heart function.

STEP 2

Taking or receiving treatment

Take with water, without food or with a low- or moderate-fat meal. For a high-fat meal, take sorafenib at least 1 hour before or 2 hours afterwards. Follow the prescribed morning and evening doses; never change the dose yourself.

STEP 3

Tests and reviews

Consider blood count, kidney and liver tests at Day 14 of the first month. Repeat these bloods and thyroid tests every 28 days. Check blood pressure weekly in month 1, then every 28 days; check international normalised ratio (blood-clotting test) (INR) as indicated if taking warfarin.

STEP 4

Specific practical instructions

Tell the team before surgery or dental procedures. Report painful palms or soles early; skin care and early dose review can help prevent severe hand-foot reactions.

STEP 5

Leaving with a clear plan

Confirm the next appointment, dose, support medicines and verified daytime and out-of-hours phone numbers.
