Tests in your plan

Baseline bloods and tests

WhenFull blood count, kidney and liver tests, blood pressure, thyroid tests and assessment of heart function.

Checks organ safety and suitability before treatment.

Treatment safety monitoring

WhenConsider blood count, kidney and liver tests at Day 14 of the first month. Repeat these bloods and thyroid tests every 28 days. Check blood pressure weekly in month 1, then every 28 days; check INR as indicated if taking warfarin.

Finds changes that may need supportive care, a dose hold or adjustment.

Specialist and cancer tests

If part of your plan
WhenThyroid tests monitor hormone balance. Thyroglobulin and other response tests depend on the cancer subtype and specialist plan; they are not substitutes for scans.

Distinguishes treatment selection, hormone monitoring and cancer response.

The whole treatment journey

Sorafenib: differentiated thyroid cancer: treatment and checks

Progressive, locally advanced or metastatic differentiated thyroid cancer that no longer responds to radioactive iodine.

Sorafenib 400 mg twice daily, every day, until progression or unacceptable side effects.

What each check is for

1Treatment and reviewTwice daily, continuously

This treatment aims to slow cancer growth and help control symptoms. Your team explains the expected benefit, uncertainty and alternatives for your situation.

Ask / act: Consider blood count, kidney and liver tests at Day 14 of the first month. Repeat these bloods and thyroid tests every 28 days. Check blood pressure weekly in month 1, then every 28 days; check INR as indicated if taking warfarin.
