2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Sorafenib

Kinase inhibitor
Painful palms and soles (hand-foot skin reaction), rash and hair thinning
Diarrhoea, tiredness, reduced appetite and weight loss
High blood pressure and thyroid hormone changes
Bleeding, heart ischaemia and impaired wound healing
Rare bowel perforation, severe liver injury and severe skin reactions
Low blood sugar, especially with diabetes treatment; possible effects on fertility and pregnancy

Discuss treatment aim, expected benefit and uncertainty, alternatives and the option of declining; each medicine’s risks, potentially lasting effects, required tests, urgent symptoms, fertility, contraception, pregnancy, breastfeeding and the individual support plan. Tell the team before surgery or dental procedures. Report painful palms or soles early; skin care and early dose review can help prevent severe hand-foot reactions.
