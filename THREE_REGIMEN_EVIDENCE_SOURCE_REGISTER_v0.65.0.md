# SACTCheck v0.65.0 evidence source register

Source review date: 15 August 2026

## NCCP 00546 Version 3

Regimen: Docetaxel 75 mg/m² plus prednisolone

Current NCCP source:
https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6382/546_v3_Docetaxel_75-prednisolone_combination_therapy.pdf

Principal evidence:

TAX 327
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15470213/

Updated survival:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18182665/

Evidence relationship: Protocol defining evidence.

Important limitation: The pivotal trial predates the modern metastatic castration resistant prostate cancer sequencing landscape. Separate docetaxel evidence in hormone sensitive metastatic disease is not used to redefine NCCP 00546.

## NCCP 00583 Version 3b

Regimen: Pembrolizumab plus axitinib

Current NCCP source:
https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6394/583_v3b_Pembrolizumab_200mg_Axitinib_therapy.pdf

Principal evidence:

KEYNOTE 426
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30779529/

Final 5 year analysis:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-025-03867-5

Evidence relationship: Protocol defining evidence.

Important limitation: The pivotal evidence is based on advanced clear cell renal cell carcinoma. Current NCCP eligibility remains the operational source for the encoded population.
