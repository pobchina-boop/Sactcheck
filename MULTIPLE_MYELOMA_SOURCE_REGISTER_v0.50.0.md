## Governance boundary

- The source links and technical encodings do not constitute independent clinical validation.
- Supportive-care/emetogenic classification remains explicitly variable pending specialist pharmacy reconciliation.
- No Haematology protocol is authorised to clear treatment.
- Missing assessment domains remain unassessed and are never assumed normal.
