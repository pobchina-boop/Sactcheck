# Clinician Facing NCCP Change Tracker Copy

## Primary purpose

The tracker helps users answer four questions.

1. Is this regimen linked to the latest reviewed NCCP source?
2. Has a newer or changed NCCP source been detected?
3. What may have changed and why might it matter?
4. Has the change been reviewed and incorporated into SACTCheck?

## Visible pathway

Detect, Compare, Review and Reconcile.

## Status language

1. Baseline registered
2. Initial source check pending
3. Source checked
4. Change requires review
5. Official source link required
6. Review complete
7. Reconciled in SACTCheck

## Governance statement

A detected source change is a signal for review. It cannot alter a clinical rule automatically. The official NCCP source, local governance and independent clinical judgement remain authoritative.
