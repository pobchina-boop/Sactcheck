Treatment Passport safety design
- No patient identifier input or persistence is added.
- Personal fields are blank and intended for handwriting on the physical card.
- QR contains only the regimen/public SACTCheck route.
- Free-text clinical rules are not mined to infer a planned number of cycles.
- Maintenance/continuous treatment uses a separate maintenance/reissue concept.

Antiemetic/supportive-care source design
- NCCP V8 2025 is the Irish default displayed regimen.
- MASCC/ESMO 2023 evidence is an explicitly labelled overlay, not a silent override.
- CUH 2021–2024 supplied sheets are retained as local-reference medication lists.
- Nystatin/chlorhexidine are labelled local mouth-care adjuncts, not universal CINV/mucositis prophylaxis.
- Metoclopramide carries the EMA adult max 30 mg/day and max 5-day duration safety note.

Governance status
This remains a clinical-review/development build. Automated testing is not independent clinical validation. Current NCCP documents, individual prescribing, clinical judgement and local oncology-pharmacy policy remain authoritative. Local clinical/pharmacy review is required before routine patient distribution or prescribing use.
