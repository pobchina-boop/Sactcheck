## Five full-suite retrospective upgrades

| NCCP | Reviewed pathway |

| 00200 | Three-weekly IV trastuzumab: distinct early and metastatic indications |
| 00201 | Weekly IV trastuzumab: metastatic disease |

| 00726 | Pertuzumab/trastuzumab: advanced-disease maintenance after chemotherapy |

These are five upgrades to existing authored suites. They add indication-specific selection rationale and colour-coded clinician evidence summaries, with line/previous-treatment context, comparator, endpoint definitions, analysis maturity, uncertainty and primary sources. Numerical efficacy remains in clinician knowledge. Patients receive plain-language explanations of treatment purpose and selection.

The evidence distinguishes early from metastatic treatment, historical single-agent response from comparative survival, and the complete induction/continuation strategy from maintenance alone. Current alternative-treatment evidence is contextualised without asserting Irish reimbursement or changing NCCP access criteria.

Each pathway includes refreshed patient knowledge, relative cycle timeline, agent-specific body map, longitudinal checks, consent, supportive care, visual guide, passport, treatment card and practical logistics. Forty PDFs were regenerated with matching compatibility copies. Maintenance has a minimal-risk support phase without routine antiemetic or steroid orders. Existing T-DM1 support prescriptions are retained. Monitoring maps describe expected checks; they do not book tests or record completion.
