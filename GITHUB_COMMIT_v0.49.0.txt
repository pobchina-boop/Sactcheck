Add Haematology portal and five plasma-cell protocols (v0.49.0)

Description:
Introduces SACTCheck v0.49.0 with a separate burgundy Haematology SACT portal and five source-linked plasma-cell disorder protocols. Existing 361 solid-tumour protocol JSON files remain unchanged. Haematology encodings remain pending independent Consultant Haematologist and haematology-pharmacy validation and are not authorised for clinical treatment clearance.
