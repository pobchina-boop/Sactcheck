Commit summary

feat(knowledge): add FLOT, osimertinib and olaparib profiles

Commit description

Expands the SACTCheck knowledge base from twenty four to twenty seven detailed regimen profiles.

Adds complete evidence audited profiles for FLOT in perioperative gastric and oesophagogastric junction adenocarcinoma, osimertinib across its T790M positive advanced, first line metastatic and adjuvant EGFR mutated NSCLC settings, and olaparib monotherapy across ovarian, prostate, breast and pancreatic cancer indications.

Adds FLOT4 AIO, AURA3, FLAURA, ADAURA, FLAURA2, SOLO1, SOLO2, PROfound, OlympiA, POLO and PAOLA 1 to the evidence library. Final or long term outcome publications are linked where they materially affect interpretation.

Separates later combination evidence from the encoded monotherapy pathways. FLAURA2 is retained as contextual evidence for osimertinib plus chemotherapy and cannot create an unencoded combination pathway. PAOLA 1 is retained as contextual evidence for olaparib plus bevacizumab and maps to the separate NCCP combination regimen rather than NCCP 00588 monotherapy.

Adds reusable osimertinib and olaparib medicine profiles and updates the primary clinical validation register so all three regimens are recognised as having a structured knowledge profile.

No deterministic treatment threshold, eligibility rule, dose action, organ function rule, treatment schedule or clinical input definition is changed. All 382 clinical protocol JSON files remain byte identical to v0.65.0. Independent consultant oncology and oncology pharmacy validation remains pending.
