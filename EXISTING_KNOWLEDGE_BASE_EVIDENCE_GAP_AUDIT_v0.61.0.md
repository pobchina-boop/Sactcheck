# Existing Knowledge-Base Evidence Gap Audit — v0.61.0

## Method

The fifteen profiles present in v0.60.3 were reviewed using a structured second-pass checklist:

1. protocol-defining trial;
2. evidence for every encoded indication;
3. later add-on or combination evidence;
4. mature follow-up;
5. maintenance or sequencing evidence;
6. biomarker-defined subgroup evidence;
7. major negative or limiting evidence;
8. exact population and schedule applicability.

This process is a targeted completeness audit, not a formal systematic review.

## Profiles with no material new primary record required

KEYNOTE-189, PACIFIC, KEYNOTE-522, CALYPSO, monarchE, IMbrave150, ASCENT and TOPAZ-1 already had the principal trial plus relevant mature follow-up or a clearly declared evidence boundary in the v0.60.3 baseline. They now receive the same visible audit structure as the expanded profiles.

## Residual declared uncertainties

- Weekly paclitaxel evidence strength differs substantially by tumour indication.
- Exact all-histology oesophageal randomised FOLFIRI evidence was not identified in this audit.
- Some upper-GI and shared-regimen mappings use closely related schedules rather than the exact encoded dose schedule.
- TCHP evidence is based on complementary phase II studies; the page does not imply that one study alone proves long-term comparative survival for the exact regimen.
- Trial eligibility, pathology review and treatment-era sequencing may not reproduce routine practice.
- Independent consultant oncology and oncology-pharmacy review remains pending for every profile.
