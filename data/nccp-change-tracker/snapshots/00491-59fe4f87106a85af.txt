NCCP National SACT Regimen

 Leuprorelin 45mg Therapy- 24 week
 INDICATIONS FOR USE:
 Regimen HSE approved
 INDICATION ICD10 Code reimburseme
 nt status*
 Treatment of hormone dependent advanced prostate cancer. C61 00491a N/A
 Treatment of high-risk localized and locally advanced hormone dependent C61 00491b N/A
 prostate cancer in combination with radiotherapy.
 * This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:

 Leuprorelin 45mg is administered subcutaneously once every 24 weeks until disease progression or
 unacceptable toxicity develops.

 The injected solution provides continuous release of leuprorelin acetate over a six -month period.

 Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Cycle
 1 Leuprorelin 45mg SC n/a Every 24 weeks

 ELIGIBILITY:
  Indications as above

 CAUTIONS:
  In patients with a history of or risk factors for QT prolongation and in patients receiving
 concomitant medicinal products that might prolong the QT interval physicians should assess
 the benefit risk ratio

 EXCLUSIONS:
  Hypersensitivity to leuprorelin or any of the excipients
  As sole treatment in prostate cancer patients with spinal cord compression or evidence of
 spinal metastases

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant with expertise in the treatment of prostate
 carcinoma.

 TESTS:
 Baseline tests:
  FBC, renal and liver profile
  Bone profile
  Blood glucose

 Disease monitoring:
 Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as directed
 by the supervising Consultant.

OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
