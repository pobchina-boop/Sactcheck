NCCP National SACT Regimen

 Fulvestrant Therapy
 INDICATIONS FOR USE:
 Regimen HSE approved
 INDICATION ICD10 Code reimbursement
 status*
 Treatment of postmenopausal women with oestrogen receptor positive, C50 00361a N/A
 locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer for disease relapse on or after
 adjuvant anti-oestrogen therapy.
 Treatment of postmenopausal women with oestrogen receptor positive, C50 00361b N/A
 locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer for disease progression on or
 after adjuvant anti-oestrogen therapy.
 * This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:
 The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
 independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.

 Fulvestrant is administered on day 1 and day 15 for cycle 1 and then on day 1 of a 28 day cycle for all
 subsequent cycles until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

 Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Cycle
 1, 15 Fulvestrant 500 mg IM N/A 1
 1 Fulvestrant 500 mg IM N/A Continuous
 Administered as two consecutive 5 ml injections by slow intramuscular injection (1-2 minutes/injection), one in each buttock (gluteal
 area).

 ELIGIBILITY:
  Indications as above
  ECOG performance status 0-2

 CAUTIONS:
  Caution should be taken while administering fulvestrant at the dorsogluteal injection site
 - site related events including sciatica, neuralgia, neuropathic pain, and peripheral
 neuropathy have been reported
  Severe renal impairment
  Mild to moderate hepatic impairment
 EXCLUSIONS:
  Hypersensitivity to fulvestrant or any of the excipients
  Severe hepatic impairment
  Pregnancy
  Breastfeeding

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Medical Oncologist

 TESTS:
 Baseline tests:
  FBC, renal and liver profile

 Regular tests:
  Liver profile as clinically indicated

 Disease monitoring:
 Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as directed by
 the supervising Consultant.

 DOSE MODIFICATIONS:
  Any dose modification should be discussed with a Consultant.

 Renal and Hepatic Impairment:

 Table 1: Dose modification of fulvestrant in renal and hepatic impairment
 Renal Impairment Hepatic Impairment
 CrCl ≥ 30mL/min: no dose adjustment is needed Child-Pugh A: no dose adjustment

 CrCl < 30 mL/min: no need for dose adjustment is expected Child-Pugh B: 50% of the original dose (250mg)

 Haemodialysis: no need for dose adjustment is expected Child-Pugh C: not recommended due to risk of
 hepatotoxicity

 Recommendations for Fulvestrant as per Giraud et al 2023

PREMEDICATIONS: None usually required.

OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE: No specific recommendations

ADVERSE EFFECTS:
 Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) for details.

REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
 Due to the intramuscular route of administration, fulvestrant should be used with caution if treating
 patients with bleeding diatheses, thrombocytopenia or those taking anticoagulant treatment.

DRUG INTERACTIONS:
 Current SmPC and drug interaction databases should be consulted for information.
