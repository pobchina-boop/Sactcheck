NCCP National SACT Regimen

 Goserelin 10.8mg Therapy- 12 weeks
 INDICATIONS FOR USE:
 Regimen HSE approved
 INDICATION ICD10 Code reimbursement
 status*
 Treatment of locally advanced or metastatic hormone sensitive prostate C61 00477a N/A
 cancer
 * This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:

 Goserelin 10.8mg is injected subcutaneously into the anterior abdominal wall once every 12 weeks until
 disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

 Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Cycle
 1 Goserelin 10.8mg SC n/a Every 12 weeks
 Use extra care when administering goserelin to patients with a low BMI and/or who are receiving full anticoagulation medication

 ELIGIBILITY:
  Indication as above

 CAUTIONS:
  Androgen deprivation therapy may prolong the QT interval. In patients with a history of or risk
 factors for QT prolongation and in patients receiving concomitant medicinal products that
 might prolong the QT interval, physicians should assess the benefit risk ratio including the
 potential for Torsade de pointes prior to initiating treatment.

 EXCLUSIONS:
  Hypersensitivity to goserelin or any of the excipients

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant with expertise in the treatment of prostate
 carcinoma.

 TESTS:
 Baseline tests:
  FBC, renal and liver profile
  Bone profile
  Blood glucose
