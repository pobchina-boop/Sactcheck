NCCP National SACT Regimen

 Gemcitabine and DOCEtaxel Intravesical Therapy
 INDICATIONS FOR USE:
 Regimen HSE approved
 INDICATION ICD10 Code Reimbursement
 status*
 BCG-unresponsive high-risk non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) C67 00894a N/A

 Patients with high risk NMIBC who are intolerant of or ineligible for C67 00894b N/A
 intravesical BCG
 * This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:
 The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
 independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.

 Induction:
 Gemcitabine and DOCEtaxel is administered by instillation into the bladder intravesically once weekly for
 6 weeks.
 Maintenance:
 For patients achieving complete response after induction or those with low grade non-invasive
 recurrences.
 Gemcitabine and DOCEtaxel is administered by instillation into the bladder intravesically once a month
 for 18 months*, starting 6 weeks after last dose of induction.
 *indicates standard duration of maintenance treatment but may vary based on patient factors and tolerance

 Order of Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate
 Admin
 1 1 Gemcitabinei 1000mga Intravesical Diluted in NaCl 0.9% to a final volume of 50mL.
 Instil into bladder via catheter (dwell time of 60 minutes)
 Drain gemcitabine from bladder via catheter until flow ceases, then:
 2 1 DOCEtaxelii 40mgb Diluted in NaCl 0.9% to a final volume of 50mL.
 Intravesical
 Instil into bladder via catheter (dwell time of 60 minutes)
 aGemcitabine dose may be rounded to 988mg where a 38mg/ml strength vial is used.

 bDOCEtaxel dose has been rounded up from 37.5mg to enable accurate preparation in line with BRIDGE study.

 Note:
 Dwell time of 90 minutes for either drug may be used where necessary.
 The final volumes of gemcitabine and docetaxel may fall outside the concentration ranges outlined in the product SmPC depending on
 the brand and vial strength used. Local agreement for use should be sought between pharmacy and the treating consultant.

 ELIGIBILITY:
 • Indications as above

 EXCLUSIONS:
 • Hypersensitivity to Gemcitabine, DOCEtaxel or any of their excipients.
 • TURBT less than 3 weeks prior to therapy
 • Recent Haematuria
 • Traumatic catheterisation
 • Active Urinary Tract Infection
 • Pregnancy and Breastfeeding

 CAUTIONS:
 • Patients with small bladder capacity due to increased of bladder contracture

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Urologist.

 TESTS:
 Pre-treatment:

 • Cystoscopy with biopsy or resection to confirm histologic diagnosis
 • Test urine for Urinary Tract Infection (Dipstick). Consult with Urologist if symptomatic for urinary
 tract infection

 Disease monitoring:
 Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as directed
 by the supervising Consultant Urologist.

 If disease free, surveillance cystoscopy with cytology is usually performed every 3 months for 2 years.

 DOSE MODIFICATIONS:
 • Dose modifications may be considered in select cases, discuss with treating urologist

 • Where side effects are severe gemcitabine may be withheld. Treatment as monotherapy with
 docetaxel may continue.

 Renal and Hepatic Impairment:
 • There are no known published dose modification recommendations available for renal and hepatic
 impairment. Given that this is a localised treatment and is expected to have limited or no systemic
 absorption there is unlikely to be a dose modification in this setting.

 SUPPORTIVE CARE:

 EMETOGENIC POTENTIAL:
 • There are no known published recommendations available for emetogenic potential. Given that
 this is a localised treatment and is expected to have limited or no systemic absorption it is unlikely
 that anti-emetic cover is required in this setting.

 • Consider prophylactic ondansetron 4mg PO in patients who report nausea in previous instillation.

 PREMEDICATIONS:
 • Sodium bicarbonate 1200mg PO (600mg tablets x 2) the night before treatment. This alkalises the
 urine to prevent gemcitabine induced bladder pain.
 • Repeat sodium bicarbonate 1200mg dosage 1 hour prior to commencement of therapy.

 OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
 • Consider Ibuprofen 400mg stat for irritative bladder symptoms
 • Consider anticholinergic therapy e.g. mirabegron 50mg PO OD if bladder spasm reported
 • Necessary protective contraception should be used for the duration of treatment.

 Patient education

 • Avoid diuretics, alcohol, and coffee for 4-6 hours prior to therapy and during drug instillation to
 minimise dilution of drug in bladder. If the individual is on a diuretic, it should be delayed until
 after treatment.
 • To assist dwell time and reduce dilution of gemcitabine and DOCEtaxel the patient should not
 drink any fluids over a period of 4 hours before the instillation and until 2 hours after the
 instillation
 • Patients may experience some bladder irritation, with more frequent or painful urination and
 some blood or tissue in the urine.
