TREATMENT:
The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.

For the treatment of malignant pleural mesothelioma PEMEtrexed and CISplatin are administered once every
21 days for a maximum of 4-6 cycles unless the patient experiences disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

For the first line treatment of locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC PEMEtrexed and CISplatin are administered
once every 21 days for a maximum of 4-6 cycles followed by maintenance PEMEtrexed (Reference NCCP regimen
00222) unless the patient experiences disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

 • Indications as above

EXCLUSIONS:
 • Hypersensitivity to PEMEtrexed, CISplatin or any of the excipients
 • PEMEtrexed
 o Creatinine clearance < 45mL/min
 • CISplatin
 o Pre-existing renal impairment
 o Pre-existing neuropathies ≥ grade 2
 o Significant hearing impairment/tinnitus
 • Pregnancy
 • Breastfeeding

PREMEDICATIONS:
 • A corticosteroid should be given the day prior to, on the day of, and the day after PEMEtrexed
 administration. The corticosteroid should be equivalent to 4 mg of dexAMETHasone administered orally
 twice a day
 • Intramuscular injection of vitamin B12 (hydroxycobolamin) (1,000 micrograms) in the week preceding the
 first dose of PEMEtrexed and once every three cycles thereafter. Subsequent vitamin B12 injections may
 be given on the same day as PEMEtrexed

OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:

 • Hydration pre and post CISplatin administration (Reference local policy or see recommendations above)

ADVERSE EFFECTS
