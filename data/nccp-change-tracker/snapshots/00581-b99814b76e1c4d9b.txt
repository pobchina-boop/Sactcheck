 * This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:
 The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
 independent medical judgement, to consider each patient’s individual clinical circumstances.
 Patients being treated for ALL require complex inpatient care in a designated cancer centre with
 comprehensive multidisciplinary team (MDT) availability.

 B (NCCP Regimen 00826 for patients ≤ 60 years or NCCP Regimen 00932 for patients > 60
 years) should be completed before beginning Hyper CVAD (ALL) Maintenance Therapy (POMP)
 (NCCP Regimen 00912).

 • Hyper CVAD (ALL) Maintenance Therapy (POMP) consists of a 28-day cycle which should
 continue for 2 years (24 months) unless the patient experiences disease progression or
 unacceptable toxicity, whichever occurs first.
 Note:
 • Treatment should commence once ANC > 0.75 x 109/L and platelets > 75 x 109/L following Cycle
 8 Part B
 o CD20 negative patients (Refer to NCCP Regimen 00826 for patients ≤ 60 years; NCCP
 Regimen 00932 for patients > 60 years)
 o CD20 positive patients (Refer to NCCP Regimen 00827 for patients ≤ 60 years; NCCP
 Regimen 00933 for patients > 60 years)
 • Dosing of mercaptopurine and methotrexate should be adjusted to maintain the neutrophil
 count between 0.75 and 1.5 x 109/L and platelet count between 75 and 150 x 109/L.
 o Mercaptopurine and methotrexate are usually prescribed every 4 weeks to support dose
 variations (see dose modifications section). If a dose increase is indicated, the dose for the
 next 4-week block should be prescribed in line with this to minimise dose alterations within
 cycle.

 Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

 ELIGIBILITY:
 • Indications as above
 • ECOG 0-2
 o Patients with ECOG 3 may be considered at consultant’s discretion
 EXCLUSIONS:
 • Hypersensitivity to prednisoLONE, mercaptopurine, methotrexate, vinCRIStine, or any of the
 excipients
 • Pregnancy
 • Breastfeeding
 • Women of childbearing potential not using effective contraception

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Haematologist working in the area of haematological
 malignancies.
 TESTS:

 Baseline tests:
 • FBC, renal and liver profile
 • Blood glucose
 • G6PD deficiency test if not completed in Hyper CVAD (ALL) Therapy Part A or Part B
 • Consider TPMT test if not already tested

 Regular tests:
 • FBC weekly for first 4 weeks of Hyper CVAD (ALL) Maintenance Therapy (POMP), and then
 monthly as clinically indicated thereafter
 • Assessment of peripheral neuropathy status prior to each cycle

 Disease monitoring:
 Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test(s) as
 directed by the supervising Consultant.

 DOSE MODIFICATIONS:
 • Any dose modification should be discussed with a Consultant

 SUPPORTIVE CARE:

 EMETOGENIC POTENTIAL:
 • As outlined in NCCP Classification Document for Systemic AntiCancer Therapy (SACT) Induced Nausea and Vomiting
 available on the NCCP website

 Mercaptopurine: Minimal to low (Refer to local policy)
 vinCRIStine: Minimal (Refer to local policy)
 Methotrexate: Minimal to low (Refer to local policy)

 For information:
 Within NCIS regimens, antiemetics have been standardised by the Medical Oncologists and Haemato-oncologists and
 information is available in the following documents:
 • NCCP Supportive Care Antiemetic Medicines for Inclusion in NCIS (Medical Oncology) available on the NCCP website
 • NCCP Supportive Care Antiemetic Medicines for Inclusion in NCIS (Haemato-oncology) available on the NCCP website

 PREMEDICATIONS: None usually required

 OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
 • Prophylactic regimen against vinCRIStine-induced constipation is recommended (Refer to local
 policy)
 • Anti-fungal prophylaxis: Avoid the concurrent use of azoles and vinCRIStine (Refer to local
 policy)
 • Anti-viral prophylaxis (Refer to local policy)
 • Proton pump inhibitor: If a PPI is used it should be held before the administration of
 methotrexate (Refer to local policy).
 • G-CSF prophylaxis (Refer to local policy)
 • PJP prophylaxis (Refer to local policy)
 o Consider interactions between methotrexate and co-trimoxazole. If co-trimoxazole cannot
 be avoided, avoid administration on the same day.
