Recommendations on Reporting on HER2 Status in Breast Cancer Patients

The NCCP Molecular Diagnostics Advisory Group have made the following updated recommendations in
relation to reporting on HER2 status in breast cancer patients.

  All HER2 testing results for breast cancer patients should indicate the HER2 classification, the
 IHC result and where appropriate ISH status using the definitions as set out in the table below:

 HER2 HER2 Test Results (IHC score and ISH status)
 Classification
 HER 2-null 0, no staining
 HER 2-ultralow 0, membranous staining in ≤10% of invasive tumour cells.
 IHC >no staining < 1+
 HER2-low 1+
 HER2-low 2+, ISH not amplified
 HER2-positive 2+, ISH amplified
 HER2-positive 3+

  Reassessment of HER2-0 results may be required in specific patient cohorts at the request of the
 Consultant Medical Oncologist as distinction between 0 no staining, 0 membranous staining in
 ≤10% of invasive tumour cells and 1+ is now clinically relevant if trastuzumab deruxtecan
 treatment is being considered,

  HER2 testing results close to the IHC 0 (no staining), IHC 0 (>no staining < 1+) and IHC 1+
 interpretative thresholds should be examined at high power magnification and consideration
 given to second pathologist review as required.

Recommendation Date: 22/9/2025
