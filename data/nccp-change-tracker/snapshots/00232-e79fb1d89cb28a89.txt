NCCP National SACT Regimen

 Mirvetuximab soravtansine Therapy
 INDICATIONS FOR USE:
 Regimen HSE approved
 INDICATION ICD10 Code reimbursement
 status*
 As monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with folate receptor- C56, 00921a N/A
 alpha (FRα) positive, platinum-resistant high grade serous epithelial C57,
 ovarian, fallopian tube, or primary peritoneal cancer who have received C48
 one to three prior systemic treatment regimens
 * This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:
 The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
 independent medical judgement, to consider each patient’s individual clinical circumstances.

 Mirvetuximab soravtansine is administered at a dose of 6mg/kg adjusted ideal body weight (AIBW) as an
 intravenous (IV) infusion on Day 1 of each cycle.

 Cycle frequency and duration:
 Each cycle is 21 days.

 Treatment should continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

 Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

 *Calculating mirvetuximab soravtansine dose using adjusted ideal body weight (AIBW):
 The total dose of mirvetuximab soravtansine is calculated based on each patient’s AIBW using the
 following formula:

 Female IBW [kg] = 0.9 x height [cm] – 92
 AIBW = IBW [kg] + 0.4 x (Actual weight [kg] – IBW)

 For example, for a female patient who is 165 cm in height and 80 kg in weight:

 First, calculate IBW: IBW = 0.9 x 165 – 92 = 56.5 kg
 Then calculate AIBW: AIBW = 56.5 + 0.4 x (80 – 56.5) = 65.9 kg
 *The IBW and AIBW formulae in this regimen are from the SmPC for Elahere®. The IBW equation is a metric
 derivation of the Devine IBW formula, and is mathematically equivalent to the “Ideal Body weight (IBW)” formula
 that is used in NCIS

 ELIGIBILITY:
 • Indication as above
 • ECOG 0-2FRα tumour status defined as ≥75% viable tumour cells demonstrating moderate
 (2+) and/or strong (3+) membrane staining by immunohistochemistry (IHC), as demonstrated
 by a validated test method.

 CAUTIONS:
 • Ocular conditions requiring ongoing treatment, including active or chronic corneal disorders
 • Patient should be advised to avoid use of contact lenses during treatment
 • Grade ≥2 peripheral neuropathy
 • Non-infectious ILD/pneumonitis

 EXCLUSIONS:
 • Hypersensitivity to mirvetuximab soravtansine or to any of the excipients
 • Pregnancy
 • Breastfeeding

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Medical Oncologist.

 TESTS:

 Baseline tests:
 • FBC, renal and liver profile
 • FRα tumour status testing using the FOLR1 RxDX assay on the VENTANA platform to score
 FRα tumour expression on the request of a Consultant Medical Oncologist, where there is
 an intention to treat with mirvetuximab soravtasine in line with this licensed indication
 • Ophthalmic assessment (including visual acuity and slit lamp exam)
 • Pregnancy test
 • Peripheral neuropathy assessment as clinically indicated

 Regular tests:
 • FBC, renal and liver profile
 • Ophthalmic assessment (including visual acuity and slit lamp exam) as clinically indicated.
 In patients with ≥ Grade 2 ocular adverse reactions, additional ophthalmic exams should
 be conducted at a minimum of every other cycle and as clinically indicated until
 resolution or return to baseline.
 • Peripheral neuropathy assessment as clinically indicated

 Disease monitoring:
 Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as
 directed by the supervising Consultant.

 DOSE MODIFICATIONS:
 • Before the start of each cycle, the patient should be advised to report any new or worsening
 symptoms.
 • In patients who develop new or worsening ocular symptoms, an ophthalmic exam should be
 conducted before dosing. The treating physician should review the patient’s ophthalmic
 examination report before dosing and determine the dose of mirvetuximab soravtansine
 based on the severity of findings in the most severely affected eye.
 • Tables 1 to 3 provide dose reductions and modifications for adverse reactions. The schedule
 of administration should be maintained at a 3-week interval between the doses.
 • Any dose modification should be discussed with a Consultant.

 PREMEDICATIONS:
 • Premedications should be administered prior to each dose of mirvetuximab soravtansine to reduce
 the incidence and severity of infusion related reactions (IRRs), nausea and vomiting.
 Recommended premedications are shown in Table 4.
 Table 4: Recommended premedications prior to each infusion of mirvetuximab soravtansine
 Drug Dose Route
 Paracetamol 1g PO 60 minutes prior to mirvetuximab soravtansine infusion
 Chlorphenamine 10mg IV bolus 60 minutes prior to mirvetuximab soravtansine infusion
 dexAMETHasone 10mg IV bolus 60 minutes prior to mirvetuximab soravtansine infusion
 Ondansetron 16mg PO 30 minutes prior to mirvetuximab soravtansine infusion

 • For patients who experience an IRR Grade ≥2, additional pre-medication with dexAMETHasone
 8mg PO BD (or equivalent) the day before mirvetuximab soravtansine administration should
 be considered. (Refer to table 3 above)

 OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
 • Mirvetuximab soravtansine has moderate influence on the ability to drive and use machines. If
 patients experience visual disturbances, peripheral neuropathy, fatigue, or dizziness during
 treatment with mirvetuximab soravtansine, they should be instructed not to drive or use
 machines until complete resolution of symptoms is confirmed.
 • Lubricating eye drops
 • Topical ophthalmic steroids (under the guidance of an ophthalmologist, for Grade ≥2 corneal
 adverse reactions)
 o For patients found to have signs of Grade ≥2 corneal adverse reactions (keratopathy)
 on slit lamp examination, secondary prophylaxis with ophthalmic topical steroids is
 recommended for subsequent cycles of mirvetuximab soravtansine.
 o Patients should be instructed to use steroid eye drops on the day of infusion and
 through the next 7 days of each subsequent cycle of mirvetuximab soravtansine.
 o Patients should be advised to wait at least 15 minutes after ophthalmic topical steroid
 administration before instilling lubricating eye drops.
 o During treatment with ophthalmic topical steroids the measurement of intraocular
 pressure and an examination with slit lamp should be carried out regularly.
 • Patients of childbearing potential should use effective contraception during treatment with
 mirvetuximab soravtansine and for 7 months after the last dose.
