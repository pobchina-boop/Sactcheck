NCCP National SACT Regimen

 Pembrolizumab in combination with chemoradiotherapy
 INDICATIONS FOR USE:
 Regimen HSE approved
 INDICATION ICD10 Code reimbursement
 status*
 Pembrolizumab in combination with chemoradiotherapy (external beam C53 00943a
 radiation therapy followed by brachytherapy), is indicated for the treatment ODMS
 of FIGO 2014 Stage III - IVA locally advanced cervical cancer in adults who 01/08/2026
 have not received prior definitive therapy.
 * This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:
 The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
 independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.

 Pembrolizumab is administered on Day 1 of each cycle.
 CISplatin is administered on Day 1, 8, 15 on cycle 1, and Day 1 and 8 of cycle 2. An additional dose of
 CISplatin on cycle 2 Day 15 may be given at the discretion of the treating clinician.
 Treatment on cycles 1 and 2 are given concomitantly with radiation therapy.

 Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

 Atypical responses (i.e., an initial transient increase in tumour size or small new lesions within the first few
 months followed by tumour shrinkage) have been observed. It is recommended to continue treatment for
 clinically stable patients with initial evidence of disease progression until disease progression is confirmed.

 Cycle frequency and duration:
 Cycles 1 to 5 are every 21 days.
 Cycles 6 to 20 are every 42 days.

 Treatment should continue for up to 24 months unless the patient experiences disease progression or
 unacceptable toxicity first.

 ELIGIBILITY:
 • Indications as above
 • ECOG 0-1*
 *ECOG 2 may be considered at the discretion of a Consultant Medical Oncologist
 • Adequate hepatic, renal, and bone marrow function

 CAUTIONS:
 • History of serious autoimmune disease
 • Immunosuppressive doses of systemic corticosteroids or other immunosuppressive
 medication(s) (defined as >10mg prednisoLONE/daily (or steroid equivalent, excluding
 inhaled or topical steroids)
 • Any active clinically significant infection requiring therapy
 • Untreated brain metastases
 • History of interstitial lung disease

 EXCLUSIONS:
 • Hypersensitivity to pembrolizumab, CISplatin or any of the excipients.
 • Information regarding prior therapy with an anti PD-1 or anti PD-L1 antibody is Available
 on the NCCP website
 • Pre-existing renal impairment
 • Significant hearing impairment/tinnitus
 • Pregnancy
 • Breastfeeding

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Medical Oncologist.

 TESTS:

 Baseline tests:
 • FBC, renal and liver profile
 • Blood glucose
 • Thyroid function test
 • Audiology referral as clinically indicated

 Regular tests:
 • FBC, renal and liver profile prior to each cycle
 • Blood glucose prior to each cycle
 • Thyroid Function test every 3 to 6 weeks
 • Cortisol as clinically indicated
 • Audiology referral as clinically indicated

 Disease monitoring:
 Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as
 directed by the supervising Consultant.

 DOSE MODIFICATIONS:
 • Any dose modification should be discussed with a Consultant.

 Pembrolizumab dose modifications:
 • Dose reduction is not recommended for pembrolizumab.
 • Management of immune-related adverse reactions may require withholding of a dose or
 permanent discontinuation of pembrolizumab therapy and institution of systemic high-
 dose corticosteroid (see table 4).

 CISplatin dose modifications:
 • Dose reductions to manage chemotherapy induced adverse reactions are permitted for
 CISplatin treatment may be delayed to allow sufficient time for recovery. Discuss with
 consultant.
