NCCP National SACT Regimen

 vinBLAStine and Methotrexate Therapy
 INDICATIONS FOR USE:
 Regimen HSE approved
 INDICATION ICD10 Code Reimbursement
 Status*
 Treatment of advanced aggressive fibromatosis M72 00554a Hospital

 *This applies to post 2012 indications

 TREATMENT:
 The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
 independent medical judgement, to consider each patient's individual clinical circumstances.

 vinBLAStine and methotrexate are administered on days 1, 8 and 15 of each cycle.

 Cycle frequency and duration:
 Each cycle is 28 days.

 Treatment should continue for a total of 12 cycles unless the patient experiences disease progression or
 unacceptable toxicity.

 Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

 ELIGIBILITY:
 • Indications as above

 EXCLUSIONS:
 • Hypersensitivity to vinBLAStine, methotrexate or any of the excipients.
 • Creatinine clearance less than 30 mL/min
 • Large third space fluid accumulations (significant ascites, large pleural effusion or other large
 lobulated fluid accumulations)
 • Pregnancy
 • Breastfeeding

 PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
 The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Medical Oncologist

REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
• Pleural effusion or ascites: Methotrexate should be used with caution in patients with pleural effusions or
 ascites, as methotrexate may accumulate in third space fluid compartments

DRUG INTERACTIONS:
 • Current SmPC and drug interaction databases should be consulted for information.
