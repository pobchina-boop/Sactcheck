Tests in your plan

Before treatment

WhenBlood count, kidney/liver profile, LDH, urate, lung-function tests, heart ultrasound, virology screen and kidney-filtration assessment. Measured GFR is preferred when feasible; hearing tests if indicated. Confirm sufficient collected stem cells and transplant fitness.

Confirm diagnosis, suitability and the required safety checks.

During treatment and follow-up

WhenBlood count and kidney/liver profile daily during therapy, with fluid balance and daily weights. The transplant team confirms infection monitoring, blood-product support and when counts have recovered.

Detect toxicity and assess response with the appropriate tests.

The whole treatment journey

High-dose carboplatin + etoposide with stem-cell rescue: treatment and checks

Relapsed germ-cell cancer · specialist transplant pathway

Carboplatin AUC7 is followed by etoposide 750 mg/m² on days −5, −4 and −3. Your stored stem cells are infused on day 0. Growth-factor injections start on day +1 and continue until the team confirms neutrophil recovery. These day numbers are relative to the stem-cell infusion.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewChemotherapy days −5 to −3 · stem cells day 0

Aim for lasting cancer control or cure in selected relapsed germ-cell cancer, using high-dose chemotherapy with your own stored stem cells.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999.
