Tests in your plan

Before treatment

WhenConfirm the FGFR3 result and previous treatment. Blood count, kidney/liver profile, phosphate, urea/electrolytes, heart history/examination, ECG and eye examination are required at baseline.

Confirm diagnosis, suitability and the required safety checks.

During treatment and follow-up

WhenPhosphate is checked on days 14–21 and monthly afterwards, more often if elevated. Eye examinations are monthly for the first four months and every three months thereafter; new symptoms need prompt review. Repeat blood count, kidney/liver tests, electrolytes and ECG under the prescribed plan.

Detect toxicity and assess response with the appropriate tests.

The whole treatment journey

Erdafitinib: targeted tablets: treatment and checks

FGFR3-selected advanced urothelial cancer

The usual starting dose is8 mg once daily. The team may increase it to9 mg after reviewing phosphate and toxicity on days 14–21. Never change the dose yourself. Treatment is continuous until progression or unacceptable toxicity; the28-day view is only a reference.

What each check is for

1Treatment reviewDaily tablets · early phosphate review on days 14–21

Help control advanced urothelial cancer with a susceptible FGFR3 alteration after previous PD1/PDL1 treatment.

Ask / act: Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999.
