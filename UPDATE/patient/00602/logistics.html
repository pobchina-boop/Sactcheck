TIP: paclitaxel, ifosfamide and cisplatin

National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) 00602 · Source-reconciled feasibility draft; independent clinician, pharmacy and patient review pending.

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before the visit

Confirm the five-day attendance/admission plan, mesna timings, hydration, allergy prevention and growth-factor prescription.

STEP 2

Time in the unit

Paclitaxel runs over three hours on day 1, cisplatin over one hour and ifosfamide over two hours on days 1–5. Mesna is given immediately before ifosfamide and again four and eight hours after the ifosfamide infusion finishes. The unit coordinates the complete schedule; do not calculate home timings yourself.

STEP 3

Access and pump care

Suitable intravenous access is needed for repeated infusions. Report drip-site pain or swelling immediately. Admission and line choice are agreed locally.

STEP 4

Home support

Cisplatin needs a high-risk anti-sickness plan and prescribed hydration/electrolytes. Ifosfamide needs mesna and adequate fluids. Paclitaxel needs prescribed allergy premedication; reconcile steroid doses with antiemetics. Aprepitant may increase ifosfamide brain toxicity, so pharmacy must review the combination. Growth factor is included from day 6. Retain prescribed mouth care, pain relief and stomach protection.

STEP 5

Next appointment

Confirm the next treatment, tests and any scan before leaving.
