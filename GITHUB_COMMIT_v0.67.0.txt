Commit summary
feat(knowledge): add three evidence audited regimen profiles

Commit description
Expands the SACTCheck knowledge base from twenty seven to thirty detailed regimen profiles.

Adds complete evidence audited profiles for atezolizumab plus carboplatin and etoposide in first line extensive stage small cell lung cancer, pertuzumab plus trastuzumab and docetaxel in first line HER2 positive metastatic breast cancer, and dabrafenib plus trametinib across metastatic and adjuvant BRAF V600 mutated melanoma.

Adds IMpower133, CLEOPATRA, COMBI d, COMBI v and COMBI AD to the principal evidence library, together with mature overall survival and long term follow up where available.

The atezolizumab profile separates the original IMpower133 trial administration from current NCCP intravenous and subcutaneous atezolizumab options. The melanoma profile separates metastatic and adjuvant evidence and explicitly retains the statistical limitation of the final COMBI AD overall survival analysis.

Adds reusable etoposide, dabrafenib and trametinib medicine profiles and updates the primary clinical validation register so all three regimens are recognised as having structured knowledge profiles.

No deterministic treatment threshold, eligibility rule, dose action, organ function rule, treatment schedule or clinical input definition is changed. All 382 clinical protocol JSON files remain byte identical to v0.66.0. Independent consultant oncology and oncology pharmacy validation remains pending.
