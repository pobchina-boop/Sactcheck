# Five-Regimen Evidence Source Register — v0.56.1

## Scope

This register documents the five detailed Regimen info pages introduced in SACTCheck v0.56.1. The current NCCP protocol remains the operational source for assessment and prescribing. The publications below provide supporting clinical context only.

## 1. NCCP 00382 Version 3 — Trifluridine and Tipiracil (Lonsurf®) Therapy

- **Protocol identifier:** `nccp-00382-v3`
- **Tumour group:** Gastrointestinal
- **Protocol indication:** Previously treated metastatic colorectal cancer; 35 mg/m² twice daily on days 1–5 and 8–12 every 28 days.
- **Official NCCP source:** https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6348/382_v3_trifluridine_and_tipiracil_Lonsurf_therapy.pdf
- **Principal study:** RECOURSE
- **Publication:** Randomized Trial of TAS-102 for Refractory Metastatic Colorectal Cancer
- **Citation:** New England Journal of Medicine (2015)
- **PMID:** 25970050
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25970050/
- **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa1414325
- **DOI link:** https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414325
- **Study design:** Phase 3 randomised placebo-controlled trial
- **Population:** 800 patients with metastatic colorectal cancer refractory to, or intolerant of, standard therapies.
- **Intervention:** Trifluridine/tipiracil plus best supportive care.
- **Comparator:** Placebo plus best supportive care.
- **Primary endpoint:** Overall survival.
- **Selected findings:**
  - Median overall survival was 7.1 months versus 5.3 months; hazard ratio for death 0.68.
  - Median progression-free survival was 2.0 months versus 1.7 months; hazard ratio 0.48.
- **Evidence match:** Exact medicine, schedule family and disease setting
- **Important limitation:** Placebo-controlled later-line population. The publication supports trifluridine/tipiracil monotherapy and does not itself establish a trifluridine/tipiracil plus bevacizumab protocol.
- **Content status:** Draft — review pending

## 2. NCCP 00568 Version 5 — Pembrolizumab, Pemetrexed and Carboplatin AUC5 Therapy

- **Protocol identifier:** `nccp-00568-v5`
- **Tumour group:** Lung
- **Protocol indication:** First-line metastatic non-squamous NSCLC without EGFR or ALK alterations.
- **Official NCCP source:** https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6891/568_V5_Pembrolizumab_PEMEtrexed_CARBOplatin.pdf
- **Principal study:** KEYNOTE-189
- **Publication:** Pembrolizumab plus Chemotherapy in Metastatic Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer
- **Citation:** New England Journal of Medicine (2018)
- **PMID:** 29658856
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29658856/
- **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa1801005
- **DOI link:** https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1801005
- **Study design:** Phase 3 randomised placebo-controlled trial
- **Population:** 616 patients with previously untreated metastatic non-squamous NSCLC without sensitising EGFR or ALK alterations.
- **Intervention:** Pembrolizumab plus pemetrexed and platinum chemotherapy, followed by protocol-defined maintenance treatment.
- **Comparator:** Placebo plus pemetrexed and platinum chemotherapy.
- **Primary endpoint:** Overall survival and progression-free survival.
- **Selected findings:**
  - Estimated 12-month overall survival was 69.2% versus 49.4%; hazard ratio for death 0.49.
  - Median progression-free survival was 8.8 months versus 4.9 months; hazard ratio 0.52.
  - At 5 years, overall survival was 19.4% versus 11.3% in the updated analysis.
- **Evidence match:** Exact immunotherapy and pemetrexed-platinum strategy; platinum selection narrowed locally
- **Important limitation:** KEYNOTE-189 allowed cisplatin or carboplatin, whereas this NCCP protocol specifies carboplatin. The current NCCP schedule and eligibility criteria remain authoritative.
- **Linked follow-up publications:**
  - Five-year outcomes: Pembrolizumab Plus Pemetrexed and Platinum in Nonsquamous Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer: 5-Year Outcomes From the Phase 3 KEYNOTE-189 Study. Journal of Clinical Oncology (2023). PMID 36809080; DOI 10.1200/JCO.22.01989.
- **Content status:** Draft — review pending

## 3. NCCP 00655 Version 3a — Durvalumab 1500 mg Monotherapy – 28 day

- **Protocol identifier:** `nccp-00655-v3a`
- **Tumour group:** Lung
- **Protocol indication:** Locally advanced, unresectable stage III NSCLC with PD-L1 expression ≥1% whose disease has not progressed following definitive platinum-based concurrent chemoradiotherapy.
- **Official NCCP source:** https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6883/655_V3a_Durvalumab_1500mg.pdf
- **Principal study:** PACIFIC
- **Publication:** Durvalumab after Chemoradiotherapy in Stage III Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer
- **Citation:** New England Journal of Medicine (2017)
- **PMID:** 28885881
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28885881/
- **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa1709937
- **DOI link:** https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1709937
- **Study design:** Phase 3 randomised placebo-controlled trial
- **Population:** 713 patients with unresectable stage III NSCLC without progression after concurrent platinum-based chemoradiotherapy.
- **Intervention:** Durvalumab 10 mg/kg every 2 weeks for up to 12 months.
- **Comparator:** Placebo every 2 weeks for up to 12 months.
- **Primary endpoint:** Progression-free survival and overall survival.
- **Selected findings:**
  - Median progression-free survival was 16.8 months versus 5.6 months; hazard ratio 0.52 in the initial analysis.
  - The 24-month overall-survival rate was 66.3% versus 55.6%; hazard ratio for death 0.68.
  - Estimated 5-year overall survival was 42.9% versus 33.4% in the updated analysis.
- **Evidence match:** Exact treatment strategy and disease setting; administration schedule differs
- **Important limitation:** The PACIFIC trial used weight-based dosing every 2 weeks. The current NCCP protocol uses 1500 mg every 28 days and applies current European and local eligibility criteria, including PD-L1 requirements.
- **Linked follow-up publications:**
  - Overall-survival analysis: Overall Survival with Durvalumab after Chemoradiotherapy in Stage III NSCLC. New England Journal of Medicine (2018). PMID 30280658; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1809697.
  - Five-year outcomes: Five-Year Survival Outcomes From the PACIFIC Trial: Durvalumab After Chemoradiotherapy in Stage III Non-Small-Cell Lung Cancer. Journal of Clinical Oncology (2022). PMID 35108059; DOI 10.1200/JCO.21.01308.
- **Content status:** Draft — review pending

## 4. NCCP 00857 Version 3 — Pembrolizumab, Carboplatin AUC 5 and weekly Paclitaxel 80 mg/m² followed by Doxorubicin and Cyclophosphamide (AC 60/600) Therapy

- **Protocol identifier:** `nccp-00857-v3`
- **Tumour group:** Breast
- **Protocol indication:** Pembrolizumab in combination with chemotherapy as neoadjuvant treatment, then continued as monotherapy as adjuvant treatment after surgery, for adults with locally advanced or early-stage triple-negative breast cancer at high risk of recurrence.
- **Official NCCP source:** https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6450/857_v3_Pembro_Carbo_5_Pacli_80_AC60_600.pdf
- **Principal study:** KEYNOTE-522
- **Publication:** Pembrolizumab for Early Triple-Negative Breast Cancer
- **Citation:** New England Journal of Medicine (2020)
- **PMID:** 32101663
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32101663/
- **DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa1910549
- **DOI link:** https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1910549
- **Study design:** Phase 3 randomised placebo-controlled trial
- **Population:** 1174 patients with previously untreated stage II or III triple-negative breast cancer.
- **Intervention:** Neoadjuvant pembrolizumab plus platinum-containing chemotherapy, followed by surgery and adjuvant pembrolizumab.
- **Comparator:** Matched placebo plus neoadjuvant chemotherapy, followed by surgery and adjuvant placebo.
- **Primary endpoint:** Pathological complete response and event-free survival; overall survival was subsequently reported.
- **Selected findings:**
  - Pathological complete response was 64.8% versus 51.2% at the first interim analysis.
  - Estimated 36-month event-free survival was 84.5% versus 76.8%; hazard ratio 0.63.
  - Estimated 60-month overall survival was 86.6% versus 81.7% in the later analysis.
- **Evidence match:** Exact perioperative treatment strategy; current phase schedule encoded separately
- **Important limitation:** The full treatment sequence, phase-specific chemotherapy details and eligibility criteria must be checked against the current NCCP protocol. Evidence outcomes apply to the complete perioperative strategy rather than an isolated phase.
- **Linked follow-up publications:**
  - Event-free survival: Event-free Survival with Pembrolizumab in Early Triple-Negative Breast Cancer. New England Journal of Medicine (2022). PMID 35139274; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2112651.
  - Overall survival: Overall Survival with Pembrolizumab in Early-Stage Triple-Negative Breast Cancer. New England Journal of Medicine (2024). PMID 39282906; DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2409932.
- **Content status:** Draft — review pending

## 5. NCCP 00624 Version 3 — Carboplatin AUC 5 and Pegylated Liposomal Doxorubicin 30 mg/m² Therapy — 28 day

- **Protocol identifier:** `nccp-00624-v3`
- **Tumour group:** Gynaecology
- **Protocol indication:** Platinum-sensitive relapsed/recurrent ovarian, primary peritoneal or fallopian tube cancer.
- **Official NCCP source:** https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6685/624_V3_Carbo_AUC5_pegylated_dox_30.pdf
- **Principal study:** CALYPSO
- **Publication:** Pegylated Liposomal Doxorubicin and Carboplatin Compared with Paclitaxel and Carboplatin for Patients with Platinum-Sensitive Ovarian Cancer in Late Relapse
- **Citation:** Journal of Clinical Oncology (2010)
- **PMID:** 20498395
- **PubMed:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20498395/
- **DOI:** 10.1200/JCO.2009.25.7519
- **DOI link:** https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.2009.25.7519
- **Study design:** Phase 3 randomised trial
- **Population:** 976 patients with platinum-sensitive relapsed or recurrent ovarian cancer more than 6 months after prior platinum and taxane treatment.
- **Intervention:** Carboplatin AUC 5 plus pegylated liposomal doxorubicin 30 mg/m² every 4 weeks.
- **Comparator:** Carboplatin AUC 5 plus paclitaxel 175 mg/m² every 3 weeks.
- **Primary endpoint:** Progression-free survival.
- **Selected findings:**
  - Median progression-free survival was 11.3 months versus 9.4 months; hazard ratio 0.821.
  - The carboplatin and pegylated liposomal doxorubicin arm had less alopecia, hypersensitivity and sensory neuropathy, but more hand-foot syndrome, nausea and mucositis.
  - Final overall survival was similar between treatment groups.
- **Evidence match:** Exact component combination, dose schedule and recurrent disease setting
- **Important limitation:** The comparator was carboplatin plus paclitaxel and the trial population had platinum-sensitive recurrence. Individual suitability and all current treatment criteria remain governed by the NCCP protocol.
- **Linked follow-up publications:**
  - Final overall-survival analysis: Final overall survival results of phase III GCIG CALYPSO trial of pegylated liposomal doxorubicin and carboplatin vs paclitaxel and carboplatin in platinum-sensitive ovarian cancer patients. British Journal of Cancer (2012). PMID 22836511; DOI 10.1038/bjc.2012.307.
- **Content status:** Draft — review pending
