# v0.58.0 Next-Five Regimen Source Register

Source-check date: 5 August 2026  
Review state: Draft — consultant oncology and oncology-pharmacy review pending

The current NCCP regimen PDF is the operational source. Trial publications provide indication-specific evidence context and do not replace the protocol.

## NCCP 00209 v10a — Modified FOLFOX-6

- Official protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6549/209_v10a_FOLFOX_6_Modified.pdf`
- Evidence context: adjuvant colon cancer
- Primary trial: MOSAIC, PMID 15175436, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa032709
- Long-term publication: PMID 26527776, DOI 10.1200/JCO.2015.63.4238
- Mapping limitation: MOSAIC used FOLFOX4 and does not establish every disease context in the shared NCCP modified FOLFOX-6 protocol.

## NCCP 00515 v7 — Modified FOLFIRINOX

- Official protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6516/515_v7_FOLFIRINOX_modified.pdf`
- Evidence context: adjuvant resected pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma
- Primary trial: PRODIGE 24/CCTG PA.6, PMID 30575490, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1809775
- Five-year publication: DOI 10.1001/jamaoncol.2022.3829
- Mapping limitation: the pivotal evidence is adjuvant and should not be presented as direct evidence for all metastatic uses of the shared protocol.

## NCCP 00722 v2 — TCHP

- Official protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6598/722_v2_TCHP.pdf`
- Evidence context: neoadjuvant HER2-positive early/locally advanced breast cancer
- Primary trial: TRYPHAENA, PMID 23704196, DOI 10.1093/annonc/mdt182
- Long-term publication: DOI 10.1016/j.ejca.2017.10.021
- Mapping limitation: phase 2 cardiac-safety and activity study; not designed as a definitive comparison between treatment arms.

## NCCP 00619 v4a — Adjuvant abemaciclib with endocrine therapy

- Official protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6415/619_Abemaciclib.pdf`
- Evidence context: high-risk, node-positive, HR-positive/HER2-negative early breast cancer
- Primary trial: monarchE, PMID 32954927, DOI 10.1200/JCO.20.02514
- Five-year publication: PMID 38194616, DOI 10.1200/JCO.23.01994
- Mapping limitation: patient selection must follow the current NCCP indication rather than historical trial cohorts or obsolete biomarker wording in isolation.

## NCCP 00831 v2a — Atezolizumab plus bevacizumab

- Official protocol: `https://healthservice.hse.ie/documents/6432/831_v2a_Atezolizumab_Bevacizumab_Therapy.pdf`
- Evidence context: first-line unresectable/advanced hepatocellular carcinoma
- Primary trial: IMbrave150, PMID 32402160, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1915745
- Updated publication: DOI 10.1016/j.jhep.2021.11.030
- Mapping limitation: trial population and protocol requirements, including liver function and bleeding/variceal assessment, must be respected; outcomes should not be extrapolated to materially different populations without clinical judgement.
