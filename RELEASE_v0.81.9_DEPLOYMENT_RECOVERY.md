# SACTCheck v0.81.9 — deployment recovery + streamlined navigation

This patch repairs the failed 12 October Pages deployment without modifying protected guide/passport/card PDFs. It restores the guide fingerprints from the last successful v0.81.9 deployment while retaining the new single-page 00568 patient treatment reference.

## UI refinement
- Clinic Workflow is reduced to one compact row/action per function.
- The redundant Treatment Passport button is removed from Clinic Workflow because the Patient Portal now links the guide, passport and SACTCard in one place.
- The patient reference page removes duplicate hero tool buttons and reduces its table of contents to six anchors.
- Printable companion tools remain available in the Patient tools section.

No dose, threshold, assessment, consent, antiemetic, passport, SACTCard or QR clinical content is changed.
