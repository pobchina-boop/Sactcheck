- All 41 have merged clinician knowledge profiles after applying the actual loader order. Shared pembrolizumab/bevacizumab colorectal contexts resolve to their correct gastrointestinal context. `baseline-inventory.json` records actual files, hashes, source versions and context selection.
- Forty contexts already have the approved three-page guide, one-page wallet sheet and seven-section full handbook. 00692 is the structural exception. File presence and seven headings alone did **not** prove full support-sheet content, which is why C02 was found separately.
- Protocol metadata source version, patient profile source version and displayed identity match across all seven checked HTML layers for all 41. Older suite/template release numbers alone were not treated as defects.
- The shared global 00455/00558 source-check stamp is older than their retained colorectal-specific November 5 audit. Do not label those colorectal contexts unaudited based solely on the shared stamp.
- All 81 existing QR PNGs decode to the intended exact regimen/context destination. All 82 rasterized guide/wallet QR checks also match the intended destination. 00692's existing legacy QR correctly points to its portal; it does not have the new reference QR yet. No live destination HTTP check is claimed here.
- Every guide PDF retains all its authored anatomy explanation strings; the rich body bitmap is retained in portal/guide HTML. One false-positive text probe caused by 00227's duplicate dictionary key was manually resolved: both descriptions exist, while the actual pin overlay defect remains C04.
- All 40 existing knowledge PDFs retain every authored day-group title, explanation and action after normalized text comparison. This checks content retention, not visual readability; C03 remains a real layout defect.
- All 41 registry guide destinations exist. Five explicitly shared route selectors reject unsupported contexts to the scope page; their CRC paths stay separate from breast/lung routes. Manual inspection confirmed appropriate scope wording for bevacizumab components, pembrolizumab, 00551 and 00890.
- 00212/00214/00215 context support filenames intentionally use `support-bevacizumab.*`; they are not the shared root regimen phase IDs. Their actual linked phase files exist, and their drafts explicitly require the separately prescribed chemotherapy support plan. They do not falsely classify the whole combination as minimal emetic risk.

## Manual clinical-content consistency review

The purpose, schedule, summary schedule and phase text were read across all 41. Detailed administration, monitoring, day groups and selected consent/support text were additionally inspected for weekly/fortnightly cetuximab, FOLFOX4, continuous fluorouracil/RT, FOLFOXIRI, oral agents, immunotherapy transitions and maintenance.

- Weekly cetuximab combinations 00328/00330/00692 have Day 8 visits; fortnightly 00331/00585/00732/00733 explicitly have no Day 8 infusion. Panitumumab 00447/00448 likewise distinguishes the 14-day schedule.
- 00210 explicitly preserves NCCP's Day 2 folinic-acid attribution ambiguity for pharmacy confirmation, rather than silently importing 00692's Day 2 folinic acid. Both use two 22-hour infusions. No generic 46-hour replacement is appropriate.
- 00421 uses fluorouracil continuously during the radiotherapy course; 00586 uses capecitabine on Days 1–5 of each seven-day cycle. 00691 separates six induction FOLFIRINOX cycles from five weeks of capecitabine/RT and does not treat radiation attendance as the infusion calendar.
- 00555/00783 main schedules correctly use 48-hour pumps. The contradictory reusable access section is explicitly captured as C01.
- 00791 does not inherit oxaliplatin or irinotecan and describes maintenance as one possible setting. Bevacizumab component routes retain independent chemotherapy plans.
- 00551 post-chemotherapy and 00900 first-line nivolumab/ipilimumab retain different starting nivolumab doses, maintenance options and overall duration limits. Both specify first nivolumab-only treatment three weeks after the last combination. These are meaningful clinical differences, not presentation inconsistency.
- Regorafenib early liver/BP checks, fruquintinib first-month BP checks and oral dosing/rest blocks are distinct. DPD, conditional ECG/INR, continuing EGFR mineral checks and immunotherapy follow-up are not replaced by a generic testing calendar. Imaging/marker intervals remain individual where no universal interval is authored.
- 00207 legitimately includes bowel and selected head-and-neck radiotherapy settings and consistently marks RT as conditional. Broad shared indications in 00209/00227/00321 are likewise explicit; they were not mistaken for context contamination.
- Placeholder daytime/out-of-hours phone numbers remain visibly marked as examples. Their presence is requested design content, not a functioning urgent-contact service. Team completion remains necessary before patient use.

## Limits

This report does not establish current clinical source validity independently of the source-audit agent, nor every clinical rule's boundary correctness. It does not certify browser interaction, camera scanning, device installation, physical print dimensions, live deployment, independent oncology/pharmacy/PPI approval, or the cross-tumour MVP consistency gate. The v0.84.36 generation should be followed by fresh read-only probes and rendered visual inspection; baseline results must not be relabelled as final passes.
