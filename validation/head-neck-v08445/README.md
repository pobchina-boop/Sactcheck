Read `release-validation.json` for the final command outcome and declared limits.
`scope.json` defines the ten protocols and nineteen clinical patient contexts.
`clinical-migration.json` binds current protocol bytes to official sources and
source-derived cases. `asset-migrations.json` is the reviewed final asset record;
its baseline hashes are verified against the pinned v0.84.44 Git commit.

`published-pdf-summary.json` combines the two complete book-verification reports.
The `books-*.json.gz` files retain exact full reports using lossless compression.
`validated-pack-baseline.json` protects the nineteen refreshed guide/card pairs.
`clinical-companions-verification-final.json` records standalone companion checks.
`canonical-retention-verification.json` independently checks preserved unique
clauses. `consistency-matrix.csv` records the affected context/service coverage.

`browser-and-visual-review.json`, `local-http-verification.json` and
`canonical-route-verification.json` distinguish local interaction, local bytes,
existing live destinations and the six unpublished routes. No local check
establishes v45 deployment, independent clinical approval or real-device behavior.

The protocol subdirectories retain retrieved sources, before snapshots, proposed
encodings, current-source tests and their results. `design-proposals`,
`provenance-proposals`, `patient-authoring` and `asset-migrations-*.json` preserve
intermediate review history; they are not alternative final implementations.
Use the repository's active tools and final records, not copied proposal scripts.
`regression-compatibility-review.json` explains final historical-test updates;
`pre-final-regression-failures.json.gz` retains the resolved failures.

The update is for manual application. No remote commit, push, PR, merge or
publication was performed during preparation.
