L67@P1: TESTS:
L68@P1: Baseline tests:
L69@P1: • FBC, renal and liver profileDPD testing prior to first treatment with 5-Fluorouracil using
L70@P1: phenotype and/or genotype testing unless patient has been previously tested
L71@P1: o In patients with moderate or severe renal impairment, blood uracil levels used for
L72@P1: dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD) phenotyping should be interpreted with
L73@P1: caution, as impaired kidney function can lead to increased uracil blood levels.
L74@P1: Consequently, there is an increased risk for incorrect diagnosis of DPD deficiency, which
L75@P1: may result in under dosing of 5-Fluorouracil or other fluoropyrimidines, leading to
L76@P1: reduced treatment efficacy. Genotype testing for DPD deficiency should be considered
L77@P1: for patients with renal impairment
L78@P1: Regular tests:
L79@P1: • FBC, renal and liver profile weekly throughout treatment
L80@P1: Disease monitoring:
L81@P1: Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as directed
L82@P1: by the supervising Consultant.

L141@P2: ADVERSE EFFECTS / REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L142@P2: The adverse effects listed are not exhaustive. Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics for full details
L143@P2: • Myocardial ischaemia and angina: Cardiotoxicity is a serious complication during treatment with 5-
L144@P2: Fluorouracil. Patients, especially those with a prior history of cardiac disease or other risk factors, treated
L145@P2: with 5-Fluorouracil, should be carefully monitored during therapy.
L146@P2: • DPD deficiency: DPD is an enzyme encoded by the DPYD gene which is responsible for the breakdown of
L147@P2: fluoropyrimidines. Patients with DPD deficiency are therefore at increased risk of fluoropyrimidine-related
L148@P2: toxicity, including for example stomatitis, diarrhoea, mucosal inflammation, neutropenia and neurotoxicity.
L149@P2: Treatment with 5-Fluorouracil, capecitabine or tegafur-containing medicinal products is contraindicated in
L150@P2: patients with known complete DPD deficiency. Consider a reduced starting dose in patients with identified

L171@P3: DRUG INTERACTIONS:
L172@P3: • Marked elevations of prothrombin time and INR have been reported in patients stabilized on warfarin
L173@P3: therapy following initiation of 5-Fluorouracil regimes.
L174@P3: • Concurrent administration of 5-Fluorouracil and phenytoin may result in increased serum levels of
L175@P3: phenytoin.
L176@P3: • 5-Fluorouracil is contraindicated in combination with brivudin, sorivudin and analogues as these are potent
L177@P3: inhibitors of the 5-Fluorouracil-metabolising enzyme DPD.
L178@P3: • Current drug interaction databases should be consulted for more information.
