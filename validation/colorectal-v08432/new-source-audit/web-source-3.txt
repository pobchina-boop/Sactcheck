L209@P4: ADVERSE EFFECTS / REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L210@P4: The adverse effects listed are not exhaustive. Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics for full
L211@P4: details.
L212@P4: • Neutropenia: Fever or other evidence of infection must be assessed promptly and treated
L213@P4: aggressively
L214@P4: • Gastrointestinal toxicity: Patients treated with fluorouracil should be closely monitored for
L215@P4: diarrhoea and managed appropriately.
L216@P4: • Myocardial ischaemia and angina: Cardiotoxicity is a serious complication during treatment with
L217@P4: fluorouracil. Patients, especially those with a prior history of cardiac disease or other risk factors,
L218@P4: treated with fluorouracil, should be carefully monitored during therapy.
L219@P4: • Dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD) deficiency: DPD is an enzyme encoded by the DPYD gene
L220@P4: which is responsible for the breakdown of fluoropyrimidines. Patients with DPD deficiency are
L221@P4: therefore at increased risk of fluoropyrimidine-related toxicity, including for example stomatitis,
L222@P4: diarrhoea, mucosal inflammation, neutropenia and neurotoxicity. Treatment with 5-Fluorouracil,
L223@P4: capecitabine or tegafur-containing medicinal products is contraindicated in patients with known
L224@P4: complete DPD deficiency. Consider a reduced starting dose in patients with identified partial DPD
L225@P4: deficiency. Initial dose reduction may impact the efficacy of treatment. In the absence of serious
L226@P4: toxicity, subsequent doses may be increased with careful monitoring. Therapeutic drug monitoring
L227@P4: (TDM) of fluorouracil may improve clinical outcomes in patients receiving continuous 5-Fluorouracil
L228@P4: infusions.
L229@P4: • Hand-foot syndrome (HFS): HFS, also known as palmar-plantar erythrodysaesthesia (PPE) has been
L230@P4: reported as an unusual complication of high dose bolus or protracted continuous therapy for 5-
L231@P4: Fluorouracil (see Table 3 for dose modifications).
L232@P4: DRUG INTERACTIONS:
L233@P4: • Marked elevations of prothrombin time and INR have been reported in patients stabilized on warfarin
L234@P4: therapy following initiation of fluorouracil regimes.
L235@P4: • Concurrent administration of 5-Fluorouracil and phenytoin may result in increased serum levels of
L236@P4: phenytoin
L237@P4: • 5-Fluorouracil is contraindicated in combination with brivudin, sorivudin and analogues as these are
L238@P4: potent inhibitors of the 5-Fluorouracil metabolising enzyme dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD).
L239@P4: • Current drug interaction databases should be consulted for more information.

L63@P1: ELIGIBILITY:
L64@P1: • Indication as above
L65@P1: • ECOG 0-2
L66@P1: • Adequate haematological, renal and liver status
L67@P1: EXCLUSIONS:
L68@P1: • Hypersensitivity to 5-Fluorouracil, folinic acid or any of the excipients
L69@P1: • Pregnancy
L70@P1: • Breast Feeding
L71@P1: • Severe liver impairment
L72@P1: • Known complete dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD) deficiency
L73@P1: PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
L74@P1: The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Medical Oncologist.
L75@P1: TESTS:
L76@P1: Baseline tests:
L77@P1: • FBC, liver and renal profile.
L78@P1: • DPD testing prior to first treatment with 5-Fluorouracil using phenotype and/or genotype testing
L79@P1: unless patient has been previously tested.
L80@P1: o In patients with moderate or severe renal impairment, blood uracil levels used for
L81@P1: dihydropyrimidine dehydrogenase (DPD) phenotyping should be interpreted with caution, as
L82@P1: impaired kidney function can lead to increased uracil blood levels. Consequently, there is an
L83@P1: increased risk for incorrect diagnosis of DPD deficiency, which may result in under dosing of
L84@P1: 5-Fluorouracil or other fluoropyrimidines, leading to reduced treatment efficacy. Genotype
L85@P1: testing for DPD deficiency should be considered for patients with renal impairment.
