L11@P0: Imatinib Therapy - GIST
L12@P0: INDICATIONS FOR USE:
L13@P0: INDICATION ICD10
L14@P0: Regimen
L15@P0: Code
L16@P0: HSE approved
L17@P0: Reimbursement
L18@P0: Status*
L19@P0: Treatment of adult patients with Kit (CD117) positive unresectable and/or
L20@P0: metastatic malignant gastrointestinal stromal tumours (GIST).
L21@P0: C16 00335a N/A
L22@P0: Adjuvant treatment of adult patients who are at significant risk of relapse
L23@P0: following resection of Kit (CD117)-positive GIST.
L24@P0: C16 00335b N/A
L25@P0: * This applies to post 2012 indications
L26@P0: TREATMENT:
L27@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L28@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L29@P0: Unresectable/Metastatic GIST:
L30@P0: Imatinib 400mg is taken orally, once daily until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity develops. The
L31@P0: dose may be increased to 800mg daily in progressing disease.
L32@P0: Adjuvant:
L33@P0: Imatinib 400mg is taken orally, once daily continuously for up to 3 years after resection unless the patient
L34@P0: experiences disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
L35@P0: Drug Dose Route Cycle
L36@P0: Imatinib 400mg once daily* PO with food Continuous
L37@P0: *For daily doses of 800mg, the dose should be administered as 400mg twice a day, in the morning and in the evening
L38@P0: Imatinib is commonly available as 100mg and 400mg/tablets
L39@P0: ELIGIBILITY:
L40@P0: • Indications as above
L41@P0: • ECOG status 0-3
L42@P0: • Adequate bone marrow, renal and liver function
L43@P0: EXCLUSIONS:
L44@P0: • Hypersensitivity to imatinib or any of the excipients
L45@P0: • Patients who have a low risk of recurrence are not eligible for adjuvant treatment
L46@P0: • Pregnancy
L47@P0: • Breastfeeding

L136@P2: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS
L137@P2: • Hypothyroidism: Hypothyroidism has been reported in thyroidectomy patients undergoing
L138@P2: thyroxine replacement during treatment with imatinib.
L139@P2: • Fluid retention: Monitor regularly for signs and symptoms of fluid retention caused by imatinib.
L140@P2: Probability increases with higher doses, age greater than 65 years and patients with a prior history
L141@P2: of cardiac disease. If severe fluid retention occurs treatment should be withheld until resolved.
L142@P2: • Cardiac Disease: Patients with cardiac disease, risk factors for cardiac failure or history of renal
L143@P2: failure should be monitored carefully, and any patient with signs or symptoms consistent with
L144@P2-3: cardiac or renal failure should be evaluated and treated. NCCP National SACT Regimen

L155@P3: • Gastrointestinal haemorrhage: In the study in patients with unresectable and/or metastatic GIST,
L156@P3: both gastrointestinal and intra-tumoural haemorrhages were reported. Based on the available data,
L157@P3: no predisposing factors (e.g. tumour size, tumour location, coagulation disorders) have been
L158@P3: identified that place patients with GIST at a higher risk of either type of haemorrhage. Since increased
L159@P3: vascularity and propensity for bleeding is a part of the nature and clinical course of GIST, standard
L160@P3: practices and procedures for the monitoring and management of haemorrhage in all patients should
L161@P3: be applied.
L162@P3: • Reactivation of Hepatitis B Virus (HBV): Cases of reactivation of HBV have occurred in patients who
L163@P3: are chronic carriers of HBV after they received BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs). Some cases
L164@P3: of HBV reactivation resulted in acute hepatic failure or fulminant hepatitis leading to liver
L165@P3: transplantation or a fatal outcome.
L166@P3: o Patients should be tested for HBV infection before initiating treatment with BCR-ABL TKIs
L167@P3: o Experts in liver disease and the treatment of HBV should be consulted before treatment in
L168@P3: patients with positive HBV serology (including those with active disease) is initiated and for
L169@P3: patients who test positive for HBV infection during treatment.
L170@P3: o Patients who are carriers of HBV requiring treatment with BCR-ABL TKIs should be closely
L171@P3: monitored for signs and symptoms of active HBV infection throughout therapy and for several
L172@P3: months following termination of therapy.
