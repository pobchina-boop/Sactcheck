L21@P1: 4. CLINICAL PARTICULARS
L22@P1: 4.1 Therapeutic indications
L23@P1: Glivec is indicated for the treatment of
L24@P1: • adult and paediatric patients with newly diagnosed Philadelphia chromosome (bcr-abl) positive
L25@P1: (Ph+) chronic myeloid leukaemia (CML) for whom bone marrow transplantation is not
L26@P1: considered as the first line of treatment.
L27@P1: • adult and paediatric patients with Ph+ CML in chronic phase after failure of interferon-alpha
L28@P1: therapy, or in accelerated phase or blast crisis.
L29@P1: • adult and paediatric patients with newly diagnosed Philadelphia chromosome positive acute
L30@P1: lymphoblastic leukaemia (Ph+ ALL) integrated with chemotherapy.
L31@P1: • adult patients with relapsed or refractory Ph+ ALL as monotherapy.
L32@P1: • adult patients with myelodysplastic/myeloproliferative diseases (MDS/MPD) associated with
L33@P1: platelet-derived growth factor receptor (PDGFR) gene re-arrangements.
L34@P1: • adult patients with advanced hypereosinophilic syndrome (HES) and/or chronic eosinophilic
L35@P1: leukaemia (CEL) with FIP1L1-PDGFR rearrangement.
L36@P1-2: The effect of Glivec on the outcome of bone marrow transplantation has not been determined.3
L37@P2: Glivec is indicated for
L38@P2: • the treatment of adult patients with Kit (CD 117) positive unresectable and/or metastatic
L39@P2: malignant gastrointestinal stromal tumours (GIST).
L40@P2: • the adjuvant treatment of adult patients who are at significant risk of relapse following resection
L41@P2: of Kit (CD117)-positive GIST. Patients who have a low or very low risk of recurrence should
L42@P2: not receive adjuvant treatment.
L43@P2: • the treatment of adult patients with unresectable dermatofibrosarcoma protuberans (DFSP) and
L44@P2: adult patients with recurrent and/or metastatic DFSP who are not eligible for surgery.
L45@P2: In adult and paediatric patients, the effectiveness of Glivec is based on overall haematological and
L46@P2: cytogenetic response rates and progression-free survival in CML, on haematological and cytogenetic
L47@P2: response rates in Ph+ ALL, MDS/MPD, on haematological response rates in HES/CEL and on
L48@P2: objective response rates in adult patients with unresectable and/or metastatic GIST and DFSP and on
L49@P2: recurrence-free survival in adjuvant GIST. The experience with Glivec in patients with MDS/MPD
L50@P2: associated with PDGFR gene re-arrangements is very limited (see section 5.1). Except in newly
L51@P2: diagnosed chronic phase CML, there are no controlled trials demonstrating a clinical benefit or
L52@P2: increased survival for these diseases.
L53@P2: 4.2 Posology and method of administration
L54@P2: Therapy should be initiated by a physician experienced in the treatment of patients with
L55@P2: haematological malignancies and malignant sarcomas, as appropriate.
L56@P2: For doses other than 400 mg and 800 mg (see dosage recommendation below), 100 mg and 400 mg
L57@P2: divisible tablets are available.
L58@P2: The prescribed dose should be administered orally with a meal and a large glass of water to minimise
L59@P2: the risk of gastrointestinal irritations. Doses of 400 mg or 600 mg should be administered once daily,
L60@P2: whereas a daily dose of 800 mg should be administered as 400 mg twice a day, in the morning and in
L61@P2: the evening.
L62@P2: For patients unable to swallow the film-coated tablets, the tablets may be dispersed in a glass of still
L63@P2: water or apple juice. The required number of tablets should be placed in the appropriate volume of
L64@P2: beverage (approximately 50 ml for a 100 mg tablet, and 200 ml for a 400 mg tablet) and stirred with a
L65@P2: spoon. The suspension should be administered immediately after complete disintegration of the
L66@P2: tablet(s).
L67@P2: Posology for CML in adult patients
L68@P2: The recommended dosage of Glivec is 400 mg/day for adult patients in chronic phase CML. Chronic
L69@P2: phase CML is defined when all of the following criteria are met: blasts < 15% in blood and bone
L70@P2: marrow, peripheral blood basophils < 20%, platelets > 100 x 109/l.
L71@P2: The recommended dosage of Glivec is 600 mg/day for adult patients in accelerated phase. Accelerated
L72@P2: phase is defined by the presence of any of the following: blasts  15% but < 30% in blood or bone
L73@P2: marrow, blasts plus promyelocytes  30% in blood or bone marrow (providing < 30% blasts),
L74@P2: peripheral blood basophils  20%, platelets < 100 x 109/l unrelated to therapy.
L75@P2: The recommended dose of Glivec is 600 mg/day for adult patients in blast crisis. Blast crisis is defined
L76@P2: as blasts  30% in blood or bone marrow or extramedullary disease other than hepatosplenomegaly.
L77@P2: Treatment duration: In clinical trials, treatment with Glivec was continued until disease progression.
L78@P2: The effect of stopping treatment after the achievement of a complete cytogenetic response has not
L79@P2-3: been investigated.4
L80@P3: Dose increases from 400 mg to 600 mg or 800 mg in patients with chronic phase disease, or from
L81@P3: 600 mg to a maximum of 800 mg (given as 400 mg twice daily) in patients with accelerated phase or
L82@P3: blast crisis may be considered in the absence of severe adverse drug reaction and severe nonleukaemia-related neutropenia or thrombocytopenia in the following circumstances: disease
L83@P3: progression (at any time); failure to achieve a satisfactory haematological response after at least
L84@P3: 3 months of treatment; failure to achieve a cytogenetic response after 12 months of treatment; or loss
L85@P3: of a previously achieved haematological and/or cytogenetic response. Patients should be monitored
L86@P3: closely following dose escalation given the potential for an increased incidence of adverse reactions at
L87@P3: higher dosages.
L88@P3: Posology for CML in children
L89@P3: Dosing for children should be on the basis of body surface area (mg/m2). The dose of 340 mg/m2daily
L90@P3: is recommended for children with chronic phase CML and advanced phase CML (not to exceed the
L91@P3: total dose of 800 mg). Treatment can be given as a once daily dose or alternatively the daily dose may
L92@P3: be split into two administrations – one in the morning and one in the evening. The dose
L93@P3: recommendation is currently based on a small number of paediatric patients (see sections 5.1 and 5.2).
L94@P3: There is no experience with the treatment of children below 2 years of age.
L95@P3: Dose increases from 340 mg/m2daily to 570 mg/m2daily (not to exceed the total dose of 800 mg) may
L96@P3: be considered in children in the absence of severe adverse drug reaction and severe non-leukaemiarelated neutropenia or thrombocytopenia in the following circumstances: disease progression (at any
L97@P3: time); failure to achieve a satisfactory haematological response after at least 3 months of treatment;
L98@P3: failure to achieve a cytogenetic response after 12 months of treatment; or loss of a previously achieved
L99@P3: haematological and/or cytogenetic response. Patients should be monitored closely following dose
L100@P3: escalation given the potential for an increased incidence of adverse reactions at higher dosages.
L101@P3: Posology for Ph+ ALL in adult patients
L102@P3: The recommended dose of Glivec is 600 mg/day for adult patients with Ph+ ALL. Haematological
L103@P3: experts in the management of this disease should supervise the therapy throughout all phases of care.
