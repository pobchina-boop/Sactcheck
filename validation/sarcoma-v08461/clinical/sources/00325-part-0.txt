L27@P0: TREATMENT:
L28@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L29@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L30@P0: The recommended dose of SUNitinib is 50mg taken orally, once daily for 4 weeks followed by a 2 week
L31@P0: rest period to comprise a complete cycle of 6 weeks (42 days).
L32@P0: Treatment is administered continuously until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

L38@P0: usual prescribed dose on the following day.
