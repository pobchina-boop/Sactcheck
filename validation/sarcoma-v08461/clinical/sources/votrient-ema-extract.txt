L18@P1: 4. CLINICAL PARTICULARS
L19@P1: 4.1 Therapeutic indications
L20@P1: Renal cell carcinoma (RCC)
L21@P1: Votrient is indicated in adults for the first-line treatment of advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) and
L22@P1: for patients who have received prior cytokine therapy for advanced disease.
L23@P1: Soft-tissue sarcoma (STS)
L24@P1: Votrient is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with selective subtypes of advanced soft-tissue
L25@P1: sarcoma (STS) who have received prior chemotherapy for metastatic disease or who have progressed
L26@P1: within 12 months after (neo) adjuvant therapy.
L27@P1: Efficacy and safety has only been established in certain STS histological tumour subtypes (see
L28@P1: section 5.1).
L29@P1: 4.2 Posology and method of administration
L30@P1: Votrient treatment should only be initiated by a physician experienced in the administration of
L31@P1-2: anti-cancer medicinal products.3
L32@P2: Posology
L33@P2: Adults
L34@P2: The recommended dose of pazopanib for the treatment of RCC or STS is 800 mg once daily.
L35@P2: Dose modifications
L36@P2: Dose modification (decrease or increase) should be in 200 mg decrements or increments in a stepwise
L37@P2: fashion based on individual tolerability in order to manage adverse reactions. The dose of pazopanib
L38@P2: should not exceed 800 mg.
L39@P2: Paediatric population
L40@P2: Pazopanib should not be used in children younger than 2 years of age because of safety concerns with
L41@P2: regard to organ growth and maturation (see sections 4.4 and 5.3).
L42@P2: The safety and efficacy of pazopanib in children aged 2 to 18 years of age have not yet been
L43@P2: established.
L44@P2: Currently available data are described in sections 4.8, 5.1 and 5.2 but no recommendation on a posology
L45@P2: can be made.
L46@P2: Elderly
L47@P2: There are limited data on the use of pazopanib in patients aged 65 years and older. In the RCC studies
L48@P2: of pazopanib, overall no clinically significant differences in safety of pazopanib were observed
L49@P2: between subjects aged at least 65 years and younger subjects. Clinical experience has not identified
L50@P2: differences in responses between the elderly and younger patients, but greater sensitivity of some
L51@P2: elderly patients cannot be ruled out.
L52@P2: Renal impairment
L53@P2: Renal impairment is unlikely to have a clinically relevant effect on pazopanib pharmacokinetics given
L54@P2: the low renal excretion of pazopanib and metabolites (see section 5.2). Therefore, no dose adjustment
L55@P2: is required in patients with creatinine clearance above 30 ml/min. Caution is advised in patients with
L56@P2: creatinine clearance below 30 ml/min as there is no experience of pazopanib in this patient population.
L57@P2: Hepatic impairment
L58@P2: Dosing recommendations in hepatically impaired patients are based on pharmacokinetic studies of
L59@P2: pazopanib in patients with varying degrees of hepatic dysfunction (see section 5.2). All patients should
L60@P2: have liver function tests to determine whether they have hepatic impairment before starting and during
L61@P2: pazopanib therapy (see section 4.4). Administration of pazopanib to patients with mild or moderate
L62@P2: hepatic impairment should be undertaken with caution and close monitoring of tolerability. 800 mg
L63@P2: pazopanib once daily is the recommended dose in patients with mild abnormalities in serum liver tests
L64@P2: (defined either as normal bilirubin and any degree of alanine aminotransferase (ALT) elevation or as
L65@P2: an elevation of bilirubin (>35% direct) up to 1.5 x upper limit of normal (ULN) regardless of the ALT
L66@P2: value). A reduced pazopanib dose of 200 mg once daily is recommended in patients with moderate
L67@P2: hepatic impairment (defined as an elevation of bilirubin >1.5 to 3 x ULN regardless of the ALT value)
L68@P2: (see section 5.2).
L69@P2: Pazopanib is not recommended in patients with severe hepatic impairment (defined as total bilirubin
L70@P2: >3 x ULN regardless of the ALT value).
L71@P2: See section 4.4 for liver monitoring and dose modification for patients with drug-induced
L72@P2: hepatotoxicity.
L73@P2: Method of administration
L74@P2: Pazopanib is for oral use. It should be taken without food, at least one hour before or two hours after a
L75@P2: meal (see section 5.2). The film-coated tablets should be taken whole with water and not broken or
L76@P2-3: crushed (see section 5.2).4
L77@P3: 4.3 Contraindications
L78@P3: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or to any of the excipients listed in section 6.1.
L79@P3: 4.4 Special warnings and precautions for use
L80@P3: Hepatic effects
L81@P3: Cases of hepatic failure (including fatalities) have been reported during use of pazopanib.
L82@P3: Administration of pazopanib to patients with mild or moderate hepatic impairment should be
L83@P3: undertaken with caution and close monitoring. 800 mg pazopanib once daily is the recommended dose
L84@P3: in patients with mild abnormalities in serum liver tests (either normal bilirubin and any degree of ALT
L85@P3: elevation or elevation of bilirubin up to 1.5 x ULN regardless of the ALT value). A reduced pazopanib
L86@P3: dose of 200 mg once daily is recommended in patients with moderate hepatic impairment (elevation of
L87@P3: bilirubin >1.5 to 3 x ULN regardless of the ALT value) (see sections 4.2 and 5.2). Pazopanib is not
L88@P3: recommended in patients with severe hepatic impairment (total bilirubin >3 x ULN regardless of the
L89@P3: ALT value) (see sections 4.2 and 5.2). Exposure at a 200 mg dose is markedly reduced, though highly
L90@P3: variable, in these patients, with values considered insufficient to obtain a clinically relevant effect.
L91@P3: In clinical studies with pazopanib, increase in serum transaminases (ALT, aspartate aminotransferase
L92@P3: [AST]) and bilirubin were observed (see section 4.8). In the majority of the cases, isolated increases in
L93@P3: ALT and AST have been reported, without concomitant elevations of alkaline phosphatase or
L94@P3: bilirubin. Patients over 60 years of age may be at greater risk for mild (>3 x ULN) to severe
L95@P3: (>8 x ULN) elevation of ALT. Patients who carry the HLA-B*57:01 allele have an increased risk of
L96@P3: pazopanib-associated ALT elevations. Liver function should be monitored in all subjects receiving
L97@P3: pazopanib, regardless of genotype or age (see section 5.1).
L98@P3: Serum liver tests should be performed before initiation of treatment with pazopanib, at weeks 3, 5, 7
L99@P3: and 9, then at months 3 and 4, with additional tests as clinically indicated. Periodic testing should then
L100@P3: continue after month 4.
