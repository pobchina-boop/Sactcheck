L14@P0: Regorafenib Monotherapy
L15@P0: INDICATIONS FOR USE:
L16@P0: INDICATION ICD10
L17@P0: Regimen
L18@P0: Code
L19@P0: HSE approved
L20@P0: reimbursement
L21@P0: Status*
L22@P0: Treatment of adult patients with metastatic colorectal cancer (mCRC) who
L23@P0: have been previously treated with, or are not considered candidates for,
L24@P0: available therapies. These include fluoropyrimidine-based chemotherapy,
L25@P0: an anti-VEGF therapy and an anti-EGFR therapy
L26@P0: C18 00244a CDS
L27@P0: Treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic gastrointestinal
L28@P0: stromal tumours (GIST) who progressed on or are intolerant to prior
L29@P0: treatment with imatinib and sunitinib
L30@P0: C26 00244b CDS
L31@P0: *This applies to post 2012 indications only
L32@P0: TREATMENT:
L33@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L34@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L35@P0: Regorafenib is taken once daily for 21 days followed by 7 days off therapy.
L36@P0: This 28 day period is considered a treatment cycle.
L37@P0: Treatment is continued until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity develops.
L38@P0: Day Drug Dose Route Cycle
L39@P0: 1-21 Regorafenib 160mg daily PO Repeat every 28 days
L40@P0: Regorafenib should be taken at the same time each day,with or without food.
L41@P0: The tablets should be swallowed whole with water after a light meal that contains less than 30% fat.
L42@P0: An example of a light (low-fat) meal would include 1 portion of cereal (about 30g), 1 glass of skimmed milk, 1 slice of toast with jam, 1 glass of apple
L43@P0: juice, and 1 cup of coffee or tea (520 calories, 2g fat).
L44@P0: If a dose is missed, then it should be taken on the same day as soon as the patient remembers.
L45@P0: The patient should not take two doses on the same day to make up for a missed dose.
L46@P0: In case of vomiting after regorafenib administration, the patient should not take additional tablets.
L47@P0: Regorafenib is commonly available as 40mg tablets.
L48@P0: ELIGIBILITY:
L49@P0:  Indications as above
L50@P0:  ECOG status 0-1
L51@P0:  Adequate bone marrow, renal and liver function
L52@P0: EXCLUSIONS:
L53@P0:  Hypersensitivity to regorafenib or any of the excipients
L54@P0:  Unstable angina or new onset angina (within 3 months), recent MI, cardiac failure
L55@P0:  Severe hepatic impairment
L56@P0-1:  Pregnancy and lactationNCCP National SACT Regimen

L95@P1: tolerability.
