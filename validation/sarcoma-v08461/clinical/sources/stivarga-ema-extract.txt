L5@P1: 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION
L6@P1: Each film-coated tablet contains 40 mg of regorafenib.
L7@P1: Excipients with known effect
L8@P1: Each daily dose of 160 mg contains 2.438 mmol (or 56.06 mg) of sodium (see section 4.4).
L9@P1: Each daily dose of 160 mg contains 1.68 mg of lecithin (derived from soya) (see section 4.4).
L10@P1: For the full list of excipients, see section 6.1.

L12@P1: Film-coated tablet.
L13@P1: Light pink film-coated tablets, oval shaped with a length of 16 mm and a width of 7 mm marked with
L14@P1: ‘BAYER’ on one side and ‘40’ on the other side.
L15@P1: 4. CLINICAL PARTICULARS
L16@P1: 4.1 Therapeutic indications
L17@P1: Stivarga is indicated as monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with
L18@P1: - metastatic colorectal cancer (CRC) who have been previously treated with, or are not considered
L19@P1: candidates for, available therapies. These include fluoropyrimidine-based chemotherapy, an
L20@P1: anti-VEGF therapy and an anti-EGFR therapy (see section 5.1)
L21@P1: - unresectable or metastatic gastrointestinal stromal tumours (GIST) who progressed on or are
L22@P1: intolerant to prior treatment with imatinib and sunitinib
L23@P1: - hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) who have been previously treated with sorafenib.
L24@P1: 4.2 Posology and method of administration
L25@P1: Stivarga should be prescribed by physicians experienced in the administration of anticancer therapy.
L26@P1: Posology
L27@P1: The recommended dose of regorafenib is 160 mg (4 tablets of 40 mg) taken once daily for 3 weeks
L28@P1: followed by 1 week off therapy. This 4-week period is considered a treatment cycle.
L29@P1: If a dose is missed, then it should be taken on the same day as soon as the patient remembers. The
L30@P1: patient should not take two doses on the same day to make up for a missed dose. In case of vomiting
L31@P1: after regorafenib administration, the patient should not take additional tablets.
L32@P1: Treatment should continue as long as benefit is observed or until unacceptable toxicity occurs (see
L33@P1: section 4.4).
L34@P1: Patients with performance status (PS) 2 or higher were excluded from clinical studies. There is limited
L35@P1-2: data in patients with PS ≥2.3
L36@P2: Posology adjustments
L37@P2: Dose interruptions and/or dose reductions may be required based on individual safety and tolerability.
L38@P2: Dose modifications are to be applied in 40 mg (one tablet) steps. The lowest recommended daily dose
L39@P2: is 80 mg. The maximum daily dose is 160 mg.

L128@P3: Hepatic impairment
L129@P3: Regorafenib is eliminated mainly via the hepatic route.
L130@P3: In clinical studies, no relevant differences in exposure, safety or efficacy were observed between
L131@P3: patients with mild hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh A) and normal hepatic function. No dose
L132@P3: adjustment is required in patients with mild hepatic impairment. Since only limited data are available
L133@P3: for patients with moderate hepatic impairment (Child Pugh B), no dose recommendation can be
L134@P3: provided. Close monitoring of overall safety is recommended in these patients (see sections 4.4
L135@P3: and 5.2).
L136@P3: Stivarga is not recommended for use in patients with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C) as
L137@P3: Stivarga has not been studied in this population.
L138@P3: Renal impairment
L139@P3: Available clinical data indicate similar exposure of regorafenib and its metabolites M-2 and M-5 in
L140@P3: patients with mild, moderate or severe renal impairment compared to patients with normal renal
L141@P3: function. No dose adjustment is required in patients with mild, moderate or severe renal impairment
L142@P3: (see also section 5.2).
L143@P3: Elderly population
L144@P3: In clinical studies, no relevant differences in exposure, safety or efficacy were observed between
L145@P3: elderly (aged 65 years and above) and younger patients (see also section 5.2).
L146@P3: Gender
L147@P3: In clinical studies, no relevant differences in exposure, safety or efficacy were observed between male
L148@P3: and female patients. No dose adjustment is necessary based on gender (see also section 5.2).
L149@P3: Ethnic differences
L150@P3: In clinical studies, no relevant differences in exposure or efficacy were observed between patients of
L151@P3: different ethnic groups. A higher incidence of hand foot skin reaction (HFSR)/palmar-plantar
L152@P3-4: erythrodysesthesia syndrome, severe liver function test abnormalities and hepatic dysfunction was 5
L153@P4: observed in Asian (in particular Japanese) patients treated with Stivarga compared with Caucasians.
