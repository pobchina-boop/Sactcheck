1. Treatment and purpose

Sunitinib for gastrointestinal stromal tumour (GIST): 14 days on, 7 days off · NCCP 00719 v2

National Cancer Control Programme 00719: sunitinib 50 mg by mouth once daily on days 1–14, followed by 7 days without sunitinib. Repeat the 21-day cycle only as confirmed by your team. Dose reductions or additional holds may be needed; symptoms and monitoring continue during the planned break.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Sunitinib · Oral targeted therapy: tyrosine kinase inhibitor

Report painful hands or feet, worsening diarrhoea, bleeding, chest pain, breathlessness, faintness, severe headache or a blistering rash promptly. Do not restart after a serious reaction without oncology review.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the intended benefit, uncertainty, alternatives including supportive care, generic targeted-therapy risks and the separate agent risks, lasting effects, fertility and the patient’s priorities.
