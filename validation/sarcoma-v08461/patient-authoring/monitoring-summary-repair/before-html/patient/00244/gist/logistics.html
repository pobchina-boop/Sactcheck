Regorafenib for gastrointestinal stromal tumour (GIST)

Practical treatment guide

Practical treatment planning

Agree the arrangements with your oncology team.

STEP 1

Before starting

Confirm that both prior imatinib and sunitinib treatments have been reviewed. Reconcile all prescription, non-prescription and herbal products, including warfarin and the liver enzyme cytochrome P450 3A4 interactions; avoid grapefruit and St John’s wort. Agree liver-test and blood-pressure appointments before starting.

STEP 2

Taking the medicine

Swallow tablets whole with water after a light, low-fat meal, at a consistent time. Your pharmacy supplies the written food and dose instructions for the product you receive.

STEP 3

Missed or vomited doses

Take a missed dose when remembered on the same day. Do not take two doses on one day. Do not take extra tablets after vomiting; contact the team if vomiting continues.

STEP 4

Supply and handling

Keep tablets or capsules in their supplied packaging, away from children, and follow the pharmacy storage instructions. Do not share medicines or alter the dose yourself. Ask the pharmacy before crushing, opening or dispersing a medicine; formulation-specific instructions differ. Return unused treatment to the pharmacy.

STEP 5

Monitoring and next review

Liver tests at least every 2 weeks for the first 2 months, then monthly or more often as indicated. Blood pressure weekly for the first 6 weeks, then before each cycle and as needed. Blood count/kidney tests regularly; thyroid/pancreatic tests and cardiac review when indicated. Monitor international normalised ratio (warfarin clotting test) closely with warfarin.
