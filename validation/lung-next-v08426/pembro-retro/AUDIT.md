# Pembrolizumab retrospective audit · v0.84.26

Checked 29 October 2026. Scope: complete lung-facing service suites for shared protocols 00455 (200 mg every 21 days) and 00558 (400 mg every 42 days), retaining every existing non-lung indication and rule. Both proposals have 61 optional inputs and 50 rules. All clinical approval flags remain false. Software tests do not constitute clinical validation.

## Findings and changes

1. **Immune attribution was missing from irreversible laboratory pathways.** Previously an isolated creatinine, bilirubin or AST/ALT value could trigger immune-toxicity permanent discontinuation even when its cause was unknown. NCCP Table 1 is explicitly for immune-related adverse reactions; EMA IV SmPC §4.4 requires evaluation and exclusion of alternative causes of hepatitis/nephritis. Added independent `pembro_immune_hepatitis_assessed` and `pembro_immune_nephritis_assessed` inputs. These record a clinician's clinical assessment, not a requirement for biopsy. Definitive immune hold/stop rules now require the corresponding true input. Abnormal unclassified results—including false, missing, null or blank attribution—produce prompt consultant review, with no automatic administration, permanent immune stop or reduction. The patient FAQ, consent context, clinical assessment and journey explain this distinction.

2. **KEYNOTE-091 final overall survival analysis is newly available.** The EORTC primary sponsor report dated 27 October 2026 describes the ESMO 2026 final analysis. Adjuvant chemotherapy-subgroup median DFS is 58.7 versus 39.1 months, HR 0.80 (95% CI 0.68–0.94); this is explicitly labelled the chemotherapy subgroup, not the overall randomised population. Final OS was not statistically significantly improved. Earlier “OS immature” language and the old DFS snapshot were replaced. Conference/sponsor reporting is labelled separately from the original peer-reviewed trial report. No secondary-only OS hazard ratio is reproduced. Patient explanations remain qualitative and do not give individual prognoses. Metastatic KEYNOTE-024 survival data remain distinct, as does the exposure-bridging limitation for six-week dosing.

3. **Sources were unchanged, not silently upgraded.** Fresh NCCP PDFs are still 00455 v16 and 00558 v13. Both and the EMA SmPC are byte-identical to the v22 retrieval; hashes and retrieval dates are recorded in sources.json. Critical NCCP pages 5–6 and EMA page 11 were rendered and inspected. The two NCCP liver-metastasis rows both use ≥50% and ≥1 week (confirmed at larger render size); no threshold change is justified.

## Exact boundaries and retained pathways

With clinically assessed immune attribution: creatinine >1.5 through 3 ×ULN withhold; >3 ×ULN permanently discontinue. AST/ALT >3 through 5 ×ULN or bilirubin >1.5 through 3 ×ULN withhold; AST/ALT >5 or bilirubin >3 ×ULN permanently discontinue. With liver metastases and baseline grade 2 AST/ALT elevation, an increase ≥50% lasting ≥7 days triggers the source permanent-stop pathway. Original compatible combined liver-rise field is retained. The creatinine legacy field name remains compatible but its unit and help explicitly require ULN, never fold-change from baseline.

Stable renal impairment, haemodialysis and chronic hepatic impairment remain separate no-dose-reduction contexts; they cannot erase an independent severe immune finding. Severe pneumonitis and other organ toxicities continue to act independently when hepatic/renal attribution is absent. Hypothyroidism can be managed with replacement without automatic interruption; controlled severe endocrinopathy requires review, and failed control has the original discontinuation pathway. Source 12-week recovery/steroid criteria, recurrent toxicity rules, suspected/confirmed SJS/TEN and infusion reactions remain intact. No dose-reduction instruction is created.

Metastatic and adjuvant indications retain distinct eligibility and durations. Adjuvant maximum 12 months; advanced 24-month sustained-response decision remains a consultant review rather than mandatory stop. Six-week dosing does not extend the source thyroid monitoring interval of every 3–6 weeks. No automatic cytotoxic antiemetics are introduced. Source CTCAE v4 terminology is retained. Shared multicancer scope remains intact, including the historical 00455a local CRC ID with official code 00455k and separate 00455-lung local ID for official 00455a. The existing urothelial CPS exact-10 source conflict remains review, not an invented cutoff. Adjuvant reimbursement remains N/A pending access verification.
