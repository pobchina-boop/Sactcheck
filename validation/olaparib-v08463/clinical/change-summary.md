# Olaparib suite — clinical handoff

**SACTCheck 0.84.63 · source review 24 November 2026**

The update provides six distinct olaparib pathways: adjuvant breast cancer, advanced prostate cancer, first-line ovarian maintenance, relapsed ovarian maintenance, pancreatic maintenance, and olaparib plus bevacizumab maintenance. Each has its own treatment purpose, molecular eligibility, duration, monitoring explanation and patient material. The scope follows **NCCP 00588 v5b and 00746 v2**; a broader drug licence is not treated as evidence of inclusion in these Irish regimens.[1,2]

The patient interface brings together treatment explanations, the short guide and detailed handbook, treatment journey, visit logistics, prescribed support, consent material, reusable passport and wallet card. These generated HTML/PDF assets are present for all six routes in the canonical checkout. Their presence is an inventory finding; this handoff does not assert final browser, CI or deployment validation. Videos remain deferred.

## Six-pathway consistency matrix

All five monotherapy pathways use continuous olaparib tablets twice daily, with a **28-day reference view**, without a planned rest week. Their NCCP routine full blood count, renal and liver profile is **every four weeks for the first 12 months, then as clinically indicated**. The reference view and passport spaces do not define a fixed course or book appointments. The combination uses the different schedule below.[1: pp2,4; 2: pp2,4–5]

|---|---|---|---|
| **Breast — 00588h** | Adjuvant, high-risk **HER2-negative early** breast cancer; **germline BRCA1/2**; previous neoadjuvant/adjuvant chemotherapy. Separate TNBC and hormone-receptor-positive high-risk branches. | **Up to one year**, or earlier recurrence/unacceptable toxicity. The detailed assessment retains additional histology, prior-treatment, timing, fitness and recovery requirements; this matrix is a concise comparison, not a complete eligibility checklist.[1,2]

## What changed clinically

**Purpose, evidence and explanations follow the selected setting.** The patient material explains PARP-associated DNA repair and why BRCA/HRD selection matters; the combination separately explains bevacizumab's effect on VEGF signalling. Evidence sections remain attached to the relevant trial populations: OlympiA, PROfound, SOLO1, SOLO2, POLO and PAOLA-1. Group outcomes are not presented as individual forecasts or evidence that SACTCheck itself improves outcomes. The relapsed-ovarian and pancreatic material retains the qualifications around overall-survival findings.[3: §5.1; canonical patient evidence sections]

**Dose and interruption rules preserve source boundaries.** The olaparib toxicity ladder is **300 → 250 → 200 mg twice daily**. The **100/150 mg twice-daily CYP3A interaction doses** are separate pathways. Normal counts preserve the previous prescribed dose; either ANC **<1 ×10⁹/L** or platelets **<100 ×10⁹/L** triggers delay/reduction review. Severe anaemia, transfusion dependence and persistent cytopenia cannot be concealed by reassuring ANC/platelet values. Suspected complications prompt assessment; confirmed MDS/AML or pneumonitis has its separate discontinuation rule.[1: p5 Tables 1–3, p6 Table 4; 2: p6 Tables 1–2, p7 Table 3, p9 Table 6; 3: §§4.2,4.4]

**Uncertainty remains visible.** At CrCl exactly **30 mL/min**, the NCCP dose band and current SmPC boundary differ; severe renal impairment and Child–Pugh C also require source reconciliation. The tool does not invent arithmetic for overlapping renal, interaction and previous-toxicity reductions. Missing measurements remain unassessed. In 00746, proteinuria, BP, bleeding, thrombosis, fistula, neurological and wound-healing decisions are bevacizumab-specific; they do not automatically stop olaparib.[1: p5 Table 3; 2: pp7–8 Tables 3–5; 3,4: §§4.2,4.4]

**Support and consent explain the reason for checks.** Written plans connect symptoms and tests to blood toxicity, infection, breathing problems, clots and liver injury; the combination adds BP, proteinuria, bleeding, wound healing, perforation/fistula and PRES. Support medicines follow the prescription/local policy, with interaction review rather than an automatic prophylactic bundle. The combination retains the longer bevacizumab breastfeeding restriction: at least six months after its last dose, alongside the olaparib one-month restriction, using the later date.[1: p6; 2: pp9–10; 3,4: §§4.4–4.6]

## Verification and release status

**Independent source-derived software checks passed 123/123 scenarios and 429/429 assertions**, covering decision-engine portions of 33 of the original 38 acceptance groups. The final unchanged replay is `independent-clinical/report-05.json`. A missing-platelet coverage-reporting defect was found and corrected: when low ANC already determined a hold, the missing platelet result had been omitted from the named gaps. It now appears first in the coverage list, including compact export summaries. The replay confirmed **no change to clinical actions, findings, component-dose rows or invalid-input behaviour**.[5]

This is source reconciliation and software verification. **Independent consultant, oncology-pharmacy and patient/PPI review remain pending.** The clinical reviews should address appropriateness and local implementation; patient/PPI review should test understanding, navigation, contact instructions and usability. Final CI, browser and deployment status must come from the separate release report; browser validation was blocked during this handoff.
