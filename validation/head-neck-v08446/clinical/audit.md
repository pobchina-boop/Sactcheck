## Findings fixed

1. **Nivolumab permanent-discontinuation history was not preserved explicitly during restart assessment.** Both protocols now retain a prior permanent-stop decision independently of current symptom grade. A proposed restart with missing history, absent consultant approval, an unknown steroid dose or unknown other immunosuppression remains withheld. Existing source recovery, steroid and immunosuppression rules remain active. NCCP pages 6–8 underpin these controls; explicit software history/approval fields are conservative implementations of the source, not new clinical thresholds.
2. **Nivolumab HNSCC eligibility depended too heavily on a broad confirmation.** Both protocols now separately assess adulthood, source histology/sites, absence of a curative local option, progression after platinum and the interval from last platinum to progression/recurrence. NCCP page 4 specifies recurrence/progression within six months. Missing or longer intervals require review. An exactly six-month, potentially rounded entry requires confirmation of the actual dates and consultant interpretation. This is deliberately identified as an implementation uncertainty, not a new absolute contraindication. The indication keys differ: `00483-source-d` and `00484-source-e`. NSCLC uses `00483-source-e` and `00484-source-f` and does not inherit HNSCC gates.
3. **00484 thyroid monitoring incorrectly said monthly.** Page 6 explicitly requires TFTs before each cycle and additionally when indicated. The clinical monitoring record now says this. 00483 retains its source-specific once-monthly schedule. Findings were sent to the retrospective agent for patient/knowledge/timeline propagation, including shared melanoma contexts.
4. **00261 HNSCC treatment constraints could be bypassed by a conflicting generic disease context.** HNSCC AUC, radiotherapy and cycle rules now recognise the selected HNSCC indication as well as the separate context field. Conflicting inputs require reconciliation. Missing AUC, cycle number or concurrent-radiotherapy confirmation is not accepted as a confirmed plan. A separate confirmation establishes why cisplatin is inappropriate. Source pages 1–2 specify AUC 5 every 21 days for three cycles with radiotherapy in this setting. Breast retains its AUC 4–6 and continuing-treatment context. An explicit optional indication-select input makes all new rule inputs reachable and schema-valid.

Twenty-seven new rules were added: five for 00261 and eleven for each nivolumab protocol. Three existing 00261 HNSCC rule predicates changed. No pembrolizumab protocol bytes required modification after this fresh review.

## Thresholds and distinctions confirmed

- **Carboplatin counts:** page 4 physically shows ANC **≥1** and platelets **≥100**, with delay below either threshold. Text extraction can lose the underline. Existing inclusive equality behavior is correct and was retained. GFR ≤20 excludes administration; >20 to ≤30 requires extreme caution; below 60 increases marrow risk. GFR cap 125, creatinine freshness within 48 hours for estimates, and measured-GFR reuse only with creatinine ≤110% of its reference value remain distinct. The source allows higher treatment thresholds for selected ECOG 2/3 patients. No numeric dose is invented from these review conditions.
- **Nivolumab:** CTCAE v4 Table 3 keeps organ-specific hold/stop grades distinct. Pneumonitis grade 2 holds and grade 3–4 stops; colitis grade 2–3 holds and grade 4 stops; nephritis grade 2–3 holds and grade 4 stops. Endocrine symptom and hormone-replacement qualifications remain. Rash, myocarditis, SJS/TEN, recurrent/persistent toxicity and steroid-taper failure retain their separate actions. Stable renal/hepatic impairment is not active immune toxicity. The 28-day IV infusion remains 60 minutes; the 14-day IV infusion is 30 minutes. IV and subcutaneous alternatives are not combined. Existing switch intervals and NSCLC response checks (before cycle 8 versus cycle 4) were checked without applying them to HNSCC. Immune effects may need monitoring for at least five months after stopping.
- **Pembrolizumab:** existing v45 HNSCC CPS ≥1, first-line/local-treatment and six-month eligibility controls, prior-stop memory and restart controls were confirmed. Table 1 is on pages 5–6 in both documents. Immune hepatitis holds at AST/ALT >3 to ≤5×ULN or bilirubin >1.5 to ≤3×ULN and stops above those upper limits, with a separate liver-metastasis baseline-grade-2 ≥50% rise lasting ≥7 days rule. Immune nephritis uses creatinine >1.5 to ≤3×ULN versus >3×ULN. The legacy internal field name mentions baseline, but its current visible label, unit and help explicitly require **ULN only**; no baseline ratio was substituted. Attribution review prevents an isolated lab result automatically becoming an immune permanent-stop diagnosis. Endocrine exceptions, recurrent pneumonitis/colitis, 12-week recovery/taper failure and severe neurologic/cardiac/skin events remain distinct. 00455 specifies TSH every 3–6 weeks; 00558 specifies TFTs every 3–6 weeks.

## Validation and limits

`node validation/head-neck-v08446/clinical/source-cases.cjs` passes **386/386** checks: 380 independently specified clinical scenarios and six structural/governance/monitoring checks. Expected outcomes come from the source tables and clinical history requirements, not an enumeration of existing rule conditions. Historical suite replays are supplementary regression evidence and are not labelled as fresh source audits. Historical reports remain frozen. Version-46 compatibility wrappers and their separately stored outcomes are owned by the regression audit.

This audit did not freshly retrieve every supplemental HPRA/EMA product document previously cited by these protocols, nor the standalone antiemetic guideline. Previously attributed SmPC-only contraception, vaccine and medicine-interaction details are retained without claiming fresh confirmation from the five NCCP PDFs. NCCP 00261 itself does not establish the patient leaflet's contraception durations. Current patient/PDF/browser validation is a separate full-service workstream. No live deployment or clinical authorisation is asserted here.
