L25@P0: TREATMENT:
L26@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L27@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L28@P0: DOXOrubicin and ifosfamide are administered on Days 1, 2 and 3 of a 21 day cycle for up to 6 cycles,
L29@P0: unless the patient experiences disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Mesna is
L30@P0: administered prior to the first dose of ifosfamide on Day 1 and is continued throughout the
L31@P0: chemotherapy up to 24 hrs after the ifosfamide infusion.

L35@P0: administered

L209@P3: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L210@P3: • Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) for details
L211@P3: • Neutropenia: Fever or other evidence of infection must be assessed promptly and treated
L212@P3: appropriately.
L213@P3: • Cardiotoxicity: DOXOrubicin is cardiotoxic and must be used with caution, if at all, in patients with
L214@P3: severe hypertension or cardiac dysfunction
L215@P3: • Ifosfamide-induced encephalopathy: This may occur in patients treated with high doses of
L216@P3: ifosfamide. Neurological function should be assessed prior to each ifosfamide dose.
L217@P3: • Renal and urothelial toxicity: Ifosfamide is both nephrotoxic and urotoxic. Glomerular and tubular
L218@P3: kidney function must be evaluated and checked before commencement of therapy, as well as during
L219@P3: and after treatment. Urinary sediment should be checked regularly for the presence of erythrocytes
L220@P3: and other signs of uro/nephrotoxicity. During or immediately after administration, adequate amounts
L221@P3: of fluid should be ingested or infused to force diuresis in order to reduce the risk of urinary tract
L222@P3: toxicity. For prophylaxis of hemorrhagic cystitis, ifosfamide should be used in combination with mesna.
L223@P3: Ifosfamide should be used with caution, if at all, in patients with active urinary tract infections.
