L26@P0: TREATMENT:
L27@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L28@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patient’s individual clinical circumstances.
L29@P0: Gemcitabine is administered on Day 1 and Day 8 and DOCEtaxel on Day 8 of a 21-day cycle for up to 6
L30@P0: cycles unless the patient experiences disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
L31@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anticancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

L236@P3: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L237@P3: Gemcitabine
L238@P3: • Renal Toxicity: Irreversible renal failure associated with hemolytic uremic syndrome may occur (rare)
L239@P3: with gemcitabine. Use caution with pre-existing renal dysfunction.
L240@P3: • Pulmonary Toxicity: Acute shortness of breath may occur with gemcitabine. Discontinue treatment
L241@P3: with gemcitabine if drug-induced pneumonitis is suspected.
L242@P3: • Cardiovascular: Due to the risk of cardiac and/or vascular disorders with gemcitabine, particular
L243@P3: caution must be exercised with patients presenting a history of cardiovascular events.
L244@P3: DOCEtaxel
L245@P3: • Fluid Retention: dexAMETHasone premedication must be given prior to administration of DOCEtaxel
L246@P3: to reduce the incidence and severity of fluid retention. It can also reduce the severity of the
L247@P3: hypersensitivity reaction.
L248@P3: • Hypersensitivity Reactions: Patients should be observed closely for hypersensitivity reactions
L249@P3: especially during the first and second infusions. Hypersensitivity reactions may occur within a few
L250@P3: minutes following the initiation of the infusion of DOCEtaxel, thus facilities for the treatment of
L251@P3: hypotension and bronchospasm should be available. If hypersensitivity reactions occur, minor
L252@P3: symptoms such as flushing or localized cutaneous reactions do not require interruption of therapy.
