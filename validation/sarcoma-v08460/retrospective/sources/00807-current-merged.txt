L27@P0: TREATMENT:
L28@P0: Induction BCG:
L29@P0: Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) is administered by instillation into the bladder intravesically once weekly for
L30@P0: 6 weeks.
L31@P0: Maintenance BCG:
L32@P0: Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) is administered by instillation into the bladder intravesically at the intervals
L33@P0: listed below.
L34@P0: High risk/ Very high risk: Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) is administered by instillation into the bladder
L35@P0: weekly for 3 consecutive weeks at 3, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36 months*.
L36@P0: Intermediate risk: Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) is administered by instillation into the bladder weekly for
L37@P0: 3 consecutive weeks at 3, 6, 12 months*.
L38@P0: *(Above indicates the ideal duration of maintenance treatment but may vary based on patient factors, tolerance and
L39@P0: BCG availability).
L40@P0: Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate
L41@P0: 1 Bacillus Calmette-Guérin
L42@P0: (BCG)a
L43@P0: One vial Intravesical Diluted in NaCl 0.9% up to 50mL.
L44@P0: Administer instillation into bladder via catheter
L45@P0: (dwell time of 1-2 hours)
L46@P0: aBrand selection based on product availability and clinician’s decision.
L47@P0: BCG strain: The strain and supplier may change from time to time. A patient would ideally receive all induction and
L48@P0: maintenance therapy with the same strain, but switching strains is recommended if necessary to ensure administration of full
L49@P0-1: dose according to conventional dosing schedule.NCCP National SACT Regimen

L62@P1: ELIGIBILITY:
L63@P1: • Indications as above
L64@P1: • Risk groups* defined as:
L65@P1: Intermediate Risk Patients without carcinoma in situ (CIS) who are not included in either the
L66@P1: low-, high-, or very high-risk groups
L67@P1: High Risk • All T1 high grade (HG)/G3 without CIS, EXCEPT those included in the
L68@P1: very high-risk group
L69@P1: • All CIS patients, EXCEPT those included in the very high-risk group
L70@P1: Stage, grade with additional clinical risk factors:
L71@P1: • Ta low grade (LG)/G2 or T1G1, no CIS with all 3 risk factors
L72@P1: • Ta HG/G3 or T1 LG, no CIS with at least 2 risk factors
L73@P1: • T1G2 no CIS with at least 1 risk factor
L74@P1: Very High Risk Stage, grade with additional clinical risk factors:
L75@P1: • Ta HG/G3 and CIS with all 3 risk factors
L76@P1: • T1G2 and CIS with at least 2 risk factors
L77@P1: • T1 HG/G3 and CIS with at least 1 risk factor
L78@P1: • T1 HG/G3 no CIS with all 3 risk factors
L79@P1: *EAU NMIBC Risk Calculator available here: https://nmibc.net/
L80@P1: EXCLUSIONS:
L81@P1: • TURBT or urethral trauma within 2 weeks
L82@P1: • Hypersensitivity to BCG or any of the excipients.
L83@P1: • Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
L84@P1: CAUTIONS:
L85@P1: • T-cell immunodeficiency (idiopathic CD4 lymphopenia, HIV with CD4 count 200-350, CLL
L86@P1: • B-cell /Antibody immunodeficiencies
L87@P1: • Severe G6PD deficiency
L88@P1: • Current or recent immunosuppressive treatment (steroids, mycophenolate, azathioprine,
L89@P1: Sirolimus, methotrexate, Tacrolimus)
L90@P1: • History of systemic chemotherapy agents such as riTUXimab, cycloPHOSphamide.

L105@P2: TESTS:
L106@P2: Pre treatment:
L107@P2:  Cystoscopy with biopsy or resection to confirm histologic diagnosis
L108@P2: Post treatment:
L109@P2:  Cystoscopy +/- biopsy (GA) and urine cytology 8 weeks after the last dose of BCG and at regular
L110@P2: intervals thereafter
L111@P2: Disease monitoring:
L112@P2: Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as
L113@P2: directed by the supervising Consultant Urologist.
L114@P2: DOSE MODIFICATIONS:
L115@P2:  Any dose modification should be discussed with the Consultant Urologist.
L116@P2: Renal and Hepatic Impairment:
L117@P2:  There are no known published dose modification recommendations available for renal and hepatic
L118@P2: impairment. Given that this is a localised treatment and is expected to have limited or no systemic
L119@P2: absorption there is unlikely to be a dose modification in this setting.
L120@P2: SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L121@P2: EMETOGENIC POTENTIAL:
L122@P2:  There are no known published recommendations available for emetogenic potential. Given that this is
L123@P2: a localised treatment and is expected to have limited or no systemic absorption it is unlikely that antiemetic cover is required in this setting.
L124@P2:  PRE-MEDICATIONS: None usually required.
L125@P2: OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L126@P2: Patient education.
L127@P2:  To assist dwell time and reduce dilution of the BCG the patient should not drink any fluids over a
L128@P2-3: period of 4 hours before the instillation and until 2 hours after the instillation.NCCP National SACT Regimen
