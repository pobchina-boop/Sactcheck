L69@P1: CARBOplatin dose:
L70@P1: The dose in mg of CARBOplatin to be administered is calculated as follows:
L71@P1:  Measured GFR (e.g. nuclear renogram) is preferred whenever feasible.
L72@P1:  Estimation of GFR may be performed using the Wright formula to estimate GFR or the Cockcroft
L73@P1: and Gault formula to estimate creatinine clearance.
L74@P1:  The GFR used to calculate the AUC dosing should not exceed 125mL/min. (1)
L75@P1:  For obese patients and those with a low serum creatinine, for example, due to low body weight or
L76@P1: post-operative asthenia, estimation using formulae may not give accurate results; measured GFR is
L77@P1: recommended.
L78@P1: • where obesity (body mass index [BMI] ≥ 30 kg/m2) or overweight (BMI 25-29.9) is likely to
L79@P1: lead to an overestimate of GFR and isotope GFR is not available the use of the adjusted
L80@P1: ideal body weight in the Cockcroft and Gault formula may be considered. (2)
L81@P1: • where serum creatinine is less than 63 micromol/L, the use of a creatinine value of 62
L82@P1: micromol/L or a steady pre-operative creatinine value may be considered (3)
L83@P1:  These comments do not substitute for the clinical judgement of a physician experienced in
L84@P1: prescription of CARBOplatin.
L85@P1: WRIGHT FORMULA
L86@P1: There are two versions of the formula depending on how serum creatinine values are obtained, by the
L87@P1: kinetic Jaffe method or the enzymatic method. (4) The formula can be further adapted if covariant creatine
L88@P1: kinase (CK) values are available (not shown).
L89@P1: 1. SCr measured using enzymatic assay:
L90@P1: GFR (mL/min) = (6230 - 32.8 x Age) x BSA x (1 - 0.23 x Sex)
L91@P1:  SCr (micromol/min)
L92@P1: 2. SCr measured using Jaffe assay:
L93@P1: GFR (mL/min) = (6580 - 38.8 x Age) x BSA x (1 - 0.168 x Sex)
L94@P1:  SCr (micromol/min)
L95@P1: 
L96@P1: Key: Sex = 1 if female, 0 if male; Age in years; BSA= DuBois BSA
L97@P1: COCKCROFT-GAULT FORMULA:
L98@P1: GFR (mL/min) = S x (140 - age in years) x wt (kg)
L99@P1:  serum creatinine (micromol/L)
L100@P1: S= 1.04 for females and 1.23 for males
