L28@P0: TREATMENT:
L29@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L30@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L31@P0: Induction:
L32@P0: Gemcitabine and DOCEtaxel is administered by instillation into the bladder intravesically once weekly for
L33@P0: 6 weeks.
L34@P0: Maintenance:
L35@P0: For patients achieving complete response after induction or those with low grade non-invasive
L36@P0: recurrences.
L37@P0: Gemcitabine and DOCEtaxel is administered by instillation into the bladder intravesically once a month
L38@P0: for 18 months*, starting 6 weeks after last dose of induction.
L39@P0: *indicates standard duration of maintenance treatment but may vary based on patient factors and tolerance

L70@P1: ELIGIBILITY:
L71@P1: • Indications as above
L72@P1: EXCLUSIONS:
L73@P1: • Hypersensitivity to Gemcitabine, DOCEtaxel or any of their excipients.
L74@P1: • TURBT less than 3 weeks prior to therapy
L75@P1: • Recent Haematuria
L76@P1: • Traumatic catheterisation
L77@P1: • Active Urinary Tract Infection
L78@P1: • Pregnancy and Breastfeeding
L79@P1: CAUTIONS:
L80@P1: • Patients with small bladder capacity due to increased of bladder contracture
L81@P1: PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
L82@P1: The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Urologist.
L83@P1: TESTS:
L84@P1: Pre-treatment:
L85@P1: • Cystoscopy with biopsy or resection to confirm histologic diagnosis
L86@P1: • Test urine for Urinary Tract Infection (Dipstick). Consult with Urologist if symptomatic for urinary
L87@P1: tract infection
L88@P1: Disease monitoring:
L89@P1: Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as directed
L90@P1: by the supervising Consultant Urologist.
L91@P1: If disease free, surveillance cystoscopy with cytology is usually performed every 3 months for 2 years.
L92@P1: DOSE MODIFICATIONS:
L93@P1: • Dose modifications may be considered in select cases, discuss with treating urologist
L94@P1: • Where side effects are severe gemcitabine may be withheld. Treatment as monotherapy with
L95@P1-2: docetaxel may continue.NCCP National SACT Regimen

L110@P2: Renal and Hepatic Impairment:
L111@P2: • There are no known published dose modification recommendations available for renal and hepatic
L112@P2: impairment. Given that this is a localised treatment and is expected to have limited or no systemic
L113@P2: absorption there is unlikely to be a dose modification in this setting.
L114@P2: SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L115@P2: EMETOGENIC POTENTIAL:
L116@P2: • There are no known published recommendations available for emetogenic potential. Given that
L117@P2: this is a localised treatment and is expected to have limited or no systemic absorption it is unlikely
L118@P2: that anti-emetic cover is required in this setting.
L119@P2: • Consider prophylactic ondansetron 4mg PO in patients who report nausea in previous instillation.
L120@P2: PREMEDICATIONS:
L121@P2: • Sodium bicarbonate 1200mg PO (600mg tablets x 2) the night before treatment. This alkalises the
L122@P2: urine to prevent gemcitabine induced bladder pain.
L123@P2: • Repeat sodium bicarbonate 1200mg dosage 1 hour prior to commencement of therapy.
L124@P2: OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L125@P2: • Consider Ibuprofen 400mg stat for irritative bladder symptoms
L126@P2: • Consider anticholinergic therapy e.g. mirabegron 50mg PO OD if bladder spasm reported
L127@P2: • Necessary protective contraception should be used for the duration of treatment.
L128@P2: Patient education
L129@P2: • Avoid diuretics, alcohol, and coffee for 4-6 hours prior to therapy and during drug instillation to
L130@P2: minimise dilution of drug in bladder. If the individual is on a diuretic, it should be delayed until
L131@P2: after treatment.
L132@P2: • To assist dwell time and reduce dilution of gemcitabine and DOCEtaxel the patient should not
L133@P2: drink any fluids over a period of 4 hours before the instillation and until 2 hours after the
L134@P2: instillation
L135@P2: • Patients may experience some bladder irritation, with more frequent or painful urination and
L136@P2-3: some blood or tissue in the urine.NCCP National SACT Regimen
