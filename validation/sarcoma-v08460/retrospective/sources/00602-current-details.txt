L320@P6:  Renal Toxicity: Nephrotoxicity is common with CISplatin. Strongly encourage oral hydration. If oral
L321@P6: hydration is not possible (e.g. excessive nausea), IV hydration is indicated. Avoid nephrotoxic drugs
L322@P6: such as aminoglycoside antibiotics where possible. Where treatment with nephrotoxic drugs must be
L323@P6: used, monitor renal function.

L325@P6: by history prior to each cycle.
L326@P6:  Hypersensitivity: Hypersensitivity reaction have been reported with CISplatin.
L327@P6: Ifosfamide
L328@P6:  Ifosfamide-induced encephalopathy: This may occur in patients treated with high doses of ifosfamide.
L329@P6: Neurological function should be assessed prior to each ifosfamide dose.
L330@P6:  Renal and urothelial toxicity: Ifosfamide is both nephrotoxic and urotoxic. Glomerular and tubular
L331@P6: kidney function must be evaluated and checked before commencement of therapy, as well as during
L332@P6: and after treatment. Urinary sediment should be checked regularly for the presence of erythrocytes
L333@P6: and other signs of uro/nephrotoxicity. During or immediately after administration, adequate amounts
L334@P6: of fluid should be ingested or infused to force diuresis in order to reduce the risk of urinary tract
L335@P6: toxicity. For prophylaxis of hemorrhagic cystitis, ifosfamide should be used in combination with mesna.
L336@P6: Ifosfamide should be used with caution, if at all, in patients with active urinary tract infections.
L337@P6: DRUG INTERACTIONS:
L338@P6:  Risk of drug interactions causing increased concentrations of PACLitaxel with CYP3A inhibitors.
L339@P6: Patients should also be counselled with regard to consumption of grapefruit juice.
L340@P6:  Risk of drug interactions causing decreased concentrations of PACLitaxel with CYP3A inducers.
L341@P6:  CISplatin may potentiate the nephrotoxic and ototoxic effects of loop diuretics and aminoglycosides
L342@P6: so concurrent use should be avoided.
L343@P6:  Concurrent administration of CISplatin and phenytoin may result in decreased serum levels of
L344@P6: phenytoin. In these patients, monitor plasma levels of phenytoin and dose adjust accordingly.
L345@P6:  Consider increased risk of ifosfamide-induced neurotoxicity due to inhibition of CYP3A4 by aprepitant.
