L23@P0: TREATMENT:
L24@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L25@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patient’s individual clinical circumstances.
L26@P0: DOXOrubicin is administered on Day 1 of a 21 day cycle for 3-6 cycles unless the patient experiences disease
L27@P0: progression or unacceptable toxicity.
L28@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

L198@P3: i Cardiotoxicity is a risk associated with anthracycline therapy that may be manifested by early (acute) or late
L199@P3: (delayed) effects.
L200@P3: Risk factors for developing anthracycline-induced cardiotoxicity include:
L201@P3: • high cumulative dose, previous therapy with other anthracyclines or anthracenediones
L202@P3: • prior or concomitant radiotherapy to the mediastinal/pericardial area
L203@P3: • pre-existing heart disease
L204@P3: • concomitant use of other potentially cardiotoxic drugs
L205@P3: In establishing the maximal cumulative dose of an anthracycline, consideration should be given to the risk
L206@P3: factors above and to the age of the patient
