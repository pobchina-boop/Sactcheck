# CMF source reconciliation for v0.84.18

Reviewed 25 October 2026 against the three current official PDFs. Proposed files are in `proposed/`; production files have not been changed by this audit. `sources.json` records URLs, dates, byte counts, SHA256 and page maps. Raw PDFs and extracted text remain in scratch research, outside the distributable. This is source/software work, not independent clinical or pharmacy approval.

## Material findings

1. Oral CMF also has a metastatic indication (00377b), missing from the baseline metadata. All three support adjuvant use when anthracyclines are unsuitable and metastatic use. The adjuvant course is six cycles for oral and IV28, eight for IV21. Metastatic treatment continues to progression or unacceptable toxicity; a fixed adjuvant cap must not be imposed.
2. Preserve the routes and days. Oral: cyclophosphamide 100 mg/m² daily Days 1–14 plus IV methotrexate 40 and 5-FU 600 mg/m² on Days 1 and 8, every 28 days. IV28: all three on Days 1 and 8, cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m². IV21: all three on Day 1, every 21 days. Administration order is 5-FU, methotrexate, cyclophosphamide. Swallow oral cyclophosphamide whole, one hour before food or on an empty stomach.
3. Counts: printed bands are ANC <1 (delay), 1–1.49 (75%), >1.5 (100%); platelets <70 (delay), 70–89 (75%), >90 (100%). Exactly 1.5/90 and gaps above 1.49/89 are not assigned. The literal OR in the full-dose row can conflict with an abnormal counterpart. Proposed rules retain the adverse finding, surface the conflict and never give full clearance from the normal value alone. The source gives one count table, not separate Day 1/Day 8 algorithms.
4. Oral and IV cyclophosphamide renal dosing differs. Oral: >20 full dose, 10–20 at 75%, <10 at 50%. IV: >30 no adjustment; 10–29 consider 75%; <10 not recommended, or consider 50% if unavoidable. The IV source leaves values >29 through 30 unassigned. Oral severe hepatic impairment is a clinical decision; IV severe hepatic impairment is not recommended because of reduced efficacy. Do not copy one route’s rule into the other.
5. Methotrexate: CrCl 20–50 at 50%, ≥50 no adjustment, so exactly 50 overlaps. Below 20 not recommended; specialist consideration if unavoidable. Dialysis has an explicit exceptional 50%/daily high-flux statement. Bilirubin >86 µmol/L: avoid. Pleural effusions and ascites can retain methotrexate and require review.
6. 5-FU: complete DPD deficiency excludes treatment; partial deficiency needs an individually reduced start, not a guessed universal percentage. Confirm baseline testing. Renal dysfunction can confound uracil phenotyping; consider genotyping. The hepatic no-adjustment statement is qualified by absence of renal impairment. Oral and IV21 say severe hepatic impairment is not recommended. **IV28 does not contain that severe-impairment sentence** (visually checked p3); the old IV28 rule falsely attributed it to the source. Proposed IV28 output calls this a source gap and requires specialist/SmPC review.
7. Hypersensitivity, pregnancy/breastfeeding, ECOG outside 0–2, unresolved tests and clinically significant toxicity were not fully surfaced. Added review/exclusion inputs preserve partial assessment. Acute FN, severe GI toxicity and chest symptoms trigger clinical safety review, with no invented future-cycle dose percentages. These safety interpretations are labelled separately from printed dosing rules.
8. The source does not specify routine echo surveillance for CMF; do not copy the anthracycline/HER2 calendar. Baseline and pre-cycle FBC, renal and liver testing are stated. Day 8 testing/review is a local planned process where applicable, not an invented separate national threshold. Patients require an urgent-symptom pathway for 5-FU cardiac symptoms despite absence of routine echo scheduling.
9. Hydration: all three suggest 2–3 L on Days 1 and 8. **IV21 has no Day 8 chemotherapy**, so the hydration note conflicts with its schedule. Record the source inconsistency and individualise hydration rather than creating a Day 8 visit. Consider cardiac/renal restrictions. No routine hypersensitivity premedication is usually required; antiemetic prophylaxis is a separate matter. Oral cyclophosphamide is moderate-to-high emetic risk throughout Days 1–14; IV cyclophosphamide is moderate, methotrexate/5-FU low. Review current route-specific NCCP antiemetic guidance when connecting support scripts.

## Proposed implementation

`build-proposed.py` snapshots the three baseline files once and creates reconciled JSON copies. It preserves stable protocol IDs and false independent-approval flags. Legacy coarse renal band selection is replaced by numeric CrCl so exact boundaries and decimals can be handled. The renal method field prevents an explicitly indexed eGFR being used as CrCl. A separate clinical renal-impairment flag handles the 5-FU hepatic qualifier without inventing a numerical 5-FU reduction threshold.

All proposed dose actions require specialist review and suppress an automatic numeric prescribed dose. Positive safe-normal findings are not generated as full treatment clearance. Omitted domains stay unassessed.

Proposed copies pass 168/168. The preserved baseline passes 68 and fails 100. Failures include new input contracts and newly tested schedule coverage; they are not 100 distinct clinical errors.

The source case test intentionally expects numeric CrCl. Historical regression fixtures using the old select-band UI may need a narrowly scoped migration; clinical boundary expectations should remain independently tested.

## Evidence

`evidence.json` contains the verified primary publication data and applicability limits. Bonadonna et al. BMJ 2005 (doi:10.1136/bmj.38314.622095.8F) reports historical adjuvant trials. The original node-positive comparison involved 386 women and 12 cycles, with median follow-up 28.5 years: relapse HR 0.71 (95% CI 0.56–0.91), death HR 0.79 (0.63–0.98). A separate 324-patient premenopausal study compared six with 12 cycles. A 90-patient node-negative/ER-negative trial tested 12 cycles of IV CMF every three weeks, not eight. Do not attach those numbers to IV28 or metastatic treatment, or claim equivalence of the three schedules. No exact IV28 comparative or CMF metastatic numerical estimate was verified here.
