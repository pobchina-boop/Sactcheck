L24@P0: TREATMENT:
L25@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L26@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L27@P0: Irinotecan and temozolomide are administered on five consecutive days (days 1-5) of a 21 day cycle.
L28@P0: Treatment should continue for a total of 6 cycles unless the patient experiences disease progression or
L29@P0: unacceptable toxicity develops, whichever occurs first.
L30@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.
L31@P0: Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Cycle
L32@P0: 1-5 Temozolomide 100mg/ m2 PO a,b,c, N/A Every 21 days for 6
L33@P0: cycles
L34@P0: 1-5 Irinotecan 50mg/m2IV infusion 100mL NaCl 0.9% over 60
L35@P0: minutes
L36@P0: Every 21 days for 6
L37@P0: cycles
L38@P0: a Temozolomide hard capsules should be administered in the fasting state.
L39@P0: The capsules must be swallowed whole with a glass of water and must not be opened or chewed.
L40@P0: If vomiting occurs after the dose is administered, a second dose should not be administered that day.
L41@P0: b Temozolomide should be administered at least one hour prior to irinotecan.
L42@P0: cTemozolomide is commonly available as 5 mg, 20 mg, 100 mg, 140 mg, 180 mg, or 250 mg hard capsules.

L224@P4: Prophylactic atropine sulphate.Atropine should not be used in patients with glaucoma.
L225@P4: OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L226@P4:  Patients should be made aware of the risk of delayed diarrhoea occurring more than 24 hours after
L227@P4: the administration of irinotecan and at any time before the next cycle
L228@P4: o As soon as the first liquid stool occurs, the patient should start drinking large volumes of
L229@P4: beverages containing electrolytes and an appropriate anti-diarrhoeal therapy must be
L230@P4: initiated immediately
L231@P4: o The currently recommended anti-diarrhoeal treatment consists of high doses of
L232@P4: loperamide (4 mg for the first intake and then 2 mg every 2 hours)
L233@P4: o This therapy should continue for 12 hours after the last liquid stool and should not be
L234@P4: modified
L235@P4: o In no instance should loperamide be administered for more than 48 consecutive hours at
L236@P4: these doses, because of the risk of paralytic ileus, nor for less than 12 hours
L237@P4: o Noting that cefixime was used in the supporting trial, this agent may be considered in adults
L238@P4: at a dose of 400 mg orally once daily from day-2 to day +7
L239@P4:  Patients should be warned about the potential for dizziness or visual disturbances which may occur
L240@P4: within 24 hours following the administration of irinotecan, and advised not to drive or operate
L241@P4: machinery if these symptoms occur
L242@P4:  PJP prophylaxis required with temozolomide (Refer to local policy)
L243@P4: ADVERSE EFFECTS:
L244@P4:  Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) for details.
L245@P4: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L246@P4:  Opportunistic infections and reactivation of infections: Opportunistic infections (such as
L247@P4: Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia) and reactivation of infections (such as HBV, CMV) have been
L248@P4: observed during the treatment with temozolomide.
L249@P4:  Hepatitis B Virus (HBV): Hepatitis due to HBV reactivation, in some cases resulting in death, has been
L250@P4: reported. Experts in liver disease should be consulted before treatment is initiated in patients with
L251@P4: positive hepatitis B serology (including those with active disease). During treatment patients should
L252@P4: be monitored and managed appropriately.
