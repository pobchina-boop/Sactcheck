L26@P0: TREATMENT:
L27@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L28@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patient’s individual clinical circumstances.
L29@P0: DOXOrubicin is administered on days 1-3 and CISplatin on Day 1 of a 21 day cycle for six cycles (THREE cycles
L30@P0: usually given pre-operatively and THREE cycles given post-operatively following review of pathology).
L31@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anticancer therapy (SACT) is administered.
L32@P0: Note: Administration volumes and fluids have been standardised to facilitate electronic prescribing system builds.

L36@P0:  Adequate organ function

L235@P4: • prior or concomitant radiotherapy to the mediastinal/pericardial area
L236@P4: • pre-existing heart disease
L237@P4: • concomitant use of other potentially cardiotoxic drugs
