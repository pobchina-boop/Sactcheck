L372@P7: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L373@P7: • Cardiotoxicity: DOXOrubicin is cardiotoxic and must be used with caution, if at all, in patients with
L374@P7: severe hypertension or cardiac dysfunction.
L375@P7: • Red discolouration of urine: This may occur for 1-2 days after administration of DOXOrubicin.
L376@P7: • Ifosfamide-induced encephalopathy: This may occur in patients treated with high doses of
L377@P7: ifosfamide. Neurological function should be assessed prior to each ifosfamide dose.
L378@P7: • Renal and urothelial toxicity: Ifosfamide is both nephrotoxic and urotoxic. Glomerular and tubular
L379@P7: kidney function must be evaluated and checked before commencement of therapy, as well as during
L380@P7: and after treatment. Urinary sediment should be checked regularly for the presence of erythrocytes
L381@P7: and other signs of uro/nephrotoxicity. During or immediately after administration, adequate
L382@P7: amounts of fluid should be ingested or infused to force diuresis in order to reduce the risk of urinary
L383@P7: tract toxicity. For prophylaxis of hemorrhagic cystitis, ifosfamide should be used in combination with
L384@P7: mesna. Ifosfamide should be used with caution, if at all, in patients with active urinary tract
L385@P7: infections.
L386@P7: • Neuropathy: vinCRIStine may cause peripheral neuropathy which is dose related and cumulative,
L387@P7: requiring monitoring before each dose is administered. The presence of pre-existing neuropathies or
L388@P7: previous treatment with other neurotoxic drugs may increase risk of peripheral neuropathy. Patients
L389@P7: with mild peripheral neuropathy can usually continue to receive full doses of vinCRIStine, but when
L390@P7: symptoms increase in severity and interfere with neurologic function, dose reduction or
L391@P7: discontinuation of the drug may be necessary. The natural history following discontinuation of
L392@P7: treatment is gradual improvement, which may take up to several months
L393@P7: • Constipation: A routine prophylactic regimen against constipation is recommended for all patients
L394@P7: receiving vinCRIStine sulphate. Paralytic ileus may occur. The ileus will reverse itself upon temporary
L395@P7: discontinuance of vinCRIStine and with symptomatic care.

L448@P8: • high cumulative dose, previous therapy with other anthracyclines or anthracenediones
L449@P8: • prior or concomitant radiotherapy to the mediastinal/pericardial area
L450@P8: • pre-existing heart disease
