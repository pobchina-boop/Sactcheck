Why this regimen?

The liver activates ifosfamide into substances that damage deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA), the cell's genetic material. Some breakdown products can injure the urinary tract. Mesna and prescribed fluids reduce bladder injury, but do not remove kidney, brain or other chemotherapy risks. Etoposide interferes with topoisomerase II, an enzyme that manages breaks in deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA), the cell's genetic material. Damaged cancer cells cannot divide normally. Blood production and other healthy tissues can also be affected. Vincristine interferes with microtubules, structures needed for cells to divide and for nerves to work normally. This helps explain numbness, weakness and bowel slowing. It is given only into a vein by trained staff; administration into spinal fluid can be fatal. Doxorubicin interferes with deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA), the cell's genetic material, and the enzymes that manage it. Cancer cells can be damaged, but blood production, the mouth and the heart can also be affected. Previous anthracycline treatment contributes to the heart-risk assessment. Cyclophosphamide is activated into substances that damage deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA), the cell's genetic material. Blood and reproductive cells can also be affected. Urinary breakdown products can injure the bladder, so the prescribed fluid and any mesna plan must be followed. This medicine is optional in the final two even-numbered administrations and is included in your consent only if selected. Dactinomycin binds to deoxyribonucleic acid (DNA), the cell's genetic material, and disrupts production of ribonucleic acid (RNA), which carries instructions for cell activity. Cancer cells and healthy blood, mouth, gut and liver tissues can be affected. For Ewing sarcoma, systemic chemotherapy is combined with surgery and/or radiotherapy for local tumour control. For rhabdomyosarcoma, the specialist combines systemic and local treatment according to site, risk and feasibility. These decisions require the relevant multidisciplinary team; Ewing trial percentages cannot be used as estimates for the other listed tumour types. Rehabilitation, symptom control and psychological support can accompany cancer treatment.

Primary study report

In a randomised trial of predominantly children and adolescents with localised Ewing sarcoma, giving alternating chemotherapy every two weeks improved event-free survival compared with every three weeks. Longer follow-up of the same study supported sustained benefit. This evidence informs interval choice but does not make the individual national calendars identical to the study.

Endpoint key and interpretation
◇ Locoregional events · Locoregional events
Recurrence or progression at the primary site or regional nodes, using the trial definition and stated follow-up. This is not overall survival.
◷ OS · Overall survival
Time to death from any cause, from the study-defined starting point.
◫ PFS · Progression-free survival
Time to progression or death; preserve the study assessment criteria.
◫ TTP · Time to progression
Time to the trial-defined progression event. Death handling varies by trial; TTP is not interchangeable with PFS.
◇ EFS · Event-free survival
Time to a study-defined event. Check the exact events included; this is not interchangeable with DFS.
◇ RFS · Relapse-free survival
Time to relapse or death as defined by the trial. Check its event definition and analysis; it is not overall survival.
◇ DFS · Disease-free survival
Time to disease recurrence or other events specified by the study, including death where defined.
◇ iDFS · Invasive disease-free survival
Time to the invasive-disease events and deaths defined by the study; review the full composite definition.
◷ DOR · Duration of response
Among responders, time from the first documented objective response to progression or death under the study definition. It is not an outcome for every enrolled patient.
◎ ORR · Objective response rate
Proportion meeting objective response criteria, generally complete plus partial responses. Not an estimate of cure.
⌕ pCR · Pathological complete response
Complete pathological response under the study definition. Specify the examined sites and residual-disease criteria; not proof of cure.
♧ QoL · Quality of life
Specify the instrument, domain, time point, score direction and clinically meaningful threshold where established.

Colour identifies the endpoint family. Effect size, statistical interpretation and maturity are separate. Median differences are not an individual's expected added lifespan. Do not rank regimens using results from separate trials.
