Day 1 → Day 21: a general pattern

This is a visual orientation, not a prediction of your personal cycle. It shows when some effects often emerge and which effects are not tied to a particular day.

Irinotecan 50 mg/m² is given intravenously on days 1–5 of each 21-day cycle. Temozolomide is taken by mouth on days 1–5: 125 mg/m² in cycle 1. Only the treating team may select 150 mg/m² from cycle 2 after toxicity review. Vincristine 1.5 mg/m², maximum 2 mg, is given on days 1 and 8. The national plan specifies 12 cycles unless progression, toxicity or the specialist plan changes treatment. Take temozolomide fasting, swallowed whole with water, at least one hour before irinotecan. If you vomit after temozolomide, do not take an extra dose.

Schedule scope: The live national catalogue places this in adult treatment, while the PDF includes a wider age range and age-specific performance scales. Paediatric use requires the appropriate specialist pathway, not automatic extension of this adult portal. Temozolomide escalation and reduction depend on actual previous doses and toxicity; a recovered grade does not erase prior events. The national source identifies unlicensed indication use: the team must explain the rationale and document consent under local policy, including the source drug-and-therapeutics committee requirement.
Symptoms: Symptoms can occur on any day. An unshaded day is not a guarantee that treatment effects have passed.
Why these bands stay broad: Educational treatment days, not dated appointments or guaranteed safe days. Support timing and any changed sequence follow the actual prescription.
