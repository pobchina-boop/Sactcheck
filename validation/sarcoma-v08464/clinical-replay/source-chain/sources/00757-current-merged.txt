L327@P5: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L328@P5: vinCRIStine:
L329@P5:  Neuropathy: vinCRIStine may cause peripheral neuropathy which is dose related and cumulative,
L330@P5: requiring monitoring before each dose is administered. The presence of pre-existing neuropathies or
L331@P5: previous treatment with other neurotoxic drugs may increase risk of peripheral neuropathy. Patients
L332@P5: with mild peripheral neuropathy can usually continue to receive full doses of vinCRIStine, but when
L333@P5: symptoms increase in severity and interfere with neurologic function, dose reduction or
L334@P5: discontinuation of the drug may be necessary. The natural history following discontinuation of
L335@P5: treatment is gradual improvement, which may take up to several months.
L336@P5:  Constipation: A routine prophylactic regimen against constipation is recommended for all patients
L337@P5: receiving vinCRIStine sulphate. Paralytic ileus may occur. The ileus will reverse itself upon temporary
L338@P5: discontinuance of vinCRIStine and with symptomatic care.
L339@P5: Temozolomide:
L340@P5:  Opportunistic infections and reactivation of infections: Opportunistic infections (such as
L341@P5: Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia) and reactivation of infections (such as HBV, CMV) have been
L342@P5: observed during the treatment with temozolomide.
L343@P5:  Hepatitis B Virus (HBV): Hepatitis due to HBV reactivation, in some cases resulting in death, has been
L344@P5: reported with temozolomide. Experts in liver disease should be consulted before treatment is
L345@P5: initiated in patients with positive hepatitis B serology (including those with active disease). During
L346@P5: treatment patients should be monitored and managed appropriately.
