L30@P0: TREATMENT:
L31@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L32@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L33@P0: Treatment consists of 7 cycles of etoposide, ifosfamide-mesna (IE therapy) alternating with 5 cycles of
L34@P0: vinCRIStine, DOXOrubicin and cycloPHOSphamide (VAC) and 2 cycles of vincristine and cycloPHOSphamide
L35@P0: +/- DACTINomycin (VDC) with or without mesna (VAC/VDC therapy) at two weekly intervals (total of 14
L36@P0: chemotherapy treatments). IE is administered on days 1 to 5 of a 14-day cycle. VAC/VDC is administered
L37@P0: on day 1 of a 14-day cycle.
L38@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti -cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.

L350@P6: ADVERSE EFFECTS / REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L351@P6: The adverse effects listed are not exhaustive. Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics for full
L352@P6: details.
L353@P6:  Neutropenia: Fever or other evidence of infection must be assessed promptly and treated
L354@P6: appropriately.
L355@P6:  Infertility: Both DOXOrubicin and ifosfamide have genotoxic effects and may cause infertility. Women
L356@P6: should not become pregnant during and up to 6 months after treatment and men are also advised not
L357@P6: to father a child during this time.
L358@P6: Etoposide
L359@P6:  Hypersensitivity: Hypersensitivity reactions have been reported with etoposide. Monitor infusion of
L360@P6: etoposide for the first 15 minutes for signs of hypotension.
L361@P6: Ifosfamide
L362@P6:  Ifosfamide-induced encephalopathy: This may occur in patients treated with high doses of ifosfamide.
L363@P6: Neurological function should be assessed prior to each ifosfamide dose.
L364@P6:  Renal and urothelial toxicity: Ifosfamide is both nephrotoxic and urotoxic. Glomerular and tubular
L365@P6: kidney function must be evaluated and checked before commencement of therapy, as well as during
L366@P6: and after treatment. Urinary sediment should be checked regularly for the presence of erythrocytes
L367@P6: and other signs of uro/nephrotoxicity. During or immediately after administration, adequate amounts
L368@P6: of fluid should be ingested or infused to force diuresis in order to reduce the risk of urinary tract
L369@P6: toxicity. For prophylaxis of hemorrhagic cystitis, ifosfamide should be used in combination with mesna.
L370@P6: Ifosfamide should be used with caution, if at all, in patients with active urinary tract infections.
L371@P6: vinCRIStine
L372@P6:  Peripheral neuropathy: vinCRIStine may cause peripheral neuropathy which is dose related and
L373@P6: cumulative, requiring monitoring before each dose is administered. The presence of pre-existing
L374@P6: neuropathies or previous treatment with other neurotoxic drugs may increase risk of peripheral
L375@P6: neuropathy. Patients with mild peripheral neuropathy can usually continue to receive full doses of
L376@P6: vinCRIStine, but when symptoms increase in severity and interfere with neurologic function, dose
L377@P6: reduction or discontinuation of the drug may be necessary. The natural history following
L378@P6: discontinuation of treatment is gradual improvement, which may take up to several months.
L379@P6: DOXOrubicin
L380@P6:  Cardiotoxicity: DOXOrubicin is cardiotoxic and must be used with caution, if at all, in patients with
L381@P6: severe hypertension or cardiac dysfunction
L382@P6:  Extravasation: DOXOrubicin and vinCRIStine cause pain and tissue necrosis if extravasated (Refer to
L383@P6: local policy).
L384@P6-7:  Red discolouration of urine: This may occur for 1-2 days after administration of DOXOrubicin.NCCP National SACT Regimen

L397@P7: DRUG INTERACTIONS:
L398@P7:  Current drug interaction databases should be consulted for more information.
L399@P7:  DOXOrubicin cardiotoxicity is enhanced by previous or concurrent use of other anthracyclines, or other
L400@P7: potentially cardiotoxic drugs (e.g. 5-FU, cycloPHOSphamide, paclitaxel or trastuzumab) or with
L401@P7: products affecting cardiac function (e.g. calcium antagonists).
L402@P7:  Increased nephrotoxicity may result from a combined effect of ifosfamide and other nephrotoxic drugs
L403@P7: e.g. aminoglycosides, platinum compounds.
L404@P7:  Increased risk of ifosfamide-induced neurotoxicity and vinca alkaloid-induced adverse effects due to
L405@P7: inhibition of CYP3A4 by aprepitant.
L406@P7:  Avoid combination of CYP3A4 inducers and ifosfamide. There is the possibility of increased toxicity of
L407@P7: ifosfamide due to increased conversion to active and toxic metabolites.
L408@P7:  Reduced efficacy of ifosfamide possible with CYP3A4 inhibitors due to decreased conversion to active
L409@P7: metabolites.
L410@P7:  Concurrent administration of vinCRIStine with allopurinol, pyridoxine or isoniazid may increase the
L411@P7: incidence of cytotoxic induced bone marrow depression.
L412@P7:  CYP3A4 enzyme inducers may increase the clearance of vinCRIStine and etoposide.
L413@P7:  CYP3A4 enzyme inhibitors may decrease the clearance of vinCRIStine and etoposide.
L414@P7:  P-gp inhibitors may decrease the clearance of etoposide
