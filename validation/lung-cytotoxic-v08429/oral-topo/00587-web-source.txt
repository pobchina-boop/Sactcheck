L139@P2: ADVERSE EFFECTS / REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS
L140@P2: The adverse effects listed are not exhaustive. Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics for full
L141@P2: details.
L142@P2:  Neutropenia; Fever or other evidence of infection must be assessed promptly and treated
L143@P2: aggressively.
L144@P2:  Diarrhoea: Proactive management of diarrhoea with anti-diarrhoeal agents is important. Severe
L145@P2: cases of diarrhoea may require administration of oral or intravenous electrolytes and fluids, and
L146@P2: interruption of topotecan therapy.
L147@P2:  Neutropenic enterocolitis: Topotecan-induced neutropenia may lead to neutropenic enterocolitis.
L148@P2: This should be considered in patients presenting with neutropenia, fever and abdominal pain.
L149@P2:  Interstitial lung disease: Topotecan has been associated with reports of interstitial lung disease (ILD),
L150@P2: some of which have been fatal. Underlying risk factors include history of ILD, pulmonary fibrosis, lung
L151@P2: cancer, thoracic exposure to radiation and use of pneumotoxic drugs and/or colony stimulating
L152@P2: factors. Patients should be monitored for pulmonary symptoms indicative of ILD (e.g. cough, fever,
L153@P2: dyspnoea and/or hypoxia), and topotecan should be discontinued if a new diagnosis of ILD is
L154@P2: confirmed.
