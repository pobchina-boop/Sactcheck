L32@P0: TREATMENT:
L33@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L34@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patient’s individual clinical circumstances.
L35@P0: Topotecan is administered on five consecutive days (days 1-5) of a 21 day cycle for 6 cycles or until
L36@P0: disease progression or unacceptable toxicity develops, whichever occurs first.
L37@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.
L38@P0: Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Cycle
L39@P0: 1-5
L40@P0: a Topotecan 1.5mg/m2
L41@P0: IV infusion 100mL NaCl 0.9%b over
L42@P0: 30 minutes
L43@P0: Every 21 days for 6 cycles
L44@P0: a May be reduced to Days 1-3 or Days 1-4 if signs of toxicity or in heavily pre-treated patients at the
L45@P0: discretion of the prescribing consultant.
L46@P0: b Topotecan should be diluted to a final concentration of between 25 and 50 microgram/mL.
L47@P0: Note: Administration volumes and fluids have been standardised to facilitate electronic prescribing system builds.
