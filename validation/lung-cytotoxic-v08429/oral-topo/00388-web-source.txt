L23@P0: TREATMENT:
L24@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L25@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L26@P0: Cycle frequency and duration:
L27@P0: Each cycle is 21 days. There are a number of different regimens for oral etoposide for this indication as outlined
L28@P0: in the treatment table below. Treatment is continued until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity develops.
L29@P0: Day Drug Dose Route Cycle
L30@P0: 1-7 Etoposide 50 mg BD PO Every 21 days
L31@P0: OR
L32@P0: 1-3 Etoposide 100 mg/m2 BD PO Every 21 days
L33@P0: OR
L34@P0: 1-21 Etoposide 50 mg/m2 daily PO Every 21 days
L35@P0: Etoposide is commonly available in 50mg and 100mg capsules
L36@P0: The capsules should be taken on an empty stomach
L37@P0: ELIGIBILITY:
L38@P0: • Indication as above
L39@P0: • ECOG 0-3
L40@P0: EXCLUSIONS:
L41@P0: • Hypersensitivity to etoposide or any of the excipients
L42@P0: • Yellow fever vaccine or other live vaccines
L43@P0: • Pregnancy
L44@P0: • Breastfeeding
