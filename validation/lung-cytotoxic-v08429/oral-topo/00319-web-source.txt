L63@P1: CARBOplatin dose:
L64@P1: The dose in mg of CARBOplatin to be administered is calculated as follows:
L65@P1: Dose (mg) = target AUC (mg/mL x minute) x (GFR mL/minute +25)
L66@P1: • Measured GFR (e.g. nuclear renogram) is preferred whenever feasible
L67@P1: • Estimation of GFR (eGFR) can be done by using the Wright formula to estimate GFR or the
L68@P1: Cockcroft and Gault formula to measure creatinine clearance
L69@P1: • The GFR used to calculate the AUC dosing should not exceed 125mL/minute
L70@P1: • For obese patients and those with a low serum creatinine, for example, due to low body
L71@P1: weight or post-operative asthenia, estimation using formulae may not give accurate results;
L72@P1: measured GFR is recommend
L73@P1:  where obesity (body mass index [BMI] ≥30kg/m2) or overweight (BMI 25-29.9) is likely
L74@P1: to lead to an overestimate of GFR and isotope GFR is not available the use of the
L75@P1: adjusted ideal body weight in the Cockcroft and Gault formula may be considered
L76@P1:  where serum creatinine is less than 63 micromol/L, the use of a creatinine value of 62
L77@P1: micromol/L or a steady pre-operative creatinine value may be considered
L78@P1: • These comments do not substitute for the clinical judgement of a physician experienced
L79@P1: in prescription of CARBOplatin
L80@P1: WRIGHT FORMULA
L81@P1: There are two versions of the formula depending on how serum creatinine values are obtained, by
L82@P1: the kinetic Jaffe method or the enzymatic method. The formula can be further adapted if covariant
L83@P1: creatine kinase (CK) values are available (not shown).
L84@P1: 1. SCr measured using enzymatic assay.
L85@P1: GFR (mL/minute) = (6230 - 32.8 x Age) x BSA x (1 - 0.23 x Sex)
L86@P1: SCr (micromol/minute)
L87@P1: 2. SCr measured using Jaffe assay
L88@P1:  GFR (mL/minute) = (6580 - 38.8 x Age) x BSA x (1 - 0.168 x Sex)
L89@P1:  SCr (micromol/minute)
L90@P1: 
L91@P1: Key: Sex = 1 if female, 0 if male; Age in years; BSA= DuBois BSA
L92@P1: COCKCROFT-GAULT FORMULA
L93@P1: GFR (ml/min) = S x (140 - age in years) x wt (kg)
L94@P1: serum creatinine (micromol/L)
L95@P1-2: S= 1.04 for females and 1.23 for males NCCP National SACT Regimen

L110@P2: ELIGIBILITY:
L111@P2: • Indication as above
L112@P2: • Patients unsuitable for treatment with CISplatin based regimens
L113@P2: • ECOG 0-2 (0-3 in patients < 70)
L114@P2: EXCLUSIONS:
L115@P2: • Hypersensitivity to CARBOplatin* etoposide or any of the excipients
L116@P2: • Pregnancy
L117@P2: • Breastfeeding
L118@P2: *If it is felt that the patient may have a major clinical benefit from CARBOplatin, it may in exceptional circumstances be feasible to
L119@P2: rechallenge a patient with a prior mild hypersensitivity reaction e.g. using a desensitisation protocol, but only with immunology
L120@P2: advice, premedication as advised, and a desensitisation protocol under carefully controlled conditions with resuscitation facilities
L121@P2: available and medical and/or ITU/ HDU supervision

L225@P4: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS
L226@P4: • Neurotoxicity and ototoxicity: Neurological evaluation and an assessment of hearing should be
L227@P4: performed on a regular basis, especially in patients receiving high dose CARBOplatin. Neurotoxicity,
L228@P4: such as parasthesia, decreased deep tendon reflexes, and ototoxicity are more likely seen in patients
L229@P4: previously treated with CISplatin, other platinum treatments and other ototoxic agents. Frequency
L230@P4: of neurologic toxicity is also increased in patients older than 65 years.
