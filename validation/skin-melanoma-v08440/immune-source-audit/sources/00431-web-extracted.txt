L32: TREATMENT:
L33: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their independent medical
L34: judgement, to consider each patient’s individual clinical circumstances.
L35: Cycles 1-4:
L36: Nivolumab and ipilimumab are administered on Day 1 of a 21-day cycle for the first 4 cycles.
L37: From cycle 5 onwards:
L38: Nivolumab is administered as monotherapy at a dose of 240mg IV or 600mg SC on Day 1 of a 14-Day cycle
L39: (Ref NCCP Regimen 00483) or 480mg IV or 1200mg SC on Day 1 of a 28-Day cycle (Ref NCCP Regimen 00484).
L40: For the monotherapy phase, the first dose of nivolumab should be administered as follows:
L41: For melanoma:
L42: • 3 weeks after the last dose of the combination of nivolumab and ipilimumab if using the 240mg IV
L43: or 600mg SC every 14 days schedule
L44: or
L45: • 6 weeks after the last dose of the combination of nivolumab and ipilimumab if using the 480mg IV
L46: or 1200mg SC every 28 days schedule
L47: For HCC:
L48: • 3 weeks after the last dose of the combination of nivolumab and ipilimumab
L49: Monotherapy phase duration for melanoma:
L50: Nivolumab should continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
L51: Monotherapy phase duration for HCC:
L52: Nivolumab should continue for up to 24 months unless patient experiences disease progression or

L447: referred to a specialist for assessment and treatment without delay. The diagnosis of myocarditis requires a high index of suspicion. Patients with

L464: combination with ipilimumab in clinical trials across doses and tumour types: pancreatitis, uveitis, demyelination, autoimmune neuropathy
