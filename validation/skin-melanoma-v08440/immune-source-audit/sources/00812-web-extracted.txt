L14: Cemiplimab Therapy
L15: INDICATIONS FOR USE:
L16: INDICATION ICD10
L17: Regimen
L18: Code
L19: HSE approved
L20: reimbursement
L21: Status*
L22: As monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with recurrent or
L23: metastatic cervical cancer and disease progression on or after platinumbased chemotherapy
L24: C53 00812a N/A
L25: As monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with
L26: metastatic or locally advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (mCSCC
L27: or laCSCC) who are not candidates for curative surgery or curative radiation
L28: C44 00812b ODMS
L29: 1/10/2024
L30: * This is for post 2012 indications only.
L31: TREATMENT:
L32: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L33: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L34: Cemiplimab is administered on day 1 of a 21 day cycle and continued until disease progression or
L35: unacceptable toxicity occurs.
L36: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.
L37: Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Cycle
L38: 1 Cemiplimab 350mg IV infusion a50mL NaCl 0.9% over 30 minutes Every 21 days
L39: Cemiplimab should be administered through an intravenous line containing a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein binding, inline or add-on filter (0.2 micron to 5 micron pore size).
L40: Other medicinal products should not be co-administered through the same infusion line.
L41: a Cemiplimab is diluted to a final concentration ranging from 1mg/mL to 20mg/mL
L42:  Note: Administration volumes and fluids have been standardised to facilitate electronic prescribing system builds.

L48: o At least 1 measurable lesion

L65: EXCLUSIONS:
L66:  Hypersensitivity to cemiplimab or any of the excipients
L67:  Immunosuppressive doses of systemic corticosteroids or other immunosuppressive
L68: medication(s) (defined as >10mg predniSOLONE/daily (or steroid equivalent, excluding inhaled
L69: or topical steroids)
L70:  Any active clinically significant infection requiring therapy
L71:  Active CNS metastases
L72:  Information regarding prior therapy with an anti PD-1 or anti PD-L1 antibody is available here
L73:  Pregnancy/breastfeeding
L74: PRESCRIPTIVE AUTHORITY:
L75:  The treatment plan must be initiated by a Consultant Medical Oncologist
L76: TESTS:
L77: Baseline tests:
L78:  FBC, renal and liver profile
L79:  Blood glucose
L80:  Thyroid function tests
L81:  Virology Screen: Hepatitis B (HBsAg, HBcoreAb) and Hepatitis C
L82: Regular tests:
L83:  FBC, renal and liver profile prior to each cycle
L84:  Blood glucose prior to each cycle
L85:  TSH every 3 to 6 weeks
L86: Disease monitoring:
L87: Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as
L88: directed by the supervising Consultant.
