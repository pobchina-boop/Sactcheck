L29@P0: TREATMENT:
L30@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L31@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L32@P0: Vismodegib is administered once daily until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity develops.
L33@P0: Benefit of continued treatment should be regularly assessed, with the optimal duration of therapy varying
L34@P0: for each individual patient.
L35@P0: Drug Dose Route Cycle
L36@P0: Vismodegib 150mg daily PO Continuous therapy
L37@P0: If a dose is missed, patients should be instructed not to take the missed dose but to resume with the next scheduled dose.
L38@P0: Take with or without food at the same time each day.
L39@P0: Capsule must be swallowed whole with 200mL water. Capsules should NOT be crushed or opened.
L40@P0: Vismodegib is commonly available as 150mg capsules.

L111@P2: PREMEDICATIONS: Not usually required
L112@P2: OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L113@P2: • Vismodegib must never be used by women who are pregnant or by women who could become
L114@P2: pregnant unless all the conditions of the Erivedge® Pregnancy Prevention Programme are met. These
L115@P2: conditions must be fulfilled for all male and female patients.
L116@P2: For male patients: Vismodegib is present in semen.
L117@P2: To avoid potential foetal exposure during pregnancy, a male patient must understand that:
L118@P2: o Vismodegib exposes a teratogenic risk to the unborn child if he engages in unprotected sexual
L119@P2: activity with a pregnant woman,
L120@P2: o He must always use the recommended contraception
L121@P2: o He will tell his healthcare provider if his female partner becomes pregnant while he is taking
L122@P2: vismodegib or during the 2 months after his final dose.
