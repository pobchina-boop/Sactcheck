# New colorectal patient content — v0.84.35

Five context profiles authored from current NCCP sources retrieved on 5 November 2026, supplementary product information and primary trial reports. Existing pembrolizumab breast/lung profiles remain untouched.

- **00455/00558:** First-line metastatic MSI-H/dMMR colorectal scope, 200 mg every 21 days or 400 mg every 42 days. Thyroid tests remain every 3–6 weeks during therapy regardless of infusion interval. A possible stop after 24 months of sustained response is an individual review, not a fixed maximum. KEYNOTE-177 tested 200 mg every three weeks; 400 mg every six weeks uses exposure bridging, not a separate randomised colorectal comparison. Full immune anatomy and late-effect warnings are retained. The 00558 passport was corrected to six weeks; thyroid follow-up after treatment is individualised.
- **00551 v7a:** Colorectal cancer after prior fluoropyrimidine-based combination chemotherapy. Four 21-day combination cycles, then nivolumab 240 mg IV or 600 mg SC every 14 days, or 1200 mg SC every 28 days. The 480 mg IV four-week schedule from RCC/melanoma is not imported. First maintenance occurs three weeks after the last combination; there is no fixed two-year limit.
- **00900 v1a:** First-line colorectal treatment. Four 21-day combination cycles, then nivolumab 240 mg IV every 14 days or 480 mg IV every 28 days, first given three weeks after the last combination. Treatment stops for progression, unacceptable toxicity or at 24 months, whichever comes first. Both nivolumab profiles distinguish combination from maintenance and retain monitoring for at least five months after the last dose.
- **00890 v2a:** Capsules for 21 days followed by seven days off, with or without food. Blood pressure weekly for the first month, then monthly; regular blood, urine and thyroid testing without an invented fixed interval. Warfarin requires INR weekly until stable, then before each cycle. The source's exact 12-hour missed-dose gap is referred to the pharmacist/team; no replacement dose after vomiting. Detailed skin, bowel, bleeding, kidney, vascular, wound-healing, pregnancy and storage guidance comes from fruquintinib sources.

All profiles retain six to eight full chapters, seven or eight anatomical cards and baseline/during/after testing. Summaries distil this content; full references retain the detail. Cards follow rolling treatment and phase plans without a false fixed total cycle count. The approved renderer clearly marks placeholder contacts.

`author-profiles.py` records initial authoring and explicit corrections. Do not blindly rerun it after subsequent layout/content integration changes; current JSON is the release input. `check-profiles.py` checks source-ID integrity, context/schedule/maintenance distinctions and inherited-text leakage. These checks do not establish independent clinical approval or PDF/browser quality.
