ACTH units must not be confused with pmol/L. Sampling conditions and paired cortisol remain relevant. Adult published reference examples are not treatment clearance, diagnostic thresholds, pregnancy-specific ranges or steroid-recovery cutoffs.

Primary interpretation guidance checked:

- European Society of Endocrinology / Endocrine Society 2024 glucocorticoid-induced adrenal insufficiency guideline: https://www.endocrine.org/clinical-practice-guidelines/glucocorticoid-induced-adrenal-insufficiency
- ESE 2022 endocrine adverse conditions during immune checkpoint inhibition guideline: https://academic.oup.com/ejendo/article/187/6/G1/6979880
- ASCO 2021 immune-related adverse events guideline update: https://ascopubs.org/doi/10.1200/JCO.21.01440 (publisher search extract available; full publisher page access was restricted).

Implementation choices based on these sources: record collection time and steroid details; retain unknown/inconsistent exposure explicitly; explain possible ACTH/endogenous cortisol suppression and assay interference; do not equate a reference-range cortisol with exclusion of adrenal insufficiency or recovery after steroids; interpret free T4 with TSH. No steroid withdrawal, replacement-dose or autonomous endocrine diagnosis rule was introduced.

Checks cover values below, at and above interval boundaries; timed-window edges and invalid times; custom bounds, blank/invalid inputs; yes/no/unknown and inconsistent steroid histories; partial NCCP assessments; and interface/document export. The source links remain visible. Independent clinical/pharmacy review remains a separate step.
