Why this regimen?

Combines docetaxel with dual HER2 blockade for first-line advanced HER2-positive disease. Phesgo supplies the antibody component subcutaneously; chemotherapy and later antibody maintenance remain distinct phases.

Treatment setting
HER2-positive metastatic or locally recurrent unresectable breast cancer
Line / previous treatment
No prior anti-HER2 treatment or chemotherapy for metastatic disease under this NCCP indication
Selection factors and alternatives
  • Confirm metastatic treatment history and validated HER2 status.
  • Balance disease-control needs against docetaxel fitness, neuropathy, marrow and liver function, and cardiac risk.
  • Plan reassessment and the transition to antibody-only treatment when the chemotherapy component finishes.

Alternatives and trade-offs

  • NCCP 00797 is the weekly-paclitaxel alternative when docetaxel is unsuitable or not tolerated, subject to its specific indication and consent requirements.
  • IV antibody administration is an alternative route; Phesgo has not been shown to add survival beyond the same antibodies given IV.
  • DESTINY-Breast09 provides newer first-line evidence for trastuzumab deruxtecan plus pertuzumab versus taxane/trastuzumab/pertuzumab, with improved PFS and important interstitial lung disease risk. This is a separate treatment choice, not an automatic replacement during stable maintenance.

Alternatives require current NCCP, local access and individual MDT review; trial publication alone does not establish Irish availability or authorize a change to this regimen.

FeDeriCa supports subcutaneous administration through pharmacokinetic non-inferiority in early breast cancer; it does not establish an additional survival benefit from changing the administration route.

Toxicity context. Docetaxel adds marrow suppression, febrile neutropenia and neuropathy; dual HER2 treatment adds diarrhoea and cardiac monitoring needs. Reassess the toxicity profile after chemotherapy ends.

Endpoint key and interpretation
◷ OS · Overall survival
Time to death from any cause, from the study-defined starting point.
◫ PFS · Progression-free survival
Time to progression or death; preserve the study assessment criteria.
◇ EFS · Event-free survival
Time to a study-defined event. Check the exact events included; this is not interchangeable with DFS.
◇ DFS · Disease-free survival
Time to disease recurrence or other events specified by the study, including death where defined.
◇ iDFS · Invasive disease-free survival
Time to the invasive-disease events and deaths defined by the study; review the full composite definition.
◎ ORR · Objective response rate
Proportion meeting objective response criteria, generally complete plus partial responses. Not an estimate of cure.
⌕ pCR · Pathological complete response
Complete pathological response under the study definition. Specify the examined sites and residual-disease criteria; not proof of cure.
♧ QoL · Quality of life
Specify the instrument, domain, time point, score direction and clinically meaningful threshold where established.

Colour identifies the endpoint family. Effect size, statistical interpretation and maturity are separate. Median differences are not an individual's expected added lifespan. Do not rank regimens using results from separate trials.

Applicability and evidence limitations
  • Do not transfer these medians to the neoadjuvant setting or to weekly paclitaxel as an identical-backbone estimate.
  • Newer first-line evidence means historical CLEOPATRA results alone should not imply universal preference over all current alternatives.
