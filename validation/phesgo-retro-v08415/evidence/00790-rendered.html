Why this regimen?

Uses an anthracycline-free paclitaxel/carboplatin backbone with dual HER2 blockade before surgery. TRAIN-2 supports the anthracycline-omission strategy, while the NCCP six-cycle schedule is not identical to the original trial schedule.

Treatment setting
Neoadjuvant locally advanced, inflammatory or high-risk early HER2-positive breast cancer
Line / previous treatment
Preoperative treatment; NCCP 00790 specifies six 21-day cycles, then surgery and pathology-directed adjuvant review
Selection factors and alternatives
  • Confirm disease risk, cardiac function, marrow reserve, renal assessment and neuropathy risk.
  • Day 1 and day 8 chemotherapy visits differ from three-weekly docetaxel TCHP.
  • Explain that clinical response and surgical pathology guide the subsequent systemic, radiation and endocrine phases.

Alternatives and trade-offs

  • Choose the taxane/platinum backbone using disease risk, comorbidity, neuropathy, marrow reserve and treatment burden. TRYPHAENA and TRAIN-2 are different studies; cross-trial response percentages cannot rank their regimens.
  • DESTINY-Breast11 reports newer neoadjuvant pCR evidence for trastuzumab deruxtecan followed by THP versus ddAC followed by THP. It did not directly compare against this NCCP regimen; survival results were immature in that report.
  • Reassess after surgery. Residual invasive disease can change the postoperative strategy: KATHERINE supports T-DM1 over trastuzumab, while DESTINY-Breast05 provides newer T-DXd versus T-DM1 evidence in a selected high-risk residual-disease population, with additional lung toxicity considerations. These decisions are separate from this neoadjuvant card.

Alternatives require current NCCP, local access and individual MDT review; trial publication alone does not establish Irish availability or authorize a change to this regimen.

Evidence at a glance

TRAIN-2 phase III — original IV-antibody strategy; FeDeriCa route bridge · 438 patients with stage II–III HER2-positive breast cancer

Nine paclitaxel/carboplatin cycles without anthracyclines versus three FEC then six paclitaxel/carboplatin cycles; both used IV trastuzumab/pertuzumab.

Important schedule difference: NCCP 00790 prescribes six cycles; the trial non-anthracycline arm used nine. These outcomes support the strategy but are not direct efficacy estimates for six-cycle Phesgo/00790.

FeDeriCa supports subcutaneous administration through pharmacokinetic non-inferiority in early breast cancer; it does not establish an additional survival benefit from changing the administration route.

Toxicity context. Anthracycline-containing treatment produced more febrile neutropenia and cardiac toxicity in TRAIN-2. The anthracycline-free arm still had substantial marrow suppression, diarrhoea and neuropathy.

Endpoint key and interpretation
◷ OS · Overall survival
Time to death from any cause, from the study-defined starting point.
◫ PFS · Progression-free survival
Time to progression or death; preserve the study assessment criteria.
◇ EFS · Event-free survival
Time to a study-defined event. Check the exact events included; this is not interchangeable with DFS.
◇ DFS · Disease-free survival
Time to disease recurrence or other events specified by the study, including death where defined.
◇ iDFS · Invasive disease-free survival
Time to the invasive-disease events and deaths defined by the study; review the full composite definition.
◎ ORR · Objective response rate
Proportion meeting objective response criteria, generally complete plus partial responses. Not an estimate of cure.
⌕ pCR · Pathological complete response
Complete pathological response under the study definition. Specify the examined sites and residual-disease criteria; not proof of cure.
♧ QoL · Quality of life
Specify the instrument, domain, time point, score direction and clinically meaningful threshold where established.

Colour identifies the endpoint family. Effect size, statistical interpretation and maturity are separate. Median differences are not an individual's expected added lifespan. Do not rank regimens using results from separate trials.

Applicability and evidence limitations
  • The six-versus-nine-cycle discrepancy is retained visibly above the outcome cards; no numerical benefit is claimed for shortening treatment.
  • pCR is a pathological response endpoint, not proof of cure. Survival analyses were descriptive and underpowered.
  • The original trial predated routine postoperative T-DM1 for residual disease. KATHERINE, DESTINY-Breast05 and newer neoadjuvant DESTINY-Breast11 evidence require separate eligibility and MDT review.
