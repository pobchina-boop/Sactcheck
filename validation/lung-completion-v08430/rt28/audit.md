## Findings carried into the proposals

1. **Different chemotherapy duration.** Both give platinum on Days 1/8 and etoposide on Days 1–5 every28 days.00456 has two initial cycles and two optional additional cycles after review without progression.00561 has two cycles total. The 00456 wallet uses a rolling record to avoid labelling four cycles as mandatory;00561 has two cycle stamps. Neither radiation fraction counts nor individual dates are invented. Radiation usually starts within24 hours of the first chemotherapy dose, but the radiation calendar remains a separate specialist prescription.
2. **Different blood-test requirements are explicit.**00456 requests FBC on Days 1/8;00561 requests weekly FBC before treatment. Current etoposide product information additionally requests blood counts before every etoposide dose, including Days 2–5. Both patient and clinical timelines show the combined requirement; the assessment detects an unavailable/unreviewed pre-dose count. Baseline kidney/liver/FBC and per-cycle kidney/liver tests are distinguished from conditional hearing/clearance tests and individual scans. No universal tumour-marker or post-treatment imaging interval is invented.
3. **ECOG 2 was incorrectly accepted by the legacy generic eligibility rule.** Both sources specify ECOG 0–1. The new rules flag ECOG 2 as well as higher scores. Indication, radiotherapy readiness and cisplatin-unsuitability for 00561 are separate checks.
4. **The blood-count table applies to etoposide.** The source75% row is not applied to platinum. ANC<1 or platelets<75 delays etoposide. ANC 1–1.49 or platelets75–99 enters source/SmPC reconciliation. The source has unassigned exact/decimal boundaries above 1.49 through 1.5 and above 99 through 100 because the normal row uses strict >1.5 and >100. These become review, not implicit full doses or rounded inputs.
5. **The etoposide blood-count conflict is visible.** The current SmPC generally advises against dosing with ANC<1.5 or platelets<100 unless caused by malignant disease, unlike the NCCP75% row. The app asks for specialist reconciliation, including cause and actual treatment day, instead of treating75% as permission to administer. Severe prior marrow events, infection and grade 3–4 toxicity prompt subsequent-dose review.
6. **00456 uses its own updated renal/hepatic table.** Cisplatin50–59 mL/min:75% of original;40–49:50%;<40 not recommended. Decimal gaps49–50 and59–60 remain review. Etoposide10–50:75% of original, with an explicit product-information conflict below 15;<10 has no routine non-dialysis dose in this source. Bilirubin≥50 micromol/L or low albumin leads to the discretionary50% etoposide pathway. The normal hepatic row requires normal albumin and renal function as well as bilirubin<50. The old generic45–59/<45 and bilirubin-ULN rules are retired.
7. **Dialysis is separate.** For 00456, selected haemodialysis excludes the ordinary numeric renal rows and displays the discretionary source considerations for both medicines, with SmPC reconciliation.00561 has no routine dialysis schedule to invent. No dose is calculated automatically from these considerations.
8. **00561 retains its distinct older etoposide table.** CrCl 15–50:75%;<15:50% in NCCP with specialist product-information review. Bilirubin26–51 micromol/L or AST60–180U/L:50%; higher values require a clinical decision. A higher result cannot be masked by the other result entering the50% row. Low albumin prompts review, without importing 00456's albumin-based percentage.
9. **Carboplatin safeguards are explicit.** GFR≤20:do not administer;>20–30:extreme caution;CrCl<60:increased marrow risk;GFR used for Calvert capped125. Estimated renal function requires a creatinine sample within48 hours of administration. A creatinine increase above 110% of the isotope-GFR baseline prompts reassessment. This tool does not issue a patient-specific carboplatin mg dose.
10. **Neuropathy and non-haematological rules are not shared blindly.**00456 pre-existing neuropathy≥2, significant hearing/tinnitus and pre-existing renal impairment are cisplatin exclusions. Its current neuropathy pathway offers a consultant choice of carboplatinAUC5 substitution or cisplatin50% after recovery; that does not automatically select00561AUC3.00456's generic table says grade 3, so grade 4 is explicitly escalated for review rather than silently extending the table.00561 says grade≥3. Mucositis and alopecia are excluded from those generic reductions; swallowing/mucositis has its own assessment and support review.
11. **Daily supportive-care risk differs.**00456 Days 1/8 are high-risk cisplatin days.00561 Days 1/8 are moderate-risk carboplatinAUC3 days, not the AUC≥4 high-risk row. Days 2–5 are etoposide-only low-risk days. Multiday/delayed antiemetics need one reconciled prescription, avoiding overlapping drugs or steroid courses. The five-day-cisplatin aprepitant extension is not copied into a regimen whose cisplatin is only on Days 1/8. Day 8 cisplatin needs its own prehydration; there is no Day 8 etoposide infusion to supply it.
12. **Evidence applicability is stated.**00456 cites [SWOG9019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12177106/), a small historical single-arm study; its outcomes are not an incremental benefit estimate.00561 cites [Albain2009/Intergroup0139](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19632716/), which used cisplatin, not carboplatinAUC3; no direct survival estimate is attributed to 00561. Patient numerical trial metrics remain empty. Later treatment, surgery and immunotherapy are separate decisions.
13. **Consent and patient content cover both modalities.** The pack includes chest-radiation swallowing, skin and respiratory effects, including symptoms after treatment; medicine-specific infection, kidney/hearing/nerve, infusion and fertility risks; and the off-label NSCLC use of etoposide. The additional generic radiotherapy consent profile makes site-specific radiation consent a separate specialist process rather than assuming one universal radiation risk list.

## Dose-display safety check

A combined-case test found that a numeric renal percentage could outrank a concurrent consultant-review action in the existing modification display: etoposide75% could be prioritised at CrCl 12 despite the SmPC conflict, or with raised bilirubin despite the discretionary hepatic pathway. These proposals explicitly block automatic numeric calculation for percentage pathways while preserving the exact source percentages in the explanations. Source-dependent choices therefore stay reviewable. Hard prohibitions remain prohibitions. This is recorded separately from any broader root-agent repair of the shared display engine.
