## Page 1 source: purpose and treatment

The two medicines damage tumour genetic material through different mechanisms to help control sarcoma. The intended role, chance of benefit and how this fits with surgery, radiotherapy or trials depend on the diagnosis and previous treatment.

Etoposide then ifosfamide on days 1–5; repeat every 21 days, up to 7 cycles

**Treatment** Etoposide 100 mg/m² is infused over 60 minutes, followed by ifosfamide 1,800 mg/m² over 60 minutes, on each of days 1–5. Courses repeat every 21 days for up to seven cycles while benefit and tolerability justify treatment. Mesna is given before each ifosfamide infusion and at 4 and 8 hours measured from its START.

Relapse is explicit for Ewing sarcoma; the national osteosarcoma indication does not add a relapse qualifier. The source gives no numeric age cutoff. Etoposide use for this indication is unlicensed in Ireland and requires the documented local discussion and governance process.

## Page 2 source: body map and risks

**Brain and alertness** New confusion, unusual sleepiness, hallucinations, unsteadiness or seizures needs immediate assessment.

**Mouth** Report ulcers, painful swallowing or difficulty eating and drinking early.

**Heart** New breathlessness, chest pain, palpitations, faintness or swelling needs urgent assessment.

**Kidneys** Report reduced urine or inability to drink. Kidney damage and electrolyte losses may need extra tests.

**Bladder** Report blood or pain when passing urine immediately. Mesna reduces bladder injury but does not remove all risk.

**Blood cells and infection** Fever, shaking chills, sudden illness or unusual bleeding requires immediate oncology advice.

**Bowel and appetite** Use prescribed anti-sickness support; report persistent vomiting, severe diarrhoea or difficulty drinking.

**Skin, hair and infusion line** Hair and skin changes may occur. Report line-site pain, burning, swelling or a severe rash immediately.

Discuss low blood counts with serious infection or sepsis, anaemia and bleeding; nausea or vomiting; mouth soreness, appetite or taste changes, diarrhoea or constipation; fatigue, hair, nail and skin changes; changes in sexual wellbeing; allergic reactions and intravenous-line complications; blood clots, including clots in the legs or lungs; fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding effects; and the possibility of lasting organ injury, rare later cancers or death from serious treatment complications. The likelihood and severity differ between medicines and people. Read this general discussion together with every medicine-specific risk.

Read the complete medicine-specific risk sections in the handbook and approved local consent discussion.

## Page 3 source: checks, support and urgent help

**Full blood count, kidney and liver tests** Before starting and each 21-day cycle; ifosfamide product information additionally specifies counts before each administration and afterwards as appropriate.

**Sodium, potassium and phosphate** At baseline and regular treatment reviews; repeat during treatment or recovery when results, fluid balance or symptoms require.

**Neurological assessment** Before each ifosfamide dose; report confusion, unusual sleepiness, hallucinations, unsteadiness or seizures immediately.

**Urine checks for blood** Before each ifosfamide dose and every 8 hours on treatment days. Report blood or urinary pain immediately.

**Blood pressure and etoposide reactions** Observe during infusion, especially its first 15 minutes; report dizziness, rash, breathlessness or faintness immediately.

**Fluid balance and daily weight** During treatment; prescribed hydration continues for 24 hours after the final ifosfamide infusion ends.

Mesna timings start from ifosfamide START. Report confusion or blood in urine immediately.

Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999. Any new confusion or unusual sleepiness, blood in urine, low urine output or infusion-related faintness needs immediate assessment.

Up to seven 21-day cycles, stopping earlier for progression or unacceptable toxicity. Recording spaces do not define duration.

Keep verified daytime and out-of-hours oncology contact numbers. The information website is not an emergency contact service.

The national source states that this use of etoposide is an unlicensed indication in Ireland. Your clinician must explain the reason, evidence, uncertainties and alternatives, obtain consent under hospital policy, and confirm acknowledgement by the local Drugs and Therapeutics Committee or equivalent.
