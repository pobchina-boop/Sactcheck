## Page 1 source: purpose and treatment

High-dose ifosfamide aims to control previously treated sarcoma and can sometimes make further local treatment possible. The specialist team weighs potential tumour shrinkage against substantial bone-marrow, brain, kidney and bladder risks.

Five consecutive 24-hour ifosfamide infusions; every 21 days, maximum 4 cycles

**Treatment** Ifosfamide 3,000 mg/m² is infused over 24 hours on each of days 1–5, giving five consecutive 24-hour infusions. Repeat every 21 days for a maximum of four cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity stops treatment sooner. Mesna starts before the first infusion, continues throughout the five-day treatment and includes a final dose on day 6 after the last ifosfamide infusion.

The maximum is four cycles. The detailed national eligibility paragraph describes Ewing sarcoma; its timing definitions must not be copied automatically into the separately listed relapsed osteosarcoma indication. No numeric age or performance-status cutoff is invented.

## Page 2 source: body map and risks

**Brain and alertness** New confusion, unusual sleepiness, hallucinations, unsteadiness or seizures needs immediate assessment.

**Mouth** Report ulcers, painful swallowing or difficulty eating and drinking early.

**Heart** New breathlessness, chest pain, palpitations, faintness or swelling needs urgent assessment.

**Kidneys** Report reduced urine or inability to drink. Kidney damage and electrolyte losses may need extra tests.

**Bladder** Report blood or pain when passing urine immediately. Mesna reduces bladder injury but does not remove all risk.

**Blood cells and infection** Fever, shaking chills, sudden illness or unusual bleeding requires immediate oncology advice.

**Bowel and appetite** Use prescribed anti-sickness support; report persistent vomiting, severe diarrhoea or difficulty drinking.

**Skin, hair and infusion line** Hair and skin changes may occur. Report line-site pain, burning, swelling or a severe rash immediately.

Discuss low blood counts with serious infection or sepsis, anaemia and bleeding; nausea or vomiting; mouth soreness, appetite or taste changes, diarrhoea or constipation; fatigue, hair, nail and skin changes; changes in sexual wellbeing; allergic reactions and intravenous-line complications; blood clots, including clots in the legs or lungs; fertility, pregnancy and breastfeeding effects; and the possibility of lasting organ injury, rare later cancers or death from serious treatment complications. The likelihood and severity differ between medicines and people. Read this general discussion together with every medicine-specific risk.

Read the complete medicine-specific risk sections in the handbook and approved local consent discussion.

## Page 3 source: checks, support and urgent help

**Full blood count, kidney and liver tests** Before starting and each 21-day cycle; ifosfamide product information additionally specifies counts before each administration and afterwards as appropriate. Kidney monitoring continues during and after treatment.

**Neurological assessment** Before each ifosfamide dose; any new confusion, sleepiness, hallucinations, unsteadiness or seizures needs immediate assessment.

**Urine checks for blood** Before each ifosfamide dose and every 8 hours on treatment days; promptly report blood or urinary pain.

**Fluid balance and daily weight** During treatment; prescribed hydration continues for 24 hours after the last ifosfamide infusion ends.

**Tumour, symptoms and function** At specialist-set review points before continuation; scans follow the individual Ewing sarcoma or osteosarcoma plan.

Five 24-hour infusions, then final mesna and continued hydration. Report confusion immediately.

Call oncology immediately for fever, shaking chills, suddenly feeling unwell, unusual bleeding, new breathlessness, very little urine, severe rash or vomiting that prevents drinking. Follow your hospital’s temperature and contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain, collapse or seizures: call 112/999. Any change in alertness, confusion, hallucinations, seizures, blood in urine or reduced urine requires immediate assessment.

Maximum four 21-day cycles, stopping earlier for progression or unacceptable toxicity. Recording spaces do not define duration.

Keep verified daytime and out-of-hours oncology contact numbers. The information website is not an emergency contact service.
