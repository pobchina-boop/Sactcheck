L26@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their

L29@P0: progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

L222@P4: Ifosfamide
L223@P4:  Ifosfamide-induced encephalopathy: This may occur in patients treated with high doses of ifosfamide.
L224@P4: Neurological function should be assessed prior to each ifosfamide dose.
L225@P4:  Renal and urothelial toxicity: Ifosfamide is both nephrotoxic and urotoxic. Glomerular and tubular
L226@P4: kidney function must be evaluated and checked before commencement of therapy, as well as during
L227@P4: and after treatment. Urinary sediment should be checked regularly for the presence of erythrocytes and
L228@P4: other signs of uro/nephrotoxicity. During or immediately after administration, adequate amounts of
L229@P4: fluid should be ingested or infused to force diuresis in order to reduce the risk of urinary tract toxicity.
L230@P4: For prophylaxis of hemorrhagic cystitis, ifosfamide should be used in combination with mesna.
L231@P4: Ifosfamide should be used with caution, if at all, in patients with active urinary tract infections
L232@P4: Etoposide
L233@P4:  Hypersensitivity: Hypersensitivity reactions have been reported with etoposide. Monitor infusion of
L234@P4: etoposide for the first 15 minutes for signs of hypotension.
L235@P4: DRUG INTERACTIONS:
L236@P4:  Current drug interaction databases should be consulted for more information.
L237@P4:  Increased nephrotoxicity may result from a combined effect of ifosfamide and other nephrotoxic drugs
L238@P4: e.g. aminoglycosides, platinum compounds Increased risk of ifosfamide-induced neurotoxicity due to
L239@P4: inhibition of CYP3A4 by aprepitant.
L240@P4:  Avoid combination of CYP3A4 inducers and ifosfamide. There is the possibility of increased toxicity of
L241@P4: ifosfamide due to increased conversion to active and toxic metabolites.
L242@P4:  Reduced efficacy of ifosfamide possible with CYP3A4 inhibitors due to decreased conversion to active
L243@P4: metabolites.
L244@P4:  CYP3A4 enzyme inducers may increase the clearance of etoposide.
L245@P4:  CYP3A4 enzyme inhibitors may decrease the clearance of etoposide.
L246@P4:  P-gp inhibitors may decrease the clearance of etoposide.
L247@P4:  Current drug interaction databases should be consulted for more information.
