L30@P0: TREATMENT:
L31@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L32@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L33@P0: The recommended dose of mifamurtide for all patients is 2 mg/m2 body surface area.
L34@P0: It should be administered as adjuvant therapy, in combination with post-operative multi-agent
L35@P0: chemotherapy, following resection and recovery from surgery (usually +/- 3 weeks post operatively): twice
L36@P0: weekly at least 3 days apart for 12 weeks, followed by once-weekly treatments for an additional 24 weeks
L37@P0: for a total of 48 infusions in 36 weeks.
L38@P0: Treatment should continue to completion or until unacceptable toxicity occurs.
L39@P0: Please Refer to NCCP regimen 00463 (MAP) Methotrexate (12000mg/m2) DOXOrubicin (37.5mg/m2/day) and
L40@P0: CISplatin (60mg/m2) Therapy which is usually used in conjunction with Mifamurtide Therapy.
L41@P0: Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Weeks
L42@P0: 1 and 4 Mifamurtide 2mg/m2IV infusion 50mL* NaCl 0.9% over 60
L43@P0: minutes
L44@P0: 1-12 inclusive
L45@P0: 1 13-36 inclusive
L46@P0: *The final volume will be greater than 50 ml as the required dose is added to 50mL 0.9% NaCl giving a total volume between
L47@P0: 50mL - 100mL.
L48@P0: Mifamurtide must be reconstituted, filtered using the filter provided in packaging and further diluted using aseptic
L49@P0: technique, prior to administration.
L50@P0: Based on the liposomal nature of the product, use of an infusion set with an in-line filter during administration is not
L51@P0: recommended.

L65@P1: ELIGIBILITY:
L66@P1: • Indications as above
L67@P1: • Patients aged from 2 to 30 years of age.
L68@P1: • Adequate haematological, hepatic and renal function.
L69@P1: EXCLUSIONS:
L70@P1: • Hypersensitivity to mifamurtide or any of the excipients.
L71@P1: • Metastatic disease at presentation.
L72@P1: • Incomplete surgical resection.
L73@P1: • Prior chemotherapy or radiotherapy other than pre-operative multiagent chemotherapy.

L135@P2: ADVERSE EFFECTS / REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS
L136@P2: The adverse effects listed are not exhaustive. Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics for full
L137@P2: details.
L138@P2: • Respiratory distress: In patients with a history of asthma or other chronic obstructive pulmonary
L139@P2: disease, consideration should be given to administration of bronchodilators on a prophylactic
L140@P2: basis. If a severe respiratory reaction occurs, administration of mifamurtide should be
L141@P2: discontinued and appropriate treatment initiated.
L142@P2: • Neutropenia: Episodes of neutropenic fever should be monitored and managed appropriately.
L143@P2: Mifamurtide may be given during periods of neutropenia, but subsequent fever attributed to the
L144@P2: treatment should be monitored closely. Fever or chills persisting for more than 8 hours after
L145@P2: mifamurtide administration should be evaluated for sepsis.
L146@P2: • Inflammatory response: Mifamurtide should be used with caution in patients with a history of
L147@P2: autoimmune, inflammatory or other collagen diseases. During administration, patients should be
L148@P2: monitored for unusual signs or symptoms, such as arthritis or synovitis, suggestive of
L149@P2: uncontrolled inflammatory reactions.
L150@P2: • Cardiovascular disorders: Patients with a history of venous thrombosis, vasculitis or unstable
L151@P2: cardiovascular disorders should be closely monitored during administration. If symptoms are
L152@P2-3: persistent and worsening, administration should be delayed or discontinued.NCCP National SACT Regimen

L165@P3: • Gastrointestinal toxicity: Nausea, vomiting and loss of appetite are very common adverse
L166@P3: reactions to mifamurtide. Gastrointestinal toxicity may be exacerbated when mifamurtide is
L167@P3: used in combination with high dose, multi-agent chemotherapy and was associated with an
L168@P3: increased use of parenteral nutrition.
L169@P3: DRUG INTERACTIONS:
L170@P3: • Current drug interaction databases should be consulted for more information.
L171@P3: • Mifamurtide is contraindicated with concurrent use of:
L172@P3: o Cyclosporin or other calcineurin inhibitors.
L173@P3: o High-dose non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs, cyclooxygenase inhibitors).
L174@P3: • The administration of mifamurtide should be separate from the administration times of
L175@P3: doxorubicin or other lipophilic medicinal products if used in the same chemotherapy regimen.
L176@P3: • Because mifamurtide acts through stimulation of the immune system, the chronic or routine use
L177@P3: of corticosteroids should be avoided during treatment with mifamurtide.
