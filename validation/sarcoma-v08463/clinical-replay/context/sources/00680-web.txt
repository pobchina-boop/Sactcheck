L24@P0: TREATMENT:
L25@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L26@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L27@P0: Ifosfamide is administered on days 1-5 of a 21 day cycle up to a maximum of 4 cycles unless the patient
L28@P0: experiences disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.
L29@P0: Mesna is administered 1 hour prior to the first dose of ifosfamide on day 1 and is continued throughout the
L30@P0: chemotherapy, a final dose is given following the last ifosfamide dose as detailed in the treatment table
L31@P0: below.
L32@P0: Note:
L33@P0:  Hydration therapy required for safe administration of ifosfamide (See Table below)

L181@P3: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L182@P3:  Ifosfamide-induced encephalopathy: This may occur in patients treated with high doses of ifosfamide.
L183@P3: Neurological function should be assessed prior to each ifosfamide dose.
L184@P3:  Renal and urothelial toxicity: Ifosfamide is both nephrotoxic and urotoxic. Glomerular and tubular
L185@P3: kidney function must be evaluated and checked before commencement of therapy, as well as during
L186@P3: and after treatment. Urinary sediment should be checked regularly for the presence of erythrocytes and
L187@P3: other signs of uro/nephrotoxicity. During or immediately after administration, adequate amounts of
L188@P3-4: fluid should be ingested or infused to force diuresis in order to reduce the risk of urinary tract toxicity. NCCP National SACT Regimen
