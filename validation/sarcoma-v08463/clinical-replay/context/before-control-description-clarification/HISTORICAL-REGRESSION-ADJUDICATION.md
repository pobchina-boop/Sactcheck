## Primary-source basis and limitations

- Current linked HPRA **Mitoxana1g**, PA2299/028/001, SmPC11September2023: §4.3 has product-specific absolute creatinine/bilirubin and transaminase/ALP restrictions; §4.4 requires interruption of administration for current CNS toxicity. Exact captured live product-page and linked product-information artifacts are pinned in `historical-adjudication-sources.json`. The restriction belongs to that named product. It is not a universal replacement for NCCP or a permanent rechallenge ban for every neurological event.
- NCCP00596v2 p3 renal table says ifosfamide CrCl<50/haemodialysis requires a clinical decision. Etoposide has separate renal bands. Its adverse-event section distinguishes grade3–4 neurological history, recovery, infusion/methylene-blue discussion and recurrent-event substitution. Source p4–5 supportive/complication instructions and the existing accepted product reconciliation remain applicable.
- NCCP00680v2 p3 likewise uses clinical decision below CrCl50; p4 Table3 explicitly says discontinue ifosfamide for neurotoxicity grade≥3. This source difference is preserved.
- Missing identity/laboratory/unit checks are **conservative reconciliation safeguards approved for this software transition**, not a claim that NCCP itself mandates a universal named-product checkbox. CrCl cannot replace serum creatinine, a selected renal band cannot establish product eligibility, and a generic restart approval cannot resolve the missing source information.

The independent product/CNS source transition was already separately reviewed. The present ledger validates its precise effect on older expectations rather than retrospectively changing those expectations. It does not imply independent clinician or pharmacy approval.
