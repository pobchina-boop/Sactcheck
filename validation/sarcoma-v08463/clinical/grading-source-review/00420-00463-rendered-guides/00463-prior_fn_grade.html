NCCP 00463 v4 Table 4 prior febrile-neutropenia bands grading — Grade documented for the previous febrile-neutropenia episode

How to assess: Review the previous episode record, including contemporaneous neutrophils, temperature, clinical severity and the grading system used. Table 4 distinguishes grade 4 from consideration for grade 3, and later recurrence despite G-CSF. Current febrile neutropenia or suspected sepsis belongs in the separate urgent current-event field; current normal counts do not erase prior history. The source addresses prior grades 3 and 4 only. Leave an unclassified episode unresolved and obtain clinical-record review; do not map unsupported or unknown historical severity to no previous episode.

Version note: The NCCP table names grade 3 and grade 4 action bands but does not define their criteria or identify a CTCAE edition. Do not equate the episode grade with an ANC-only neutropenia grade or apply v5/v6 haematology bands automatically. Values 1 and 2 are not defined by this source table. The no-previous-episode choice is an absence value, not a formal NCCP grade 0.

  1. Grade 0No previous febrile-neutropenia episode recorded; this is not a severity grade for an active episode.
  2. Grade 3Previous episode documented as grade 3; confirm its recorded criteria and source grading system.
  3. Grade 4Previous episode documented as grade 4; confirm its recorded criteria and source grading system.
