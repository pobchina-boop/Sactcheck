NCCP 00463 v4 Table 6 hearing criterion grading — Current hearing impairment grade from audiology

How to assess: Use the current audiology report to record a documented grade and the lowest affected frequency separately. Table 6 links a grade of at least 2 with hearing loss reaching 2 kHz or lower. This is a combined treatment-action criterion, not a grading scale. Preserve any previous cisplatin discontinuation even if the current assessment improves.

Version note: The NCCP table does not define its hearing grades or name a grading edition. Do not treat generic CTCAE severity anchors or the 2 kHz boundary as a hearing-grade definition. Audiology must identify the applicable grading system and patient context.
