CTCAE v5.0 grading — Current peripheral neuropathy grade

How to assess: Document current sensory and motor findings, gait, dexterity and effects on daily activities. Name the exact peripheral event; paraesthesia alone and central neurological toxicity are not interchangeable with peripheral neuropathy. Preserve any previous cisplatin omission separately. NCCP 00420 v4, page 3 Table 3 does not identify a CTCAE edition.
