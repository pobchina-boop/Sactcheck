CTCAE v5.0 grading — Worst previous cisplatin-related peripheral neuropathy grade

How to assess: Record the worst documented previous peripheral event, exact grading system and functional effects. Do not replace this history with the current recovered grade. Distinguish sensory or motor neuropathy from isolated paraesthesia and central events. NCCP 00463 v4, page 8 Table 6 does not identify a CTCAE edition. This panel is CTCAE v5.0 educational orientation, not proof that the source bands use v5.0; confirm the documented grading basis before applying the regimen rule.

Version note: This field is explicitly tied to CTCAE v5.0 metadata in the encoded protocol. The current NCI release is v6.0, but SACTCheck will not silently reinterpret a source rule against a different CTCAE version.

  1. Grade 0No peripheral sensory or motor neuropathy.
  2. Grade 1Asymptomatic; clinical or diagnostic observations only. CTCAE v5.0 grades symptomatic paraesthesia separately when that is the more accurate term.
  3. Grade 2Moderate symptoms that limit instrumental activities of daily living.
  4. Grade 3Severe symptoms that limit self-care activities of daily living.
