CTCAE v5.0 grading — Current diarrhoea after methotrexate: grade

How to assess: Assess stools above the patient's baseline, ostomy output, continence, hydration and functional impact. Keep diarrhoea distinct from mucositis. Record event duration and the separately confirmed source Day 29 context; calendar week 29 alone does not establish it. NCCP 00463 v4, page 8 Table 6 does not identify a CTCAE edition. This panel is CTCAE v5.0 educational orientation, not proof that the source bands use v5.0; confirm the documented grading basis before applying the regimen rule.

Version note: This field is explicitly tied to CTCAE v5.0 metadata in the encoded protocol. The current NCI release is v6.0, but SACTCheck will not silently reinterpret a source rule against a different CTCAE version.

  1. Grade 0No increase above the patient's usual stool frequency or ostomy output.
  2. Grade 1Increase of fewer than 4 stools per day above baseline, or mild increase in ostomy output.
  3. Grade 2Increase of 4–6 stools per day above baseline, or moderate increase in ostomy output; limits instrumental activities of daily living.
  4. Grade 3Increase of 7 or more stools per day above baseline, incontinence, hospital care indicated, or severe ostomy-output increase; limits self-care activities of daily living.
  5. Grade 4Life-threatening consequences; urgent intervention required.
