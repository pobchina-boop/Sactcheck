1. Treatment and purpose

High-dose ifosfamide for recurrent Ewing sarcoma or relapsed osteosarcoma · NCCP 00680 v2

Ifosfamide 3,000 mg/m² is infused over 24 hours on each of days 1–5, giving five consecutive 24-hour infusions. Repeat every 21 days for a maximum of four cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity stops treatment sooner. Mesna starts before the first infusion, continues throughout the five-day treatment and includes a final dose on day 6 after the last ifosfamide infusion.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Ifosfamide · Chemotherapy

New confusion, unusual sleepiness, hallucinations, unsteadiness, seizures, blood in urine or reduced urine needs immediate assessment.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the individual aim, realistic benefit and uncertainty, treatment burden, alternatives, serious and lasting risks, fertility, personal priorities and the option to decline or reconsider. The maximum is four cycles. The detailed national eligibility paragraph describes Ewing sarcoma; its timing definitions must not be copied automatically into the separately listed relapsed osteosarcoma indication. No numeric age or performance-status cutoff is invented.
