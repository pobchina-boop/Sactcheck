1. Treatment and purpose

Mifamurtide after osteosarcoma surgery · NCCP 00100 v6

At least three days between the two weekly infusions: 24 infusions in this phase. Another 24 infusions, for 48 total over 36 weeks. The clock is independent of chemotherapy.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Mifamurtide · Innate immune activator

Report fever or sudden illness immediately, even when fever has happened after an earlier infusion. New wheeze, breathing difficulty, chest pain or faintness needs urgent assessment.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss the individual aim, realistic benefit and uncertainty, treatment burden, alternatives, serious and lasting risks, fertility, personal priorities and the option to decline or reconsider. Only the completely resected, nonmetastatic postoperative context is covered. European product information describes evidence in patients aged 2–30 at initial diagnosis; the team reconciles this with the national age wording. Mifamurtide is an innate immune activator, not a checkpoint inhibitor, and should not automatically receive checkpoint-specific consent or steroid pathways.
