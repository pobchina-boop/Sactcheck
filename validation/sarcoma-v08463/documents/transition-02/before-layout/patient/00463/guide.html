Methotrexate, doxorubicin and cisplatin for osteosarcoma

The three medicines act through different mechanisms against osteosarcoma throughout the body. Surgery removes the visible tumour, and chemotherapy before and after surgery aims to reduce recurrence risk.

High-grade osteosarcoma of an extremity or axial skeleton, excluding craniofacial sites, with all disease sites amenable to complete surgical resection; national eligibility age 40 years or younger · NCCP 00463 v4 · Document revised 18 November 2026

Your personal plan: this is the standard regimen schedule. Your team confirms your prescribed doses, treatment dates, reviews and changes in your written plan.

This course is limited to the stated age, site and complete-resection context. Up to two additional preoperative methotrexate doses may be prescribed if surgery is delayed. These are not automatic. Poor response on surgical pathology does not automatically justify adding ifosfamide/etoposide.

Between treatments

  • Side effects can occur on any day. Use the verified oncology contact plan promptly if unwell.
  • Cisplatin has high nausea/vomiting risk; doxorubicin and high-dose methotrexate have moderate risk. Each visit needs its own reconciled anti-sickness prescription. Methotrexate hydration, alkalinisation, level monitoring and folinic acid rescue must continue until the specialist team confirms safe clearance. Delayed clearance may need intensified rescue or glucarpidase. No fixed discharge day is promised. Low blood counts or a delay to the next chemotherapy dose must not automatically withhold or stop folinic acid rescue for methotrexate already given.

Bring the medicine list and treatment/contact record. Report new symptoms before treatment.

When to get help

Call 112 / 999 for severe breathing difficulty, severe chest pain, collapse or a seizure.

Call oncology immediately: temperature 37.5°C or above, 36°C or below, shivering or suddenly feeling unwell, even with a normal temperature. Follow your hospital’s temperature instructions. If you cannot reach the team, seek urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for the next appointment.

The team dates and initials each completed treatment visit. Record actual dates, omitted doses and changes to the agreed course. Delays or a changed plan need a note or a replacement card. A recorded treatment visit does not approve the next treatment. Keep any medicine-specific alert card too.
