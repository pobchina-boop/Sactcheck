Treatment context
The aim is to control advanced sarcoma and help symptoms. The strongest trial evidence concerns previously treated liposarcoma and leiomyosarcoma; the team explains expected benefit for your particular subtype.
Schedule
Trabectedin 1.5 mg/m² is infused over 24 hours starting on day 1 of each 21-day cycle. Treatment continues while benefit and tolerability justify it. The actual dose, start time and any delay require a new safety review.

General treatment discussion

Possible short-term, long-term, unexpected and unlisted effects; benefits are not guaranteed.
Severe complications can require hospital admission and can rarely be fatal.
Infection / sepsis, allergic or infusion reactions and leakage outside a vein (extravasation) should be discussed where applicable to the medicines and route.
Pregnancy, contraception, breastfeeding, sexual health and fertility preservation should be discussed where relevant.
Treatment can be delayed, changed or stopped if risks outweigh benefit. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn.

2. Regimen / agent-specific material risks

Trabectedin

Chemotherapy
Low blood counts, serious infection or sepsis, anaemia and bleeding
Nausea, vomiting, appetite loss, constipation, mouth soreness and fatigue
Liver injury, sometimes severe; prescribed steroid premedication and blood checks are essential
Muscle breakdown (rhabdomyolysis), which can cause kidney failure; muscle-enzyme monitoring is required
Heart muscle injury or heart failure; leakage of fluid from small blood vessels can cause swelling and low blood pressure
Allergic reactions and severe tissue damage if treatment leaks outside the vein; fertility and pregnancy harm

Discuss realistic benefit, uncertainty, alternatives including observation or symptom-focused care when appropriate, serious and lasting risks, personal priorities and the option to decline or reconsider treatment. This is later-line single-agent trabectedin over 24 hours. It is not first-line doxorubicin plus trabectedin with maintenance, and it is not the shorter ovarian-cancer combination infusion. Severe kidney impairment, heart problems or previous serious toxicity require individual oncology/pharmacy review before treatment can continue.
