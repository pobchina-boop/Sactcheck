1. Treatment and purpose

Vinblastine and methotrexate for aggressive fibromatosis · NCCP 00554 v3

Vinblastine 6 mg/m² and methotrexate 30 mg/m² are given on days 1, 8 and 15 of each 28-day cycle. Day 22 is a planned break. National Cancer Control Programme describes 12 cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity requires a change.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Vinblastine · Vinca alkaloid chemotherapy

Report fever, painful constipation with vomiting or abdominal swelling, new weakness or numbness, hearing changes and infusion-site pain promptly.

Methotrexate · Antimetabolite chemotherapy

Report severe mouth ulcers or diarrhoea, dry cough or breathlessness, fever, yellow eyes, reduced urine or new neurological symptoms urgently. Ask the team before starting any medicine or supplement.

These are selected discussion prompts, not a complete risk list. Discuss serious, potentially life-threatening and lasting effects; tailor the conversation to comorbidities and the patient’s priorities. Discuss realistic benefit, uncertainty, alternatives including observation or symptom-focused care when appropriate, serious and lasting risks, personal priorities and the option to decline or reconsider treatment. This injected regimen gives three weekly doses followed by a one-week break, for up to 12 cycles. Follow the written injection calendar; never substitute daily oral methotrexate. Kidney or liver problems, ulcers or new breathing symptoms require individual oncology/pharmacy review before further treatment. Desmoid treatment is outside the listed licensed indications of the examined vinblastine and methotrexate products. The national regimen supports this use; your team should explain the product-specific off-label rationale and document consent under local policy.
