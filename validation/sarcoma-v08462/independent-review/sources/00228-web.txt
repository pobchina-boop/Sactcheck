L32@P0: TREATMENT:
L33@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L34@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L35@P0: eriBULin is administered on day 1 and day 8 of a 21 day cycle until disease progression or unacceptable
L36@P0: toxicity occurs.
L37@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is administered.
L38@P0: Day Drug Dose Route Diluent & Rate Cycle
L39@P0: 1 and 8 a, b, ceriBULin 1.23mg/m2IV infusion d, e 50mL NaCl 0.9% over 5
L40@P0: minutes
L41@P0: Repeat every 21 days
L42@P0: aNote: Breast cancer - eriBULIn may be used in combination with trastuzumab therapy (Ref NCCP Regimen 00200 Trastuzumab (IV) Monotherapy -21 days).
L43@P0: b
L44@P0: In the EU the recommended dose refers to the base of the active substance (eriBULin).
L45@P0: Calculation of the individual dose to be administered to a patient must be based on the strength of the ready to use solution that contains 0.44 mg/mL
L46@P0: eriBULin and the dose recommendation of 1.23 mg/m2.
L47@P0: The dose reduction recommendations shown below (Table 1, 2 and 3) are also shown as the dose of eriBULin to be administered based on the strength of
L48@P0: the ready to use solution.
L49@P0: c
L50@P0: In the EMBRACE trial and other trials, the corresponding publications and in some other regions e.g. the US and Switzerland, the recommended dose is
L51@P0: based on the salt form (eriBULin mesylate). The equivalent dose of eriBULin mesylate is 1.4mg/m2.
L52@P0: deriBULin should not be diluted in 5% glucose.
L53@P0: eFinal dose concentration should be 0.018 - 0.18mg/mL.

L87@P1: Disease monitoring should be in line with the patient’s treatment plan and any other test/s as
L88@P1: directed by the supervising Consultant.
