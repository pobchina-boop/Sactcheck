L31@P0: TREATMENT:
L32@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L33@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L34@P0: Treatment is administered once every 4 weeks for typically 6 cycles or may be continued until disease
L35@P0: progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.
L36@P0: Facilities to treat anaphylaxis MUST be present when systemic anti-cancer therapy (SACT) is
L37@P0: administered.

L186@P3: OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L187@P3: Other strategies to prevent and treat PPE, which may be initiated for 4 to 7 days after treatment with
L188@P3: pegylated liposomal DOXOrubicin include keeping hands and feet cool, by exposing them to cool water
L189@P3: (soaks, baths, or swimming), avoiding excessive heat/hot water and keeping them unrestricted (no socks,
L190@P3: gloves, or shoes that are tight fitting) (Refer to local policy).

L192@P3:  Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) for details.
L193@P3: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L194@P3:  Cardiotoxicity: Frequent ECG monitoring is recommended. Reduction of the QRS complex suggests
L195@P3: cardiac toxicity. LVEF monitoring using ECHO or MUGA should be applied during treatment. The
L196@P3: evaluation of LVEF is considered to be mandatory before each additional administration of pegylated
L197@P3: liposomal DOXOrubicin that exceeds a lifetime cumulative anthracycline dose of 450mg/m2. Cardiac
L198@P3: toxicity also may occur at cumulative anthracycline doses lower than 450mg/m2in patients with prior
L199@P3: mediastinal irradiation or in those receiving concurrent cyclophosphamide therapy.
L200@P3:  Acute Infusion Reaction: Usually seen during the first infusion. For patients who experience an
L201@P3: infusion reaction, the method of infusion should be modified as follows: 5% of the total dose should
L202@P3: be infused slowly over the first 15 minutes. If tolerated without reaction, the infusion rate may then
L203@P3: be doubled for the next 15 minutes. If tolerated, the infusion may then be completed over the next
L204@P3: hour for a total infusion time of 90 minutes.
