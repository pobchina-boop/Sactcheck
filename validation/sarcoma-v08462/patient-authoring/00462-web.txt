L28@P0: TREATMENT:
L29@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L30@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L31@P0: Treatment is administered once every 21 days* for a maximum of 6 cycles or until disease progression or
L32@P0: unacceptable toxicity occurs.
L33@P0: * In some patients, a 14 day cycle may be more appropriate.

L174@P3: PREMEDICATIONS: None usually required
L175@P3: OTHER SUPPORTIVE CARE:
L176@P3: Other strategies to prevent and treat PPE, which may be initiated for 4 to 7 days after treatment with
L177@P3: pegylated liposomal DOXOrubicin include keeping hands and feet cool, by exposing them to cool water
L178@P3: (soaks, baths, or swimming), avoiding excessive heat/hot water and keeping them unrestricted (no socks,
L179@P3: gloves, or shoes that are tight fitting) (Refer to local policy).
L180@P3: ADVERSE EFFECTS:
L181@P3:  Please refer to the relevant Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) for details.
L182@P3: REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L183@P3:  Cardiotoxicity: Frequent ECG monitoring is recommended. Reduction of the QRS complex suggests
L184@P3: cardiac toxicity. LVEF monitoring using ECHO or MUGA should be applied during treatment. The
L185@P3: evaluation of LVEF is considered to be mandatory before each additional administration of pegylated
L186@P3: liposomal DOXOrubicin that exceeds a lifetime cumulative anthracycline dose of 450mg/m2. Cardiac
L187@P3: toxicity also may occur at cumulative anthracycline doses lower than 450mg/m2in patients with prior
L188@P3: mediastinal irradiation or in those receiving concurrent cyclophosphamide therapy.
L189@P3:  Acute Infusion Reaction: Usually seen during the first infusion. For patients who experience an
L190@P3: infusion reaction, the method of infusion should be modified as follows: 5% of the total dose should
L191@P3: be infused slowly over the first 15 minutes. If tolerated without reaction, the infusion rate may then
L192@P3: be doubled for the next 15 minutes. If tolerated, the infusion may then be completed over the next
L193@P3: hour for a total infusion time of 90 minutes.
