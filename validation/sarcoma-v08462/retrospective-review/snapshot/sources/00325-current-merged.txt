L27@P0: TREATMENT:
L28@P0: The starting dose of the drugs detailed below may be adjusted downward by the prescribing clinician, using their
L29@P0: independent medical judgement, to consider each patients individual clinical circumstances.
L30@P0: The recommended dose of SUNitinib is 50mg taken orally, once daily for 4 weeks followed by a 2 week
L31@P0: rest period to comprise a complete cycle of 6 weeks (42 days).
L32@P0: Treatment is administered continuously until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.
L33@P0: Day Drug Dose Route
L34@P0: 1-28 SUNitinib 50mg daily PO once daily with or without food.
L35@P0: Swallow whole with a glass of water.
L36@P0: 29-42 REST PERIOD
L37@P0: If a dose is missed, the patient should NOT be given an additional dose. The patient should take the
L38@P0: usual prescribed dose on the following day.
L39@P0: SUNitinib is commonly available in 12.5mg/25mg/37.5 mg and 50mg hard capsules

L169@P3:  REGIMEN SPECIFIC COMPLICATIONS:
L170@P3:  Haemorrhage and tumour bleeding: Haemorrhagic events, some of which were fatal, reported
L171@P3: through post-marketing experience have included gastro-intestinal, respiratory, urinary tract and
L172@P3: brain haemorrhages. Patients receiving concomitant treatment with anticoagulants) may be
L173@P3: periodically monitored by complete blood counts (platelets), coagulation factors (PT/INR) and
L174@P3: physical examination.
L175@P3:  Hypertension: Patients should be screened for hypertension and controlled as appropriate.
L176@P3: Temporary suspension is recommended in patients with severe hypertension that is not controlled
L177@P3: with medical management. Treatment may be resumed once hypertension is appropriately
L178@P3: controlled.
L179@P3:  Aneurysms and artery dissections: The use of vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) pathway
L180@P3: inhibitors in patients with or without hypertension may promote the formation of aneurysms and/or
L181@P3: artery dissections. Before initiating SUNitinib, this risk should be carefully considered in patients with
L182@P3: risk factors such as hypertension or history of aneurysm.
L183@P3:  Hypothyroidism: Baseline laboratory measurement of thyroid function is recommended in all
L184@P3: patients. Patients with pre- existing hypothyroidism or hyperthyroidism should be treated as per
L185@P3: standard medical practice prior to the start of SUNitinib treatment. During SUNitinib treatment,
L186@P3: routine monitoring of thyroid function should be performed. Patients who develop thyroid
L187@P3: dysfunction should be treated as per standard medical practice. Hypothyroidism has been observed
L188@P3: to occur early as well as late during treatment with SUNitinib.
L189@P3:  Skin and tissue disorders: Skin discolouration, possibly due to the active substance colour (yellow),
L190@P3: is a very common adverse reaction occurring in approximately 30% of patients. Patients should be
L191@P3: advised that depigmentation of the hair or skin may also occur during treatment with SUNitinib.
L192@P3: Other possible dermatologic effects may include dryness, thickness or cracking of the skin, blisters,
L193@P3: or occasional rash on the palms of the hands and soles of the feet. The above reactions were not
L194@P3: cumulative, were typically reversible and generally did not result in treatment discontinuation.
L195@P3: Cases of pyoderma gangrenosum, generally reversible after drug discontinuation, have been
L196@P3: reported. Severe cutaneous reactions have been reported, including cases of erythema multiforme
L197@P3: (EM) and cases suggestive of Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) and toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN),
L198@P3: some of which were fatal. If signs or symptoms of SJS, TEN, or EM (e.g.progressive skin rash often
L199@P3: with blisters or mucosal lesions) are present, SUNitinib treatment should be discontinued. If the
L200@P3: diagnosis of SJS or TEN is confirmed, treatment must not be re-started. In some cases of suspected
L201@P3: EM, patients tolerated the reintroduction of SUNitinib therapy at a lower dose after resolution of
L202@P3: the reaction; some of these patients also received concomitant treatment with corticosteroids or
L203@P3-4: antihistamines.NCCP National SACT Regimen
