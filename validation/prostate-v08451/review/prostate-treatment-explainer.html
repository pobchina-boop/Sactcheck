Prostate cancer: why ADT, when to intensify, and where radiotherapy fits

Treatment depends on two separate questions: where is the cancer, and is it progressing despite a castrate testosterone level? A rising PSA alone does not identify the correct treatment. Establish stage, PSA kinetics, testosterone, symptoms, previous treatment and the patient’s goals.

This educational overview explains treatment choices. It does not select a regimen or change SACTCheck eligibility, dose or treatment-status rules. Guideline evidence, an EMA licence and an NCCP funded indication are separate checks. Verify the current Irish prescribing and reimbursement route for the exact setting.

What each treatment does

Most prostate adenocarcinomas use androgen receptor signalling to grow. Androgen deprivation therapy (ADT) reduces testicular testosterone. GnRH agonists, such as leuprorelin, initially stimulate and then suppress pituitary signalling; antagonists, such as degarelix or relugolix, block it directly without the initial testosterone flare. Orchiectomy achieves permanent testicular androgen suppression.

An androgen receptor pathway inhibitor (ARPI) adds a different level of blockade. Abiraterone is an ARPI: it inhibits CYP17-mediated androgen synthesis, including production outside the testes. Enzalutamide, apalutamide and darolutamide act at the androgen receptor. They are alternatives selected for a particular setting, not a routine four-drug combination.

Abiraterone also reduces cortisol synthesis. Increased ACTH drive can cause mineralocorticoid excess: hypertension, low potassium and fluid retention. Prescribed prednisolone suppresses this drive. NCCP 00577 uses 5 mg daily for mHSPC; 00103 uses 10 mg daily, or 5 mg twice daily, for mCRPC. Do not interchange these schedules or stop the steroid abruptly without a plan.

Castration resistance means progression despite castrate testosterone; it does not mean androgen signalling is irrelevant. Continue medical ADT in these five NCCP regimens unless the patient has had surgical castration. Confirm testosterone suppression and adherence when assessing progression.

Choosing intensification for the person in front of you

Abiraterone: review cardiovascular disease, blood pressure, potassium, oedema, liver function, diabetes, steroid suitability and fasting instructions. Its monitoring and steroid burden differ from the receptor inhibitors.

Enzalutamide: review fatigue, cognition, falls and fracture risk, seizure predisposition, blood pressure and interactions. It can reduce exposure to other medicines through enzyme induction. A pharmacist should check anticoagulants and every prescription, supplement and herbal product.

Apalutamide: review rash and severe skin reactions, thyroid function, falls/fractures, seizure and cardiovascular history, interactions and new pulmonary symptoms. Use the current NCCP version 5, reviewed 1 July 2026, rather than the superseded version 4.

Darolutamide: assess kidney and liver function, cardiovascular history and interactions, including transporter-related statin interactions. A different interaction and CNS profile does not make it risk-free. The patient must be able to follow twice-daily dosing with food.

Docetaxel adds cytotoxic treatment and a different burden: marrow suppression, infection, neuropathy and infusion visits. ARASENS tested adding darolutamide to ADT plus docetaxel; it did not directly prove that triplet therapy is better than ADT plus an ARPI. Do not rank drugs by comparing hazard ratios from separate trials.

Radiotherapy has several different jobs

CHAARTED high volume means visceral metastases or at least four bone metastases, with at least one outside the spine and pelvis. Otherwise it is low volume. LATITUDE high risk means at least two of Gleason score ≥8, at least three bone lesions, and measurable visceral metastases. These definitions are not interchangeable, and the original conventional-imaging definitions should not be silently replaced by PSMA PET lesion counts.

What the pivotal trials actually showed

Overall survival (OS) measures survival from any cause. Metastasis-free survival (MFS) measures time to metastasis or death; it is not the same endpoint. A hazard ratio (HR) compares event rates over follow-up, not the percentage of people cured. The figures below are population results from the stated analyses, not personalised forecasts.

The support plan starts with ADT

Explain hot flushes, sexual effects, fatigue, loss of muscle and bone, metabolic changes and mood symptoms. Record a plan for exercise, bone health and cardiometabolic monitoring, and ask directly about sexual function and wellbeing.

Review fracture risk and bone density when starting long-term ADT. Bone treatment for osteoporosis and treatment to prevent metastatic skeletal complications are separate indications. Reconcile all medicines and agree who monitors blood pressure, bloods, symptoms, PSA and testosterone. The selected regimen supplies its own schedule; a general explainer must not invent a universal scan interval.
