## Exact source boundaries and unresolved differences

1. **Renal:** NCCP >30/no adjustment and <30/50% original daily dose leaves **exactly 30** unallocated. EMA includes 30 in moderate impairment without adjustment and gives **250 mg once daily for <30 without dialysis**; after at least 4 weeks, 200 mg twice daily may be considered if tolerated. Legacy `le30` cannot resolve the boundary. Dialysis is not silently equated to non-dialysis severe impairment: NCCP describes a haemodialysis reduction as expected; EMA excludes both dialysis forms from its supported severe-renal schedule.
2. **Baseline hepatic:** EMA NCI categories, not Child-Pugh. Bilirubin **>1.5 to ≤3 ×ULN**, any AST → 200 mg twice daily; **>3 ×ULN** → 250 mg once daily. ≤1.5 does not select these reductions. Numeric bilirubin needs explicit baseline context; otherwise review only. The legacy hepatic bands are explicitly labelled established baseline categories. NCCP severe row has a QC typo beside 250 mg QD and allows increase if tolerated; no hepatic escalation is automated.
3. **Treatment hepatotoxicity:** grade ≥3 ALT/AST holds; with bilirubin≤grade 1, recover to ≤grade 1 or baseline before source 250 mg once-daily restart and possible 200 mg twice-daily escalation. ALT/AST **grade ≥2 plus bilirubin grade ≥2** permanently stops only with **confirmed absence of cholestasis/haemolysis**. Missing cause context holds for evaluation. Raw ULN multiples do not establish grades/attribution; preserved marked-ALT safety triage is labelled evaluation, not a sourced automatic restart algorithm. Further severe recurrence stops independently. Known adverse grade information survives discordant low numeric inputs.
4. **QT:** NCCP pretreatment exclusion is **strictly >500 ms**, while current EMA calls for immediate cardiology advice at **≥500 ms**. The tool holds at ≥500 pending review and adds NCCP exclusion only for baseline >500. **QT increase≥60 ms** is independently actionable and cannot disappear because current QT is missing/normal. Grade3 holds to ≤grade 1 with corrected electrolytes; NCCP says restart 200 mg twice daily, EMA says next lower dose. The difference remains explicit. Grade4 or further severe recurrence permanently stops.
5. **Bradycardia:** grade 2/3 symptomatic holds to ≤grade 1 or **heart rate≥60**. Resume previous schedule only when contributor identified and stopped/adjusted; otherwise reduced-dose review. Grade4 without contributor permanently stops; if contributor corrected, source permits 250 mg daily after recovery/frequent monitoring. Recurrence stops. Current adult SmPC footnote c minimum-dose evaluation is implemented for relevant bradycardia pathways. Missing cause/recurrence/recovery never authorises a restart.
6. **Eyes:** adult new severe visual loss (best corrected acuity **<6/60** in either eye / grade 4 ocular disorder) discontinues during urgent evaluation. Adult resumption evidence is uncertain. Paediatric grade 3/4 rules and routine baseline/month 1/every 3 months eye schedule are not imported.
7. **Monitoring:** NCCP liver tests every 2 weeks for 2 months conflicts with current EMA **weekly for 2 months**, then monthly. The suite states both and adopts the more intensive weekly approach pending local reconciliation. FBC/renal monthly, NCCP chest X-ray monthly, disease monitoring per consultant. “ECG every 2 cycles” is undefined in a continuous regimen; the suite asks the treating service to establish explicit periodic ECG/electrolyte/pulse/BP timing rather than inventing a cycle length.
8. **Interactions:** strong CYP3A inhibitors, strong/moderate inducers, narrow-index CYP3A substrates and QT/bradycardic drugs require avoidance/reconciliation; moderate inhibitors require caution. No unsupported fixed crizotinib adjustment is invented for unavoidable interactions.

## Dose-renderer protection

All actions name the exact crizotinib component. Organ starting schedules and conditional restart schedules are given in plain source-linked recommendations with `dose_calculation: specialist_review`; review actions use `show_in_dose_actions: true`. This deliberately prevents 50% of the daily dose becoming 125 mg per administration, prevents multiplying renal and hepatic reductions, and prevents comparing 200 mg twice daily to 250 mg once daily solely by the per-dose number. Standard 250 mg twice-daily administration is rendered, but no unauthorised individual modified dose is calculated. Concurrent holds/stops govern the rendered action and all underlying restrictions remain visible.
