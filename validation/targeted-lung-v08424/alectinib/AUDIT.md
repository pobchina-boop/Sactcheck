## Material corrections

- Split the former combined indication into 00401a advanced after crizotinib, 00401b advanced first-line and 00401c adjuvant after complete resection. Adjuvant eligibility is NCCP stage IB (tumour ≥4 cm) to IIIA, ECOG 0–1. The stage labels reflect the study’s AJCC/UICC seventh edition context; current-stage mapping requires MDT review. No adjuvant ECOG limit is automatically imposed on advanced disease.
- Add the minimum seven-day post-crizotinib washout and adjuvant 24-month maximum. Advanced treatment continues until progression or unacceptable toxicity. All service authoring uses continuous oral dosing with `cycle_days: null`; no chemotherapy cycle is invented.
- Correct any-grade diagnosed ILD/pneumonitis to immediate interruption. Permanent cessation requires that no other potential cause has been identified; the prior code both missed grade 1 and overcalled permanence without cause assessment.
- Correct liver branches: ALT/AST **>5 ×ULN** with bilirubin **≤2 ×ULN** → withhold until baseline or **≤3 ×ULN**, then reduced-dose review. ALT/AST **>3 ×ULN** and bilirubin **>2 ×ULN** → permanent stop only when **cholestasis and haemolysis have been excluded**. The former combined row overcalled permanent cessation from labs alone. Missing cause context produces a conservative interruption/investigation message, explicitly identified as implementation safety handling. Isolated bilirubin gives contextual review rather than an attributed drug-injury diagnosis.
- Differentiate CPK: first episode **>5 to ≤10 ×ULN** → withhold to baseline or **≤2.5 ×ULN**, then same dose; **>10 ×ULN** or second qualifying **>5 ×ULN** episode → reduced-dose restart review after the same recovery criterion. Missing recurrence history does not assume first occurrence. A positive recurrence flag is explicitly defined as a confirmed qualifying current episode, not merely a historical episode.
- Add haemolytic anaemia: Hb **<10 g/dL** (100 g/L) plus suspected haemolysis triggers interruption and investigation under current SmPC 4.4; confirmed haemolysis with Hb <10 leads to reduced-dose review after resolution. Hb alone does not establish haemolysis. Exactly 10 g/dL does not enter the strict source row.
- Replace the single generic bradycardia rule with grade/cause/recurrent-event branches. Grade 2–3: hold to grade ≤1/asymptomatic or HR ≥60; previous dose if a contributing medicine was stopped/adjusted, otherwise reduced-dose review. Grade 4 without an identified contributing medicine: stop permanently. Grade 4 with that medicine stopped/adjusted: reduced-dose review after recovery and frequent monitoring. Recurrent grade 4: permanent stop. Grade 4 with an unmodified contributing medicine remains held with specialist review; no unsupported restart is inferred.
- Retain the legacy symptomatic-bradycardia signal independently so a discordant grade cannot erase it. The legacy QTcF field is retained as context, but its borrowed numeric ≥500-ms interruption rule is removed: neither NCCP nor current EMA alectinib tables specify this drug-specific cutoff. This does not state that a clinically concerning ECG is safe.
- Replace unsupported automatic grade-3 diarrhoea/rash reduced-dose advice with prompt clinical-review findings. NCCP/SmPC give no symptom-specific grade/dose row here. No fabricated restart is calculated. Source CTCAE terminology has no specified edition; the fields no longer falsely declare CTCAE v5.
- Child-Pugh C source starting dose is 450 mg twice daily. A numeric dose appears only with explicit first-prescription context; ongoing/current-dose uncertainty requires review. No dose percentages are multiplied. Withholding/stopping outranks a simultaneous starting-dose reference. A/B do not imply a baseline reduction. CrCl ≥30 needs no adjustment; <30 and haemodialysis have no adjustment expected in NCCP. SmPC notes severe renal impairment was not studied. The preserved renal band has original decision values 0/1/2, used by the rule engine and tested with the original `ge30`, `lt30`, `dialysis` entries.
- Add intolerance of 300 mg twice daily → permanent stop. Preserve 600 →450 →300 mg twice-daily reference levels without computing a restart when recovery/current-dose/concurrent-toxicity context is missing.
- Distinguish actual hypersensitivity contraindication from pregnancy and breastfeeding warnings. These use specialist hold/review pathways rather than falsely calling pregnancy an SmPC 4.3 absolute contraindication. Current advice includes female contraception during treatment and ≥5 weeks after; men with female partners who could become pregnant during treatment and ≥3 months after. Oral-contraceptive effectiveness may be reduced. Breastfeeding is advised against.
- Supplemental SmPC pathways cover gastrointestinal perforation (urgent management; consider discontinuation, not an invented unconditional permanent stop) and interaction review. Strong CYP3A inhibitors/inducers do not automatically mandate alectinib reduction; monitoring is advised. P-gp/BCRP substrate monitoring and heart-slowing medicines are handled individually.

## Monitoring, patient material and support

Baseline ALK confirmation, FBC, renal/liver profile, CPK, blood pressure and ECG. Liver profile every two weeks for three months, then every four weeks or as indicated. FBC/renal every four weeks. CPK every two weeks for the first month, then clinically indicated and for muscle symptoms. Blood pressure/pulse as indicated. Disease monitoring follows the Consultant plan.

**Retained source ambiguity:** NCCP asks for ECG “prior to cycle 2” but defines treatment as continuous oral without a cycle length. Every relevant timeline describes the need to confirm the first repeat ECG appointment locally. It does not invent a four-week ECG cadence or a 28-day treatment cycle.

Capsules are taken twice daily with food, swallowed whole, never opened/dissolved. A missed dose may be made up only when the next dose is more than six hours away; no double dose. After vomiting, take the next scheduled dose without replacement. Sun protection extends for seven days after stopping; broad-spectrum UVA/UVB sunscreen and lip balm SPF ≥50.

NCCP oral classification V6 Table 5 specifies **as-required antiemetics only** for minimal–low oral risk; Table 8 includes alectinib. NCIS V8 parenteral defaults were checked separately and are not applied as a routine infusion prescription. The support plan contains no automatic preventive drug course. Rescue selection remains individual/local.

`authoring.json` includes full targeted patient content, knowledge-base content, setting-specific evidence, practical logistics, relative patient journey, anatomy callouts, consent discussion support, selective support plan, source links and exact indication card contexts. `proposed/alectinib-consent-profile.json` supplies a missing alectinib-specific consent generator profile without borrowing osimertinib QT/cardiomyopathy wording or unrelated frequency estimates.

## Evidence and access distinctions

- **ALINA adjuvant:** updated ESMO 2025 author data, efficacy cutoff 8 December 2024. Stage II–IIIA: 48-month DFS 74.5% versus 46.3%, HR 0.36 (95% CI 0.23–0.56), 231 patients. ITT stage IB–IIIA: 75.5% versus 47.0%, HR 0.35 (95% CI 0.23–0.54), 257 patients. Time origin is randomisation. Comparator was four cycles of platinum chemotherapy; the trial did not test routine alectinib added after adjuvant chemotherapy. OS remained immature. Primary 2024 publication is retained separately; 2026 stage-reclassification exploratory analysis is not treated as a new population survival result.
- **Source discrepancy:** the ALINA conference abstract assigns rounded arm-specific median follow-up differently from the author slide (slide: alectinib 48.0 months, chemotherapy 47.4). Authored endpoint cards omit that disputed arm-specific follow-up. Shared cutoff, populations and DFS results are corroborated visually.
- **ALEX first-line advanced:** final analysis cutoff 28 April 2025, 303 patients. Median OS 81.1 versus 54.2 months; HR 0.78 (95% CI 0.56–1.08). The final comparison **did not establish a statistically significant OS difference**. No individual survival promise or head-to-head ranking against other modern ALK inhibitors is made.
- **Post-crizotinib:** Ou global phase II is described separately: single-arm 138 treated, 122 response-evaluable, independent-review ORR 50%, median PFS 8.9 months. It does not justify importing ALEX or adjuvant survival estimates.
- All patient evidence metrics stay empty and patient material uses qualitative explanations; quantitative results are clinician-facing and explicitly population-specific.
- NCCP lists 00401a/b CDS dates and 00401c reimbursement **N/A**. The printed entry is retained without interpreting it as a current funding decision. EU licence, NCCP indication, study population and current reimbursement/access are separate facts.
