# SACTCheck v0.67.0

## Knowledge base expansion

This cumulative release expands the detailed regimen knowledge base from 27 to 30 profiles.

### Atezolizumab, carboplatin and etoposide

NCCP 00689 Version 4.

The profile covers first line extensive stage small cell lung cancer, four cycle induction treatment and subsequent atezolizumab maintenance. It also reflects the current NCCP administration options for intravenous or subcutaneous atezolizumab and the permitted oral etoposide option for days 2 and 3.

Evidence includes IMpower133, updated overall survival and five year survival follow up.

### Pertuzumab, trastuzumab and docetaxel

NCCP 00204 Version 11.

The profile covers first line HER2 positive metastatic or locally recurrent unresectable breast cancer, patient selection, cardiac surveillance, docetaxel premedication, marrow toxicity, neuropathy, mucositis and infusion safety.

Evidence includes CLEOPATRA, the final overall survival analysis and the end of study analysis.

### Dabrafenib and trametinib

NCCP 00415 Version 4.

The profile separately represents the two current NCCP settings: unresectable or metastatic BRAF V600 mutated melanoma and adjuvant treatment after complete resection of stage III BRAF V600 mutated melanoma.

Evidence includes COMBI d, COMBI v, pooled five year metastatic outcomes, COMBI AD, five year adjuvant follow up and the 2024 final overall survival analysis. The final COMBI AD overall survival result is presented with the important limitation that the prespecified statistical significance threshold was not met.

## Evidence completeness standard

Each new profile received a two pass evidence review covering the protocol defining trial, mature follow up, later evidence, indication specific applicability, schedule applicability and limitations.

The new profiles add 5 principal evidence records and 3 reusable medicine profiles.
