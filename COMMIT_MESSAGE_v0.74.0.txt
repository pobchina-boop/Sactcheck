SUMMARY
Unify clinician and patient workflows with printable regimen passport

DESCRIPTION
- Reframes SACTCheck as one regimen engine feeding two connected interfaces: Clinician workspace and Patient-facing support.
- Adds the SACTCheck logo lockup and the strapline 'SACT support at point of care' across the main product presentation.
- Removes the unused Add regimen / protocol-importer affordance from the normal interface.
- Stabilises regimen-card actions so Clinic workflow and Patient support appear as soon as the card renders rather than after slower metadata-enrichment passes.
- Adds background pre-warming for patient-support and regimen-workflow data to reduce first-open delay.
- Adds a patient-facing treatment-at-a-glance view with regimen-specific toxicity separated by Know, Contact and Urgent action language.
- Adds a plain-language treatment schedule view using encoded treatment phases, cycle length, treatment day, medicine and route where available.
- Adds a patient toxicity passport with a simple 0-4 symptom communication scale while explicitly stating that it is not a CTCAE assessment.
- Adds a print-first patient-agnostic treatment passport with blank handwritten identifier/contact fields, cycle/visit record and a regimen-only QR/link.
- Keeps patient information out of browser persistence: no patient name, MRN, DOB, symptom scores, diary entries or treatment dates are stored by SACTCheck.
- Reframes the clinician workflow consent action as consent and patient support and surfaces the printable patient passport as a ready workflow output.
- Preserves the existing supportive-medication and extravasation workflow.
- Makes no change to protocol JSON, treatment thresholds, eligibility rules, dose actions or other deterministic assessment logic.
