SUMMARY
Add regimen-specific SACT consent builder

DESCRIPTION
- Adds a Consent button to regimen cards across the SACTCheck protocol library.
- Generates a regimen-specific consent discussion draft from the selected NCCP regimen and its exact medicines.
- Adds a generic cytotoxic chemotherapy consent layer when chemotherapy is present.
- Adds a generic immune-checkpoint-inhibitor consent layer with pneumonitis, colitis, hepatitis, endocrinopathy, nephritis, skin toxicity, infusion reactions, delayed toxicity and rarer serious immune-mediated toxicity.
- Adds structured agent-specific material-risk prompts for more than 50 commonly used oncology medicines.
- Clearly flags unmapped medicines for manual clinician/pharmacy review rather than guessing consent content.
- Requires clinician completion of expected benefit, treatment intent, reasonable alternatives and the consequences of declining or deferring treatment.
- Supports additional patient-specific material risks, fertility/contraception discussion and patient questions.
- Provides a live consent preview, selectable risk prompts, copyable text and Print / Save PDF output.
- Preserves patient-agnostic architecture by not requesting or persisting patient name, MRN or DOB; identifiers remain blank on the printable form for completion in the approved clinical record.
- Links the consent workflow to the current NCCP regimen source and national HSE/NCCP consent resources.
- Keeps current NCCP sources authoritative and marks consent content as pending independent Consultant Oncology and oncology-pharmacy validation.
- Preserves the v0.70.0/v0.70.1 source-reconciliation layers, engine-first UI, search-security remediation, knowledge base and sustainability content.
- Makes no change to protocol JSON, treatment thresholds, deterministic assessment rules or dose actions.
