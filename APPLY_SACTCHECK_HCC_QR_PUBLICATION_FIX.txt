SACTCheck v0.76.2 HCC QR publication fix (consolidated drop-in)

What the screenshot means
The QR points to https://sactcheck.com/docs/patient/00831/ . That page returns
404 on the currently published site, which still displays v0.68.0. The earlier
v0.76.2 drop-in already contains the page. Its files must reach the deployed
GitHub Pages artifact before a printed QR can open the detailed patient guide.

Install
1. Unzip this archive. Copy the contents of its js/, css/, data/, assets/,
   docs/, tests/ and tools/ folders into the matching folders at the top of
   your SACTCheck repository, replacing files when prompted. Do not put the
   archive itself into the repository.
2. Commit the changed files, including docs/patient/00831/index.html and
   docs/patient/00831/*.pdf. Push to the main branch used by .github/workflows/pages.yml,
   or merge your work from develop into main. Pushing only to develop does not
   run this site's GitHub Pages publishing workflow.
3. In GitHub, check Actions > Deploy SACTCheck Pages for a successful run. If
   another regression or security check fails, fix that error before expecting
   the live QR destination to change.
4. Open https://sactcheck.com/docs/patient/00831/ in a fresh browser tab.
   Confirm that the HCC guide, anatomy image and page links load. Then scan
   the QR from the printed guide or card again.

This package includes the full v0.76.2 HCC visual and public guide, plus a
tools/validate-pages-site.js gate: the publishing workflow now fails when
the HCC QR destination, anatomy art, icons or linked printable files are absent
from the final deployable artifact. It does not itself publish your website.
