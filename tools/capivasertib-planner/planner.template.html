My 28-day schedule

Tablets means take your prescribed capivasertib dose morning and evening, about 12 hours apart. Rest means no capivasertib that day. Injection is your fulvestrant visit.

    $days

Follow the dates and dose agreed with your team. If they pause treatment, ask for your new plan before restarting.

Blood sugar checks

Capivasertib can raise blood sugar. Before starting, your team checks fasting glucose and HbA1c, your longer-term blood sugar level.

Fasting checks are due in treatment weeks 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8, then monthly. The preferred time is before a tablet on the third or fourth dosing day of the week. HbA1c is usually checked every three months.

With diabetes, check fasting glucose daily for the first two weeks, then follow your agreed plan. Diabetes, prediabetes or high starting glucose also calls for HbA1c at week 4.

If a tablet dose is missed

Up to four hours late, you can take the missed dose if the next dose will be at least eight hours later. After four hours, skip it. Never double up. If you vomit after a dose, take your next dose at the usual time.

Before your next visit

Bring your glucose readings and medicine list. Check new medicines with your pharmacist and avoid grapefruit. Follow your own diabetes plan on tablet and rest days.

When to get help

Call your oncology team immediately for unusual thirst or passing more urine, new diarrhoea or rash. Vomiting or tummy pain with fast or deep breathing, confusion or sleepiness can mean a blood sugar emergency. Seek emergency care immediately. Do not wait for a glucose reading. In Ireland, call 112 or 999 for emergency help.
