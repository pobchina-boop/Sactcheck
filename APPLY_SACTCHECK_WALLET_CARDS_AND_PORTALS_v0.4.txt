INSTALL
1. Unzip into the root of your current local Sactcheck repository. Preserve
   the docs/patient/<code>/ directory structure. Merge/replace ONLY the eight
   listed public files. Do not replace the repository with this archive.
2. Have oncology, nursing and pharmacy review regimen wording, local SOS
   instructions, contact details and CUH supportive-medicine text before use.
3. Commit and push to main. The existing Pages workflow copies docs/ into
   the public site. After deployment verify both pages, guides, passports and
   contact cards at these route prefixes:
     https://sactcheck.com/docs/patient/00209/
     https://sactcheck.com/docs/patient/00831/
   The linked PDFs live beside each page as guide.pdf, passport.pdf and
   contact-card.pdf. Scan a card QR and confirm it opens its public route.

PRINTING THE WALLET CARDS
Print contact-card.pdf on A4 at Actual Size / 100% (disable Fit to Page).
Cut the outside rectangle of a chosen copy and fold on the dashed centre
line, with the blank sides touching so printed front and back face outward.
Each folded card measures about 85.6 x 54 mm. Use thick paper or laminate
after writing if desired. Fill in the confirmed 24-hour SOS number and
relevant personal details by hand. The QR contains only the regimen's public
URL: no name, phone number or patient information is encoded or transmitted.
The PDF QR area is clickable when opened digitally.

For the HCC regimen, the SACTCheck contact card complements rather than
replaces the manufacturer's Tecentriq patient alert card. The EMA says that
the manufacturer card should be carried and shown to healthcare staff.

Neither the domain/CNAME nor the clinician app's index, JavaScript, regimen
engine or build workflow is changed. The clinician-facing patient-information
button still needs integration against the latest local app source. This is
patient education and a contact aid; it is not an individual prescription,
consent record or replacement for emergency instructions.
