PASSPORT DESIGN
FOLFOX6: 14-day cycles; cover + cycles 1–12 + a reusable blank page.
HCC: 21-day cycles; cover + cycles 1–6 + a reusable blank page.
The patient records the worst 0–4 symptom score each week, dates, objective
details and advice. A separate column is for the clinical team's CTCAE grade.
This original patient scale is not a formal CTCAE grade or the NCI PRO-CTCAE
instrument. Urgent red flags require a call at any score. The PDFs are blank
printable forms; nothing entered on paper is sent to the site.

This additive package does not alter the SACTCheck app's index, JavaScript,
regimen engine, build workflow, domain or CNAME. The clinician-facing
patient-information button is not linked to these routes yet: integrate that
against the latest local app source, rather than replacing newer work with an
older public snapshot. These are patient-education prototypes and are not
individual prescriptions, triage tools or consent records.

Sources: NCCP 00209 v10a, NCCP 00831 v2a, NCI CTCAE v6, HSE SOS advice.
Product risks are supported by the relevant medicines' product information.
FOLFOX supportive-medication wording mirrors the CUH moderate emetogenic
proforma V2 05.21 and requires confirmation against the currently approved
local chart; it includes the PPI named there.
