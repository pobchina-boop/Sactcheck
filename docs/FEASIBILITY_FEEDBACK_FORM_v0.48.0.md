# SACTCheck hypothetical-scenario feasibility feedback form

## Participant context
- Clinical role
- Oncology experience (years)
- Current practice area / tumour group
- Number of hypothetical scenarios completed

## Feasibility measures
1. I could find the intended regimen quickly. — 1 strongly disagree to 5 strongly agree
2. The tissue-specific indication was clear. — 1 to 5
3. The assessment inputs were understandable. — 1 to 5
4. The output was clear and clinically interpretable. — 1 to 5
5. I could understand why the result was produced. — 1 to 5
6. The official NCCP source was easy to access. — 1 to 5
7. SACTCheck would save time compared with my usual protocol-search method. — 1 to 5
8. I would be willing to use SACTCheck during routine day-ward work after validation. — 1 to 5

## Time and completion
- Time to complete each scenario
- Was the scenario completed without assistance? Yes / No
- Did any technical problem prevent completion? Yes / No; describe

## Safety and clarity
- Did any result appear incorrect, misleading or unsafe?
- Were any relevant clinical factors missing?
- Was it clear that missing information remained unassessed?
- Was the decision-support disclaimer clear without obstructing use?

- Most useful feature
- Most confusing feature
