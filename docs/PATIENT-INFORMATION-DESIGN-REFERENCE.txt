SACTCheck patient information design reference
Agreed with Paul O'Brien, 3 November 2026

PURPOSE
Preserve the quality, clinical detail and established anatomical illustrations. Present information in increasing depth. Simplifying the layout must never mean deleting information or replacing the full reference with a summary.

PATIENT JOURNEY
1. Consent establishes the treatment decision, expected benefits, risks, alternatives and agreed boundaries. Formal consent remains a separate document.
2. A three-page patient companion distils the essentials and accompanies consent or the first clinic visit.
3. The automatically populated, regimen-specific QR opens the full OncoWiki. Information is organised into progressively deeper sections without loss of content.

THREE-PAGE COMPANION
Page 1: regimen name and aim; treatment at a glance; broad phases, including chemotherapy, radiotherapy and maintenance where applicable; cycle schedule; required and individually indicated tests before, during and after treatment; between-cycle expectations. Distinguish source requirements from local or individual plans.
Page 2: established full anatomical illustration, connected numbered organ anchors, regimen-specific toxicity descriptions, medicine attribution, symptoms and clear actions. Preserve all authored detail. Do not substitute generic stick figures, floating labels or unanchored lists. Urgent advice must be prominent.
Page 3: My Treatment Card, front and back; correct regimen QR; cycle record; clear cutting and folding instructions; contacts, resources and instructions for saving the treatment information as a web app. Paul will provide final local numbers/resources. Placeholder numbers must be clearly marked and replaced before patient use.

WALLET CARD
Print at actual size with clear cut and fold boundaries. Use finite stamps only where the prescribed treatment course supports a fixed count. Use an appropriate rolling record for variable or continuing treatment. Distinguish cycles from weekly administrations and treatment phases. Paper stamps do not update the website or authorise subsequent treatment. QR codes link to the exact regimen/context and full information without containing patient identifiers.

ONCOWIKI
Keep the full existing content, including medicine mechanisms, evidence, monitoring, phase-specific treatment, supportive care, practical logistics and patient passport. Use simple navigation for overall treatment, between-cycle expectations, tests and timing, passport, and extended resources. Expandable sections reveal detail; urgent help remains readily accessible.

COMPLETE PAPER ALTERNATIVE
Patients must be able to receive every part of the information without using technology. Provide both a three-page companion PDF and a complete patient handbook PDF, plus individual section printing.
The complete handbook should have a contents page, page numbers, readable text, clear headings, labelled visuals and useful written URLs. Include all expandable content. Nothing essential may depend on clicking, hovering, colour alone, QR scanning or internet access.
Logical sequence: urgent help and contacts near the front; regimen and treatment aim; medicine mechanisms; broad treatment phases and cycle schedule; preparation and testing before/during/after treatment; anatomical toxicities, symptoms and actions; between-treatment expectations and supportive medicines; daily life and practical advice; passport and treatment record; recovery/follow-up; contacts, resources, glossary and sources.
The complete handbook is additional to the companion, and neither replaces consent.

PRIORITY AND ACCEPTANCE
Fix and verify the existing anatomical output before continuing new regimen development. Preserve bespoke HCC artwork and context-specific or phase-specific maps. Do not guess a treatment phase on selector pages.
Check screen and PDF layouts, organ-to-label connectors, all toxicity text, medicine attribution, mobile readability, page boundaries, readable print size, working QR destinations and matching download copies. Check that full reference material and passports are preserved. Run relevant regression checks before delivering an update.
Do not claim an update is ready, committed, pushed or deployed until that is verified. Supply a tested update package and copyable GitHub summary/description when ready.

STATUS AT SAVING
This document records agreed requirements, not a claim that all features are implemented. Anatomical repairs are in the working v0.84.31 update. Complete handbook generation and wider release compatibility checks remain outstanding. Further regimen expansion is paused until the anatomical correction is verified.
