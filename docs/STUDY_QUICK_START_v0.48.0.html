SACTCheck Quick-Start Guide

Hypothetical scenario feasibility evaluation · v0.48.0

SACTCheck is a clinician-facing web application that compares hypothetical clinical information with encoded NCCP regimen criteria. The purpose of this study build is to evaluate usability, clarity, speed and perceived clinical usefulness.

1

Find the regimen

Search by regimen, drug, brand name, indication or NCCP number. Confirm the tissue-specific indication and protocol version.

2

Enter the scenario

Enter only the hypothetical values relevant to the case. Missing fields remain explicitly unassessed and are not assumed normal.

3

Review the result

Review the entered value beside the encoded criterion, the unassessed domains, the rule explanation and the official NCCP source.

Study-use boundary: Use hypothetical, non-identifiable scenarios only. SACTCheck does not prescribe, authorise or confirm treatment safety. The current official NCCP protocol, complete clinical assessment, local policy and independent oncology judgement remain decisive.

What to assess

  • Was the intended regimen easy to find?
  • Was the correct indication clear?
  • Were the inputs understandable?
  • Was partial assessment behaviour clear?
  • Was the key output easy to interpret?
  • Could you verify why the result occurred?
  • Did the app appear faster than normal protocol lookup?
  • Did anything appear misleading, missing or unsafe?
Suggested test: Complete at least two hypothetical cases—one uncomplicated case and one case containing a laboratory or toxicity threshold that should trigger review, delay or dose-modification criteria.
