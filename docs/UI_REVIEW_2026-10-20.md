## Scope
Read-only live desktop review of the homepage, search/results, Clinic workflow, Treatment assessment engine, clinician knowledge base and patient 00568 portal/reference. This is a design review of sampled journeys, not a full clinical audit or formal usability/accessibility certification. A fresh mobile-browser review was not completed in this session.

## Keep
- SACTCheck name, shield/check identity and navy/teal palette.
- The three clear regimen-card destinations: Clinic workflow, Patient platform and NCCP source.
- Regimen-specific patient graphics, cycle orientation, treatment phases and source traceability.
- Persistent access to urgent patient advice.

## Implemented in v0.84.5 following the user's screenshots
1. All patient workflow actions occupy one equal-width vertical column.
2. Routine immunotherapy glucose/ketone controls are removed and the laboratory layout uses labelled organ groups and wider responsive fields. Underlying safety rules remain unchanged and omitted domains stay unassessed.
3. Manual anonymous assessment ID entry and its empty panel are removed. Automatic output references and conditional assessment profiles remain.

## Brand direction
Keep the existing identity and make it more consistent. A possible public-facing line is “Clear treatment information, from clinic to home.” Clinician screens should prioritise rapid access and precise context; patient screens should use plain language, larger readable text and focused choices. Avoid implying NCCP or HSE endorsement.

## Acceptance checks for the broader redesign
Verify keyboard navigation and focus, text contrast, zoom/reflow, long drug names, mobile layouts and button targets; verify exact-code search separately from drug-name/alias search. Compare patient comprehension and clinician task completion in observed sessions with representative users. Preserve rule inputs, source provenance, urgent guidance and treatment-profile selection.
