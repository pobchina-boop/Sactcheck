How this medicine supports your treatment

Control advanced cancer and symptoms while balancing side effects and treatment burden.

Oral PARP inhibitor

Talazoparib

Disrupts deoxyribonucleic acid (the cell’s genetic material) (DNA) repair in BRCA-associated cancer
PARP inhibited / trapped↓DNA damage accumulates↓BRCA-deficient cells less able to repair

Talazoparib inhibits PARP and traps it on damaged DNA. Cancer cells with inherited BRCA1/2 changes may have less ability to repair this damage. Normal bone marrow can also be affected, so blood-count monitoring is essential.

Report fever, infections, unusual bruising, persistent fatigue or breathlessness.

Questions to ask

Why was this treatment chosen for me?

Talazoparib targets a DNA-repair vulnerability associated with inherited BRCA1/2 changes. Suitability also depends on HER2 status, previous anthracycline/taxane treatment and, for hormone-receptor-positive disease, previous endocrine treatment or its suitability.

What happens if a dose is delayed or missed?

Do not replace a missed or vomited dose. Take the next dose at the usual time. The team decides whether and at what dose to restart after a hold.

Who arranges the checks and explains the results?

Agree the due window, booking team, result reviewer and contact route if a test or appointment is missing. This page does not record completed checks.

Can I take other medicines or supplements?

Ask the pharmacist to review P-glycoprotein inhibitors and all new medicines or supplements. Changing kidney function or an interacting medicine can change the prescribed dose.

Explain the plan back: Which medicines and doses are prescribed, when are they due, which checks are needed, and what would make you call the team today?
