Sorafenib: confirm your cancer treatment

Differentiated thyroid cancer

For progressive differentiated thyroid cancer that no longer responds to radioactive iodine. NCCP 00294c; the usual starting schedule is 400 mg twice daily.

Liver or kidney cancer

NCCP 00294a covers hepatocellular carcinoma and 00294b covers renal cell carcinoma. Their treatment information and dose-reduction schedules differ. Ask your team for the matching information; this thyroid guide must not be used as a substitute.
