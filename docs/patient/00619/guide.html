Adjuvant abemaciclib + endocrine therapy

Breast · NCCP 00619 · version 4a
Treatment journey
Every day

Abemaciclib 150 mg twice daily, with or without food, at approximately the same times each day unless your dose has been changed.

Alongside it

Continue the endocrine treatment prescribed for you.

First 2 months

Blood count, kidney/liver and bone profile are checked every 2 weeks.

Next 2 months

These tests are usually monthly, then as clinically indicated.

Treatment duration

Abemaciclib is taken continuously for 2 years or until progression/unacceptable toxicity, according to the NCCP source and your treatment plan.

Important medicine-specific effects

Abemaciclib

  • Diarrhoea is common and can be severe, especially early in treatment.
  • Low neutrophils, liver injury and blood clots can occur.
  • Rare inflammatory lung toxicity can cause new cough or breathlessness.

Endocrine therapy

  • The exact endocrine medicine determines its risks.
  • Common issues can include hot flushes, joint/muscle symptoms, sexual/vaginal symptoms and effects on bone health.
Other / shared treatment effects: The anatomy page highlights important symptom patterns for this exact regimen, but it cannot show every possible effect. Fatigue, nausea, appetite change, mouth/bowel symptoms or blood-count changes may occur depending on the medicines used. Report new or worsening symptoms even if they are not listed here.

Monitoring / medicines

  • Start anti-diarrhoeal treatment at the first sign of loose stools exactly as advised by your oncology team.
  • Swallow tablets whole; do not chew, split or crush.
  • Avoid grapefruit/grapefruit juice. If a dose is missed or vomited, take the next dose at the scheduled time rather than an extra dose.

Bring / track

  • Your current medicines, including non-prescription products.
  • A note of new symptoms, when they started and what they stopped you doing.
  • Any temperature, blood-pressure or bowel-count readings your team asked you to record.
  • Questions about the purpose of treatment, scan/review timing or changes to the plan.

When to call - do not wait for the next appointment

Call oncology urgently for fever, persistent or severe diarrhoea, dehydration, new breathlessness, one-sided leg swelling, jaundice, significant bleeding or marked weakness. Severe chest pain or breathing difficulty: call 112/999. If your team has given you different local thresholds or emergency instructions, follow those instructions.
