SACTCHECK · PATIENT TREATMENT PASSPORT

Pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + carboplatin

Lung · NCCP 00568 · version 5

My treatment

Cycles 1–4: pembrolizumab + pemetrexed + carboplatin every 21 days.

Maintenance: pembrolizumab + pemetrexed every 21 days if treatment continues.

How to use this passport

Record the symptom you actually felt, not just the organ it might come from. Use the simple severity guide on page 2 and bring the passport to reviews.

Symptoms to watch for

These are the same symptoms highlighted in the visual anatomy guide, translated into patient language.

SymptomMain medicine linkWhat to notice
Shortness of breath / new coughPembrolizumabNew breathlessness, worsening cough or breathing that feels harder than usual.
Diarrhoea / bowel changePembrolizumab / chemotherapyLoose or more frequent stools, tummy pain, blood or mucus, or difficulty keeping fluids up.
Fatigue / new weaknessPembrolizumab / chemotherapyTiredness that is new or clearly worse, especially if rest does not help or normal activities become difficult.
Rash / skin changesPembrolizumab / chemotherapyNew rash, itch, blistering, skin pain or mouth/eye involvement.
Numbness / tingling / weaknessPembrolizumabNew numbness, tingling, muscle weakness or difficulty using hands, walking or standing.
VomitingPemetrexed / carboplatinRepeated vomiting, inability to keep fluids down or vomiting despite anti-sickness medicines.
Do not wait to grade these red flags

Fever / shivers / suddenly unwell, chest pain or fainting, yellow eyes/skin or dark urine, severe headache/confusion/seizure, or markedly reduced urine/new swelling: contact oncology urgently. Severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain: call 112/999.
