02 / treatment journey

How the schedule fits together

Day 1

Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² IV followed by carboplatin AUC 5 IV.

Every 21 days

Treatment repeats every 21 days if review and blood tests allow.

Mesothelioma

Usually up to 4-6 cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

NSCLC

Usually 4-6 cycles, then maintenance pemetrexed (NCCP 00222) where appropriate.

A delay, dose change or omitted medicine can be appropriate. Your team should explain what changed and why.

04 / monitoring and support

What helps make treatment safer

Before and during treatment

  • Folic acid and vitamin B12 support are required with pemetrexed.
  • A corticosteroid is used around pemetrexed to reduce skin reactions.
  • Blood count, kidney and liver profile are checked before each cycle.

Bring to each review

  • Carboplatin dosing depends on renal function.
  • Follow the regimen-specific antiemetic prescription.
05 / when to call

Do not wait for the next appointment

Use your oncology SOS instructionsContact oncology urgently for fever or sudden illness, significant bleeding, new breathing difficulty or infusion reaction, uncontrolled vomiting/diarrhoea, severe mouth soreness, marked reduction in urine or rapidly worsening symptoms. For a medical emergency call 112/999.

If your team has given you different local thresholds or emergency instructions, follow those instructions.
