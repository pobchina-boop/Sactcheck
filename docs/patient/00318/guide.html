Pemetrexed + carboplatin

Lung · NCCP 00318 · version 6
Treatment journey
Day 1

Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² IV followed by carboplatin AUC 5 IV.

Every 21 days

Treatment repeats every 21 days if review and blood tests allow.

Mesothelioma

Usually up to 4-6 cycles unless progression or unacceptable toxicity occurs.

NSCLC

Usually 4-6 cycles, then maintenance pemetrexed (NCCP 00222) where appropriate.

Important medicine-specific effects

Pemetrexed

  • Low blood counts can increase infection, anaemia or bruising risk.
  • Mouth/gut inflammation and skin rash can occur.
  • Kidney function can deteriorate and is monitored.

Carboplatin

  • Low blood counts, especially neutropenia and low platelets, can occur.
  • Allergic infusion reactions can occur, particularly after repeated exposure.
  • Nausea and vomiting may occur despite preventative medicines.
Other / shared treatment effects: The anatomy page highlights important symptom patterns for this exact regimen, but it cannot show every possible effect. Fatigue, nausea, appetite change, mouth/bowel symptoms or blood-count changes may occur depending on the medicines used. Report new or worsening symptoms even if they are not listed here.

Monitoring / medicines

  • Folic acid and vitamin B12 support are required with pemetrexed.
  • A corticosteroid is used around pemetrexed to reduce skin reactions.
  • Blood count, kidney and liver profile are checked before each cycle.

Bring / track

  • Carboplatin dosing depends on renal function.
  • Follow the regimen-specific antiemetic prescription.

When to call - do not wait for the next appointment

Contact oncology urgently for fever or sudden illness, significant bleeding, new breathing difficulty or infusion reaction, uncontrolled vomiting/diarrhoea, severe mouth soreness, marked reduction in urine or rapidly worsening symptoms. For a medical emergency call 112/999.
