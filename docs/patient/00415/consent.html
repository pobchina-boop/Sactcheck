1. Treatment and purpose

Dabrafenib and trametinib for advanced BRAF-mutated melanoma · NCCP 00415 v5

Take dabrafenib twice daily, around 12 hours apart, and trametinib once daily. Both are taken every day unless the team instructs a hold. Treatment for advanced melanoma continues while beneficial and tolerable; there is no preset cycle total. A 28-day calendar is a review period, not a treatment-free week.

Review the treatment journey, tests and phase transitions with the patient.

2. Risks and supportive care

Dabrafenib · Oral BRAF inhibitor

Fever needs the written hold/contact plan. Check other medicines and contraception because interactions can matter.

Trametinib · Oral MEK inhibitor

Attend cardiac tests. New eye symptoms, cough, breathlessness, swelling or severe diarrhoea need prompt review.
