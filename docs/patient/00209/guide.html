Treatment journey
Day 1

Oxaliplatin and folinic acid are given by drip, then 5-FU is started.

Days 1-3

A portable pump gives 5-FU continuously for about 46 hours.

Around day 3

Pump disconnect. Follow your local line and pump instructions.

Days 4-14

Recovery and monitoring. Symptoms can occur on any day.

Day 15

Next cycle if review and blood tests allow.

Important medicine-specific effects

Oxaliplatin

  • Cold-triggered tingling or throat/jaw sensations can occur soon after treatment.
  • Persistent peripheral neuropathy can develop with cumulative treatment.
  • Infusion hypersensitivity can occasionally be severe.

5-fluorouracil (5-FU)

  • Severe toxicity can occur with reduced DPD activity.
  • Diarrhoea and mouth/gut inflammation can become severe.
  • New chest pain or rhythm symptoms require urgent assessment; hand-foot soreness can occur.
Other / shared treatment effects: The anatomy page highlights important symptom patterns for this exact regimen, but it cannot show every possible effect. Fatigue, nausea, appetite change, mouth/bowel symptoms or blood-count changes may occur depending on the medicines used. Report new or worsening symptoms even if they are not listed here.

Monitoring / medicines

  • DPD status is checked before fluoropyrimidine treatment.
  • Before each cycle: blood count, kidney/liver bloods and assessment of nerve symptoms.
  • Follow your own labelled anti-sickness, mouth-care and diarrhoea medicines.

Bring / track

  • Your current medicines, including non-prescription products.
  • A note of new symptoms, when they started and what they stopped you doing.
  • Any temperature, blood-pressure or bowel-count readings your team asked you to record.
  • Questions about the purpose of treatment, scan/review timing or changes to the plan.

When to call - do not wait for the next appointment

Call your oncology SOS service for fever or shivers, sudden illness, 4 or more diarrhoea episodes in a day, vomiting despite medicines, mouth sores stopping fluids, bleeding, new chest pain or breathlessness. Severe chest pain or breathing difficulty: call 112/999. If your team has given you different local thresholds or emergency instructions, follow those instructions.
