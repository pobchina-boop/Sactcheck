How this medicine supports your treatment

Entrectinib aims to control a cancer with a matching gene fusion. It may help shrink or slow cancer, including some cancer in the brain. Your team explains the treatment aim, uncertainty and alternatives for your exact setting.

ROS1 / TRK targeted therapy

Entrectinib

Blocks abnormal ROS1 or TRK growth signals
Matching gene fusion identified↓Abnormal growth signalling blocked↓Cancer growth may slow

Entrectinib inhibits proteins made by selected ROS1 or NTRK gene fusions. Blocking these signals can slow cancer growth. The relevant fusion, tumour setting and previous medicines determine eligibility; ROS1 and NTRK are different indications.

Before and during treatment

Regular checks help find changes early.

Before the first dose

Before starting: confirm fusion, previous targeted treatment, access and pregnancy precautions; blood count, kidney/liver tests, uric acid, ECG/electrolytes and cognitive symptoms. Heart-pumping assessment is needed for heart-failure symptoms or risk; brain imaging follows the Consultant plan.

NCCP 00702 v2 · official regimen · EMA Rozlytrek product information · updated 27 March 2025

Ongoing reviews

During treatment: repeat blood count, kidney/liver tests and uric acid at agreed reviews. Repeat ECG/electrolytes after one month, then periodically as indicated. Assess thinking/memory, heart symptoms, vision and bone pain; scans follow the Consultant plan.

Taking entrectinib

Follow your own prescription.

Whole capsules and missed doses

Take whole capsules with or without food; do not crush or chew. Avoid grapefruit, grapefruit products and Seville oranges. If a dose is missed, take it unless the next dose is due within 12 hours; confirm the exact boundary with your pharmacist. The leaflet permits repeating whole capsules after immediate vomiting: follow your written plan and call if uncertain. Suspension or tube administration requires separate pharmacy instructions.

NCCP 00702 v2 · official regimen · EMA Rozlytrek product information · updated 27 March 2025

Interactions

Give the team a complete medicine and supplement list, including St John’s wort. CYP3A inhibitors/inducers and medicines affecting heart rhythm need pharmacy review. Do not change the cancer dose yourself; temporary interaction doses differ from toxicity reductions.

NCCP 00702 v2 · official regimen · EMA Rozlytrek product information · updated 27 March 2025

Swallowing difficulty

Ask pharmacy for the approved method. Current SmPC allows capsule suspension and enteral administration; the older NCCP says not to open capsules. Instructions and vomiting advice depend on the formulation.

Living with treatment

Report changes between visits.

Thinking, vision and driving

Do not drive or use machinery while experiencing dizziness, fainting, blurred vision or cognitive effects. Ask family/supporters to report noticeable memory or behaviour changes.

Bones and falls

New focal pain, changed gait or mobility needs prompt review for a fracture; reduce fall hazards where possible.

Pregnancy, fertility and breastfeeding

Avoid pregnancy. Women who could become pregnant need highly effective contraception during treatment and for at least five weeks afterwards; add a barrier method if using systemic hormonal contraception. Men with partners who could become pregnant need highly effective contraception during treatment and for at least three months afterwards. Do not breastfeed during treatment. Discuss fertility and family plans before starting.

EMA Rozlytrek product information · updated 27 March 2025

Support medicines

Use prescribed symptom treatment. No routine steroid, granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (white-cell growth support) (G-CSF) or antimicrobial prophylaxis is specified for this monotherapy; avoid borrowing an infusion antiemetic schedule.

When to get help

Do not wait for the next appointment.

Call urgently

Call the oncology team urgently for new or worsening breathlessness, swelling, palpitations or fainting; marked confusion or memory change; fever, jaundice, severe diarrhoea or inability to drink. Report new visual disturbance and focal bone pain promptly. Follow your unit’s temperature/contact instructions. Severe breathing difficulty, chest pain or collapse needs emergency help.

What studies can tell us

Separate studies found tumour shrinkage and periods of cancer control in ROS1-positive lung cancer and NTRK fusion-positive tumours, including some brain metastases. These studies generally had no randomised comparison group; they do not establish an individual survival benefit or superiority over other targeted medicines.

Your decision

Ask how the correct molecular and tumour-specific study population compares with your situation. Study averages do not establish personal prognosis or rank current therapies.

Practical support

Symptom medicines

NCCP rates entrectinib minimal-to-low oral emetic risk. Agree individual rescue treatment and check heart-rhythm interactions; no routine infusion premedication is specified.

Daily life and medicines

Keep the medicine list current. Avoid driving or machinery while dizzy, faint, visually affected or cognitively affected. Review falls risk and report bone pain.

Pregnancy and breastfeeding

Use the agreed contraception plan during and after treatment. Do not breastfeed during treatment; ask about fertility and family plans before starting.

Questions to ask

Which gene fusion is this treating?

This adult suite covers entrectinib alone for ROS1-positive advanced NSCLC before any ROS1 inhibitor (00702a), or a separate NTRK gene-fusion solid-tumour indication with no previous NTRK inhibitor and no satisfactory treatment options (00702b). Molecular tests and Irish access criteria must be confirmed. The adult 600 mg schedule does not apply to children.

How long do I take it?

The usual adult starting dose is 600 mg by mouth once daily, every day without a planned rest week. Use your prescribed dose. Treatment continues while helping and side effects remain acceptable; there is no fixed number of cycles.

What if I miss or vomit a dose?

Take whole capsules with or without food; do not crush or chew. Avoid grapefruit, grapefruit products and Seville oranges. If a dose is missed, take it unless the next dose is due within 12 hours; confirm the exact boundary with your pharmacist. The leaflet permits repeating whole capsules after immediate vomiting: follow your written plan and call if uncertain. Suspension or tube administration requires separate pharmacy instructions.

Why do I need an ECG?

Entrectinib can affect heart rhythm. ECG/electrolytes are checked before treatment, after one month and periodically as indicated; new fainting or palpitations needs urgent advice.

Can I drive?

Do not drive or use machinery while dizzy, faint, cognitively affected or visually affected. Ask your team when it is safe to resume.

Do I need to enter dates?

No. The timeline shows relative stages. The team books actual tests and reviews the results.
