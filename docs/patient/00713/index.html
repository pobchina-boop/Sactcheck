02 / treatment journey

How the schedule fits together

Cycle 1 · day 1

Nivolumab + ipilimumab + pemetrexed + carboplatin.

Cycle 1 · day 22

Nivolumab + pemetrexed + carboplatin.

After cycle 1

Continue nivolumab on days 1 and 22 and ipilimumab on day 1 of each 42-day cycle.

Duration

Immunotherapy continues until progression/unacceptable toxicity or up to 24 months in patients without progression, according to the NCCP source.

A delay, dose change or omitted medicine can be appropriate. Your team should explain what changed and why.

04 / monitoring and support

What helps make treatment safer

Before and during treatment

  • Folic acid and vitamin B12 support are required with pemetrexed.
  • Blood count, kidney/liver profile and endocrine monitoring support treatment safety.

Bring to each review

  • Use the regimen-specific antiemetic prescription for the chemotherapy phase.
  • Immune adverse events can begin during treatment or after it stops; report new symptoms early.
05 / when to call

Do not wait for the next appointment

Use your oncology SOS instructionsContact oncology urgently for fever, new breathlessness, persistent diarrhoea, jaundice, severe rash, chest pain, marked weakness, confusion, reduced urine or rapidly worsening symptoms. For severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizure or severe chest pain call 112/999.

If your team has given you different local thresholds or emergency instructions, follow those instructions.
