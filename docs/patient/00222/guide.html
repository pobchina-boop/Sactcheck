Pemetrexed monotherapy

Lung · NCCP 00222 · version 5
Treatment journey
Day 1

Pemetrexed 500 mg/m² by IV infusion.

Every 21 days

Treatment repeats every 21 days if review and blood tests allow.

During treatment

Take folic acid as prescribed; vitamin B12 support and steroid rash prophylaxis are part of pemetrexed care.

Duration

For maintenance/second-line use, treatment may continue until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity, according to the treatment plan.

Important medicine-specific effects

Pemetrexed

  • Low blood counts can increase infection, anaemia or bruising risk.
  • Mouth/gut inflammation and skin rash can occur.
  • Kidney function can deteriorate and is monitored.
Other / shared treatment effects: The anatomy page highlights important symptom patterns for this exact regimen, but it cannot show every possible effect. Fatigue, nausea, appetite change, mouth/bowel symptoms or blood-count changes may occur depending on the medicines used. Report new or worsening symptoms even if they are not listed here.

Monitoring / medicines

  • FBC, kidney and liver profile are checked before each cycle.
  • Folic acid must be taken as prescribed during therapy and after the final pemetrexed dose.
  • Vitamin B12 is given according to the regimen schedule.

Bring / track

  • A corticosteroid is used around pemetrexed to reduce skin reactions.
  • Bring a list of current medicines and report new symptoms before the next dose.

When to call - do not wait for the next appointment

Contact oncology urgently for fever or sudden illness, new significant breathlessness, uncontrolled vomiting or diarrhoea, severe mouth soreness preventing fluids, marked reduction in urine, significant bleeding or a rapidly worsening rash. For a medical emergency call 112/999.
